v1 Then Job answered and said,
v2 I know of a truth that it is so: for how shall a mortal man be just before the Lord?
v3 For if he would enter into judgment with him, God would not hearken to him, so that he should answer to one of his charges of a thousand.
v4 For he is wise in mind, and mighty, and great: who has hardened himself against him and endured?
v5 Who wears out the mountains, and men know it not: who overturns them in anger.
v6 Who shakes the earth under heaven from its foundations, and its pillars totter.
v7 Who commands the sun, and it rises not; and he seals up the stars.
v8 Who alone has stretched out the heavens, and walks on the sea as on firm ground.
v9 Who makes Pleias, and Hesperus, and Arcturus, and the chambers of the south.
v10 Who does great and unsearchable things; glorious also and excellent things, innumerable.
v11 If ever he should go beyond me, I shall not see him: if he should pass by me, neither thus have I known it .
v12 If he would take away, who shall turn him back? or who shall say to him, What hast thou done?
v13 For if he has turned away his anger, the whales under heaven have stooped under him.
v14 Oh then that he would hearken to me, or judge my cause.
v15 For though I be righteous, he will not hearken to me: I will intreat his judgment.
v16 And if I should call and he should not hearken, I cannot believe that he has listened to my voice.
v17 Let him not crush me with a dark storm: but he has made my bruises many without cause.
v18 For he suffers me not to take breath, but he has filled me with bitterness.
v19 For indeed he is strong in power: who then shall resist his judgment?
v20 For though I should seem righteous, my mouth will be profane: and though I should seem blameless, I shall be proved perverse.
v21 For even if I have sinned, I know it not in my soul: but my life is taken away.
v22 Wherefore I said, Wrath slays the great and mighty man.
v23 For the worthless die, but the righteous are laughed to scorn.
v24 For they are delivered into the hands of the unrighteous man : he covers the faces of the judges of the earth : but if it be not he, who is it?
v25 But my life is swifter than a post: my days have fled away, and they knew it not.
v26 Or again, is there a trace of their path left by ships? or is there one of the flying eagle as it seeks its prey?
v27 And if I should say, I will forget to speak, I will bow down my face and groan;
v28 I quake in all my limbs, for I know that thou wilt not leave me alone as innocent.
v29 But since I am ungodly, why have I not died?
v30 For if I should wash myself with snow, and purge myself with pure hands,
v31 thou hadst thoroughly plunged me in filth, and my garment had abhorred me.
v32 For thou art not man like me, with whom I could contend, that we might come together to judgment.
v33 Would that he our mediator were present , and a reprover, and one who should hear the cause between both.
v34 Let him remove his rod from me, and let not his fear terrify me:
v35 so shall I not be afraid, but I will speak: for I am not thus conscious of guilt .
