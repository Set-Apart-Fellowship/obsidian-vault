v1 Then Sophar the Minæan answered and said,
v2 He that speaks much, should also hear on the other side: or does the fluent speaker think himself to be righteous? blessed is the short-lived offspring of woman.
v3 Be not a speaker of many words; for is there none to answer thee?
v4 For say not, I am pure in my works, and blameless before him.
v5 But oh that the Lord would speak to thee, and open his lips with thee!
v6 Then shall he declare to thee the power of wisdom; for it shall be double of that which is with thee: and then shalt thou know, that a just recompence of thy sins has come to thee from the Lord.
v7 Wilt thou find out the traces of the Lord? or hast thou come to the end of that which the Almighty has made?
v8 Heaven is high; and what wilt thou do? and there are deeper things than those in hell; what dost thou know?
v9 Or longer than the measure of the earth, or the breadth of the sea.
v10 And if he should overthrow all things, who will say to him, What hast thou done?
v11 For he knows the works of transgressors; and when he sees wickedness, he will not overlook it .
v12 But man vainly buoys himself up with words; and a mortal born of woman is like an ass of the desert.
v13 For if thou hast made thine heart pure, and liftest up thine hands towards him;
v14 if there is any iniquity in thy hands, put it far from thee, and let not unrighteousness lodge in thy habitation.
v15 For thus shall thy countenance shine again, as pure water; and thou shalt divest thyself of uncleanness, and shalt not fear.
v16 And thou shalt forget trouble, as a wave that has passed by; and thou shalt not be scared.
v17 And thy prayer shall be as the morning star, and life shall arise to thee as from the noon-day.
v18 And thou shalt be confident, because thou hast hope; and peace shall dawn to thee from out of anxiety and care.
v19 For thou shalt be at ease, and there shall be no one to fight against thee; and many shall charge, and make supplication to thee.
v20 But safety shall fail them; for their hope is destruction, and the eyes of the ungodly shall waste away.
