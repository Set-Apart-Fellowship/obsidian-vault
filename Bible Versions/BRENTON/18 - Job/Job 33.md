v1 Howbeit hear, Job, my words, and hearken to my speech.
v2 For behold, I have opened my mouth, and my tongue has spoken.
v3 My heart shall be found pure by my words; and the understanding of my lips shall meditate purity.
v4 The Divine Spirit is that which formed me, and the breath of the Almighty that which teaches me.
v5 If thou canst, give me an answer: wait therefore; stand against me, and I will stand against thee.
v6 Thou art formed out of the clay as also I: we have been formed out of the same substance .
v7 My fear shall not terrify thee, neither shall my hand be heavy upon thee.
v8 But thou hast said in mine ears, (I have heard the voice of thy words;) because thou sayest, I am pure, not having sinned;
v9 I am blameless, for I have not transgressed.
v10 Yet he has discovered a charge against me, and he has reckoned me as an adversary.
v11 And he has put my foot in the stocks, and has watched all my ways.
v12 For how sayest thou, I am righteous, yet he has not hearkened to me? for he that is above mortals is eternal.
v13 But thou sayest, Why has he not heard every word of my cause?
v14 For when the Lord speaks once, or a second time,
v15 sending a dream, or in the meditation of the night; (as when a dreadful alarm happens to fall upon men, in slumberings on the bed:)
v16 then opens he the understanding of men: he scares them with such fearful visions:
v17 to turn a man from unrighteousness, and he delivers his body from a fall.
v18 He spares also his soul from death, and suffers him not to fall in war.
v19 And again, he chastens him with sickness on his bed, and the multitude of his bones is benumbed.
v20 And he shall not be able to take any food, though his soul shall desire meat;
v21 until his flesh shall be consumed, and he shall shew his bones bare.
v22 His soul also draws nigh to death, and his life is in Hades.
v23 Though there should be a thousand messengers of death, not one of them shall wound him: if he should purpose in his heart to turn to the Lord, and declare to man his fault, and shew his folly;
v24 he will support him, that he should not perish, and will restore his body as fresh plaster upon a wall; and he will fill his bones with marrow.
v25 And he will make his flesh tender as that of a babe, and he will restore him among men in his full strength.
v26 And he shall pray to the Lord, and his prayer shall be accepted of him; he shall enter with a cheerful countenance, with a full expression of praise : for he will render to men their due.
v27 Even then a man shall blame himself, saying, What kind of things have I done? and he has not punished me according to the full amount of my sins.
v28 Deliver my soul, that it may not go to destruction, and my life shall see the light.
v29 Behold, all these things the Mighty One works in a threefold manner with a man.
v30 And he has delivered my soul from death, that my life may praise him in the light.
v31 Hearken, Job, and hear me: be silent, and I will speak.
v32 If thou hast words, answer me: speak, for I desire thee to be justified.
v33 If not, do thou hear me: be silent, and I will teach thee.
