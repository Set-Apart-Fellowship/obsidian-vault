v1 And Elius continued, and said,
v2 Hear me, ye wise men; hearken, ye that have knowledge.
v3 For the ear tries words, and the mouth tastes meat.
v4 Let us choose judgment to ourselves: let us know among ourselves what is right.
v5 For Job has said, I am righteous: the Lord has removed my judgment.
v6 And he has erred in my judgment: my wound is severe without unrighteousness of mine .
v7 What man is as Job, drinking scorning like water?
v8 saying , I have not sinned, nor committed ungodliness, nor had fellowship with workers of iniquity, to go with the ungodly.
v9 For thou shouldest not say, There shall be no visitation of a man, whereas there is a visitation on him from the Lord.
v10 Wherefore hear me, ye that are wise in heart: far be it from me to sin before the Lord, and to pervert righteousness before the Almighty.
v11 Yea, he renders to a man accordingly as each of them does, and in a man's path he will find him.
v12 And thinkest thou that the Lord will do wrong, or will the Almighty who made the earth wrest judgment?
v13 And who is he that made the whole world under heaven, and all things therein?
v14 For if he would confine, and restrain his spirit with himself;
v15 all flesh would die together, and every mortal would return to the earth, whence also he was formed.
v16 Take heed lest he rebuke thee : hear this, hearken to the voice of words.
v17 Behold then the one that hates iniquities, and that destroys the wicked, who is for ever just.
v18 He is ungodly that says to a king, Thou art a transgressor, that says to princes, O most ungodly one.
v19 Such a one as would not reverence the face of an honourable man, neither knows how to give honour to the great, so as that their persons should be respected.
v20 But it shall turn out vanity to them, to cry and beseech a man; for they dealt unlawfully, the poor being turned aside from their right .
v21 For he surveys the works of men, and nothing of what they do has escaped him.
v22 Neither shall there be a place for the workers of iniquity to hide themselves.
v23 For he will not lay upon a man more than right .
v24 For the Lord looks down upon all men, who comprehends unsearchable things, glorious also and excellent things without number.
v25 Who discovers their works, and will bring night about upon them , and they shall be brought low.
v26 And he quite destroys the ungodly, for they are seen before him.
v27 Because they turned aside from the law of God, and did not regard his ordinances,
v28 so as to bring before him the cry of the needy; for he will hear the cry of the poor.
v29 And he will give quiet, and who will condemn? and he will hide his face, and who shall see him? whether it be done against a nation, or against a man also:
v30 causing a hypocrite to be king, because of the waywardness of the people.
v31 For there is one that says to the Mighty One, I have received blessings ; I will not take a pledge:
v32 I will see apart from myself: do thou shew me if I have done unrighteousness; I will not do so any more.
v33 Will he take vengeance for it on thee, whereas thou wilt put it far from thee ? for thou shalt choose, and not I; and what thou knowest, speak thou.
v34 Because the wise in heart shall say this, and a wise man listens to my word.
v35 But Job has not spoken with understanding, his words are not uttered with knowledge.
v36 Howbeit do thou learn, Job: no longer make answer as the foolish:
v37 that we add not to our sins: for iniquity will be reckoned against us, if we speak many words before the Lord.
