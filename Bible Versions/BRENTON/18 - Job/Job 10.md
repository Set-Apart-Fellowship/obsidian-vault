v1 Weary in my soul, I will pour my words with groans upon him: I will speak being straitened in the bitterness of my soul.
v2 And I will say to the Lord, Do not teach me to be impious; and wherefore hast thou thus judged me?
v3 Is it good before thee if I be unrighteous? for thou hast disowned the work of thy hands, and attended to the counsel of the ungodly.
v4 Or dost thou see as a mortal sees? or wilt thou look as a man sees?
v5 Or is thy life human, or thy years the years of a man,
v6 that thou hast enquired into mine iniquity, and searched out my sins?
v7 For thou knowest that I have not committed iniquity: but who is he that can deliver out of thy hands?
v8 Thy hands have formed me and made me; afterwards thou didst change thy mind , and smite me.
v9 Remember that thou hast made me as clay, and thou dost turn me again to earth.
v10 Hast thou not poured me out like milk, and curdled me like cheese?
v11 And thou didst clothe me with skin and flesh, and frame me with bones and sinews.
v12 And thou didst bestow upon me life and mercy, and thy oversight has preserved my spirit.
v13 Having these things in thyself, I know that thou canst do all things; for nothing is impossible with thee.
v14 And if I should sin, thou watchest me; and thou hast not cleared me from iniquity.
v15 Or if I should be ungodly, woe is me: and if I should be righteous, I cannot lift myself up, for I am full of dishonour.
v16 For I am hunted like a lion for slaughter; for again thou hast changed and art terribly destroying me;
v17 renewing against me my torture: and thou hast dealt with me in great anger, and thou hast brought trials upon me.
v18 Why then didst thou bring me out of the womb? and why did I not die, and no eye see me,
v19 and I become as if I had not been? for why was I not carried from the womb to the grave?
v20 Is not the time of my life short? suffer me to rest a little,
v21 before I go whence I shall not return, to a land of darkness and gloominess;
v22 to a land of perpetual darkness, where there is no light, neither can any one see the life of mortals.
