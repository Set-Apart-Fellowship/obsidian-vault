v1 For a mortal born of a woman is short-lived, and full of wrath.
v2 Or he falls like a flower that has bloomed; and he departs like a shadow, and cannot continue.
v3 Hast thou not taken account even of him, and caused him to enter into judgment before thee?
v4 For who shall be pure from uncleanness? not even one;
v5 if even his life should be but one day upon the earth: and his months are numbered by him: thou hast appointed him for a time, and he shall by no means exceed it .
v6 Depart from him, that he may be quiet, and take pleasure in his life, though as a hireling.
v7 For there is hope for a tree, even if it should be cut down, that it shall blossom again, and its branch shall not fail.
v8 For though its root should grow old in the earth, and its stem die in the rock;
v9 it will blossom from the scent of water, and will produce a crop, as one newly planted.
v10 But a man that has died is utterly gone; and when a mortal has fallen, he is no more.
v11 For the sea wastes in length of time, and a river fails and is dried up.
v12 And man that has lain down in death shall certainly not rise again till the heaven be dissolved, and they shall not awake from their sleep.
v13 For oh that thou hadst kept me in the grave, and hadst hidden me until thy wrath should cease, and thou shouldest set me a time in which thou wouldest remember me!
v14 For if a man should die, shall he live again , having accomplished the days of his life? I will wait till I exist again?
v15 Then shalt thou call, and I will hearken to thee: but do not thou reject the work of thine hands.
v16 But thou hast numbered my devices: and not one of my sins shall escape thee?
v17 And thou hast sealed up my transgressions in a bag, and marked if I have been guilty of any transgression unawares.
v18 And verily a mountain falling will utterly be destroyed, and a rock shall be worn out of its place.
v19 The waters wear the stones, and waters falling headlong overflow a heap of the earth: and thou destroyest the hope of man.
v20 Thou drivest him to an end, and he is gone: thou settest thy face against him, and sendest him away;
v21 and though his children be multiplied, he knows it not; and if they be few, he is not aware.
v22 But his flesh is in pain, and his soul mourns.
