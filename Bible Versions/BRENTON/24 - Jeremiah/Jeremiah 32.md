v15 Thus said the Lord God of Israel; Take the cup of this unmixed wine from mine hand, and thou shalt cause all the nations to drink, to whom I send thee.
v16 And they shall drink, and vomit, and be mad, because of the sword which I send among them.
v17 So I took the cup out of the Lord's hand, and caused the nations to whom the Lord sent me to drink:
v18 Jerusalem, and the cities of Juda, and the kings of Juda, and his princes, to make them a desert place, a desolation, and a hissing;
v19 and Pharao king of Egypt, and his servants, and his nobles, and all his people;
v20 and all the mingled people , and all the kings of the Philistines, and Ascalon, and Gaza, and Accaron, and the remnant of Azotus,
v21 and Idumea, and the land of Moab, and the children of Ammon,
v22 and the kings of Tyre, and the kings of Sidon, and the kings in the country beyond the sea,
v23 and Dædan, and Thæman, and Ros, and every one that is shaved round about the face,
v24 and all the mingled people lodging in the wilderness,
v25 and all the kings of Ælam, and all the kings of the Persians,
v26 and all the kings from the north, the far and the near, each one with his brother, and all the kingdoms which are on the face of the earth.
v27 And thou shalt say to them, Thus said the Lord Almighty; Drink ye, be ye drunken; and ye shall vomit, and shall fall, and shall in nowise rise, because of the sword which I send among you.
v28 And it shall come to pass, when they refuse to take the cup out of thine hand, to drink it, that thou shalt say, Thus said the Lord; Ye shall surely drink.
v29 For I am beginning to afflict the city whereon my name is called, and ye shall by no means be held guiltless: for I am calling a sword upon all that dwell upon the earth.
v30 And thou shalt prophesy against them these words, and shalt say, The Lord shall speak from on high, from his sanctuary he will utter his voice; he will pronounce a declaration on his place; and these shall answer like men gathering grapes: and destruction is coming on them that dwell on the earth,
v31 even upon the extreme part of the earth; for the Lord has a controversy with the nations, he is pleading with all flesh, and the ungodly are given to the sword, saith the Lord.
v32 Thus said the Lord; Behold, evils are proceeding from nation to nation, and a great whirlwind goes forth from the end of the earth.
v33 And the slain of the Lord shall be in the day of the Lord from one end of the earth even to the other end of the earth: they shall not be buried; they shall be as dung on the face of the earth.
v34 Howl, ye shepherds, and cry; and lament, ye rams of the flock: for your days have been completed for slaughter, and ye shall fall as the choice rams.
v35 And flight shall perish from the shepherds, and safety from the rams of the flock.
v36 A voice of the crying of the shepherds, and a moaning of the sheep and the rams: for the Lord has destroyed their pastures.
v37 And the peaceable abodes that remain shall be destroyed before the fierceness of my anger.
v38 He has forsaken his lair, as a lion: for their land is become desolate before the great sword.
