v1 And these are the generations of Esau; this is Edom.
v2 And Esau took to himself wives of the daughters of the Chananites; Ada, the daughter of Ælom the Chettite; and Olibema, daughter of Ana the son of Sebegon, the Evite;
v3 and Basemath, daughter of Ismael, sister of Nabaioth.
v4 And Ada bore to him Eliphas; and Basemath bore Raguel.
v5 And Olibema bore Jeus, and Jeglom, and Core; these are the sons of Esau, which were born to him in the land of Chanaan.
v6 And Esau took his wives, and his sons, and his daughters, and all the persons of his house, and all his possessions, and all his cattle, and all that he had got, and all things whatsoever he had acquired in the land of Chanaan; and Esau went forth from the land of Chanaan, from the face of his brother Jacob.
v7 For their substance was too great for them to dwell together; and the land of their sojourning could not bear them, because of the abundance of their possessions.
v8 And Esau dwelt in mount Seir; Esau, he is Edom.
v9 And these are the generations of Esau, the father of Edom in the mount Seir.
v10 And these are the names of the sons of Esau. Eliphas, the son of Ada, the wife of Esau; and Raguel, the son of Basemath, wife of Esau.
v11 And the sons of Eliphas were Thæman, Omar, Sophar, Gothom, and Kenez.
v12 And Thamna was a concubine of Eliphaz, the son of Esau; and she bore Amalec to Eliphas. These are the sons of Ada, the wife of Esau.
v13 And these are the sons of Raguel; Nachoth, Zare, Some, and Moze. These were the sons of Basemath, wife of Esau.
v14 And these are the sons of Olibema, the daughter of Ana, the son of Sebegon, the wife of Esau; and she bore to Esau, Jeus, and Jeglom, and Core.
v15 These are the chiefs of the son of Esau, even the sons of Eliphas, the first-born of Esau; chief Thæman, chief Omar, chief Sophar, chief Kenez,
v16 chief Core, chief Gothom, chief Amalec. These are the chiefs of Eliphas, in the land of Edom; these are the sons of Ada.
v17 And these are the sons of Raguel, the son of Esau; chief Nachoth, chief Zare, chief Some, chief Moze. These are the chiefs of Raguel, in the land of Edom; these are the sons of Basemath, wife of Esau.
v18 And these are the sons of Olibema, wife of Esau; chief Jeus, chief Jeglom, chief Core. These are the chiefs of Olibema, daughter of Ana, wife of Esau.
v19 These are the sons of Esau, and these are the chiefs; these are the sons of Edom.
v20 And these are the sons of Seir, the Chorrhite, who inhabited the land; Lotan, Sobal, Sebegon, Ana,
v21 and Deson, and Asar, and Rison. These are the chiefs of the Chorrhite, the son of Seir, in the land of Edom.
v22 And the sons of Lotan were Chorrhi and Hæman; and the sister of Lotan, Thamna.
v23 And these are the sons of Sobal; Golam, and Manachath, and Gæbel, and Sophar, and Omar.
v24 And these are the sons of Sebegon; Aïe, and Ana; this is the Ana who found Jamin in the wilderness, when he tended the beasts of his father Sebegon.
v25 And these are the sons of Ana; Deson—and Olibema was daughter of Ana.
v26 And these are the sons of Deson; Amada, and Asban, and Ithran, and Charrhan.
v27 And these are the sons of Asar; Balaam, and Zucam, and Jucam.
v28 And these are the sons of Rison; Hos, and Aran.
v29 And these are the chiefs of Chorri; chief Lotan, chief Sobal, chief Sebegon, chief Ana,
v30 chief Deson, chief Asar, chief Rison. These are the chiefs of Chorri, in their principalities in the land of Edom.
v31 And these are the kings which reigned in Edom, before a king reigned in Israel.
v32 And Balac, son of Beor, reigned in Edom; and the name of his city was Dennaba.
v33 And Balac died; and Jobab, son of Zara, from Bosorrha reigned in his stead.
v34 And Jobab died; and Asom, from the land of the Thæmanites, reigned in his stead.
v35 And Asom died; and Adad son of Barad, who cut off Madiam in the plain of Moab, ruled in his stead; and the name of his city was Getthaim.
v36 And Adad died; and Samada of Massecca reigned in his stead.
v37 And Samada died; and Saul of Rhoöboth by the river reigned in his stead.
v38 And Saul died; and Ballenon the son of Achobor reigned in his stead.
v39 And Ballenon the son of Achobor died; and Arad the son of Barad reigned in his stead; and the name of his city was Phogor; and the name of his wife was Metebeel, daughter of Matraith, son of Maizoöb.
v40 These are the names of the chiefs of Esau, in their tribes, according to their place, in their countries, and in their nations; chief Thamna, chief Gola, chief Jether,
v41 chief Olibema, chief Helas, chief Phinon,
v42 chief Kenez, chief Thæman, chief Mazar,
v43 chief Magediel, chief Zaphoin. These are the chiefs of Edom in their dwelling-places, in the land of their possession; this is Esau, the father of Edom.
