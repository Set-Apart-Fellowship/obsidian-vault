v1 And Moses gathered all the congregation of the children of Israel together, and said, These are the words which the Lord has spoken for you to do them.
v2 Six days shalt thou perform works, but on the seventh day shall be rest—a holy sabbath—a rest for the Lord: every one that does work on it, let him die.
v3 Ye shall not burn a fire in any of your dwellings on the sabbath-day; I am the Lord.
v4 And Moses spoke to all the congregation of the children of Israel, saying, This is the thing which the Lord has appointed you, saying,
v5 Take of yourselves an offering for the Lord: every one that engages in his heart shall bring the first-fruits to the Lord; gold, silver, brass,
v6 blue, purple, double scarlet spun, and fine linen spun, and goats' hair,
v7 and rams' skins dyed red, and skins dyed blue, and incorruptible wood,
v9 and sardine stones, and stones for engraving for the shoulder-piece and full-length robe.
v10 And every man that is wise in heart among you, let him come and work all things whatsoever the Lord has commanded.
v11 The tabernacle, and the cords, and the coverings, and the rings, and the bars, and the posts,
v12 and the ark of the testimony, and its staves, and its propitiatory, and the veil,
v13 and the table and all its furniture,
v14 and the candlestick for the light and all its furniture,
v16 and the altar and all its furniture;
v19 and the holy garments of Aaron the priest, and the garments in which they shall do service; and the garments of priesthood for the sons of Aaron and the anointing oil, and the compound incense.
v20 And all the congregation of the children of Israel went out from Moses. And they brought, they whose heart prompted them, and they to whomsoever it seemed good in their mind, each an offering:
v21 and they brought an offering to the Lord for all the works of the tabernacle of witness, and all its services, and for all the robes of the sanctuary.
v22 And the men, even every one to whom it seemed good in his heart, brought from the women, even brought seals and ear-rings, and finger-rings, and necklaces, and bracelets, every article of gold.
v23 And all as many as brought ornaments of gold to the Lord, and with whomsoever fine linen was found; and they brought skins dyed blue, and rams' skins dyed red.
v24 And every one that offered an offering brought silver and brass, the offerings to the Lord; and they with whom was found incorruptible wood; and they brought offerings for all the works of the preparation.
v25 And every woman skilled in her heart to spin with her hands, brought spun articles , the blue, and purple, and scarlet and fine linen.
v26 And all the women to whom it seemed good in their heart in their wisdom, spun the goats' hair.
v27 And the rulers brought the emerald stones, and the stones for setting in the ephod, and the oracle,
v28 and the compounds both for the anointing oil, and the composition of the incense.
v29 And every man and woman whose mind inclined them to come in and do all the works as many as the Lord appointed them to do by Moses— they the children of Israel brought an offering to the Lord.
v30 And Moses said to the children of Israel, Behold, God has called by name Beseleel the son of Urias the son of Or, of the tribe of Juda,
v31 and has filled him with a divine spirit of wisdom and understanding, and knowledge of all things,
v32 to labour skilfully in all works of cunning workmanship, to form the gold and the silver and the brass,
v33 and to work in stone, and to fashion the wood, and to work in every work of wisdom.
v34 And God gave improvement in understanding both to him, and to Eliab the son of Achisamach of the tribe of Dan.
v35 And God filled them with wisdom, understanding and perception, to understand to work all the works of the sanctuary, and to weave the woven and embroidered work with scarlet and fine linen, to do all work of curious workmanship and embroidery.
