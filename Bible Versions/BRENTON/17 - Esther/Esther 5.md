v1 And it came to pass on the third day, when she had ceased praying, that she put off her mean dress, and put on her glorious apparel.
v2 And having raised the golden sceptre he laid it upon her neck, and embraced her, and said, Speak to me.
v3 And the king said, What wilt thou, Esther? and what is thy request? ask even to the half of my kingdom, and it shall be thine.
v4 And Esther said, To-day is my great day: if then it seem good to the king, let both him and Aman come to the feast which I will prepare this day.
v5 And the king said, Hasten Aman hither, that we may perform the word of Esther. So they both come to the feast of which Esther had spoken.
v6 And at the banquet the king said to Esther, What is thy request , queen Esther? speak , and thou shalt have all that thou requirest.
v7 And she said, My request and my petition are :
v8 if I have found favour in the sight of the king, let the king and Aman come again to-morrow to the feast which I shall prepare for them, and to-morrow I will do the same.
v9 So Aman went out from the king very glad and merry: but when Aman saw Mardochæus the Jew in the court, he was greatly enraged.
v10 And having gone into his own house, he called his friends, and his wife Zosara.
v11 And he shewed them his wealth, and the glory with which the king had invested him, and how he had caused him to take precedence and bear chief rule in the kingdom.
v12 And Aman said, The queen has called no one to the feast with the king but me, and I am invited to-morrow.
v13 But these things please me not, while I see Mardochæus the Jew in the court.
v14 And Zosara his wife and his friends said to him, Let there be a gallows made for thee of fifty cubits, and in the morning do thou speak to the king, and let Mardochæus be hanged on the gallows: but do thou go in to the feast with the king, and be merry. And the saying pleased Aman, and the gallows was prepared.
