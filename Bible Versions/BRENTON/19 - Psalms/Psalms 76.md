v1 For the end, for Idithun, a Psalm of Asaph.
v2 I cried to the Lord with my voice, yea, my voice was addressed to God; and he gave heed to me.
v3 In the day of mine affliction I earnestly sought the Lord; even with my hands by night before him, and I was not deceived; my soul refused to be comforted.
v4 I remembered God, and rejoiced; I poured out my complaint, and my soul fainted. Pause.
v5 All mine enemies set a watch against me : I was troubled, and spoke not.
v6 I considered the days of old, and remembered ancient years.
v7 And I meditated; I communed with my heart by night, and diligently searched my spirit, saying ,
v8 Will the Lord cast off for ever? and will he be well pleased no more?
v9 Will he cut off his mercy for ever, even for ever and ever?
v10 Will God forget to pity? or will he shut up his compassions in his wrath? Pause.
v11 And I said, Now I have begun; this is the change of the right hand of the Most High.
v12 I remembered the works of the Lord; for I will remember thy wonders from the beginning.
v13 And I will meditate on all thy works, and will consider thy doings.
v14 O God, thy way is in the sanctuary; who is a great God as our God?
v15 Thou art the God that doest wonders; thou hast made known thy power among the nations.
v16 Thou hast with thine arm redeemed thy people, the sons of Jacob and Joseph. Pause.
v17 The waters saw thee, O God, the waters saw thee, and feared; and the depths were troubled.
v18 There was an abundant sound of waters: the clouds uttered a voice; for thine arrows went abroad.
v19 The voice of thy thunder was abroad, and around thy lightnings appeared to the world; the earth trembled and quaked.
v20 Thy way is in the sea, and thy paths in many waters, and thy footsteps cannot be known.
v21 Thou didst guide thy people as sheep by the hand of Moses and Aaron.
