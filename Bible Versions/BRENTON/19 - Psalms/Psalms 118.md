v1 Alleluia. Blessed are the blameless in the way, who walk in the law of the Lord.
v2 Blessed are they that search out his testimonies: they will diligently seek him with the whole heart.
v3 For they that work iniquity have not walked in his ways.
v4 Thou hast commanded us diligently to keep thy precepts.
v5 O that my ways were directed to keep thine ordinances.
v6 Then shall I not be ashamed, when I have respect to all thy commandments.
v7 I will give thee thanks with uprightness of heart, when I have learnt the judgments of thy righteousness.
v8 I will keep thine ordinances: O forsake me not greatly.
v9 Wherewith shall a young man direct his way? by keeping thy words.
v10 With my whole heart have I diligently sought thee: cast me not away from thy commandments.
v11 I have hidden thine oracles in my heart, that I might not sin against thee.
v12 Blessed art thou, O Lord: teach me thine ordinances.
v13 With my lips have I declared all the judgments of thy mouth.
v14 I have delighted in the way of thy testimonies, as much as in all riches.
v15 I will meditate on thy commandments, and consider thy ways.
v16 I will meditate on thine ordinances: I will not forget thy words.
v17 Render a recompence to thy servant: so shall I live, and keep thy words.
v18 Unveil thou mine eyes, and I shall perceive wondrous things out of thy law.
v19 I am a stranger in the earth: hide not thy commandments from me.
v20 My soul has longed exceedingly for thy judgments at all times.
v21 Thou hast rebuked the proud: cursed are they that turn aside from thy commandments.
v22 Remove from me reproach and contempt; for I have sought out thy testimonies.
v23 For princes sat and spoke against me: but thy servant was meditating on thine ordinances.
v24 For thy testimonies are my meditation, and thine ordinances are my counsellors.
v25 My soul has cleaved to the ground; quicken thou me according to thy word.
v26 I declared my ways, and thou didst hear me; teach me thine ordinances.
v27 Instruct me in the way of thine ordinances; and I will meditate on thy wondrous works.
v28 My soul has slumbered for sorrow; strengthen thou me with thy words.
v29 Remove from me the way of iniquity; and be merciful to me by thy law.
v30 I have chosen the way of truth; and have not forgotten thy judgments.
v31 I have cleaved to thy testimonies, O Lord; put me not to shame.
v32 I ran the way of thy commandments, when thou didst enlarge my heart.
v33 Teach me, O Lord, the way of thine ordinances, and I will seek it out continually.
v34 Instruct me, and I will search out thy law, and will keep it with my whole heart.
v35 Guide me in the path of thy commandments; for I have delighted in it.
v36 Incline mine heart to thy testimonies, and not to covetousness.
v37 Turn away mine eyes that I may not behold vanity: quicken thou me in thy way.
v38 Confirm thine oracle to thy servant, that he may fear thee.
v39 Take away my reproach which I have feared: for thy judgments are good.
v40 Behold, I have desired thy commandments: quicken me in thy righteousness.
v41 And let thy mercy come upon me, O Lord; even thy salvation, according to thy word.
v42 And so I shall render an answer to them that reproach me: for I have trusted in thy words.
v43 And take not the word of truth utterly out of my mouth; for I have hoped in thy judgments.
v44 So shall I keep thy law continually, for ever and ever.
v45 I walked also at large: for I sought out thy commandments.
v46 And I spoke of thy testimonies before kings, and was not ashamed.
v47 And I meditated on thy commandments, which I loved exceedingly.
v48 And I lifted up my hands to thy commandments which I loved; and I meditated in thine ordinances.
v49 Remember thy words to thy servant, wherein thou hast made me hope.
v50 This has comforted me in mine affliction: for thine oracle has quickened me.
v51 The proud have transgressed exceedingly; but I swerved not from thy law.
v52 I remembered thy judgments of old, O Lord; and was comforted.
v53 Despair took hold upon me, because of the sinners who forsake thy law.
v54 Thine ordinances were my songs in the place of my sojourning.
v55 I remembered thy name, O Lord, in the night, and kept thy law.
v56 This I had, because I diligently sought thine ordinances.
v57 Thou art my portion, O Lord: I said that I would keep thy law.
v58 I besought thy favour with my whole heart: have mercy upon me according to thy word.
v59 I thought on thy ways, and turned my feet to thy testimonies.
v60 I prepared myself, (and was not terrified,) to keep thy commandments.
v61 The snares of sinners entangled me: but I forgot not thy law.
v62 At midnight I arose, to give thanks to thee for the judgments of thy righteousness.
v63 I am a companion of all them that fear thee, and of them that keep thy commandments.
v64 O Lord, the earth is full of thy mercy: teach me thine ordinances.
v65 Thou hast wrought kindly with thy servant, O Lord, according to thy word.
v66 Teach me kindness, and instruction, and knowledge: for I have believed thy commandments.
v67 Before I was afflicted, I transgressed; therefore have I kept thy word.
v68 Good art thou, O Lord; therefore in thy goodness teach me thine ordinances.
v69 The injustice of the proud has been multiplied against me: but I will search out thy commandments with all my heart.
v70 Their heart has been curdled like milk; but I have meditated on thy law.
v71 It is good for me that thou hast afflicted me; that I might learn thine ordinances.
v72 The law of thy mouth is better to me than thousands of gold and silver.
v73 Thy hands have made me, and fashioned me: instruct me, that I may learn thy commandments.
v74 They that fear thee will see me and rejoice: for I have hoped in thy words.
v75 I know, O Lord, that thy judgments are righteousness, and that thou in truthfulness hast afflicted me.
v76 Let, I pray thee, thy mercy be to comfort me, according to thy word to thy servant.
v77 Let thy compassions come to me, that I may live: for thy law is my meditation.
v78 Let the proud be ashamed; for they transgressed against me unjustly: but I will meditate in thy commandments.
v79 Let those that fear thee, and those that know thy testimonies, turn to me.
v80 Let mine heart be blameless in thine ordinances, that I may not be ashamed.
v81 My soul faints for thy salvation: I have hoped in thy words.
v82 Mine eyes failed in waiting for thy word, saying, When wilt thou comfort me?
v83 For I am become as a bottle in the frost: yet I have not forgotten thine ordinances.
v84 How many are the days of thy servant? when wilt thou execute judgment for me on them that persecute me?
v85 Transgressors told me idle tales ; but not according to thy law, O Lord.
v86 All thy commandments are truth; they persecuted me unjustly; help thou me.
v87 They nearly made an end of me in the earth; but I forsook not thy commandments.
v88 Quicken me according to thy mercy; so shall I keep the testimonies of thy mouth.
v89 Thy word, O Lord, abides in heaven for ever.
v90 Thy truth endures to all generations; thou hast founded the earth, and it abides.
v91 The day continues by thy arrangement; for all things are thy servants.
v92 Were it not that thy law is my meditation, then I should have perished in mine affliction.
v93 I will never forget thine ordinances; for with them thou hast quickened me.
v94 I am thine, save me; for I have sought out thine ordinances.
v95 Sinners laid wait for me to destroy me; but I understood thy testimonies.
v96 I have seen an end of all perfection; but thy commandment is very broad.
v97 How I have loved thy law, O Lord! it is my meditation all the day.
v98 Thou hast made me wiser than mine enemies in thy commandment; for it is mine for ever.
v99 I have more understanding than all my teachers; for thy testimonies are my meditation.
v100 I understand more than the aged; because I have sought out thy commandments.
v101 I have kept back my feet from every evil way, that I might keep thy words.
v102 I have not declined from thy judgments; for thou hast instructed me.
v103 How sweet are thine oracles to my throat! more so than honey to my mouth!
v104 I gain understanding by thy commandments: therefore I have hated every way of unrighteousness.
v105 Thy law is a lamp to my feet, and a light to my paths.
v106 I have sworn and determined to keep the judgments of thy righteousness.
v107 I have been very greatly afflicted, O Lord: quicken me, according to thy word.
v108 Accept, I pray thee, O Lord, the freewill-offerings of my mouth, and teach me thy judgments.
v109 My soul is continually in thine hands; and I have not forgotten thy law.
v110 Sinners spread a snare for me; but I erred not from thy commandments.
v111 I have inherited thy testimonies for ever; for they are the joy of my heart.
v112 I have inclined my heart to perform thine ordinances for ever, in return for thy mercies .
v113 I have hated transgressors; but I have loved thy law.
v114 Thou art my helper and my supporter; I have hoped in thy words.
v115 Depart from me, ye evil-doers; for I will search out the commandments of my God.
v116 Uphold me according to thy word, and quicken me; and make me not ashamed of my expectation.
v117 Help me, and I shall be saved; and I will meditate in thine ordinances continually.
v118 Thou hast brought to nought all that depart from thine ordinances; for their inward thought is unrighteous.
v119 I have reckoned all the sinners of the earth as transgressors; therefore have I loved thy testimonies.
v120 Penetrate my flesh with thy fear; for I am afraid of thy judgments.
v121 I have done judgment and justice; deliver me not up to them that injure me.
v122 Receive thy servant for good: let not the proud accuse me falsely.
v123 Mine eyes have failed for thy salvation, and for the word of thy righteousness.
v124 Deal with thy servant according to thy mercy, and teach me thine ordinances.
v125 I am thy servant; instruct me, and I shall know thy testimonies.
v126 It is time for the Lord to work: they have utterly broken thy law.
v127 Therefore have I loved thy commandments more than gold, or the topaz.
v128 Therefore I directed myself according to all thy commandments: I have hated every unjust way.
v129 Thy testimonies are wonderful: therefore my soul has sought them out.
v130 The manifestation of thy words will enlighten, and instruct the simple.
v131 I opened my mouth, and drew breath: for I earnestly longed after thy commandments.
v132 Look upon me and have mercy upon me, after the manner of them that love thy name.
v133 Order my steps according to thy word: and let not any iniquity have dominion over me.
v134 Deliver me from the false accusation of men: so will I keep thy commandments.
v135 Cause thy face to shine upon thy servant: and teach me thine ordinances.
v136 Mine eyes have been bathed in streams of water, because I kept not thy law.
v137 Righteous art thou, O Lord, and upright are thy judgments.
v138 Thou hast commanded righteousness and perfect truth, as thy testimonies.
v139 Thy zeal has quite wasted me: because mine enemies have forgotten thy words.
v140 Thy word has been very fully tried; and thy servant loves it.
v141 I am young and despised: yet I have not forgotten thine ordinances.
v142 Thy righteousness is an everlasting righteousness, and thy law is truth.
v143 Afflictions and distresses found me: but thy commandments were my meditation.
v144 Thy testimonies are an everlasting righteousness: instruct me, and I shall live.
v145 I cried with my whole heart; hear me, O Lord: I will search out thine ordinances.
v146 I cried to thee; save me, and I will keep thy testimonies.
v147 I arose before the dawn, and cried: I hoped in thy words.
v148 Mine eyes prevented the dawn, that I might meditate on thine oracles.
v149 Hear my voice, O Lord, according to thy mercy; quicken me according to thy judgment.
v150 They have drawn nigh who persecuted me unlawfully; and they are far removed from thy law.
v151 Thou art near, O Lord; and all thy ways are truth.
v152 I have known of old concerning thy testimonies, that thou hast founded them for ever.
v153 Look upon mine affliction, and rescue me; for I have not forgotten thy law.
v154 Plead my cause, and ransom me: quicken me because of thy word.
v155 Salvation is far from sinners: for they have not searched out thine ordinances.
v156 Thy mercies, O Lord, are many: quicken me according to thy judgment.
v157 Many are they that persecute me and oppress me: but I have not declined from thy testimonies.
v158 I beheld men acting foolishly, and I pined away; for they kept not thine oracles.
v159 Behold, I have loved thy commandments, O Lord: quicken me in thy mercy.
v160 The beginning of thy words is truth; and all the judgments of thy righteousness endure for ever.
v161 Princes persecuted me without a cause, but my heart feared because of thy words.
v162 I will exult because of thine oracles, as one that finds much spoil.
v163 I hate and abhor unrighteousness; but I love thy law.
v164 Seven times in a day have I praised thee because of the judgments of thy righteousness.
v165 Great peace have they that love thy law: and there is no stumbling-block to them.
v166 I waited for thy salvation, O Lord, and have loved thy commandments.
v167 My soul has kept thy testimonies, and loved them exceedingly.
v168 I have kept thy commandments and thy testimonies; for all my ways are before thee, O Lord.
v169 Let my supplication come near before thee, O Lord; instruct me according to thine oracle.
v170 Let my petition come in before thee, O Lord; deliver me according to thine oracle.
v171 Let my lips utter a hymn, when thou shalt have taught me thine ordinances.
v172 Let my tongue utter thine oracles; for all thy commandments are righteous.
v173 Let thine hand be prompt to save me; for I have chosen thy commandments.
v174 I have longed after thy salvation, O Lord; and thy law is my meditation.
v175 My soul shall live, and shall praise thee; and thy judgments shall help me.
v176 I have gone astray like a lost sheep; seek thy servant; for I have not forgotten thy commandments.
