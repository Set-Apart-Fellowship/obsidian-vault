v1 A Psalm of David. Bless the Lord, O my soul. O Lord my God, thou art very great; thou hast clothed thyself with praise and honour:
v2 who dost robe thyself with light as with a garment; spreading out the heaven as a curtain.
v3 Who covers his chambers with waters; who makes the clouds his chariot; who walks on the wings of the wind.
v4 Who makes his angels spirits, and his ministers a flaming fire.
v5 Who establishes the earth on her sure foundation: it shall not be moved for ever.
v6 The deep, as it were a garment, is his covering: the waters shall stand on the hills.
v7 At thy rebuke they shall flee; at the voice of thy thunder they shall be alarmed.
v8 They go up to the mountains, and down to the plains, to the place which thou hast founded for them.
v9 Thou hast set a bound which they shall not pass, neither shall they turn again to cover the earth.
v10 He sends forth his fountains among the valleys: the waters shall run between the mountains.
v11 They shall give drink to all the wild beasts of the field: the wild asses shall take of them to quench their thirst.
v12 By them shall the birds of the sky lodge: they shall utter a voice out of the midst of the rocks.
v13 He waters the mountains from his chambers: the earth shall be satisfied with the fruit of thy works.
v14 He makes grass to grow for the cattle, and green herb for the service of men, to bring bread out of the earth;
v15 and wine makes glad the heart of man, to make his face cheerful with oil: and bread strengthens man's heart.
v16 The trees of the plain shall be full of sap; even the cedars of Libanus which he has planted.
v17 There the sparrows will build their nests; and the house of the heron takes the lead among them.
v18 The high mountains are a refuge for the stags, and the rock for the rabbits.
v19 He appointed the moon for seasons: the sun knows his going down.
v20 Thou didst make darkness, and it was night; in it all the wild beasts of the forest will be abroad:
v21 even young lions roaring for prey, and to seek meat for themselves from God.
v22 The sun arises, and they shall be gathered together, and shall lie down in their dens.
v23 Man shall go forth to his work, and to his labour till evening.
v24 How great are thy works, O Lord! in wisdom hast thou wrought them all: the earth is filled with thy creation.
v25 So is this great and wide sea: there are things creeping innumerable, small animals and great.
v26 There go the ships; and this dragon whom thou hast made to play in it.
v27 All wait upon thee, to give them their food in due season.
v28 When thou hast given it them, they will gather it ; and when thou hast opened thine hand, they shall all be filled with good.
v29 But when thou hast turned away thy face, they shall be troubled: thou wilt take away their breath, and they shall fail, and return to their dust.
v30 Thou shalt send forth thy Spirit, and they shall be created; and thou shalt renew the face of the earth.
v31 Let the glory of the Lord be for ever: the Lord shall rejoice in his works;
v32 who looks upon the earth, and makes it tremble; who touches the mountains, and they smoke.
v33 I will sing to the Lord while I live; I will sing praise to my God while I exist.
v34 Let my meditation be sweet to him: and I will rejoice in the Lord.
v35 Let the sinners fail from off the earth, and transgressors, so that they shall be no more. Bless the Lord, O my soul.
