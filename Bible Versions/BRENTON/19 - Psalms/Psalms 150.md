v1 Alleluia. Praise God in his holy places: praise him in the firmament of his power.
v2 Praise him on account of his mighty acts: praise him according to his abundant greatness.
v3 Praise him with the sound of a trumpet: praise him with psaltery and harp.
v4 Praise him with timbrel and dance: praise him with stringed instruments and the organ.
v5 Praise him with melodious cymbals: praise him with loud cymbals.
v6 Let every thing that has breath praise the Lord.
