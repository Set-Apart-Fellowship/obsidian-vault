v1 A Psalm of David for the fourth day of the week. The Lord is a God of vengeance; the God of vengeance has declared himself.
v2 Be thou exalted, thou that judgest the earth: render a reward to the proud.
v3 How long shall sinners, O Lord, how long shall sinners boast?
v4 They will utter and speak unrighteousness; all the workers of iniquity will speak so .
v5 They have afflicted thy people, O Lord, and hurt thine heritage.
v6 They have slain the widow and fatherless, and murdered the stranger.
v7 And they said, The Lord shall not see, neither shall the God of Jacob understand.
v8 Understand now, ye simple among the people; and ye fools, at length be wise.
v9 He that planted the ear, does he not hear? or he that formed the eye, does not he perceive?
v10 He that chastises the heathen, shall not he punish, even he that teaches man knowledge?
v11 The Lord knows the thoughts of men, that they are vain.
v12 Blessed is the man whomsoever thou shalt chasten, O Lord, and shalt teach him out of thy law;
v13 to give him rest from evil days, until a pit be digged for the sinful one.
v14 For the Lord will not cast off his people, neither will he forsake his inheritance;
v15 until righteousness return to judgment, and all the upright in heart shall follow it. Pause.
v16 Who will rise up for me against the transgressors? or who will stand up with me against the workers of iniquity?
v17 If the Lord had not helped me, my soul had almost sojourned in Hades.
v18 If I said, My foot has been moved;
v19 thy mercy, O Lord, helped me. O Lord, according to the multitude of my griefs within my heart, thy consolations have soothed my soul.
v20 Shall the throne of iniquity have fellowship with thee, which frames mischief by an ordinance?
v21 They will hunt for the soul of the righteous, and condemn innocent blood.
v22 But the Lord was my refuge; and my God the helper of my hope.
v23 And he will recompense to them their iniquity and their wickedness: the Lord our God shall utterly destroy them.
