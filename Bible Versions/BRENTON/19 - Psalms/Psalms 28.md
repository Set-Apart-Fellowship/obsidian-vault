v1 A Psalm of David on the occasion of the solemn assembly of the Tabernacle. Bring to the Lord, ye sons of God, bring to the Lord young rams; bring to the Lord glory and honour.
v2 Bring to the Lord glory, due to his name; worship the Lord in his holy court.
v3 The voice of the Lord is upon the waters: the God of glory has thundered: the Lord is upon many waters.
v4 The voice of the Lord is mighty; the voice of the Lord is full of majesty.
v5 There is the voice of the Lord who breaks the cedars; the Lord will break the cedars of Libanus.
v6 And he will beat them small, even Libanus itself, like a calf; and the beloved one is as a young unicorn.
v7 There is a voice of the Lord who divides a flame of fire.
v8 A voice of the Lord who shakes the wilderness; the Lord will shake the wilderness of Cades.
v9 The voice of the Lord strengthens the hinds, and will uncover the thickets: and in his temple every one speaks of his glory.
v10 The Lord will dwell on the waterflood: and the Lord will sit a king for ever.
v11 The Lord will give strength to his people; the Lord will bless his people with peace.
