v1 A Psalm of instruction for Ætham the Israelite.
v2 I will sing of thy mercies, O Lord, for ever: I will declare thy truth with my mouth to all generations.
v3 For thou hast said, Mercy shall be built up for ever: thy truth shall be established in the heavens.
v4 I made a covenant with my chosen ones, I sware unto David my servant.
v5 I will establish thy seed for ever, and build up thy throne to all generations. Pause.
v6 The heavens shall declare thy wonders, O Lord; and thy truth in the assembly of the saints.
v7 For who in the heavens shall be compared to the Lord? and who shall be likened to the Lord among the sons of God?
v8 God is glorified in the council of the saints; great and terrible toward all that are round about him.
v9 O Lord God of hosts, who is like to thee? thou art mighty, O Lord, and thy truth is round about thee.
v10 Thou rulest the power of the sea; and thou calmest the tumult of its waves.
v11 Thou hast brought down the proud as one that is slain; and with the arm of thy power thou hast scattered thine enemies.
v12 The heavens are thine, and the earth is thine: thou hast founded the world, and the fulness of it.
v13 Thou hast created the north and the west: Thabor and Hermon shall rejoice in thy name.
v14 Thine is the mighty arm: let thy hand be strengthened, let thy right hand be exalted.
v15 Justice and judgment are the establishment of thy throne: mercy and truth shall go before thy face.
v16 Blessed is the people that knows the joyful sound: they shall walk, O Lord, in the light of thy countenance.
v17 And in thy name shall they rejoice all the day: and in thy righteousness shall they be exalted.
v18 For thou art the boast of their strength; and in thy good pleasure shall our horn be exalted,
v19 for our help is of the Lord; and of the Holy One of Israel, our king.
v20 Then thou spokest in vision to thy children, and saidst, I have laid help on a mighty one; I have exalted one chosen out of my people.
v21 I have found David my servant; I have anointed him by my holy mercy.
v22 For my hand shall support him; and mine arm shall strengthen him.
v23 The enemy shall have no advantage against him; and the son of transgression shall not hurt him again.
v24 And I will hew down his foes before him, and put to flight those that hate him.
v25 But my truth and my mercy shall be with him; and in my name shall his horn be exalted.
v26 And I will set his hand in the sea, and his right hand in the rivers.
v27 He shall call upon me, saying , Thou art my Father, my God, and the helper of my salvation.
v28 And I will make him my first-born, higher than the kings of the earth.
v29 I will keep my mercy for him for ever, and my covenant shall be firm with him.
v30 And I will establish his seed for ever and ever, and his throne as the days of heaven.
v31 If his children should forsake my law, and walk not in my judgments;
v32 if they should profane my ordinances, and not keep my commandments;
v33 I will visit their transgressions with a rod, and their sins with scourges.
v34 But my mercy I will not utterly remove from him, nor wrong my truth.
v35 Neither will I by any means profane my covenant; and I will not make void the things that proceed out of my lips.
v36 Once have I sworn by my holiness, that I will not lie to David.
v37 His seed shall endure for ever, and his throne as the sun before me;
v38 and as the moon that is established for ever, and as the faithful witness in heaven. Pause.
v39 But thou hast cast off and set at nought, thou hast rejected thine anointed.
v40 Thou hast overthrown the covenant of thy servant; thou hast profaned his sanctuary, casting it to the ground.
v41 Thou hast broken down all his hedges; thou hast made his strong holds a terror.
v42 All that go by the way have spoiled him: he is become a reproach to his neighbours.
v43 Thou hast exalted the right hand of his enemies; thou hast made all his enemies to rejoice.
v44 Thou hast turned back the help of his sword, and hast not helped him in the battle.
v45 Thou hast deprived him of glory: thou hast broken down his throne to the ground.
v46 Thou hast shortened the days of his throne: thou hast poured shame upon him. Pause.
v47 How long, O Lord, wilt thou turn away, for ever? shall thine anger flame out as fire?
v48 Remember what my being is: for hast thou created all the sons of men in vain?
v49 What man is there who shall live, and not see death? shall any one deliver his soul from the hand of Hades? Pause.
v50 Where are thine ancient mercies, O Lord, which thou swarest to David in thy truth?
v51 Remember, O Lord, the reproach of thy servants, which I have borne in my bosom, even the reproach of many nations;
v52 wherewith thine enemies have reviled, O Lord: wherewith they have reviled the recompence of thine anointed.
v53 Blessed be the Lord for ever. So be it, so be it.
