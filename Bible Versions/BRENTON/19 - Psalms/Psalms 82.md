v1 A Song of a Psalm for Asaph.
v2 O God, who shall be compared to thee? be not silent, neither be still, O God.
v3 For behold, thine enemies have made a noise; and they that hate thee have lifted up the head.
v4 Against thy people they have craftily imagined a device, and have taken counsel against thy saints.
v5 They have said, Come, and let us utterly destroy them out of the nation; and let the name of Israel be remembered no more at all.
v6 For they have taken counsel together with one consent: they have made a confederacy against thee;
v7 even the tents of the Idumeans, and the Ismaelites; Moab, and the Agarenes;
v8 Gebal, and Ammon, and Amalec; the Philistines also, with them that dwell at Tyre.
v9 Yea, Assur too is come with them: they have become a help to the children of Lot. Pause.
v10 Do thou to them as to Madiam, and to Sisera; as to Jabin at the brook of Kison.
v11 They were utterly destroyed at Aendor: they became as dung for the earth.
v12 Make their princes as Oreb and Zeb, and Zebee and Salmana; even all their princes:
v13 who said, Let us take to ourselves the altar of God as an inheritance.
v14 O my God, make them as a wheel; as stubble before the face of the wind.
v15 As fire which shall burn up a wood, as the flame may consume the mountains;
v16 so shalt thou persecute them with thy tempest, and trouble them in thine anger.
v17 Fill their faces with dishonour; so shall they seek thy name, O Lord.
v18 Let them be ashamed and troubled for evermore; yea, let them be confounded and destroyed.
v19 And let them know that thy name is Lord; that thou alone art Most High over all the earth.
