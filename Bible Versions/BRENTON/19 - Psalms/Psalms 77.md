v1 A Psalm of instruction for Asaph. Give heed, O my people, to my law: incline your ear to the words of my mouth.
v2 I will open my mouth in parables: I will utter dark sayings which have been from the beginning.
v3 All which we have heard and known, and our fathers have declared to us.
v4 They were not hid from their children to a second generation; the fathers declaring the praises of the Lord, and his mighty acts, and his wonders which he wrought.
v5 And he raised up a testimony in Jacob, and appointed a law in Israel, which he commanded our fathers, to make it known to their children:
v6 that another generation might know, even the sons which should be born; and they should arise and declare them to their children.
v7 That they might set their hope on God, and not forget the works of God, but diligently seek his commandments.
v8 That they should not be as their fathers, a perverse and provoking generation; a generation which set not its heart aright, and its spirit was not steadfast with God.
v9 The children of Ephraim, bending and shooting with the bow, turned back in the day of battle.
v10 They kept not the covenant of God, and would not walk in his law.
v11 And they forgot his benefits, and his miracles which he had shewed them;
v12 the miracles which he wrought before their fathers, in the land of Egypt, in the plain of Tanes.
v13 He clave the sea, and led them through: he made the waters to stand as in a bottle.
v14 And he guided them with a cloud by day, and all the night with a light of fire.
v15 He clave a rock in the wilderness, and made them drink as in a great deep.
v16 And he brought water out of the rock, and caused waters to flow down as rivers.
v17 And they sinned yet more against him; they provoked the Most High in the wilderness.
v18 And they tempted God in their hearts, in asking meat for the desire of their souls.
v19 They spoke also against God, and said, Will God be able to prepare a table in the wilderness?
v20 Forasmuch as he smote the rock, and the waters flowed, and the torrents ran abundantly; will he be able also to give bread, or prepare a table for his people?
v21 Therefore the Lord heard, and was provoked: and fire was kindled in Jacob, and wrath went up against Israel.
v22 Because they believed not in God, and trusted not in his salvation.
v23 Yet he commanded the clouds from above, and opened the doors of heaven,
v24 and rained upon them manna to eat, and gave them the bread of heaven.
v25 Man ate angels' bread; he sent them provision to the full.
v26 He removed the south wind from heaven; and by his might he brought in the south-west wind.
v27 And he rained upon them flesh like dust, and feathered birds like the sand of the seas.
v28 And they fell into the midst of their camp, round about their tents.
v29 So they ate, and were completely filled; and he gave them their desire.
v30 They were not disappointed of their desire: but when their food was yet in their mouth,
v31 then the indignation of God rose up against them, and slew the fattest of them, and overthrew the choice men of Israel.
v32 In the midst of all this they sinned yet more, and believed not his miracles.
v33 And their days were consumed in vanity, and their years with anxiety.
v34 When he slew them, they sought him: and they returned and called betimes upon God.
v35 And they remembered that God was their helper, and the most high God was their redeemer.
v36 Yet they loved him only with their mouth, and lied to him with their tongue.
v37 For their heart was not right with him, neither were they steadfast in his covenant.
v38 But he is compassionate, and will forgive their sins, and will not destroy them : yea, he will frequently turn away his wrath, and will not kindle all his anger.
v39 And he remembered that they are flesh; a wind that passes away, and returns not.
v40 How often did they provoke him in the wilderness, and anger him in a dry land!
v41 Yea, they turned back, and tempted God, and provoked the Holy One of Israel.
v42 They remembered not his hand, the day in which he delivered them from the hand of the oppressor.
v43 How he had wrought his signs in Egypt, and his wonders in the field of Tanes:
v44 and had changed their rivers into blood; and their streams, that they should not drink.
v45 He sent against them the dog-fly, and it devoured them; and the frog, and it spoiled them.
v46 And he gave their fruit to the canker worm, and their labours to the locust.
v47 He killed their vines with hail, and their sycamores with frost.
v48 And he gave up their cattle to hail, and their substance to the fire.
v49 He sent out against them the fury of his anger, wrath, and indignation, and affliction, a message by evil angels.
v50 He made a way for his wrath; he spared not their souls from death, but consigned their cattle to death;
v51 and smote every first-born in the land of Egypt; the first-fruits of their labours in the tents of Cham.
v52 And he removed his people like sheep; he led them as a flock in the wilderness.
v53 And he guided them with hope, and they feared not: but the sea covered their enemies.
v54 And he brought them in to the mountain of his sanctuary, this mountain which his right hand had purchased.
v55 And he cast out the nations from before them, and made them to inherit by a line of inheritance, and made the tribes of Israel to dwell in their tents.
v56 Yet they tempted and provoked the most high God, and kept not his testimonies.
v57 And they turned back, and broke covenant, even as also their fathers: they became like a crooked bow.
v58 And they provoked him with their high places, and moved him to jealousy with their graven images.
v59 God heard and lightly regarded them , and greatly despised Israel.
v60 And he rejected the tabernacle of Selom, his tent where he dwelt among men.
v61 And he gave their strength into captivity, and their beauty into the enemy's hand.
v62 And he gave his people to the sword; and disdained his inheritance.
v63 Fire devoured their young men; and their virgins mourned not.
v64 Their priests fell by the sword; and their widows shall not be wept for.
v65 So the Lord awaked as one out of sleep, and as a mighty man who has been heated with wine.
v66 And he smote his enemies in the hinder parts: he brought on them a perpetual reproach.
v67 And he rejected the tabernacle of Joseph, and chose not the tribe of Ephraim;
v68 but chose the tribe of Juda, the mount Sion which he loved.
v69 And he built his sanctuary as the place of unicorns; he founded it for ever on the earth.
v70 He chose David also his servant, and took him up from the flocks of sheep.
v71 He took him from following the ewes great with young, to be the shepherd of Jacob his servant, and Israel his inheritance.
v72 So he tended them in the innocency of his heart; and guided them by the skilfulness of his hands.
