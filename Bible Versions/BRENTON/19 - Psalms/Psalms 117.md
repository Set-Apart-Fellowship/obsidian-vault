v1 Alleluia. Give thanks to the Lord; for he is good: for his mercy endures for ever.
v2 Let now the house of Israel say, that he is good: for his mercy endures for ever.
v3 Let now the house of Aaron say, that he is good: for his mercy endures for ever.
v4 Let now all that fear the Lord say, that he is good: for his mercy endures for ever.
v5 I called on the Lord out of affliction: and he hearkened to me, so as to bring me into a wide place.
v6 The Lord is my helper; and I will not fear what man shall do to me.
v7 The Lord is my helper; and I shall see my desire upon mine enemies.
v8 It is better to trust in the Lord than to trust in man.
v9 It is better to hope in the Lord, than to hope in princes.
v10 All nations compassed me about: but in the name of the Lord I repulsed them.
v11 They completely compassed me about: but in the name of the Lord I repulsed them.
v12 They compassed me about as bees do a honeycomb, and they burst into flame as fire among thorns: but in the name of the Lord I repulsed them.
v13 I was thrust, and sorely shaken, that I might fall: but the Lord helped me.
v14 The Lord is my strength and my song, and is become my salvation.
v15 The voice of exultation and salvation is in the tabernacles of the righteous: the right hand of the Lord has wrought mightily.
v16 The right hand of the Lord has exalted me: the right hand of the Lord has wrought powerfully.
v17 I shall not die, but live, and recount the works of the Lord.
v18 The Lord has chastened me sore: but he has not given me up to death.
v19 Open to me the gates of righteousness: I will go into them, and give praise to the Lord.
v20 This is the gate of the Lord: the righteous shall enter by it.
v21 I will give thanks to thee; because thou hast heard me, and art become my salvation.
v22 The stone which the builders rejected, the same is become the head of the corner.
v23 This has been done of the Lord; and it is wonderful in our eyes.
v24 This is the day which the Lord has made: let us exult and rejoice in it.
v25 O Lord, save now: O Lord, send now prosperity.
v26 Blessed is he that comes in the name of the Lord: we have blessed you out of the house of the Lord.
v27 God is the Lord, and he has shined upon us: celebrate the feast with thick branches, binding the victims even to the horns of the altar.
v28 Thou art my God, and I will give thee thanks: thou art my God, and I will exalt thee. I will give thanks to thee, for thou hast heard me, and art become my salvation.
v29 Give thanks to the Lord; for he is good: for his mercy endures for ever.
