v1 A Song of Degrees. When the Lord turned the captivity of Sion, we became as comforted ones.
v2 Then was our mouth filled with joy, and our tongue with exultation: then would they say among the Gentiles,
v3 The Lord has done great things among them. The Lord has done great things for us, we became joyful.
v4 Turn, O Lord, our captivity, as the streams in the south.
v5 They that sow in tears shall reap in joy.
v6 They went on and wept as they cast their seeds; but they shall surely come with exultation, bringing their sheaves with them .
