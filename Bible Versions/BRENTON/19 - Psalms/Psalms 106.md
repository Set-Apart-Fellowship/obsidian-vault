v1 Alleluia. Give thanks to the Lord, for he is good; for his mercy endures for ever.
v2 Let them say so who have been redeemed by the Lord, whom he has redeemed from the hand of the enemy;
v3 and gathered them out of the countries, from the east, and west, and north, and south.
v4 They wandered in the wilderness in a dry land; they found no way to a city of habitation.
v5 Hungry and thirsty, their soul fainted in them.
v6 Then they cried to the Lord in their affliction, and he delivered them out of their distresses.
v7 And he guided them into a straight path, that they might go to a city of habitation.
v8 Let them acknowledge to the Lord his mercies, and his wonderful works to the children of men.
v9 For he satisfies the empty soul, and fills the hungry soul with good things,
v10 even them that sit in darkness and the shadow of death, fettered in poverty and iron;
v11 because they rebelled against the words of God, and provoked the counsel of the Most High.
v12 So their heart was brought low with troubles; they were weak, and there was no helper.
v13 Then they cried to the Lord in their affliction, and he saved them out of their distresses.
v14 And he brought them out of darkness and the shadow of death, and broke their bonds asunder.
v15 Let them acknowledge to the Lord his mercies, and his wonders to the children of men.
v16 For he broke to pieces the brazen gates, and crushed the iron bars.
v17 He helped them out of the way of their iniquity; for they were brought low because of their iniquities.
v18 Their soul abhorred all meat; and they drew near to the gates of death.
v19 Then they cried to the Lord in their affliction, and he saved them out of their distresses.
v20 He sent his word, and healed them, and delivered them out of their destructions.
v21 Let them acknowledge to the Lord his mercies, and his wonderful works to the children of men.
v22 And let them offer to him the sacrifice of praise, and proclaim his works with exultation.
v23 They that go down to the sea in ships, doing business in many waters;
v24 these men have seen the works of the Lord, and his wonders in the deep.
v25 He speaks, and the stormy wind arises, and its waves are lifted up.
v26 They go up to the heavens, and go down to the depths; their soul melts because of troubles.
v27 They are troubled, they stagger as a drunkard, and all their wisdom is swallowed up.
v28 Then they cry to the Lord in their affliction, and he brings them out of their distresses.
v29 And he commands the storm, and it is calmed into a gentle breeze, and its waves are still.
v30 And they are glad, because they are quiet; and he guides them to their desired haven.
v31 Let them acknowledge to the Lord his mercies, and his wonderful works to the children of men.
v32 Let them exalt him in the congregation of the people, and praise him in the seat of the elders.
v33 He turns rivers into a desert, and streams of water into a dry land;
v34 a fruitful land into saltness, for the wickedness of them that dwell in it.
v35 He turns a wilderness into pools of water, and a dry land into streams of water.
v36 And there he causes the hungry to dwell, and they establish for themselves cities of habitation.
v37 And they sow fields, and plant vineyards, and they yield fruit of increase.
v38 And he blesses them, and they multiply exceedingly, and he diminishes not the number of their cattle.
v39 Again they become few, and are brought low, by the pressure of evils and pain.
v40 Contempt is poured upon their princes, and he causes them to wander in a desert and trackless land.
v41 But he helps the poor out of poverty, and makes him families as a flock.
v42 The upright shall see and rejoice; and all iniquity shall stop her mouth.
v43 Who is wise, and will observe these things, and understand the mercies of the Lord?
