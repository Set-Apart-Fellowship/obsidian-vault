v1 For the end, a Psalm of David, concerning the secrets of the Son.
v2 I will give thanks to thee, O Lord, with my whole heart; I will recount all thy wonderful works.
v3 I will be glad and exult in thee: I will sing to thy name, O thou Most High.
v4 When mine enemies are turned back, they shall be feeble and perish at thy presence.
v5 For thou hast maintained my cause and my right; thou satest on the throne, that judgest righteousness.
v6 Thou hast rebuked the nations, and the ungodly one has perished; thou hast blotted out their name for ever, even for ever and ever.
v7 The swords of the enemy have failed utterly; and thou hast destroyed cities: their memorial has been destroyed with a noise,
v8 but the Lord endures for ever: he has prepared his throne for judgment.
v9 And he will judge the world in righteousness, he will judge the nations in uprightness.
v10 The Lord also is become a refuge for the poor, a seasonable help, in affliction.
v11 And let them that know thy name hope in thee: for thou, O Lord, hast not failed them that diligently seek thee.
v12 Sing praises to the Lord, who dwells in Sion: declare his dealings among the nations.
v13 For he remembered them, in making inquisition for blood: he has not forgotten the supplication of the poor.
v14 Have mercy upon me, O Lord; look upon my affliction which I suffer of mine enemies, thou that liftest me up from the gates of death:
v15 that I may declare all thy praises in the gates of the daughter of Sion: I will exult in thy salvation.
v16 The heathen are caught in the destruction which they planned: in the very snare which they hid is their foot taken.
v17 The Lord is known as executing judgments: the sinner is taken in the works of his hands. A song of Pause.
v18 Let sinners be driven away into Hades, even all the nations that forget God.
v19 For the poor shall not be forgotten for ever: the patience of the needy ones shall not perish for ever.
v20 Arise, O Lord, let not man prevail: let the heathen be judged before thee.
v21 Appoint, O Lord, a lawgiver over them: let the heathen know that they are men. Pause.
v22 Why standest thou afar off, O Lord? why dost thou overlook us in times of need, in affliction?
v23 While the ungodly one acts proudly, the poor is hotly pursued: the wicked are taken in the crafty counsels which they imagine.
v24 Because the sinner praises himself for the desires of his heart; and the unjust one blesses himself.
v25 The sinner has provoked the Lord: according to the abundance of his pride he will not seek after him : God is not before him.
v26 His ways are profane at all times; thy judgments are removed from before him: he will gain the mastery over all his enemies.
v27 For he has said in his heart, I shall not be moved, continuing without evil from generation to generation.
v28 Whose mouth is full of cursing, and bitterness, and fraud: under his tongue are trouble and pain.
v29 He lies in wait with rich men in secret places, in order to slay the innocent: his eyes are set against the poor.
v30 He lies in wait in secret as a lion in his den: he lies in wait to ravish the poor, to ravish the poor when he draws him after him : he will bring him down in his snare.
v31 He will bow down and fall when he has mastered the poor.
v32 For he has said in his heart, God has forgotten: he has turned away his face so as never to look.
v33 Arise, O Lord God; let thy hand be lifted up: forget not the poor.
v34 Wherefore has the wicked provoked God? for he has said in his heart, He will not require it .
v35 Thou seest it ; for thou dost observe trouble and wrath, to deliver them into thy hands: the poor has been left to thee; thou wast a helper to the orphan.
v36 Break thou the arm of the sinner and wicked man: his sin shall be sought for, and shall not be found.
v37 The Lord shall reign for ever, even for ever and ever: ye Gentiles shall perish out of his land.
v38 The Lord has heard the desire of the poor: thine ear has inclined to the preparation of their heart;
v39 to plead for the orphan and afflicted, that man may no more boast upon the earth.
