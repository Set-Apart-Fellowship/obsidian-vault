v1 A Psalm of a Song for the Sabbath-day.
v2 It is a good thing to give thanks to the Lord, and to sing praises to thy name, O thou Most High;
v3 to proclaim thy mercy in the morning, and thy truth by night,
v4 on a psaltery of ten strings, with a song on the harp.
v5 For thou, O Lord, hast made me glad with thy work: and in the operations of thy hands will I exult.
v6 How have thy works been magnified, O Lord! thy thoughts are very deep.
v7 A foolish man will not know, and a senseless man will not understand this.
v8 When the sinners spring up as the grass, and all the workers of iniquity have watched; it is that they may be utterly destroyed for ever.
v9 But thou, O Lord, art most high for ever.
v10 For, behold, thine enemies shall perish; and all the workers of iniquity shall be scattered.
v11 But my horn shall be exalted as the horn of a unicorn; and mine old age with rich mercy.
v12 And mine eye has seen mine enemies, and mine ear shall hear the wicked that rise up against me.
v13 The righteous shall flourish as a palm-tree: he shall be increased as the cedar in Libanus.
v14 They that are planted in the house of the Lord shall flourish in the courts of our God.
v15 Then shall they be increased in a fine old age; and they shall be prosperous; that they may declare
v16 that the Lord my God is righteous, and there is no iniquity in him.
