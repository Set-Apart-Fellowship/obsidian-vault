v1 Alleluia. Sing to the Lord a new song: his praise is in the assembly of the saints.
v2 Let Israel rejoice in him that made him; and let the children of Sion exult in their king.
v3 Let them praise his name in the dance: let them sing praises to him with timbrel and psaltery.
v4 For the Lord takes pleasure in his people; and will exalt the meek with salvation.
v5 The saints shall rejoice in glory; and shall exult on their beds.
v6 The high praises of God shall be in their throat, and two-edged swords in their hands;
v7 to execute vengeance on the nations, and punishments among the peoples;
v8 to bind their kings with fetters, and their nobles with manacles of iron;
v9 to execute on them the judgment written: this honour have all his saints.
