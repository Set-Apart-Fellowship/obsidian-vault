v1 Alleluia. Give thanks to the Lord: for he is good: for his mercy endures for ever.
v2 Give thanks to the God of gods: for his mercy endures for ever.
v3 Give thanks to the Lord of lords: for his mercy endures for ever.
v4 To him who alone has wrought great wonders: for his mercy endures for ever.
v5 To him who made the heavens by understanding; for his mercy endures for ever.
v6 To him who established the earth on the waters; for his mercy endures for ever.
v7 To him who alone made great lights; for his mercy endures for ever.
v8 The sun to rule the day; for his mercy endures for ever.
v9 The moon and the stars to rule the night; for his mercy endures for ever.
v10 To him who smote Egypt with their first-born; for his mercy endures for ever.
v11 And brought Israel out of the midst of them; for his mercy endures for ever:
v12 with a strong hand, and a high arm: for his mercy endures for ever.
v13 To him who divided the Red Sea into parts: for his mercy endures for ever:
v14 and brought Israel through the midst of it: for his mercy endures for ever:
v15 and overthrew Pharao and his host in the Red Sea: for his mercy endures for ever.
v16 To him who led his people through the wilderness: for his mercy endures for ever.
v17 To him who smote great kings: for his mercy endures for ever:
v18 and slew mighty kings; for his mercy endures for ever:
v19 Seon king of the Amorites: for his mercy endures for ever:
v20 and Og king of Basan: for his mercy endures for ever:
v21 and gave their land for an inheritance: for his mercy endures for ever:
v22 even an inheritance to Israel his servant: for his mercy endures for ever.
v23 For the Lord remembered us in our low estate; for his mercy endures for ever:
v24 and redeemed us from our enemies; for his mercy endures for ever.
v25 Who gives food to all flesh; for his mercy endures for ever.
v26 Give thanks to the God of heaven; for his mercy endures for ever.
