v1 Alleluia, a Psalm of Aggæus and Zacharias. Praise ye the Lord: for psalmody is a good thing; let praise be sweetly sung to our God.
v2 The Lord builds up Jerusalem; and he will gather together the dispersed of Israel.
v3 He heals the broken in heart, and binds up their wounds.
v4 He numbers the multitudes of stars; and calls them all by names.
v5 Great is our Lord, and great is his strength; and his understanding is infinite.
v6 The Lord lifts up the meek; but brings sinners down to the ground.
v7 Begin the song with thanksgiving to the Lord; sing praises on the harp to our God:
v8 who covers the heaven with clouds, who prepares rain for the earth, who causes grass to spring up on the mountains, [and green herb for the service of men;]
v9 and gives to cattle their food, and to the young ravens that call upon him.
v10 He will not take pleasure in the strength of a horse; neither is he well-pleased with the legs of a man.
v11 The Lord takes pleasure in them that fear him, and in all that hope in his mercy.
