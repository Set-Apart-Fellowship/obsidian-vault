v1 A Song of Degrees. Out of the depths have I cried to thee, O Lord.
v2 O Lord, hearken to my voice; let thine ears be attentive to the voice of my supplication.
v3 If thou, O Lord, shouldest mark iniquities, O Lord, who shall stand?
v4 For with thee is forgiveness: for thy name's sake
v5 have I waited for thee, O Lord, my soul has waited for thy word.
v6 My soul has hoped in the Lord; from the morning watch till night.
v7 Let Israel hope in the Lord: for with the Lord is mercy, and with him is plenteous redemption.
v8 And he shall redeem Israel from all his iniquities.
