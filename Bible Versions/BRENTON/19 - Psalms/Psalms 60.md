v1 For the end, among the Hymns of David.
v2 O God, hearken to my petition; attend to my prayer.
v3 From the ends of the earth have I cried to thee, when my heart was in trouble: thou liftedst me up on a rock, thou didst guide me:
v4 because thou wert my hope, a tower of strength from the face of the enemy.
v5 I will dwell in thy tabernacle for ever; I will shelter myself under the shadow of thy wings. Pause.
v6 For thou, O God, hast heard my prayers; thou hast given an inheritance to them that fear thy name.
v7 Thou shalt add days to the days of the king; thou shalt lengthen his years to all generations.
v8 He shall endure for ever before God: which of them will seek out his mercy and truth?
v9 So will I sing to thy name for ever and ever, that I may daily perform my vows.
