v1 A Psalm of David for remembrance concerning the Sabbath-day.
v2 O Lord, rebuke me not in thy wrath, neither chasten me in thine anger.
v3 For thy weapons are fixed in me, and thou hast pressed thy hand heavily upon me.
v4 For there is no health in my flesh because of thine anger; there is no peace to my bones because of my sins.
v5 For my transgressions have gone over mine head: they have pressed heavily upon me like a weighty burden.
v6 My bruises have become noisome and corrupt, because of my foolishness.
v7 I have been wretched and bowed down continually: I went with a mourning countenance all the day.
v8 For my soul is filled with mockings; and there is no health in my flesh.
v9 I have been afflicted and brought down exceedingly: I have roared for the groaning of my heart.
v10 But all my desire is before thee; and my groaning is not hidden from thee.
v11 My heart is troubled, my strength has failed me; and the light of mine eyes is not with me.
v12 My friends and my neighbours drew near before me, and stood still; and my nearest of kin stood afar off.
v13 While they pressed hard upon me that sought my soul: and they that sought my hurt spoke vanities, and devised deceits all the day.
v14 But I, as a deaf man, heard not; and was as a dumb man not opening his mouth.
v15 And I was as a man that hears not, and who has no reproofs in his mouth.
v16 For I hoped in thee, O Lord: thou wilt hear, O Lord my God.
v17 For I said, Lest mine enemies rejoice against me: for when my feet were moved, they spoke boastingly against me.
v18 For I am ready for plagues, and my grief is continually before me.
v19 For I will declare mine iniquity, and be distressed for my sin.
v20 But mine enemies live, and are mightier than I: and they that hate me unjustly are multiplied.
v21 They that reward evil for good slandered me; because I followed righteousness.
v22 Forsake me not, O Lord my God: depart not from me.
v23 Draw nigh to my help, O Lord of my salvation.
