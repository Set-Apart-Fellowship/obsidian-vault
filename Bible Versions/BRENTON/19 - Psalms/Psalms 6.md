v1 For the end, a Psalm of David among the Hymns for the eighth.
v2 O Lord, rebuke me not in thy wrath, neither chasten me in thine anger.
v3 Pity me, O Lord; for I am weak: heal me, O Lord; for my bones are vexed.
v4 My soul also is grievously vexed: but thou, O Lord, how long?
v5 Return, O Lord, deliver my soul: save me for thy mercy's sake.
v6 For in death no man remembers thee: and who will give thee thanks in Hades?
v7 I am wearied with my groaning; I shall wash my bed every night; I shall water my couch with my tears.
v8 Mine eye is troubled because of my wrath; I am worn out because of all my enemies.
v9 Depart from me, all ye that work iniquity; for the Lord has heard the voice of my weeping.
v10 The Lord has hearkened to my petition; the Lord has accepted my prayer.
v11 Let all mine enemies be put to shame and sore troubled: let them be turned back and grievously put to shame speedily.
