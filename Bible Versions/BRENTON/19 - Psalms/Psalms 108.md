v1 For the end, a Psalm of David. O God, pass not over my praise in silence;
v2 for the mouth of the sinner and the mouth of the crafty man have been opened against me: they have spoken against me with a crafty tongue.
v3 And they have compassed me with words of hatred; and fought against me without a cause.
v4 Instead of loving me, they falsely accused me: but I continued to pray.
v5 And they rewarded me evil for good, and hatred for my love.
v6 Set thou a sinner against him; and let the devil stand at his right hand.
v7 When he is judged, let him go forth condemned: and let his prayer become sin.
v8 Let his days be few: and let another take his office of overseer.
v9 Let his children be orphans, and his wife a widow.
v10 Let his children wander without a dwelling-place, and beg: let them be cast out of their habitations.
v11 Let his creditor exact all that belongs to him: and let strangers spoil his labours.
v12 Let him have no helper; neither let there be any one to have compassion on his fatherless children.
v13 Let his children be given up to utter destruction: in one generation let his name be blotted out.
v14 Let the iniquity of his fathers be remembered before the Lord; and let not the sin of his mother be blotted out.
v15 Let them be before the Lord continually; and let their memorial be blotted out from the earth.
v16 Because he remembered not to shew mercy, but persecuted the needy and poor man, and that to slay him that was pricked in the heart.
v17 He loved cursing also, and it shall come upon him; and he took not pleasure in blessing, so it shall be removed far from him.
v18 Yea, he put on cursing as a garment, and it is come as water into his bowels, and as oil into his bones.
v19 Let it be to him as a garment which he puts on, and as a girdle with which he girds himself continually.
v20 This is the dealing of the Lord with those who falsely accuse me, and of them that speak evil against my soul.
v21 But thou, O Lord, Lord, deal mercifully with me, for thy name's sake: for thy mercy is good.
v22 Deliver me, for I am poor and needy; and my heart is troubled within me.
v23 I am removed as a shadow in its going down: I am tossed up and down like locusts.
v24 My knees are weakened through fasting, and my flesh is changed by reason of the want of oil.
v25 I became also a reproach to them: when they saw me they shook their heads.
v26 Help me, O Lord my God; and save me according to thy mercy.
v27 And let them know that this is thy hand; and that thou, Lord, hast wrought it.
v28 Let them curse, but thou shalt bless: let them that rise up against me be ashamed, but let thy servant rejoice.
v29 Let those that falsely accuse me be clothed with shame, and let them cover themselves with their shame as with a mantle.
v30 I will give thanks to the Lord abundantly with my mouth; and in the midst of many I will praise him.
v31 For he stood on the right hand of the poor, to save me from them that persecute my soul.
