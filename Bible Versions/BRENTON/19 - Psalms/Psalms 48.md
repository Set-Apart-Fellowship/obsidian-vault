v1 For the end, a Psalm for the sons of Core.
v2 Hear these words, all ye nations, hearken, all ye that dwell upon the earth:
v3 both the sons of mean men, and sons of great men; the rich and poor man together.
v4 My mouth shall speak of wisdom; and the meditation of my heart shall bring forth understanding.
v5 I will incline mine ear to a parable: I will open my riddle on the harp.
v6 Wherefore should I fear in the evil day? the iniquity of my heel shall compass me.
v7 They that trust in their strength, and boast themselves in the multitude of their wealth—
v8 A brother does not redeem, shall a man redeem? he shall not give to God a ransom for himself,
v9 or the price of the redemption of his soul, though he labour for ever,
v10 and live to the end, so that he should not see corruption.
v11 When he shall see wise men dying, the fool and the senseless one shall perish together; and they shall leave their wealth to strangers.
v12 And their sepulchres are their houses for ever, even their tabernacles to all generations: they have called their lands after their own names.
v13 And man being in honour, understands not: he is compared to the senseless cattle, and is like to them.
v14 This their way is an offence to them: yet afterwards men will commend their sayings. Pause.
v15 They have laid them as sheep in Hades; death shall feed on them; and the upright shall have dominion over them in the morning, and their help shall fail in Hades from their glory.
v16 But God shall deliver my soul from the power of Hades, when he shall receive me. Pause.
v17 Fear not when a man is enriched, and when the glory of his house is increased.
v18 For he shall take nothing when he dies; neither shall his glory descend with him.
v19 For his soul shall be blessed in his life: he shall give thanks to thee when thou dost well to him.
v20 Yet he shall go in to the generation of his fathers; he shall never see light.
v21 Man that is in honour, understands not: he is compared to the senseless cattle, and is like them.
