v1 Alleluia. I believed, wherefore I have spoken: but I was greatly afflicted.
v2 And I said in mine amazement, Every man is a liar.
v3 What shall I render to the Lord for all the things wherein he has rewarded me?
v4 I will take the cup of salvation, and call upon the name of the Lord.
v5 I will pay my vows to the Lord, in the presence of all his people.
v6 Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of his saints.
v7 O Lord, I am thy servant; I am thy servant, and the son of thine handmaid: thou hast burst my bonds asunder.
v8 I will offer to thee the sacrifice of praise, and will call upon the name of the Lord.
v9 I will pay my vows unto the Lord, in the presence of all his people,
v10 in the courts of the Lord's house, in the midst of thee, Jerusalem.
