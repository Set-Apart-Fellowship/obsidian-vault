v1 A Prayer for the Poor; when he is deeply afflicted, and pours out his supplication before the Lord.
v2 Hear my prayer, O Lord, and let my cry come to thee.
v3 Turn not away thy face from me: in the day when I am afflicted, incline thine ear to me: in the day when I shall call upon thee, speedily hear me.
v4 For my days have vanished like smoke, and my bones have been parched like a stick.
v5 I am blighted like grass, and my heart is dried up; for I have forgotten to eat my bread.
v6 By reason of the voice of my groaning, my bone has cleaved to my flesh.
v7 I have become like a pelican of the wilderness;
v8 I have become like an owl in a ruined house. I have watched, and am become as a sparrow dwelling alone on a roof.
v9 All the day long mine enemies have reproached me; and they that praised me have sworn against me.
v10 For I have eaten ashes as it were bread, and mingled my drink with weeping;
v11 because of thine anger and thy wrath: for thou hast lifted me up, and dashed me down.
v12 My days have declined like a shadow; and I am withered like grass.
v13 But thou, Lord, endurest for ever, and thy memorial to generation and generation.
v14 Thou shalt arise, and have mercy upon Sion: for it is time to have mercy upon her, for the set time is come.
v15 For thy servants have taken pleasure in her stones, and they shall pity her dust.
v16 So the nations shall fear thy name, O Lord, and all kings thy glory.
v17 For the Lord shall build up Sion, and shall appear in his glory.
v18 He has had regard to the prayer of the lowly, and has not despised their petition.
v19 Let this be written for another generation; and the people that shall be created shall praise the Lord.
v20 For he has looked out from the height of his sanctuary; the Lord looked upon the earth from heaven;
v21 to hear the groaning of the fettered ones, to loosen the sons of the slain;
v22 to proclaim the name of the Lord in Sion, and his praise in Jerusalem;
v23 when the people are gathered together, and the kings, to serve the Lord.
v24 He answered him in the way of his strength: tell me the fewness of my days.
v25 Take me not away in the midst of my days: thy years are through all generations.
v26 In the beginning thou, O Lord, didst lay the foundation of the earth; and the heavens are the works of thine hands.
v27 They shall perish, but thou remainest: and they all shall wax old as a garment; and as a vesture shalt thou fold them, and they shall be changed.
v28 But thou art the same, and thy years shall not fail.
v29 The children of thy servants shall dwell securely , and their seed shall prosper for ever.
