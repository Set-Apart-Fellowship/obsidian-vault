v1 A Psalm for Asaph. God stands in the assembly of gods; and in the midst of them will judge gods.
v2 How long will ye judge unrighteously, and accept the persons of sinners? Pause.
v3 Judge the orphan and poor: do justice to the low and needy.
v4 Rescue the needy, and deliver the poor out of the hand of the sinner.
v5 They know not, nor understand; they walk on in darkness: all the foundations of the earth shall be shaken.
v6 I have said, Ye are gods; and all of you children of the Most High.
v7 But ye die as men, and fall as one of the princes.
v8 Arise, O God, judge the earth: for thou shalt inherit all nations.
