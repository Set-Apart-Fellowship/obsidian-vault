v1 Alleluia. Give thanks to the Lord, and call upon his name; declare his works among the heathen.
v2 Sing to him, yea, sing praises to him: tell forth all his wonderful works.
v3 Glory in his holy name: let the heart of them that seek the Lord rejoice.
v4 Seek ye the Lord, and be strengthened; seek his face continually.
v5 Remember his wonderful works that he has done; his wonders, and the judgments of his mouth;
v6 ye seed of Abraam, his servants, ye children of Jacob, his chosen ones.
v7 He is the Lord our God; his judgments are in all the earth.
v8 He has remembered his covenant for ever, the word which he commanded for a thousand generations:
v9 which he established as a covenant to Abraam, and he remembered his oath to Isaac.
v10 And he established it to Jacob for an ordinance, and to Israel for an everlasting covenant;
v11 saying, To thee will I give the land of Chanaan, the line of your inheritance:
v12 when they were few in number, very few, and sojourners in it.
v13 And they went from nation to nation, and from one kingdom to another people.
v14 He suffered no man to wrong them; and he rebuked kings for their sakes:
v15 saying , Touch not my anointed ones; and do my prophets no harm.
v16 Moreover he called for a famine upon the land; he broke the whole support of bread.
v17 He sent a man before them; Joseph was sold for a slave.
v18 They hurt his feet with fetters; his soul passed into iron,
v19 until the time that his cause came on; the word of the Lord tried him as fire.
v20 The king sent and loosed him; even the prince of the people, and let him go free.
v21 He made him lord over his house, and ruler of all his substance;
v22 to chastise his rulers at his pleasure, and to teach his elders wisdom.
v23 Israel also came into Egypt, and Jacob sojourned in the land of Cham.
v24 And he increased his people greatly, and made them stronger than their enemies.
v25 And he turned their heart to hate his people, to deal craftily with his servants.
v26 He sent forth Moses his servant, and Aaron whom he had chosen.
v27 He established among them his signs, and his wonders in the land of Cham.
v28 He sent forth darkness, and made it dark; yet they rebelled against his words.
v29 He turned their waters into blood, and slew their fish.
v30 Their land produced frogs abundantly, in the chambers of their kings.
v31 He spoke, and the dog-fly came, and lice in all their coasts.
v32 He turned their rain into hail, and sent flaming fire in their land.
v33 And he smote their vines and their fig-trees; and broke every tree of their coast.
v34 He spoke, and the locust came, and caterpillars innumerable,
v35 and devoured all the grass in their land, and devoured the fruit of their ground.
v36 He smote also every first-born of their land, the first-fruits of all their labour.
v37 And he brought them out with silver and gold; and there was not a feeble one among their tribes.
v38 Egypt rejoiced at their departing; for the fear of them fell upon them.
v39 He spread out a cloud for a covering to them, and fire to give them light by night.
v40 They asked, and the quail came, and he satisfied them with the bread of heaven.
v41 He clave the rock, and the waters flowed; rivers ran in dry places.
v42 For he remembered his holy word, which he promised to Abraam his servant.
v43 And he brought out his people with exultation, and his chosen with joy;
v44 and gave them the lands of the heathen; and they inherited the labours of the people;
v45 that they might keep his ordinances, and diligently seek his law.
