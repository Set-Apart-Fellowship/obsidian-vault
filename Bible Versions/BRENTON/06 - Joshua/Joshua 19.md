v1 And the second lot came out for the children of Symeon; and their inheritance was in the midst of the lots of the children of Juda.
v2 And their lot was Beersabee, and Samaa, and Caladam,
v3 and Arsola, and Bola, and Jason,
v4 and Erthula, and Bula, and Herma,
v5 and Sikelac, and Bæthmachereb, and Sarsusin,
v6 and Batharoth, and their fields, thirteen cities, and their villages.
v7 Eremmon, and Thalcha, and Jether, and Asan; four cities and their villages,
v8 round about their cities as far as Balec as men go to Bameth southward: this is the inheritance of the tribe of the children of Symeon according to their families.
v9 The inheritance of the tribe of the children of Symeon was a part of the lot of Juda, for the portion of the children of Juda was greater than theirs; and the children of Symeon inherited in the midst of their lot.
v10 And the third lot came out to Zabulon according to their families: the bounds of their inheritance shall be—Esedekgola shall be their border,
v11 the sea and Magelda, and it shall reach to Bætharaba in the valley, which is opposite Jekman.
v12 And the border returned from Sedduc in a contrary direction eastward from Bæthsamys, to the borders of Chaselothaith, and shall pass on to Dabiroth, and shall proceed upward to Phangai.
v13 And thence it shall come round in the opposite direction eastward to Gebere to the city of Catasem, and shall go on to Remmonaa Matharaoza.
v14 And the borders shall come round northward to Amoth, and their going out shall be at Gæphael,
v15 and Catanath, and Nabaal, and Symoön, and Jericho, and Bæthman.
v16 This is the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Zabulon according to their families, these cities and their villages.
v17 And the fourth lot came out to Issachar.
v18 And their borders were Jazel, and Chasaloth, and Sunam,
v19 and Agin, and Siona, and Reeroth,
v20 and Anachereth, and Dabiron, and Kison, and Rebes,
v21 and Remmas, and Jeon, and Tomman, and Æmarec, and Bersaphes.
v22 And the boundaries shall border upon Gæthbor, and upon Salim westward, and Bæthsamys; and the extremity of his bounds shall be Jordan.
v23 This is the inheritance of the tribe of the children of Issachar according to their families, the cities and their villages.
v24 And the fifth lot came out to Aser according to their families.
v25 And their borders were Exeleketh, and Aleph, and Bæthok, and Keaph,
v26 and Elimelech, and Amiel, and Maasa, and the lot will border on Carmel westward, and on Sion, and Labanath.
v27 And it will return westward from Bæthegeneth, and will join Zabulon and Ekgai, and Phthæel northwards, and the borders will come to Saphthæbæthme, and Inael, and will go on to Chobamasomel,
v28 and Elbon, and Raab, and Ememaon, and Canthan to great Sidon.
v29 And the borders shall turn back to Rama, and to the fountain of Masphassat, and the Tyrians; and the borders shall return to Jasiph, and their going forth shall be the sea, and Apoleb, and Echozob,
v30 and Archob, and Aphec, and Raau.
v31 This is the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Aser according to their families, the cities and their villages.
v32 And the sixth lot came out to Nephthali.
v33 And their borders were Moolam, and Mola, and Besemiin, and Arme, and Naboc, and Jephthamai, as far as Dodam; and their goings out were Jordan.
v34 And the coasts will return westward by Athabor, and will go out thence to Jacana, and will border on Zabulon southward, and Aser will join it westward, and Jordan eastward.
v35 And the walled cities of the Tyrians, Tyre, and Omathadaketh, and Kenereth,
v36 and Armaith, and Arael, and Asor,
v37 and Cades, and Assari, and the well of Asor;
v38 and Keroe, and Megalaarim, and Bætthame, and Thessamys.
v39 This is the inheritance of the tribe of the children of Nephthali.
v40 And the seventh lot came out to Dan.
v41 And their borders were Sarath, and Asa, and the cities of Sammaus,
v42 and Salamin, and Ammon, and Silatha,
v43 and Elon, and Thamnatha, and Accaron;
v44 and Alcatha, and Begethon, and Gebeëlan,
v45 and Azor, and Banæbacat, and Gethremmon.
v46 And westward of Hieracon the border was near to Joppa.
v47 This is the inheritance of the tribe of the children of Dan, according to their families, these are their cities and their villages:
v48 And the sons of Dan went and fought against Lachis, and took it, and smote it with the edge of the sword; and they dwelt in it, and called the name of it Lasendan:
v49 And they proceeded to take possession of the land according to their borders, and the children of Israel gave an inheritance to Joshua the son of Naue among them,
v50 by the command of God, and they gave him the city which he asked for, Thamnasarach, which is in the mount of Ephraim; and he built the city, and dwelt in it.
v51 These are the divisions which Eleazar the priest divided by lot, and Joshua the son of Naue, and the heads of families among the tribes of Israel, according to the lots, in Selo before the Lord by the doors of the tabernacle of testimony, and they went to take possession of the land.
