v1 And the borders of the tribe of Juda according to their families were from the borders of Idumea from the wilderness of Sin, as far as Cades southward.
v2 And their borders were from the south as far as a part of the salt sea from the high country that extends southward.
v3 And they proceed before the ascent of Acrabin, and go out round Sena, and go up from the south to Cades Barne; and go out to Asoron, and proceed up to Sarada, and go out by the way that is west of Cades.
v4 And they go out to Selmona, and issue at the valley of Egypt; and the termination of its boundaries shall be at the sea: these are their boundaries southward.
v5 And their boundaries eastward are all the salt sea as far as Jordan; and their borders from the north, and from the border of the sea, and from part of Jordan—
v6 the borders go up to Bæthaglaam, and they go along from the north to Bætharaba, and the borders go on up to the stone of Bæon the son of Ruben.
v7 And the borders continue on to the fourth part of the valley of Achor, and go down to Galgal, which is before the approach of Adammin, which is southward in the valley, and terminate at the water of the fountain of the sun; and their going forth shall be the fountain of Rogel.
v8 And the borders go up to the valley of Ennom, behind Jebus southward; this is Jerusalem: and the borders terminate at the top of the mountain, which is before the valley of Ennom toward the sea, which is by the side of the land of Raphain northward.
v9 And the border going forth from the top of the mountain terminates at the fountain of the water of Naphtho, and terminates at mount Ephron; and the border will lead to Baal; this is the city of Jarim.
v10 And the border will go round from Baal to the sea, and will go on to the mount of Assar behind the city of Jarin northwards; this is Chaslon: and it will come down to the city of the Sun, and will go on to the south.
v11 And the border terminates behind Accaron northward, and the borders will terminate at Socchoth, and the borders will go on to the south, and will terminate at Lebna, and the issue of the borders will be at the sea; and their borders shall be toward the sea, the great sea shall be the boundary.
v12 These are the borders of the children of Juda round about according to their families.
v13 And to Chaleb the son of Jephone he gave a portion in the midst of the children of Juda by the command of God; and Joshua gave him the city of Arboc the metropolis of Enac; this is Chebron.
v14 And Chaleb the son of Jephone destroyed thence the three sons of Enac, Susi, and Tholami, and Achima.
v15 And Chaleb went up thence to the inhabitants of Dabir; and the name of Dabir before was the city of Letters.
v16 And Chaleb said, Whosoever shall take and destroy the city of Letters, and master it, to him will I give my daughter Ascha to wife.
v17 And Gothoniel the son of Chenez the brother of Chaleb took it; and he gave him Ascha his daughter to wife.
v18 And it came to pass as she went out that she counselled him, saying, I will ask of my father a field; and she cried from off her ass; and Chaleb said to her, What is it?
v19 And she said to him, Give me a blessing, for thou hast set me in the land of Nageb; give me Botthanis: and he gave her Gonæthla the upper, and Gonæthla the lower.
v20 This is the inheritance of the tribe of the children of Juda.
v21 And their cities were cities belonging to the tribe of the children of Juda on the borders of Edom by the wilderness, and Bæseleel, and Ara, and Asor,
v22 and Icam, and Regma, and Aruel,
v23 and Cades, and Asorionain, and Mænam,
v24 and Balmænan, and their villages,
v25 and the cities of Aseron, this is Asor,
v26 and Sen, and Salmaa, and Molada,
v27 and Seri, and Bæphalath,
v28 and Cholaseola, and Beersabee; and their villages, and their hamlets,
v29 Bala and Bacoc, and Asom,
v30 and Elboüdad, and Bæthel, and Herma,
v31 and Sekelac, and Macharim, and Sethennac,
v32 and Labos, and Sale, and Eromoth; twenty-nine cities, and their villages.
v33 In the plain country Astaol, and Raa, and Assa,
v34 and Ramen, and Tano, and Iluthoth, and Mæani,
v35 and Jermuth, and Odollam, and Membra, and Saocho, and Jazeca.
v36 And Sacarim and Gadera, and its villages; fourteen cities, and their villages;
v37 Senna, and Adasan, and Magadalgad,
v38 and Dalad, and Maspha, and Jachareel,
v39 and Basedoth, and Ideadalea;
v40 and Chabra, and Maches, and Maachos,
v41 and Geddor, and Bagadiel, and Noman, and Machedan: sixteen cities, and their villages;
v42 Lebna, and Ithac, and Anoch,
v43 and Jana, and Nasib,
v44 and Keilam, and Akiezi, and Kezib, and Bathesar, and Ælom: ten cities, and their villages;
v45 Accaron and her villages, and their hamlets:
v46 from Accaron, Gemna, and all the cities that are near Asedoth; and their villages.
v47 Asiedoth, and her villages, and her hamlets; Gaza, and its villages and its hamlets as far as the river of Egypt, and the great sea is the boundary.
v48 And in the hill country Samir, and Jether, and Socha,
v49 and Renna and the city of Letters, this is Dabir;
v50 and Anon, and Es, and Man, and Æsam,
v51 and Gosom, and Chalu, and Channa, and Gelom: eleven cities, and their villages;
v52 Ærem, and Remna, and Soma,
v53 and Jemain, and Bæthachu, and Phacua,
v54 and Euma, and the city Arboc, this is Chebron, and Soraith: nine cities, and their villages:
v55 Maor, and Chermel, and Ozib, and Itan,
v56 and Jariel, and Aricam, and Zacanaim,
v57 and Gabaa, and Thamnatha; nine cities, and their villages;
v58 Ælua, and Bethsur, and Geddon,
v59 and Magaroth, and Bæthanam, and Thecum; six cities, and their villages;
v60 Cariathbaal, this is the city of Jarim, and Sotheba: two cities, and their villages:
v61 and Baddargeis, and Tharabaam, and Ænon;
v62 and Æochioza, and Naphlazon, and the cities of Sadon, and Ancades; seven cities, and their villages.
v63 And the Jebusite dwelt in Jerusalem, and the children of Juda could not destroy them; and the Jebusites dwelt in Jerusalem to this day.
