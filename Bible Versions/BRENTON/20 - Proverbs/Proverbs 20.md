v1 Wine is an intemperate thing, and strong drink full of violence: but every fool is entangled with them.
v2 The threat of a king differs not from the rage of a lion; and he that provokes him sins against his own soul.
v3 It is a glory to a man to turn aside from railing; but every fool is entangled with such matters.
v4 A sluggard when reproached is not ashamed: so also he who borrows corn in harvest.
v5 Counsel in a man's heart is deep water; but a prudent man will draw it out.
v6 A man is valuable, and a merciful man precious: but it is hard to find a faithful man.
v7 He that walks blameless in justice, shall leave his children blessed.
v8 Whenever a righteous king sits on the throne, no evil thing can stand before his presence.
v9 Who will boast that he has a pure heart? or who will boldly say that he is pure from sins?
v10 A large and small weight, and divers measures, are even both of them unclean before the Lord; and so is he that makes them.
v11 A youth when in company with a godly man, will be restrained in his devices, and then his way will be straight.
v12 The ear hears, and the eye sees: even both of them are the Lord's work.
v13 Love not to speak ill, lest thou be cut off: open thine eyes, and be filled with bread.
v23 A double weight is an abomination to the Lord; and a deceitful balance is not good in his sight.
v24 A man's goings are directed of the Lord: how then can a mortal understand his ways?
v25 It is a snare to a man hastily to consecrate some of his own property: for in that case repentance comes after vowing.
v26 A wise king utterly crushes the ungodly, and will bring a wheel upon them.
v27 The spirit of man is a light of the Lord, who searches the inmost parts of the belly.
v28 Mercy and truth are a guard to a king, and will surround his throne with righteousness.
v29 Wisdom is an ornament to young men; and grey hairs are the glory of old men.
v30 Bruises and contusions befall bad men; and plagues shall come into the inward parts of their belly.
