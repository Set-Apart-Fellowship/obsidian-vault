v10 Who shall find a virtuous woman? for such a one is more valuable than precious stones.
v11 The heart of her husband trusts in her: such a one shall stand in no need of fine spoils.
v12 For she employs all her living for her husband's good.
v13 Gathering wool and flax, she makes it serviceable with her hands.
v14 She is like a ship trading from a distance: so she procures her livelihood.
v15 And she rises by night, and gives food to her household, and appointed tasks to her maidens.
v16 She views a farm, and buys it: and with the fruit of her hands she plants a possession.
v17 She strongly girds her loins, and strengthens her arms for work.
v18 And she finds by experience that working is good; and her candle goes not out all night.
v19 She reaches forth her arms to needful works , and applies her hands to the spindle.
v20 And she opens her hands to the needy, and reaches out fruit to the poor.
v21 Her husband is not anxious about those at home when he tarries anywhere abroad: for all her household are clothed.
v22 She makes for her husband clothes of double texture, and garments for herself of fine linen and scarlet.
v23 And her husband becomes a distinguished person in the gates, when he sits in council with the old inhabitants of the land.
v24 She makes fine linens, and sells girdles to the Chananites: she opens her mouth heedfully and with propriety, and controls her tongue.
v25 She puts on strength and honour; and rejoices in the last days.
v26 The ways of her household are careful, and she eats not the bread of idleness.
v27 But she opens her mouth wisely, and according to law.
v28 And her kindness to them sets up her children for them, and they grow rich, and her husband praises her.
v29 Many daughters have obtained wealth, many have wrought valiantly; but thou hast exceeded, thou hast surpassed all.
v30 Charms are false, and woman's beauty is vain: for it is a wise woman that is blessed, and let her praise the fear of the Lord.
v31 Give her of the fruit of her lips; and let her husband be praised in the gates.
