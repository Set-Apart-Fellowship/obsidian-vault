v1 The Proverbs of Solomon son of David, who reigned in Israel;
v2 to know wisdom and instruction, and to perceive words of understanding;
v3 to receive also hard sayings, and to understand true justice, and how to direct judgment;
v4 that he might give subtlety to the simple, and to the young man discernment and understanding.
v5 For by the hearing of these a wise man will be wiser, and the man of understanding will gain direction;
v6 and will understand a parable, and a dark speech; the sayings of the wise also, and riddles.
v7 The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom; and there is good understanding to all that practise it: and piety toward God is the beginning of discernment; but the ungodly will set at nought wisdom and instruction.
v8 Hear, my son, the instruction of thy father, and reject not the rules of thy mother.
v9 For thou shalt receive for thine head a crown of graces, and a chain of gold round thy neck.
v10 My son, let not ungodly men lead thee astray, neither consent thou to them .
v11 If they should exhort thee, saying, Come with us, partake in blood, and let us unjustly hide the just man in the earth:
v12 and let us swallow him alive, as Hades would , and remove the memorial of him from the earth:
v13 let us seize on his valuable property, and let us fill our houses with spoils:
v14 but do thou cast in thy lot with us, and let us all provide a common purse, and let us have one pouch:
v15 go not in the way with them, but turn aside thy foot from their paths:
v17 for nets are not without cause spread for birds.
v18 For they that are concerned in murder store up evils for themselves; and the overthrow of transgressors is evil.
v19 These are the ways of all that perform lawless deeds; for by ungodliness they destroy their own life.
v20 Wisdom sings aloud in passages, and in the broad places speaks boldly.
v21 And she makes proclamation on the top of the walls, and sits by the gates of princes; and at the gates of the city boldly says,
v22 So long as the simple cleave to justice, they shall not be ashamed: but the foolish being lovers of haughtiness, having become ungodly have hated knowledge, and are become subject to reproofs.
v23 Behold, I will bring forth to you the utterance of my breath, and I will instruct you in my speech.
v24 Since I called, and ye did not hearken; and I spoke at length, and ye gave no heed;
v25 but ye set at nought my counsels, and disregarded my reproofs;
v26 therefore I also will laugh at your destruction; and I will rejoice against you when ruin comes upon you:
v27 yea when dismay suddenly comes upon you, and your overthrow shall arrive like a tempest; and when tribulation and distress shall come upon you, or when ruin shall come upon you.
v28 For it shall be that when ye call upon me, I will not hearken to you: wicked men shall seek me, but shall not find me .
v29 For they hated wisdom, and did not choose the word of the Lord:
v30 neither would they attend to my counsels, but derided my reproofs.
v31 Therefore shall they eat the fruits of their own way, and shall be filled with their own ungodliness.
v32 For because they wronged the simple, they shall be slain; and an inquisition shall ruin the ungodly.
v33 But he that hearkens to me shall dwell in confidence, and shall rest securely from all evil.
