v1 My son, envy not bad men, nor desire to be with them.
v2 For their heart meditates falsehoods, and their lips speak mischiefs.
v3 A house is built by wisdom, and is set up by understanding.
v4 By discretion the chambers are filled with all precious and excellent wealth.
v5 A wise man is better than a strong man; and a man who has prudence than a large estate.
v6 War is carried on with generalship, and aid is supplied to the heart of a counsellor.
v7 Wisdom and good understanding are in the gates of the wise: the wise turn not aside from the mouth of the Lord,
v8 but deliberate in council. Death befalls uninstructed men .
v9 The fool also dies in sins; and uncleanness attaches to a pestilent man.
v10 He shall be defiled in the evil day, and in the day of affliction, until he be utterly consumed.
v11 Deliver them that are led away to death, and redeem them that are appointed to be slain; spare not thy help .
v12 But if thou shouldest say, I know not this man; know that the Lord knows the hearts of all; and he that formed breath for all, he knows all things, who renders to every man according to his works.
v13 My son, eat honey, for the honeycomb is good, that thy throat may be sweetened.
v14 Thus shalt thou perceive wisdom in thy soul: for if thou find it, thine end shall be good, and hope shall not fail thee.
v15 Bring not an ungodly man into the dwelling of the righteous: neither be deceived by the feeding of the belly.
v16 For a righteous man will fall seven times, and rise again : but the ungodly shall be without strength in troubles.
v17 If thine enemy should fall, rejoice not over him, neither be elated at his overthrow.
v18 For the Lord will see it , and it will not please him, and he will turn away his wrath from him.
v19 Rejoice not in evil-doers, neither be envious of sinners.
v20 For the evil man shall have no posterity: and the light of the wicked shall be put out.
v21 My son, fear God and the king; and do not disobey either of them.
v22 For they will suddenly punish the ungodly, and who can know the vengeance inflicted by both?
v23 And this thing I say to you that are wise for you to learn: It is not good to have respect of persons in judgment.
v24 He that says of the ungodly, He is righteous, shall be cursed by peoples, and hateful among the nations.
v25 But they that reprove him shall appear more excellent, and blessing shall come upon them;
v26 and men will kiss lips that answer well.
v27 Prepare thy works for thy going forth, and prepare thyself for the field; and come after me, and thou shalt rebuild thine house.
v28 Be not a false witness against thy fellow citizen, neither exaggerate with thy lips.
v29 Say not, As he has treated me, so will I treat him, and I will avenge myself on him for that wherein he has injured me.
v30 A foolish man is like a farm, and a senseless man is like a vineyard.
v31 If thou let him alone, he will altogether remain barren and covered with weeds; and he becomes destitute, and his stone walls are broken down.
v32 Afterwards I reflected, I looked that I might receive instruction.
v33 The sluggard says , I slumber a little, and I sleep a little, and for a little while I fold my arms across my breast.
v34 But if thou do this, thy poverty will come speedily; and thy want like a swift courier.
v35 The horse-leech had three dearly-beloved daughters: and these three did not satisfy her; and the fourth was not contented so as to say, Enough.
v36 The grave, and the love of a woman, and the earth not filled with water; water also and fire will not say, It is enough.
v37 The eye that laughs to scorn a father, and dishonours the old age of a mother, let the ravens of the valleys pick it out, and let the young eagles devour it.
v38 Moreover there are three things impossible for me to comprehend, and the fourth I know not:
v39 the track of a flying eagle; and the ways of a serpent on a rock; and the paths of a ship passing through the sea; and the ways of a man in youth.
v40 Such is the way of an adulterous woman, who having washed herself from what she has done, says she has done nothing amiss.
v41 By three things the earth is troubled, and the fourth it cannot bear:
v42 if a servant reign; or a fool be filled with food;
v43 or if a maid-servant should cast out her own mistress; and if a hateful woman should marry a good man.
v44 And there are four very little things upon the earth, but these are wiser than the wise:
v45 the ants which are weak, and yet prepare their food in summer;
v46 the rabbits also are a feeble race, who make their houses in the rocks.
v47 The locusts have no king, and yet march orderly at one command.
v48 And the eft, which supports itself by its hands, and is easily taken, dwells in the fortresses of kings.
v49 And there are three things which go well, and a fourth which passes along finely.
v50 A lion's whelp, stronger than all other beasts, which turns not away, nor fears any beast;
v51 and a cock walking in boldly among the hens, and a goat leading the herd; and a king publicly speaking before a nation.
v52 If thou abandon thyself to mirth, and stretch forth thine hand in a quarrel, thou shalt be disgraced.
v53 Milk out milk, and there shall be butter, and if thou wring one's nostrils there shall come out blood: so if thou extort words, there will come forth quarrels and strifes.
v54 My words have been spoken by God—the oracular answer of a king, whom his mother instructed.
v55 What wilt thou keep, my son, what? the words of God. My firstborn son, I speak to thee: what? son of my womb? what? son of my vows?
v56 Give not thy wealth to women, nor thy mind and living to remorse. Do all things with counsel: drink wine with counsel.
v57 Princes are prone to anger: let them then not drink wine:
v58 lest they drink, and forget wisdom, and be not able to judge the poor rightly.
v59 Give strong drink to those that are in sorrow, and the wine to drink to those in pain:
v60 that they may forget their poverty, and may not remember their troubles any more.
v61 Open thy mouth with the word of God, and judge all fairly.
v62 Open thy mouth and judge justly, and plead the cause of the poor and weak.
