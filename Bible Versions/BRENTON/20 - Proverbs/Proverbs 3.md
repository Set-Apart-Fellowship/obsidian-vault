v1 My son, forget not my laws; but let thine heart keep my words:
v2 for length of existence, and years of life, and peace, shall they add to thee.
v3 Let not mercy and truth forsake thee; but bind them about thy neck:
v4 so shalt thou find favour: and do thou provide things honest in the sight of the Lord, and of men.
v5 Trust in God with all thine heart; and be not exalted in thine own wisdom.
v6 In all thy ways acquaint thyself with her, that she may rightly direct thy paths.
v7 Be not wise in thine own conceit; but fear God, and depart from all evil.
v8 Then shall there be health to thy body, and good keeping to thy bones.
v9 Honour the Lord with thy just labours, and give him the first of thy fruits of righteousness:
v10 that thy storehouses may be completely filled with corn, and that thy presses may burst forth with wine.
v11 My son, despise not the chastening of the Lord; nor faint when thou art rebuked of him:
v12 for whom the Lord loves, he rebukes, and scourges every son whom he receives.
v13 Blessed is the man who has found wisdom, and the mortal who knows prudence.
v14 For it is better to traffic for her, than for treasures of gold and silver.
v15 And she is more valuable than precious stones: no evil thing shall resist her: she is well known to all that approach her, and no precious thing is equal to her in value.
v16 For length of existence and years of life are in her right hand; and in her left hand are wealth and glory:
v17 Her ways are good ways, and all her paths are peaceful.
v18 She is a tree of life to all that lay hold upon her; and she is a secure help to all that stay themselves on her, as on the Lord.
v19 God by wisdom founded the earth, and by prudence he prepared the heavens.
v20 By understanding were the depths broken up, and the clouds dropped water.
v21 My son, let them not pass from thee , but keep my counsel and understanding:
v22 that thy soul may live, and that there may be grace round thy neck;
v23 that thou mayest go confidently in peace in all thy ways, and that thy foot may not stumble.
v24 For if thou rest, thou shalt be undismayed; and if thou sleep, thou shalt slumber sweetly.
v25 And thou shalt not be afraid of alarm coming upon thee, neither of approaching attacks of ungodly men.
v26 For the Lord shall be over all thy ways, and shall establish thy foot that thou be not moved.
v27 Forbear not to do good to the poor, whensoever thy hand may have power to help him .
v28 Say not, Come back another time, to-morrow I will give; while thou art able to do him good: for thou knowest not what the next day will bring forth.
v29 Devise not evil against thy friend, living near thee and trusting in thee.
v30 Be not ready to quarrel with a man without a cause, lest he do thee some harm.
v31 Procure not the reproaches of bad men, neither do thou covet their ways.
v32 For every transgressor is unclean before the Lord; neither does he sit among the righteous.
v33 The curse of God is in the houses of the ungodly; but the habitations of the just are blessed.
v34 The Lord resists the proud; but he gives grace to the humble.
v35 The wise shall inherit glory; but the ungodly have exalted their own dishonour.
