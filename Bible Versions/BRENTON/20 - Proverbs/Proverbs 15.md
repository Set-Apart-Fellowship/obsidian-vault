v1 Anger slays even wise men; yet a submissive answer turns away wrath: but a grievous word stirs up anger.
v2 The tongue of the wise knows what is good: but the mouth of the foolish tells out evil things.
v3 The eyes of the Lord behold both the evil and the good in every place.
v4 The wholesome tongue is a tree of life, and he that keeps it shall be filled with understanding.
v5 A fool scorns his father's instruction: but he that keeps his commandments is more prudent. In abounding righteousness is great strength: but the ungodly shall utterly perish from the earth.
v6 In the houses of the righteous is much strength: but the fruits of the ungodly shall perish.
v7 The lips of the wise are bound by discretion: but the hearts of the foolish are not safe.
v8 The sacrifices of the ungodly are an abomination to the Lord; but the prayers of them that walk honestly are acceptable with him.
v9 The ways of an ungodly man are an abomination to the Lord; but he loves those that follow after righteousness.
v10 The instruction of the simple is known by them that pass by; but they that hate reproofs die disgracefully.
v11 Hell and destruction are manifest to the Lord; how shall not also be the hearts of men?
v12 An uninstructed person will not love those that reprove him; neither will he associate with the wise.
v13 When the heart rejoices the countenance is cheerful; but when it is in sorrow, the countenance is sad.
v14 An upright heart seeks discretion; but the mouth of the uninstructed will experience evils.
v15 The eyes of the wicked are always looking for evil things; but the good are always quiet.
v16 Better is a small portion with the fear of the Lord, than great treasures without the fear of the Lord .
v17 Better is an entertainment of herbs with friendliness and kindness, than a feast of calves, with enmity.
v18 A passionate man stirs up strife; but he that is slow to anger appeases even a rising one.
v19 The ways of sluggards are strewn with thorns; but those of the diligent are made smooth.
v20 A wise son gladdens his father; but a foolish son sneers at his mother.
v21 The ways of a foolish man are void of sense; but a wise man proceeds on his way aright.
v22 They that honour not councils put off deliberation; but counsel abides in the hearts of counsellors.
v23 A bad man will by no means attend to counsel; neither will he say anything seasonable, or good for the common weal .
v24 The thoughts of the wise are ways of life, that he may turn aside and escape from hell.
v25 The Lord pulls down the houses of scorners; but he establishes the border of the widow.
v26 An unrighteous thought is abomination to the Lord; but the sayings of the pure are held in honour.
v27 A receiver of bribes destroys himself; but he that hates the receiving of bribes is safe.
v28 The hearts of the righteous meditate faithfulness; but the mouth of the ungodly answers evil things.
v29 God is far from the ungodly; but he hearkens to the prayers of the righteous.
v30 The eye that sees rightly rejoices the heart; and a good report fattens the bones.
v31 Let the heart of a man think justly, that his steps may be rightly ordered of God.
v32 He that rejects instruction hates himself; but he that minds reproofs loves his soul.
v33 The fear of the Lord is instruction and wisdom; and the highest honour will correspond therewith.
