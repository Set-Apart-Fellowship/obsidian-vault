v1 Wise women build houses: but a foolish one digs hers down with her hands.
v2 He that walks uprightly fears the Lord; but he that is perverse in his ways shall be dishonoured.
v3 Out of the mouth of fools comes a rod of pride; but the lips of the wise preserve them.
v4 Where no oxen are, the cribs are clean; but where there is abundant produce, the strength of the ox is apparent.
v5 A faithful witness does not lie; but an unjust witness kindles falsehoods.
v6 Thou shalt seek wisdom with bad men, and shalt not find it; but discretion is easily available with the prudent.
v7 All things are adverse to a foolish man; but wise lips are the weapons of discretion.
v8 The wisdom of the prudent will understand their ways; but the folly of fools leads astray.
v9 The houses of transgressors will need purification; but the houses of the just are acceptable.
v10 If a man's mind is intelligent, his soul is sorrowful; and when he rejoices, he has no fellowship with pride.
v11 The houses of ungodly men shall be utterly destroyed; but the tabernacles of them that walk uprightly shall stand.
v12 There is a way which seems to be right with men, but the ends of it reach to the depth of hell.
v13 Grief mingles not with mirth; and joy in the end comes to grief.
v14 A stout-hearted man shall be filled with his own ways; and a good man with his own thoughts.
v15 The simple believes every word: but the prudent man betakes himself to after-thought.
v16 A wise man fears, and departs from evil; but the fool trusts in himself, and joins himself with the transgressor.
v17 A passionate man acts inconsiderately; but a sensible man bears up under many things.
v18 Fools shall have mischief for their portion; but the prudent shall take fast hold of understanding.
v19 Evil men shall fall before the good; and the ungodly shall attend at the gates of the righteous.
v20 Friends will hate poor friends; but the friends of the rich are many.
v21 He that dishonours the needy sins: but he that has pity on the poor is most blessed.
v22 They that go astray devise evils: but the good devise mercy and truth. The framers of evil do not understand mercy and truth: but compassion and faithfulness are with the framers of good.
v23 With every one who is careful there is abundance: but the pleasure-taking and indolent shall be in want.
v24 A prudent man is the crown of the wise: but the occupation of fools is evil.
v25 A faithful witness shall deliver a soul from evil: but a deceitful man kindles falsehoods.
v26 In the fear of the Lord is strong confidence: and he leaves his children a support.
v27 The commandment of the Lord is a fountain of life; and it causes men to turn aside from the snare of death.
v28 In a populous nation is the glory of a king: but in the failure of people is the ruin of a prince.
v29 A man slow to wrath abounds in wisdom: but a man of impatient spirit is very foolish.
v30 A meek-spirited man is a healer of the heart: but a sensitive heart is a corruption of the bones.
v31 He that oppresses the needy provokes his Maker: but he that honours him has pity upon the poor.
v32 The ungodly shall be driven away in his wickedness: but he who is secure in his own holiness is just.
v33 There is wisdom in the good heart of a man: but in the heart of fools it is not discerned.
v34 Righteousness exalts a nation: but sins diminish tribes.
v35 An understanding servant is acceptable to a king; and by his good behaviour he removes disgrace.
