v1 A fair name is better than much wealth, and good favour is above silver and gold.
v2 The rich and the poor meet together; but the Lord made them both.
v3 An intelligent man seeing a bad man severely punished is himself instructed, but fools pass by and are punished.
v4 The fear of the Lord is the offspring of wisdom, and wealth, and glory, and life.
v5 Thistles and snares are in perverse ways; but he that keeps his soul will refrain from them.
v7 The rich will rule over the poor, and servants will lend to their own masters.
v8 He that sows wickedness shall reap troubles; and shall fully receive the punishment of his deeds.
v9 He that has pity on the poor shall himself be maintained; for he has given of his own bread to the poor.
v10 Cast out a pestilent person from the council, and strife shall go out with him; for when he sits in the council he dishonours all.
v11 The Lord loves holy hearts, and all blameless persons are acceptable with him: a king rules with his lips.
v12 But the eyes of the Lord preserve discretion; but the transgressor despises wise words.
v13 The sluggard makes excuses, and says, There is a lion in the ways, and murderers in the streets.
v14 The mouth of a transgressor is a deep pit; and he that is hated of the Lord shall fall into it.
v15 Folly is attached to the heart of a child, but the rod and instruction are then far from him.
v16 He that oppresses the poor, increases his own substance, yet gives to the rich so as to make it less.
v17 Incline thine ear to the words of wise men: hear also my word, and apply thine heart,
v18 that thou mayest know that they are good: and if thou lay them to heart, they shall also gladden thee on thy lips.
v19 That thy hope may be in the Lord, and he may make thy way known to thee.
v20 And do thou too repeatedly record them for thyself on the table of thine heart, for counsel and knowledge.
v21 I therefore teach thee truth, and knowledge good to hear; that thou mayest answer words of truth to them that question thee.
v22 Do no violence to the poor, for he is needy: neither dishonour the helpless man in the gates.
v23 For the Lord will plead his cause, and thou shalt deliver thy soul in safety.
v24 Be not companion to a furious man; neither lodge with a passionate man:
v25 lest thou learn of his ways, and get snares to thy soul.
v26 Become not surety from respect of a man's person.
v27 For if those have not whence to give compensation, they will take the bed that is under thee.
v28 Remove not the old landmarks, which thy fathers placed.
v29 It is fit that an observant man and one diligent in his business should attend on kings, and not attend on slothful men.
