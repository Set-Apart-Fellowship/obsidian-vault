v1 Thou shalt proclaim wisdom, that understanding may be obedient to thee.
v2 For she is on lofty eminences, and stands in the midst of the ways.
v3 For she sits by the gates of princes, and sings in the entrances, saying ,
v4 You, O men, I exhort; and utter my voice to the sons of men.
v5 O ye simple, understand subtlety, and ye that are untaught, imbibe knowledge.
v6 Hearken to me; for I will speak solemn truths ; and will produce right sayings from my lips.
v7 For my throat shall meditate truth; and false lips are an abomination before me.
v8 All the words of my mouth are in righteousness; there is nothing in them wrong or perverse.
v9 They are all evident to those that understand, and right to those that find knowledge.
v10 Receive instruction, and not silver; and knowledge rather than tried gold.
v11 For wisdom is better than precious stones; and no valuable substance is of equal worth with it.
v12 I wisdom have dwelt with counsel and knowledge, and I have called upon understanding.
v13 The fear of the Lord hates unrighteousness, and insolence, and pride, and the ways of wicked men; and I hate the perverse ways of bad men.
v14 Counsel and safety are mine: prudence is mine, and strength is mine.
v15 By me kings reign, and princes decree justice.
v16 By me nobles become great, and monarchs by me rule over the earth.
v17 I love those that love me; and they that seek me shall find me .
v18 Wealth and glory belong to me; yea, abundant possessions and righteousness.
v19 It is better to have my fruit than to have gold and precious stones; and my produce is better than choice silver.
v20 I walk in ways of righteousness, and am conversant with the paths of judgment;
v21 that I may divide substance to them that love me, and may fill their treasures with good things.
v22 The Lord made me the beginning of his ways for his works.
v23 He established me before time was in the beginning, before he made the earth:
v24 even before he made the depths; before the fountains of water came forth:
v25 before the mountains were settled, and before all hills, he begets me.
v26 The Lord made countries and uninhabited tracts , and the highest inhabited parts of the world.
v27 When he prepared the heaven, I was present with him; and when he prepared his throne upon the winds:
v28 and when he strengthened the clouds above; and when he secured the fountains of the earth:
v29 and when he strengthened the foundations of the earth:
v30 I was by him, suiting myself to him , I was that wherein he took delight; and daily I rejoiced in his presence continually.
v31 For he rejoiced when he had completed the world, and rejoiced among the children of men.
v32 Now then, my son, hear me: blessed is the man who shall hearken to me, and the mortal who shall keep my ways;
v34 watching daily at my doors, waiting at the posts of my entrances.
v35 For my outgoings are the outgoings of life, and in them is prepared favour from the Lord.
v36 But they that sin against me act wickedly against their own souls: and they that hate me love death.
