v1 These are the miscellaneous instructions of Solomon, which the friends of Ezekias king of Judea copied out.
v2 The glory of God conceals a matter: but the glory of a king honours business.
v3 Heaven is high, and earth is deep, and a king's heart is unsearchable.
v4 Beat the drossy silver, and it shall be made entirely pure.
v5 Slay the ungodly from before the king, and his throne shall prosper in righteousness.
v6 Be not boastful in the presence of the king, and remain not in the places of princes;
v7 for it is better for thee that it should be said, Come up to me, than that one should humble thee in the presence of the prince; speak of that which thine eyes have seen.
v8 Get not suddenly into a quarrel, lest thou repent at last.
v9 Whenever thy friend shall reproach thee, retreat backward, despise him not;
v10 lest thy friend continue to reproach thee, so thy quarrel and enmity shall not depart, but shall be to thee like death.
v11 As a golden apple in a necklace of sardius, so is it to speak a wise word.
v12 In an ear-ring of gold a precious sardius is also set; so is a wise word to an obedient ear.
v13 As a fall of snow in the time of harvest is good against heat, so a faithful messenger refreshes those that send him; for he helps the souls of his employers.
v14 As winds and clouds and rains are most evident objects , so is he that boasts of a false gift.
v15 In long-suffering is prosperity to kings, and a soft tongue breaks the bones.
v16 Having found honey, eat only what is enough, lest haply thou be filled, and vomit it up.
v17 Enter sparingly into thy friend's house, lest he be satiated with thy company, and hate thee.
v18 As a club, and a dagger, and a pointed arrow, so also is a man who bears false witness against his friend.
v19 The way of the wicked and the foot of the transgressor shall perish in an evil day.
v20 As vinegar is bad for a sore, so trouble befalling the body afflicts the heart.
v21 If thine enemy hunger, feed him; if he thirst, give him drink;
v22 for so doing thou shalt heap coals of fire upon his head, and the Lord shall reward thee with good.
v23 The north wind raises clouds; so an impudent face provokes the tongue.
v24 It is better to dwell on a corner of the roof, than with a railing woman in an open house.
v25 As cold water is agreeable to a thirsting soul, so is a good message from a land far off.
v26 As if one should stop a well, and corrupt a spring of water, so is it unseemly for a righteous man to fall before an ungodly man.
v27 It is not good to eat much honey; but it is right to honour venerable sayings.
v28 As a city whose walls are broken down, and which is unfortified, so is a man who does anything without counsel.
