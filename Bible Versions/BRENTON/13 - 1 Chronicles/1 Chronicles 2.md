v1 These are the names of the sons of Israel;
v2 Ruben, Symeon, Levi, Juda, Issachar, Zabulon, Dan, Joseph, Benjamin, Nephthali, Gad, Aser.
v3 The sons of Juda; Er, Aunan, Selom. These three were born to him of the daughter of Sava the Chananitish woman: and Er, the first-born of Juda, was wicked before the Lord, and he slew him.
v4 And Thamar his daughter-in-law bore to him Phares, and Zara: all the sons of Juda were five.
v5 The sons of Phares, Esrom, and Jemuel.
v6 And the sons of Zara, Zambri, and Ætham, and Æmuan, and Calchal, and Darad, in all five.
v7 And the sons of Charmi; Achar the troubler of Israel, who was disobedient in the accursed thing.
v8 And the sons of Ætham; Azarias,
v9 and the sons of Esrom who were born to him; Jerameel, and Aram, and Chaleb.
v10 And Aram begot Aminadab, and Aminadab begot Naasson, chief of the house of Juda.
v11 And Naasson begot Salmon, and Salmon begot Booz,
v12 and Booz begot Obed, and Obed begot Jessæ.
v13 And Jessæ begot his first-born Eliab, Aminadab was the second, Samaa the third,
v14 Nathanael the fourth, Zabdai the fifth,
v15 Asam the sixth, David the seventh.
v16 And their sister was Saruia, and another Abigaia: and the sons of Saruia were Abisa, and Joab, and Asael, three.
v17 And Abigaia bore Amessab: and the father of Amessab was Jothor the Ismaelite.
v18 And Chaleb the son of Esrom took Gazuba to wife, and Jerioth: and these were her sons; Jasar, and Subab, and Ardon.
v19 And Gazuba died; and Chaleb took to himself Ephrath, and she bore to him Or.
v20 And Or begot Uri, and Uri begot Beseleel.
v21 And after this Esron went in to the daughter of Machir the father of Galaad, and he took her when he was sixty-five years old; and she bore him Seruch.
v22 And Seruch begot Jair, and he had twenty-three cities in Galaad.
v23 And he took Gedsur and Aram, the towns of Jair from them; with Canath and its towns, sixty cities. All these belonged to the sons of Machir the father of Galaad.
v24 And after the death of Esron, Chaleb came to Ephratha; and the wife of Esron was Abia; and she bore him Ascho the father of Thecoe.
v25 And the sons of Jerameel the first-born of Esron were , the first-born Ram, and Banaa, and Aram, and Asan his brother.
v26 And Jerameel had another wife, and her name was Atara: she is the mother of Ozom.
v27 And the sons of Ram the first-born of Jerameel were Maas, and Jamin, and Acor.
v28 And the sons of Ozom were, Samai, and Jadae: and the sons of Samai; Nadab, and Abisur.
v29 And the name of the wife of Abisur was Abichaia, and she bore him Achabar, and Moel.
v30 And the sons of Nadab; Salad and Apphain; and Salad died without children.
v31 And the sons of Apphain, Isemiel; and the sons of Isemiel, Sosan; and the sons of Sosan, Dadai.
v32 And the sons of Dadai, Achisamas, Jether, Jonathan: and Jether died childless.
v33 And the sons of Jonathan; Phaleth, and Hozam. These were the sons of Jerameel.
v34 And Sosan had no sons, but daughters. And Sosan had an Egyptian servant, and his name was Jochel.
v35 And Sosan gave his daughter to Jochel his servant to wife; and she bore him Ethi.
v36 And Ethi begot Nathan, and Nathan begot Zabed,
v37 and Zabed begot Aphamel, and Aphamel begot Obed.
v38 And Obed begot Jeu, and Jeu begot Azarias,
v39 and Azarias begot Chelles, and Chelles begot Eleasa,
v40 and Eleasa begot Sosomai, and Sosomai begot Salum,
v41 and Salum begot Jechemias, and Jechemias begot Elisama, and Elisama begot Ismael.
v42 And the sons of Chaleb the brother of Jerameel were , Marisa his first-born, he is the father of Ziph:—and the sons of Marisa the father of Chebron.
v43 And the sons of Chebron; Core, and Thapphus, and Recom, and Samaa.
v44 And Samaa begot Raem the father of Jeclan: and Jeclan begot Samai.
v45 And his son was Maon: and Maon is the father of Bæthsur.
v46 And Gæpha the concubine of Chaleb bore Aram, and Mosa, and Gezue.
v47 And the sons of Addai were Ragem, and Joatham, and Sogar, and Phalec, and Gæpha, and Sagae.
v48 And Chaleb's concubine Mocha bore Saber, and Tharam.
v49 She bore also Sagae the father of Madmena, and Sau the father of Machabena, and the father of Gæbal: and the daughter of Chaleb was Ascha.
v50 These were the sons of Chaleb: the sons of Or the first-born of Ephratha; Sobal the father of Cariathiarim,
v51 Salomon the father of Bætha, Lammon the father of Bæthalaem, and Arim the father of Bethgedor.
v52 And the sons of Sobal the father of Cariathiarim were Araa, and Æsi, and Ammanith,
v53 and Umasphaë, cities of Jair; Æthalim, and Miphithim, and Hesamathim, and Hemasaraim; from these went forth the Sarathæans, and the sons of Esthaam.
v54 The sons of Salomon; Bæthalaem, the Netophathite, Ataroth of the house of Joab, and half of the family of Malathi, Esari.
v55 The families of the scribes dwelling in Jabis; Thargathiim, and Samathiim, and Sochathim, these are the Kinæans that came of Hemath, the father of the house of Rechab.
