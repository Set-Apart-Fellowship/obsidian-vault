v1 And these are they that came to Sikelag, when he yet kept himself close because of Saul the son of Kis; and these were among the mighty, aiding him in war,
v2 and using the bow with the right hand and with the left, and slingers with stones, and shooters with bows. Of the brethren of Saul of Benjamin,
v3 the chief was Achiezer, and Joas son of Asma the Gabathite, and Joel and Jophalet, sons of Asmoth, and Berchia, and Jeul of Anathoth,
v4 and Samaias the Gabaonite a mighty man among the thirty, and over the thirty;
v5 and Jeremia, and Jeziel, and Joanan, and Jozabath of Gadarathiim,
v6 Azai and Arimuth, and Baalia, and Samaraia, and Saphatias of Charæphiel,
v7 Helcana, and Jesuni, and Ozriel, and Jozara, and Sobocam, and the Corites,
v8 and Jelia and Zabadia, sons of Iroam, and the men of Gedor.
v9 And from Gad these separated themselves to David from the wilderness, strong mighty men of war, bearing shields and spears, and their faces were as the face of a lion, and they were nimble as roes upon the mountains in speed.
v10 Aza the chief, Abdia the second, Eliab the third,
v11 Masmana the fourth, Jeremias the fifth,
v12 Jethi the sixth, Eliab the seventh,
v13 Joanan the eighth, Eleazer the ninth,
v14 Jeremia the tenth, Melchabanai the eleventh.
v15 These were chiefs of the army of the sons of Gad, the least one commander of a hundred, and the greatest one of a thousand.
v16 These are the men that crossed over Jordan in the first month, and it had overflowed all its banks; and they drove out all the inhabitants of the valleys, from the east to the west.
v17 And there came some of the sons of Benjamin and Juda to the assistance of David.
v18 And David went out to meet them, and said to them, If ye are come peaceably to me, let my heart be at peace with you: but if ye are come to betray me to my enemies unfaithfully, the God of your fathers look upon it, and reprove it.
v19 And the Spirit came upon Amasai, a captain of the thirty, and he said, Go, David, son of Jesse, thou and thy people, peace, peace be to thee, and peace to thy helpers, for thy God has helped thee. And David received them, and made them captains of the forces.
v20 And some came to David from Manasse, when the Philistines came against Saul to war: and he helped them not, because the captains of the Philistines took counsel, saying, With the heads of those men will he return to his master Saul.
v21 When David was going to Sikelag, there came to him of Manasse, Edna and Jozabath, and Rodiel, and Michael, and Josabaith, and Elimuth, and Semathi: these are the captains of thousands of Manasse.
v22 And they fought on the side of David against a troop, for they were all men of might; and they were commanders in the army, because of their might.
v23 For daily men came to David, till they amounted to a great force, as the force of God.
v24 And these are the names of the commanders of the army, who came to David to Chebron, to turn the kingdom of Saul to him according to the word of the Lord.
v25 The sons of Juda, bearing shields and spears, six thousand and eight hundred mighty in war.
v26 Of the sons of Symeon mighty for battle, seven thousand and a hundred.
v27 Of the sons of Levi, four thousand and six hundred.
v28 And Joadas the chief of the family of Aaron, and with him three thousand and seven hundred.
v29 And Sadoc, a young man mighty in strength, and there were twenty-two leaders of his father's house.
v30 And of the sons of Benjamin, the brethren of Saul, three thousand: and still the greater part of them kept the guard of the house of Saul.
v31 And of the sons of Ephraim, twenty thousand and eight hundred mighty men, famous in the houses of their fathers.
v32 And of the half-tribe of Manasse, eighteen thousand, even those who were named by name, to make David king.
v33 And of the sons of Issachar having wisdom with regard to the times, knowing what Israel should do, two hundred; and all their brethren with them.
v34 And of Zabulon they that went out to battle, with all weapons of war, were fifty thousand to help David, not weak-handed.
v35 And of Nephthali a thousand captains, and with them men with shields and spears, thirty-seven thousand.
v36 And of the Danites men ready for war twenty-eight thousand and eight hundred.
v37 And of Aser, they that went out to give aid in war, forty thousand.
v38 And from the country beyond Jordan, from Ruben, and the Gadites, and from the half-tribe of Manasse, a hundred and twenty thousand, with all weapons of war.
v39 All these were men of war, setting the army in battle array, with a peaceful mind towards him , and they came to Chebron to make David king over all Israel: and the rest of Israel were of one mind to make David king.
v40 And they were there three days eating and drinking, for their brethren had made preparations.
v41 And their neighbours, as far as Issachar and Zabulon and Nephthali, brought to them upon camels, and asses, and mules, and upon calves, victuals, meal, cakes of figs, raisins, wine, and oil, calves and sheep abundantly: for there was joy in Israel.
