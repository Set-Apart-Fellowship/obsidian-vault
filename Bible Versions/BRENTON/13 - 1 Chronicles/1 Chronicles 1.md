v1 Adam , Seth, Enos,
v2 and Cainan, Maleleel, Jared,
v3 Enoch, Mathusala, Lamech,
v4 Noe: the sons of Noe, Sem, Cham, Japheth.
v5 The sons of Japheth, Gamer, Magog, Madaim, Jovan, Helisa, Thobel, Mosoch, and Thiras.
v6 And the sons of Gamer, Aschanaz, and Riphath, and Thorgama.
v7 And the sons of Jovan, Helisa, and Tharsis, the Citians, and Rhodians.
v8 And the sons of Cham, Chus, and Mesraim, Phud and Chanaan.
v9 And the sons of Chus, Saba, and Evila, and Sabatha, and Regma, and Sebethaca: and the sons of Regma, Saba, and Dadan.
v10 And Chus begot Nebrod: he began to be a mighty hunter on the earth.
v17 The sons of Sem, Ælam, and Assur,
v24 and Arphaxad, Sala,
v25 Eber, Pheleg, Ragan,
v26 Seruch, Nachor, Tharrha,
v27 Abraam.
v28 And the sons of Abraam, Isaac, and Ismael.
v29 And these are their generations: the first-born of Ismael, Nabæoth, and Kedar, Nabdeel, Massam,
v30 Masma, Iduma, Masse, Chondan, Thæman,
v31 Jettur, Naphes, Kedma: these are the sons of Ismael.
v32 And the sons of Chettura Abraam's concubine:—and she bore him Zembram, Jexan, Madiam, Madam, Sobac, Soe: and the sons of Jexan; Dædan, and Sabai;
v33 and the sons of Madiam; Gephar, and Opher, and Enoch, and Abida, and Eldada; all these were the sons of Chettura.
v34 And Abraam begot Isaac: and the sons of Isaac were Jacob, and Esau.
v35 The sons of Esau, Eliphaz, and Raguel, and Jeul, and Jeglom, and Core.
v36 The sons of Eliphaz: Thæman, and Omar, Sophar, and Goötham, and Kenez, and Thamna, and Amalec.
v37 And the sons of Raguel, Naches, Zare, Some, and Moze.
v38 The sons of Seir, Lotan, Sobal, Sebegon, Ana, Deson, Osar, and Disan.
v39 And the sons of Lotan, Chorri, and Æman; and the sister of Lotan was Thamna.
v40 The sons of Sobal; Alon, Machanath, Tæbel, Sophi, and Onan: and the sons of Sebegon; Æth, and Sonan.
v41 The sons of Sonan, Dæson: and the sons of Dæson; Emeron, and Asebon, and Jethram, and Charran.
v42 And the sons of Hosar, Balaam, and Zucam, and Acan: the sons of Disan, Os, and Aran.
v43 And these are their kings, Balac the son of Beor; and the name of his city was Dennaba.
v44 And Balac died, and Jobab the son of Zara of Bosorrha reigned in his stead.
v45 And Jobab died, and Asom of the land of the Thæmanites reigned in his stead.
v46 And Asom died, and Adad the son of Barad reigned in his stead, who smote Madiam in the plain of Moab: and the name of his city was Gethaim.
v47 And Adad died, and Sebla of Masecca reigned in his stead.
v48 And Sebla died, and Saul of Rhoboth by the river reigned in his stead.
v49 And Saul died, and Balaennor son of Achobor reigned in his stead.
v50 And Balaennor died, and Adad son of Barad reigned in his stead; and the name of his city was Phogor.
v51 The princes of Edom: prince Thamna, prince Golada, prince Jether,
v52 prince Elibamas, prince Elas, prince Phinon,
v53 prince Kenez, prince Thæman, prince Babsar, prince Magediel,
v54 prince Zaphoin. These are the princes of Edom.
