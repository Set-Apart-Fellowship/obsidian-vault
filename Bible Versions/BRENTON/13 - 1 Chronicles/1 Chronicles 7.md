v1 And as to the sons of Issachar, they were Thola, and Phua, and Jasub, and Semeron, four.
v2 And the sons of Thola; Ozi, Raphaia, and Jeriel, and Jamai, and Jemasan, and Samuel, chiefs of their fathers' houses belonging to Thola, men of might according to their generations; their number in the days of David was twenty and two thousand and six hundred.
v3 And the sons of Ozi; Jezraia: and the sons of Jezraia; Michael, Abdiu, and Joel, and Jesia, five, all rulers.
v4 And with them, according to their generations, according to the houses of their families, were men mighty to set armies in array for war, thirty and six thousand, for they had multiplied their wives and children.
v5 And their brethren among all the families of Issachar, also mighty men, were eighty-seven thousand— this was the number of them all.
v6 The sons of Benjamin; Bale, and Bachir, and Jediel, three.
v7 And the sons of Bale; Esebon, and Ozi, and Oziel, and Jerimuth, and Uri, five; heads of houses of families, mighty men; and their number was twenty and two thousand and thirty-four.
v8 And the sons of Bachir; Zemira, and Joas, and Eliezer, and Elithenan, and Amaria, and Jerimuth, and Abiud, and Anathoth, and Eleemeth: all these were the sons of Bachir.
v9 And their number according to their generations, ( they were chiefs of their fathers' houses, men of might), was twenty thousand and two hundred.
v10 And the sons of Jediel; Balaan: and the sons of Balaan; Jaüs, and Benjamin, and Aoth, and Chanana, and Zæthan, and Tharsi, and Achisaar.
v11 All these were the sons of Jediel, chiefs of their families, men of might, seventeen thousand and two hundred, going forth to war with might.
v12 And Sapphin, and Apphin, and the sons of Or, Asom, whose son was Aor.
v13 The sons of Nephthali; Jasiel, Goni, and Aser, and Sellum, his sons, Balam his son.
v14 The sons of Manasse; Esriel, whom his Syrian concubine bore; and she bore to him also Machir the father of Galaad.
v15 And Machir took a wife for Apphin and Sapphin, and his sister's name was Moöcha; and the name of the second son was Sapphaad; and to Sapphaad were born daughters.
v16 And Moöcha the wife of Machir bore a son, and called his name Phares; and his brother's name was Surus; his sons were Ulam, and Rocom.
v17 And the sons of Ulam; Badam. These were the sons of Galaad, the son of Machir, the son of Manasse.
v18 And his sister Malecheth bore Isud, and Abiezer, and Maela.
v19 And the sons of Semira were, Aim, and Sychem, and Lakim, and Anian.
v20 And the sons of Ephraim; Sothalath, and Barad his son, and Thaath his son, Elada his son, Saath his son,
v21 and Zabad his son, Sothele his son, and Azer, and Elead: and the men of Geth who were born in the land slew them, because they went down to take their cattle.
v22 And their father Ephraim mourned many days, and his brethren came to comfort him.
v23 And he went in to his wife, and she conceived, and bore a son, and he called his name Beria, because, said he , He was afflicted in my house.
v24 And his daughter was Saraa, and he was among them that were left, and he built Bæthoron the upper and the lower. And the descendants of Ozan were Seera,
v25 and Raphe his son, Saraph and Thalees his sons, Thaen his son.
v26 To Laadan his son was born his son Amiud, his son Helisamai, his son
v27 Nun, his son Jesue, these were his sons.
v28 And their possession and their dwelling were Bæthel and her towns, to the east Noaran, westward Gazer and her towns, and Sychem and her towns, as far as Gaza and her towns.
v29 And as far as the borders of the sons of Manasse, Bæthsaan and her towns, Thanach and her towns, Mageddo and her towns, Dor and her towns. In this the children of Joseph the son of Israel dwelt.
v30 The sons of Aser; Jemna, and Suia, and Isui, and Beria, and Sore their sister.
v31 And the sons of Beria; Chaber, and Melchiel; he was the father of Berthaith.
v32 And Chaber begot Japhlet, and Samer, and Chothan, and Sola their sister.
v33 And the sons of Japhlet; Phasec, and Bamael, and Asith: these are the sons of Japhlet.
v34 And the sons of Semmer; Achir, and Rooga, and Jaba, and Aram.
v35 And the sons of Elam his brother; Sopha, and Imana, and Selles, and Amal.
v36 The sons of Sopha; Sue, and Arnaphar, and Suda, and Barin, and Imran,
v37 and Basan, and Oa, and Sama, and Salisa, and Jethra, and Beëra.
v38 And the sons of Jether, Jephina, and Phaspha, and Ara.
v39 And the sons of Ola; Orech, Aniel, and Rasia.
v40 All these were the sons of Aser, all heads of families, choice, mighty men, chief leaders: their number for battle array—their number was twenty-six thousand men.
