v1 Now Benjamin begot Bale his first-born, and Asbel his second son , Aara the third, Noa the fourth,
v2 and Rapha the fifth.
v3 And the sons of Bale were, Adir, and Gera, and Abiud,
v4 and Abessue, and Noama, and Achia,
v5 and Gera, and Sephupham, and Uram.
v6 These were the sons of Aod: these are the heads of families to them that dwell in Gabee, and they removed them to Machanathi:
v7 and Nooma, and Achia and Gera, he removed them, and he begot Aza, and Jachicho.
v8 And Saarin begot children in the plain of Moab, after that he had sent away Osin and Baada his wives.
v9 And he begot of his wife Ada, Jolab, and Sebia, and Misa, and Melchas,
v10 and Jebus, and Zabia, and Marma: these were heads of families.
v11 And of Osin he begot Abitol, and Alphaal.
v12 And the sons of Alphaal; Obed, Misaal, Semmer: he built Ona, and Lod, and its towns:
v13 and Beria, and Sama; these were heads of families among the dwellers in Elam, and they drove out the inhabitants of Geth.
v14 And his brethren were Sosec, and Arimoth,
v15 and Zabadia, and Ored, and Eder,
v16 and Michael, and Jespha, and Joda, the sons of Beria:
v17 and Zabadia, and Mosollam, and Azaki, and Abar,
v18 and Isamari, and Jexlias, and Jobab, the sons of Elphaal:
v19 and Jakim, and Zachri, and Zabdi,
v20 and Elionai, and Salathi,
v21 and Elieli, and Adaia, and Baraia, and Samarath, sons of Samaith:
v22 and Jesphan, and Obed, and Eliel,
v23 and Abdon, and Zechri, and Anan,
v24 and Anania, and Ambri, and Ælam, and Anathoth,
v25 and Jathin, and Jephadias, and Phanuel, the sons of Sosec:
v26 and Samsari, and Saarias, and Gotholia,
v27 and Jarasia, and Eria, and Zechri, son of Iroam.
v28 These were heads of families, chiefs according to their generations: these dwelt in Jerusalem.
v29 And the father of Gabaon dwelt in Gabaon; and his wife's name was Moacha.
v30 And her first-born son was Abdon, and Sur, and Kis, and Baal, and Nadab, and Ner,
v31 and Gedur and his brother, and Zacchur, and Makeloth.
v32 And Makeloth begot Samaa: for these dwelt in Jerusalem in the presence of their brethren with their brethren.
v33 And Ner begot Kis, and Kis begot Saul, and Saul begot Jonathan, and Melchisue, and Aminadab, and Asabal.
v34 And the son of Jonathan was Meribaal; and Meribaal begot Micha.
v35 And the sons of Micha; Phithon, and Melach, and Tharach, and Achaz.
v36 And Achaz begot Jada, and Jada begot Salæmath, and Asmoth, and Zambri; and Zambri begot Mæsa;
v37 and Mæsa begot Baana: Rhaphæa, was his son, Elasa his son, Esel his son.
v38 And Esel had six sons, and these were their names; Ezricam his first-born, and Ismael, and Saraia, and Abdia, and Anan, and Asa: all these were the sons of Esel.
v39 And the sons of Asel his brother; Ælam his first-born, and Jas the second, and Eliphalet the third.
v40 And the sons of Ælam were mighty men, bending the bow, and multiplying sons and grandsons, a hundred and fifty. All these were of the sons of Benjamin.
