v1 So they brought in the ark of God, and set it in the midst of the tabernacle which David pitched for it; and they offered whole-burnt-offerings and peace-offerings before God.
v2 And David finished offering up whole-burnt-offerings and peace-offerings, and blessed the people in the name of the Lord.
v3 And he divided to every man of Israel (both men and women), to every man one baker's loaf, and a cake.
v4 And he appointed before the ark of the covenant of the Lord, Levites to minister and lift up the voice, and to give thanks and praise the Lord God of Israel:
v5 Asaph was the chief, and next to him Zacharias, Jeiel, Semiramoth, and Jeiel, Mattathias, Eliab, and Banæas, and Abdedom: and Jeiel sounding with musical instruments, lutes and harps, and Asaph with cymbals:
v6 and Banæas and Oziel the priests sounding continually with trumpets before the ark of the covenant of God in that day.
v7 Then David first gave orders to praise the Lord by the hand of Asaph and his brethren.
v8 Song. Give thanks to the Lord, call upon him by his name, make known his designs among the people.
v9 Sing songs to him, and sing hymns to him, relate to all people his wonderful deeds, which the Lord has wrought.
v10 Praise his holy name, the heart that seeks his pleasure shall rejoice.
v11 Seek the Lord and be strong, seek his face continually.
v12 Remember his wonderful works which he has wrought, his wonders, and the judgments of his mouth;
v13 ye seed of Israel his servants, ye seed of Jacob his chosen ones.
v14 He is the Lord our God; his judgments are in all the earth.
v15 Let us remember his covenant for ever, his word which he commanded to a thousand generations,
v16 which he covenanted with Abraham, and his oath sworn to Isaac.
v17 He confirmed it to Jacob for an ordinance, to Israel as an everlasting covenant,
v18 saying, To thee will I give the land of Chanaan, the line of your inheritance:
v19 when they were few in number, when they were but little, and dwelt as strangers in it;
v20 and went from nation to nation, and from one kingdom to another people.
v21 He suffered not a man to oppress them, and he reproved kings for their sakes,
v22 saying, Touch not my anointed ones, and deal not wrongfully with my prophets.
v23 Sing ye to the Lord, all the earth; proclaim his salvation from day to day.
v24 Declare among the nations his glory, his wondrous deeds among all peoples.
v25 For the Lord is great, and greatly to be praised: he is to be feared above all gods.
v26 For all the gods of the nations are idols; but our God made the heavens.
v27 Glory and praise are in his presence; strength and rejoicing are in his place.
v28 Give to the Lord, ye families of the nations, give to the Lord glory and strength.
v29 Give to the Lord the glory belonging to his name: take gifts and offer them before him; and worship the Lord in his holy courts.
v30 Let the whole earth fear before him; let the earth be established, and not be moved.
v31 Let the heavens rejoice, and let the earth exult; and let them say among the nations, The Lord reigns.
v32 The sea with its fulness shall resound and the tree of the field, and all things in it.
v33 Then shall the trees of the wood rejoice before the Lord, for he is come to judge the earth.
v34 Give thanks to the Lord, for it is good, for his mercy is for ever.
v35 And say ye, Save us, O God of our salvation, and gather us, and rescue us from among the heathen, that we may praise thy holy name, and glory in thy praises.
v36 Blessed be the Lord God of Israel from everlasting and to everlasting: And all the people shall say, Amen. So they praised the Lord.
v37 And they left there Asaph and his brethren before the ark of the covenant of the Lord, to minister before the ark continually, according to the service of each day: from day to day.
v38 And Abdedom and his brethren were sixty and eight; and Abdedom the son of Idithun, and Osa, were to be door-keepers.
v39 And they appointed Sadoc the priest, and his brethren the priests, before the tabernacle of the Lord in the high place in Gabaon,
v40 to offer up whole-burnt-offerings to the Lord on the altar of whole-burnt-offerings continually morning and evening, and according to all things written in the law of the Lord, which he commanded the children of Israel by Moses the servant of God.
v41 And with him were Æman and Idithun, and the rest chosen out by name to praise the Lord, for his mercy endures for ever.
v42 And with them there were trumpets and cymbals to sound aloud, and musical instruments for the songs of God: and the sons of Idithun were at the gate.
v43 And all the people went every one to his home: and David returned to bless his house.
