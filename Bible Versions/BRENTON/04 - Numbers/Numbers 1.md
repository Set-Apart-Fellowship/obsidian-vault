v1 And the Lord spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sina, in the tabernacle of witness, on the first day of the second month, in the second year of their departure from the land of Egypt, saying,
v2 Take the sum of all the congregation of Israel according to their kindreds, according to the houses of their fathers' families, according to their number by their names, according to their heads: every male
v3 from twenty years old and upwards, every one that goes forth in the forces of Israel, take account of them with their strength; thou and Aaron take account of them.
v4 And with you there shall be each one of the rulers according to the tribe of each: they shall be according to the houses of their families.
v5 And these are the names of the men who shall be present with you; of the tribe of Ruben, Elisur the son of Sediur.
v6 Of Symeon, Salamiel the son of Surisadai.
v7 Of Juda, Naasson the son of Aminadab.
v8 Of Issachar, Nathanael the son of Sogar.
v9 Of Zabulon, Eliab the son of Chælon.
v10 Of the sons of Joseph, of Ephraim, Elisama the son of Emiud: of Manasses, Gamaliel the son of Phadasur.
v11 Of Benjamin, Abidan the son of Gadeoni.
v12 Of Dan, Achiezer the son of Amisadai.
v13 Of Aser, Phagaiel the son of Echran.
v14 Of Gad, Elisaph the son of Raguel.
v15 Of Nephthali, Achire the son of Ænan.
v16 These were famous men of the congregation, heads of the tribes according to their families: these are heads of thousands in Israel.
v17 And Moses and Aaron took these men who were called by name.
v18 And they assembled all the congregation on the first day of the month in the second year; and they registered them after their lineage, after their families, after the number of their names, from twenty years old and upwards, every male according to their number:
v19 as the Lord commanded Moses, so they were numbered in the wilderness of Sina.
v20 And the sons of Ruben the first-born of Israel according to their kindreds, according to their divisions, according to the houses of their families, according to the number of their names, according to their heads, were—all males from twenty years old and upward, every one that went out with the host—
v21 the numbering of them of the tribe of Ruben, was forty-six thousand and four hundred.
v22 For the children of Symeon according to their kindreds, according to their divisions, according to the houses of their families, according to the number of their names, according to their polls, all males from twenty years old and upward, every one that goes out with the host,
v23 the numbering of them of the tribe of Symeon, was fifty-nine thousand and three hundred.
v24 For the sons of Juda according to their kindreds, according to their divisions, according to the houses of their families, according to the number of their names, according to their polls, all males from twenty years old and upward, every one that goes forth with the host,
v25 the numbering of them of the tribe of Juda, was seventy-four thousand and six hundred.
v26 For the sons of Issachar according to their kindreds, according to their divisions, according to the houses of their families, according to the number of their names, according to their polls, all males from twenty years old and upward, every one that goes forth with the host,
v27 the numbering of them of the tribe of Issachar, was fifty-four thousand and four hundred.
v28 For the sons of Zabulon according to their kindreds, according to their divisions, according to the houses of their families, according to the number of their names, according to their polls, all males from twenty years old and upward, every one that goes out with the host,
v29 the numbering of them of the tribe of Zabulon, was fifty-seven thousand and four hundred.
v30 For the sons of Joseph, the sons of Ephraim, according to their kindreds, according to their divisions, according to the houses of their families, according to the number of their names, according to their polls, all males from twenty years old and upward, every one that goes out with the host,
v31 the numbering of them of the tribe of Ephraim, was forty thousand and five hundred.
v32 For the sons of Manasse according to their kindreds, according to their divisions, according to the houses of their families, according to the number of their names, according to their polls, all males from twenty years old and upward, every one that goes out with the host,
v33 the numbering of them of the tribe of Manasse, was thirty-two thousand and two hundred.
v34 For the sons of Benjamin according to their kindreds, according to their divisions, according to the houses of their families, according to the number of their names, according to their polls, every male from twenty years old and upward, every one that goes forth with the host,
v35 the numbering of them of the tribe of Benjamin, was thirty-five thousand and four hundred.
v36 For the sons of Gad according to their kindreds, according to their divisions, according to the houses of their families, according to the number of their names, according to their polls, all males from twenty years old and upward, every one that goes forth with the host,
v37 the numbering of them of the tribe of Gad, was forty and five thousand and six hundred and fifty.
v38 For the sons of Dan according to their kindreds, according to their divisions, according to the houses of their families, according to the number of their names, according to their polls, all males from twenty years old and upward, every one that goes forth with the host,
v39 the numbering of them of the tribe of Dan, was sixty and two thousand and seven hundred.
v40 For the sons of Aser according to their kindreds, according to their divisions, according to the houses of their families, according to the number of their names, according to their polls, every male from twenty years old and upward, every one that goes forth with the host,
v41 the numbering of them of the tribe of Aser, was forty and one thousand and five hundred.
v42 For the sons of Nephthali according to their kindreds, according to their divisions, according to the houses of their families, according to the number of their names, according to their polls, every male from twenty years old and upward, every one who goes forth with the host,
v43 the numbering of them of the tribe of Nephthali, was fifty-three thousand and four hundred.
v44 This is the numbering which Moses and Aaron and the rulers of Israel, being twelve men, conducted: there was a man for each tribe, they were according to the tribe of the houses of their family.
v45 And the whole numbering of the children of Israel with their host from twenty years old and upward, every one that goes out to set himself in battle array in Israel, came to
v46 six hundred thousand and three thousand and five hundred and fifty.
v47 But the Levites of the tribe of their family were not counted among the children of Israel.
v48 And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,
v49 See, thou shalt not muster the tribe of Levi, and thou shalt not take their numbers, in the midst of the children of Israel.
v50 And do thou set the Levites over the tabernacle of witness, and over all its furniture, and over all things that are in it; and they shall do service in it, and they shall encamp round about the tabernacle.
v51 And in removing the tabernacle, the Levites shall take it down, and in pitching the tabernacle they shall set it up: and let the stranger that advances to touch it die.
v52 And the children of Israel shall encamp, every man in his own order, and every man according to his company, with their host.
v53 But let the Levites encamp round about the tabernacle of witness fronting it, and so there shall be no sin among the children of Israel; and the Levites themselves shall keep the guard of the tabernacle of witness.
v54 And the children of Israel did according to all that the Lord commanded Moses and Aaron, so did they.
