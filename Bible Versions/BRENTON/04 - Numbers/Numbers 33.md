v1 And these are the stages of the children of Israel, as they went out from the land of Egypt with their host by the hand of Moses and Aaron.
v2 And Moses wrote their removals and their stages, by the word of the Lord: and these are the stages of their journeying.
v3 They departed from Ramesses in the first month, on the fifteenth day of the first month; on the day after the passover the children of Israel went forth with a high hand before all the Egyptians.
v4 And the Egyptians buried those that died of them, even all that the Lord smote, every first-born in the land of Egypt; also the Lord executed vengeance on their gods.
v5 And the children of Israel departed from Ramesses, and encamped in Socchoth:
v6 and they departed from Socchoth and encamped in Buthan, which is a part of the wilderness.
v7 And they departed from Buthan and encamped at the mouth of Iroth, which is opposite Beel-sepphon, and encamped opposite Magdol.
v8 And they departed from before Iroth, and crossed the middle of the sea into the wilderness; and they went a journey of three days through the wilderness, and encamped in Picriæ.
v9 And they departed from Picriæ, and came to Ælim; and in Ælim were twelve fountains of water, and seventy palm-trees, and they encamped there by the water.
v10 And they departed from Ælim, and encamped by the Red Sea.
v11 And they departed from the Red Sea, and encamped in the wilderness of Sin.
v12 And they departed from the wilderness of Sin, and encamped in Raphaca.
v13 And they departed from Raphaca, and encamped in Ælus.
v14 And they departed from Ælus, and encamped in Raphidin; and there was no water there for the people to drink.
v15 And they departed from Raphidin, and encamped in the wilderness of Sina.
v16 And they departed from the wilderness of Sina, and encamped at the Graves of Lust.
v17 And they departed from the Graves of Lust, and encamped in Aseroth.
v18 And they departed from Aseroth, and encamped in Rathama.
v19 And they departed from Rathama, and encamped in Remmon Phares.
v20 And they departed from Remmon Phares, and encamped in Lebona.
v21 And they departed from Lebona, and encamped in Ressan.
v22 And they departed from Ressan, and encamped in Makellath.
v23 And they departed from Makellath, and encamped in Saphar.
v24 And they departed from Saphar, and encamped in Charadath.
v25 And they departed from Charadath, and encamped in Makeloth.
v26 And they departed from Makeloth, and encamped in Kataath.
v27 And they departed from Kataath, and encamped in Tarath.
v28 And they departed from Tarath, and encamped in Mathecca.
v29 And they departed from Mathecca, and encamped in Selmona.
v30 And they departed from Selmona, and encamped in Masuruth.
v31 And they departed from Masuruth, and encamped in Banæa.
v32 And they departed from Banæa, and encamped in the mountain Gadgad.
v33 And they departed from the mountain Gadgad, and encamped in Etebatha.
v34 And they departed from Etebatha, and encamped in Ebrona.
v35 And they departed from Ebrona, and encamped in Gesion Gaber.
v36 And they departed from Gesion Gaber, and encamped in the wilderness of Sin; and they departed from the wilderness of Sin, and encamped in the wilderness of Pharan; this is Cades.
v37 And they departed from Cades, and encamped in mount Or near the land of Edom.
v38 And Aaron the priest went up by the command of the Lord, and died there in the fortieth year of the departure of the children of Israel from the land of Egypt, in the fifth month, on the first day of the month.
v39 And Aaron was a hundred and twenty-three years old, when he died in mount Or.
v40 And Arad the Chananitish king (he too dwelt in the land of Chanaan) having heard when the children of Israel were entering the land —
v41 then they departed from mount Or, and encamped in Selmona.
v42 And they departed from Selmona, and encamped in Phino.
v43 And they departed from Phino, and encamped in Oboth.
v44 And they departed from Oboth, and encamped in Gai, on the other side Jordan on the borders of Moab.
v45 And they departed from Gai, and encamped in Dæbon Gad.
v46 And they departed from Dæbon Gad, and encamped in Gelmon Deblathaim.
v47 And they departed from Gelmon Deblathaim, and encamped on the mountains of Abarim, over against Nabau.
v48 And they departed from the mountains of Abarim, and encamped on the west of Moab, at Jordan by Jericho.
v49 And they encamped by Jordan between Æsimoth, as far as Belsa to the west of Moab.
v50 And the Lord spoke to Moses at the west of Moab by Jordan at Jericho, saying,
v51 Speak to the children of Israel, and thou shalt say to them, Ye are to pass over Jordan into the land of Chanaan.
v52 And ye shall destroy all that dwell in the land before your face, and ye shall abolish their high places, and all their molten images ye shall destroy, and ye shall demolish all their pillars.
v53 And ye shall destroy all the inhabitants of the land, and ye shall dwell in it, for I have given their land to you for an inheritance.
v54 And ye shall inherit their land according to your tribes; to the greater number ye shall give the larger possession, and to the smaller ye shall give the less possession; to whatsoever part a man's name shall go forth by lot , there shall be his property : ye shall inherit according to the tribes of your families.
v55 But if ye will not destroy the dwellers in the land from before you, then it shall come to pass that whomsoever of them ye shall leave shall be thorns in your eyes, and darts in your sides, and they shall be enemies to you on the land on which ye shall dwell;
v56 and it shall come to pass that as I had determined to do to them, so I will do to you.
