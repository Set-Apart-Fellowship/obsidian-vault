v1 And it came to pass in the day in which Moses finished the setting-up of the tabernacle, that he anointed it, and consecrated it, and all its furniture, and the altar and all its furniture, he even anointed them, and consecrated them.
v2 And the princes of Israel brought gifts , twelve princes of their fathers' houses: these were the heads of tribes, these are they that presided over the numbering.
v3 And they brought their gift before the Lord, six covered waggons, and twelve oxen; a waggon from two princes, and a calf from each: and they brought them before the tabernacle.
v4 And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,
v5 Take of them, and they shall be for the works of the services of the tabernacle of witness: and thou shalt give them to the Levites, to each one according to his ministration.
v6 And Moses took the waggons and the oxen, and gave them to the Levites.
v7 And he gave two waggons and four oxen to the sons of Gedson, according to their ministrations.
v8 And four waggons and eight oxen he gave to the sons of Merari according to their ministrations, by Ithamar the son of Aaron the priest.
v9 But to the sons of Caath he gave them not, because they have the ministrations of the sacred things: they shall bear them on their shoulders.
v10 And the rulers brought gifts for the dedication of the altar, in the day in which he anointed it, and the rulers brought their gifts before the altar.
v11 And the Lord said to Moses, One chief each day, they shall offer their gifts a chief each day for the dedication of the altar.
v12 And he that offered his gift on the first day, was Naasson the son of Aminadab, prince of the tribe of Juda.
v13 And he brought his gift, one silver charger of a hundred and thirty shekels was its weight, one silver bowl, of seventy shekels according to the holy shekel; both full of fine flour kneaded with oil for a meat-offering.
v14 One golden censer of ten shekels full of incense.
v15 One calf of the herd, one ram, one he-lamb of a year old for a whole-burnt-offering;
v16 and one kid of the goats for a sin-offering.
v17 And for a sacrifice of peace-offering, two heifers, five rams, five he goats, five ewe-lambs of a year old: this was the gift of Naasson the son of Aminadab.

v89 When Moses went into the tabernacle of witness to speak to God, then he heard the voice of the Lord speaking to him from off the mercy-seat, which is upon the ark of the testimony, between the two cherubs; and he spoke to him.
