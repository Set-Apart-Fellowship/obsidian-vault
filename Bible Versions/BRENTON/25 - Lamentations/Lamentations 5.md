v1 Remember, O Lord, what has happened to us: behold, and look on our reproach.
v2 Our inheritance has been turned away to aliens, our houses to strangers:
v3 we are become orphans, we have no father, our mothers are as widows.
v4 We have drunk our water for money; our wood is sold to us for a burden on our neck:
v5 we have been persecuted, we have laboured, we have had no rest.
v6 Egypt gave the hand to us , Assur to their own satisfaction.
v7 Our fathers sinned, and are not: we have borne their iniquities.
v8 Servants have ruled over us: there is none to ransom us out of their hand.
v9 We shall bring in our bread with danger of our lives, because of the sword of the wilderness.
v10 Our skin is blackened like an oven; they are convulsed, because of the storms of famine.
v11 They humbled the women in Sion, the virgins in the cities of Juda.
v12 Princes were hanged up by their hands: the elders were not honoured.
v13 The chosen men lifted up the voice in weeping, and the youths fainted under the wood.
v14 And the elders ceased from the gate, the chosen men ceased from their music.
v15 The joy of our heart has ceased; our dance is turned into mourning.
v16 The crown has fallen from our head: yea, woe to us! for we have sinned.
v17 For this has grief come; our heart is sorrowful: for this our eyes are darkened.
v18 Over the mountain of Sion, because it is made desolate, foxes have walked therein.
v19 But thou, O Lord, shalt dwell for ever; thy throne shall endure to generation and generation.
v20 Wherefore wilt thou utterly forget us, and abandon us a long time?
v21 Turn us, O Lord, to thee, and we shall be turned; and renew our days as before.
v22 For thou hast indeed rejected us; thou hast been very wroth against us.
