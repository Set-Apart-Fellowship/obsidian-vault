v1 Aleph. I am the man that sees poverty, through the rod of his wrath upon me.
v2 He has taken me, and led me away into darkness, and not into light.
v3 Nay, against me has he turned his hand all the day.
v4 He has made old my flesh and my skin; he has broken my bones.
v5 Beth. He has built against me, and compassed my head, and brought travail upon me .
v6 He has set me in dark places, as them that have long been dead.
v7 He has builded against me, and I cannot come forth: he has made my brazen chain heavy.
v8 Gimel. Yea, though I cry and shout, he shuts out my prayer.
v9 Daleth. He has built up my ways, he has hedged my paths;
v10 he has troubled me, as a she-bear lying in wait: he is to me as a lion in secret places.
v11 He pursued me after I departed, and brought me to a stand: he has utterly ruined me.
v12 He. He has bent his bow, and set me as a mark for the arrow.
v13 He has caused the arrows of his quiver to enter into my reins.
v14 I became a laughing-stock to all my people; and their song all the day.
v15 Vau. He has filled me with bitterness, he has drenched me with gall.
v16 And he has dashed out my teeth with gravel, he has fed me with ashes.
v17 He has also removed my soul from peace: I forgot prosperity.
v18 Therefore my success has perished, and my hope from the Lord.
v19 Zain. I remembered by reason of my poverty, and because of persecution my bitterness and gall shall be remembered;
v20 and my soul shall meditate with me.
v21 This will I lay up in my heart, therefore will I endure.
v22 Heth. It is the mercies of the Lord, that he has not failed me, because his compassions are not exhausted. Pity us , O Lord, early every month: for we are not brought to an end, because his compassions are not exhausted.
v23 They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness.
v24 The Lord is my portion, says my soul; therefore will I wait for him.
v25 Teth. The Lord is good to them that wait for him: the soul which shall seek him
v26 is good, and shall wait for, and quietly expect salvation of the Lord.
v27 Teth. It is good for a man when he bears a yoke in his youth.
v28 He will sit alone, and be silent, because he has borne it upon him.
v30 Jod. He will give his cheek to him that smites him: he will be filled full with reproaches.
v31 For the Lord will not reject for ever.
v32 Chaph. For he that has brought down will pity, and that according to the abundance of his mercy.
v33 He has not answered in anger from his heart, though he has brought low the children of a man.
v34 Lamed. To bring down under his feet all the prisoners of the earth,
v35 to turn aside the judgment of a man before the face of the Most High,
v36 to condemn a man unjustly in his judgment, the Lord has not given commandment.
v37 Who has thus spoken, and it has come to pass? the Lord has not commanded it.
v38 Out of the mouth of the Most High there shall not come forth evil and good.
v39 Mem. Why should a living man complain, a man concerning his sin?
v40 Nun. Our way has been searched out and examined, and we will turn to the Lord.
v41 Let us lift up our hearts with our hands to the lofty One in heaven.
v42 We have sinned, we have transgressed; and thou hast not pardoned.
v43 Samech. Thou hast visited us in wrath, and driven us away: thou hast slain, thou hast not pitied.
v44 Thou hast veiled thyself with a cloud because of prayer, that I might be blind,
v45 and be cast off. Ain. Thou hast set us alone in the midst of the nations.
v46 All our enemies have opened their mouth against us.
v47 Fear and wrath are come upon us, suspense and destruction.
v48 Mine eye shall pour down torrents of water, for the destruction of the daughter of my people.
v49 Phe. Mine eye is drowned with tears , and I will not be silent, so that there shall be no rest,
v50 until the Lord look down, and behold from heaven.
v51 Mine eye shall prey upon my soul, because of all the daughters of the city.
v52 Tsade. The fowlers chased me as a sparrow, all mine enemies destroyed my life in a pit without cause,
v53 and laid a stone upon me.
v54 Water flowed over my head: I said, I am cut off.
v55 Koph. I called upon thy name, O Lord, out of the lowest dungeon.
v56 Thou heardest my voice: close not thine ears to my supplication.
v57 Thou drewest nigh to my help: in the day wherein I called upon thee thou saidst to me, Fear not.
v58 Rechs. O Lord, thou hast pleaded the causes of my soul; thou hast redeemed my life.
v59 Thou hast seen, O Lord, my troubles: thou hast judged my cause.
v60 Thou hast seen all their vengeance, thou hast looked on all their devices against me.
v61 Chsen. Thou hast heard their reproach and all their devices against me;
v62 the lips of them that rose up against me, and their plots against me all the day;
v63 their sitting down and their rising up: look thou upon their eyes.
v64 Thou wilt render them a recompence, O Lord, according to the works of their hands.
v65 Thau. Thou wilt give them as a covering, the grief of my heart.
v66 Thou wilt persecute them in anger, and wilt consume them from under the heaven, O Lord.
