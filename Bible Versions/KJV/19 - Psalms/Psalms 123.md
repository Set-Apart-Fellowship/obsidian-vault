# Psalms 123 (King James Version)

[[Bible Versions/KJV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 122|← Psalms 122]] | [[Bible Versions/KJV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/KJV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 124|Psalms 124 →]]

***

v1 A Song of degrees. Unto thee lift I up mine eyes, O thou that dwellest in the heavens.

v2 Behold, as the eyes of servants look unto the hand of their masters, and as the eyes of a maiden unto the hand of her mistress; so our eyes wait upon the LORD our God, until that he have mercy upon us.

v3 Have mercy upon us, O LORD, have mercy upon us: for we are exceedingly filled with contempt.

v4 Our soul is exceedingly filled with the scorning of those that are at ease, and with the contempt of the proud.
