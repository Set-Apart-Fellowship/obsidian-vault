# Psalms 134 (King James Version)

[[Bible Versions/KJV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 133|← Psalms 133]] | [[Bible Versions/KJV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/KJV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 135|Psalms 135 →]]

***

v1 A Song of degrees. Behold, bless ye the LORD, all ye servants of the LORD, which by night stand in the house of the LORD.

v2 Lift up your hands in the sanctuary, and bless the LORD.

v3 The LORD that made heaven and earth bless thee out of Zion.
