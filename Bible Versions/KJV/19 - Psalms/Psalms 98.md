# Psalms 98 (King James Version)

[[Bible Versions/KJV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 97|← Psalms 97]] | [[Bible Versions/KJV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/KJV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 99|Psalms 99 →]]

***

v1 A Psalm. O sing unto the LORD a new song; for he hath done marvellous things: his right hand, and his holy arm, hath gotten him the victory.

v2 The LORD hath made known his salvation: his righteousness hath he openly showed in the sight of the heathen.

v3 He hath remembered his mercy and his truth toward the house of Israel: all the ends of the earth have seen the salvation of our God.

v4 Make a joyful noise unto the LORD, all the earth: make a loud noise, and rejoice, and sing praise.

v5 Sing unto the LORD with the harp; with the harp, and the voice of a psalm.

v6 With trumpets and sound of cornet make a joyful noise before the LORD, the King.

v7 Let the sea roar, and the fulness thereof; the world, and they that dwell therein.

v8 Let the floods clap their hands: let the hills be joyful together

v9 Before the LORD; for he cometh to judge the earth: with righteousness shall he judge the world, and the people with equity.
