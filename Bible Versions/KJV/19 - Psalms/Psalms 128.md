# Psalms 128 (King James Version)

[[Bible Versions/KJV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 127|← Psalms 127]] | [[Bible Versions/KJV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/KJV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 129|Psalms 129 →]]

***

v1 A Song of degrees. Blessed is every one that feareth the LORD; that walketh in his ways.

v2 For thou shalt eat the labour of thine hands: happy shalt thou be, and it shall be well with thee.

v3 Thy wife shall be as a fruitful vine by the sides of thine house: thy children like olive plants round about thy table.

v4 Behold, that thus shall the man be blessed that feareth the LORD.

v5 The LORD shall bless thee out of Zion: and thou shalt see the good of Jerusalem all the days of thy life.

v6 Yea, thou shalt see thy children's children, and peace upon Israel.
