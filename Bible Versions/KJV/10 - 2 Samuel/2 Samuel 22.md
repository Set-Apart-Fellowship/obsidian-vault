# 2 Samuel 22 (King James Version)

[[Bible Versions/KJV/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 21|← 2 Samuel 21]] | [[Bible Versions/KJV/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel]] | [[Bible Versions/KJV/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 23|2 Samuel 23 →]]

***

v1 And David spake unto the LORD the words of this song in the day that the LORD had delivered him out of the hand of all his enemies, and out of the hand of Saul:

v2 And he said, The LORD is my rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer;

v3 The God of my rock; in him will I trust: he is my shield, and the horn of my salvation, my high tower, and my refuge, my saviour; thou savest me from violence.

v4 I will call on the LORD, who is worthy to be praised: so shall I be saved from mine enemies.

v5 When the waves of death compassed me, the floods of ungodly men made me afraid;

v6 The sorrows of hell compassed me about; the snares of death prevented me;

v7 In my distress I called upon the LORD, and cried to my God: and he did hear my voice out of his temple, and my cry did enter into his ears.

v8 Then the earth shook and trembled; the foundations of heaven moved and shook, because he was wroth.

v9 There went up a smoke out of his nostrils, and fire out of his mouth devoured: coals were kindled by it.

v10 He bowed the heavens also, and came down; and darkness was under his feet.

v11 And he rode upon a cherub, and did fly: and he was seen upon the wings of the wind.

v12 And he made darkness pavilions round about him, dark waters, and thick clouds of the skies.

v13 Through the brightness before him were coals of fire kindled.

v14 The LORD thundered from heaven, and the most High uttered his voice.

v15 And he sent out arrows, and scattered them; lightning, and discomfited them.

v16 And the channels of the sea appeared, the foundations of the world were discovered, at the rebuking of the LORD, at the blast of the breath of his nostrils.

v17 He sent from above, he took me; he drew me out of many waters;

v18 He delivered me from my strong enemy, and from them that hated me: for they were too strong for me.

v19 They prevented me in the day of my calamity: but the LORD was my stay.

v20 He brought me forth also into a large place: he delivered me, because he delighted in me.

v21 The LORD rewarded me according to my righteousness: according to the cleanness of my hands hath he recompensed me.

v22 For I have kept the ways of the LORD, and have not wickedly departed from my God.

v23 For all his judgments were before me: and as for his statutes, I did not depart from them.

v24 I was also upright before him, and have kept myself from mine iniquity.

v25 Therefore the LORD hath recompensed me according to my righteousness; according to my cleanness in his eye sight.

v26 With the merciful thou wilt show thyself merciful, and with the upright man thou wilt show thyself upright.

v27 With the pure thou wilt show thyself pure; and with the froward thou wilt show thyself unsavoury.

v28 And the afflicted people thou wilt save: but thine eyes are upon the haughty, that thou mayest bring them down.

v29 For thou art my lamp, O LORD: and the LORD will lighten my darkness.

v30 For by thee I have run through a troop: by my God have I leaped over a wall.

v31 As for God, his way is perfect; the word of the LORD is tried: he is a buckler to all them that trust in him.

v32 For who is God, save the LORD? and who is a rock, save our God?

v33 God is my strength and power: And he maketh my way perfect.

v34 He maketh my feet like hinds' feet: and setteth me upon my high places.

v35 He teacheth my hands to war; so that a bow of steel is broken by mine arms.

v36 Thou hast also given me the shield of thy salvation: and thy gentleness hath made me great.

v37 Thou hast enlarged my steps under me; so that my feet did not slip.

v38 I have pursued mine enemies, and destroyed them; and turned not again until I had consumed them.

v39 And I have consumed them, and wounded them, that they could not arise: yea, they are fallen under my feet.

v40 For thou hast girded me with strength to battle: them that rose up against me hast thou subdued under me.

v41 Thou hast also given me the necks of mine enemies, that I might destroy them that hate me.

v42 They looked, but there was none to save; even unto the LORD, but he answered them not.

v43 Then did I beat them as small as the dust of the earth, I did stamp them as the mire of the street, and did spread them abroad.

v44 Thou also hast delivered me from the strivings of my people, thou hast kept me to be head of the heathen: a people which I knew not shall serve me.

v45 Strangers shall submit themselves unto me: as soon as they hear, they shall be obedient unto me.

v46 Strangers shall fade away, and they shall be afraid out of their close places.

v47 The LORD liveth; and blessed be my rock; and exalted be the God of the rock of my salvation.

v48 It is God that avengeth me, and that bringeth down the people under me,

v49 And that bringeth me forth from mine enemies: thou also hast lifted me up on high above them that rose up against me: thou hast delivered me from the violent man.

v50 Therefore I will give thanks unto thee, O LORD, among the heathen, and I will sing praises unto thy name.

v51 He is the tower of salvation for his king: and showeth mercy to his anointed, unto David, and to his seed for evermore.
