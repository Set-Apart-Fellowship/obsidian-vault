# Mark 5 (Berean Standard Bible)
[[Bible Versions/BSB/41 - Mark/Mark 4|← Mark 4]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/41 - Mark/Mark]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/41 - Mark/Mark 6|Mark 6 →]]
***
v1 On the other side of the sea, they arrived in the region of the Gerasenes.
v2 As soon as Jesus got out of the boat, He was met by a man with an unclean spirit, who was coming from the tombs.
v3 This man had been living in the tombs and could no longer be restrained, even with chains.
v4 Though he was often bound with chains and shackles, he had broken the chains and shattered the shackles. Now there was no one with the strength to subdue him.
v5 Night and day in the tombs and in the mountains he kept crying out and cutting himself with stones.
v6 When the man saw Jesus from a distance, he ran and fell on his knees before Him.
v7 And he shouted in a loud voice, “What do You want with me, Jesus, Son of the Most High God? I beg You before God not to torture me!”
v8 For Jesus had already declared, “Come out of this man, you unclean spirit!”
v9 “What is your name?” Jesus asked. “My name is Legion,” he replied, “for we are many.”
v10 And he begged Jesus repeatedly not to send them out of that region.
v11 There on the nearby hillside a large herd of pigs was feeding.
v12 So the demons begged Jesus, “Send us to the pigs, so that we may enter them.”
v13 He gave them permission, and the unclean spirits came out and went into the pigs, and the herd of about two thousand rushed down the steep bank into the sea and drowned in the water.
v14 Those tending the pigs ran off and reported this in the town and countryside, and the people went out to see what had happened.
v15 When they came to Jesus, they saw the man who had been possessed by the legion of demons sitting there, clothed and in his right mind; and they were afraid.
v16 Those who had seen it described what had happened to the demon-possessed man and also to the pigs.
v17 And the people began to beg Jesus to leave their region.
v18 As He was getting into the boat, the man who had been possessed by the demons begged to go with Him.
v19 But Jesus would not allow him. “Go home to your own people,” He said, “and tell them how much the Lord has done for you, and what mercy He has shown you.”
v20 So the man went away and began to proclaim throughout the Decapolis how much Jesus had done for him. And everyone was amazed.
v21 When Jesus had again crossed by boat to the other side, a large crowd gathered around Him beside the sea.
v22 A synagogue leader named Jairus arrived, and seeing Jesus, he fell at His feet
v23 and pleaded with Him urgently, “My little daughter is near death. Please come and place Your hands on her, so that she will be healed and live.”
v24 So Jesus went with him, and a large crowd followed and pressed around Him.
v25 And a woman was there who had suffered from bleeding for twelve years.
v26 She had borne much agony under the care of many physicians and had spent all she had, but to no avail. Instead, her condition had only grown worse.
v27 When the woman heard about Jesus, she came up through the crowd behind Him and touched His cloak.
v28 For she kept saying, “If only I touch His garments, I will be healed.”
v29 Immediately her bleeding stopped, and she sensed in her body that she was healed of her affliction.
v30 At once Jesus was aware that power had gone out from Him. Turning to the crowd, He asked, “Who touched My garments?”
v31 His disciples answered, “You can see the crowd pressing in on You, and yet You ask, ‘Who touched Me?’”
v32 But He kept looking around to see who had done this.
v33 Then the woman, knowing what had happened to her, came and fell down before Him trembling in fear, and she told Him the whole truth.
v34 “Daughter,” said Jesus, “your faith has healed you. Go in peace and be free of your affliction.”
v35 While He was still speaking, messengers from the house of Jairus arrived and said, “Your daughter is dead; why bother the Teacher anymore?”
v36 But Jesus overheard their conversation and said to Jairus, “Do not be afraid; just believe.”
v37 And He did not allow anyone to accompany Him except Peter, James, and John the brother of James.
v38 When they arrived at the house of the synagogue leader, Jesus saw the commotion and the people weeping and wailing loudly.
v39 He went inside and asked, “Why all this commotion and weeping? The child is not dead, but asleep.”
v40 And they laughed at Him. After He had put them all outside, He took the child’s father and mother and His own companions, and went in to see the child.
v41 Taking her by the hand, Jesus said, “Talitha koum!” which means, “Little girl, I say to you, get up!”
v42 Immediately the girl got up and began to walk around (she was twelve years old). And at once they were utterly astounded.
v43 Then Jesus gave strict orders that no one should know about this, and He told them to give her something to eat.
