# Mark 3 (Berean Standard Bible)
[[Bible Versions/BSB/41 - Mark/Mark 2|← Mark 2]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/41 - Mark/Mark]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/41 - Mark/Mark 4|Mark 4 →]]
***
v1 Once again Jesus entered the synagogue, and a man with a withered hand was there.
v2 In order to accuse Jesus, they were watching to see if He would heal on the Sabbath.
v3 Then Jesus said to the man with the withered hand, “Stand up among us.”
v4 And He asked them, “Which is lawful on the Sabbath: to do good or to do evil, to save life or to destroy it?” But they were silent.
v5 Jesus looked around at them with anger and sorrow at their hardness of heart. Then He said to the man, “Stretch out your hand.” So he stretched it out, and it was restored.
v6 At this, the Pharisees went out and began plotting with the Herodians how they might kill Jesus.
v7 So Jesus withdrew with His disciples to the sea, accompanied by a large crowd from Galilee, Judea,
v8 Jerusalem, Idumea, the region beyond the Jordan, and the vicinity of Tyre and Sidon. The large crowd came to Him when they heard what great things He was doing.
v9 Jesus asked His disciples to have a boat ready for Him so that the crowd would not crush Him.
v10 For He had healed so many that all who had diseases were pressing forward to touch Him.
v11 And when the unclean spirits saw Him, they fell down before Him and cried out, “You are the Son of God!”
v12 But He warned them sternly not to make Him known.
v13 Then Jesus went up on the mountain and called for those He wanted, and they came to Him.
v14 He appointed twelve of them, whom He designated as apostles, to accompany Him, to be sent out to preach,
v15 and to have authority to drive out demons.
v16 These are the twelve He appointed: Simon (whom He named Peter),
v17 James son of Zebedee and his brother John (whom He named Boanerges, meaning “Sons of Thunder”),
v18 Andrew, Philip, Bartholomew, Matthew, Thomas, James son of Alphaeus, Thaddaeus, Simon the Zealot,
v19 and Judas Iscariot, who betrayed Jesus.
v20 Then Jesus went home, and once again a crowd gathered, so that He and His disciples could not even eat.
v21 When His family heard about this, they went out to take custody of Him, saying, “He is out of His mind.”
v22 And the scribes who had come down from Jerusalem were saying, “He is possessed by Beelzebul,” and, “By the prince of the demons He drives out demons.”
v23 So Jesus called them together and began to speak to them in parables: “How can Satan drive out Satan?
v24 If a kingdom is divided against itself, it cannot stand.
v25 If a house is divided against itself, it cannot stand.
v26 And if Satan is divided and rises against himself, he cannot stand; his end has come.
v27 Indeed, no one can enter a strong man’s house to steal his possessions unless he first ties up the strong man. Then he can plunder his house.
v28 Truly I tell you, the sons of men will be forgiven all sins and blasphemies, as many as they utter.
v29 But whoever blasphemes against the Holy Spirit will never be forgiven; he is guilty of eternal sin.”
v30 Jesus made this statement because they were saying, “He has an unclean spirit.”
v31 Then Jesus’ mother and brothers came and stood outside. They sent someone in to summon Him,
v32 and a crowd was sitting around Him. “Look,” He was told, “Your mother and brothers are outside, asking for You.”
v33 But Jesus replied, “Who are My mother and My brothers?”
v34 Looking at those seated in a circle around Him, He said, “Here are My mother and My brothers!
v35 For whoever does the will of God is My brother and sister and mother.”
