# Romans 11 (Berean Standard Bible)
[[Bible Versions/BSB/45 - Romans/Romans 10|← Romans 10]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/45 - Romans/Romans]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/45 - Romans/Romans 12|Romans 12 →]]
***
v1 I ask then, did God reject His people? Certainly not! I am an Israelite myself, a descendant of Abraham, from the tribe of Benjamin.
v2 God did not reject His people, whom He foreknew. Do you not know what the Scripture says about Elijah, how he appealed to God against Israel:
v3 “Lord, they have killed Your prophets and torn down Your altars. I am the only one left, and they are seeking my life as well”?
v4 And what was the divine reply to him? “I have reserved for Myself seven thousand men who have not bowed the knee to Baal.”
v5 In the same way, at the present time there is a remnant chosen by grace.
v6 And if it is by grace, then it is no longer by works. Otherwise, grace would no longer be grace.
v7 What then? What Israel was seeking, it failed to obtain, but the elect did. The others were hardened,
v8 as it is written: “God gave them a spirit of stupor, eyes that could not see, and ears that could not hear, to this very day.”
v9 And David says: “May their table become a snare and a trap, a stumbling block and a retribution to them.
v10 May their eyes be darkened so they cannot see, and their backs be bent forever.”
v11 I ask then, did they stumble so as to fall beyond recovery? Certainly not! However, because of their trespass, salvation has come to the Gentiles to make Israel jealous.
v12 But if their trespass means riches for the world, and their failure means riches for the Gentiles, how much greater riches will their fullness bring!
v13 I am speaking to you Gentiles. Inasmuch as I am the apostle to the Gentiles, I magnify my ministry
v14 in the hope that I may provoke my own people to jealousy and save some of them.
v15 For if their rejection is the reconciliation of the world, what will their acceptance be but life from the dead?
v16 If the first part of the dough is holy, so is the whole batch; if the root is holy, so are the branches.
v17 Now if some branches have been broken off, and you, a wild olive shoot, have been grafted in among the others to share in the nourishment of the olive root,
v18 do not boast over those branches. If you do, remember this: You do not support the root, but the root supports you.
v19 You will say then, “Branches were broken off so that I could be grafted in.”
v20 That is correct: They were broken off because of unbelief, but you stand by faith. Do not be arrogant, but be afraid.
v21 For if God did not spare the natural branches, He will certainly not spare you either.
v22 Take notice, therefore, of the kindness and severity of God: severity to those who fell, but kindness to you, if you continue in His kindness. Otherwise you also will be cut off.
v23 And if they do not persist in unbelief, they will be grafted in, for God is able to graft them in again.
v24 For if you were cut from a wild olive tree, and contrary to nature were grafted into one that is cultivated, how much more readily will these, the natural branches, be grafted into their own olive tree!
v25 I do not want you to be ignorant of this mystery, brothers, so that you will not be conceited: A hardening in part has come to Israel, until the full number of the Gentiles has come in.
v26 And so all Israel will be saved, as it is written: “The Deliverer will come from Zion; He will remove godlessness from Jacob.
v27 And this is My covenant with them when I take away their sins.”
v28 Regarding the gospel, they are enemies on your account; but regarding election, they are loved on account of the patriarchs.
v29 For God’s gifts and His call are irrevocable.
v30 Just as you who formerly disobeyed God have now received mercy through their disobedience,
v31 so they too have now disobeyed, in order that they too may now receive mercy through the mercy shown to you.
v32 For God has consigned everyone to disobedience so that He may have mercy on everyone.
v33 O, the depth of the riches of the wisdom and knowledge of God! How unsearchable are His judgments, and untraceable His ways!
v34 “Who has known the mind of the Lord? Or who has been His counselor?”
v35 “Who has first given to God, that God should repay him?”
v36 For from Him and through Him and to Him are all things. To Him be the glory forever! Amen.
