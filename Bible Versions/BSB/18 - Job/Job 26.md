# Job 26 (Berean Standard Bible)
[[Bible Versions/BSB/18 - Job/Job 25|← Job 25]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/18 - Job/Job 27|Job 27 →]]
***
v1 Then Job answered:
v2 “How you have helped the powerless and saved the arm that is feeble!
v3 How you have counseled the unwise and provided fully sound insight!
v4 To whom have you uttered these words? And whose spirit spoke through you?
v5 The dead tremble—those beneath the waters and those who dwell in them.
v6 Sheol is naked before God, and Abaddon has no covering.
v7 He stretches out the north over empty space; He hangs the earth upon nothing.
v8 He wraps up the waters in His clouds, yet the clouds do not burst under their own weight.
v9 He covers the face of the full moon, spreading over it His cloud.
v10 He has inscribed a horizon on the face of the waters at the boundary between light and darkness.
v11 The foundations of heaven quake, astounded at His rebuke.
v12 By His power He stirred the sea; by His understanding He shattered Rahab.
v13 By His breath the skies were cleared; His hand pierced the fleeing serpent.
v14 Indeed, these are but the fringes of His ways; how faint is the whisper we hear of Him! Who then can understand the thunder of His power?”
