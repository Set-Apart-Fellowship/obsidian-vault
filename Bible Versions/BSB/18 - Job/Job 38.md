# Job 38 (Berean Standard Bible)
[[Bible Versions/BSB/18 - Job/Job 37|← Job 37]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/18 - Job/Job 39|Job 39 →]]
***
v1 Then the LORD answered Job out of the whirlwind and said:
v2 “Who is this who obscures My counsel by words without knowledge?
v3 Now brace yourself like a man; I will question you, and you shall inform Me.
v4 Where were you when I laid the foundations of the earth? Tell Me, if you have understanding.
v5 Who fixed its measurements? Surely you know! Or who stretched a measuring line across it?
v6 On what were its foundations set, or who laid its cornerstone,
v7 while the morning stars sang together and all the sons of God shouted for joy?
v8 Who enclosed the sea behind doors when it burst forth from the womb,
v9 when I made the clouds its garment and thick darkness its blanket,
v10 when I fixed its boundaries and set in place its bars and doors,
v11 and I declared: ‘You may come this far, but no farther; here your proud waves must stop’?
v12 In your days, have you commanded the morning or assigned the dawn its place,
v13 that it might spread to the ends of the earth and shake the wicked out of it?
v14 The earth takes shape like clay under a seal; its hills stand out like the folds of a garment.
v15 Light is withheld from the wicked, and their upraised arm is broken.
v16 Have you journeyed to the vents of the sea or walked in the trenches of the deep?
v17 Have the gates of death been revealed to you? Have you seen the gates of the shadow of death?
v18 Have you surveyed the extent of the earth? Tell Me, if you know all this.
v19 Where is the way to the home of light? Do you know where darkness resides,
v20 so you can lead it back to its border? Do you know the paths to its home?
v21 Surely you know, for you were already born! And the number of your days is great!
v22 Have you entered the storehouses of snow or observed the storehouses of hail,
v23 which I hold in reserve for times of trouble, for the day of war and battle?
v24 In which direction is the lightning dispersed, or the east wind scattered over the earth?
v25 Who cuts a channel for the flood or clears a path for the thunderbolt,
v26 to bring rain on a barren land, on a desert where no man lives,
v27 to satisfy the parched wasteland and make it sprout with tender grass?
v28 Does the rain have a father? Who has begotten the drops of dew?
v29 From whose womb does the ice emerge? Who gives birth to the frost from heaven,
v30 when the waters become hard as stone and the surface of the deep is frozen?
v31 Can you bind the chains of the Pleiades or loosen the belt of Orion?
v32 Can you bring forth the constellations in their seasons or lead out the Bear and her cubs?
v33 Do you know the laws of the heavens? Can you set their dominion over the earth?
v34 Can you command the clouds so that a flood of water covers you?
v35 Can you send the lightning bolts on their way? Do they report to you, ‘Here we are’?
v36 Who has put wisdom in the heart or given understanding to the mind?
v37 Who has the wisdom to count the clouds? Or who can tilt the water jars of the heavens
v38 when the dust hardens into a mass and the clods of earth stick together?
v39 Can you hunt the prey for a lioness or satisfy the hunger of young lions
v40 when they crouch in their dens and lie in wait in the thicket?
v41 Who provides food for the raven when its young cry out to God as they wander about for lack of food?
