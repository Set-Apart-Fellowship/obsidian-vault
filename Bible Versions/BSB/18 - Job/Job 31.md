# Job 31 (Berean Standard Bible)
[[Bible Versions/BSB/18 - Job/Job 30|← Job 30]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/18 - Job/Job 32|Job 32 →]]
***
v1 “I have made a covenant with my eyes. How then could I gaze with desire at a virgin?
v2 For what is the allotment of God from above, or the heritage from the Almighty on high?
v3 Does not disaster come to the unjust and calamity to the workers of iniquity?
v4 Does He not see my ways and count my every step?
v5 If I have walked in falsehood or my foot has rushed to deceit,
v6 let God weigh me with honest scales, that He may know my integrity.
v7 If my steps have turned from the path, if my heart has followed my eyes, or if impurity has stuck to my hands,
v8 then may another eat what I have sown, and may my crops be uprooted.
v9 If my heart has been enticed by my neighbor’s wife, or I have lurked at his door,
v10 then may my own wife grind grain for another, and may other men sleep with her.
v11 For that would be a heinous crime, an iniquity to be judged.
v12 For it is a fire that burns down to Abaddon; it would root out my entire harvest.
v13 If I have rejected the cause of my manservant or maidservant when they made a complaint against me,
v14 what will I do when God rises to judge? How will I answer when called to account?
v15 Did not He who made me in the womb also make them? Did not the same One form us in the womb?
v16 If I have denied the desires of the poor or allowed the widow’s eyes to fail,
v17 if I have eaten my morsel alone, not sharing it with the fatherless—
v18 though from my youth I reared him as would a father, and from my mother’s womb I guided the widow—
v19 if I have seen one perish for lack of clothing, or a needy man without a cloak,
v20 if his heart has not blessed me for warming him with the fleece of my sheep,
v21 if I have lifted up my hand against the fatherless because I saw that I had support in the gate,
v22 then may my arm fall from my shoulder and be torn from its socket.
v23 For calamity from God terrifies me, and His splendor I cannot overpower.
v24 If I have put my trust in gold or called pure gold my security,
v25 if I have rejoiced in my great wealth because my hand had gained so much,
v26 if I have beheld the sun in its radiance or the moon moving in splendor,
v27 so that my heart was secretly enticed and my hand threw a kiss from my mouth,
v28 this would also be an iniquity to be judged, for I would have denied God on high.
v29 If I have rejoiced in my enemy’s ruin, or exulted when evil befell him—
v30 I have not allowed my mouth to sin by asking for his life with a curse—
v31 if the men of my house have not said, ‘Who is there who has not had his fill?’—
v32 but no stranger had to lodge on the street, for my door has been open to the traveler—
v33 if I have covered my transgressions like Adam by hiding my guilt in my heart,
v34 because I greatly feared the crowds and the contempt of the clans terrified me, so that I kept silent and would not go outside—
v35 (Oh, that I had one to hear me! Here is my signature. Let the Almighty answer me; let my accuser compose an indictment.
v36 Surely I would carry it on my shoulder and wear it like a crown.
v37 I would give account of all my steps; I would approach Him like a prince.)—
v38 if my land cries out against me and its furrows weep together,
v39 if I have devoured its produce without payment or broken the spirit of its tenants,
v40 then let briers grow instead of wheat and stinkweed instead of barley.” Thus conclude the words of Job.
