# Isaiah 3 (Berean Standard Bible)
[[Bible Versions/BSB/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 2|← Isaiah 2]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 4|Isaiah 4 →]]
***
v1 For behold, the Lord GOD of Hosts is about to remove from Jerusalem and Judah both supply and support: the whole supply of food and water,
v2 the mighty man and the warrior, the judge and the prophet, the soothsayer and the elder,
v3 the commander of fifty and the dignitary, the counselor, the cunning magician, and the clever enchanter.
v4 “I will make mere lads their leaders, and children will rule over them.”
v5 The people will oppress one another, man against man, neighbor against neighbor; the young will rise up against the old, and the base against the honorable.
v6 A man will seize his brother within his father’s house: “You have a cloak—you be our leader! Take charge of this heap of rubble.”
v7 On that day he will cry aloud: “I am not a healer. I have no food or clothing in my house. Do not make me leader of the people!”
v8 For Jerusalem has stumbled and Judah has fallen because they spoke and acted against the LORD, defying His glorious presence.
v9 The expression on their faces testifies against them, and like Sodom they flaunt their sin; they do not conceal it. Woe to them, for they have brought disaster upon themselves.
v10 Tell the righteous it will be well with them, for they will enjoy the fruit of their labor.
v11 Woe to the wicked; disaster is upon them! For they will be repaid with what their hands have done.
v12 Youths oppress My people, and women rule over them. O My people, your guides mislead you; they turn you from your paths.
v13 The LORD arises to contend; He stands to judge the people.
v14 The LORD brings this charge against the elders and leaders of His people: “You have devoured the vineyard; the plunder of the poor is in your houses.
v15 Why do you crush My people and grind the faces of the poor?” declares the Lord GOD of Hosts.
v16 The LORD also says: “Because the daughters of Zion are haughty—walking with heads held high and wanton eyes, prancing and skipping as they go, jingling the bracelets on their ankles—
v17 the Lord will bring sores on the heads of the daughters of Zion, and the LORD will make their foreheads bare.”
v18 In that day the Lord will take away their finery: their anklets and headbands and crescents;
v19 their pendants, bracelets, and veils;
v20 their headdresses, ankle chains, and sashes; their perfume bottles and charms;
v21 their signet rings and nose rings;
v22 their festive robes, capes, cloaks, and purses;
v23 and their mirrors, linen garments, tiaras, and shawls.
v24 Instead of fragrance there will be a stench; instead of a belt, a rope; instead of styled hair, baldness; instead of fine clothing, sackcloth; instead of beauty, shame.
v25 Your men will fall by the sword, and your warriors in battle.
v26 And the gates of Zion will lament and mourn; destitute, she will sit on the ground.
