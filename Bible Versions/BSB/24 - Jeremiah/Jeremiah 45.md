# Jeremiah 45 (Berean Standard Bible)
[[Bible Versions/BSB/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 44|← Jeremiah 44]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 46|Jeremiah 46 →]]
***
v1 This is the word that Jeremiah the prophet spoke to Baruch son of Neriah when he wrote these words on a scroll at the dictation of Jeremiah in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah:
v2 “This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says to you, Baruch:
v3 You have said, ‘Woe is me because the LORD has added sorrow to my pain! I am worn out with groaning and have found no rest.’”
v4 Thus Jeremiah was to say to Baruch: “This is what the LORD says: Throughout the land I will demolish what I have built and uproot what I have planted.
v5 But as for you, do you seek great things for yourself? Stop seeking! For I will bring disaster on every living creature, declares the LORD, but wherever you go, I will grant your life as a spoil of war.”
