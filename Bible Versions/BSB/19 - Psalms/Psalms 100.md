# Psalms 100 (Berean Standard Bible)
[[Bible Versions/BSB/19 - Psalms/Psalms 99|← Psalms 99]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/19 - Psalms/Psalms 101|Psalms 101 →]]
***
v1 A Psalm of thanksgiving. Make a joyful noise to the LORD, all the earth.
v2 Serve the LORD with gladness; come into His presence with joyful songs.
v3 Know that the LORD is God. It is He who made us, and we are His; we are His people, and the sheep of His pasture.
v4 Enter His gates with thanksgiving and His courts with praise; give thanks to Him and bless His name.
v5 For the LORD is good, and His loving devotion endures forever; His faithfulness continues to all generations.
