# Psalms 127 (Berean Standard Bible)
[[Bible Versions/BSB/19 - Psalms/Psalms 126|← Psalms 126]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/19 - Psalms/Psalms 128|Psalms 128 →]]
***
v1 A song of ascents. Of Solomon. Unless the LORD builds the house, its builders labor in vain; unless the LORD protects the city, its watchmen stand guard in vain.
v2 In vain you rise early and stay up late, toiling for bread to eat—for He gives sleep to His beloved.
v3 Children are indeed a heritage from the LORD, and the fruit of the womb is His reward.
v4 Like arrows in the hand of a warrior, so are children born in one’s youth.
v5 Blessed is the man whose quiver is full of them. He will not be put to shame when he confronts the enemies at the gate.
