# Psalms 142 (Berean Standard Bible)
[[Bible Versions/BSB/19 - Psalms/Psalms 141|← Psalms 141]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/19 - Psalms/Psalms 143|Psalms 143 →]]
***
v1 A Maskil of David, when he was in the cave. A prayer. I cry aloud to the LORD; I lift my voice to the LORD for mercy.
v2 I pour out my complaint before Him; I reveal my trouble to Him.
v3 Although my spirit grows faint within me, You know my way. Along the path I travel they have hidden a snare for me.
v4 Look to my right and see; no one attends to me. There is no refuge for me; no one cares for my soul.
v5 I cry to You, O LORD: “You are my refuge, my portion in the land of the living.”
v6 Listen to my cry, for I am brought quite low. Rescue me from my pursuers, for they are too strong for me.
v7 Free my soul from prison, that I may praise Your name. The righteous will gather around me because of Your goodness to me.
