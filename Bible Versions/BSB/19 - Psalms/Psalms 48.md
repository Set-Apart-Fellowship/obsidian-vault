# Psalms 48 (Berean Standard Bible)
[[Bible Versions/BSB/19 - Psalms/Psalms 47|← Psalms 47]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/19 - Psalms/Psalms 49|Psalms 49 →]]
***
v1 A song. A Psalm of the sons of Korah. Great is the LORD, and greatly to be praised in the city of our God, His holy mountain.
v2 Beautiful in loftiness, the joy of all the earth, like the peaks of Zaphon is Mount Zion, the city of the great King.
v3 God is in her citadels; He has shown Himself to be a fortress.
v4 For behold, the kings assembled; they all advanced together.
v5 They saw and were astounded; they fled in terror.
v6 Trembling seized them there, anguish like a woman in labor.
v7 With a wind from the east You wrecked the ships of Tarshish.
v8 As we have heard, so we have seen in the city of the LORD of Hosts, in the city of our God: God will establish her forever. Selah
v9 Within Your temple, O God, we contemplate Your loving devotion.
v10 Your name, O God, like Your praise, reaches to the ends of the earth; Your right hand is full of righteousness.
v11 Mount Zion is glad, the daughters of Judah rejoice, on account of Your judgments.
v12 March around Zion, encircle her, count her towers,
v13 consider her ramparts, tour her citadels, that you may tell the next generation.
v14 For this God is our God forever and ever; He will be our guide even till death.
