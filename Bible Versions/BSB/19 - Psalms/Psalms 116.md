# Psalms 116 (Berean Standard Bible)
[[Bible Versions/BSB/19 - Psalms/Psalms 115|← Psalms 115]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/19 - Psalms/Psalms 117|Psalms 117 →]]
***
v1 I love the LORD, for He has heard my voice—my appeal for mercy.
v2 Because He has inclined His ear to me, I will call on Him as long as I live.
v3 The ropes of death entangled me; the anguish of Sheol overcame me; I was confronted by trouble and sorrow.
v4 Then I called on the name of the LORD: “O LORD, deliver my soul!”
v5 The LORD is gracious and righteous; our God is full of compassion.
v6 The LORD preserves the simplehearted; I was helpless, and He saved me.
v7 Return to your rest, O my soul, for the LORD has been good to you.
v8 For You have delivered my soul from death, my eyes from tears, my feet from stumbling.
v9 I will walk before the LORD in the land of the living.
v10 I believed, therefore I said, “I am greatly afflicted.”
v11 In my alarm I said, “All men are liars!”
v12 How can I repay the LORD for all His goodness to me?
v13 I will lift the cup of salvation and call on the name of the LORD.
v14 I will fulfill my vows to the LORD in the presence of all His people.
v15 Precious in the sight of the LORD is the death of His saints.
v16 Truly, O LORD, I am Your servant; I am Your servant, the son of Your maidservant; You have broken my bonds.
v17 I will offer to You a sacrifice of thanksgiving and call on the name of the LORD.
v18 I will fulfill my vows to the LORD in the presence of all His people,
v19 in the courts of the LORD’s house, in your midst, O Jerusalem. Hallelujah!
