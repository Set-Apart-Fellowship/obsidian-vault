# Psalms 101 (Berean Standard Bible)
[[Bible Versions/BSB/19 - Psalms/Psalms 100|← Psalms 100]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/19 - Psalms/Psalms 102|Psalms 102 →]]
***
v1 A Psalm of David. I will sing of Your loving devotion and justice; to You, O LORD, I will sing praises.
v2 I will ponder the way that is blameless—when will You come to me? I will walk in my house with integrity of heart.
v3 I will set no worthless thing before my eyes. I hate the work of those who fall away; it shall not cling to me.
v4 A perverse heart shall depart from me; I will know nothing of evil.
v5 Whoever slanders his neighbor in secret, I will put to silence; the one with haughty eyes and a proud heart, I will not endure.
v6 My eyes favor the faithful of the land, that they may dwell with me; he who walks in the way of integrity shall minister to me.
v7 No one who practices deceit shall dwell in my house; no one who tells lies shall stand in my presence.
v8 Every morning I will remove all the wicked of the land, that I may cut off every evildoer from the city of the LORD.
