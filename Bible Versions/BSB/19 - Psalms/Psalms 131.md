# Psalms 131 (Berean Standard Bible)
[[Bible Versions/BSB/19 - Psalms/Psalms 130|← Psalms 130]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/19 - Psalms/Psalms 132|Psalms 132 →]]
***
v1 A song of ascents. Of David. My heart is not proud, O LORD, my eyes are not haughty. I do not aspire to great things or matters too lofty for me.
v2 Surely I have stilled and quieted my soul; like a weaned child with his mother, like a weaned child is my soul within me.
v3 O Israel, put your hope in the LORD, both now and forevermore.
