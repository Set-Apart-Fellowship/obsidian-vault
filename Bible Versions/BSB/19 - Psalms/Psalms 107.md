# Psalms 107 (Berean Standard Bible)
[[Bible Versions/BSB/19 - Psalms/Psalms 106|← Psalms 106]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/19 - Psalms/Psalms 108|Psalms 108 →]]
***
v1 Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good; His loving devotion endures forever.
v2 Let the redeemed of the LORD say so, whom He has redeemed from the hand of the enemy
v3 and gathered from the lands, from east and west, from north and south.
v4 Some wandered in desert wastelands, finding no path to a city in which to dwell.
v5 They were hungry and thirsty; their soul fainted within them.
v6 Then they cried out to the LORD in their trouble, and He delivered them from their distress.
v7 He led them on a straight path to reach a city where they could live.
v8 Let them give thanks to the LORD for His loving devotion and His wonders to the sons of men.
v9 For He satisfies the thirsty and fills the hungry with good things.
v10 Some sat in darkness and in the shadow of death, prisoners in affliction and chains,
v11 because they rebelled against the words of God and despised the counsel of the Most High.
v12 He humbled their hearts with hard labor; they stumbled, and there was no one to help.
v13 Then they cried out to the LORD in their trouble, and He saved them from their distress.
v14 He brought them out of darkness and the shadow of death and broke away their chains.
v15 Let them give thanks to the LORD for His loving devotion and His wonders to the sons of men.
v16 For He has broken down the gates of bronze and cut through the bars of iron.
v17 Fools, in their rebellious ways, and through their iniquities, suffered affliction.
v18 They loathed all food and drew near to the gates of death.
v19 Then they cried out to the LORD in their trouble, and He saved them from their distress.
v20 He sent forth His word and healed them; He rescued them from the Pit.
v21 Let them give thanks to the LORD for His loving devotion and His wonders to the sons of men.
v22 Let them offer sacrifices of thanksgiving and declare His works with rejoicing.
v23 Others went out to sea in ships, conducting trade on the mighty waters.
v24 They saw the works of the LORD, and His wonders in the deep.
v25 For He spoke and raised a tempest that lifted the waves of the sea.
v26 They mounted up to the heavens, then sunk to the depths; their courage melted in their anguish.
v27 They reeled and staggered like drunkards, and all their skill was useless.
v28 Then they cried out to the LORD in their trouble, and He brought them out of their distress.
v29 He calmed the storm to a whisper, and the waves of the sea were hushed.
v30 They rejoiced in the silence, and He guided them to the harbor they desired.
v31 Let them give thanks to the LORD for His loving devotion and His wonders to the sons of men.
v32 Let them exalt Him in the assembly of the people and praise Him in the council of the elders.
v33 He turns rivers into deserts, springs of water into thirsty ground,
v34 and fruitful land into fields of salt, because of the wickedness of its dwellers.
v35 He turns a desert into pools of water and a dry land into flowing springs.
v36 He causes the hungry to settle there, that they may establish a city in which to dwell.
v37 They sow fields and plant vineyards that yield a fruitful harvest.
v38 He blesses them, and they multiply greatly; He does not let their herds diminish.
v39 When they are decreased and humbled by oppression, evil, and sorrow,
v40 He pours out contempt on the nobles and makes them wander in a trackless wasteland.
v41 But He lifts the needy from affliction and increases their families like flocks.
v42 The upright see and rejoice, and all iniquity shuts its mouth.
v43 Let him who is wise pay heed to these things and consider the loving devotion of the LORD.
