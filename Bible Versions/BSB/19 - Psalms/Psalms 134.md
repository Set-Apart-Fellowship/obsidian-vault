# Psalms 134 (Berean Standard Bible)

***
v1 A song of ascents. Come, bless the LORD, all you servants of the LORD who serve by night in the house of the LORD!
v2 Lift up your hands to the sanctuary and bless the LORD!
v3 May the LORD, the Maker of heaven and earth, bless you from Zion.
