# Genesis 5 (Berean Standard Bible)
[[Bible Versions/BSB/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4|← Genesis 4]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/01 - Genesis/Genesis]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6|Genesis 6 →]]
***
v1 This is the book of the generations of Adam. In the day that God created man, He made him in His own likeness.
v2 Male and female He created them, and He blessed them. And in the day they were created, He called them “man.”
v3 When Adam was 130 years old, he had a son in his own likeness, after his own image; and he named him Seth.
v4 And after he had become the father of Seth, Adam lived 800 years and had other sons and daughters.
v5 So Adam lived a total of 930 years, and then he died.
v6 When Seth was 105 years old, he became the father of Enosh.
v7 And after he had become the father of Enosh, Seth lived 807 years and had other sons and daughters.
v8 So Seth lived a total of 912 years, and then he died.
v9 When Enosh was 90 years old, he became the father of Kenan.
v10 And after he had become the father of Kenan, Enosh lived 815 years and had other sons and daughters.
v11 So Enosh lived a total of 905 years, and then he died.
v12 When Kenan was 70 years old, he became the father of Mahalalel.
v13 And after he had become the father of Mahalalel, Kenan lived 840 years and had other sons and daughters.
v14 So Kenan lived a total of 910 years, and then he died.
v15 When Mahalalel was 65 years old, he became the father of Jared.
v16 And after he had become the father of Jared, Mahalalel lived 830 years and had other sons and daughters.
v17 So Mahalalel lived a total of 895 years, and then he died.
v18 When Jared was 162 years old, he became the father of Enoch.
v19 And after he had become the father of Enoch, Jared lived 800 years and had other sons and daughters.
v20 So Jared lived a total of 962 years, and then he died.
v21 When Enoch was 65 years old, he became the father of Methuselah.
v22 And after he had become the father of Methuselah, Enoch walked with God 300 years and had other sons and daughters.
v23 So Enoch lived a total of 365 years.
v24 Enoch walked with God, and then he was no more, because God had taken him away.
v25 When Methuselah was 187 years old, he became the father of Lamech.
v26 And after he had become the father of Lamech, Methuselah lived 782 years and had other sons and daughters.
v27 So Methuselah lived a total of 969 years, and then he died.
v28 When Lamech was 182 years old, he had a son.
v29 And he named him Noah, saying, “May this one comfort us in the labor and toil of our hands caused by the ground that the LORD has cursed.”
v30 And after he had become the father of Noah, Lamech lived 595 years and had other sons and daughters.
v31 So Lamech lived a total of 777 years, and then he died.
v32 After Noah was 500 years old, he became the father of Shem, Ham, and Japheth.
