# Numbers 17 (Berean Standard Bible)
[[Bible Versions/BSB/04 - Numbers/Numbers 16|← Numbers 16]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/04 - Numbers/Numbers]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18|Numbers 18 →]]
***
v1 And the LORD said to Moses,
v2 “Speak to the Israelites and take from them twelve staffs, one from the leader of each tribe. Write each man’s name on his staff,
v3 and write Aaron’s name on the staff of Levi, because there must be one staff for the head of each tribe.
v4 Place the staffs in the Tent of Meeting in front of the Testimony, where I meet with you.
v5 The staff belonging to the man I choose will sprout, and I will rid Myself of the constant grumbling of the Israelites against you.”
v6 So Moses spoke to the Israelites, and each of their leaders gave him a staff—one for each of the leaders of their tribes, twelve staffs in all. And Aaron’s staff was among them.
v7 Then Moses placed the staffs before the LORD in the Tent of the Testimony.
v8 The next day Moses entered the Tent of the Testimony and saw that Aaron’s staff, representing the house of Levi, had sprouted, put forth buds, blossomed, and produced almonds.
v9 Then Moses brought out all the staffs from the LORD’s presence to all the Israelites. They saw them, and each man took his own staff.
v10 The LORD said to Moses, “Put Aaron’s staff back in front of the Testimony, to be kept as a sign for the rebellious, so that you may put an end to their grumbling against Me, lest they die.”
v11 So Moses did as the LORD had commanded him.
v12 Then the Israelites declared to Moses, “Look, we are perishing! We are lost; we are all lost!
v13 Anyone who comes near the tabernacle of the LORD will die. Are we all going to perish?”
