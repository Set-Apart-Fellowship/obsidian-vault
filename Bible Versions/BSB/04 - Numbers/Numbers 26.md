# Numbers 26 (Berean Standard Bible)
[[Bible Versions/BSB/04 - Numbers/Numbers 25|← Numbers 25]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/04 - Numbers/Numbers]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/04 - Numbers/Numbers 27|Numbers 27 →]]
***
v1 After the plague had ended, the LORD said to Moses and Eleazar son of Aaron the priest,
v2 “Take a census of the whole congregation of Israel by the houses of their fathers—all those twenty years of age or older who can serve in the army of Israel.”
v3 So on the plains of Moab by the Jordan, across from Jericho, Moses and Eleazar the priest issued the instruction,
v4 “Take a census of the men twenty years of age or older, as the LORD has commanded Moses.” And these were the Israelites who came out of the land of Egypt:
v5 Reuben was the firstborn of Israel. These were the descendants of Reuben: The Hanochite clan from Hanoch, the Palluite clan from Pallu,
v6 the Hezronite clan from Hezron, and the Carmite clan from Carmi.
v7 These were the clans of Reuben, and their registration numbered 43,730.
v8 Now the son of Pallu was Eliab,
v9 and the sons of Eliab were Nemuel, Dathan, and Abiram. It was Dathan and Abiram, chosen by the congregation, who rebelled against Moses and Aaron with the followers of Korah who rebelled against the LORD.
v10 And the earth opened its mouth and swallowed them along with Korah, whose followers died when the fire consumed 250 men. They serve as a warning sign.
v11 However, the line of Korah did not die out.
v12 These were the descendants of Simeon by their clans: The Nemuelite clan from Nemuel, the Jaminite clan from Jamin, the Jachinite clan from Jachin,
v13 the Zerahite clan from Zerah, and the Shaulite clan from Shaul.
v14 These were the clans of Simeon, and there were 22,200 men.
v15 These were the descendants of Gad by their clans: The Zephonite clan from Zephon, the Haggite clan from Haggi, the Shunite clan from Shuni,
v16 the Oznite clan from Ozni, the Erite clan from Eri,
v17 the Arodite clan from Arod, and the Arelite clan from Areli.
v18 These were the clans of Gad, and their registration numbered 40,500.
v19 The sons of Judah were Er and Onan, but they died in the land of Canaan.
v20 These were the descendants of Judah by their clans: The Shelanite clan from Shelah, the Perezite clan from Perez, and the Zerahite clan from Zerah.
v21 And these were the descendants of Perez: the Hezronite clan from Hezron and the Hamulite clan from Hamul.
v22 These were the clans of Judah, and their registration numbered 76,500.
v23 These were the descendants of Issachar by their clans: The Tolaite clan from Tola, the Punite clan from Puvah,
v24 the Jashubite clan from Jashub, and the Shimronite clan from Shimron.
v25 These were the clans of Issachar, and their registration numbered 64,300.
v26 These were the descendants of Zebulun by their clans: The Seredite clan from Sered, the Elonite clan from Elon, and the Jahleelite clan from Jahleel.
v27 These were the clans of Zebulun, and their registration numbered 60,500.
v28 The descendants of Joseph included the clans of Manasseh and Ephraim.
v29 These were the descendants of Manasseh: The Machirite clan from Machir, the father of Gilead, and the Gileadite clan from Gilead.
v30 These were the descendants of Gilead: the Iezerite clan from Iezer, the Helekite clan from Helek,
v31 the Asrielite clan from Asriel, the Shechemite clan from Shechem,
v32 the Shemidaite clan from Shemida, and the Hepherite clan from Hepher.
v33 Now Zelophehad son of Hepher had no sons but only daughters. The names of his daughters were Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah.
v34 These were the clans of Manasseh, and their registration numbered 52,700.
v35 These were the descendants of Ephraim by their clans: The Shuthelahite clan from Shuthelah, the Becherite clan from Becher, and the Tahanite clan from Tahan.
v36 And the descendants of Shuthelah were the Eranite clan from Eran.
v37 These were the clans of Ephraim, and their registration numbered 32,500. These clans were the descendants of Joseph.
v38 These were the descendants of Benjamin by their clans: The Belaite clan from Bela, the Ashbelite clan from Ashbel, the Ahiramite clan from Ahiram,
v39 the Shuphamite clan from Shupham, and the Huphamite clan from Hupham.
v40 And the descendants of Bela from Ard and Naaman were the Ardite clan from Ard and the Naamite clan from Naaman.
v41 These were the clans of Benjamin, and their registration numbered 45,600.
v42 These were the descendants of Dan by their clans: The Shuhamite clan from Shuham. These were the clans of Dan.
v43 All of them were Shuhamite clans, and their registration numbered 64,400.
v44 These were the descendants of Asher by their clans: The Imnite clan from Imnah, the Ishvite clan from Ishvi, and the Beriite clan from Beriah.
v45 And these were the descendants of Beriah: the Heberite clan from Heber and the Malchielite clan from Malchiel.
v46 And the name of Asher’s daughter was Serah.
v47 These were the clans of Asher, and their registration numbered 53,400.
v48 These were the descendants of Naphtali by their clans: The Jahzeelite clan from Jahzeel, the Gunite clan from Guni,
v49 the Jezerite clan from Jezer, and the Shillemite clan from Shillem.
v50 These were the clans of Naphtali, and their registration numbered 45,400.
v51 These men of Israel numbered 601,730 in all.
v52 Then the LORD said to Moses,
v53 “The land is to be divided among the tribes as an inheritance, according to the number of names.
v54 Increase the inheritance for a large tribe and decrease it for a small one; each tribe is to receive its inheritance according to the number of those registered.
v55 Indeed, the land must be divided by lot; they shall receive their inheritance according to the names of the tribes of their fathers.
v56 Each inheritance is to be divided by lot among the larger and smaller tribes.”
v57 Now these were the Levites numbered by their clans: The Gershonite clan from Gershon, the Kohathite clan from Kohath, and the Merarite clan from Merari.
v58 These were the families of the Levites: The Libnite clan, the Hebronite clan, the Mahlite clan, the Mushite clan, and the Korahite clan. Now Kohath was the father of Amram,
v59 and Amram’s wife was named Jochebed. She was also a daughter of Levi, born to Levi in Egypt. To Amram she bore Aaron, Moses, and their sister Miriam.
v60 Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar were born to Aaron,
v61 but Nadab and Abihu died when they offered unauthorized fire before the LORD.
v62 The registration of the Levites totaled 23,000, every male a month old or more; they were not numbered among the other Israelites, because no inheritance was given to them among the Israelites.
v63 These were the ones numbered by Moses and Eleazar the priest when they counted the Israelites on the plains of Moab by the Jordan, across from Jericho.
v64 Among all these, however, there was not one who had been numbered by Moses and Aaron the priest when they counted the Israelites in the Wilderness of Sinai.
v65 For the LORD had told them that they would surely die in the wilderness. Not one was left except Caleb son of Jephunneh and Joshua son of Nun.
