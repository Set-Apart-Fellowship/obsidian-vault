# Numbers 33 (Berean Standard Bible)
[[Bible Versions/BSB/04 - Numbers/Numbers 32|← Numbers 32]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/04 - Numbers/Numbers]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/04 - Numbers/Numbers 34|Numbers 34 →]]
***
v1 These are the journeys of the Israelites when they came out of the land of Egypt by their divisions under the leadership of Moses and Aaron.
v2 At the LORD’s command, Moses recorded the stages of their journey. These are the stages listed by their starting points:
v3 On the fifteenth day of the first month, on the day after the Passover, the Israelites set out from Rameses. They marched out defiantly in full view of all the Egyptians,
v4 who were burying all their firstborn, whom the LORD had struck down among them; for the LORD had executed judgment against their gods.
v5 The Israelites set out from Rameses and camped at Succoth.
v6 They set out from Succoth and camped at Etham, on the edge of the wilderness.
v7 They set out from Etham and turned back to Pi-hahiroth, opposite Baal-zephon, and they camped near Migdol.
v8 They set out from Pi-hahiroth and crossed through the sea, into the wilderness, and they journeyed three days into the Wilderness of Etham and camped at Marah.
v9 They set out from Marah and came to Elim, where there were twelve springs and seventy palm trees, and they camped there.
v10 They set out from Elim and camped by the Red Sea.
v11 They set out from the Red Sea and camped in the Desert of Sin.
v12 They set out from the Desert of Sin and camped at Dophkah.
v13 They set out from Dophkah and camped at Alush.
v14 They set out from Alush and camped at Rephidim, where there was no water for the people to drink.
v15 They set out from Rephidim and camped in the Wilderness of Sinai.
v16 They set out from the Wilderness of Sinai and camped at Kibroth-hattaavah.
v17 They set out from Kibroth-hattaavah and camped at Hazeroth.
v18 They set out from Hazeroth and camped at Rithmah.
v19 They set out from Rithmah and camped at Rimmon-perez.
v20 They set out from Rimmon-perez and camped at Libnah.
v21 They set out from Libnah and camped at Rissah.
v22 They set out from Rissah and camped at Kehelathah.
v23 They set out from Kehelathah and camped at Mount Shepher.
v24 They set out from Mount Shepher and camped at Haradah.
v25 They set out from Haradah and camped at Makheloth.
v26 They set out from Makheloth and camped at Tahath.
v27 They set out from Tahath and camped at Terah.
v28 They set out from Terah and camped at Mithkah.
v29 They set out from Mithkah and camped at Hashmonah.
v30 They set out from Hashmonah and camped at Moseroth.
v31 They set out from Moseroth and camped at Bene-jaakan.
v32 They set out from Bene-jaakan and camped at Hor-haggidgad.
v33 They set out from Hor-haggidgad and camped at Jotbathah.
v34 They set out from Jotbathah and camped at Abronah.
v35 They set out from Abronah and camped at Ezion-geber.
v36 They set out from Ezion-geber and camped at Kadesh in the Wilderness of Zin.
v37 They set out from Kadesh and camped at Mount Hor, on the outskirts of the land of Edom.
v38 At the LORD’s command, Aaron the priest climbed Mount Hor and died there on the first day of the fifth month, in the fortieth year after the Israelites had come out of the land of Egypt.
v39 Aaron was 123 years old when he died on Mount Hor.
v40 Now the Canaanite king of Arad, who lived in the Negev in the land of Canaan, heard that the Israelites were coming.
v41 And the Israelites set out from Mount Hor and camped at Zalmonah.
v42 They set out from Zalmonah and camped at Punon.
v43 They set out from Punon and camped at Oboth.
v44 They set out from Oboth and camped at Iye-abarim on the border of Moab.
v45 They set out from Iyim and camped at Dibon-gad.
v46 They set out from Dibon-gad and camped at Almon-diblathaim.
v47 They set out from Almon-diblathaim and camped in the mountains of Abarim facing Nebo.
v48 They set out from the mountains of Abarim and camped on the plains of Moab by the Jordan across from Jericho.
v49 And there on the plains of Moab they camped by the Jordan, from Beth-jeshimoth to Abel-shittim.
v50 On the plains of Moab by the Jordan across from Jericho, the LORD said to Moses,
v51 “Speak to the Israelites and tell them: When you cross the Jordan into the land of Canaan,
v52 you must drive out before you all the inhabitants of the land, destroy all their carved images and cast idols, and demolish all their high places.
v53 You are to take possession of the land and settle in it, for I have given you the land to possess.
v54 And you are to divide the land by lot according to your clans. Give a larger inheritance to a larger clan and a smaller inheritance to a smaller one. Whatever falls to each one by lot will be his. You will receive an inheritance according to the tribes of your fathers.
v55 But if you do not drive out the inhabitants of the land before you, those you allow to remain will become barbs in your eyes and thorns in your sides; they will harass you in the land where you settle.
v56 And then I will do to you what I had planned to do to them.”
