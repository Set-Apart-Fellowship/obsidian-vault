# Numbers 29 (Berean Standard Bible)
[[Bible Versions/BSB/04 - Numbers/Numbers 28|← Numbers 28]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/04 - Numbers/Numbers]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/04 - Numbers/Numbers 30|Numbers 30 →]]
***
v1 “On the first day of the seventh month, you are to hold a sacred assembly, and you must not do any regular work. This will be a day for you to sound the trumpets.
v2 As a pleasing aroma to the LORD, you are to present a burnt offering of one young bull, one ram, and seven male lambs a year old, all unblemished,
v3 together with their grain offerings of fine flour mixed with oil—three-tenths of an ephah with the bull, two-tenths of an ephah with the ram,
v4 and a tenth of an ephah with each of the seven male lambs.
v5 Include one male goat as a sin offering to make atonement for you.
v6 These are in addition to the monthly and daily burnt offerings with their prescribed grain offerings and drink offerings. They are a pleasing aroma, a food offering to the LORD.
v7 On the tenth day of this seventh month, you are to hold a sacred assembly, and you shall humble yourselves; you must not do any work.
v8 Present as a pleasing aroma to the LORD a burnt offering of one young bull, one ram, and seven male lambs a year old, all unblemished,
v9 together with their grain offerings of fine flour mixed with oil—three-tenths of an ephah with the bull, two-tenths of an ephah with the ram,
v10 and a tenth of an ephah with each of the seven lambs.
v11 Include one male goat for a sin offering, in addition to the sin offering of atonement and the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offerings.
v12 On the fifteenth day of the seventh month, you are to hold a sacred assembly; you must not do any regular work, and you shall observe a feast to the LORD for seven days.
v13 As a pleasing aroma to the LORD, you are to present a food offering, a burnt offering of thirteen young bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old, all unblemished,
v14 along with the grain offering of three-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil with each of the thirteen bulls, two-tenths of an ephah with each of the two rams,
v15 and a tenth of an ephah with each of the fourteen lambs.
v16 Include one male goat as a sin offering, in addition to the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offering.
v17 On the second day you are to present twelve young bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old, all unblemished,
v18 along with the grain and drink offerings for the bulls, rams, and lambs, according to the number prescribed.
v19 Include one male goat as a sin offering, in addition to the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offering.
v20 On the third day you are to present eleven bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old, all unblemished,
v21 along with the grain and drink offerings for the bulls, rams, and lambs, according to the number prescribed.
v22 Include one male goat as a sin offering, in addition to the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offering.
v23 On the fourth day you are to present ten bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old, all unblemished,
v24 along with the grain and drink offerings for the bulls, rams, and lambs, according to the number prescribed.
v25 Include one male goat as a sin offering, in addition to the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offering.
v26 On the fifth day you are to present nine bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old, all unblemished,
v27 along with the grain and drink offerings for the bulls, rams, and lambs, according to the number prescribed.
v28 Include one male goat as a sin offering, in addition to the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offering.
v29 On the sixth day you are to present eight bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old, all unblemished,
v30 along with the grain and drink offerings for the bulls, rams, and lambs, according to the number prescribed.
v31 Include one male goat as a sin offering, in addition to the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offering.
v32 On the seventh day you are to present seven bulls, two rams, and fourteen male lambs a year old, all unblemished,
v33 along with the grain and drink offerings for the bulls, rams, and lambs, according to the number prescribed.
v34 Include one male goat as a sin offering, in addition to the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offering.
v35 On the eighth day you are to hold a solemn assembly; you must not do any regular work.
v36 As a pleasing aroma to the LORD, you are to present a food offering, a burnt offering of one bull, one ram, and seven male lambs a year old, all unblemished,
v37 along with the grain and drink offerings for the bulls, rams, and lambs, according to the number prescribed.
v38 Include one male goat as a sin offering, in addition to the regular burnt offering with its grain offering and drink offering.
v39 You are to present these offerings to the LORD at your appointed times, in addition to your vow and freewill offerings, whether burnt offerings, grain offerings, drink offerings, or peace offerings.”
v40 So Moses spoke all this to the Israelites just as the LORD had commanded him.
