# Lamentations 5 (Berean Standard Bible)
[[Bible Versions/BSB/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 4|← Lamentations 4]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations]]
***
v1 Remember, O LORD, what has happened to us. Look and see our disgrace!
v2 Our inheritance has been turned over to strangers, our houses to foreigners.
v3 We have become fatherless orphans; our mothers are widows.
v4 We must buy the water we drink; our wood comes at a price.
v5 We are closely pursued; we are weary and find no rest.
v6 We submitted to Egypt and Assyria to get enough bread.
v7 Our fathers sinned and are no more, but we bear their punishment.
v8 Slaves rule over us; there is no one to deliver us from their hands.
v9 We get our bread at the risk of our lives because of the sword in the wilderness.
v10 Our skin is as hot as an oven with fever from our hunger.
v11 Women have been ravished in Zion, virgins in the cities of Judah.
v12 Princes have been hung up by their hands; elders receive no respect.
v13 Young men toil at millstones; boys stagger under loads of wood.
v14 The elders have left the city gate; the young men have stopped their music.
v15 Joy has left our hearts; our dancing has turned to mourning.
v16 The crown has fallen from our head. Woe to us, for we have sinned!
v17 Because of this, our hearts are faint; because of these, our eyes grow dim—
v18 because of Mount Zion, which lies desolate, patrolled by foxes.
v19 You, O LORD, reign forever; Your throne endures from generation to generation.
v20 Why have You forgotten us forever? Why have You forsaken us for so long?
v21 Restore us to Yourself, O LORD, so we may return; renew our days as of old,
v22 unless You have utterly rejected us and remain angry with us beyond measure.
