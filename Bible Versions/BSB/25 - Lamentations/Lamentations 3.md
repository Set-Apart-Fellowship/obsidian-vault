# Lamentations 3 (Berean Standard Bible)
[[Bible Versions/BSB/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 2|← Lamentations 2]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 4|Lamentations 4 →]]
***
v1 I am the man who has seen affliction under the rod of God’s wrath.
v2 He has driven me away and made me walk in darkness instead of light.
v3 Indeed, He keeps turning His hand against me all day long.
v4 He has worn away my flesh and skin; He has shattered my bones.
v5 He has besieged me and surrounded me with bitterness and hardship.
v6 He has made me dwell in darkness like those dead for ages.
v7 He has walled me in so I cannot escape; He has weighed me down with chains.
v8 Even when I cry out and plead for help, He shuts out my prayer.
v9 He has barred my ways with cut stones; He has made my paths crooked.
v10 He is a bear lying in wait, a lion hiding in ambush.
v11 He forced me off my path and tore me to pieces; He left me without help.
v12 He bent His bow and set me as the target for His arrow.
v13 He pierced my kidneys with His arrows.
v14 I am a laughingstock to all my people; they mock me in song all day long.
v15 He has filled me with bitterness; He has intoxicated me with wormwood.
v16 He has ground my teeth with gravel and trampled me in the dust.
v17 My soul has been deprived of peace; I have forgotten what prosperity is.
v18 So I say, “My strength has perished, along with my hope from the LORD.”
v19 Remember my affliction and wandering, the wormwood and the gall.
v20 Surely my soul remembers and is humbled within me.
v21 Yet I call this to mind, and therefore I have hope:
v22 Because of the loving devotion of the LORD we are not consumed, for His mercies never fail.
v23 They are new every morning; great is Your faithfulness!
v24 “The LORD is my portion,” says my soul, “therefore I will hope in Him.”
v25 The LORD is good to those who wait for Him, to the soul who seeks Him.
v26 It is good to wait quietly for the salvation of the LORD.
v27 It is good for a man to bear the yoke while he is still young.
v28 Let him sit alone in silence, for the LORD has laid it upon him.
v29 Let him bury his face in the dust—perhaps there is still hope.
v30 Let him offer his cheek to the one who would strike him; let him be filled with reproach.
v31 For the Lord will not cast us off forever.
v32 Even if He causes grief, He will show compassion according to His abundant loving devotion.
v33 For He does not willingly afflict or grieve the sons of men.
v34 To crush underfoot all the prisoners of the land,
v35 to deny a man justice before the Most High,
v36 to subvert a man in his lawsuit—of these the Lord does not approve.
v37 Who has spoken and it came to pass, unless the Lord has ordained it?
v38 Do not both adversity and good come from the mouth of the Most High?
v39 Why should any mortal man complain, in view of his sins?
v40 Let us examine and test our ways, and turn back to the LORD.
v41 Let us lift up our hearts and hands to God in heaven:
v42 “We have sinned and rebelled; You have not forgiven.”
v43 You have covered Yourself in anger and pursued us; You have killed without pity.
v44 You have covered Yourself with a cloud that no prayer can pass through.
v45 You have made us scum and refuse among the nations.
v46 All our enemies open their mouths against us.
v47 Panic and pitfall have come upon us—devastation and destruction.
v48 Streams of tears flow from my eyes over the destruction of the daughter of my people.
v49 My eyes overflow unceasingly, without relief,
v50 until the LORD looks down from heaven and sees.
v51 My eyes bring grief to my soul because of all the daughters of my city.
v52 Without cause my enemies hunted me like a bird.
v53 They dropped me alive into a pit and cast stones upon me.
v54 The waters flowed over my head, and I thought I was going to die.
v55 I called on Your name, O LORD, out of the depths of the Pit.
v56 You heard my plea: “Do not ignore my cry for relief.”
v57 You drew near when I called on You; You said, “Do not be afraid.”
v58 You defend my cause, O Lord; You redeem my life.
v59 You have seen, O LORD, the wrong done to me; vindicate my cause!
v60 You have seen all their malice, all their plots against me.
v61 O LORD, You have heard their insults, all their plots against me—
v62 the slander and murmuring of my assailants against me all day long.
v63 When they sit and when they rise, see how they mock me in song.
v64 You will pay them back what they deserve, O LORD, according to the work of their hands.
v65 Put a veil of anguish over their hearts; may Your curse be upon them!
v66 You will pursue them in anger and exterminate them from under Your heavens, O LORD.
