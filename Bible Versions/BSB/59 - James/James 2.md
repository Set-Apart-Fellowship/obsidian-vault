# James 2 (Berean Standard Bible)
[[Bible Versions/BSB/59 - James/James 1|← James 1]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/59 - James/James]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/59 - James/James 3|James 3 →]]
***
v1 My brothers, as you hold out your faith in our glorious Lord Jesus Christ, do not show favoritism.
v2 Suppose a man comes into your meeting wearing a gold ring and fine clothes, and a poor man in shabby clothes also comes in.
v3 If you lavish attention on the man in fine clothes and say, “Here is a seat of honor,” but say to the poor man, “You must stand” or “Sit at my feet,”
v4 have you not discriminated among yourselves and become judges with evil thoughts?
v5 Listen, my beloved brothers: Has not God chosen the poor of this world to be rich in faith and to inherit the kingdom He promised those who love Him?
v6 But you have dishonored the poor. Is it not the rich who oppress you and drag you into court?
v7 Are they not the ones who blaspheme the noble name by which you have been called?
v8 If you really fulfill the royal law stated in Scripture, “Love your neighbor as yourself,” you are doing well.
v9 But if you show favoritism, you sin and are convicted by the law as transgressors.
v10 Whoever keeps the whole law but stumbles at just one point is guilty of breaking all of it.
v11 For He who said, “Do not commit adultery,” also said, “Do not murder.” If you do not commit adultery, but do commit murder, you have become a lawbreaker.
v12 Speak and act as those who are going to be judged by the law that gives freedom.
v13 For judgment without mercy will be shown to anyone who has not been merciful. Mercy triumphs over judgment.
v14 What good is it, my brothers, if someone claims to have faith, but has no deeds? Can such faith save him?
v15 Suppose a brother or sister is without clothes and daily food.
v16 If one of you tells him, “Go in peace; stay warm and well fed,” but does not provide for his physical needs, what good is that?
v17 So too, faith by itself, if it does not result in action, is dead.
v18 But someone will say, “You have faith and I have deeds.” Show me your faith without deeds, and I will show you my faith by my deeds.
v19 You believe that God is one. Good for you! Even the demons believe that—and shudder.
v20 O foolish man, do you want evidence that faith without deeds is worthless?
v21 Was not our father Abraham justified by what he did when he offered his son Isaac on the altar?
v22 You see that his faith was working with his actions, and his faith was perfected by what he did.
v23 And the Scripture was fulfilled that says, “Abraham believed God, and it was credited to him as righteousness,” and he was called a friend of God.
v24 As you can see, a man is justified by his deeds and not by faith alone.
v25 In the same way, was not even Rahab the prostitute justified by her actions when she welcomed the spies and sent them off on another route?
v26 As the body without the spirit is dead, so faith without deeds is dead.
