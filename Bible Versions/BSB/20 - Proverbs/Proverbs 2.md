# Proverbs 2 (Berean Standard Bible)
[[Bible Versions/BSB/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 1|← Proverbs 1]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 3|Proverbs 3 →]]
***
v1 My son, if you accept my words and hide my commandments within you,
v2 if you incline your ear to wisdom and direct your heart to understanding,
v3 if you truly call out to insight and lift your voice to understanding,
v4 if you seek it like silver and search it out like hidden treasure,
v5 then you will discern the fear of the LORD and discover the knowledge of God.
v6 For the LORD gives wisdom; from His mouth come knowledge and understanding.
v7 He stores up sound wisdom for the upright; He is a shield to those who walk with integrity,
v8 to guard the paths of justice and protect the way of His saints.
v9 Then you will discern righteousness and justice and equity—every good path.
v10 For wisdom will enter your heart, and knowledge will delight your soul.
v11 Discretion will watch over you, and understanding will guard you,
v12 to deliver you from the way of evil, from the man who speaks perversity,
v13 from those who leave the straight paths to walk in the ways of darkness,
v14 from those who enjoy doing evil and rejoice in the twistedness of evil,
v15 whose paths are crooked and whose ways are devious.
v16 It will rescue you from the forbidden woman, from the stranger with seductive words
v17 who abandons the partner of her youth and forgets the covenant of her God.
v18 For her house sinks down to death, and her tracks to the departed spirits.
v19 None who go to her return or negotiate the paths of life.
v20 So you will follow in the ways of the good, and keep to the paths of the righteous.
v21 For the upright will inhabit the land, and the blameless will remain in it;
v22 but the wicked will be cut off from the land, and the unfaithful will be uprooted.
