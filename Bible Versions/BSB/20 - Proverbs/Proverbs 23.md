# Proverbs 23 (Berean Standard Bible)
[[Bible Versions/BSB/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 22|← Proverbs 22]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 24|Proverbs 24 →]]
***
v1 When you sit down to dine with a ruler, consider carefully what is set before you,
v2 and put a knife to your throat if you possess a great appetite.
v3 Do not crave his delicacies, for that food is deceptive.
v4 Do not wear yourself out to get rich; be wise enough to restrain yourself.
v5 When you glance at wealth, it disappears, for it makes wings for itself and flies like an eagle to the sky.
v6 Do not eat the bread of a stingy man, and do not crave his delicacies;
v7 for he is keeping track, inwardly counting the cost. “Eat and drink,” he says to you, but his heart is not with you.
v8 You will vomit up what little you have eaten and waste your pleasant words.
v9 Do not speak to a fool, for he will despise the wisdom of your words.
v10 Do not move an ancient boundary stone or encroach on the fields of the fatherless,
v11 for their Redeemer is strong; He will take up their case against you.
v12 Apply your heart to instruction and your ears to words of knowledge.
v13 Do not withhold discipline from a child; although you strike him with a rod, he will not die.
v14 Strike him with a rod, and you will deliver his soul from Sheol.
v15 My son, if your heart is wise, my own heart will indeed rejoice.
v16 My inmost being will rejoice when your lips speak what is right.
v17 Do not let your heart envy sinners, but always continue in the fear of the LORD.
v18 For surely there is a future, and your hope will not be cut off.
v19 Listen, my son, and be wise, and guide your heart on the right course.
v20 Do not join those who drink too much wine or gorge themselves on meat.
v21 For the drunkard and the glutton will come to poverty, and drowsiness will clothe them in rags.
v22 Listen to your father who gave you life, and do not despise your mother when she is old.
v23 Invest in truth and never sell it—in wisdom and instruction and understanding.
v24 The father of a righteous man will greatly rejoice, and he who fathers a wise son will delight in him.
v25 May your father and mother be glad, and may she who gave you birth rejoice!
v26 My son, give me your heart, and let your eyes delight in my ways.
v27 For a prostitute is a deep pit, and an adulteress is a narrow well.
v28 Like a robber she lies in wait and multiplies the faithless among men.
v29 Who has woe? Who has sorrow? Who has contentions? Who has complaints? Who has needless wounds? Who has bloodshot eyes?
v30 Those who linger over wine, who go to taste mixed drinks.
v31 Do not gaze at wine while it is red, when it sparkles in the cup and goes down smoothly.
v32 In the end it bites like a snake and stings like a viper.
v33 Your eyes will see strange things, and your mind will utter perversities.
v34 You will be like one sleeping on the high seas or lying on the top of a mast:
v35 “They struck me, but I feel no pain! They beat me, but I did not know it! When can I wake up to search for another drink?”
