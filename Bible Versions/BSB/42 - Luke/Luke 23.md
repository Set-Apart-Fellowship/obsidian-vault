# Luke 23 (Berean Standard Bible)
[[Bible Versions/BSB/42 - Luke/Luke 22|← Luke 22]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/42 - Luke/Luke]] | [[Bible Versions/BSB/42 - Luke/Luke 24|Luke 24 →]]
***
v1 Then the whole council rose and led Jesus away to Pilate.
v2 And they began to accuse Him, saying, “We found this man subverting our nation, forbidding payment of taxes to Caesar, and proclaiming Himself to be Christ, a King.”
v3 So Pilate asked Him, “Are You the King of the Jews?” “You have said so,” Jesus replied.
v4 Then Pilate said to the chief priests and the crowds, “I find no basis for a charge against this man.”
v5 But they kept insisting, “He stirs up the people all over Judea with His teaching. He began in Galilee and has come all the way here.”
v6 When Pilate heard this, he asked if the man was a Galilean.
v7 And learning that Jesus was under Herod’s jurisdiction, he sent Him to Herod, who himself was in Jerusalem at that time.
v8 When Herod saw Jesus, he was greatly pleased. He had wanted to see Him for a long time, because he had heard about Him and was hoping to see Him perform a miracle.
v9 Herod questioned Jesus at great length, but He gave no answer.
v10 Meanwhile, the chief priests and scribes stood there, vehemently accusing Him.
v11 And even Herod and his soldiers ridiculed and mocked Him. Dressing Him in a fine robe, they sent Him back to Pilate.
v12 That day Herod and Pilate became friends; before this time they had been enemies.
v13 Then Pilate called together the chief priests, the rulers, and the people,
v14 and said to them, “You brought me this man as one who was inciting the people to rebellion. I have examined Him here in your presence and found Him not guilty of your charges against Him.
v15 Neither has Herod, for he sent Him back to us. As you can see, He has done nothing deserving of death.
v16 Therefore I will punish Him and release Him.”
v18 But they all cried out in unison: “Away with this man! Release Barabbas to us!”
v19 (Barabbas had been imprisoned for an insurrection in the city, and for murder.)
v20 Wanting to release Jesus, Pilate addressed them again,
v21 but they kept shouting, “Crucify Him! Crucify Him!”
v22 A third time he said to them, “What evil has this man done? I have found in Him no offense worthy of death. So after I punish Him, I will release Him.”
v23 But they were insistent, demanding with loud voices for Jesus to be crucified. And their clamor prevailed.
v24 So Pilate sentenced that their demand be met.
v25 As they had requested, he released the one imprisoned for insurrection and murder, and he handed Jesus over to their will.
v26 As the soldiers led Him away, they seized Simon of Cyrene on his way in from the country, and they put the cross on him to carry behind Jesus.
v27 A great number of people followed Him, including women who kept mourning and wailing for Him.
v28 But Jesus turned to them and said, “Daughters of Jerusalem, do not weep for Me, but weep for yourselves and for your children.
v29 Look, the days are coming when people will say, ‘Blessed are the barren women, the wombs that never bore, and breasts that never nursed!’
v30 At that time ‘they will say to the mountains, “Fall on us!” and to the hills, “Cover us!”’
v31 For if men do these things while the tree is green, what will happen when it is dry?”
v32 Two others, who were criminals, were also led away to be executed with Jesus.
v33 When they came to the place called The Skull, they crucified Him there, along with the criminals, one on His right and the other on His left.
v34 Then Jesus said, “Father, forgive them, for they do not know what they are doing.” And they divided up His garments by casting lots.
v35 The people stood watching, and the rulers sneered at Him, saying, “He saved others; let Him save Himself if He is the Christ of God, the Chosen One.”
v36 The soldiers also mocked Him and came up to offer Him sour wine.
v37 “If You are the King of the Jews,” they said, “save Yourself!”
v38 Above Him was posted an inscription: THIS IS THE KING OF THE JEWS.
v39 One of the criminals who hung there heaped abuse on Him. “Are You not the Christ?” he said. “Save Yourself and us!”
v40 But the other one rebuked him, saying, “Do you not even fear God, since you are under the same judgment?
v41 We are punished justly, for we are receiving what our actions deserve. But this man has done nothing wrong.”
v42 Then he said, “Jesus, remember me when You come into Your kingdom!”
v43 And Jesus said to him, “Truly I tell you, today you will be with Me in Paradise.”
v44 It was now about the sixth hour, and darkness came over all the land until the ninth hour.
v45 The sun was darkened, and the veil of the temple was torn down the middle.
v46 Then Jesus called out in a loud voice, “Father, into Your hands I commit My Spirit.” And when He had said this, He breathed His last.
v47 When the centurion saw what had happened, he gave glory to God, saying, “Surely this was a righteous man.”
v48 And when all the people who had gathered for this spectacle saw what had happened, they returned home beating their breasts.
v49 But all those who knew Jesus, including the women who had followed Him from Galilee, stood at a distance watching these things.
v50 Now there was a Council member named Joseph, a good and righteous man,
v51 who had not consented to their decision or action. He was from the Judean town of Arimathea and was waiting for the kingdom of God.
v52 He went to Pilate to ask for the body of Jesus.
v53 Then he took it down, wrapped it in a linen cloth, and placed it in a tomb cut into the rock, where no one had yet been laid.
v54 It was Preparation Day, and the Sabbath was beginning.
v55 The women who had come with Jesus from Galilee followed, and they saw the tomb and how His body was placed.
v56 Then they returned to prepare spices and perfumes. And they rested on the Sabbath, according to the commandment.
