# Luke 17 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/42 - Luke/Luke 16|← Luke 16]] | [[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/42 - Luke/Luke]] | [[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/42 - Luke/Luke 18|Luke 18 →]]

***

v1 He said to the disciples, "It is impossible that occasions of stumbling should not come, but woe to him through whom they come!

v2 It would be better for him if a millstone were hanged about his neck, and he were thrown into the sea, rather than that he should cause one of these little ones to stumble.

v3 Be careful. If your brother sins against you, rebuke him. If he repents, forgive him.

v4 If he sins against you seven times in the day, and seven times turns again, saying, 'I repent,' you shall forgive him."

v5 The apostles said to the Lord, "Increase our faith."

v6 The Lord said, "If you had faith as a grain of mustard seed, you would tell this sycamore tree, 'Be uprooted, and be planted in the sea,' and it would obey you.

v7 But who is there of you, having a servant plowing or keeping sheep, that will say, when he comes in from the field, 'Come immediately and sit down at the table,'

v8 and will not rather tell him, 'Prepare my supper, clothe yourself properly, and serve me, while I eat and drink. Afterward you will eat and drink?'

v9 Does he thank that servant because he did the things that were commanded? I think not.

v10 Even so you also, when you have done all the things that are commanded you, say, 'We are unworthy servants. We have done our duty.'"

v11 It happened, as he was on his way to Jerusalem, that he was passing along the borders of Samaria and Galilee.

v12 As he entered into a certain village, ten men who were lepers met him, who stood far away.

v13 They lifted up their voices, saying, "Jesus, Master, have mercy on us!"

v14 When he saw them, he said to them, "Go and show yourselves to the priests." It happened, as they went, they were cleansed.

v15 One of them, when he saw that he was healed, turned back, glorifying God with a loud voice.

v16 He fell on his face at his feet, giving him thanks. He was a Samaritan.

v17 Jesus answered, "Weren't the ten cleansed? But where are the nine?

v18 Were there none found who returned to give glory to God, except this stranger?"

v19 He said to him, "Get up, and go your way. Your faith has healed you."

v20 Being asked by the Pharisees when the kingdom of God would come, he answered them, "The kingdom of God doesn't come with observation;

v21 neither will they say, 'Look, here!' or, 'Look, there!' for behold, the kingdom of God is within you."

v22 He said to the disciples, "The days will come, when you will desire to see one of the days of the Son of Man, and you will not see it.

v23 They will tell you, 'Look, here!' or 'Look, there!' Don't go away, nor follow after them,

v24 for as the lightning, when it flashes out of the one part under the sky, shines to the other part under the sky; so will the Son of Man be in his day.

v25 But first, he must suffer many things and be rejected by this generation.

v26 As it happened in the days of Noah, even so will it be also in the days of the Son of Man.

v27 They ate, they drank, they married, they were given in marriage, until the day that Noah entered into the ark, and the flood came, and destroyed them all.

v28 Likewise, even as it happened in the days of Lot: they ate, they drank, they bought, they sold, they planted, they built;

v29 but in the day that Lot went out from Sodom, it rained fire and sulfur from the sky, and destroyed them all.

v30 It will be the same way in the day that the Son of Man is revealed.

v31 In that day, he who will be on the housetop, and his goods in the house, let him not go down to take them away. Let him who is in the field likewise not turn back.

v32 Remember Lot's wife!

v33 Whoever seeks to gain his life loses it, but whoever loses his life preserves it.

v34 I tell you, in that night there will be two people in one bed. The one will be taken, and the other will be left.

v35 There will be two women grinding together. The one will be taken, and the other will be left."

v36 

v37 They answering, asked him, "Where, Lord?"   He said to them, "Where the body is, there will the vultures also be gathered together."
