# Psalms 70 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 69|← Psalms 69]] | [[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 71|Psalms 71 →]]

***

v1 Hurry, God, to deliver me.  Come quickly to help me, Yahweh.

v2 Let them be put to shame and confounded who seek my soul.  Let those who desire my ruin be turned back in disgrace.

v3 Let them be turned because of their shame  Who say, "Aha! Aha!"

v4 Let all those who seek you rejoice and be glad in you.  Let those who love your salvation continually say,  "Let God be exalted!"

v5 But I am poor and needy.  Come to me quickly, God. You are my help and my deliverer,  Yahweh, don't delay.   Psalm 71
