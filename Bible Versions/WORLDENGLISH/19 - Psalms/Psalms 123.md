# Psalms 123 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 122|← Psalms 122]] | [[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 124|Psalms 124 →]]

***

v1 To you I do lift up my eyes,  You who sit in the heavens.

v2 Behold, as the eyes of servants look to the hand of their    master,  As the eyes of a maid to the hand of her mistress;  So our eyes look to Yahweh, our God,  Until he has mercy on us.

v3 Have mercy on us, Yahweh, have mercy on us,  For we have endured much contempt.

v4 Our soul is exceedingly filled with the scoffing of those who    are at ease,  With the contempt of the proud.   Psalm 124    A Song of Ascents. By David.
