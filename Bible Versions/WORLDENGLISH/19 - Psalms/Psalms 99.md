# Psalms 99 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 98|← Psalms 98]] | [[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 100|Psalms 100 →]]

***

v1 Yahweh reigns! Let the peoples tremble.  He sits enthroned among the cherubim.  Let the earth be moved.

v2 Yahweh is great in Zion.  He is high above all the peoples.

v3 Let them praise your great and awesome name.  He is Holy!

v4 The King's strength also loves justice.  You do establish equity.  You execute justice and righteousness in Jacob.

v5 Exalt Yahweh our God.  Worship at his footstool.  He is Holy!

v6 Moses and Aaron were among his priests,  Samuel among those who call on his name;  They called on Yahweh, and he answered them.

v7 He spoke to them in the pillar of cloud.  They kept his testimonies,  The statute that he gave them.

v8 You answered them, Yahweh our God.  You are a God who forgave them,  Although you took vengeance for their doings.

v9 Exalt Yahweh, our God.  Worship at his holy hill,  For Yahweh, our God, is holy!   Psalm 100    A Psalm of thanksgiving.
