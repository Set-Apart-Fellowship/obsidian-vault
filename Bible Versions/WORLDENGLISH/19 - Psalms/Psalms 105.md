# Psalms 105 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 104|← Psalms 104]] | [[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 106|Psalms 106 →]]

***

v1 Give thanks to Yahweh! Call on his name!  Make his doings known among the peoples.

v2 Sing to him, sing praises to him!  Tell of all his marvelous works.

v3 Glory in his holy name.  Let the heart of them rejoice who seek Yahweh.

v4 Seek Yahweh and his strength.  Seek his face forever more.

v5 Remember his marvelous works that he has done;  His wonders, and the judgments of his mouth,

v6 You seed of Abraham, his servant,  You children of Jacob, his chosen ones.

v7 He is Yahweh, our God.  His judgments are in all the earth.

v8 He has remembered his covenant forever,  The word which he commanded to a thousand generations,

v9 The covenant which he made with Abraham,  His oath to Isaac,

v10 And confirmed the same to Jacob for a statute;  To Israel for an everlasting covenant,

v11 Saying, "To you I will give the land of Canaan,  The lot of your inheritance;"

v12 When they were but a few men in number,  Yes, very few, and sojourners in it.

v13 They went about from nation to nation,  From one kingdom to another people.

v14 He allowed no one to do them wrong.  Yes, he reproved kings for their sakes,

v15 "Don't touch my anointed ones.  Do my prophets no harm."

v16 He called for a famine on the land.  He destroyed the food supplies.

v17 He sent a man before them.  Joseph was sold for a slave.

v18 They bruised his feet with shackles.  His neck was locked in irons,

v19 Until the time that his word happened,  And Yahweh's word proved him true.

v20 The king sent and freed him;  Even the ruler of peoples, and let him go free.

v21 He made him lord of his house,  And ruler of all of his possessions;

v22 To discipline his princes at his pleasure,  And to teach his elders wisdom.

v23 Israel also came into Egypt.  Jacob sojourned in the land of Ham.

v24 He increased his people greatly,  And made them stronger than their adversaries.

v25 He turned their heart to hate his people,  To conspire against his servants.

v26 He sent Moses, his servant,  And Aaron, whom he had chosen.

v27 They performed miracles among them,  And wonders in the land of Ham.

v28 He sent darkness, and made it dark.  They didn't rebel against his words.

v29 He turned their waters into blood,  And killed their fish.

v30 Their land swarmed with frogs,  Even in the chambers of their kings.

v31 He spoke, and swarms of flies came,  And lice in all their borders.

v32 He gave them hail for rain,  With lightning in their land.

v33 He struck their vines and also their fig trees,  And shattered the trees of their country.

v34 He spoke, and the locusts came,  And the grasshoppers, without number,

v35 Ate up every plant in their land;  Ate up the fruit of their ground.

v36 He struck also all the firstborn in their land,  The chief of all their strength.

v37 He brought them forth with silver and gold.  There was not one feeble person among his tribes.

v38 Egypt was glad when they departed,  For the fear of them had fallen on them.

v39 He spread a cloud for a covering,  Fire to give light in the night.

v40 They asked, and he brought quails,  And satisfied them with the bread of the sky.

v41 He opened the rock, and waters gushed out.  They ran as a river in the dry places.

v42 For he remembered his holy word,  And Abraham, his servant.

v43 He brought forth his people with joy,  His chosen with singing.

v44 He gave them the lands of the nations.  They took the labor of the peoples in possession,

v45 That they might keep his statutes,  And observe his laws.  Praise Yah!   Psalm 106
