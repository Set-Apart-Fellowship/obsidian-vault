# Psalms 131 (World English Bible)

***

v1 Yahweh, my heart isn't haughty, nor my eyes lofty;  Neither do I concern myself with great matters,  Or things too wonderful for me.

v2 Surely I have stilled and quieted my soul,  Like a weaned child with his mother,  Like a weaned child is my soul within me.

v3 Israel, hope in Yahweh,  From this time forth and forevermore.   Psalm 132 A Song of Ascents.
