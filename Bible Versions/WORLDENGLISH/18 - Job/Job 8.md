# Job 8 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/18 - Job/Job 7|← Job 7]] | [[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/18 - Job/Job 9|Job 9 →]]

***

v1 Then Bildad the Shuhite answered,

v2 "How long will you speak these things?  Shall the words of your mouth be a mighty wind?

v3 Does God pervert justice?  Or does the Almighty pervert righteousness?

v4 If your children have sinned against him,  He has delivered them into the hand of their disobedience;

v5 If you want to seek God diligently,  Make your supplication to the Almighty.

v6 If you were pure and upright,  Surely now he would awaken for you, And make the habitation of your righteousness prosperous.

v7 Though your beginning was small, Yet your latter end would greatly increase.

v8 "Please inquire of past generations,  Find out about the learning of their fathers.

v9 (For we are but of yesterday, and know nothing,  Because our days on earth are a shadow.)

v10 Shall they not teach you, tell you,  And utter words out of their heart?

v11 "Can the papyrus grow up without mire?  Can the rushes grow without water?

v12 While it is yet in its greenness, not cut down,  It withers before any other reed.

v13 So are the paths of all who forget God.  The hope of the godless man shall perish,

v14 Whose confidence shall break apart,  Whose trust is a spider's web.

v15 He shall lean on his house, but it shall not stand.  He shall cling to it, but it shall not endure.

v16 He is green before the sun,  His shoots go forth over his garden.

v17 His roots are wrapped around the rock pile,  He sees the place of stones.

v18 If he is destroyed from his place,  Then it shall deny him, saying, 'I have not seen you.'

v19 Behold, this is the joy of his way:  Out of the earth shall others spring.

v20 "Behold, God will not cast away a blameless man,  Neither will he uphold the evil-doers.

v21 He will still fill your mouth with laughter,  Your lips with shouting.

v22 Those who hate you shall be clothed with shame.  The tent of the wicked shall be no more."
