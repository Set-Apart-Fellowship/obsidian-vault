# Job 38 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/18 - Job/Job 37|← Job 37]] | [[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/18 - Job/Job 39|Job 39 →]]

***

v1 Then Yahweh answered Job out of the whirlwind,

v2 "Who is this who darkens counsel  By words without knowledge?

v3 Brace yourself like a man,  For I will question you, then you answer me!

v4 "Where were you when I laid the foundations of the earth?  Declare, if you have understanding.

v5 Who determined the measures of it, if you know?  Or who stretched the line on it?

v6 Whereupon were the foundations of it fastened?  Or who laid its cornerstone,

v7 When the morning stars sang together,  And all the sons of God shouted for joy?

v8 "Or who shut up the sea with doors,  When it broke forth from the womb,

v9 When I made clouds the garment of it,  Thick darkness a swaddling-band for it,

v10 Marked out for it my bound,  Set bars and doors,

v11 And said, 'Here you may come, but no further;  Here shall your proud waves be stayed?'

v12 "Have you commanded the morning in your days,  And caused the dawn to know its place;

v13 That it might take hold of the ends of the earth,  And shake the wicked out of it?

v14 It is changed as clay under the seal,  And stands forth as a garment.

v15 From the wicked, their light is withheld,  The high arm is broken.

v16 "Have you entered into the springs of the sea?  Or have you walked in the recesses of the deep?

v17 Have the gates of death been revealed to you?  Or have you seen the gates of the shadow of death?

v18 Have you comprehended the earth in its breadth?  Declare, if you know it all.

v19 "What is the way to the dwelling of light?  As for darkness, where is the place of it,

v20 That you should take it to the bound of it,  That you should discern the paths to the house of it?

v21 Surely you know, for you were born then,  And the number of your days is great!

v22 Have you entered the treasuries of the snow,  Or have you seen the treasures of the hail,

v23 Which I have reserved against the time of trouble,  Against the day of battle and war?

v24 By what way is the lightning distributed,  Or the east wind scattered on the earth?

v25 Who has cut a channel for the flood water,  Or the path for the thunderstorm;

v26 To cause it to rain on a land where no man is;  On the wilderness, in which there is no man;

v27 To satisfy the waste and desolate ground,  To cause the tender grass to spring forth?

v28 Does the rain have a father?  Or who fathers the drops of dew?

v29 Out of whose womb came the ice?  The gray frost of the sky, who has given birth to it?

v30 The waters become hard like stone,  When the surface of the deep is frozen.

v31 "Can you bind the cluster of the Pleiades,  Or loosen the cords of Orion?

v32 Can you lead forth the constellations in their season?  Or can you guide the Bear with her cubs?

v33 Do you know the laws of the heavens?  Can you establish the dominion of it over the earth?

v34 "Can you lift up your voice to the clouds,  That abundance of waters may cover you?

v35 Can you send forth lightnings, that they may go?  Do they report to you, 'Here we are?'

v36 Who has put wisdom in the inward parts?  Or who has given understanding to the mind?

v37 Who can number the clouds by wisdom?  Or who can pour out the bottles of the sky,

v38 When the dust runs into a mass,  And the clods of earth stick together?

v39 "Can you hunt the prey for the lioness,  Or satisfy the appetite of the young lions,

v40 When they crouch in their dens,  And lie in wait in the thicket?

v41 Who provides for the raven his prey,  When his young ones cry to God,  And wander for lack of food?
