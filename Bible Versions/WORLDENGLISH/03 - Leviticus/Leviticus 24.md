# Leviticus 24 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 23|← Leviticus 23]] | [[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus]] | [[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 25|Leviticus 25 →]]

***

v1 Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

v2 Command the children of Israel, that they bring to you pure olive oil beaten for the light, to cause a lamp to burn continually.

v3 Outside of the veil of the testimony, in the tent of meeting, shall Aaron keep it in order from evening to morning before Yahweh continually: it shall be a statute forever throughout your generations.

v4 He shall keep in order the lamps on the pure lampstand before Yahweh continually.

v5 You shall take fine flour, and bake twelve cakes of it: two tenth parts [of an ephah] shall be in one cake.

v6 You shall set them in two rows, six on a row, on the pure table before Yahweh.

v7 You shall put pure frankincense on each row, that it may be to the bread for a memorial, even an offering made by fire to Yahweh.

v8 Every Sabbath day he shall set it in order before Yahweh continually; it is on the behalf of the children of Israel, an everlasting covenant.

v9 It shall be for Aaron and his sons; and they shall eat it in a holy place: for it is most holy to him of the offerings of Yahweh made by fire by a perpetual statute.

v10 The son of an Israelite woman, whose father was an Egyptian, went out among the children of Israel; and the son of the Israelite woman and a man of Israel strove together in the camp:

v11 and the son of the Israelite woman blasphemed the Name, and cursed; and they brought him to Moses. His mother's name was Shelomith, the daughter of Dibri, of the tribe of Dan.

v12 They put him in custody, that it might be declared to them at the mouth of Yahweh.

v13 Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

v14 Bring forth him who has cursed outside of the camp; and let all who heard him lay their hands on his head, and let all the congregation stone him.

v15 You shall speak to the children of Israel, saying, Whoever curses his God shall bear his sin.

v16 He who blasphemes the name of Yahweh, he shall surely be put to death; all the congregation shall certainly stone him: as well the sojourner, as the home-born, when he blasphemes the name [of Yahweh], shall be put to death.

v17 He who strikes any man mortally shall surely be put to death.

v18 He who strikes a animal mortally shall make it good, life for life.

v19 If a man cause a blemish in his neighbor; as he has done, so shall it be done to him:

v20 breach for breach, eye for eye, tooth for tooth; as he has caused a blemish in a man, so shall it be rendered to him.

v21 He who kills a animal shall make it good: and he who kills a man shall be put to death.

v22 You shall have one manner of law, as well for the sojourner, as for the home-born: for I am Yahweh your God.

v23 Moses spoke to the children of Israel; and they brought forth him who had cursed out of the camp, and stoned him with stones. The children of Israel did as Yahweh commanded Moses.
