# Lamentations 3 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 2|← Lamentations 2]] | [[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations]] | [[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 4|Lamentations 4 →]]

***

v1 I am the man that has seen affliction by the rod of his wrath.

v2 He has led me and caused me to walk in darkness, and not in light.

v3 Surely against me he turns his hand again and again all the day.

v4 My flesh and my skin has he made old; he has broken my bones.

v5 He has built against me, and compassed me with gall and travail.

v6 He has made me to dwell in dark places, as those that have been    long dead.

v7 He has walled me about, that I can't go forth; he has made my    chain heavy.

v8 Yes, when I cry, and call for help, he shuts out my prayer.

v9 He has walled up my ways with hewn stone; he has made my paths    crooked.

v10 He is to me as a bear lying in wait, as a lion in secret places.

v11 He has turned aside my ways, and pulled me in pieces; he has    made me desolate.

v12 He has bent his bow, and set me as a mark for the arrow.

v13 He has caused the shafts of his quiver to enter into my reins.

v14 I am become a derision to all my people, and their song all the    day.

v15 He has filled me with bitterness, he has sated me with wormwood.

v16 He has also broken my teeth with gravel stones; he has covered    me with ashes.

v17 You have removed my soul far off from peace; I forgot prosperity.

v18 I said, My strength is perished, and my expectation from Yahweh.

v19 Remember my affliction and my misery, the wormwood and the gall.

v20 My soul still remembers them, and is bowed down within me.

v21 This I recall to my mind; therefore have I hope.

v22 [It is of] Yahweh's lovingkindnesses that we are not consumed,    because his compassion doesn't fail.

v23 They are new every morning; great is your faithfulness.

v24 Yahweh is my portion, says my soul; therefore will I hope in him.

v25 Yahweh is good to those who wait for him, to the soul that seeks    him.

v26 It is good that a man should hope and quietly wait for the    salvation of Yahweh.

v27 It is good for a man that he bear the yoke in his youth.

v28 Let him sit alone and keep silence, because he has laid it on    him.

v29 Let him put his mouth in the dust, if so be there may be hope.

v30 Let him give his cheek to him who strikes him; let him be filled    full with reproach.

v31 For the Lord will not cast off forever.

v32 For though he cause grief, yet will he have compassion according    to the multitude of his lovingkindnesses.

v33 For he does not afflict willingly, nor grieve the children of    men.

v34 To crush under foot all the prisoners of the earth,

v35 To turn aside the right of a man before the face of the Most    High,

v36 To subvert a man in his cause, the Lord doesn't approve.

v37 Who is he who says, and it comes to pass, when the Lord doesn't    command it?

v38 Doesn't evil and good come out of the mouth of the Most High?

v39 Why does a living man complain, a man for the punishment of his    sins?

v40 Let us search and try our ways, and turn again to Yahweh.

v41 Let us lift up our heart with our hands to God in the heavens.

v42 We have transgressed and have rebelled; you have not pardoned.

v43 You have covered with anger and pursued us; you have killed, you    have not pitied.

v44 You have covered yourself with a cloud, so that no prayer can    pass through.

v45 You have made us an off-scouring and refuse in the midst of the    peoples.

v46 All our enemies have opened their mouth wide against us.

v47 Fear and the pit are come on us, devastation and destruction.

v48 My eye runs down with streams of water, for the destruction of    the daughter of my people.

v49 My eye pours down, and doesn't cease, without any intermission,

v50 Until Yahweh look down, and see from heaven.

v51 My eye affects my soul, because of all the daughters of my city.

v52 They have chased me sore like a bird, those who are my enemies    without cause.

v53 They have cut off my life in the dungeon, and have cast a stone    on me.

v54 Waters flowed over my head; I said, I am cut off.

v55 I called on your name, Yahweh, out of the lowest dungeon.

v56 You heard my voice; don't hide your ear at my breathing, at my    cry.

v57 You drew near in the day that I called on you; you said, Don't    be afraid.

v58 Lord, you have pleaded the causes of my soul; you have redeemed    my life.

v59 Yahweh, you have seen my wrong; judge you my cause.

v60 You have seen all their vengeance and all their devices against    me.

v61 You have heard their reproach, Yahweh, and all their devices    against me,

v62 The lips of those that rose up against me, and their device    against me all the day.

v63 See you their sitting down, and their rising up; I am their song.

v64 You will render to them a recompense, Yahweh, according to the    work of their hands.

v65 You will give them hardness of heart, your curse to them.

v66 You will pursue them in anger, and destroy them from under the    heavens of Yahweh.
