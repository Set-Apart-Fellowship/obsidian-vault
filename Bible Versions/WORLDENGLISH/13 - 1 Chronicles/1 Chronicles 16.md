# 1 Chronicles 16 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 15|← 1 Chronicles 15]] | [[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles]] | [[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 17|1 Chronicles 17 →]]

***

v1 They brought in the ark of God, and set it in the midst of the tent that David had pitched for it: and they offered burnt offerings and peace-offerings before God.

v2 When David had made an end of offering the burnt offering and the peace-offerings, he blessed the people in the name of Yahweh.

v3 He dealt to everyone of Israel, both man and woman, to every one a loaf of bread, and a portion [of flesh], and a cake of raisins.

v4 He appointed certain of the Levites to minister before the ark of Yahweh, and to celebrate and to thank and praise Yahweh, the God of Israel:

v5 Asaph the chief, and second to him Zechariah, Jeiel, and Shemiramoth, and Jehiel, and Mattithiah, and Eliab, and Benaiah, and Obed-edom, and Jeiel, with psalteries and with harps; and Asaph with cymbals, sounding aloud;

v6 and Benaiah and Jahaziel the priests with trumpets continually, before the ark of the covenant of God.

v7 Then on that day David first ordained to give thanks to Yahweh, by the hand of Asaph and his brothers.

v8 Oh give thanks to Yahweh, call on his name; Make known his doings among the peoples.

v9 Sing to him, sing praises to him; Talk you of all his marvelous works.

v10 Glory you in his holy name; Let the heart of them rejoice who seek Yahweh.

v11 Seek you Yahweh and his strength; Seek his face forever more.

v12 Remember his marvelous works that he has done, His wonders, and the judgments of his mouth,

v13 You seed of Israel his servant, You children of Jacob, his chosen ones.

v14 He is Yahweh our God; His judgments are in all the earth.

v15 Remember his covenant forever, The word which he commanded to a thousand generations,

v16 [The covenant] which he made with Abraham, His oath to Isaac,

v17 Confirmed the same to Jacob for a statute, To Israel for an everlasting covenant,

v18 Saying, To you will I give the land of Canaan, The lot of your inheritance;

v19 When you were but a few men in number, Yes, very few, and sojourners in it;

v20 They went about from nation to nation, From one kingdom to another people.

v21 He allowed no man to do them wrong; Yes, he reproved kings for their sakes,

v22 [Saying], Don't touch my anointed ones, Do my prophets no harm.

v23 Sing to Yahweh, all the earth; Show forth his salvation from day to day.

v24 Declare his glory among the nations, His marvelous works among all the peoples.

v25 For great is Yahweh, and greatly to be praised: He also is to be feared above all gods.

v26 For all the gods of the peoples are idols: But Yahweh made the heavens.

v27 Honor and majesty are before him: Strength and gladness are in his place.

v28 Ascribe to Yahweh, you relatives of the peoples, Ascribe to Yahweh glory and strength;

v29 Ascribe to Yahweh the glory due to his name: Bring an offering, and come before him: Worship Yahweh in holy array.

v30 Tremble before him, all the earth: The world also is established that it can't be moved.

v31 Let the heavens be glad, and let the earth rejoice; Let them say among the nations, Yahweh reigns.

v32 Let the sea roar, and the fullness of it; Let the field exult, and all that is therein;

v33 Then shall the trees of the wood sing for joy before Yahweh; For he comes to judge the earth.

v34 Oh give thanks to Yahweh; for he is good; For his lovingkindness endures forever.

v35 Say you, Save us, God of our salvation, Gather us together and deliver us from the nations, To give thanks to your holy name, To triumph in your praise.

v36 Blessed be Yahweh, the God of Israel, From everlasting even to everlasting.   All the people said, Amen, and praised Yahweh.

v37 So he left there, before the ark of the covenant of Yahweh, Asaph and his brothers, to minister before the ark continually, as every day's work required;

v38 and Obed-edom with their brothers, sixty-eight; Obed-edom also the son of Jeduthun and Hosah to be doorkeepers;

v39 and Zadok the priest, and his brothers the priests, before the tent of Yahweh in the high place that was at Gibeon,

v40 to offer burnt offerings to Yahweh on the altar of burnt offering continually morning and evening, even according to all that is written in the law of Yahweh, which he commanded to Israel;

v41 and with them Heman and Jeduthun, and the rest who were chosen, who were mentioned by name, to give thanks to Yahweh, because his lovingkindness endures forever;

v42 and with them Heman and Jeduthun [with] trumpets and cymbals for those that should sound aloud, and [with] instruments for the songs of God; and the sons of Jeduthun to be at the gate.

v43 All the people departed every man to his house: and David returned to bless his house.
