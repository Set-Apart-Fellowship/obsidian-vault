# 1 Chronicles 1 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles]] | [[Bible Versions/WORLDENGLISH/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 2|1 Chronicles 2 →]]

***

v1 Adam, Seth, Enosh,

v2 Kenan, Mahalalel, Jared,

v3 Enoch, Methuselah, Lamech,

v4 Noah, Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

v5 The sons of Japheth: Gomer, and Magog, and Madai, and Javan, and Tubal, and Meshech, and Tiras.

v6 The sons of Gomer: Ashkenaz, and Diphath, and Togarmah.

v7 The sons of Javan: Elishah, and Tarshish, Kittim, and Rodanim.

v8 The sons of Ham: Cush, and Mizraim, Put, and Canaan.

v9 The sons of Cush: Seba, and Havilah, and Sabta, and Raama, and Sabteca. The sons of Raamah: Sheba, and Dedan.

v10 Cush became the father of Nimrod; he began to be a mighty one in the earth.

v11 Mizraim became the father of Ludim, and Anamim, and Lehabim, and Naphtuhim,

v12 and Pathrusim, and Casluhim (from whence came the Philistines), and Caphtorim.

v13 Canaan became the father of Sidon his firstborn, and Heth,

v14 and the Jebusite, and the Amorite, and the Girgashite,

v15 and the Hivite, and the Arkite, and the Sinite,

v16 and the Arvadite, and the Zemarite, and the Hamathite.

v17 The sons of Shem: Elam, and Asshur, and Arpachshad, and Lud, and Aram, and Uz, and Hul, and Gether, and Meshech.

v18 Arpachshad became the father of Shelah, and Shelah became the father of Eber.

v19 To Eber were born two sons: the name of the one was Peleg; for in his days the earth was divided; and his brother's name was Joktan.

v20 Joktan became the father of Almodad, and Sheleph, and Hazarmaveth, and Jerah,

v21 and Hadoram, and Uzal, and Diklah,

v22 and Ebal, and Abimael, and Sheba,

v23 and Ophir, and Havilah, and Jobab. All these were the sons of Joktan.

v24 Shem, Arpachshad, Shelah,

v25 Eber, Peleg, Reu,

v26 Serug, Nahor, Terah,

v27 Abram (the same is Abraham).

v28 The sons of Abraham: Isaac, and Ishmael.

v29 These are their generations: the firstborn of Ishmael, Nebaioth; then Kedar, and Adbeel, and Mibsam,

v30 Mishma, and Dumah, Massa, Hadad, and Tema,

v31 Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah. These are the sons of Ishmael.

v32 The sons of Keturah, Abraham's concubine: she bore Zimran, and Jokshan, and Medan, and Midian, and Ishbak, and Shuah. The sons of Jokshan: Sheba, and Dedan.

v33 The sons of Midian: Ephah, and Epher, and Hanoch, and Abook_ida, and Eldaah. All these were the sons of Keturah.

v34 Abraham became the father of Isaac. The sons of Isaac: Esau, and Israel.

v35 The sons of Esau: Eliphaz, Reuel, and Jeush, and Jalam, and Korah.

v36 The sons of Eliphaz: Teman, and Omar, Zephi, and Gatam, Kenaz, and Timna, and Amalek.

v37 The sons of Reuel: Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah.

v38 The sons of Seir: Lotan, and Shobal, and Zibeon, and Anah, and Dishon, and Ezer, and Dishan.

v39 The sons of Lotan: Hori, and Homam; and Timna was Lotan's sister.

v40 The sons of Shobal: Alian, and Manahath, and Ebal, Shephi, and Onam. The sons of Zibeon: Aiah, and Anah.

v41 The sons of Anah: Dishon. The sons of Dishon: Hamran, and Eshban, and Ithran, and Cheran.

v42 The sons of Ezer: Bilhan, and Zaavan, Jaakan. The sons of Dishan: Uz, and Aran.

v43 Now these are the kings who reigned in the land of Edom, before there resigned any king over the children of Israel: Bela the son of Beor; and the name of his city was Dinhabah.

v44 Bela died, and Jobab the son of Zerah of Bozrah reigned in his place.

v45 Jobab died, and Husham of the land of the Temanites reigned in his place.

v46 Husham died, and Hadad the son of Bedad, who struck Midian in the field of Moab, reigned in his place; and the name of his city was Avith.

v47 Hadad died, and Samlah of Masrekah reigned in his place.

v48 Samlah died, and Shaul of Rehoboth by the River reigned in his place.

v49 Shaul died, and Baal-hanan the son of Achbor reigned in his place.

v50 Baal-hanan died, and Hadad reigned in his place; and the name of his city was Pai: and his wife's name was Mehetabel, the daughter of Matred, the daughter of Me-zahab.

v51 Hadad died. The chiefs of Edom were: chief Timna, chief Aliah, chief Jetheth,

v52 chief Oholibamah, chief Elah, chief Pinon,

v53 chief Kenaz, chief Teman, chief Mibzar,

v54 chief Magdiel, chief Iram. These are the chiefs of Edom.
