# Ephesians 5 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 4|← Ephesians 4]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 6|Ephesians 6 →]]

***

v1 Become, then, followers of God, as children beloved,

v2 and walk in love, as also the Christ did love us, and did give himself for us, an offering and a sacrifice to God for an odour of a sweet smell,

v3 and whoredom, and all uncleanness, or covetousness, let it not even be named among you, as becometh saints;

v4 also filthiness, and foolish talking, or jesting, -- the things not fit -- but rather thanksgiving;

v5 for this ye know, that every whoremonger, or unclean, or covetous person, who is an idolater, hath no inheritance in the reign of the Christ and God.

v6 Let no one deceive you with vain words, for because of these things cometh the anger of God upon the sons of the disobedience,

v7 become not, then, partakers with them,

v8 for ye were once darkness, and now light in the Lord; as children of light walk ye,

v9 for the fruit of the Spirit [is] in all goodness, and righteousness, and truth,

v10 proving what is well-pleasing to the Lord,

v11 and have no fellowship with the unfruitful works of the darkness and rather even convict,

v12 for the things in secret done by them it is a shame even to speak of,

v13 and all the things reproved by the light are manifested, for everything that is manifested is light;

v14 wherefore he saith, 'Arouse thyself, thou who art sleeping, and arise out of the dead, and the Christ shall shine upon thee.'

v15 See, then, how exactly ye walk, not as unwise, but as wise,

v16 redeeming the time, because the days are evil;

v17 because of this become not fools, but -- understanding what [is] the will of the Lord,

v18 and be not drunk with wine, in which is dissoluteness, but be filled in the Spirit,

v19 speaking to yourselves in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing and making melody in your heart to the Lord,

v20 giving thanks always for all things, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, to the God and Father;

v21 subjecting yourselves to one another in the fear of God.

v22 The wives! to your own husbands subject yourselves, as to the Lord,

v23 because the husband is head of the wife, as also the Christ [is] head of the assembly, and he is saviour of the body,

v24 but even as the assembly is subject to Christ, so also [are] the wives to their own husbands in everything.

v25 The husbands! love your own wives, as also the Christ did love the assembly, and did give himself for it,

v26 that he might sanctify it, having cleansed [it] with the bathing of the water in the saying,

v27 that he might present it to himself the assembly in glory, not having spot or wrinkle, or any of such things, but that it may be holy and unblemished;

v28 so ought the husbands to love their own wives as their own bodies: he who is loving his own wife -- himself he doth love;

v29 for no one ever his own flesh did hate, but doth nourish and cherish it, as also the Lord -- the assembly,

v30 because members we are of his body, of his flesh, and of his bones;

v31 'for this cause shall a man leave his father and mother, and shall be joined to his wife, and they shall be -- the two -- for one flesh;'

v32 this secret is great, and I speak in regard to Christ and to the assembly;

v33 but ye also, every one in particular -- let each his own wife so love as himself, and the wife -- that she may reverence the husband.
