# Jeremiah 45 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 44|← Jeremiah 44]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 46|Jeremiah 46 →]]

***

v1 The word that Jeremiah the prophet hath spoken unto Baruch son of Neriah, in his writing these words on a book from the mouth of Jeremiah, in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah king of Judah, saying:

v2 'Thus said Jehovah, God of Israel, concerning thee, O Baruch:

v3 'Thou hast said, Wo to me, now, for Jehovah hath added sorrow to my pain, I have been wearied with my sighing, and rest I have not found.

v4 Thus dost thou say unto him: Thus said Jehovah: Lo, that which I have built I am throwing down, and that which I have planted I am plucking up, even the whole land itself.

v5 And thou -- thou seekest for thee great things -- do not seek, for lo, I am bringing in evil on all flesh -- an affirmation of Jehovah -- and I have given to thee thy life for a spoil, in all places whither thou goest.'
