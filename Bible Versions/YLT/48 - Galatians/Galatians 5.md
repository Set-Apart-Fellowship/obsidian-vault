# Galatians 5 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/48 - Galatians/Galatians 4|← Galatians 4]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/48 - Galatians/Galatians]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/48 - Galatians/Galatians 6|Galatians 6 →]]

***

v1 In the freedom, then, with which Christ did make you free -- stand ye, and be not held fast again by a yoke of servitude;

v2 lo, I Paul do say to you, that if ye be circumcised, Christ shall profit you nothing;

v3 and I testify again to every man circumcised, that he is a debtor to do the whole law;

v4 ye were freed from the Christ, ye who in law are declared righteous; from the grace ye fell away;

v5 for we by the Spirit, by faith, a hope of righteousness do wait for,

v6 for in Christ Jesus neither circumcision availeth anything, nor uncircumcision, but faith through love working.

v7 Ye were running well; who did hinder you -- not to obey the truth?

v8 the obedience [is] not of him who is calling you!

v9 a little leaven the whole lump doth leaven;

v10 I have confidence in regard to you in the Lord, that ye will be none otherwise minded; and he who is troubling you shall bear the judgment, whoever he may be.

v11 And I, brethren, if uncircumcision I yet preach, why yet am I persecuted? then hath the stumbling-block of the cross been done away;

v12 O that even they would cut themselves off who are unsettling you!

v13 For ye -- to freedom ye were called, brethren, only not the freedom for an occasion to the flesh, but through the love serve ye one another,

v14 for all the law in one word is fulfilled -- in this: 'Thou shalt love thy neighbor as thyself;'

v15 and if one another ye do bite and devour, see -- that ye may not by one another be consumed.

v16 And I say: In the Spirit walk ye, and the desire of the flesh ye may not complete;

v17 for the flesh doth desire contrary to the Spirit, and the Spirit contrary to the flesh, and these are opposed one to another, that the things that ye may will -- these ye may not do;

v18 and if by the Spirit ye are led, ye are not under law.

v19 And manifest also are the works of the flesh, which are: Adultery, whoredom, uncleanness, lasciviousness,

v20 idolatry, witchcraft, hatred, strifes, emulations, wraths, rivalries, dissensions, sects,

v21 envyings, murders, drunkennesses, revellings, and such like, of which I tell you before, as I also said before, that those doing such things the reign of God shall not inherit.

v22 And the fruit of the Spirit is: Love, joy, peace, long-suffering, kindness, goodness, faith,

v23 meekness, temperance: against such there is no law;

v24 and those who are Christ's, the flesh did crucify with the affections, and the desires;

v25 if we may live in the Spirit, in the Spirit also we may walk;

v26 let us not become vain-glorious -- one another provoking, one another envying!
