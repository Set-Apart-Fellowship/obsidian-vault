# Job 15 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 14|← Job 14]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 16|Job 16 →]]

***

v1 And Eliphaz the Temanite answereth and saith: --

v2 Doth a wise man answer [with] vain knowledge? And fill [with] an east wind his belly?

v3 To reason with a word not useful? And speeches -- no profit in them?

v4 Yea, thou dost make reverence void, And dost diminish meditation before God.

v5 For thy mouth teacheth thine iniquity, And thou chooseth the tongue of the subtile.

v6 Thy mouth declareth thee wicked, and not I, And thy lips testify against thee.

v7 The first man art thou born? And before the heights wast thou formed?

v8 Of the secret counsel of God dost thou hear? And withdrawest thou unto thee wisdom?

v9 What hast thou known, and we know not? Understandest thou -- and it is not with us?

v10 Both the gray-headed And the very aged [are] among us -- Greater than thy father [in] days.

v11 Too few for thee are the comforts of God? And a gentle word [is] with thee,

v12 What -- doth thine heart take thee away? And what -- are thine eyes high?

v13 For thou turnest against God thy spirit? And hast brought out words from thy mouth:

v14 What [is] man that he is pure, And that he is righteous, one born of woman?

v15 Lo, in His holy ones He putteth no credence, And the heavens have not been pure in His eyes.

v16 Also -- surely abominable and filthy Is man drinking as water perverseness.

v17 I shew thee -- hearken to me -- And this I have seen and declare:

v18 Which the wise declare -- And have not hid -- from their fathers.

v19 To them alone was the land given, And a stranger passed not over into their midst:

v20 'All days of the wicked he is paining himself, And few years have been laid up for the terrible one.

v21 A fearful voice [is] in his ears, In peace doth a destroyer come to him.

v22 He believeth not to return from darkness, And watched [is] he for the sword.

v23 He is wandering for bread -- 'Where [is] it?' He hath known that ready at his hand Is a day of darkness.

v24 Terrify him do adversity and distress, They prevail over him As a king ready for a boaster.

v25 For he stretched out against God his hand, And against the Mighty he maketh himself mighty.

v26 He runneth unto Him with a neck, With thick bosses of his shields.

v27 For he hath covered his face with his fat, And maketh vigour over [his] confidence.

v28 And he inhabiteth cities cut off, houses not dwelt in, That have been ready to become heaps.

v29 He is not rich, nor doth his wealth rise, Nor doth he stretch out on earth their continuance.

v30 He turneth not aside from darkness, His tender branch doth a flame dry up, And he turneth aside at the breath of His mouth!

v31 Let him not put credence in vanity, He hath been deceived, For vanity is his recompence.

v32 Not in his day is it completed, And his bending branch is not green.

v33 He shaketh off as a vine his unripe fruit, And casteth off as an olive his blossom.

v34 For the company of the profane [is] gloomy, And fire hath consumed tents of bribery.

v35 To conceive misery, and to bear iniquity, Even their heart doth prepare deceit.
