# Job 18 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 17|← Job 17]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 19|Job 19 →]]

***

v1 And Bildad the Shuhite answereth and saith: --

v2 When do ye set an end to words? Consider ye, and afterwards do we speak.

v3 Wherefore have we been reckoned as cattle? We have been defiled in your eyes!

v4 (He is tearing himself in his anger.) For thy sake is earth forsaken? And removed is a rock from its place?

v5 Also, the light of the wicked is extinguished. And there doth not shine a spark of his fire.

v6 The light hath been dark in his tent, And his lamp over him is extinguished.

v7 Straitened are the steps of his strength, And cast him down doth his own counsel.

v8 For he is sent into a net by his own feet, And on a snare he doth walk habitually.

v9 Seize on the heel doth a gin, Prevail over him do the designing.

v10 Hidden in the earth is his cord, And his trap on the path.

v11 Round about terrified him have terrors, And they have scattered him -- at his feet.

v12 Hungry is his sorrow, And calamity is ready at his side.

v13 It consumeth the parts of his skin, Consume his parts doth death's first-born.

v14 Drawn from his tent is his confidence, And it causeth him to step to the king of terrors.

v15 It dwelleth in his tent -- out of his provender, Scattered over his habitation is sulphur.

v16 From beneath his roots are dried up, And from above cut off is his crop.

v17 His memorial hath perished from the land, And he hath no name on the street.

v18 They thrust him from light unto darkness, And from the habitable earth cast him out.

v19 He hath no continuator, Nor successor among his people, And none is remaining in his dwellings.

v20 At this day westerns have been astonished And easterns have taken fright.

v21 Only these [are] tabernacles of the perverse, And this the place God hath not known.
