# Job 19 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 18|← Job 18]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 20|Job 20 →]]

***

v1 And Job answereth and saith: --

v2 Till when do ye afflict my soul, And bruise me with words?

v3 These ten times ye put me to shame, ye blush not. Ye make yourselves strange to me --

v4 And also -- truly, I have erred, With me doth my error remain.

v5 If, truly, over me ye magnify yourselves, And decide against me my reproach;

v6 Know now, that God turned me upside down, And His net against me hath set round,

v7 Lo, I cry out -- violence, and am not answered, I cry aloud, and there is no judgment.

v8 My way He hedged up, and I pass not over, And on my paths darkness He placeth.

v9 Mine honour from off me He hath stripped, And He turneth the crown from my head.

v10 He breaketh me down round about, and I go, And removeth like a tree my hope.

v11 And He kindleth against me His anger, And reckoneth me to Him as His adversaries.

v12 Come in do His troops together, And they raise up against me their way, And encamp round about my tent.

v13 My brethren from me He hath put far off, And mine acquaintances surely Have been estranged from me.

v14 Ceased have my neighbours And my familiar friends have forgotten me,

v15 Sojourners of my house and my maids, For a stranger reckon me: An alien I have been in their eyes.

v16 To my servant I have called, And he doth not answer, With my mouth I make supplication to him.

v17 My spirit is strange to my wife, And my favours to the sons of my [mother's] womb.

v18 Also sucklings have despised me, I rise, and they speak against me.

v19 Abominate me do all the men of my counsel, And those I have loved, Have been turned against me.

v20 To my skin and to my flesh Cleaved hath my bone, And I deliver myself with the skin of my teeth.

v21 Pity me, pity me, ye my friends, For the hand of God hath stricken against me.

v22 Why do you pursue me as God? And with my flesh are not satisfied?

v23 Who doth grant now, That my words may be written? Who doth grant that in a book they may be graven?

v24 With a pen of iron and lead -- For ever in a rock they may be hewn.

v25 That -- I have known my Redeemer, The Living and the Last, For the dust he doth rise.

v26 And after my skin hath compassed this [body], Then from my flesh I see God:

v27 Whom I -- I see on my side, And mine eyes have beheld, and not a stranger, Consumed have been my reins in my bosom.

v28 But ye say, 'Why do we pursue after him?' And the root of the matter hath been found in me.

v29 Be ye afraid because of the sword, For furious [are] the punishments of the sword, That ye may know that [there is] a judgment.
