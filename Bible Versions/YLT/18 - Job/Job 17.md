# Job 17 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 16|← Job 16]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 18|Job 18 →]]

***

v1 My spirit hath been destroyed, My days extinguished -- graves [are] for me.

v2 If not -- mockeries [are] with me. And in their provocations mine eye lodgeth.

v3 Place, I pray Thee, my pledge with Thee; Who is he that striketh hand with me?

v4 For their heart Thou hast hidden From understanding, Therefore Thou dost not exalt them.

v5 For a portion he sheweth friendship, And the eyes of his sons are consumed.

v6 And he set me up for a proverb of the peoples, And a wonder before them I am.

v7 And dim from sorrow is mine eye, And my members as a shadow all of them.

v8 Astonished are the upright at this, And the innocent against the profane Stirreth himself up.

v9 And the righteous layeth hold [on] his way, And the clean of hands addeth strength, And -- dumb are they all.

v10 Return, and come in, I pray you, And I find not among you a wise man.

v11 My days have passed by, My devices have been broken off, The possessions of my heart!

v12 Night for day they appoint, Light [is] near because of darkness.

v13 If I wait -- Sheol [is] my house, In darkness I have spread out my couch.

v14 To corruption I have called: -- 'Thou [art] my father.' 'My mother' and 'my sister' -- to the worm.

v15 And where [is] now my hope? Yea, my hope, who doth behold it?

v16 [To] the parts of Sheol ye go down, If together on the dust we may rest.
