# Job 35 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 34|← Job 34]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 36|Job 36 →]]

***

v1 And Elihu answereth and saith: --

v2 This hast thou reckoned for judgment: Thou hast said -- 'My righteousness [is] more than God's?'

v3 For thou sayest, 'What doth it profit Thee! What do I profit from my sin?'

v4 I return thee words, and thy friends with thee,

v5 Behold attentively the heavens -- and see, And behold the clouds, They have been higher than thou.

v6 If thou hast sinned, what dost thou against Him? And thy transgressions have been multiplied, What dost thou to Him?

v7 If thou hast been righteous, What dost thou give to Him? Or what from thy hand doth He receive?

v8 For a man like thyself [is] thy wickedness, And for a son of man thy righteousness.

v9 Because of the multitude of oppressions They cause to cry out, They cry because of the arm of the mighty.

v10 And none said, 'Where [is] God my maker? Giving songs in the night,

v11 Teaching us more than the beasts of the earth, Yea, than the fowl of the heavens He maketh us wiser.'

v12 There they cry, and He doth not answer, Because of the pride of evil doers.

v13 Surely vanity God doth not hear, And the Mighty doth not behold it.

v14 Yea, though thou sayest thou dost not behold Him, Judgment [is] before Him, and stay for Him.

v15 And, now, because there is not, He hath appointed His anger, And He hath not known in great extremity.

v16 And Job [with] vanity doth open his mouth, Without knowledge words he multiplieth.
