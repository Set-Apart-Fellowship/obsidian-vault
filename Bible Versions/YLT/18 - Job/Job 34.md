# Job 34 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 33|← Job 33]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 35|Job 35 →]]

***

v1 And Elihu answereth and saith:

v2 Hear, O wise men, my words, And, O knowing ones, give ear to me.

v3 For the ear doth try words, And the palate tasteth to eat.

v4 Judgment let us choose for ourselves, Let us know among ourselves what [is] good.

v5 For Job hath said, 'I have been righteous, And God hath turned aside my right,

v6 Against my right do I lie? Mortal [is] mine arrow -- without transgression.'

v7 Who [is] a man like Job? He drinketh scoffing like water,

v8 And he hath travelled for company With workers of iniquity, So as to go with men of wickedness.

v9 For he hath said, 'It doth not profit a man, When he delighteth himself with God.'

v10 Therefore, O men of heart, hearken to me; Far be it from God to do wickedness, And [from] the Mighty to do perverseness:

v11 For the work of man he repayeth to him, And according to the path of each He doth cause him to find.

v12 Yea, truly, God doth not do wickedly, And the Mighty doth not pervert judgment.

v13 Who hath inspected for Himself the earth? And who hath placed all the habitable world?

v14 If He doth set on him His heart, His spirit and his breath unto Him He gathereth.

v15 Expire doth all flesh together, And man to dust returneth.

v16 And if [there is] understanding, hear this, Give ear to the voice of my words.

v17 Yea, doth one hating justice govern? Or the Most Just dost thou condemn?

v18 Who hath said to a king -- 'Worthless,' Unto princes -- 'Wicked?'

v19 That hath not accepted the person of princes, Nor hath known the rich before the poor, For a work of His hands [are] all of them.

v20 [In] a moment they die, and at midnight Shake do people, and they pass away, And they remove the mighty without hand.

v21 For His eyes [are] on the ways of each, And all his steps He doth see.

v22 There is no darkness nor death-shade, For workers of iniquity to be hidden there;

v23 For He doth not suffer man any more, To go unto God in judgment,

v24 He breaketh the mighty -- no searching! And He appointeth others in their stead.

v25 Therefore He knoweth their works, And He hath overturned by night, And they are bruised.

v26 As wicked He hath stricken them, In the place of beholders.

v27 Because that against right They have turned aside from after Him, And none of His ways have considered wisely,

v28 To cause to come in unto Him The cry of the poor, And the cry of the afflicted He heareth.

v29 And He giveth rest, and who maketh wrong? And hideth the face, and who beholdeth it? And in reference to a nation and to a man, [It is] the same.

v30 From the reigning of a profane man, From the snares of a people;

v31 For unto God hath any said: 'I have taken away, I do not corruptly,

v32 Besides [that which] I see, shew Thou me, If iniquity I have done -- I do not add?'

v33 By thee doth He recompense, That thou hast refused -- That thou dost choose, and not I? And what thou hast known, speak.

v34 Let men of heart say to me, And a wise man is hearkening to me.

v35 Job -- not with knowledge doth he speak, And his words [are] not with wisdom.

v36 My Father! let Job be tried -- unto victory, Because of answers for men of iniquity,

v37 For he doth add to his sin, Transgression among us he vomiteth, And multiplieth his sayings to God.
