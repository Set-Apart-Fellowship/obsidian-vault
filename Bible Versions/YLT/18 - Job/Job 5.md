# Job 5 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 4|← Job 4]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 6|Job 6 →]]

***

v1 Pray, call, is there any to answer thee? And unto which of the holy ones dost thou turn?

v2 For provocation slayeth the perverse, And envy putteth to death the simple,

v3 I -- I have seen the perverse taking root, And I mark his habitation straightway,

v4 Far are his sons from safety, And they are bruised in the gate, And there is no deliverer.

v5 Whose harvest the hungry doth eat, And even from the thorns taketh it, And the designing swallowed their wealth.

v6 For sorrow cometh not forth from the dust, Nor from the ground springeth up misery.

v7 For man to misery is born, And the sparks go high to fly.

v8 Yet I -- I inquire for God, And for God I give my word,

v9 Doing great things, and there is no searching. Wonderful, till there is no numbering.

v10 Who is giving rain on the face of the land, And is sending waters on the out-places.

v11 To set the low on a high place, And the mourners have been high [in] safety.

v12 Making void thoughts of the subtile, And their hands do not execute wisdom.

v13 Capturing the wise in their subtilty, And the counsel of wrestling ones was hastened,

v14 By day they meet darkness, And as night -- they grope at noon.

v15 And He saveth the wasted from their mouth, And from a strong hand the needy,

v16 And there is hope to the poor, And perverseness hath shut her mouth.

v17 Lo, the happiness of mortal man, God doth reprove him: And the chastisement of the Mighty despise not,

v18 For He doth pain, and He bindeth up, He smiteth, and His hands heal.

v19 In six distresses He delivereth thee, And in seven evil striketh not on thee.

v20 In famine He hath redeemed thee from death, And in battle from the hands of the sword.

v21 When the tongue scourgeth thou art hid, And thou art not afraid of destruction, When it cometh.

v22 At destruction and at hunger thou mockest, And of the beast of the earth, Thou art not afraid.

v23 (For with sons of the field [is] thy covenant, And the beast of the field Hath been at peace with thee.)

v24 And thou hast known that thy tent [is] peace, And inspected thy habitation, and errest not,

v25 And hast known that numerous [is] Thy seed, And thine offspring as the herb of the earth;

v26 Thou comest in full age unto the grave, As the going up of a stalk in its season.

v27 Lo, this -- we searched it out -- it [is] right, hearken; And thou, know for thyself!
