# Job 25 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 24|← Job 24]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 26|Job 26 →]]

***

v1 And Bildad the Shuhite answereth and saith: --

v2 The rule and fear [are] with Him, Making peace in His high places.

v3 Is their [any] number to His troops? And on whom ariseth not His light?

v4 And what? is man righteous with God? And what? is he pure -- born of a woman?

v5 Lo -- unto the moon, and it shineth not, And stars have not been pure in His eyes.

v6 How much less man -- a grub, And the son of man -- a worm!
