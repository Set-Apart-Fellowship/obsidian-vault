# Job 36 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 35|← Job 35]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 37|Job 37 →]]

***

v1 And Elihu addeth and saith: --

v2 Honour me a little, and I shew thee, That yet for God [are] words.

v3 I lift up my knowledge from afar, And to my Maker I ascribe righteousness.

v4 For, truly, my words [are] not false, The perfect in knowledge [is] with thee.

v5 Lo, God [is] mighty, and despiseth not, Mighty [in] power [and] heart.

v6 He reviveth not the wicked, And the judgment of the poor appointeth;

v7 He withdraweth not from the righteous His eyes, And [from] kings on the throne, And causeth them to sit for ever, and they are high,

v8 And if prisoners in fetters They are captured with cords of affliction,

v9 Then He declareth to them their work, And their transgressions, Because they have become mighty,

v10 And He uncovereth their ear for instruction, And saith that they turn back from iniquity.

v11 If they do hear and serve, They complete their days in good, And their years in pleasantness.

v12 And if they do not hearken, By the dart they pass away, And expire without knowledge.

v13 And the profane in heart set the face, They cry not when He hath bound them.

v14 Their soul dieth in youth, And their life among the defiled.

v15 He draweth out the afflicted in his affliction, And uncovereth in oppression their ear.

v16 And also He moved thee from a strait place, [To] a broad place -- no straitness under it, And the sitting beyond of thy table Hath been full of fatness.

v17 And the judgment of the wicked thou hast fulfilled, Judgment and justice are upheld -- because of fury,

v18 Lest He move thee with a stroke, And the abundance of an atonement turn thee not aside.

v19 Doth He value thy riches? He hath gold, and all the forces of power.

v20 Desire not the night, For the going up of peoples in their stead.

v21 Take heed -- do not turn unto iniquity, For on this thou hast fixed Rather than [on] affliction.

v22 Lo, God doth sit on high by His power, Who [is] like Him -- a teacher?

v23 Who hath appointed unto Him his way? And who said, 'Thou hast done iniquity?'

v24 Remember that thou magnify His work That men have beheld.

v25 All men have looked on it, Man looketh attentively from afar.

v26 Lo, God [is] high, And we know not the number of His years, Yea, there [is] no searching.

v27 When He doth diminish droppings of the waters, They refine rain according to its vapour,

v28 Which clouds do drop, They distil on man abundantly.

v29 Yea, doth [any] understand The spreadings out of a cloud? The noises of His tabernacle?

v30 Lo, He hath spread over it His light, And the roots of the sea He hath covered,

v31 For by them He doth judge peoples, He giveth food in abundance.

v32 By two palms He hath covered the light, And layeth a charge over it in meeting,

v33 He sheweth by it [to] his friend substance, Anger against perversity.
