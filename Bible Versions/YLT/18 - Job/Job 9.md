# Job 9 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 8|← Job 8]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 10|Job 10 →]]

***

v1 And Job answereth and saith: --

v2 Truly I have known that [it is] so, And what -- is man righteous with God?

v3 If he delight to strive with Him -- He doth not answer him one of a thousand.

v4 Wise in heart and strong in power -- Who hath hardened toward Him and is at peace?

v5 Who is removing mountains, And they have not known, Who hath overturned them in His anger.

v6 Who is shaking earth from its place, And its pillars move themselves.

v7 Who is speaking to the sun, and it riseth not, And the stars He sealeth up.

v8 Stretching out the heavens by Himself, And treading on the heights of the sea,

v9 Making Osh, Kesil, and Kimah, And the inner chambers of the south.

v10 Doing great things till there is no searching, And wonderful, till there is no numbering.

v11 Lo, He goeth over by me, and I see not, And He passeth on, and I attend not to it.

v12 Lo, He snatches away, who bringeth it back? Who saith unto Him, 'What dost Thou?'

v13 God doth not turn back His anger, Under Him bowed have proud helpers.

v14 How much less do I -- I answer Him? Choose out my words with Him?

v15 Whom, though I were righteous, I answer not, For my judgment I make supplication.

v16 Though I had called and He answereth me, I do not believe that He giveth ear [to] my voice.

v17 Because with a tempest He bruiseth me, And hath multiplied my wounds for nought.

v18 He permitteth me not to refresh my spirit, But filleth me with bitter things.

v19 If of power, lo, the Strong One; And if of judgment -- who doth convene me?

v20 If I be righteous, Mine mouth doth declare me wicked, Perfect I am! -- it declareth me perverse.

v21 Perfect I am! -- I know not my soul, I despise my life.

v22 It is the same thing, therefore I said, 'The perfect and the wicked He is consuming.'

v23 If a scourge doth put to death suddenly, At the trial of the innocent He laugheth.

v24 Earth hath been given Into the hand of the wicked one. The face of its judges he covereth, If not -- where, who [is] he?

v25 My days have been swifter than a runner, They have fled, they have not seen good,

v26 They have passed on with ships of reed, As an eagle darteth on food.

v27 Though I say, 'I forget my talking, I forsake my corner, and I brighten up!'

v28 I have been afraid of all my griefs, I have known that Thou dost not acquit me.

v29 I -- I am become wicked; why [is] this? [In] vain I labour.

v30 If I have washed myself with snow-water, And purified with soap my hands,

v31 Then in corruption Thou dost dip me, And my garments have abominated me.

v32 But if a man like myself -- I answer him, We come together into judgment.

v33 If there were between us an umpire, He doth place his hand on us both.

v34 He doth turn aside from off me his rod, And His terror doth not make me afraid,

v35 I speak, and do not fear Him, But I am not right with myself.
