# Job 6 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 5|← Job 5]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 7|Job 7 →]]

***

v1 And Job answereth and saith: --

v2 O that my provocation were thoroughly weighed, And my calamity in balances They would lift up together!

v3 For now, than the sands of the sea it is heavier, Therefore my words have been rash.

v4 For arrows of the Mighty [are] with me, Whose poison is drinking up my spirit. Terrors of God array themselves [for] me!

v5 Brayeth a wild ass over tender grass? Loweth an ox over his provender?

v6 Eaten is an insipid thing without salt? Is there sense in the drivel of dreams?

v7 My soul is refusing to touch! They [are] as my sickening food.

v8 O that my request may come, That God may grant my hope!

v9 That God would please -- and bruise me, Loose His hand and cut me off!

v10 And yet it is my comfort, (And I exult in pain -- He doth not spare,) That I have not hidden The sayings of the Holy One.

v11 What [is] my power that I should hope? And what mine end That I should prolong my life?

v12 Is my strength the strength of stones? Is my flesh brazen?

v13 Is not my help with me, And substance driven from me?

v14 To a despiser of his friends [is] shame, And the fear of the Mighty he forsaketh.

v15 My brethren have deceived as a brook, As a stream of brooks they pass away.

v16 That are black because of ice, By them doth snow hide itself.

v17 By the time they are warm they have been cut off, By its being hot they have been Extinguished from their place.

v18 Turn aside do the paths of their way, They ascend into emptiness, and are lost.

v19 Passengers of Tema looked expectingly, Travellers of Sheba hoped for them.

v20 They were ashamed that one hath trusted, They have come unto it and are confounded.

v21 Surely now ye have become the same! Ye see a downfall, and are afraid.

v22 Is it because I said, Give to me? And, By your power bribe for me?

v23 And, Deliver me from the hand of an adversary? And, From the hand of terrible ones ransom me?

v24 Shew me, and I -- I keep silent, And what I have erred, let me understand.

v25 How powerful have been upright sayings, And what doth reproof from you reprove?

v26 For reproof -- do you reckon words? And for wind -- sayings of the desperate.

v27 Anger on the fatherless ye cause to fall, And are strange to your friend.

v28 And, now, please, look upon me, Even to your face do I lie?

v29 Turn back, I pray you, let it not be perverseness, Yea, turn back again -- my righteousness [is] in it.

v30 Is there in my tongue perverseness? Discerneth not my palate desirable things?
