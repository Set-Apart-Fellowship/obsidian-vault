# Job 4 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 3|← Job 3]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 5|Job 5 →]]

***

v1 And Eliphaz the Temanite answereth and saith: --

v2 Hath one tried a word with thee? -- Thou art weary! And to keep in words who is able?

v3 Lo, thou hast instructed many, And feeble hands thou makest strong.

v4 The stumbling one do thy words raise up, And bowing knees thou dost strengthen.

v5 But now, it cometh in unto thee, And thou art weary; It striketh unto thee, and thou art troubled.

v6 Is not thy reverence thy confidence? Thy hope -- the perfection of thy ways?

v7 Remember, I pray thee, Who, being innocent, hath perished? And where have the upright been cut off?

v8 As I have seen -- ploughers of iniquity, And sowers of misery, reap it!

v9 From the breath of God they perish, And from the spirit of His anger consumed.

v10 The roaring of a lion, And the voice of a fierce lion, And teeth of young lions have been broken.

v11 An old lion is perishing without prey, And the whelps of the lioness do separate.

v12 And unto me a thing is secretly brought, And receive doth mine ear a little of it.

v13 In thoughts from visions of the night, In the falling of deep sleep on men,

v14 Fear hath met me, and trembling, And the multitude of my bones caused to fear.

v15 And a spirit before my face doth pass, Stand up doth the hair of my flesh;

v16 It standeth, and I discern not its aspect, A similitude [is] over-against mine eyes, Silence! and a voice I hear:

v17 'Is mortal man than God more righteous? Than his Maker is a man cleaner?

v18 Lo, in His servants He putteth no credence, Nor in His messengers setteth praise.'

v19 Also -- the inhabitants of houses of clay, (Whose foundation [is] in the dust, They bruise them before a moth.)

v20 From morning to evening are beaten down, Without any regarding, for ever they perish.

v21 Hath not their excellency been removed with them? They die, and not in wisdom!
