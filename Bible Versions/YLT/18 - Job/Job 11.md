# Job 11 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 10|← Job 10]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 12|Job 12 →]]

***

v1 And Zophar the Naamathite answereth and saith: --

v2 Is a multitude of words not answered? And is a man of lips justified?

v3 Thy devices make men keep silent, Thou scornest, and none is causing blushing!

v4 And thou sayest, 'Pure [is] my discourse, And clean I have been in Thine eyes.'

v5 And yet, O that God had spoken! And doth open His lips with thee.

v6 And declare to thee secrets of wisdom, For counsel hath foldings. And know thou that God forgetteth for thee, [Some] of thine iniquity.

v7 By searching dost thou find out God? Unto perfection find out the Mighty One?

v8 Heights of the heavens! -- what dost thou? Deeper than Sheol! -- what knowest thou?

v9 Longer than earth [is] its measure, And broader than the sea.

v10 If He pass on, and shut up, and assemble, Who then dost reverse it?

v11 For he hath known men of vanity, And He seeth iniquity, And one doth not consider [it]!

v12 And empty man is bold, And the colt of a wild ass man is born.

v13 If thou -- thou hast prepared thy heart, And hast spread out unto Him thy hands,

v14 If iniquity [is] in thy hand, put it far off, And let not perverseness dwell in thy tents.

v15 For then thou liftest up thy face from blemish, And thou hast been firm, and fearest not.

v16 For thou dost forget misery, As waters passed away thou rememberest.

v17 And above the noon doth age rise, Thou fliest -- as the morning thou art.

v18 And thou hast trusted because their is hope, And searched -- in confidence thou liest down,

v19 And thou hast rested, And none is causing trembling, And many have entreated thy face;

v20 And the eyes of the wicked are consumed, And refuge hath perished from them, And their hope [is] a breathing out of soul!
