# Job 38 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 37|← Job 37]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 39|Job 39 →]]

***

v1 And Jehovah answereth Job out of the whirlwind, and saith: --

v2 Who [is] this -- darkening counsel, By words without knowledge?

v3 Gird, I pray thee, as a man, thy loins, And I ask thee, and cause thou Me to know.

v4 Where wast thou when I founded earth? Declare, if thou hast known understanding.

v5 Who placed its measures -- if thou knowest? Or who hath stretched out upon it a line?

v6 On what have its sockets been sunk? Or who hath cast its corner-stone?

v7 In the singing together of stars of morning, And all sons of God shout for joy,

v8 And He shutteth up with doors the sea, In its coming forth, from the womb it goeth out.

v9 In My making a cloud its clothing, And thick darkness its swaddling band,

v10 And I measure over it My statute, And place bar and doors,

v11 And say, 'Hitherto come thou, and add not, And a command is placed On the pride of thy billows.'

v12 Hast thou commanded morning since thy days? Causest thou the dawn to know its place?

v13 To take hold on the skirts of the earth, And the wicked are shaken out of it,

v14 It turneth itself as clay of a seal And they station themselves as clothed.

v15 And withheld from the wicked is their light, And the arm lifted up is broken.

v16 Hast thou come in to springs of the sea? And in searching the deep Hast thou walked up and down?

v17 Revealed to thee were the gates of death? And the gates of death-shade dost thou see?

v18 Thou hast understanding, Even unto the broad places of earth! Declare -- if thou hast known it all.

v19 Where [is] this -- the way light dwelleth? And darkness, where [is] this -- its place?

v20 That thou dost take it unto its boundary, And that thou dost understand The paths of its house.

v21 Thou hast known -- for then thou art born And the number of thy days [are] many!

v22 Hast thou come in unto the treasure of snow? Yea, the treasures of hail dost thou see?

v23 That I have kept back for a time of distress, For a day of conflict and battle.

v24 Where [is] this, the way light is apportioned? It scattereth an east wind over the earth.

v25 Who hath divided for the flood a conduit? And a way for the lightning of the voices?

v26 To cause [it] to rain on a land -- no man, A wilderness -- no man in it.

v27 To satisfy a desolate and waste place, And to cause to shoot up The produce of the tender grass?

v28 Hath the rain a father? Or who hath begotten the drops of dew?

v29 From whose belly came forth the ice? And the hoar-frost of the heavens, Who hath begotten it?

v30 As a stone waters are hidden, And the face of the deep is captured.

v31 Dost thou bind sweet influences of Kimah? Or the attractions of Kesil dost thou open?

v32 Dost thou bring out Mazzaroth in its season? And Aysh for her sons dost thou comfort?

v33 Hast thou known the statutes of heaven? Or dost thou appoint Its dominion in the earth?

v34 Dost thou lift up to the cloud thy voice, And abundance of water doth cover thee?

v35 Dost thou send out lightnings, and they go And say unto thee, 'Behold us?'

v36 Who hath put in the inward parts wisdom? Or who hath given To the covered part understanding?

v37 Who doth number the clouds by wisdom? And the bottles of the heavens, Who doth cause to lie down,

v38 In the hardening of dust into hardness, And clods cleave together?

v39 Dost thou hunt for a lion prey? And the desire of young lions fulfil?

v40 When they bow down in dens -- Abook_ide in a thicket for a covert?

v41 Who doth prepare for a raven his provision, When his young ones cry unto God? They wander without food.
