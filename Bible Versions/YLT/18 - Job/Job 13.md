# Job 13 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 12|← Job 12]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 14|Job 14 →]]

***

v1 Lo, all -- hath mine eye seen, Heard hath mine ear, and it attendeth to it.

v2 According to your knowledge I have known -- also I. I am not fallen more than you.

v3 Yet I for the Mighty One do speak, And to argue for God I delight.

v4 And yet, ye [are] forgers of falsehood, Physicians of nought -- all of you,

v5 O that ye would keep perfectly silent, And it would be to you for wisdom.

v6 Hear, I pray you, my argument, And to the pleadings of my lips attend,

v7 For God do ye speak perverseness? And for Him do ye speak deceit?

v8 His face do ye accept, if for God ye strive?

v9 Is [it] good that He doth search you, If, as one mocketh at a man, ye mock at Him?

v10 He doth surely reprove you, if in secret ye accept faces.

v11 Doth not His excellency terrify you? And His dread fall upon you?

v12 Your remembrances [are] similes of ashes, For high places of clay your heights.

v13 Keep silent from me, and I speak, And pass over me doth what?

v14 Wherefore do I take my flesh in my teeth? And my soul put in my hand?

v15 Lo, He doth slay me -- I wait not! Only, my ways unto His face I argue.

v16 Also -- He [is] to me for salvation, For the profane cometh not before Him.

v17 Hear ye diligently my word, And my declaration with your ears.

v18 Lo, I pray you, I have set in order the cause, I have known that I am righteous.

v19 Who [is] he that doth strive with me? For now I keep silent and gasp.

v20 Only two things, O God, do with me: Then from Thy face I am not hidden.

v21 Thy hand put far off from me, And Thy terror let not terrify me.

v22 And call Thou, and I -- I answer, Or -- I speak, and answer Thou me.

v23 How many iniquities and sins have I? My transgression and my sin let me know.

v24 Why dost Thou hide Thy face? And reckonest me for an enemy to Thee?

v25 A leaf driven away dost Thou terrify? And the dry stubble dost Thou pursue?

v26 For Thou writest against me bitter things, And causest me to possess iniquities of my youth:

v27 And puttest in the stocks my feet, And observest all my paths, On the roots of my feet Thou settest a print,

v28 And he, as a rotten thing, weareth away, As a garment hath a moth consumed him.
