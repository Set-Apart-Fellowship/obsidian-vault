# Job 3 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 2|← Job 2]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 4|Job 4 →]]

***

v1 After this hath Job opened his mouth, and revileth his day.

v2 And Job answereth and saith: --

v3 Let the day perish in which I am born, And the night that hath said: 'A man-child hath been conceived.'

v4 That day -- let it be darkness, Let not God require it from above, Nor let light shine upon it.

v5 Let darkness and death-shade redeem it, Let a cloud tabernacle upon it, Let them terrify it as the most bitter of days.

v6 That night -- let thick darkness take it, Let it not be united to days of the year, Into the number of months let it not come.

v7 Lo! that night -- let it be gloomy, Let no singing come into it.

v8 Let the cursers of day mark it, Who are ready to wake up Leviathan.

v9 Let the stars of its twilight be dark, Let it wait for light, and there is none, And let it not look on the eyelids of the dawn.

v10 Because it hath not shut the doors Of the womb that was mine! And hide misery from mine eyes.

v11 Why from the womb do I not die? From the belly I have come forth and gasp!

v12 Wherefore have knees been before me? And what [are] breasts, that I suck?

v13 For now, I have lain down, and am quiet, I have slept -- then there is rest to me,

v14 With kings and counsellors of earth, These building wastes for themselves.

v15 Or with princes -- they have gold, They are filling their houses [with] silver.

v16 (Or as a hidden abortion I am not, As infants -- they have not seen light.)

v17 There the wicked have ceased troubling, And there rest do the wearied in power.

v18 Together prisoners have been at ease, They have not heard the voice of an exactor,

v19 Small and great [are] there the same. And a servant [is] free from his lord.

v20 Why giveth He to the miserable light, and life to the bitter soul?

v21 Who are waiting for death, and it is not, And they seek it above hid treasures.

v22 Who are glad -- unto joy, They rejoice when they find a grave.

v23 To a man whose way hath been hidden, And whom God doth shut up?

v24 For before my food, my sighing cometh, And poured out as waters [are] my roarings.

v25 For a fear I feared and it meeteth me, And what I was afraid of doth come to me.

v26 I was not safe -- nor was I quiet -- Nor was I at rest -- and trouble cometh!
