# Job 31 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 30|← Job 30]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 32|Job 32 →]]

***

v1 A covenant I made for mine eyes, And what -- do I attend to a virgin?

v2 And what [is] the portion of God from above? And the inheritance of the Mighty from the heights?

v3 Is not calamity to the perverse? And strangeness to workers of iniquity?

v4 Doth not He see my ways, And all my steps number?

v5 If I have walked with vanity, And my foot doth hasten to deceit,

v6 He doth weigh me in righteous balances, And God doth know my integrity.

v7 If my step doth turn aside from the way, And after mine eyes hath my heart gone, And to my hands cleaved hath blemish,

v8 Let me sow -- and another eat, And my products let be rooted out.

v9 If my heart hath been enticed by woman, And by the opening of my neighbour I laid wait,

v10 Grind to another let my wife, And over her let others bend.

v11 For it [is] a wicked thing, and a judicial iniquity;

v12 For a fire it [is], to destruction it consumeth, And among all mine increase doth take root,

v13 If I despise the cause of my man-servant, And of my handmaid, In their contending with me,

v14 Then what do I do when God ariseth? And when He doth inspect, What do I answer Him?

v15 Did not He that made me in the womb make him? Yea, prepare us in the womb doth One.

v16 If I withhold from pleasure the poor, And the eyes of the widow do consume,

v17 And I do eat my morsel by myself, And the orphan hath not eat of it,

v18 (But from my youth He grew up with me as [with] a father, And from the belly of my mother I am led.)

v19 If I see [any] perishing without clothing, And there is no covering to the needy,

v20 If his loins have not blessed me, And from the fleece of my sheep He doth not warm himself,

v21 If I have waved at the fatherless my hand, When I see in [him] the gate of my court,

v22 My shoulder from its blade let fall, And mine arm from the bone be broken.

v23 For a dread unto me [is] calamity [from] God, And because of His excellency I am not able.

v24 If I have made gold my confidence, And to the pure gold have said, 'My trust,'

v25 If I rejoice because great [is] my wealth, And because abundance hath my hand found,

v26 If I see the light when it shineth, And the precious moon walking,

v27 And my heart is enticed in secret, And my hand doth kiss my mouth,

v28 It also [is] a judicial iniquity, For I had lied to God above.

v29 If I rejoice at the ruin of my hater, And stirred up myself when evil found him,

v30 Yea, I have not suffered my mouth to sin, To ask with an oath his life.

v31 If not -- say ye, O men of my tent, 'O that we had of his flesh, we are not satisfied.'

v32 In the street doth not lodge a stranger, My doors to the traveller I open.

v33 If I have covered as Adam my transgressions, To hide in my bosom mine iniquity,

v34 Because I fear a great multitude, And the contempt of families doth affright me, Then I am silent, I go not out of the opening.

v35 Who giveth to me a hearing? lo, my mark. The Mighty One doth answer me, And a bill hath mine adversary written.

v36 If not -- on my shoulder I take it up, I bind it a crown on myself.

v37 The number of my steps I tell Him, As a leader I approach Him.

v38 If against me my land doth cry out, And together its furrows weep,

v39 If its strength I consumed without money, And the life of its possessors, I have caused to breathe out,

v40 Instead of wheat let a thorn go forth, And instead of barley a useless weed! The words of Job are finished.
