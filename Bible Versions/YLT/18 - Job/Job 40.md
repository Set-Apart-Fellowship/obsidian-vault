# Job 40 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 39|← Job 39]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 41|Job 41 →]]

***

v1 And Jehovah doth answer Job, and saith: --

v2 Is the striver with the Mighty instructed? The reprover of God, let him answer it.

v3 And Job answereth Jehovah, and saith: --

v4 Lo, I have been vile, What do I return to Thee? My hand I have placed on my mouth.

v5 Once I have spoken, and I answer not, And twice, and I add not.

v6 And Jehovah answereth Job out of the whirlwind, and saith: --

v7 Gird, I pray thee, as a man, thy loins, I ask thee, and cause thou Me to know.

v8 Dost thou also make void My judgment? Dost thou condemn Me, That thou mayest be righteous?

v9 And an arm like God hast thou? And with a voice like Him dost thou thunder?

v10 Put on, I pray thee, excellency and loftiness, Yea, honour and beauty put on.

v11 Scatter abroad the wrath of thine anger, And see every proud one, and make him low.

v12 See every proud one -- humble him, And tread down the wicked in their place.

v13 Hide them in the dust together, Their faces bind in secret.

v14 And even I -- I do praise thee, For thy right hand giveth salvation to thee.

v15 Lo, I pray thee, Behemoth, that I made with thee: Grass as an ox he eateth.

v16 Lo, I pray thee, his power [is] in his loins, And his strength in the muscles of his belly.

v17 He doth bend his tail as a cedar, The sinews of his thighs are wrapped together,

v18 His bones [are] tubes of brass, His bones [are] as a bar of iron.

v19 He [is] a beginning of the ways of God, His Maker bringeth nigh his sword;

v20 For food do mountains bear for him, And all the beasts of the field play there.

v21 Under shades he lieth down, In a secret place of reed and mire.

v22 Cover him do shades, [with] their shadow, Cover him do willows of the brook.

v23 Lo, a flood oppresseth -- he doth not haste, He is confident though Jordan Doth come forth unto his mouth.

v24 Before his eyes doth [one] take him, With snares doth [one] pierce the nose?
