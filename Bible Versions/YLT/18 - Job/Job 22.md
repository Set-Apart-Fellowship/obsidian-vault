# Job 22 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 21|← Job 21]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 23|Job 23 →]]

***

v1 And Eliphaz the Temanite answereth and saith: --

v2 To God is a man profitable, Because a wise man to himself is profitable?

v3 Is it a delight to the Mighty One That thou art righteous? is it gain, That thou makest perfect thy ways?

v4 Because of thy reverence Doth He reason [with] thee? He entereth with thee into judgment:

v5 Is not thy wickedness abundant? And there is no end to thine iniquities.

v6 For thou takest a pledge of thy brother for nought, And the garments of the naked Thou dost strip off.

v7 Thou causest not the weary to drink water, And from the hungry thou withholdest bread.

v8 As to the man of arm -- he hath the earth, And the accepted of face -- he dwelleth in it.

v9 Widows thou hast sent away empty, And the arms of the fatherless are bruised.

v10 Therefore round about thee [are] snares, And trouble thee doth fear suddenly.

v11 Or darkness -- thou dost not see, And abundance of waters doth cover thee.

v12 Is not God high [in] heaven? And see the summit of the stars, That they are high.

v13 And thou hast said, 'What -- hath God known? Through thickness doth He judge?

v14 Thick clouds [are] a secret place to Him, And He doth not see;' And the circle of the heavens He walketh habitually,

v15 The path of the age dost thou observe, That men of iniquity have trodden?

v16 Who have been cut down unexpectedly, A flood is poured out on their foundation.

v17 Those saying to God, 'Turn aside from us,' And what doth the Mighty One to them?

v18 And he hath filled their houses [with] good: (And the counsel of the wicked Hath been far from me.)

v19 See do the righteous and they rejoice, And the innocent mocketh at them,

v20 'Surely our substance hath not been cut off, And their excellency hath fire consumed.'

v21 Acquaint thyself, I pray thee, with Him, And be at peace, Thereby thine increase [is] good.

v22 Receive, I pray thee, from His mouth a law, And set His sayings in thy heart.

v23 If thou dost return unto the Mighty Thou art built up, Thou puttest iniquity far from thy tents.

v24 So as to set on the dust a defence, And on a rock of the valleys a covering.

v25 And the Mighty hath been thy defence, And silver [is] strength to thee.

v26 For then on the Mighty thou delightest thyself, And dost lift up unto God thy face,

v27 Thou dost make supplication unto Him, And He doth hear thee, And thy vows thou completest.

v28 And thou decreest a saying, And it is established to thee, And on thy ways hath light shone.

v29 For they have made low, And thou sayest, 'Lift up.' And the bowed down of eyes he saveth.

v30 He delivereth the not innocent, Yea, he hath been delivered By the cleanness of thy hands.
