# Job 33 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 32|← Job 32]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 34|Job 34 →]]

***

v1 And yet, I pray thee, O Job, Hear my speech and [to] all my words give ear.

v2 Lo, I pray thee, I have opened my mouth, My tongue hath spoken in the palate.

v3 Of the uprightness of my heart [are] my sayings, And knowledge have my lips clearly spoken.

v4 The Spirit of God hath made me, And the breath of the Mighty doth quicken me.

v5 If thou art able -- answer me, Set in array before me -- station thyself.

v6 Lo, I [am], according to thy word, for God, From the clay I -- I also, have been formed.

v7 Lo, my terror doth not frighten thee, And my burden on thee is not heavy.

v8 Surely -- thou hast said in mine ears, And the sounds of words I hear:

v9 'Pure [am] I, without transgression, Innocent [am] I, and I have no iniquity.

v10 Lo, occasions against me He doth find, He doth reckon me for an enemy to Him,

v11 He doth put in the stocks my feet, He doth watch all my paths.'

v12 Lo, [in] this thou hast not been righteous, I answer thee, that greater is God than man.

v13 Wherefore against Him hast thou striven, When [for] all His matters He answereth not?

v14 For once doth God speak, and twice, (He doth not behold it.)

v15 In a dream -- a vision of night, In the falling of deep sleep on men, In slumberings on a bed.

v16 Then He uncovereth the ear of men, And for their instruction sealeth:

v17 To turn aside man [from] doing, And pride from man He concealeth.

v18 He keepeth back his soul from corruption, And his life from passing away by a dart.

v19 And he hath been reproved With pain on his bed, And the strife of his bones [is] enduring.

v20 And his life hath nauseated bread, And his soul desirable food.

v21 His flesh is consumed from being seen, And high are his bones, they were not seen!

v22 And draw near to the pit doth his soul, And his life to those causing death.

v23 If there is by him a messenger, An interpreter -- one of a thousand, To declare for man his uprightness:

v24 Then He doth favour him and saith, 'Ransom him from going down to the pit, I have found an atonement.'

v25 Fresher [is] his flesh than a child's, He returneth to the days of his youth.

v26 He maketh supplication unto God, And He accepteth him. And he seeth His face with shouting, And He returneth to man His righteousness.

v27 He looketh on men, and saith, 'I sinned, And uprightness I have perverted, And it hath not been profitable to me.

v28 He hath ransomed my soul From going over into the pit, And my life on the light looketh.'

v29 Lo, all these doth God work, Twice -- thrice with man,

v30 To bring back his soul from the pit, To be enlightened with the light of the living.

v31 Attend, O Job, hearken to me, Keep silent, and I -- I do speak.

v32 If there are words -- answer me, Speak, for I have a desire to justify thee.

v33 If there are not -- hearken thou to me, Keep silent, and I teach thee wisdom.
