# Job 10 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 9|← Job 9]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 11|Job 11 →]]

***

v1 My soul hath been weary of my life, I leave off my talking to myself, I speak in the bitterness of my soul.

v2 I say unto God, 'Do not condemn me, Let me know why Thou dost strive [with] me.

v3 Is it good for Thee that Thou dost oppress? That Thou despisest the labour of Thy hands, And on the counsel of the wicked hast shone?

v4 Eyes of flesh hast Thou? As man seeth -- seest Thou?

v5 As the days of man [are] Thy days? Thy years as the days of a man?

v6 That Thou inquirest for mine iniquity, And for my sin seekest?

v7 For Thou knowest that I am not wicked, And there is no deliverer from Thy hand.

v8 Thy hands have taken pains about me, And they make me together round about, And Thou swallowest me up!

v9 Remember, I pray Thee, That as clay Thou hast made me, And unto dust Thou dost bring me back.

v10 Dost Thou not as milk pour me out? And as cheese curdle me?

v11 Skin and flesh Thou dost put on me, And with bones and sinews dost fence me.

v12 Life and kindness Thou hast done with me. And Thy inspection hath preserved my spirit.

v13 And these Thou hast laid up in Thy heart, I have known that this [is] with Thee.

v14 If I sinned, then Thou hast observed me, And from mine iniquity dost not acquit me,

v15 If I have done wickedly -- wo to me, And righteously -- I lift not up my head, Full of shame -- then see my affliction,

v16 And it riseth -- as a lion Thou huntest me. And Thou turnest back -- Thou shewest Thyself wonderful in me.

v17 Thou renewest Thy witnesses against me, And dost multiply Thine anger with me, Changes and warfare [are] with me.

v18 And why from the womb Hast Thou brought me forth? I expire, and the eye doth not see me.

v19 As I had not been, I am, From the belly to the grave I am brought,

v20 Are not my days few? Cease then, and put from me, And I brighten up a little,

v21 Before I go, and return not, Unto a land of darkness and death-shade,

v22 A land of obscurity as thick darkness, Death-shade -- and no order, And the shining [is] as thick darkness.'
