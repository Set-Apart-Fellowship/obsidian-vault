# Job 27 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 26|← Job 26]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 28|Job 28 →]]

***

v1 And Job addeth to lift up his simile, and saith: --

v2 God liveth! He turned aside my judgment, And the Mighty -- He made my soul bitter.

v3 For all the while my breath [is] in me, And the spirit of God in my nostrils.

v4 My lips do not speak perverseness, And my tongue doth not utter deceit.

v5 Pollution to me -- if I justify you, Till I expire I turn not aside mine integrity from me.

v6 On my righteousness I have laid hold, And I do not let it go, My heart doth not reproach me while I live.

v7 As the wicked is my enemy, And my withstander as the perverse.

v8 For what [is] the hope of the profane, When He doth cut off? When God doth cast off his soul?

v9 His cry doth God hear, When distress cometh on him?

v10 On the Mighty doth he delight himself? Call God at all times?

v11 I shew you by the hand of God, That which [is] with the Mighty I hide not.

v12 Lo, ye -- all of you -- have seen, And why [is] this -- ye are altogether vain?

v13 This [is] the portion of wicked man with God, And the inheritance of terrible ones From the Mighty they receive.

v14 If his sons multiply -- for them [is] a sword. And his offspring [are] not satisfied [with] bread.

v15 His remnant in death are buried, And his widows do not weep.

v16 If he heap up as dust silver, And as clay prepare clothing,

v17 He prepareth -- and the righteous putteth [it] on, And the silver the innocent doth apportion.

v18 He hath built as a moth his house, And as a booth a watchman hath made.

v19 Rich he lieth down, and he is not gathered, His eyes he hath opened, and he is not.

v20 Overtake him as waters do terrors, By night stolen him away hath a whirlwind.

v21 Take him up doth an east wind, and he goeth, And it frighteneth him from his place,

v22 And it casteth at him, and doth not spare, From its hand he diligently fleeth.

v23 It clappeth at him its hands, And it hisseth at him from his place.
