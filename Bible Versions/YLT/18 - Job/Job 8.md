# Job 8 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 7|← Job 7]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/18 - Job/Job 9|Job 9 →]]

***

v1 And Bildad the Shuhite answereth and saith: --

v2 Till when dost thou speak these things? And a strong wind -- sayings of thy mouth?

v3 Doth God pervert judgment? And doth the Mighty One pervert justice?

v4 If thy sons have sinned before Him, And He doth send them away, By the hand of their transgression,

v5 If thou dost seek early unto God, And unto the Mighty makest supplication,

v6 If pure and upright thou [art], Surely now He waketh for thee, And hath completed The habitation of thy righteousness.

v7 And thy beginning hath been small, And thy latter end is very great.

v8 For, ask I pray thee of a former generation, And prepare to a search of their fathers,

v9 (For of yesterday we [are], and we know not, For a shadow [are] our days on earth.)

v10 Do they not shew thee -- speak to thee, And from their heart bring forth words?

v11 'Doth a rush wise without mire? A reed increase without water?

v12 While it [is] in its budding -- uncropt, Even before any herb it withereth.

v13 So [are] the paths of all forgetting God, And the hope of the profane doth perish,

v14 Whose confidence is loathsome, And the house of a spider his trust.

v15 He leaneth on his house -- and it standeth not: He taketh hold on it -- and it abook_ideth not.

v16 Green he [is] before the sun, And over his garden his branch goeth out.

v17 By a heap his roots are wrapped, A house of stones he looketh for.

v18 If [one] doth destroy him from his place, Then it hath feigned concerning him, I have not seen thee!

v19 Lo, this [is] the joy of his way, And from the dust others spring up.'

v20 Lo, God doth not reject the perfect, Nor taketh hold on the hand of evil doers.

v21 While he filleth with laughter thy mouth, And thy lips with shouting,

v22 Those hating thee do put on shame, And the tent of the wicked is not!
