# Joshua 19 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/06 - Joshua/Joshua 18|← Joshua 18]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/06 - Joshua/Joshua]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/06 - Joshua/Joshua 20|Joshua 20 →]]

***

v1 And the second lot goeth out for Simeon, for the tribe of the sons of Simeon, for their families; and their inheritance is in the midst of the inheritance of the sons of Judah,

v2 and they have in their inheritance Beer-Sheba, and Sheba, and Moladah,

v3 and Hazar-Shual, and Balah, and Azem,

v4 and Eltolad, and Bethul, and Hormah,

v5 and Ziklag, and Beth-Marcaboth, and Hazar-Susah,

v6 and Beth-Lebaoth, and Sharuhen; thirteen cities and their villages.

v7 Ain, Remmon, and Ether, and Ashan; four cities and their villages;

v8 also all the villages which [are] round about these cities, unto Baalath-Beer, Ramoth of the south. This [is] the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Simeon, for their families;

v9 out of the portion of the sons of Judah [is] the inheritance of the sons of Simeon, for the portion of the sons of Judah hath been too much for them, and the sons of Simeon inherit in the midst of their inheritance.

v10 And the third lot goeth up for the sons of Zebulun, for their families; and the border of their inheritance is unto Sarid,

v11 and their border hath gone up towards the sea, and Maralah, and come against Dabbasheth, and come unto the brook which [is] on the front of Jokneam,

v12 and turned back from Sarid eastward, at the sun-rising, by the border of Chisloth-Tabor, and gone out unto Daberath, and gone up to Japhia,

v13 and thence it hath passed over eastward, to the east, to Gittah-Hepher, [to] Ittah-Kazin, and gone out [to] Rimmon-Methoar to Neah;

v14 and the border hath gone round about it, from the north to Hannathon; and its outgoings have been [in] the valley of Jiphthah-El,

v15 and Kattath, and Nahallal, and Shimron, and Idalah, and Beth-Lehem; twelve cities and their villages.

v16 This [is] the inheritance of the sons of Zebulun, for their families, these cities and their villages.

v17 For Issachar hath the fourth lot gone out, for the sons of Issachar, for their families;

v18 and their border is [at] Jezreel, and Chesulloth, and Shunem,

v19 and Haphraim, and Shihon, and Anaharath,

v20 and Rabbith, and Kishion, and Abez,

v21 and Remeth, and En-Gannim, and En-Haddah, and Beth-Pazzez;

v22 and the border hath touched against Tabor, and Shahazimah, and Beth-Shemesh, and the outgoings of their border have been [at] the Jordan; sixteen cities and their villages.

v23 This [is] the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Issachar, for their families, the cities and their villages.

v24 And the fifth lot goeth out for the tribe of the sons of Asher, for their families;

v25 and their border is Helkath, and Hali, and Beten, and Achshaph,

v26 and Alammelech, and Amad, and Misheal; and it toucheth against Carmel westward, and against Shihor-Libnath;

v27 and hath turned back, at the sun-rising, [to] Beth-Dagon, and come against Zebulun, and against the valley of Jiphthah-El toward the north of Beth-Emek, and Neiel, and hath gone out unto Cabul on the left,

v28 and Hebron, and Rehob, and Hammon, and Kanah, unto great Zidon;

v29 and the border hath turned back to Ramah, and unto the fenced city Tyre; and the border hath turned back to Hosah, and its outgoings are at the sea, from the coast to Achzib,

v30 and Ummah, and Aphek, and Rehob; twenty and two cities and their villages.

v31 This [is] the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Asher, for their families, these cities and their villages.

v32 For the sons of Naphtali hath the sixth lot gone out, for the sons of Naphtali, for their families;

v33 and their border is from Heleph, from Allon in Zaanannim, and Adami, Nekeb, and Jabneel, unto Lakkum, and its outgoings are [at] the Jordan;

v34 and the border hath turned back westward [to] Aznoth-Tabor, and gone out thence to Hukkok, and touched against Zebulun on the south, and against Asher it hath touched on the west, and against Judah [at] the Jordan, at the sun-rising;

v35 and the cities of defence [are] Ziddim, Zer, and Hammath, Rakkath, and Chinnereth,

v36 and Adamah, and Ramah, and Hazor,

v37 and Kedesh, and Edrei, and En-Hazor,

v38 and Iron, and Migdal-El, Horem, and Beth-Anath, and Beth-Shemesh; nineteen cities and their villages.

v39 This [is] the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Naphtali, for their families, the cities and their villages.

v40 For the tribe of the sons of Dan, for their families, hath the seventh lot gone out;

v41 and the border of their inheritance is Zorah, and Eshtaol, and Ir-Shemesh,

v42 and Shalabbin, and Aijalon, and Jethlah,

v43 and Elon, and Thimnathah, and Ekron,

v44 and Eltekeh, and Gibbethon, and Baalath,

v45 and Jehud, and Bene-Barak, and Gath-Rimmon,

v46 and Me-Jarkon, and Rakkon, with the border over-against Japho.

v47 And the border of the sons of Dan goeth out from them, and the sons of Dan go up and fight with Leshem, and capture it, and smite it by the mouth of the sword, and possess it, and dwell in it, and call Leshem, Dan, according to the name of Dan their father.

v48 This [is] the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of Dan, for their families, these cities and their villages.

v49 And they finish to give the land in inheritance, by its borders, and the sons of Israel give an inheritance to Joshua son of Nun in their midst;

v50 by the command of Jehovah they have given to him the city which he asked, Timnath-Serah, in the hill-country of Ephraim, and he buildeth the city and dwelleth in it.

v51 These [are] the inheritances which Eleazar the priest, and Joshua son of Nun, and the heads of the fathers of the tribes of the sons of Israel, have caused to inherit by lot, in Shiloh, before Jehovah, at the opening of the tent of meeting; and they finish to apportion the land.
