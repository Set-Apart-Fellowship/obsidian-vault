# 1 Peter 2 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1|← 1 Peter 1]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 3|1 Peter 3 →]]

***

v1 Having put aside, then, all evil, and all guile, and hypocrisies, and envyings, and all evil speakings,

v2 as new-born babes the word's pure milk desire ye, that in it ye may grow,

v3 if so be ye did taste that the Lord [is] gracious,

v4 to whom coming -- a living stone -- by men, indeed, having been disapproved of, but with God choice, precious,

v5 and ye yourselves, as living stones, are built up, a spiritual house, a holy priesthood, to offer up spiritual sacrifices acceptable to God through Jesus Christ.

v6 Wherefore, also, it is contained in the Writing: 'Lo, I lay in Zion a chief corner-stone, choice, precious, and he who is believing on him may not be put to shame;'

v7 to you, then, who are believing [is] the preciousness; and to the unbelieving, a stone that the builders disapproved of, this one did become for the head of a corner,

v8 and a stone of stumbling and a rock of offence -- who are stumbling at the word, being unbelieving, -- to which also they were set;

v9 and ye [are] a choice race, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a people acquired, that the excellences ye may shew forth of Him who out of darkness did call you to His wondrous light;

v10 who [were] once not a people, and [are] now the people of God; who had not found kindness, and now have found kindness.

v11 Beloved, I call upon [you], as strangers and sojourners, to keep from the fleshly desires, that war against the soul,

v12 having your behaviour among the nations right, that in that which they speak against you as evil-doers, of the good works having beheld, they may glorify God in a day of inspection.

v13 Be subject, then, to every human creation, because of the Lord, whether to a king, as the highest,

v14 whether to governors, as to those sent through him, for punishment, indeed, of evil-doers, and a praise of those doing good;

v15 because, so is the will of God, doing good, to put to silence the ignorance of the foolish men;

v16 as free, and not having the freedom as the cloak of the evil, but as servants of God;

v17 to all give ye honour; the brotherhood love ye; God fear ye; the king honour ye.

v18 The domestics! be subjecting yourselves in all fear to the masters, not only to the good and gentle, but also to the cross;

v19 for this [is] gracious, if because of conscience toward God any one doth endure sorrows, suffering unrighteously;

v20 for what renown [is it], if sinning and being buffeted, ye do endure [it]? but if, doing good and suffering [for it], ye do endure, this [is] gracious with God,

v21 for to this ye were called, because Christ also did suffer for you, leaving to you an example, that ye may follow his steps,

v22 who did not commit sin, nor was guile found in his mouth,

v23 who being reviled -- was not reviling again, suffering -- was not threatening, and was committing himself to Him who is judging righteously,

v24 who our sins himself did bear in his body, upon the tree, that to the sins having died, to the righteousness we may live; by whose stripes ye were healed,

v25 for ye were as sheep going astray, but ye turned back now to the shepherd and overseer of your souls.
