# Philippians 2 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/50 - Philippians/Philippians 1|← Philippians 1]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/50 - Philippians/Philippians]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/50 - Philippians/Philippians 3|Philippians 3 →]]

***

v1 If, then, any exhortation [is] in Christ, if any comfort of love, if any fellowship of spirit, if any bowels and mercies,

v2 fulfil ye my joy, that ye may mind the same thing -- having the same love -- of one soul -- minding the one thing,

v3 nothing in rivalry or vain-glory, but in humility of mind one another counting more excellent than yourselves --

v4 each not to your own look ye, but each also to the things of others.

v5 For, let this mind be in you that [is] also in Christ Jesus,

v6 who, being in the form of God, thought [it] not robbery to be equal to God,

v7 but did empty himself, the form of a servant having taken, in the likeness of men having been made,

v8 and in fashion having been found as a man, he humbled himself, having become obedient unto death -- death even of a cross,

v9 wherefore, also, God did highly exalt him, and gave to him a name that [is] above every name,

v10 that in the name of Jesus every knee may bow -- of heavenlies, and earthlies, and what are under the earth --

v11 and every tongue may confess that Jesus Christ [is] Lord, to the glory of God the Father.

v12 So that, my beloved, as ye always obey, not as in my presence only, but now much more in my absence, with fear and trembling your own salvation work out,

v13 for God it is who is working in you both to will and to work for His good pleasure.

v14 All things do without murmurings and reasonings,

v15 that ye may become blameless and harmless, children of God, unblemished in the midst of a generation crooked and perverse, among whom ye do appear as luminaries in the world,

v16 the word of life holding forth, for rejoicing to me in regard to a day of Christ, that not in vain did I run, nor in vain did I labour;

v17 but if also I am poured forth upon the sacrifice and service of your faith, I rejoice and joy with you all,

v18 because of this do ye also rejoice and joy with me.

v19 And I hope, in the Lord Jesus, Timotheus to send quickly to you, that I also may be of good spirit, having known the things concerning you,

v20 for I have no one like-minded, who sincerely for the things concerning you will care,

v21 for the whole seek their own things, not the things of the Christ Jesus,

v22 and the proof of him ye know, that as a child [serveth] a father, with me he did serve in regard to the good news;

v23 him, indeed, therefore, I hope to send, when I may see through the things concerning me -- immediately;

v24 and I trust in the Lord that I myself also shall quickly come.

v25 And I thought [it] necessary Epaphroditus -- my brother, and fellow-workman, and fellow-soldier, and your apostle and servant to my need -- to send unto you,

v26 seeing he was longing after you all, and in heaviness, because ye heard that he ailed,

v27 for he also ailed nigh to death, but God did deal kindly with him, and not with him only, but also with me, that sorrow upon sorrow I might not have.

v28 The more eagerly, therefore, I did send him, that having seen him again ye may rejoice, and I may be the less sorrowful;

v29 receive him, therefore, in the Lord, with all joy, and hold such in honour,

v30 because on account of the work of the Christ he drew near to death, having hazarded the life that he might fill up your deficiency of service unto me.
