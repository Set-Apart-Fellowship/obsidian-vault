# James 4 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/59 - James/James 3|← James 3]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/59 - James/James]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/59 - James/James 5|James 5 →]]

***

v1 Whence [are] wars and fightings among you? not thence -- out of your passions, that are as soldiers in your members?

v2 ye desire, and ye have not; ye murder, and are zealous, and are not able to attain; ye fight and war, and ye have not, because of your not asking;

v3 ye ask, and ye receive not, because evilly ye ask, that in your pleasures ye may spend [it].

v4 Adulterers and adulteresses! have ye not known that friendship of the world is enmity with God? whoever, then, may counsel to be a friend of the world, an enemy of God he is set.

v5 Do ye think that emptily the Writing saith, 'To envy earnestly desireth the spirit that did dwell in us,'

v6 and greater grace he doth give, wherefore he saith, 'God against proud ones doth set Himself up, and to lowly ones He doth give grace?'

v7 be subject, then, to God; stand up against the devil, and he will flee from you;

v8 draw nigh to God, and He will draw nigh to you; cleanse hands, ye sinners! and purify hearts, ye two-souled!

v9 be exceeding afflicted, and mourn, and weep, let your laughter to mourning be turned, and the joy to heaviness;

v10 be made low before the Lord, and He shall exalt you.

v11 Speak not one against another, brethren; he who is speaking against a brother, and is judging his brother, doth speak against law, and doth judge law, and if law thou dost judge, thou art not a doer of law but a judge;

v12 one is the lawgiver, who is able to save and to destroy; thou -- who art thou that dost judge the other?

v13 Go, now, ye who are saying, 'To-day and to-morrow we will go on to such a city, and will pass there one year, and traffic, and make gain;'

v14 who do not know the thing of the morrow; for what is your life? for it is a vapour that is appearing for a little, and then is vanishing;

v15 instead of your saying, 'If the Lord may will, we shall live, and do this or that;'

v16 and now ye glory in your pride; all such glorying is evil;

v17 to him, then, knowing to do good, and not doing, sin it is to him.
