# 1 Chronicles 8 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 7|← 1 Chronicles 7]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 9|1 Chronicles 9 →]]

***

v1 And Benjamin begat Bela his first-born, Ashbel the second, and Aharah the third,

v2 Nohah the fourth, and Rapha the fifth.

v3 And there are sons to Bela: Addar, and Gera,

v4 and Abihud, and Abishua, and Naaman, and Ahoah,

v5 and Gera, and Shephuphan, and Huram.

v6 And these [are] sons of Ehud: they are heads of fathers to the inhabitants of Geba, and they remove them unto Manahath;

v7 and Naaman, and Ahiah, and Gera, he removed them, and begat Uzza and Ahihud.

v8 And Shaharaim begat in the field of Moab, after his sending them away; Hushim and Baara [are] his wives.

v9 And he begetteth of Hodesh his wife, Jobab, and Zibia, and Mesha, and Malcham,

v10 and Jeuz, and Shachiah, and Mirmah. These [are] his sons, heads of fathers.

v11 And of Hushim he begat Ahitub and Elpaal.

v12 And sons of Elpaal: Eber, and Misheam, and Shamer, (he built Ono and Lod and its small towns),

v13 and Beriah and Shema, (they [are] the heads of fathers to the inhabitants of Aijalon -- they caused to flee the inhabitants of Gath),

v14 and Ahio, Shashak, and Jeremoth,

v15 and Zebadiah, and Arad, and Ader,

v16 and Michael, and Ispah, and Joha, sons of Beriah,

v17 and Zebadiah, and Meshullam, and Hezeki, and Heber,

v18 and Ishmerai, and Jezliah, and Jobab, sons of Elpaal;

v19 And Jakim, and Zichri, and Zabdi,

v20 and Elienai, and Zillethai, and Eliel,

v21 and Adaiah, and Beraiah, and Shimrath, sons of Shimei;

v22 And Ishpan, and Heber, and Eliel,

v23 and Abdon, and Zichri, and Hanan,

v24 and Hananiah, and Elam, and Antothijah,

v25 and Iphedeiah, and Penuel, sons of Shashak;

v26 And Shamsherai, and Shehariah, and Athaliah,

v27 and Jaareshiah, and Eliah, and Zichri, sons of Jeroham.

v28 These [are] heads of fathers, by their generations, heads; these dwelt in Jerusalem.

v29 And in Gibeon hath the father of Gibeon dwelt, and the name of his wife [is] Maachah;

v30 and his son, the first-born, [is] Abdon, and Zur, and Kish, and Baal, and Nadab,

v31 and Gedor, and Ahio, and Zacher;

v32 and Mikloth begat Shimeah. And they also over-against their brethren dwelt in Jerusalem with their brethren.

v33 And Ner begat Kish, and Kish begat Saul, and Saul begat Jonathan, and Malchi-Shua, and Abinadab, and Esh-Baal.

v34 And a son of Jonathan [is] Merib-Baal, and Merib-Baal begat Micah;

v35 and sons of Micah: Pithon, and Melech, and Tarea, and Ahaz:

v36 and Ahaz begat Jehoadah, and Jehoadah begat Alemeth, and Azmaveth, and Zimri; and Zimri begat Moza,

v37 and Moza begat Binea, Raphah [is] his son, Eleasah his son, Azel his son.

v38 And to Azel [are] six sons, and these [are] their names: Azrikam, Bocheru, and Ishmael, and Sheariah, and Obadiah, and Hanan. All these [are] sons of Azel.

v39 And sons of Eshek his brother: Ulam his first-born, Jehush the second, and Eliphelet the third.

v40 And the sons of Ulam are men mighty in valour, treading bow, and multiplying sons and son's sons, a hundred and fifty. All these [are] of the sons of Benjamin.
