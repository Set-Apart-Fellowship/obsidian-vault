# 1 Chronicles 3 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 2|← 1 Chronicles 2]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 4|1 Chronicles 4 →]]

***

v1 And these were sons of David, who were born to him in Hebron: the first-born Amnon, of Ahinoam the Jezreelitess; second Daniel, of Abigail the Carmelitess;

v2 the third Absalom, son of Maachah daughter of Talmai king of Geshur; the fourth Adonijah, son of Haggith;

v3 the fifth Shephatiah, of Abital; the sixth Ithream, of Eglah his wife.

v4 Six have been borne to him in Hebron, and he reigneth there seven years and six months, and thirty and three years he hath reigned in Jerusalem.

v5 And these were born to him in Jerusalem: Shimea, and Shobab, and Nathan, and Solomon -- four, of Bath-Sheba daughter of Ammiel:

v6 also Ibhar, and Elishama, and Eliphelet,

v7 and Nogah, and Nepheg, and Japhia,

v8 and Elishama, and Eliada, and Eliphelet, nine.

v9 All [are] sons of David, apart from sons of the concubines, and Tamar their sister.

v10 And the son of Solomon [is] Rehoboam, Abijah his son, Asa his son, Jehoshaphat his son,

v11 Joram his son, Ahaziah his son, Joash his son,

v12 Amaziah his son, Azariah his son, Jotham his son,

v13 Ahaz his son, Hezekiah his son, Manasseh his son,

v14 Amon his son, Josiah his son.

v15 And sons of Josiah: the first-born Johanan, the second Jehoiakim, the third Zedekiah, the fourth Shallum.

v16 And sons of Jehoiakim: Jeconiah his son, Zedekiah his son.

v17 And sons of Jeconiah: Assir; Salathiel his son;

v18 also Malchiram and Pedaiah, and Shenazzar, Jecamiah, Hoshama, and Nedabiah.

v19 And sons of Pedaiah: Zerubbabel, and Shimei. And sons of Zerubbabel: Meshullam, and Hananiah, and Shelomith their sister,

v20 and Hashubah, and Ohel, and Berechiah, and Hasadiah, Jushab-Hesed, five.

v21 And sons of Hananiah: Pelatiah, and Jesaiah, sons of Rephaiah, sons of Arnan, sons of Obadiah, sons of Shechaniah.

v22 And sons of Shechaniah: Shemaiah; and sons of Shemaiah: Hattush, and Igeal, and Bariah, and Neariah, and Shaphat, six.

v23 And sons of Neariah: Elioenai, and Hezekiah, and Azrikam, three.

v24 And sons of Elioenai: Hodaiah, and Eliashib, and Pelaiah, and Akkub, and Johanan, and Delaiah, and Anani, seven.
