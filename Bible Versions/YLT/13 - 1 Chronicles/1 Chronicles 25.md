# 1 Chronicles 25 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 24|← 1 Chronicles 24]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 26|1 Chronicles 26 →]]

***

v1 And David and the heads of the host separate for service, of the sons of Asaph, and Heman, and Jeduthun, who are prophesying with harps, with psalteries, and with cymbals, and the number of the workmen is according to their service.

v2 Of sons of Asaph: Zaccur, and Joseph, and Nethaniah, and Asharelah, sons of Asaph, [are] by the side of Asaph, who is prophesying by the side of the king.

v3 Of Jeduthun: sons of Jeduthun, Gedaliah, and Zeri, and Jeshaiah, Hashabiah, and Mattithiah, Shisshah, [are] by the side of their father Jeduthun; with a harp he is prophesying, for giving of thanks and of praise to Jehovah.

v4 Of Heman: sons of Heman: Bukkiah, Mattaniah, Uzziel, Shebuel, and Jerimoth, Hananiah, Hanani, Eliathah, Giddalti, and Romamti-Ezer, Joshbekashah, Mallothi, Hothir, Mahazioth;

v5 all these [are] sons of Heman -- seer of the king in the things of God -- to lift up a horn; and God giveth to Heman fourteen sons and three daughters.

v6 All these [are] by the side of their father in the song of the house of Jehovah, with cymbals, psalteries, and harps, for the service of the house of God; by the side of the king [are] Asaph, and Jeduthun, and Heman.

v7 And their number, with their brethren -- taught in the song of Jehovah, all who are intelligent -- is two hundred, eighty and eight.

v8 And they cause to fall lots -- charge over-against [charge], as well the small as the great, the intelligent with the learner.

v9 And the first lot goeth out for Asaph to Joseph; [to] Gedaliah the second; he, and his brethren and his sons, twelve;

v10 the third [to] Zaccur, his sons and his brethren, twelve;

v11 the fourth to Izri, his sons and his brethren, twelve;

v12 the fifth [to] Nethaniah, his sons and his brethren, twelve;

v13 the sixth [to] Bukkiah, his sons and his brethren, twelve;

v14 the seventh [to] Jesharelah, his sons and his brethren, twelve;

v15 the eighth [to] Jeshaiah, his sons and his brethren, twelve;

v16 the ninth [to] Mattaniah, his sons and his brethren, twelve;

v17 the tenth [to] Shimei, his sons and his brethren, twelve:

v18 eleventh [to] Azareel, his sons and his brethren, twelve;

v19 the twelfth [to] Hashabiah, his sons and his brethren, twelve;

v20 at the thirteenth [to] Shubael, his sons and his brethren, twelve;

v21 at the fourteenth [to] Mattithiah, his sons and his brethren, twelve;

v22 at the fifteenth [to] Jeremoth, his sons and his brethren, twelve;

v23 at the sixteenth [to] Hananiah, his sons and his brethren, twelve;

v24 at the seventeenth [to] Joshbekashah, his sons and his brethren, twelve;

v25 at the eighteenth [to] Hanani, his sons and his brethren, twelve;

v26 at the nineteenth [to] Mallothi, his sons and his brethren, twelve;

v27 at the twentieth [to] Eliathah, his sons and his brethren, twelve;

v28 at the one and twentieth [to] Hothir, his sons and his brethren, twelve;

v29 at the two and twentieth [to] Giddalti, his sons and his brethren, twelve;

v30 at the three and twentieth [to] Mahazioth, his sons and his brethren, twelve;

v31 at the four and twentieth [to] Romamti-Ezer, his sons and his brethren, twelve.
