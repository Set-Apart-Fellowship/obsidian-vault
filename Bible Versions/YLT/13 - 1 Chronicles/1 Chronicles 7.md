# 1 Chronicles 7 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 6|← 1 Chronicles 6]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 8|1 Chronicles 8 →]]

***

v1 And sons of Issachar; Tola, and Puah, Jashub, and Shimron, four.

v2 And sons of Tola: Uzzi, and Rephaiah, and Jeriel, and Jahmai, and Jibsam, and Shemuel, heads of the house of their fathers, [even] of Tola, mighty of valour in their generations: their number in the days of David [is] twenty and two thousand and six hundred.

v3 And sons of Uzzi: Izrahiah; and sons of Izrahiah: Michael, and Obadiah, and Joel, Ishiah, Hamishah -- all of them heads.

v4 And beside them, by their generations, of the house of their fathers, [are] troops of the host of battle, thirty and six thousand, for they multiplied wives and sons;

v5 and their brethren of all the families of Issachar [are] mighty of valour, eighty and seven thousand, all have their genealogy.

v6 Of Benjamin: Bela, and Becher, and Jediael, three.

v7 And sons of Bela: Ezbon, and Uzzi, and Uzziel, and Jerimoth, and Iri, five; heads of a house of fathers, mighty of valour, with their genealogy, twenty and two thousand, and thirty and four.

v8 And sons of Becher: Zemirah, and Joash, and Eliezar, and Elioenai, and Omri, and Jerimoth, and Abijah, and Anathoth, and Alameth. All these [are] sons of Becher,

v9 with their genealogy, after their generations, heads of a house of their fathers, mighty of valour, twenty thousand and two hundred.

v10 And sons of Jediael: Bilhan; and sons of Bilhan: Jeush, and Benjamin, and Ehud, and Chenaanah, and Zethan, and Tarshish, and Ahishahar.

v11 All these [are] sons of Jediael, even heads of the fathers, mighty in valour, seventeen thousand and two hundred going out to the host for battle.

v12 And Shuppim and Huppim [are] sons of Ir; Hushim son of Aher.

v13 Sons of Naphtali: Jahziel, and Guni, and Jezer, and Shallum, sons of Bilhah.

v14 Sons of Manasseh: Ashriel, whom Jaladah his Aramaean concubine bare, with Machir father of Gilead.

v15 And Machir took wives for Huppim and for Shuppim, and the name of the one [is] Maachah, and the name of the second Zelophehad, and Zelophehad hath daughters.

v16 And Maachah wife of Machir beareth a son and calleth his name Peresh, and the name of his brother [is] Sheresh, and his sons [are] Ulam and Rakem.

v17 And son of Ulam: Bedan. These [are] sons of Gilead son of Machir, son of Manasseh.

v18 And his sister Hammolecheth bare Ishhod, and Abiezer, and Mahalah.

v19 And the sons of Shemida are Ahian, and Shechem, and Likhi, and Aniam.

v20 And sons of Ephraim: Shuthelah, and Bered his son, and Tahath his son, and Eladah his son, and Tahath his son,

v21 and Zabad his son, and Shuthelah his son, and Ezer, and Elead; and slain them have men of Gath who are born in the land, because they came down to take their cattle.

v22 And Ephraim their father mourneth many days, and his brethren come in to comfort him,

v23 and he goeth in unto his wife, and she conceiveth and beareth a son, and he calleth his name Beriah, because in evil had been his house, --

v24 and his daughter [is] Sherah, and she buildeth Beth-Horon, the lower and the upper, and Uzzen-Sherah --

v25 and Rephah [is] his son, and Resheph, and Telah his son, and Tahan his son,

v26 Laadan his son, Ammihud his son, Elishama his son,

v27 Non his son, Jehoshua his son.

v28 And their possession and their dwellings [are] Beth-El and its small towns, and to the east Naaran, and to the west Gezer and its small towns, and Shechem and its small towns, unto Gaza and its small towns;

v29 and by the parts of the sons of Manasseh, Beth-Shean and its small towns, Taanach and its small towns, Megiddo and its small towns, Dor and its small towns; in these dwelt the sons of Joseph son of Israel.

v30 Son of Asher: Imnah, and Ishve, and Ishvi, and Beriah, and Serah their sister.

v31 And sons of Beriah: Heber, and Malchiel -- he [is] father of Birzavith.

v32 And Heber begat Japhlet, and Shomer, and Hotham, and Shua their sister.

v33 And sons of Japhlet: Pasach, and Bimhal, and Ashvath; these [are] sons of Japhlet.

v34 and sons of Shamer: Ahi, and Rohgah, Jehubbah, and Aram.

v35 And son of Helem his brother: Zophah, and Imna, and Shelesh, and Amal.

v36 Sons of Zophah: Suah, and Harnepher, and Shual, and Beri, and Imrah,

v37 Bezer, and Hod, and Shamma, and Shilshah, and Ithran, and Beera.

v38 And sons of Jether: Jephunneh, and Pispah, and Ara.

v39 And sons of Ulla: Arah, and Hanniel, and Rezia.

v40 All these [are] sons of Asher, heads of the house of the fathers, chosen ones, mighty in valour, heads of the princes, with their genealogy, for the host, for battle, their number [is] twenty and six thousand men.
