# 1 Chronicles 2 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 1|← 1 Chronicles 1]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 3|1 Chronicles 3 →]]

***

v1 These [are] sons of Israel: Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun,

v2 Dan, Joseph, and Benjamin, Naphtali, Gad, and Asher.

v3 Sons of Judah: Er, and Onan, and Shelah, three have been born to him of a daughter of Shua the Canaanitess. And Er, first-born of Judah, is evil in the eyes of Jehovah, and He putteth him to death.

v4 And Tamar his daughter-in-law hath borne to him Pharez and Zerah. All the sons of Judah [are] five.

v5 Sons of Pharez: Hezron, and Hamul.

v6 And sons of Zerah: Zimri, and Ethan, and Heman, and Calcol, and Dara; all of them five.

v7 And sons of Carmi: Achar, troubler of Israel, who trespassed in the devoted thing.

v8 And sons of Ethan: Azariah.

v9 And sons of Hezron who were born to him; Jerahmeel, and Ram, and Chelubai.

v10 And Ram begat Amminadab, and Amminadab begat Nahshon, prince of the sons of Judah;

v11 and Nahshon begat Salma, and Salma begat Boaz,

v12 and Boaz begat Obed, and Obed begat Jesse;

v13 and Jesse begat his first-born Eliab, and Abinadab the second, and Shimea the third,

v14 Nethaneel the fourth, Raddai the fifth,

v15 Ozem the sixth, David the seventh,

v16 and their sisters Zeruiah and Abigail. And sons of Zeruiah: Abishai, and Joab, and Asah-El -- three.

v17 And Abigail hath borne Amasa, and the father of Amasa [is] Jether the Ishmeelite.

v18 And Caleb son of Hezron hath begotten Azubah, Isshah, and Jerioth; and these [are] her sons: Jesher, and Shobab, and Ardon.

v19 And Azubah dieth, and Caleb taketh to him Ephrath, and she beareth to him Hur.

v20 And Hur begat Uri, and Uri begat Bezaleel.

v21 And afterwards hath Hezron gone in unto a daughter of Machir father of Gilead, and he hath taken her, and he [is] a son of sixty years, and she beareth to him Segub.

v22 And Segub begat Jair, and he hath twenty and three cities in the land of Gilead,

v23 and he taketh Geshur and Aram, the small villages of Jair, from them, with Kenath and its small towns, sixty cities -- all these [belonged to] the sons of Machir father of Gilead.

v24 And after the death of Hezron in Caleb-Ephratah, then the wife of Hezron, Abijah, even beareth to him Asshur, father of Tekoa.

v25 And sons of Jerahmeel, first-born of Hezron, are: the first-born Ram, and Bunah, and Oren, and Ozem, Ahijah.

v26 And Jerahmeel hath another wife, and her name [is] Atarah, she [is] mother of Onam.

v27 And sons of Ram, first-born of Jerahmeel, are Maaz, and Jamin, and Eker.

v28 And sons of Onam are Shammai and Jada. And sons of Shammai: Nadab and Abishur.

v29 And the name of the wife of Abishur [is] Abihail, and she beareth to him Ahban and Molid.

v30 And sons of Nadab: Seled, and Appaim; and Seled dieth without sons.

v31 And sons of Appaim: Ishi. And sons of Ishi: Sheshan. And sons of Sheshan: Ahlai.

v32 And sons of Jada, brother of Shammai: Jether, and Jonathan: and Jether dieth without sons.

v33 And sons of Jonathan: Peleth, and Zaza. These were sons of Jerahmeel.

v34 And Sheshan had no sons, but daughters, and Sheshan hath a servant, an Egyptian, and his name [is] Jarha,

v35 and Sheshan giveth his daughter to Jarha his servant for a wife, and she beareth to him Attai;

v36 and Attai begat Nathan, and Nathan begat Zabad,

v37 and Zabad begat Ephlal, and Ephlal begat Obed,

v38 and Obed begat Jehu,

v39 and Jehu begat Azariah, and Azariah begat Helez, and Helez begat Eleasah,

v40 and Eleasah begat Sismai, and Sismai begat Shallum,

v41 and Shallum begat Jekamiah, and Jekamiah begat Elishama.

v42 And sons of Caleb brother of Jerahmeel: Mesha his first-born, he [is] father of Ziph; and sons of Mareshah: Abi-Hebron.

v43 And sons of Hebron: Korah, and Tappuah, and Rekem, and Shema.

v44 And Shema begat Raham father of Jorkoam, and Rekem begat Shammai.

v45 And a son of Shammai [is] Maon, and Maon [is] father of Beth-Zur.

v46 And Ephah concubine of Caleb bare Haran, and Moza, and Gazez; and Haran begat Gazez.

v47 And sons of Jahdai: Regem, and Jotham, and Geshem, and Pelet, and Ephah, and Shaaph.

v48 The concubine of Caleb, Maachah, bare Sheber and Tirhanah;

v49 and she beareth Shaaph father of Madmannah, Sheva father of Machbenah, and father of Gibea; and a daughter of Caleb [is] Achsa.

v50 These were sons of Caleb son of Hur, first-born of Ephrathah: Shobal father of Kirjath-Jearim,

v51 Salma father of Beth-Lehem, Hareph father of Beth-Gader.

v52 And there are sons to Shobal father of Kirjath-Jearim: Haroeh, half of the Menuhothite;

v53 and the families of Kirjath-Jearim: the Ithrite, and the Puhite, and the Shumathite, and the Mishraite: from these went out the Zareathite, and the Eshtaulite.

v54 Sons of Salma: Beth-Lehem, and the Netophathite, Atroth, Beth-Joab, and half of the Menuhothite, the Zorite;

v55 and the families of the scribes the inhabitants of Jabez: Tirathites, Shimeathites, Suchathites. They [are] the Kenites, those coming of Hammath father of the house of Rechab.
