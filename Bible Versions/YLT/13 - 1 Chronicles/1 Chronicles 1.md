# 1 Chronicles 1 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 2|1 Chronicles 2 →]]

***

v1 Adam, Sheth, Enosh,

v2 Kenan, Mahalaleel, Jered,

v3 Henoch, Methuselah, Lamech,

v4 Noah, Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

v5 Sons of Japheth: Gomer and Magog, and Madai, and Javan, and Tubal, and Meshech, and Tiras.

v6 And sons of Gomer: Ashchenaz, and Riphath, and Togarmah.

v7 And sons of Javan: Elisha, and Tarshishah, Kittim, and Dodanim.

v8 Sons of Ham: Cush, and Mizraim, Put, and Canaan.

v9 And sons of Cush: Seba and Havilah, and Sabta, and Raamah, and Sabtecka. And sons of Raamah: Sheba and Dedan.

v10 And Cush begat Nimrod: he began to be a mighty one in the land.

v11 And Mizraim begat the Ludim, and the Anamim, and the Lehabim, and the Naphtuhim,

v12 and the Pathrusim, and the Casluhim (from whom came out the Philistim), and the Caphtorim.

v13 And Canaan begat Zidon his first born, and Heth,

v14 and the Jebusite, and the Amorite, and the Girgashite,

v15 and the Hivite, and the Arkite, and the Sinite,

v16 and the Arvadite, and the Zemarite, and the Hamathite.

v17 Sons of Shem: Elam and Asshur, and Arphaxad, and Lud, and Aram, and Uz, and Hul, and Gether, and Meshech.

v18 And Arphaxad begat Shelah, and Shelah begat Eber.

v19 And to Eber have been born two sons, the name of the one [is] Peleg, for in his days hath the land been divided, and the name of his brother is Joktan.

v20 And Joktan begat Almodad, and Sheleph, and Hazarmaveth, and Jerah,

v21 and Hadoram, and Uzal, and Diklah,

v22 and Ebal, and Abimael, and Sheba,

v23 and Ophir, and Havilah, and Jobab; all these [are] sons of Joktan.

v24 Shem, Arphaxad, Shelah,

v25 Eber, Peleg, Reu,

v26 Serug, Nahor, Terah,

v27 Abram -- he [is] Abraham.

v28 Sons of Abraham: Isaac and Ishmael.

v29 These [are] their generations: first-born of Ishmael, Nebaioth, and Kedar, and Adheel, and Mibsam,

v30 Mishma, and Dumah, Massa, Hadad, and Tema,

v31 Jetur, Naphish, and Kedema. These are sons of Ishmael.

v32 And sons of Keturah, Abraham's concubine: she bare Zimran, and Jokshan, and Medan, and Midian, and Ishbak, and Shuah. And sons of Jokshan: Sheba and Dedan.

v33 And sons of Midian: Ephah and Epher, and Henoch, and Abook_ida, and Eldaah; all these [are] sons of Keturah.

v34 And Abraham begetteth Isaac. Sons of Isaac: Esau and Israel.

v35 Sons of Esau: Eliphaz, Reuel, and Jeush, and Jaalam, and Korah.

v36 Sons of Eliphaz: Teman, and Omar, Zephi, and Gatam, Kenaz, and Timna, and Amalek.

v37 Sons of Reuel: Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah.

v38 And sons of Seir: Lotan, and Shobal, and Zibeon, and Anah, and Dishon, and Ezar, and Dishan.

v39 And sons of Lotan: Hori and Homam, and sister of Lotan [is] Timna.

v40 Sons of Shobal: Alian, and Manahath, and Ebal, Shephi, and Onam. And sons of Zideon: Aiah and Anah.

v41 The sons of Anah: Dishon. and sons of Dishon: Amram, and Eshban, and Ithran, and Cheran.

v42 Sons of Ezer: Bilhan, and Zavan, Jakan. Sons of Dishan: Uz and Aran.

v43 And these [are] the kings who reigned in the land of Edom before the reigning of a king of the sons of Israel: Bela son of Beor, and the name of his city [is] Dinhabah.

v44 And Bela dieth, and reign in his stead doth Jobab son of Zerah from Bozrali;

v45 and Jobab dieth, and reign in his stead doth Husham from the land of the Temanite;

v46 and Husham dieth, and reign in his stead doth Hadad, son of Bedad (who smiteth Midian in the field of Moab) and the name of his city [is] Avith;

v47 and Hadad dieth, and reign in his stead doth Samlah from Masrekah;

v48 and Samlah dieth, and reign in his stead doth Shaul from Rehoboth of the River;

v49 and Shaul dieth, and reign in his stead doth Baal-Hanan son of Achbor;

v50 and Baal-Hanan dieth, and reign in his stead doth Hadad, and the name of his city [is] Pai, and the name of his wife [is] Mehetabel daughter of Matred, daughter of Me-Zahab; Hadad also dieth.

v51 And chiefs of Edom are: chief Timnah, chief Aliah, chief Jetheth,

v52 chief Aholibamah, chief Elah, chief Pinon,

v53 chief Kenaz, chief Teman, chief Mibzar,

v54 chief Magdiel, chief Iram. These [are] chiefs of Edom.
