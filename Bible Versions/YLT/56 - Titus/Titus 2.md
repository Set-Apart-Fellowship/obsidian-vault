# Titus 2 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/56 - Titus/Titus 1|← Titus 1]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/56 - Titus/Titus]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/56 - Titus/Titus 3|Titus 3 →]]

***

v1 And thou -- be speaking what doth become the sound teaching;

v2 aged men to be temperate, grave, sober, sound in the faith, in the love, in the endurance;

v3 aged women, in like manner, in deportment as doth become sacred persons, not false accusers, to much wine not enslaved, of good things teachers,

v4 that they may make the young women sober-minded, to be lovers of [their] husbands, lovers of [their] children,

v5 sober, pure, keepers of [their own] houses, good, subject to their own husbands, that the word of God may not be evil spoken of.

v6 The younger men, in like manner, be exhorting to be sober-minded;

v7 concerning all things thyself showing a pattern of good works; in the teaching uncorruptedness, gravity, incorruptibility,

v8 discourse sound, irreprehensible, that he who is of the contrary part may be ashamed, having nothing evil to say concerning you.

v9 Servants -- to their own masters [are] to be subject, in all things to be well-pleasing, not gainsaying,

v10 not purloining, but showing all good stedfastness, that the teaching of God our Saviour they may adorn in all things.

v11 For the saving grace of God was manifested to all men,

v12 teaching us, that denying the impiety and the worldly desires, soberly and righteously and piously we may live in the present age,

v13 waiting for the blessed hope and manifestation of the glory of our great God and Saviour Jesus Christ,

v14 who did give himself for us, that he might ransom us from all lawlessness, and might purify to himself a peculiar people, zealous of good works;

v15 these things be speaking, and exhorting, and convicting, with all charge; let no one despise thee!
