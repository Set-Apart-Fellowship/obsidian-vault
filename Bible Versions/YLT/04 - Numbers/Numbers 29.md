# Numbers 29 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/04 - Numbers/Numbers 28|← Numbers 28]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/04 - Numbers/Numbers]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/04 - Numbers/Numbers 30|Numbers 30 →]]

***

v1 'And in the seventh month, in the first of the month, a holy convocation ye have, ye do no servile work; a day of shouting it is to you;

v2 and ye have prepared a burnt-offering, for sweet fragrance to Jehovah: one bullock, a son of the herd, one ram, seven lambs, sons of a year, perfect ones;

v3 and their present, flour mixed with oil, three-tenth deals for the bullock, two-tenth deals for the ram,

v4 and one-tenth deal for the one lamb, for the seven lambs;

v5 and one kid of the goats, a sin-offering, to make atonement for you;

v6 apart from the burnt-offering of the month, and its present, and the continual burnt-offering, and its present, and their libations, according to their ordinance, for sweet fragrance, a fire-offering to Jehovah.

v7 'And on the tenth of this seventh month a holy convocation ye have, and ye have humbled your souls; ye do no work;

v8 and ye have brought near a burnt-offering to Jehovah, a sweet fragrance, one bullock, a son of the herd, one ram, seven lambs, sons of a year, perfect ones they are for you,

v9 and their present, flour mixed with oil, three-tenth deals for the bullock, two-tenth deals for the one ram,

v10 a several tenth deal for the one lamb, for the seven lambs,

v11 one kid of the goats, a sin-offering; apart from the sin-offering of the atonements, and the continual burnt-offering, and its present, and their libations.

v12 'And on the fifteenth day of the seventh month a holy convocation ye have; ye do no servile work; and ye have celebrated a festival to Jehovah seven days,

v13 and have brought near a burnt-offering, a fire-offering, a sweet fragrance, to Jehovah; thirteen bullocks, sons of the herd, two rams, fourteen lambs, sons of a year; perfect ones they are;

v14 and their present, flour mixed with oil, three-tenth deals to the one bullock, for the thirteen bullocks, two-tenth deals to the one ram, for the two rams,

v15 and a several tenth deal to the one lamb, for the fourteen lambs,

v16 and one kid of the goats, a sin-offering; apart from the continual burnt-offering, its present, and its libation.

v17 'And on the second day twelve bullocks, sons of the herd, two rams, fourteen lambs, sons of a year, perfect ones;

v18 and their present, and their libations, for the bullocks, for the rams, and for the sheep, in their number, according to the ordinance;

v19 and one kid of the goats, a sin-offering; apart from the continual burnt-offering, and its present, and their libations.

v20 'And on the third day eleven bullocks, two rams, fourteen lambs, sons of a year, perfect ones;

v21 and their present, and their libations, for the bullocks, for the rams, and for the lambs, in their number, according to the ordinance;

v22 and one goat, a sin-offering; apart from the continual burnt-offering, and its present, and its libation.

v23 'And on the fourth day ten bullocks, two rams, fourteen lambs, sons of a year, perfect ones;

v24 their present, and their libations, for the bullocks, for the rams, and for the lambs, in their number, according to the ordinance;

v25 and one kid of the goats, a sin-offering, apart from the continual burnt-offering, its present, and its libation.

v26 'And on the fifth day nine bullocks, two rams, fourteen lambs, sons of a year, perfect ones;

v27 and their present, and their libations, for the bullocks, for the rams, and for the lambs, in their number, according to the ordinance;

v28 and one goat, a sin-offering; apart from the continual burnt-offering, and its present, and its libation.

v29 'And on the sixth day eight bullocks, two rams, fourteen lambs, sons of a year, perfect ones;

v30 and their present, and their libations, for the bullocks, for the rams, and for the lambs, in their number, according to the ordinance;

v31 and one goat, a sin-offering; apart from the continual burnt-offering, its present, and its libation.

v32 'And on the seventh day seven bullocks, two rams, fourteen lambs, sons of a year, perfect ones;

v33 and their present, and their libations, for the bullocks, for the rams, and for the lambs, in their number, according to the ordinance;

v34 and one goat, a sin-offering; apart from the continual burnt-offering, its present, and its libation.

v35 'On the eighth day a restraint ye have, ye do no servile work;

v36 and ye have brought near a burnt-offering, a fire-offering, a sweet fragrance, to Jehovah; one bullock, one ram, seven lambs, sons of a year, perfect ones;

v37 their present, and their libations, for the bullock, for the ram, and for the lambs, in their number, according to the ordinance;

v38 and one goat, a sin-offering; apart from the continual burnt-offering, and its present, and its libation.

v39 'These ye prepare to Jehovah in your appointed seasons, apart from your vows, and your free-will offerings, for your burnt-offerings, and for your presents, and for your libations, and for your peace-offerings.'

v40 And Moses saith unto the sons of Israel according to all that Jehovah hath commanded Moses.
