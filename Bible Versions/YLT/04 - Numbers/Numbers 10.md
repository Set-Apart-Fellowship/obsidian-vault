# Numbers 10 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/04 - Numbers/Numbers 9|← Numbers 9]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/04 - Numbers/Numbers]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/04 - Numbers/Numbers 11|Numbers 11 →]]

***

v1 And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,

v2 'Make to thee two trumpets of silver; beaten work thou dost make them, and they have been to thee for the convocation of the company, and for the journeying of the camps;

v3 and they have blown with them, and all the company have met together unto thee, unto the opening of the tent of meeting.

v4 And if with one they blow, then have the princes, heads of the thousands of Israel, met together unto thee;

v5 'And ye have blown -- a shout, and the camps which are encamping eastward have journeyed.

v6 'And ye have blown -- a second shout, and the camps which are encamping southward have journeyed; a shout they blow for their journeys.

v7 'And in the assembling of the assembly ye blow, and do not shout;

v8 and sons of Aaron, the priests, blow with the trumpets; and they have been to you for a statute age-during to your generations.

v9 'And when ye go into battle in your land against the adversary who is distressing you, then ye have shouted with the trumpets, and ye have been remembered before Jehovah your God, and ye have been saved from your enemies.

v10 'And in the day of your gladness, and in your appointed seasons, and in the beginnings of your months, ye have blown also with the trumpets over your burnt-offerings, and over the sacrifices of your peace-offerings, and they have been to you for a memorial before your God; I, Jehovah, [am] your God.'

v11 And it cometh to pass -- in the second year, in the second month, in the twentieth of the month -- the cloud hath gone up from off the tabernacle of the testimony,

v12 and the sons of Israel journey in their journeyings from the wilderness of Sinai, and the cloud doth tabernacle in the wilderness of Paran;

v13 and they journey at first, by the command of Jehovah, in the hand of Moses.

v14 And the standard of the camp of the sons of Judah journeyeth in the first [place], by their hosts, and over its host [is] Nahshon son of Amminadab.

v15 And over the host of the tribe of the sons of Issachar [is] Nathaneel son of Zuar.

v16 And over the host of the tribe of the sons of Zebulun [is] Eliab son of Helon;

v17 And the tabernacle hath been taken down, and the sons of Gershon and the sons of Merari have journeyed, bearing the tabernacle.

v18 And the standard of the camp of Reuben hath journeyed, by their hosts, and over its host [is] Elizur son of Shedeur.

v19 And over the host of the tribe of the sons of Simeon [is] Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai.

v20 And over the host of the tribe of the sons of Gad [is] Eliasaph son of Deuel;

v21 And the Kohathites have journeyed, bearing the tabernacle, and the [others] have raised up the tabernacle until their coming in.

v22 And the standard of the camp of the sons of Ephraim hath journeyed, by their hosts, and over its host [is] Elishama son of Ammihud.

v23 And over the host of the tribe of the sons of Manasseh [is] Gamalial son of Pedahzur.

v24 And over the host of the tribe of the sons of Benjamin [is] Abook_idan son of Gideoni.

v25 And the standard of the camp of the sons of Dan hath journeyed (rearward to all the camps), by their hosts, and over its host [is] Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai.

v26 And over the host of the tribe of the sons of Asher [is] Pagiel son of Ocran.

v27 And over the host of the tribe of the sons of Naphtali [is] Ahira son of Enan.

v28 These [are] journeyings of the sons of Israel by their hosts -- and they journey.

v29 And Moses saith to Hobab son of Raguel the Midianite, father-in-law of Moses, 'We are journeying unto the place of which Jehovah hath said, I give it to you; go with us, and we have done good to thee; for Jehovah hath spoken good concerning Israel.'

v30 And he saith unto him, 'I do not go; but unto my land and unto my kindred do I go.'

v31 And he saith, 'I pray thee, forsake us not, because thou hast known our encamping in the wilderness, and thou hast been to us for eyes;

v32 and it hath come to pass when thou goest with us, yea, it hath come to pass -- that good which Jehovah doth kindly with us -- it we have done kindly to thee.'

v33 And they journey from the mount of Jehovah a journey of three days; and the ark of the covenant of Jehovah is journeying before them the journey of three days, to spy out for them a resting-place;

v34 and the cloud of Jehovah [is] on them by day, in their journeying from the camp.

v35 And it cometh to pass in the journeying of the ark, that Moses saith, 'Rise, O Jehovah, and Thine enemies are scattered, and those hating Thee flee from Thy presence.'

v36 And in its resting he saith, 'Return, O Jehovah, [to] the myriads, the thousands of Israel.'
