# Numbers 2 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/04 - Numbers/Numbers 1|← Numbers 1]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/04 - Numbers/Numbers]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/04 - Numbers/Numbers 3|Numbers 3 →]]

***

v1 And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, and unto Aaron, saying,

v2 'Each by his standard, with ensigns of the house of their fathers, do the sons of Israel encamp; over-against round about the tent of meeting they encamp.'

v3 And those encamping eastward towards the sun-rising, [are of] the standard of the camp of Judah, by their hosts; and the prince of the sons of Judah [is] Nahshon, son of Amminadab;

v4 and his host, and their numbered ones, [are] four and seventy thousand and six hundred.

v5 And those encamping by him [are of] the tribe of Issachar; and the prince of the sons of Issachar [is] Nethaneel son of Zuar;

v6 and his host, and its numbered ones, [are] four and fifty thousand and four hundred.

v7 The tribe of Zebulun; and the prince of the sons of Zebulun [is] Eliab son of Helon;

v8 and his host, and its numbered ones, [are] seven and fifty thousand and four hundred;

v9 all those numbered of the camp of Judah [are] a hundred thousand, and eighty thousand, and six thousand, and four hundred, by their hosts; they journey first.

v10 The standard of the camp of Reuben [is] southward, by their hosts; and the prince of the sons of Reuben [is] Elizur son of Shedeur;

v11 and his host, and its numbered ones, [are] six and forty thousand and five hundred.

v12 And those encamping by him [are of] the tribe of Simeon; and the prince of the sons of Simeon [is] Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai;

v13 and his host, and their numbered ones, [are] nine and fifty thousand and three hundred.

v14 And the tribe of Gad; and the prince of the sons of Gad [is] Eliasaph son of Reuel;

v15 and his host, and their numbered ones, [are] five and forty thousand and six hundred and fifty.

v16 All those numbered of the camp of Reuben [are] a hundred thousand, and one and fifty thousand, and four hundred and fifty, by their hosts; and they journey second.

v17 And the tent of meeting -- the camp of the Levites -- hath journeyed in the midst of the camps; as they encamp so they journey, each at his station by their standards.

v18 The standard of the camp of Ephraim, by their hosts, [is] westward; and the prince of the sons of Ephraim [is] Elishama son of Ammihud;

v19 and his host, and their numbered ones, [are] forty thousand and five hundred.

v20 And by him [is] the tribe of Manasseh; and the prince of the sons of Manasseh [is] Gamaliel son of Pedahzur;

v21 and his host, and their numbered ones, [are] two and thirty thousand, and two hundred.

v22 And the tribe of Benjamin; and the prince of the sons of Benjamin [is] Abook_idan son of Gideoni;

v23 and his host, and their numbered ones, [are] five and thirty thousand and four hundred.

v24 All those numbered of the camp of Ephraim [are] a hundred thousand, and eight thousand, and a hundred, by their hosts; and they journey third.

v25 The standard of the camp of Dan [is] northward, by their hosts; and the prince of the sons of Dan [is] Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai;

v26 and his host, and their numbered ones, [are] two and sixty thousand and seven hundred.

v27 And those encamping by him [are of] the tribe of Asher; and the prince of the sons of Asher [is] Pagiel son of Ocran;

v28 and his host, and their numbered ones, [are] one and forty thousand and five hundred.

v29 And the tribe of Naphtali; and the prince of the sons of Naphtali [is] Ahira son of Enan;

v30 and his host, and their numbered ones, [are] three and fifty thousand and four hundred.

v31 All those numbered of the camp of Dan [are] a hundred thousand, and seven and fifty thousand, and six hundred; at the rear they journey, by their standards.

v32 These [are] those numbered of the sons of Israel by the house of their fathers; all those numbered of the camps by their hosts [are] six hundred thousand, and three thousand, and five hundred and fifty.

v33 And the Levites have not numbered themselves in the midst of the sons of Israel, as Jehovah hath commanded Moses.

v34 And the sons of Israel do according to all that Jehovah hath commanded Moses; so they have encamped by their standards, and so they have journeyed; each by his families, by the house of his fathers.
