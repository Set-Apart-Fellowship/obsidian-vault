# Numbers 1 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/04 - Numbers/Numbers]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/04 - Numbers/Numbers 2|Numbers 2 →]]

***

v1 And Jehovah speaketh unto Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, in the tent of meeting, on the first of the second month, in the second year of their going out of the land of Egypt, saying:

v2 'Take ye up the sum of all the company of the sons of Israel by their families, by the house of their fathers, in the number of names -- every male by their polls;

v3 from a son of twenty years and upward, every one going out to the host in Israel, ye do number them by their hosts, thou and Aaron;

v4 and with you there is a man for a tribe, each is a head to the house of his fathers.

v5 'And these [are] the names of the men who stand with you: 'For Reuben -- Elizur son of Shedeur.

v6 'For Simeon -- Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai.

v7 'For Judah -- Nahshon son of Amminadab.

v8 'For Issachar -- Nathaneel son of Zuar.

v9 'For Zebulun -- Eliab son of Helon.

v10 'For the sons of Joseph -- for Ephraim: Elishama son of Ammihud: for Manasseh -- Gamaliel son of Pedahzur.

v11 'For Benjamin -- Abook_idan son of Gideoni.

v12 'For Dan -- Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai.

v13 'For Asher -- Pagiel son of Ocran.

v14 'For Gad -- Eliasaph son of Deuel.

v15 'For Naphtali -- Ahira son of Enan.'

v16 These [are] those called of the company, princes of the tribes of their fathers; they [are] heads of the thousands of Israel.

v17 And Moses taketh -- Aaron also -- these men, who were defined by name,

v18 and all the company they assembled on the first of the second month, and they declare their births, by their families, by the house of their fathers, in the number of names from a son of twenty years and upward, by their polls,

v19 as Jehovah hath commanded Moses; and he numbereth them in the wilderness of Sinai.

v20 And the sons of Reuben, Israel's first-born -- their births, by their families, by the house of their fathers, in the number of names, by their polls, every male from a son of twenty years and upward, every one going out to the host --

v21 their numbered ones, for the tribe of Reuben, are six and forty thousand and five hundred.

v22 Of the sons of Simeon -- their births, by their families, by the house of their fathers, its numbered ones in the number of names, by their polls, every male from a son of twenty years and upward, every one going out to the host --

v23 their numbered ones, for the tribe of Simeon, [are] nine and fifty thousand and three hundred.

v24 Of the sons of Gad -- their births, by their families, by the house of their fathers, in the number of names, from a son of twenty years and upward, every one going out to the host --

v25 their numbered ones, for the tribe of Gad, [are] five and forty thousand and six hundred and fifty.

v26 Of the sons of Judah -- their births, by their families, by the house of their fathers, in the number of names, from a son of twenty years and upward, every one going out to the host --

v27 their numbered ones, for the tribe of Judah, [are] four and seventy thousand and six hundred.

v28 Of the sons of Issachar -- their births, by their families, by the house of their fathers, in the number of names, from a son of twenty years and upward, every one going out to the host --

v29 their numbered ones, for the tribe of Issachar, [are] four and fifty thousand and four hundred.

v30 Of the sons of Zebulun -- their births, by their families, by the house of their fathers, in the number of names, from a son of twenty years and upward, every one going out to the host --

v31 their numbered ones, for the tribe of Zebulun, [are] seven and fifty thousand and four hundred.

v32 Of the sons of Joseph -- of the sons of Ephraim -- their births, by their families, by the house of their fathers, in the number of names, from a son of twenty years and upward, every one going out to the host --

v33 their numbered ones, for the tribe of Ephraim, [are] forty thousand and five hundred.

v34 Of the sons of Manasseh -- their births, by their families, by the house of their fathers, in the number of names, from a son of twenty years and upward, every one going out to the host --

v35 their numbered ones, for the tribe of Manasseh, [are] two and thirty thousand and two hundred.

v36 Of the sons of Benjamin -- their births, by their families, by the house of their fathers, in the number of names, from a son of twenty years and upward, every one going out to the host --

v37 their numbered ones, for the tribe of Benjamin, [are] five and thirty thousand and four hundred.

v38 Of the sons of Dan -- their births, by their families, by the house of their fathers, in the number of names, from a son of twenty years and upward, every one going out to the host --

v39 their numbered ones, for the tribe of Dan, [are] two and sixty thousand and seven hundred.

v40 Of the sons of Asher -- their births, by their families, by the house of their fathers, in the number of names, from a son of twenty years and upward, every one going out to the host --

v41 their numbered ones, for the tribe of Asher, [are] one and forty thousand and five hundred.

v42 [Of] the sons of Naphtali -- their births, by their families, by the house of their fathers, in the number of names, from a son of twenty years and upward, every one going out to the host --

v43 their numbered ones, for the tribe of Naphtali, [are] three and fifty thousand and four hundred.

v44 These [are] those numbered, whom Moses numbered -- Aaron also, and the princes of Israel, twelve men -- each for the house of his fathers, they have been.

v45 And they are, all those numbered of the sons of Israel, by the house of their fathers, from a son of twenty years and upward, every one going out to the host in Israel,

v46 yea, all those numbered are six hundred thousand, and three thousand, and five hundred and fifty.

v47 And the Levites, for the tribe of their fathers, have not numbered themselves in their midst,

v48 seeing Jehovah speaketh unto Moses, saying,

v49 'Only, the tribe of Levi thou dost not number, and their sum thou dost not take up in the midst of the sons of Israel;

v50 and thou, appoint the Levites over the tabernacle of the testimony, and over all its vessels, and over all that it hath; they bear the tabernacle, and all its vessels, and they serve it; and round about the tabernacle they encamp.

v51 'And in the journeying of the tabernacle, the Levites take it down, and in the encamping of the tabernacle, the Levites raise it up; and the stranger who is coming near is put to death.'

v52 And the sons of Israel have encamped, each by his camp, and each by his standard, by their hosts;

v53 and the Levites encamp round about the tabernacle of the testimony; and there is no wrath on the company of the sons of Israel, and the Levites have kept the charge of the tabernacle of the testimony.

v54 And the sons of Israel do according to all that Jehovah hath commanded Moses; so they have done.
