# 2 Corinthians 9 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 8|← 2 Corinthians 8]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 10|2 Corinthians 10 →]]

***

v1 For, indeed, concerning the ministration that [is] for the saints, it is superfluous for me to write to you,

v2 for I have known your readiness of mind, which in your behalf I boast of to Macedonians, that Achaia hath been prepared a year ago, and the zeal of you did stir up the more part,

v3 and I sent the brethren, that our boasting on your behalf may not be made vain in this respect; that, according as I said, ye may be ready,

v4 lest if Macedonians may come with me, and find you unprepared, we -- we may be put to shame (that we say not -- ye) in this same confidence of boasting.

v5 Necessary, therefore, I thought [it] to exhort the brethren, that they may go before to you, and may make up before your formerly announced blessing, that this be ready, as a blessing, and not as covetousness.

v6 And this: He who is sowing sparingly, sparingly also shall reap; and he who is sowing in blessings, in blessings also shall reap;

v7 each one, according as he doth purpose in heart, not out of sorrow or out of necessity, for a cheerful giver doth God love,

v8 and God [is] able all grace to cause to abound to you, that in every thing always all sufficiency having, ye may abound to every good work,

v9 (according as it hath been written, 'He dispersed abroad, he gave to the poor, his righteousness doth remain to the age,')

v10 and may He who is supplying seed to the sower, and bread for food, supply and multiply your seed sown, and increase the fruits of your righteousness,

v11 in every thing being enriched to all liberality, which doth work through us thanksgiving to God,

v12 because the ministration of this service not only is supplying the wants of the saints, but is also abounding through many thanksgivings to God,

v13 through the proof of this ministration glorifying God for the subjection of your confession to the good news of the Christ, and [for] the liberality of the fellowship to them and to all,

v14 and by their supplication in your behalf, longing after you because of the exceeding grace of God upon you;

v15 thanks also to God for His unspeakable gift!
