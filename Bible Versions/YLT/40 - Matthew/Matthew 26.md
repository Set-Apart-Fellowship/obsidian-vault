# Matthew 26 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25|← Matthew 25]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/40 - Matthew/Matthew]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/40 - Matthew/Matthew 27|Matthew 27 →]]

***

v1 And it came to pass, when Jesus finished all these words, he said to his disciples,

v2 'Ye have known that after two days the passover cometh, and the Son of Man is delivered up to be crucified.'

v3 Then were gathered together the chief priests, and the scribes, and the elders of the people, to the court of the chief priest who was called Caiaphas;

v4 and they consulted together that they might take Jesus by guile, and kill [him],

v5 and they said, 'Not in the feast, that there may not be a tumult among the people.'

v6 And Jesus having been in Bethany, in the house of Simon the leper,

v7 there came to him a woman having an alabaster box of ointment, very precious, and she poured on his head as he is reclining (at meat).

v8 And having seen [it], his disciples were much displeased, saying, 'To what purpose [is] this waste?

v9 for this ointment could have been sold for much, and given to the poor.'

v10 And Jesus having known, said to them, 'Why do ye give trouble to the woman? for a good work she wrought for me;

v11 for the poor always ye have with you, and me ye have not always;

v12 for she having put this ointment on my body -- for my burial she did [it].

v13 Verily I say to you, Wherever this good news may be proclaimed in the whole world, what this [one] did shall also be spoken of -- for a memorial of her.'

v14 Then one of the twelve, who is called Judas Iscariot, having gone unto the chief priests, said,

v15 'What are ye willing to give me, and I will deliver him up to you?' and they weighed out to him thirty silverlings,

v16 and from that time he was seeking a convenient season to deliver him up.

v17 And on the first [day] of the unleavened food came the disciples near to Jesus, saying to him, 'Where wilt thou [that] we may prepare for thee to eat the passover?'

v18 and he said, 'Go away to the city, unto such a one, and say to him, The Teacher saith, My time is nigh; near thee I keep the passover, with my disciples;'

v19 and the disciples did as Jesus appointed them, and prepared the passover.

v20 And evening having come, he was reclining (at meat) with the twelve,

v21 and while they are eating, he said, 'Verily I say to you, that one of you shall deliver me up.'

v22 And being grieved exceedingly, they began to say to him, each of them, 'Is it I, Sir?'

v23 And he answering said, 'He who did dip with me the hand in the dish, he will deliver me up;

v24 the Son of Man doth indeed go, as it hath been written concerning him, but wo to that man through whom the Son of Man is delivered up! good it were for him if that man had not been born.'

v25 And Judas -- he who delivered him up -- answering said, 'Is it I, Rabbi?' He saith to him, 'Thou hast said.'

v26 And while they were eating, Jesus having taken the bread, and having blessed, did brake, and was giving to the disciples, and said, 'Take, eat, this is my body;'

v27 and having taken the cup, and having given thanks, he gave to them, saying, 'Drink ye of it -- all;

v28 for this is my blood of the new covenant, that for many is being poured out -- to remission of sins;

v29 and I say to you, that I may not drink henceforth on this produce of the vine, till that day when I may drink it with you new in the reign of my Father.'

v30 And having sung a hymn, they went forth to the mount of the Olives;

v31 then saith Jesus to them, 'All ye shall be stumbled at me this night; for it hath been written, I will smite the shepherd, and the sheep of the flock shall be scattered abroad;

v32 but, after my having risen, I will go before you to Galilee.'

v33 And Peter answering said to him, 'Even if all shall be stumbled at thee, I will never be stumbled.'

v34 Jesus said to him, 'Verily I say to thee, that, this night, before cock-crowing, thrice thou wilt deny me.'

v35 Peter saith to him, 'Even if it may be necessary for me to die with thee, I will not deny thee;' in like manner also said all the disciples.

v36 Then come with them doth Jesus to a place called Gethsemane, and he saith to the disciples, 'Sit ye here, till having gone away, I shall pray yonder.'

v37 And having taken Peter, and the two sons of Zebedee, he began to be sorrowful, and to be very heavy;

v38 then saith he to them, 'Exceedingly sorrowful is my soul -- unto death; abook_ide ye here, and watch with me.'

v39 And having gone forward a little, he fell on his face, praying, and saying, 'My Father, if it be possible, let this cup pass from me; nevertheless, not as I will, but as Thou.'

v40 And he cometh unto the disciples, and findeth them sleeping, and he saith to Peter, 'So! ye were not able one hour to watch with me!

v41 watch, and pray, that ye may not enter into temptation: the spirit indeed is forward, but the flesh weak.'

v42 Again, a second time, having gone away, he prayed, saying, 'My Father, if this cup cannot pass away from me except I drink it, Thy will be done;'

v43 and having come, he findeth them again sleeping, for their eyes were heavy.

v44 And having left them, having gone away again, he prayed a third time, saying the same word;

v45 then cometh he unto his disciples, and saith to them, 'Sleep on henceforth, and rest! lo, the hour hath come nigh, and the Son of Man is delivered up to the hands of sinners.

v46 Rise, let us go; lo, he hath come nigh who is delivering me up.'

v47 And while he is yet speaking, lo, Judas, one of the twelve did come, and with him a great multitude, with swords and sticks, from the chief priests and elders of the people.

v48 And he who did deliver him up did give them a sign, saying, 'Whomsoever I will kiss, it is he: lay hold on him;'

v49 and immediately, having come to Jesus, he said, 'Hail, Rabbi,' and kissed him;

v50 and Jesus said to him, 'Comrade, for what art thou present?' Then having come near, they laid hands on Jesus, and took hold on him.

v51 And lo, one of those with Jesus, having stretched forth the hand, drew his sword, and having struck the servant of the chief priest, he took off his ear.

v52 Then saith Jesus to him, 'Turn back thy sword to its place; for all who did take the sword, by the sword shall perish;

v53 dost thou think that I am not able now to call upon my Father, and He will place beside me more than twelve legions of messengers?

v54 how then may the Writings be fulfilled, that thus it behoveth to happen?'

v55 In that hour said Jesus to the multitudes, 'As against a robber ye did come forth, with swords and sticks, to take me! daily with you I was sitting teaching in the temple, and ye did not lay hold on me;

v56 but all this hath come to pass, that the Writings of the prophets may be fulfilled;' then all the disciples, having left him, fled.

v57 And those laying hold on Jesus led [him] away unto Caiaphas the chief priest, where the scribes and the elders were gathered together,

v58 and Peter was following him afar off, unto the court of the chief priest, and having gone in within, he was sitting with the officers, to see the end.

v59 And the chief priests, and the elders, and all the council, were seeking false witness against Jesus, that they might put him to death,

v60 and they did not find; and many false witnesses having come near, they did not find; and at last two false witnesses having come near,

v61 said, 'This one said, I am able to throw down the sanctuary of God, and after three days to build it.'

v62 And the chief priest having stood up, said to him, 'Nothing thou dost answer! what do these witness against thee?

v63 and Jesus was silent. And the chief priest answering said to him, 'I adjure thee, by the living God, that thou mayest say to us, if thou art the Christ -- the Son of God.'

v64 Jesus saith to him, 'Thou hast said; nevertheless I say to you, hereafter ye shall see the Son of Man sitting on the right hand of the power, and coming upon the clouds, of the heaven.'

v65 Then the chief priest rent his garments, saying, -- 'He hath spoken evil; what need have we yet of witnesses? lo, now ye heard his evil speaking;

v66 what think ye?' and they answering said, 'He is worthy of death.'

v67 Then did they spit in his face and buffet him, and others did slap,

v68 saying, 'Declare to us, O Christ, who he is that struck thee?'

v69 And Peter without was sitting in the court, and there came near to him a certain maid, saying, 'And thou wast with Jesus of Galilee!'

v70 And he denied before all, saying, 'I have not known what thou sayest.'

v71 And he having gone forth to the porch, another female saw him, and saith to those there, 'And this one was with Jesus of Nazareth;'

v72 and again did he deny with an oath -- 'I have not known the man.'

v73 And after a little those standing near having come, said to Peter, 'Truly thou also art of them, for even thy speech doth make thee manifest.'

v74 Then began he to anathematise, and to swear -- 'I have not known the man;' and immediately did a cock crow,

v75 and Peter remembered the saying of Jesus, he having said to him -- 'Before cock-crowing, thrice thou wilt deny me;' and having gone without, he did weep bitterly.
