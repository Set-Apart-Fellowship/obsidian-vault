# Psalms 134 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 133|← Psalms 133]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 135|Psalms 135 →]]

***

v1 A Song of the Ascents. Lo, bless Jehovah, all servants of Jehovah, Who are standing in the house of Jehovah by night.

v2 Lift up your hands [in] the sanctuary, And bless ye Jehovah.

v3 Jehovah doth bless thee out of Zion, The maker of the heavens and earth!
