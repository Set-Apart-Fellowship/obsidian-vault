# Psalms 99 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 98|← Psalms 98]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 100|Psalms 100 →]]

***

v1 Jehovah hath reigned, peoples tremble, The Inhabitant of the cherubs, the earth shaketh.

v2 Jehovah in Zion [is] great, And high He [is] over all the peoples.

v3 They praise Thy name, 'Great, and fearful, holy [it] is.'

v4 And the strength of the king Hath loved judgment, Thou -- Thou hast established uprightness; Judgment and righteousness in Jacob, Thou -- Thou hast done.

v5 Exalt ye Jehovah our God, And bow yourselves at His footstool, holy [is] He.

v6 Moses and Aaron among His priests, And Samuel among those proclaiming His name. They are calling unto Jehovah, And He doth answer them.

v7 In a pillar of cloud He speaketh unto them, They have kept His testimonies, And the statute He hath given to them.

v8 O Jehovah, our God, Thou hast afflicted them, A God forgiving Thou hast been to them, And taking vengeance on their actions.

v9 Exalt ye Jehovah our God, And bow yourselves at His holy hill, For holy [is] Jehovah our God!
