# Psalms 100 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 99|← Psalms 99]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 101|Psalms 101 →]]

***

v1 A Psalm of Thanksgiving. Shout to Jehovah, all the earth.

v2 Serve Jehovah with joy, come before him with singing.

v3 Know that Jehovah He [is] God, He made us, and we are His, His people -- and the flock of His pasture.

v4 Enter ye His gates with thanksgiving, His courts with praise, Give ye thanks to Him, bless ye His Name.

v5 For good [is] Jehovah, to the age His kindness, And to generation and generation His faithfulness!
