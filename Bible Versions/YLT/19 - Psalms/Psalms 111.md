# Psalms 111 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 110|← Psalms 110]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 112|Psalms 112 →]]

***

v1 Praise ye Jah! I thank Jehovah with the whole heart, In the secret meeting of the upright, And of the company.

v2 Great [are] the works of Jehovah, Sought out by all desiring them.

v3 Honourable and majestic is His work, And His righteousness is standing for ever.

v4 A memorial He hath made of His wonders, Gracious and merciful [is] Jehovah.

v5 Prey He hath given to those fearing Him, He remembereth to the age His covenant.

v6 The power of His works He hath declared to His people, To give to them the inheritance of nations.

v7 The works of His hands [are] true and just, Stedfast [are] all His appointments.

v8 They are sustained for ever to the age. They are made in truth and uprightness.

v9 Redemption He hath sent to His people, He hath appointed to the age His covenant, Holy and fearful [is] His name.

v10 The beginning of wisdom [is] fear of Jehovah, Good understanding have all doing them, His praise [is] standing for ever!
