# Psalms 102 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 101|← Psalms 101]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 103|Psalms 103 →]]

***

v1 A Prayer of the afflicted when he is feeble, and before Jehovah poureth out his plaint. O Jehovah, hear my prayer, yea, my cry to Thee cometh.

v2 Hide not Thou Thy face from me, In a day of mine adversity, Incline unto me Thine ear, In the day I call, haste, answer me.

v3 For consumed in smoke have been my days, And my bones as a fire-brand have burned.

v4 Smitten as the herb, and withered, is my heart, For I have forgotten to eat my bread.

v5 From the voice of my sighing Hath my bone cleaved to my flesh.

v6 I have been like to a pelican of the wilderness, I have been as an owl of the dry places.

v7 I have watched, and I am As a bird alone on the roof.

v8 All the day mine enemies reproached me, Those mad at me have sworn against me.

v9 Because ashes as bread I have eaten, And my drink with weeping have mingled,

v10 From Thine indignation and Thy wrath, For Thou hast lifted me up, And dost cast me down.

v11 My days as a shadow [are] stretched out, And I -- as the herb I am withered.

v12 And Thou, O Jehovah, to the age abook_idest, And Thy memorial to all generations.

v13 Thou -- Thou risest -- Thou pitiest Zion, For the time to favour her, For the appointed time hath come.

v14 For Thy servants have been pleased with her stones, And her dust they favour.

v15 And nations fear the name of Jehovah, And all kings of the earth Thine honour,

v16 For Jehovah hath builded Zion, He hath been seen in His honour,

v17 He turned unto the prayer of the destitute, And He hath not despised their prayer.

v18 This is written for a later generation, And the people created do praise Jah.

v19 For He hath looked From the high place of His sanctuary. Jehovah from heaven unto earth looked attentively,

v20 To hear the groan of the prisoner, To loose sons of death,

v21 To declare in Zion the name of Jehovah, And His praise in Jerusalem,

v22 In the peoples being gathered together, And the kingdoms -- to serve Jehovah.

v23 He hath humbled in the way my power, He hath shortened my days.

v24 I say, 'My God, take me not up in the midst of my days,' Through all generations [are] Thine years.

v25 Beforetime the earth Thou didst found, And the work of Thy hands [are] the heavens.

v26 They -- They perish, and Thou remainest, And all of them as a garment become old, As clothing Thou changest them, And they are changed.

v27 And Thou [art] the same, and Thine years are not finished.

v28 The sons of Thy servants do continue, And their seed before Thee is established!
