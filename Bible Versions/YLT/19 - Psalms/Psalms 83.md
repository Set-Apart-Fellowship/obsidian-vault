# Psalms 83 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 82|← Psalms 82]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 84|Psalms 84 →]]

***

v1 A Song, -- A Psalm of Asaph. O God, let there be no silence to Thee, Be not silent, nor be quiet, O God.

v2 For, lo, Thine enemies do roar, And those hating Thee have lifted up the head,

v3 Against Thy people they take crafty counsel, And consult against Thy hidden ones.

v4 They have said, 'Come, And we cut them off from [being] a nation, And the name of Israel is not remembered any more.'

v5 For they consulted in heart together, Against Thee a covenant they make,

v6 Tents of Edom, and Ishmaelites, Moab, and the Hagarenes,

v7 Gebal, and Ammon, and Amalek, Philistia with inhabitants of Tyre,

v8 Asshur also is joined with them, They have been an arm to sons of Lot. Selah.

v9 Do to them as [to] Midian, As [to] Sisera, as [to] Jabin, at the stream Kishon.

v10 They were destroyed at Endor, They were dung for the ground!

v11 Make their nobles as Oreb and as Zeeb, And as Zebah and Zalmunna all their princes,

v12 Who have said, 'Let us occupy for ourselves The comely places of God.'

v13 O my God, make them as a rolling thing, As stubble before wind.

v14 As a fire doth burn a forest, And as a flame setteth hills on fire,

v15 So dost Thou pursue them with Thy whirlwind, And with Thy hurricane troublest them.

v16 Fill their faces [with] shame, And they seek Thy name, O Jehovah.

v17 They are ashamed and troubled for ever, Yea, they are confounded and lost.

v18 And they know that Thou -- (Thy name [is] Jehovah -- by Thyself,) [Art] the Most High over all the earth!
