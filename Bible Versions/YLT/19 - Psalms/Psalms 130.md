# Psalms 130 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 129|← Psalms 129]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 131|Psalms 131 →]]

***

v1 A Song of the Ascents. From depths I have called Thee, Jehovah.

v2 Lord, hearken to my voice, Thine ears are attentive to the voice of my supplications.

v3 If iniquities Thou dost observe, O Lord, who doth stand?

v4 But with Thee [is] forgiveness, that Thou mayest be feared.

v5 I hoped [for] Jehovah -- hoped hath my soul, And for His word I have waited.

v6 My soul [is] for the Lord, More than those watching for morning, Watching for morning!

v7 Israel doth wait on Jehovah, For with Jehovah [is] kindness, And abundant with Him [is] redemption.

v8 And He doth redeem Israel from all his iniquities!
