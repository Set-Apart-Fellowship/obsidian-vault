# Psalms 107 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 106|← Psalms 106]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 108|Psalms 108 →]]

***

v1 'Give ye thanks to Jehovah, For good, for to the age [is] His kindness:'

v2 Let the redeemed of Jehovah say, Whom He redeemed from the hand of an adversary.

v3 And from the lands hath gathered them, From east and from west, From north, and from the sea.

v4 They wandered in a wilderness, in a desert by the way, A city of habitation they have not found.

v5 Hungry -- yea -- thirsty, Their soul in them becometh feeble,

v6 And they cry unto Jehovah in their adversity, From their distress He delivereth them,

v7 And causeth them to tread in a right way, To go unto a city of habitation.

v8 They confess to Jehovah His kindness, And His wonders to the sons of men.

v9 For He hath satisfied a longing soul, And a hungry soul hath filled [with] goodness.

v10 Inhabitants of dark places and death-shade, Prisoners of affliction and of iron,

v11 Because they changed the saying of God, And the counsel of the Most High despised.

v12 And He humbleth with labour their heart, They have been feeble, and there is no helper.

v13 And they cry unto Jehovah in their adversity, From their distresses He saveth them.

v14 He bringeth them out from the dark place, And death-shade, And their bands He draweth away.

v15 They confess to Jehovah His kindness, And His wonders to the sons of men.

v16 For He hath broken doors of brass, And bars of iron He hath cut.

v17 Fools, by means of their transgression, And by their iniquities, afflict themselves.

v18 All food doth their soul abominate, And they come nigh unto the gates of death,

v19 And cry unto Jehovah in their adversity, From their distresses He saveth them,

v20 He sendeth His word and healeth them, And delivereth from their destructions.

v21 They confess to Jehovah His kindness, And His wonders to the sons of men,

v22 And they sacrifice sacrifices of thanksgiving, And recount His works with singing.

v23 Those going down [to] the sea in ships, Doing business in many waters,

v24 They have seen the works of Jehovah, And His wonders in the deep.

v25 And He saith, and appointeth a tempest, And it lifteth up its billows,

v26 They go up [to] the heavens, they go down [to] the depths, Their soul in evil is melted.

v27 They reel to and fro, and move as a drunkard, And all their wisdom is swallowed up.

v28 And they cry to Jehovah in their adversity, And from their distresses He bringeth them out.

v29 He establisheth a whirlwind to a calm, And hushed are their billows.

v30 And they rejoice because they are quiet, And He leadeth them to the haven of their desire.

v31 They confess to Jehovah His kindness, And His wonders to the sons of men,

v32 And they exalt Him in the assembly of the people, And in the seat of the elders praise Him.

v33 He maketh rivers become a wilderness, And fountains of waters become dry land.

v34 A fruitful land becometh a barren place, For the wickedness of its inhabitants.

v35 He maketh a wilderness become a pool of water, And a dry land become fountains of waters.

v36 And He causeth the hungry to dwell there, And they prepare a city of habitation.

v37 And they sow fields, and plant vineyards, And they make fruits of increase.

v38 And He blesseth them, and they multiply exceedingly, And their cattle He doth not diminish.

v39 And they are diminished, and bow down, By restraint, evil, and sorrow.

v40 He is pouring contempt upon nobles, And causeth them to wander in vacancy -- no way.

v41 And setteth on high the needy from affliction, And placeth families as a flock.

v42 The upright do see and rejoice, And all perversity hath shut her mouth.

v43 Who [is] wise, and observeth these? They understand the kind acts of Jehovah!
