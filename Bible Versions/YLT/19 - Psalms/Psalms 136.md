# Psalms 136 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 135|← Psalms 135]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 137|Psalms 137 →]]

***

v1 Give ye thanks to Jehovah, For good, for to the age [is] His kindness.

v2 Give ye thanks to the God of gods, For to the age [is] His kindness.

v3 Give ye thanks to the Lord of lords, For to the age [is] His kindness.

v4 To Him doing great wonders by Himself alone, For to the age [is] His kindness.

v5 To Him making the heavens by understanding, For to the age [is] His kindness.

v6 To Him spreading the earth over the waters, For to the age [is] His kindness.

v7 To Him making great lights, For to the age [is] His kindness.

v8 The sun to rule by day, For to the age [is] His kindness.

v9 The moon and stars to rule by night, For to the age [is] His kindness.

v10 To Him smiting Egypt in their first-born, For to the age [is] His kindness.

v11 And bringing forth Israel from their midst, For to the age [is] His kindness.

v12 By a strong hand, and a stretched-out-arm, For to the age [is] His kindness.

v13 To Him cutting the sea of Suph into parts, For to the age [is] His kindness,

v14 And caused Israel to pass through its midst, For to the age [is] His kindness,

v15 And shook out Pharaoh and his force in the sea of Suph, For to the age [is] His kindness.

v16 To Him leading His people in a wilderness, For to the age [is] His kindness.

v17 To Him smiting great kings, For to the age [is] His kindness.

v18 Yea, He doth slay honourable kings, For to the age [is] His kindness.

v19 Even Sihon king of the Amorite, For to the age [is] His kindness.

v20 And Og king of Bashan, For to the age [is] His kindness.

v21 And He gave their land for inheritance, For to the age [is] His kindness.

v22 An inheritance to Israel His servant, For to the age [is] His kindness.

v23 Who in our lowliness hath remembered us, For to the age [is] His kindness.

v24 And He delivereth us from our adversaries, For to the age [is] His kindness.

v25 Giving food to all flesh, For to the age [is] His kindness.

v26 Give ye thanks to the God of the heavens, For to the age [is] His kindness!
