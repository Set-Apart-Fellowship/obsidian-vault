# Psalms 37 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 36|← Psalms 36]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 38|Psalms 38 →]]

***

v1 By David. Do not fret because of evil doers, Be not envious against doers of iniquity,

v2 For as grass speedily they are cut off, And as the greenness of the tender grass do fade.

v3 Trust in Jehovah, and do good, Dwell [in] the land, and enjoy faithfulness,

v4 And delight thyself on Jehovah, And He giveth to thee the petitions of thy heart.

v5 Roll on Jehovah thy way, And trust upon Him, and He worketh,

v6 And hath brought out as light thy righteousness, And thy judgment as noon-day.

v7 Be silent for Jehovah, and stay thyself for Him, Do not fret because of him Who is making prosperous his way, Because of a man doing wicked devices.

v8 Desist from anger, and forsake fury, Fret not thyself only to do evil.

v9 For evil doers are cut off, As to those waiting on Jehovah, they possess the land.

v10 And yet a little, and the wicked is not, And thou hast considered his place, and it is not.

v11 And the humble do possess the land, And they have delighted themselves In the abundance of peace.

v12 The wicked is devising against the righteous, And gnashing against him his teeth.

v13 The Lord doth laugh at him, For He hath seen that his day cometh.

v14 A sword have the wicked opened, And they have trodden their bow, To cause to fall the poor and needy, To slaughter the upright of the way.

v15 Their sword doth enter into their own heart, And their bows are shivered.

v16 Better [is] the little of the righteous, Than the store of many wicked.

v17 For the arms of the wicked are shivered, And Jehovah is sustaining the righteous.

v18 Jehovah knoweth the days of the perfect, And their inheritance is -- to the age.

v19 They are not ashamed in a time of evil, And in days of famine they are satisfied.

v20 But the wicked perish, and the enemies of Jehovah, As the preciousness of lambs, Have been consumed, In smoke they have been consumed.

v21 The wicked is borrowing and repayeth not, And the righteous is gracious and giving.

v22 For His blessed ones do possess the land, And His reviled ones are cut off.

v23 From Jehovah [are] the steps of a man, They have been prepared, And his way he desireth.

v24 When he falleth, he is not cast down, For Jehovah is sustaining his hand.

v25 Young I have been, I have also become old, And I have not seen the righteous forsaken, And his seed seeking bread.

v26 All the day he is gracious and lending, And his seed [is] for a blessing.

v27 Turn aside from evil, and do good, and dwell to the age.

v28 For Jehovah is loving judgment, And He doth not forsake His saintly ones, To the age they have been kept, And the seed of the wicked is cut off.

v29 The righteous possess the land, And they dwell for ever on it.

v30 The mouth of the righteous uttereth wisdom, And his tongue speaketh judgment.

v31 The law of his God [is] his heart, His steps do not slide.

v32 The wicked is watching for the righteous, And is seeking to put him to death.

v33 Jehovah doth not leave him in his hand, Nor condemn him in his being judged.

v34 Look unto Jehovah, and keep His way, And He doth exalt thee to possess the land, In the wicked being cut off -- thou seest!

v35 I have seen the wicked terrible, And spreading as a green native plant,

v36 And he passeth away, and lo, he is not, And I seek him, and he is not found!

v37 Observe the perfect, and see the upright, For the latter end of each [is] peace.

v38 And transgressors were destroyed together, The latter end of the wicked was cut off.

v39 And the salvation of the righteous [is] from Jehovah, Their strong place in a time of adversity.

v40 And Jehovah doth help them and deliver them, He delivereth them from the wicked, And saveth them, Because they trusted in Him!
