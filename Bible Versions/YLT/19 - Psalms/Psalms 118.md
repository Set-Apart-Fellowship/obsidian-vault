# Psalms 118 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 117|← Psalms 117]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 119|Psalms 119 →]]

***

v1 Give ye thanks to Jehovah, For good, for to the age [is] His kindness.

v2 I pray you, let Israel say, That, to the age [is] His kindness.

v3 I pray you, let the house of Aaron say, That, to the age [is] His kindness.

v4 I pray you, let those fearing Jehovah say, That, to the age [is] His kindness.

v5 From the straitness I called Jah, Jah answered me in a broad place.

v6 Jehovah [is] for me, I do not fear what man doth to me.

v7 Jehovah [is] for me among my helpers, And I -- I look on those hating me.

v8 Better to take refuge in Jehovah than to trust in man,

v9 Better to take refuge in Jehovah, Than to trust in princes.

v10 All nations have compassed me about, In the name of Jehovah I surely cut them off.

v11 They have compassed me about, Yea, they have compassed me about, In the name of Jehovah I surely cut them off.

v12 They compassed me about as bees, They have been extinguished as a fire of thorns, In the name of Jehovah I surely cut them off.

v13 Thou hast sorely thrust me to fall, And Jehovah hath helped me.

v14 My strength and song [is] Jah, And He is to me for salvation.

v15 A voice of singing and salvation, [Is] in the tents of the righteous, The right hand of Jehovah is doing valiantly.

v16 The right hand of Jehovah is exalted, The right hand of Jehovah is doing valiantly.

v17 I do not die, but live, And recount the works of Jah,

v18 Jah hath sorely chastened me, And to death hath not given me up.

v19 Open ye to me gates of righteousness, I enter into them -- I thank Jah.

v20 This [is] the gate to Jehovah, The righteous enter into it.

v21 I thank Thee, for Thou hast answered me, And art to me for salvation.

v22 A stone the builders refused Hath become head of a corner.

v23 From Jehovah hath this been, It [is] wonderful in our eyes,

v24 This [is] the day Jehovah hath made, We rejoice and are glad in it.

v25 I beseech Thee, O Jehovah, save, I pray Thee, I beseech Thee, O Jehovah, prosper, I pray Thee.

v26 Blessed [is] he who is coming In the name of Jehovah, We blessed you from the house of Jehovah,

v27 God [is] Jehovah, and He giveth to us light, Direct ye the festal-sacrifice with cords, Unto the horns of the altar.

v28 My God Thou [art], and I confess Thee, My God, I exalt Thee.

v29 Give ye thanks to Jehovah, For good, for to the age, [is] His kindness!
