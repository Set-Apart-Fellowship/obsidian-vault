# Psalms 77 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 76|← Psalms 76]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 78|Psalms 78 →]]

***

v1 To the Overseer, for Jeduthun. -- A Psalm of Asaph. My voice [is] to God, and I cry, my voice [is] to God, And He hath given ear unto me.

v2 In a day of my distress the Lord I sought, My hand by night hath been spread out, And it doth not cease, My soul hath refused to be comforted.

v3 I remember God, and make a noise, I meditate, and feeble is my spirit. Selah.

v4 Thou hast taken hold of the watches of mine eyes, I have been moved, and I speak not.

v5 I have reckoned the days of old, The years of the ages.

v6 I remember my music in the night, With my heart I meditate, and my spirit doth search diligently:

v7 To the ages doth the Lord cast off? Doth He add to be pleased no more?

v8 Hath His kindness ceased for ever? The saying failed to all generations?

v9 Hath God forgotten [His] favours? Hath He shut up in anger His mercies? Selah.

v10 And I say: 'My weakness is, The changes of the right hand of the Most High.'

v11 I mention the doings of Jah, For I remember of old Thy wonders,

v12 And I have meditated on all Thy working, And I talk concerning Thy doings.

v13 O God, in holiness [is] Thy way, Who [is] a great god like God?

v14 Thou [art] the God doing wonders. Thou hast made known among the peoples Thy strength,

v15 Thou hast redeemed with strength Thy people, The sons of Jacob and Joseph. Selah.

v16 The waters have seen Thee, O God, The waters have seen Thee, They are afraid -- also depths are troubled.

v17 Poured out waters have thick clouds, The skies have given forth a noise, Also -- Thine arrows go up and down.

v18 The voice of Thy thunder [is] in the spheres, Lightnings have lightened the world, The earth hath trembled, yea, it shaketh.

v19 In the sea [is] Thy way, And Thy paths [are] in many waters, And Thy tracks have not been known.

v20 Thou hast led as a flock Thy people, By the hand of Moses and Aaron!
