# Psalms 73 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 72|← Psalms 72]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 74|Psalms 74 →]]

***

v1 A Psalm of Asaph. Only -- good to Israel [is] God, to the clean of heart. And I -- as a little thing, My feet have been turned aside,

v2 As nothing, have my steps slipped, For I have been envious of the boastful,

v3 The peace of the wicked I see, That there are no bands at their death,

v4 And their might [is] firm.

v5 In the misery of mortals they are not, And with common men they are not plagued.

v6 Therefore hath pride encircled them, Violence covereth them as a dress.

v7 Their eye hath come out from fat. The imaginations of the heart transgressed;

v8 They do corruptly, And they speak in the wickedness of oppression, From on high they speak.

v9 They have set in the heavens their mouth, And their tongue walketh in the earth.

v10 Therefore do His people return hither, And waters of fulness are wrung out to them.

v11 And they have said, 'How hath God known? And is there knowledge in the Most High?'

v12 Lo, these [are] the wicked and easy ones of the age, They have increased strength.

v13 Only -- a vain thing! I have purified my heart, And I wash in innocency my hands,

v14 And I am plagued all the day, And my reproof [is] every morning.

v15 If I have said, 'I recount thus,' Lo, a generation of Thy sons I have deceived.

v16 And I think to know this, Perverseness it [is] in mine eyes,

v17 Till I come in to the sanctuaries of God, I attend to their latter end.

v18 Only, in slippery places Thou dost set them, Thou hast caused them to fall to desolations.

v19 How have they become a desolation as in a moment, They have been ended -- consumed from terrors.

v20 As a dream from awakening, O Lord, In awaking, their image Thou despisest.

v21 For my heart doth show itself violent, And my reins prick themselves,

v22 And I am brutish, and do not know. A beast I have been with Thee.

v23 And I [am] continually with Thee, Thou hast laid hold on my right hand.

v24 With Thy counsel Thou dost lead me, And after honour dost receive me.

v25 Whom have I in the heavens? And with Thee none I have desired in earth.

v26 Consumed hath been my flesh and my heart, The rock of my heart and my portion [is] God to the age.

v27 For, lo, those far from Thee do perish, Thou hast cut off every one, Who is going a whoring from Thee.

v28 And I -- nearness of God to me [is] good, I have placed in the Lord Jehovah my refuge, To recount all Thy works!
