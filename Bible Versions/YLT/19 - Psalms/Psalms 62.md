# Psalms 62 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 61|← Psalms 61]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 63|Psalms 63 →]]

***

v1 To the Overseer, for Jeduthun. -- A Psalm of David. Only -- toward God [is] my soul silent, From Him [is] my salvation.

v2 Only -- He [is] my rock, and my salvation, My tower, I am not much moved.

v3 Till when do ye devise mischief against a man? Ye are destroyed all of you, As a wall inclined, a hedge that is cast down.

v4 Only -- from his excellency They have consulted to drive away, They enjoy a lie, with their mouth they bless, And with their heart revile. Selah.

v5 Only -- for God, be silent, O my soul, For from Him [is] my hope.

v6 Only -- He [is] my rock and my salvation, My tower, I am not moved.

v7 On God [is] my salvation, and my honour, The rock of my strength, my refuge [is] in God.

v8 Trust in Him at all times, O people, Pour forth before Him your heart, God [is] a refuge for us. Selah.

v9 Only -- vanity [are] the low, a lie the high. In balances to go up they than vanity [are] lighter.

v10 Trust not in oppression, And in robbery become not vain, Wealth -- when it increaseth -- set not the heart.

v11 Once hath God spoken, twice I heard this, That 'strength [is] with God.'

v12 And with Thee, O Lord, [is] kindness, For Thou dost recompense to each, According to his work!
