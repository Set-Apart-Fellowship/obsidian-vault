# Psalms 87 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 86|← Psalms 86]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 88|Psalms 88 →]]

***

v1 By sons of Korah. -- A Psalm, a song. His foundation [is] in holy mountains.

v2 Jehovah is loving the gates of Zion Above all the tabernacles of Jacob.

v3 Honourable things are spoken in Thee, O city of God. Selah.

v4 I mention Rahab and Babel to those knowing Me, Lo, Philistia, and Tyre, with Cush! This [one] was born there.

v5 And of Zion it is said: Each one was born in her, And He, the Most High, doth establish her.

v6 Jehovah doth recount in the describing of the peoples, 'This [one] was born there.' Selah.

v7 Singers also as players on instruments, All my fountains [are] in Thee!
