# Psalms 97 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 96|← Psalms 96]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 98|Psalms 98 →]]

***

v1 Jehovah hath reigned, The earth is joyful, many isles rejoice.

v2 Cloud and darkness [are] round about Him, Righteousness and judgment the basis of His throne.

v3 Fire before Him goeth, And burneth round about His adversaries.

v4 Lightened have His lightnings the world, The earth hath seen, and is pained.

v5 Hills, like wax, melted before Jehovah, Before the Lord of all the earth.

v6 The heavens declared His righteousness, And all the peoples have seen His honour.

v7 Ashamed are all servants of a graven image, Those boasting themselves in idols, Bow yourselves to him, all ye gods.

v8 Zion hath heard and rejoiceth, And daughters of Judah are joyful, Because of Thy judgments, O Jehovah.

v9 For Thou, Jehovah, [art] Most High over all the earth, Greatly Thou hast been exalted over all gods.

v10 Ye who love Jehovah, hate evil, He is keeping the souls of His saints, From the hand of the wicked he delivereth them.

v11 Light [is] sown for the righteous, And for the upright of heart -- joy.

v12 Rejoice, ye righteous, in Jehovah, And give thanks at the remembrance of his holiness!
