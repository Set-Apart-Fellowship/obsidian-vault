# Psalms 91 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 90|← Psalms 90]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 92|Psalms 92 →]]

***

v1 He who is dwelling In the secret place of the Most High, In the shade of the Mighty lodgeth habitually,

v2 He is saying of Jehovah, 'My refuge, and my bulwark, my God, I trust in Him,'

v3 For He delivereth thee from the snare of a fowler, From a calamitous pestilence.

v4 With His pinion He covereth thee over, And under His wings thou dost trust, A shield and buckler [is] His truth.

v5 Thou art not afraid of fear by night, Of arrow that flieth by day,

v6 Of pestilence in thick darkness that walketh, Of destruction that destroyeth at noon,

v7 There fall at thy side a thousand, And a myriad at thy right hand, Unto thee it cometh not nigh.

v8 But with thine eyes thou lookest, And the reward of the wicked thou seest,

v9 (For Thou, O Jehovah, [art] my refuge,) The Most High thou madest thy habitation.

v10 Evil happeneth not unto thee, And a plague cometh not near thy tent,

v11 For His messengers He chargeth for thee, To keep thee in all thy ways,

v12 On the hands they bear thee up, Lest thou smite against a stone thy foot.

v13 On lion and asp thou treadest, Thou trampest young lion and dragon.

v14 Because in Me he hath delighted, I also deliver him -- I set him on high, Because he hath known My name.

v15 He doth call Me, and I answer him, I [am] with him in distress, I deliver him, and honour him.

v16 With length of days I satisfy him, And I cause him to look on My salvation!
