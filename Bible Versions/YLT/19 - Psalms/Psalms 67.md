# Psalms 67 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 66|← Psalms 66]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 68|Psalms 68 →]]

***

v1 To the Overseer, with stringed instruments. -- A Psalm, a Song. God doth favour us and bless us, Doth cause His face to shine with us. Selah.

v2 For the knowledge in earth of Thy way, among all nations of Thy salvation.

v3 Praise Thee do peoples, O God, Praise Thee do peoples, all of them.

v4 Rejoice and sing do nations, For Thou judgest peoples uprightly, And peoples on earth comfortest. Selah.

v5 Confess Thee do peoples, O God, Confess Thee do peoples -- all of them.

v6 Earth hath given her increase, God doth bless us -- our God,

v7 God doth bless us, and all ends of earth fear Him!
