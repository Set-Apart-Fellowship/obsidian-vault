# Psalms 114 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 113|← Psalms 113]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 115|Psalms 115 →]]

***

v1 In the going out of Israel from Egypt, The house of Jacob from a strange people,

v2 Judah became His sanctuary, Israel his dominion.

v3 The sea hath seen, and fleeth, The Jordan turneth backward.

v4 The mountains have skipped as rams, Heights as sons of a flock.

v5 What -- to thee, O sea, that thou fleest? O Jordan, thou turnest back!

v6 O mountains, ye skip as rams! O heights, as sons of a flock!

v7 From before the Lord be afraid, O earth, From before the God of Jacob,

v8 He is turning the rock to a pool of waters, The flint to a fountain of waters!
