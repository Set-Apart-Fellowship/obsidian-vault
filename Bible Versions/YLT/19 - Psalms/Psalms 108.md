# Psalms 108 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 107|← Psalms 107]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 109|Psalms 109 →]]

***

v1 A Song, a Psalm of David. Prepared is my heart, O God, I sing, yea, I sing praise, also my honour.

v2 Awake, psaltery and harp, I awake the dawn.

v3 I thank Thee among peoples, O Jehovah, And I praise Thee among the nations.

v4 For great above the heavens [is] Thy kindness, And unto the clouds Thy truth.

v5 Be Thou exalted above the heavens, O God, And above all the earth Thy honour.

v6 That Thy beloved ones may be delivered, Save [with] Thy right hand, and answer us.

v7 God hath spoken in His holiness: I exult, I apportion Shechem, And the valley of Succoth I measure,

v8 Mine [is] Gilead, mine [is] Manasseh, And Ephraim [is] the strength of my head, Judah [is] my lawgiver,

v9 Moab [is] a pot for my washing, Upon Edom I cast my shoe, Over Philistia I shout habitually.

v10 Who doth bring me in to the fenced city? Who hath led me unto Edom?

v11 Hast not Thou, O God, cast us off? And Thou goest not out, O God, with our hosts!

v12 Give to us help from adversity, And vain is the salvation of man.

v13 In God we do mightily, And He doth tread down our adversaries!
