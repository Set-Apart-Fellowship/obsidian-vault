# Psalms 98 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 97|← Psalms 97]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 99|Psalms 99 →]]

***

v1 A Psalm. Sing ye to Jehovah a new song, For wonders He hath done, Given salvation to Him hath His right hand and His holy arm.

v2 Jehovah hath made known His salvation, Before the eyes of the nations, He hath revealed His righteousness,

v3 He hath remembered His kindness, And His faithfulness to the house of Israel, All ends of earth have seen the salvation of our God.

v4 Shout to Jehovah, all the earth, Break forth, and cry aloud, and sing.

v5 Sing to Jehovah with harp, With harp, and voice of praise,

v6 With trumpets, and voice of a cornet, Shout ye before the king Jehovah.

v7 Roar doth the sea and its fulness, The world and the inhabitants in it.

v8 Floods clap hand, together hills cry aloud,

v9 Before Jehovah, For He hath come to judge the earth, He judgeth the world in righteousness, And the people in uprightness!
