# Psalms 81 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 80|← Psalms 80]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 82|Psalms 82 →]]

***

v1 To the Overseer. -- 'On the Gittith.' By Asaph. Cry aloud to God our strength, Shout to the God of Jacob.

v2 Lift up a song, and give out a timbrel, A pleasant harp with psaltery.

v3 Blow in the month a trumpet, In the new moon, at the day of our festival,

v4 For a statute to Israel it [is], An ordinance of the God of Jacob.

v5 A testimony on Joseph He hath placed it, In his going forth over the land of Egypt. A lip, I have not known -- I hear.

v6 From the burden his shoulder I turned aside, His hands from the basket pass over.

v7 In distress thou hast called and I deliver thee, I answer thee in the secret place of thunder, I try thee by the waters of Meribah. Selah.

v8 Hear, O My people, and I testify to thee, O Israel, if thou dost hearken to me:

v9 There is not in thee a strange god, And thou bowest not thyself to a strange god.

v10 I [am] Jehovah thy God, Who bringeth thee up out of the land of Egypt. Enlarge thy mouth, and I fill it.

v11 But, My people hearkened not to My voice, And Israel hath not consented to Me.

v12 And I send them away in the enmity of their heart, They walk in their own counsels.

v13 O that My people were hearkening to Me, Israel in My ways would walk.

v14 As a little thing their enemies I cause to bow, And against their adversaries I turn back My hand,

v15 Those hating Jehovah feign obedience to Him, But their time is -- to the age.

v16 He causeth him to eat of the fat of wheat, And [with] honey from a rock I satisfy thee!
