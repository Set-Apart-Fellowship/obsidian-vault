# Psalms 74 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 73|← Psalms 73]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 75|Psalms 75 →]]

***

v1 An Instruction of Asaph. Why, O God, hast Thou cast off for ever? Thine anger smoketh against the flock of Thy pasture.

v2 Remember Thy company. Thou didst purchase of old, Thou didst redeem the rod of Thy inheritance, This mount Zion -- Thou didst dwell in it.

v3 Lift up Thy steps to the perpetual desolations, Everything the enemy did wickedly in the sanctuary.

v4 Roared have thine adversaries, In the midst of Thy meeting-places, They have set their ensigns as ensigns.

v5 He is known as one bringing in on high Against a thicket of wood -- axes.

v6 And now, its carvings together With axe and hatchet they break down,

v7 They have sent into fire Thy sanctuary, to the earth they polluted the tabernacle of Thy name,

v8 They said in their hearts, 'Let us oppress them together,' They did burn all the meeting-places of God in the land.

v9 Our ensigns we have not seen, There is no more a prophet, Nor with us is one knowing how long.

v10 Till when, O God, doth an adversary reproach? Doth an enemy despise thy name for ever?

v11 Why dost Thou turn back Thy hand, Even Thy right hand? From the midst of Thy bosom remove [it].

v12 And God [is] my king of old, Working salvation in the midst of the earth.

v13 Thou hast broken by Thy strength a sea-[monster], Thou hast shivered Heads of dragons by the waters,

v14 Thou hast broken the heads of leviathan, Thou makest him food, For the people of the dry places.

v15 Thou hast cleaved a fountain and a stream, Thou hast dried up perennial flowings.

v16 Thine [is] the day, also Thine [is] the night, Thou hast prepared a light giver -- the sun.

v17 Thou hast set up all the borders of earth, Summer and winter Thou hast formed them.

v18 Remember this -- an enemy reproached Jehovah, And a foolish people have despised Thy name.

v19 Give not up to a company, The soul of Thy turtle-dove, The company of Thy poor ones forget not for ever.

v20 Look attentively to the covenant, For the dark places of earth, Have been full of habitations of violence.

v21 Let not the oppressed turn back ashamed, Let the poor and needy praise Thy name,

v22 Arise, O God, plead Thy plea, Remember Thy reproach from a fool all the day.

v23 Forget not the voice of Thine adversaries, The noise of Thy withstanders is going up continually!
