# Psalms 93 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 92|← Psalms 92]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 94|Psalms 94 →]]

***

v1 Jehovah hath reigned, Excellency He hath put on, Jehovah put on strength, He girded Himself, Also -- established is the world, unmoved.

v2 Established is Thy throne since then, From the age Thou [art].

v3 Floods have lifted up, O Jehovah, Floods have lifted up their voice, Floods lift up their breakers.

v4 Than the voices of many mighty waters, Breakers of a sea, mighty on high [is] Jehovah,

v5 Thy testimonies have been very stedfast, To Thy house comely [is] holiness, O Jehovah, for length of days!
