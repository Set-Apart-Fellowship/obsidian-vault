# Psalms 60 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 59|← Psalms 59]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 61|Psalms 61 →]]

***

v1 To the Overseer. -- 'Concerning the Lily of Testimony,' a secret treasure of David, to teach, in his striving with Aram-Naharaim, and with Aram-Zobah, and Joab turneth back and smiteth Edom in the valley of Salt -- twelve thousand. O God, Thou hadst cast us off, Thou hadst broken us -- hadst been angry! -- Thou dost turn back to us.

v2 Thou hast caused the land to tremble, Thou hast broken it, Heal its breaches, for it hath moved.

v3 Thou hast shewn Thy people a hard thing, Thou hast caused us to drink wine of trembling.

v4 Thou hast given to those fearing thee an ensign. To be lifted up as an ensign Because of truth. Selah.

v5 That Thy beloved ones may be drawn out, Save [with] Thy right hand, and answer us.

v6 God hath spoken in His holiness: I exult -- I apportion Shechem, And the valley of Succoth I measure,

v7 Mine [is] Gilead, and mine [is] Manasseh, And Ephraim [is] the strength of my head, Judah [is] my lawgiver,

v8 Moab [is] my pot for washing, over Edom I cast my shoe, Shout, concerning me, O Philistia.

v9 Who doth bring me [to] a city of bulwarks? Who hath led me unto Edom?

v10 Is it not Thou, O God? hast Thou cast us off? And dost Thou not go forth, O God, with our hosts!

v11 Give to us help from adversity, And vain [is] the deliverance of man.

v12 In God we do mightily, And He treadeth down our adversaries!
