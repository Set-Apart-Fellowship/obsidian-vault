# Psalms 25 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 24|← Psalms 24]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 26|Psalms 26 →]]

***

v1 By David. Unto Thee, O Jehovah, my soul I lift up.

v2 My God, in Thee I have trusted, Let me not be ashamed, Let not mine enemies exult over me.

v3 Also let none waiting on Thee be ashamed, Let the treacherous dealers without cause be ashamed.

v4 Thy ways, O Jehovah, cause me to know, Thy paths teach Thou me.

v5 Cause me to tread in Thy truth, and teach me, For Thou [art] the God of my salvation, Near Thee I have waited all the day.

v6 Remember Thy mercies, O Jehovah, And Thy kindnesses, for from the age [are] they.

v7 Sins of my youth, and my transgressions, Do not Thou remember. According to Thy kindness be mindful of me, For Thy goodness' sake, O Jehovah.

v8 Good and upright [is] Jehovah, Therefore He directeth sinners in the way.

v9 He causeth the humble to tread in judgment, And teacheth the humble His way.

v10 All the paths of Jehovah [are] kindness and truth, To those keeping His covenant, And His testimonies.

v11 For Thy name's sake, O Jehovah, Thou hast pardoned mine iniquity, for it [is] great.

v12 Who [is] this -- the man fearing Jehovah? He directeth him in the way He doth choose.

v13 His soul in good doth remain, And his seed doth possess the land.

v14 The secret of Jehovah [is] for those fearing Him, And His covenant -- to cause them to know.

v15 Mine eyes [are] continually unto Jehovah, For He bringeth out from a net my feet.

v16 Turn Thou unto me, and favour me, For lonely and afflicted [am] I.

v17 The distresses of my heart have enlarged themselves, From my distresses bring me out.

v18 See mine affliction and my misery, And bear with all my sins.

v19 See my enemies, for they have been many, And with violent hatred they have hated me.

v20 Keep my soul, and deliver me, Let me not be ashamed, for I trusted in Thee.

v21 Integrity and uprightness do keep me, For I have waited [on] Thee.

v22 Redeem Israel, O God, from all his distresses!
