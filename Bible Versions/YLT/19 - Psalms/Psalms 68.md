# Psalms 68 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 67|← Psalms 67]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 69|Psalms 69 →]]

***

v1 To the Overseer. -- A Psalm, a song of David. Rise doth God -- scattered are His enemies! And those hating Him flee from His face.

v2 As the driving away of smoke Thou drivest away, As the melting of wax before fire, The wicked perish at the presence of God.

v3 And the righteous rejoice, they exult before God, And they joy with gladness.

v4 Sing ye to God -- praise His name, Raise up a highway for Him who is riding in deserts, In Jah [is] His name, and exult before Him.

v5 Father of the fatherless, and judge of the widows, [Is] God in His holy habitation.

v6 God -- causing the lonely to dwell at home, Bringing out bound ones into prosperity, Only -- the refractory have inhabited a dry place.

v7 O God, in Thy going forth before Thy people, In Thy stepping through the wilderness, Selah.

v8 The earth hath shaken, Yea, the heavens have dropped before God, This Sinai -- before God, the God of Israel.

v9 A shower of free-will gifts thou shakest out, O God. Thine inheritance, when it hath been weary, Thou hast established it.

v10 Thy company have dwelt in it, Thou preparest in Thy goodness for the poor, O God.

v11 The Lord doth give the saying, The female proclaimers [are] a numerous host.

v12 Kings of hosts flee utterly away, And a female inhabitant of the house apportioneth spoil.

v13 Though ye do lie between two boundaries, Wings of a dove covered with silver, And her pinions with yellow gold.

v14 When the Mighty spreadeth kings in it, It doth snow in Salmon.

v15 A hill of God [is] the hill of Bashan, A hill of heights [is] the hill of Bashan.

v16 Why do ye envy, O high hills, The hill God hath desired for His seat? Jehovah also doth tabernacle for ever.

v17 The chariots of God [are] myriads, thousands of changes, The Lord [is] among them, in Sinai, in the sanctuary.

v18 Thou hast ascended on high, Thou hast taken captive captivity, Thou hast taken gifts for men, That even the refractory may rest, O Jah God.

v19 Blessed [is] the Lord, day by day He layeth on us. God Himself [is] our salvation. Selah.

v20 God Himself [is] to us a God for deliverances, And Jehovah Lord hath the outgoings of death.

v21 Only -- God doth smite The head of His enemies, The hairy crown of a habitual walker in his guilt.

v22 The Lord said: 'From Bashan I bring back, I bring back from the depths of the sea.

v23 So that thou dashest thy foot in blood, [In the blood of] enemies -- the tongue of Thy dogs.'

v24 They have seen Thy goings, O God, Goings of my God, my king, in the sanctuary.

v25 Singers have been before, Behind [are] players on instruments, In the midst virgins playing with timbrels.

v26 In assemblies bless ye God, The Lord -- from the fountain of Israel.

v27 There [is] little Benjamin their ruler, Heads of Judah their defence, Heads of Zebulun -- heads of Naphtali.

v28 Thy God hath commanded thy strength, Be strong, O God, this Thou hast wrought for us.

v29 Because of Thy temple at Jerusalem, To Thee do kings bring a present.

v30 Rebuke a beast of the reeds, a company of bulls, With calves of the peoples, Each humbling himself with pieces of silver, Scatter Thou peoples delighting in conflicts.

v31 Come do fat ones out of Egypt, Cush causeth her hands to run to God.

v32 Kingdoms of the earth, sing ye to God, Praise ye the Lord. Selah.

v33 To him who is riding on the heavens of the heavens of old, Lo, He giveth with His voice a strong voice.

v34 Ascribe ye strength to God, Over Israel [is] His excellency, and His strength in the clouds.

v35 Fearful, O God, out of Thy sanctuaries, The God of Israel Himself, Giving strength and might to the people. Blessed [is] God!
