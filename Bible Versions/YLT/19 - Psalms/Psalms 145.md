# Psalms 145 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 144|← Psalms 144]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 146|Psalms 146 →]]

***

v1 Praise by David. I exalt Thee, my God, O king, And bless Thy name to the age and for ever.

v2 Every day do I bless Thee, And praise Thy name to the age and for ever.

v3 Great [is] Jehovah, and praised greatly, And of His greatness there is no searching.

v4 Generation to generation praiseth Thy works, And Thy mighty acts they declare.

v5 The honour -- the glory of Thy majesty, And the matters of Thy wonders I declare.

v6 And the strength of Thy fearful acts they tell, And Thy greatness I recount.

v7 The memorial of the abundance of Thy goodness they send forth. And Thy righteousness they sing.

v8 Gracious and merciful [is] Jehovah, Slow to anger, and great in kindness.

v9 Good [is] Jehovah to all, And His mercies [are] over all His works.

v10 Confess Thee O Jehovah, do all Thy works, And Thy saints do bless Thee.

v11 The honour of Thy kingdom they tell, And [of] Thy might they speak,

v12 To make known to sons of men His mighty acts, The honour of the majesty of His kingdom.

v13 Thy kingdom [is] a kingdom of all ages, And Thy dominion [is] in all generations.

v14 Jehovah is supporting all who are falling, And raising up all who are bowed down.

v15 The eyes of all unto Thee do look, And Thou art giving to them their food in its season,

v16 Opening Thy hand, and satisfying The desire of every living thing.

v17 Righteous [is] Jehovah in all His ways, And kind in all His works.

v18 Near [is] Jehovah to all those calling Him, To all who call Him in truth.

v19 The desire of those fearing Him He doth, And their cry He heareth, and saveth them.

v20 Jehovah preserveth all those loving Him, And all the wicked He destroyeth.

v21 The praise of Jehovah my mouth speaketh, And all flesh doth bless His holy name, To the age and for ever!
