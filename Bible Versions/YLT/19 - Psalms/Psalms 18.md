# Psalms 18 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 17|← Psalms 17]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 19|Psalms 19 →]]

***

v1 To the Overseer. -- By a servant of Jehovah, by David, who hath spoken to Jehovah the words of this song in the day Jehovah delivered him from the hand of all his enemies, and from the hand of Saul, and he saith: -- I love Thee, O Jehovah, my strength.

v2 Jehovah [is] my rock, and my bulwark, And my deliverer, My God [is] my rock, I trust in Him: My shield, and a horn of my salvation, My high tower.

v3 The 'Praised One' I call Jehovah, And from my enemies I am saved.

v4 Compassed me have cords of death, And streams of the worthless make me afraid.

v5 Cords of Sheol have surrounded me, Before me have been snares of death.

v6 In mine adversity I call Jehovah, And unto my God I cry. He heareth from His temple my voice, And My cry before Him cometh into His ears.

v7 And shake and tremble doth the earth, And foundations of hills are troubled, And they shake -- because He hath wrath.

v8 Gone up hath smoke by His nostrils, And fire from His mouth consumeth, Coals have been kindled by it.

v9 And He inclineth the heavens, and cometh down, And thick darkness [is] under His feet.

v10 And He rideth on a cherub, and doth fly, And He flieth on wings of wind.

v11 He maketh darkness His secret place, Round about Him His tabernacle, Darkness of waters, thick clouds of the skies.

v12 From the brightness over-against Him His thick clouds have passed on, Hail and coals of fire.

v13 And thunder in the heavens doth Jehovah, And the Most High giveth forth His voice, Hail and coals of fire.

v14 And He sendeth His arrows and scattereth them, And much lightning, and crusheth them.

v15 And seen are the streams of waters, And revealed are foundations of the earth. From Thy rebuke, O Jehovah, From the breath of the spirit of Thine anger.

v16 He sendeth from above -- He taketh me, He draweth me out of many waters.

v17 He delivereth me from my strong enemy, And from those hating me, For they have been stronger than I.

v18 They go before me in a day of my calamity And Jehovah is for a support to me.

v19 And He bringeth me forth to a large place, He draweth me out, because He delighted in me.

v20 Jehovah doth recompense me According to my righteousness, According to the cleanness of my hands, He doth return to me.

v21 For I have kept the ways of Jehovah, And have not done wickedly against my God.

v22 For all His judgments [are] before me, And His statutes I turn not from me.

v23 And I am perfect with him, And I keep myself from mine iniquity.

v24 And Jehovah doth return to me, According to my righteousness, According to the cleanness of my hands, Over-against his eyes.

v25 With the kind Thou showest Thyself kind, With a perfect man showest Thyself perfect.

v26 With the pure Thou showest Thyself pure, And with the perverse showest Thyself a wrestler,

v27 For Thou a poor people savest, And the eyes of the high causest to fall.

v28 For Thou -- Thou lightest my lamp, Jehovah my God enlighteneth my darkness.

v29 For by Thee I run -- a troop! And by my God I leap a wall.

v30 God! perfect [is] His way, The saying of Jehovah is tried, A shield [is] He to all those trusting in Him.

v31 For who [is] God besides Jehovah? And who [is] a rock save our God?

v32 God! who is girding me [with] strength, And He maketh perfect my way.

v33 Making my feet like hinds, And on my high places causeth me to stand.

v34 Teaching my hands for battle, And a bow of brass was brought down by my arms.

v35 And Thou givest to me the shield of Thy salvation, And Thy right hand doth support me, And Thy lowliness maketh me great.

v36 Thou enlargest my step under me, And mine ankles have not slidden.

v37 I pursue mine enemies, and overtake them, And turn back not till they are consumed.

v38 I smite them, and they are not able to rise, They fall under my feet,

v39 And Thou girdest me [with] strength for battle, Causest my withstanders to bow under me.

v40 As to mine enemies -- Thou hast given to me the neck, As to those hating me -- I cut them off.

v41 They cry, and there is no saviour, On Jehovah, and He doth not answer them.

v42 And I beat them as dust before wind, As mire of the streets I empty them out.

v43 Thou dost deliver me From the strivings of the people, Thou placest me for a head of nations, A people I have not known do serve me.

v44 At the hearing of the ear they hearken to me, Sons of a stranger feign obedience to me,

v45 Sons of a stranger fade away, And are slain out of their close places.

v46 Jehovah liveth -- and blessed [is] my rock, And exalted is the God of my salvation.

v47 God -- who is giving vengeance to me, And He subdueth peoples under me,

v48 My deliverer from mine enemies, Above my withstanders Thou raisest me, From a man of violence dost deliver me.

v49 Therefore I confess Thee among nations, O Jehovah, And to Thy name I sing praise,

v50 Magnifying the salvation of His king, And doing kindness to His anointed, To David, and to his seed -- unto the age!
