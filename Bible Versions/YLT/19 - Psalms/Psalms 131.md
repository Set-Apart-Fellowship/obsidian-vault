# Psalms 131 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 130|← Psalms 130]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 132|Psalms 132 →]]

***

v1 A Song of the Ascents, by David. Jehovah, my heart hath not been haughty, Nor have mine eyes been high, Nor have I walked in great things, And in things too wonderful for me.

v2 Have I not compared, and kept silent my soul, As a weaned one by its mother? As a weaned one by me [is] my soul.

v3 Israel doth wait on Jehovah, From henceforth, and unto the age!
