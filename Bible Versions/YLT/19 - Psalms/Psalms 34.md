# Psalms 34 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 33|← Psalms 33]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 35|Psalms 35 →]]

***

v1 By David, in his changing his behaviour before Abimelech, and he driveth him away, and he goeth. I do bless Jehovah at all times, Continually His praise [is] in my mouth.

v2 In Jehovah doth my soul boast herself, Hear do the humble and rejoice.

v3 Ascribe ye greatness to Jehovah with me, And we exalt His name together.

v4 I sought Jehovah, and He answered me, And from all my fears did deliver me.

v5 They looked expectingly unto Him, And they became bright, And their faces are not ashamed.

v6 This poor [one] called, and Jehovah heard, And from all his distresses saved him.

v7 A messenger of Jehovah is encamping, Round about those who fear Him, And He armeth them.

v8 Taste ye and see that Jehovah [is] good, O the happiness of the man who trusteth in Him.

v9 Fear Jehovah, ye His holy ones, For there is no lack to those fearing Him.

v10 Young lions have lacked and been hungry, And those seeking Jehovah lack not any good,

v11 Come ye, children, hearken to me, The fear of Jehovah I do teach you.

v12 Who [is] the man that is desiring life? Loving days to see good?

v13 Keep thy tongue from evil, And thy lips from speaking deceit.

v14 Turn aside from evil and do good, Seek peace and pursue it.

v15 The eyes of Jehovah [are] unto the righteous, And His ears unto their cry.

v16 (The face of Jehovah [is] on doers of evil, To cut off from earth their memorial.)

v17 They cried, and Jehovah heard, And from all their distresses delivered them.

v18 Near [is] Jehovah to the broken of heart, And the bruised of spirit He saveth.

v19 Many [are] the evils of the righteous, Out of them all doth Jehovah deliver him.

v20 He is keeping all his bones, One of them hath not been broken.

v21 Evil doth put to death the wicked, And those hating the righteous are desolate.

v22 Jehovah redeemeth the soul of His servants, And none trusting in Him are desolate!
