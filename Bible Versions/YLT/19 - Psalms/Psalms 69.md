# Psalms 69 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 68|← Psalms 68]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 70|Psalms 70 →]]

***

v1 To the Overseer. -- 'On the Lilies,' by David. Save me, O God, for come have waters unto the soul.

v2 I have sunk in deep mire, And there is no standing, I have come into the depths of the waters, And a flood hath overflown me.

v3 I have been wearied with my calling, Burnt hath been my throat, Consumed have been mine eyes, waiting for my God.

v4 Those hating me without cause Have been more than the hairs of my head, Mighty have been my destroyers, My lying enemies, That which I took not away -- I bring back.

v5 O God, Thou -- Thou hast known Concerning my overturn, And my desolations from Thee have not been hid.

v6 Let not those waiting on Thee be ashamed because of me, O Lord, Jehovah of Hosts, Let not those seeking Thee Blush because of me, O God of Israel.

v7 For because of Thee I have borne reproach, Shame hath covered my face.

v8 A stranger I have been to my brother, And a foreigner to sons of my mother.

v9 For zeal for Thy house hath consumed me, And the reproaches of Thy reproachers Have fallen upon me.

v10 And I weep in the fasting of my soul, And it is for a reproach to me.

v11 And I make my clothing sackcloth, And I am to them for a simile.

v12 Those sitting at the gate meditate concerning me, And those drinking strong drink, Play on instruments.

v13 And I -- my prayer [is] to Thee, O Jehovah, A time of good pleasure, O God, In the abundance of Thy kindness, Answer me in the truth of Thy salvation.

v14 Deliver me from the mire, and let me not sink, Let me be delivered from those hating me, And from deep places of waters.

v15 Let not a flood of waters overflow me, Nor let the deep swallow me up, Nor let the pit shut her mouth upon me.

v16 Answer me, O Jehovah, for good [is] Thy kindness, According to the abundance Of Thy mercies turn Thou unto me,

v17 And hide not Thy face from Thy servant, For I am in distress -- haste, answer me.

v18 Be near unto my soul -- redeem it, Because of mine enemies ransom me.

v19 Thou -- Thou hast known my reproach, And my shame, and my blushing, Before Thee [are] all mine adversaries.

v20 Reproach hath broken my heart, and I am sick, And I look for a bemoaner, and there is none, And for comforters, and I have found none.

v21 And they give for my food gall, And for my thirst cause me to drink vinegar.

v22 Their table before them is for a snare, And for a recompence -- for a trap.

v23 Darkened are their eyes from seeing, And their loins continually shake Thou.

v24 Pour upon them Thine indignation, And the fierceness of Thine anger doth seize them.

v25 Their tower is desolated, In their tents there is no dweller.

v26 For they have pursued him Thou hast smitten, And recount of the pain of Thy pierced ones.

v27 Give punishment for their iniquity, And they enter not into Thy righteousness.

v28 They are blotted out of the book of life, And with the righteous are not written.

v29 And I [am] afflicted and pained, Thy salvation, O God, doth set me on high.

v30 I praise the name of God with a song, And I magnify Him with thanksgiving,

v31 And it is better to Jehovah than an ox, A bullock -- horned -- hoofed.

v32 The humble have seen -- they rejoice, Ye who seek God -- and your heart liveth.

v33 For Jehovah hearkeneth unto the needy, And His bound ones He hath not despised.

v34 The heavens and earth do praise Him, Seas, and every moving thing in them.

v35 For God doth save Zion, And doth build the cities of Judah, And they have dwelt there, and possess it.

v36 And the seed of His servants inherit it, And those loving His name dwell in it!
