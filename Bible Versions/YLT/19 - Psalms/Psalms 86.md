# Psalms 86 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 85|← Psalms 85]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 87|Psalms 87 →]]

***

v1 A Prayer of David. Incline, O Jehovah, Thine ear, Answer me, for I [am] poor and needy.

v2 Keep my soul, for I [am] pious, Save Thy servant -- who is trusting to Thee, O Thou, my God.

v3 Favour me, O Lord, for to Thee I call all the day.

v4 Rejoice the soul of Thy servant, For unto Thee, O Lord, my soul I lift up.

v5 For Thou, Lord, [art] good and forgiving. And abundant in kindness to all calling Thee.

v6 Hear, O Jehovah, my prayer, And attend to the voice of my supplications.

v7 In a day of my distress I call Thee, For Thou dost answer me.

v8 There is none like Thee among the gods, O Lord, And like Thy works there are none.

v9 All nations that Thou hast made Come and bow themselves before Thee, O Lord, And give honour to Thy name.

v10 For great [art] Thou, and doing wonders, Thou [art] God Thyself alone.

v11 Show me, O Jehovah, Thy way, I walk in Thy truth, My heart doth rejoice to fear Thy name.

v12 I confess Thee, O Lord my God, with all my heart, And I honour Thy name to the age.

v13 For Thy kindness [is] great toward me, And Thou hast delivered my soul from the lowest Sheol.

v14 O God, the proud have risen up against me, And a company of the terrible sought my soul, And have not placed Thee before them,

v15 And Thou, O Lord, [art] God, merciful and gracious, Slow to anger, and abundant in kindness and truth.

v16 Look unto me, and favour me, Give Thy strength to Thy servant, And give salvation to a son of Thine handmaid.

v17 Do with me a sign for good, And those hating me see and are ashamed, For Thou, O Jehovah, hast helped me, Yea, Thou hast comforted me!
