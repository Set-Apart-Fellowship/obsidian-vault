# Psalms 119 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 118|← Psalms 118]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 120|Psalms 120 →]]

***

v1 [Aleph.] O the happiness of those perfect in the way, They are walking in the law of Jehovah,

v2 O the happiness of those keeping His testimonies, With the whole heart they seek Him.

v3 Yea, they have not done iniquity, In His ways they have walked.

v4 Thou hast commanded us Thy precepts to keep diligently,

v5 O that my ways were prepared to keep Thy statutes,

v6 Then I am not ashamed In my looking unto all Thy commands.

v7 I confess Thee with uprightness of heart, In my learning the judgments of Thy righteousness.

v8 Thy statutes I keep, leave me not utterly!

v9 [Beth.] With what doth a young man purify his path? To observe -- according to Thy word.

v10 With all my heart I have sought Thee, Let me not err from Thy commands.

v11 In my heart I have hid Thy saying, That I sin not before Thee.

v12 Blessed [art] Thou, O Jehovah, teach me Thy statutes.

v13 With my lips I have recounted All the judgments of Thy mouth.

v14 In the way of Thy testimonies I have joyed, As over all wealth.

v15 In Thy precepts I meditate, And I behold attentively Thy paths.

v16 In Thy statutes I delight myself, I do not forget Thy word.

v17 [Gimel.] Confer benefits on Thy servant, I live, and I keep Thy word.

v18 Uncover mine eyes, and I behold wonders out of Thy law.

v19 A sojourner I [am] on earth, Hide not from me Thy commands.

v20 Broken hath my soul for desire Unto Thy judgments at all times.

v21 Thou hast rebuked the cursed proud, Who are erring from Thy commands.

v22 Remove from me reproach and contempt, For Thy testimonies I have kept.

v23 Princes also sat -- against me they spoke, Thy servant doth meditate in Thy statutes,

v24 Thy testimonies also [are] my delight, The men of my counsel!

v25 [Daleth.] Cleaved to the dust hath my soul, Quicken me according to Thy word.

v26 My ways I have recounted, And Thou answerest me, teach me Thy statutes,

v27 The way of Thy precepts cause me to understand, And I meditate in Thy wonders.

v28 My soul hath dropped from affliction, Establish me according to Thy word.

v29 The way of falsehood turn aside from me And with Thy law favour me.

v30 The way of faithfulness I have chosen, Thy judgments I have compared,

v31 I have adhered to Thy testimonies, O Jehovah, put me not to shame.

v32 The way of Thy commands I run, For Thou dost enlarge my heart!

v33 [He.] Show me, O Jehovah, the way of Thy statutes, And I keep it -- [to] the end.

v34 Cause me to understand, and I keep Thy law, And observe it with the whole heart.

v35 Cause me to tread in the path of Thy commands, For in it I have delighted.

v36 Incline my heart unto Thy testimonies, And not unto dishonest gain.

v37 Remove mine eyes from seeing vanity, In Thy way quicken Thou me.

v38 Establish to Thy servant Thy saying, That [is] concerning Thy fear.

v39 Remove my reproach that I have feared, For Thy judgments [are] good.

v40 Lo, I have longed for Thy precepts, In Thy righteousness quicken Thou me,

v41 [Waw.] And meet me doth Thy kindness, O Jehovah, Thy salvation according to Thy saying.

v42 And I answer him who is reproaching me a word, For I have trusted in Thy word.

v43 And Thou takest not utterly away From my mouth the word of truth, Because for Thy judgment I have hoped.

v44 And I keep Thy law continually, To the age and for ever.

v45 And I walk habitually in a broad place, For Thy precepts I have sought.

v46 And I speak of Thy testimonies before kings, And I am not ashamed.

v47 And I delight myself in Thy commands, That I have loved,

v48 And I lift up my hands unto Thy commands, That I have loved, And I do meditate on Thy statutes!

v49 [Zain.] Remember the word to Thy servant, On which Thou hast caused me to hope.

v50 This [is] my comfort in mine affliction, That Thy saying hath quickened me.

v51 The proud have utterly scorned me, From Thy law I have not turned aside.

v52 I remembered Thy judgments of old, O Jehovah, And I comfort myself.

v53 Horror hath seized me, Because of the wicked forsaking Thy law.

v54 Songs have been to me Thy statutes, In the house of my sojournings.

v55 I have remembered in the night Thy name, O Jehovah, And I do keep Thy law.

v56 This hath been to me, That Thy precepts I have kept!

v57 [Cheth.] My portion [is] Jehovah; I have said -- to keep Thy words,

v58 I appeased Thy face with the whole heart, Favour me according to Thy saying.

v59 I have reckoned my ways, And turn back my feet unto Thy testimonies.

v60 I have made haste, And delayed not, to keep Thy commands.

v61 Cords of the wicked have surrounded me, Thy law I have not forgotten.

v62 At midnight I rise to give thanks to Thee, For the judgments of Thy righteousness.

v63 A companion I [am] to all who fear Thee, And to those keeping Thy precepts.

v64 Of Thy kindness, O Jehovah, the earth is full, Thy statutes teach Thou me!

v65 [Teth.] Good Thou didst with Thy servant, O Jehovah, According to Thy word.

v66 The goodness of reason and knowledge teach me, For in Thy commands I have believed.

v67 Before I am afflicted, I -- I am erring, And now Thy saying I have kept.

v68 Good Thou [art], and doing good, Teach me Thy statutes.

v69 Forged against me falsehood have the proud, I with the whole heart keep Thy precepts.

v70 Insensate as fat hath been their heart, I -- in Thy law I have delighted.

v71 Good for me that I have been afflicted, That I might learn Thy statutes.

v72 Better to me [is] the law of Thy mouth Than thousands of gold and silver!

v73 [Yod.] Thy hands made me and establish me, Cause me to understand, and I learn Thy commands.

v74 Those fearing Thee see me and rejoice, Because for Thy word I have hoped.

v75 I have known, O Jehovah, That righteous [are] Thy judgments, And [in] faithfulness Thou hast afflicted me.

v76 Let, I pray Thee, Thy kindness be to comfort me, According to Thy saying to Thy servant.

v77 Meet me do Thy mercies, and I live, For Thy law [is] my delight.

v78 Ashamed are the proud, For [with] falsehood they dealt perversely with me. I meditate in Thy precepts.

v79 Those fearing Thee turn back to me, And those knowing Thy testimonies.

v80 My heart is perfect in Thy statutes, So that I am not ashamed.

v81 [Kaph.] Consumed for Thy salvation hath been my soul, For Thy word I have hoped.

v82 Consumed have been mine eyes for Thy word, Saying, 'When doth it comfort me?'

v83 For I have been as a bottle in smoke, Thy statutes I have not forgotten.

v84 How many [are] the days of Thy servant? When dost Thou execute Against my pursuers judgment?

v85 The proud have digged for me pits, That [are] not according to Thy law.

v86 All Thy commands [are] faithfulness, [With] falsehood they have pursued me, Help Thou me.

v87 Almost consumed me on earth have they, And I -- I have not forsaken Thy precepts.

v88 According to Thy kindness quicken Thou me, And I keep the testimony of Thy mouth!

v89 [Lamed.] To the age, O Jehovah, Thy word is set up in the heavens.

v90 To all generations Thy faithfulness, Thou didst establish earth, and it standeth.

v91 According to Thine ordinances They have stood this day, for the whole [are] Thy servants.

v92 Unless Thy law [were] my delights, Then had I perished in mine affliction.

v93 To the age I forget not Thy precepts, For by them Thou hast quickened me.

v94 I [am] Thine, save Thou me, For Thy precepts I have sought.

v95 Thy wicked waited for me to destroy me, Thy testimonies I understand.

v96 Of all perfection I have seen an end, Broad [is] Thy command -- exceedingly!

v97 [Mem.] O how I have loved Thy law! All the day it [is] my meditation.

v98 Than mine enemies Thy command maketh me wiser, For it [is] before me to the age.

v99 Above all my teachers I have acted wisely. For Thy testimonies [are] my meditation.

v100 Above elders I understand more, For Thy precepts I have kept.

v101 From every evil path I restrained my feet, So that I keep Thy word.

v102 From Thy judgments I turned not aside, For Thou -- Thou hast directed me.

v103 How sweet to my palate hath been Thy saying, Above honey to my mouth.

v104 From Thy precepts I have understanding, Therefore I have hated every false path!

v105 [Nun.] A lamp to my foot [is] Thy word, And a light to my path.

v106 I have sworn, and I confirm [it], To keep the judgments of Thy righteousness.

v107 I have been afflicted very much, O Jehovah, quicken me, according to Thy word.

v108 Free-will-offerings of my mouth, Accept, I pray Thee, O Jehovah, And Thy judgments teach Thou me.

v109 My soul [is] in my hand continually, And Thy law I have not forgotten.

v110 The wicked have laid a snare for me, And from thy precepts I wandered not.

v111 I have inherited Thy testimonies to the age, For the joy of my heart [are] they.

v112 I have inclined my heart To do Thy statutes, to the age -- [to] the end!

v113 [Samech.] Doubting ones I have hated, And Thy law I have loved.

v114 My hiding place and my shield [art] Thou, For Thy word I have hoped.

v115 Turn aside from me, ye evil-doers, And I keep the commands of my God.

v116 Sustain me according to Thy saying, And I live, and Thou puttest me not to shame Because of my hope.

v117 Support Thou me, and I am saved, And I look on Thy statutes continually.

v118 Thou hast trodden down All going astray from Thy statutes, For falsehood [is] their deceit.

v119 Dross! Thou hast caused to cease All the wicked of the earth; Therefore I have loved Thy testimonies.

v120 Trembled from Thy fear hath my flesh, And from Thy judgments I have been afraid!

v121 [Ain.] I have done judgment and righteousness, Leave me not to mine oppressors.

v122 Make sure Thy servant for good, Let not the proud oppress me.

v123 Mine eyes have been consumed for Thy salvation. And for the saying of Thy righteousness.

v124 Do with Thy servant according to Thy kindness. And Thy statutes teach Thou me.

v125 Thy servant [am] I -- cause me to understand, And I know Thy testimonies.

v126 Time for Jehovah to work! they have made void Thy law.

v127 Therefore I have loved Thy commands Above gold -- even fine gold.

v128 Therefore all my appointments I have declared wholly right, Every path of falsehood I have hated!

v129 [Pe.] Wonderful [are] Thy testimonies, Therefore hath my soul kept them.

v130 The opening of Thy words enlighteneth, Instructing the simple.

v131 My mouth I have opened, yea, I pant, For, for Thy commands I have longed.

v132 Look unto me, and favour me, As customary to those loving Thy name.

v133 My steps establish by Thy saying, And any iniquity doth not rule over me.

v134 Ransom me from the oppression of man, And I observe Thy precepts,

v135 Thy face cause to shine on Thy servant, And teach me Thy statutes.

v136 Rivulets of waters have come down mine eyes, Because they have not kept Thy law!

v137 [Tzade.] Righteous [art] Thou, O Jehovah, And upright [are] Thy judgments.

v138 Thou hast appointed Thy testimonies, Righteous and exceeding faithful,

v139 Cut me off hath my zeal, For mine adversaries forgot Thy words.

v140 Tried [is] thy saying exceedingly, And Thy servant hath loved it.

v141 Small I [am], and despised, Thy precepts I have not forgotten.

v142 Thy righteousness [is] righteousness to the age, And Thy law [is] truth.

v143 Adversity and distress have found me, Thy commands [are] my delights.

v144 The righteousness of Thy testimonies [is] to Cause me to understand, and I live!

v145 [Koph.] I have called with the whole heart, Answer me, O Jehovah, Thy statutes I keep,

v146 I have called Thee, save Thou me, And I do keep Thy testimonies.

v147 I have gone forward in the dawn, and I cry, For Thy word I have hoped.

v148 Mine eyes have gone before the watches, To meditate in Thy saying.

v149 My voice hear, according to Thy kindness, Jehovah, according to Thy judgment quicken me.

v150 Near have been my wicked pursuers, From Thy law they have been far off.

v151 Near [art] Thou, O Jehovah, And all Thy commands [are] truth.

v152 Of old I have known Thy testimonies, That to the age Thou hast founded them!

v153 [Resh.] See my affliction, and deliver Thou me, For Thy law I have not forgotten.

v154 Plead my plea, and redeem me, According to Thy saying quicken me.

v155 Far from the wicked [is] salvation, For Thy statutes they have not sought.

v156 Thy mercies [are] many, O Jehovah, According to Thy judgments quicken me.

v157 Many [are] my pursuers, and adversaries, From Thy testimonies I have not turned aside.

v158 I have seen treacherous ones, And grieve myself, Because Thy saying they have not kept.

v159 See, for thy precepts I have loved, Jehovah, According to Thy kindness quicken me.

v160 The sum of Thy word [is] truth, And to the age [is] every judgment of Thy righteousness!

v161 [Shin.] Princes have pursued me without cause, And because of Thy words was my heart afraid.

v162 I do rejoice concerning Thy saying, As one finding abundant spoil.

v163 Falsehood I have hated, yea I abominate [it], Thy law I have loved.

v164 Seven [times] in a day I have praised Thee, Because of the judgments of Thy righteousness.

v165 Abundant peace have those loving Thy law, And they have no stumbling-block.

v166 I have waited for Thy salvation, O Jehovah, And Thy commands I have done.

v167 Kept hath my soul Thy testimonies, And I do love them exceedingly.

v168 I have kept Thy precepts and Thy testimonies, For all my ways are before Thee!

v169 [Taw.] My loud cry cometh near before Thee, O Jehovah; According to Thy word cause me to understand.

v170 My supplication cometh in before Thee, According to Thy saying deliver Thou me.

v171 My lips do utter praise, For Thou dost teach me Thy statutes.

v172 My tongue doth sing of Thy saying, For all Thy commands [are] righteous.

v173 Thy hand is for a help to me, For Thy commands I have chosen.

v174 I have longed for Thy salvation, O Jehovah, And Thy law [is] my delight.

v175 My soul liveth, and it doth praise Thee, And Thy judgments do help me.

v176 I wandered as a lost sheep, seek Thy servant, For Thy precepts I have not forgotten!
