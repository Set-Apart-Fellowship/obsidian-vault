# Psalms 106 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 105|← Psalms 105]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 107|Psalms 107 →]]

***

v1 Praise ye Jah, give thanks to Jehovah, For good, for to the age, [is] His kindness.

v2 Who doth utter the mighty acts of Jehovah? Soundeth all His praise?

v3 O the happiness of those keeping judgment, Doing righteousness at all times.

v4 Remember me, O Jehovah, With the favour of Thy people, Look after me in Thy salvation.

v5 To look on the good of Thy chosen ones, To rejoice in the joy of Thy nation, To boast myself with Thine inheritance.

v6 We have sinned with our fathers, We have done perversely, we have done wickedly.

v7 Our fathers in Egypt, Have not considered wisely Thy wonders, They have not remembered The abundance of Thy kind acts, And provoke by the sea, at the sea of Suph.

v8 And He saveth them for His name's sake, To make known His might,

v9 And rebuketh the sea of Suph, and it is dried up, And causeth them to go Through depths as a wilderness.

v10 And He saveth them from the hand Of him who is hating, And redeemeth them from the hand of the enemy.

v11 And waters cover their adversaries, One of them hath not been left.

v12 And they believe in His words, they sing His praise,

v13 They have hasted -- forgotten His works, They have not waited for His counsel.

v14 And they lust greatly in a wilderness, And try God in a desert.

v15 And He giveth to them their request, And sendeth leanness into their soul.

v16 And they are envious of Moses in the camp, Of Aaron, Jehovah's holy one.

v17 Earth openeth, and swalloweth up Dathan, And covereth over the company of Abiram.

v18 And fire burneth among their company, A flame setteth on fire the wicked.

v19 They make a calf in Horeb, And bow themselves to a molten image,

v20 And change their Honour Into the form of an ox eating herbs.

v21 They have forgotten God their saviour, The doer of great things in Egypt,

v22 Of wonderful things in the land of Ham, Of fearful things by the sea of Suph.

v23 And He saith to destroy them, Unless Moses, His chosen one, Had stood in the breach before Him, To turn back His wrath from destroying.

v24 And they kick against the desirable land, They have not given credence to His word.

v25 And they murmur in their tents, They have not hearkened to the voice of Jehovah.

v26 And He lifteth up His hand to them, To cause them to fall in a wilderness,

v27 And to cause their seed to fall among nations, And to scatter them through lands.

v28 And they are coupled to Baal-Peor, And eat the sacrifices of the dead,

v29 And they provoke to anger by their actions, And a plague breaketh forth upon them,

v30 And Phinehas standeth, and executeth judgment, And the plague is restrained,

v31 And it is reckoned to him to righteousness, To all generations -- unto the age.

v32 And they cause wrath by the waters of Meribah, And it is evil to Moses for their sakes,

v33 For they have provoked his spirit, And he speaketh wrongfully with his lips.

v34 They have not destroyed the peoples, As Jehovah had said to them,

v35 And mix themselves among nations, and learn their works,

v36 And serve their idols, And they are to them for a snare.

v37 And they sacrifice their sons And their daughters to destroyers,

v38 And they shed innocent blood -- Blood of their sons and of their daughters, Whom they have sacrificed to idols of Canaan, And the land is profaned with blood.

v39 And they are defiled with their works, And commit whoredom in their habitual doings.

v40 And the anger of Jehovah Is kindled against His people, And He doth abominate His inheritance.

v41 And giveth them into the hand of nations, And those hating them rule over them,

v42 And their enemies oppress them, And they are humbled under their hand.

v43 Many times He doth deliver them, And they rebel in their counsel, And they are brought low in their iniquity.

v44 And He looketh on their distress When He heareth their cry,

v45 And remembereth for them His covenant, And is comforted, According to the abundance of His kindness.

v46 And He appointeth them for mercies Before all their captors.

v47 Save us, O Jehovah our God, and gather us from the nations, To give thanks to Thy holy name, To glory in Thy praise.

v48 Blessed [is] Jehovah, God of Israel, From the age even unto the age. And all the people said, 'Amen, praise Jah!'
