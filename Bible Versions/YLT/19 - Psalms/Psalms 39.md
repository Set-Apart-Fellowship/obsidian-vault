# Psalms 39 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 38|← Psalms 38]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 40|Psalms 40 →]]

***

v1 To the Overseer, to Jeduthun. -- A Psalm of David. I have said, 'I observe my ways, Against sinning with my tongue, I keep for my mouth a curb, while the wicked [is] before me.'

v2 I was dumb [with] silence, I kept silent from good, and my pain is excited.

v3 Hot [is] my heart within me, In my meditating doth the fire burn, I have spoken with my tongue.

v4 'Cause me to know, O Jehovah, mine end, And the measure of my days -- what it [is],' I know how frail I [am].

v5 Lo, handbreadths Thou hast made my days, And mine age [is] as nothing before Thee, Only, all vanity [is] every man set up. Selah.

v6 Only, in an image doth each walk habitually, Only, [in] vain, they are disquieted, He heapeth up and knoweth not who gathereth them.

v7 And, now, what have I expected? O Lord, my hope -- it [is] of Thee.

v8 From all my transgressions deliver me, A reproach of the fool make me not.

v9 I have been dumb, I open not my mouth, Because Thou -- Thou hast done [it].

v10 Turn aside from off me Thy stroke, From the striving of Thy hand I have been consumed.

v11 With reproofs against iniquity, Thou hast corrected man, And dost waste as a moth his desirableness, Only, vanity [is] every man. Selah.

v12 Hear my prayer, O Jehovah, And [to] my cry give ear, Unto my tear be not silent, For a sojourner I [am] with Thee, A settler like all my fathers.

v13 Look from me, and I brighten up before I go and am not!
