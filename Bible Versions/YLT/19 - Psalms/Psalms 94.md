# Psalms 94 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 93|← Psalms 93]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 95|Psalms 95 →]]

***

v1 God of vengeance -- Jehovah! God of vengeance, shine forth.

v2 Be lifted up, O Judge of the earth, Send back a recompence on the proud.

v3 Till when [do] the wicked, O Jehovah? Till when do the wicked exult?

v4 They utter -- they speak an old saw, All working iniquity do boast themselves.

v5 Thy people, O Jehovah, they bruise, And Thine inheritance they afflict.

v6 Widow and sojourner they slay, And fatherless ones they murder.

v7 And they say, 'Jehovah doth not see, And the God of Jacob doth not consider.'

v8 Consider, ye brutish among the people, And ye foolish, when do ye act wisely?

v9 He who planteth the ear doth He not hear? He who formeth the eye doth He not see?

v10 He who is instructing nations, Doth He not reprove? He who is teaching man knowledge [is] Jehovah.

v11 He knoweth the thoughts of man, that they [are] vanity.

v12 O the happiness of the man Whom Thou instructest, O Jah, And out of Thy law teachest him,

v13 To give rest to him from days of evil, While a pit is digged for the wicked.

v14 For Jehovah leaveth not His people, And His inheritance forsaketh not.

v15 For to righteousness judgment turneth back, And after it all the upright of heart,

v16 Who riseth up for me with evil doers? Who stationeth himself for me with workers of iniquity?

v17 Unless Jehovah [were] a help to me, My soul had almost inhabited silence.

v18 If I have said, 'My foot hath slipped,' Thy kindness, O Jehovah, supporteth me.

v19 In the abundance of my thoughts within me, Thy comforts delight my soul.

v20 Is a throne of mischief joined [with] Thee? A framer of perverseness by statute?

v21 They decree against the soul of the righteous, And innocent blood declare wicked.

v22 And Jehovah is for a high place to me, And my God [is] for a rock -- my refuge,

v23 And turneth back on them their iniquity, And in their wickedness cutteth them off; Jehovah our God doth cut them off!
