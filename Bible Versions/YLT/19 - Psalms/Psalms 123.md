# Psalms 123 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 122|← Psalms 122]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 124|Psalms 124 →]]

***

v1 A Song of the Ascents. Unto Thee I have lifted up mine eyes, O dweller in the heavens.

v2 Lo, as eyes of men-servants [Are] unto the hand of their masters, As eyes of a maid-servant [Are] unto the hand of her mistress, So [are] our eyes unto Jehovah our God, Till that He doth favour us.

v3 Favour us, O Jehovah, favour us, For greatly have we been filled with contempt,

v4 Greatly hath our soul been filled With the scorning of the easy ones, With the contempt of the arrogant!
