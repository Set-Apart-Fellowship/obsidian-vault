# Psalms 44 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 43|← Psalms 43]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 45|Psalms 45 →]]

***

v1 To the Overseer. -- By sons of Korah. An Instruction. O God, with our ears we have heard, Our fathers have recounted to us, The work Thou didst work in their days, In the days of old.

v2 Thou, [with] Thy hand, nations hast dispossessed. And Thou dost plant them. Thou afflictest peoples, and sendest them away.

v3 For, not by their sword Possessed they the land, And their arm gave not salvation to them, But Thy right hand, and Thine arm, And the light of Thy countenance, Because Thou hadst accepted them.

v4 Thou [art] He, my king, O God, Command the deliverances of Jacob.

v5 By Thee our adversaries we do push, By Thy name tread down our withstanders,

v6 For, not in my bow do I trust, And my sword doth not save me.

v7 For Thou hast saved us from our adversaries, And those hating us Thou hast put to shame.

v8 In God we have boasted all the day, And Thy name to the age we thank. Selah.

v9 In anger Thou hast cast off and causest us to blush, And goest not forth with our hosts.

v10 Thou causest us to turn backward from an adversary, And those hating us, Have spoiled for themselves.

v11 Thou makest us food like sheep, And among nations Thou hast scattered us.

v12 Thou sellest Thy people -- without wealth, And hast not become great by their price.

v13 Thou makest us a reproach to our neighbours, A scorn and a reproach to our surrounders.

v14 Thou makest us a simile among nations, A shaking of the head among peoples.

v15 All the day my confusion [is] before me, And the shame of my face hath covered me.

v16 Because of the voice of a reproacher and reviler, Because of an enemy and a self-avenger.

v17 All this met us, and we did not forget Thee, Nor have we dealt falsely in Thy covenant.

v18 We turn not backward our heart, Nor turn aside doth our step from Thy path.

v19 But Thou hast smitten us in a place of dragons, And dost cover us over with death-shade.

v20 If we have forgotten the name of our God, And spread our hands to a strange God,

v21 Doth not God search out this? For He knoweth the secrets of the heart.

v22 Surely, for Thy sake we have been slain all the day, Reckoned as sheep of the slaughter.

v23 Stir up -- why dost Thou sleep, O Lord? Awake, cast us not off for ever.

v24 Why Thy face hidest Thou? Thou forgettest our afflictions and our oppression,

v25 For bowed to the dust hath our soul, Cleaved to the earth hath our belly.

v26 Arise, a help to us, And ransom us for thy kindness' sake.
