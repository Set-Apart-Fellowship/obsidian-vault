# Psalms 12 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 11|← Psalms 11]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 13|Psalms 13 →]]

***

v1 To the Overseer, on the octave. -- A Psalm of David. Save, Jehovah, for the saintly hath failed, For the stedfast have ceased From the sons of men:

v2 Vanity they speak each with his neighbour, Lip of flattery! With heart and heart they speak.

v3 Jehovah doth cut off all lips of flattery, A tongue speaking great things,

v4 Who said, 'By our tongue we do mightily: Our lips [are] our own; who [is] lord over us?'

v5 Because of the spoiling of the poor, Because of the groaning of the needy, Now do I arise, saith Jehovah, I set in safety [him who] doth breathe for it.

v6 Sayings of Jehovah [are] pure sayings; Silver tried in a furnace of earth refined sevenfold.

v7 Thou, O Jehovah, dost preserve them, Thou keepest us from this generation to the age.

v8 Around the wicked walk continually, According as vileness is exalted by sons of men!
