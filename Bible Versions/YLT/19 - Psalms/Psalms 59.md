# Psalms 59 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 58|← Psalms 58]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 60|Psalms 60 →]]

***

v1 To the Overseer. -- 'Destroy not,' by David. -- A secret treasure, in Saul's sending, and they watch the house to put him to death. Deliver me from mine enemies, O my God, From my withstanders set me on high.

v2 Deliver me from workers of iniquity, And from men of blood save me.

v3 For, lo, they laid wait for my soul, Assembled against me are strong ones, Not my transgression nor my sin, O Jehovah.

v4 Without punishment they run and prepare themselves, Stir up to meet me, and see.

v5 And Thou, Jehovah, God of Hosts, God of Israel, Awake to inspect all the nations. Favour not any treacherous dealers of iniquity. Selah.

v6 They turn back at evening, They make a noise like a dog, And go round about the city.

v7 Lo, they belch out with their mouths, Swords [are] in their lips, for 'Who heareth?'

v8 And Thou, O Jehovah dost laugh at them, Thou dost mock at all the nations.

v9 O my Strength, unto Thee I take heed, For God [is] my tower -- the God of my kindness.

v10 God doth go before me, He causeth me to look on mine enemies.

v11 Slay them not, lest my people forget, Shake them by Thy strength, And bring them down, O Lord our shield.

v12 The sin of their mouth [is] a word of their lips, And they are captured in their pride, And from the curse and lying they recount.

v13 Consume in fury, consume and they are not, And they know that God is ruling in Jacob, To the ends of the earth. Selah.

v14 And they turn back at evening, They make a noise like a dog, And they go round about the city.

v15 They -- they wander for food, If they are not satisfied -- then they murmur.

v16 And I -- I sing [of] Thy strength, And I sing at morn [of] Thy kindness, For thou hast been a tower to me, And a refuge for me in a day of adversity.

v17 O my Strength, unto Thee I sing praise, For God [is] my tower, the God of my kindness!
