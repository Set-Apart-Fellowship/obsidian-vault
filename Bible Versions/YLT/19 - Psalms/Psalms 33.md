# Psalms 33 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 32|← Psalms 32]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 34|Psalms 34 →]]

***

v1 Sing, ye righteous, in Jehovah, For upright ones praise [is] comely.

v2 Give ye thanks to Jehovah with a harp, With psaltery of ten strings sing praise to Him,

v3 Sing ye to Him a new song, Play skilfully with shouting.

v4 For upright [is] the word of Jehovah, And all His work [is] in faithfulness.

v5 Loving righteousness and judgment, Of the kindness of Jehovah is the earth full.

v6 By the word of Jehovah The heavens have been made, And by the breath of His mouth all their host.

v7 Gathering as a heap the waters of the sea, Putting in treasuries the depths.

v8 Afraid of Jehovah are all the earth, Of Him are all the inhabitants of the world afraid.

v9 For He hath said, and it is, He hath commanded, and it standeth.

v10 Jehovah made void the counsel of nations, He disallowed the thoughts of the peoples.

v11 The counsel of Jehovah to the age standeth, The thoughts of His heart to all generations.

v12 O the happiness of the nation whose God [is] Jehovah, Of the people He did choose, For an inheritance to Him.

v13 From the heavens hath Jehovah looked, He hath seen all the sons of men.

v14 From the fixed place of His dwelling, He looked unto all inhabitants of the earth;

v15 Who is forming their hearts together, Who is attending unto all their works.

v16 The king is not saved by the multitude of a force. A mighty man is not delivered, By abundance of power.

v17 A false thing [is] the horse for safety, And by the abundance of his strength He doth not deliver.

v18 Lo, the eye of Jehovah [is] to those fearing Him, To those waiting for His kindness,

v19 To deliver from death their soul, And to keep them alive in famine.

v20 Our soul hath waited for Jehovah, Our help and our shield [is] He,

v21 For in Him doth our heart rejoice, For in His holy name we have trusted.

v22 Let Thy kindness, O Jehovah, be upon us, As we have waited for Thee!
