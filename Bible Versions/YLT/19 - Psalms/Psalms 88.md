# Psalms 88 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 87|← Psalms 87]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 89|Psalms 89 →]]

***

v1 A Song, a Psalm, by sons of Korah, to the Overseer, 'Concerning the Sickness of Afflictions.' -- An instruction, by Heman the Ezrahite. O Jehovah, God of my salvation, Daily I have cried, nightly before Thee,

v2 My prayer cometh in before Thee, Incline Thine ear to my loud cry,

v3 For my soul hath been full of evils, And my life hath come to Sheol.

v4 I have been reckoned with those going down [to] the pit, I have been as a man without strength.

v5 Among the dead -- free, As pierced ones lying in the grave, Whom Thou hast not remembered any more, Yea, they by Thy hand have been cut off.

v6 Thou hast put me in the lowest pit, In dark places, in depths.

v7 Upon me hath Thy fury lain, And [with] all Thy breakers Thou hast afflicted. Selah.

v8 Thou hast put mine acquaintance far from me, Thou hast made me an abomination to them, Shut up -- I go not forth.

v9 Mine eye hath grieved because of affliction, I called Thee, O Jehovah, all the day, I have spread out unto Thee my hands.

v10 To the dead dost Thou do wonders? Do Rephaim rise? do they thank Thee? Selah.

v11 Is Thy kindness recounted in the grave? Thy faithfulness in destruction?

v12 Are Thy wonders known in the darkness? And Thy righteousness in the land of forgetfulness?

v13 And I, unto Thee, O Jehovah, I have cried, And in the morning doth my prayer come before Thee.

v14 Why, O Jehovah, castest Thou off my soul? Thou hidest Thy face from me.

v15 I [am] afflicted, and expiring from youth, I have borne Thy terrors -- I pine away.

v16 Over me hath Thy wrath passed, Thy terrors have cut me off,

v17 They have surrounded me as waters all the day, They have gone round against me together,

v18 Thou hast put far from me lover and friend, Mine acquaintance [is] the place of darkness!
