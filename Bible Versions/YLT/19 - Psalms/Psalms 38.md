# Psalms 38 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 37|← Psalms 37]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 39|Psalms 39 →]]

***

v1 A Psalm of David, 'To cause to remember.' Jehovah, in Thy wrath reprove me not, Nor in Thy fury chastise me.

v2 For Thine arrows have come down on me, And Thou lettest down upon me Thy hand.

v3 Soundness is not in my flesh, Because of Thine indignation, Peace is not in my bones because of my sin.

v4 For mine iniquities have passed over my head, As a heavy burden -- too heavy for me.

v5 Stunk -- become corrupt have my wounds, Because of my folly.

v6 I have been bent down, I have been bowed down -- unto excess, All the day I have gone mourning.

v7 For my flanks have been full of drought, And soundness is not in my flesh.

v8 I have been feeble and smitten -- unto excess, I have roared from disquietude of heart.

v9 Lord, before Thee [is] all my desire, And my sighing from Thee hath not been hid.

v10 My heart [is] panting, my power hath forsaken me, And the light of mine eyes, Even they are not with me.

v11 My lovers and my friends over-against my plague stand. And my neighbours afar off have stood.

v12 And those seeking my soul lay a snare, And those seeking my evil Have spoken mischievous things, And they do deceits meditate all the day.

v13 And I, as deaf, hear not. And as a dumb one who openeth not his mouth.

v14 Yea, I am as a man who heareth not, And in his mouth are no reproofs.

v15 Because for Thee, O Jehovah, I have waited, Thou dost answer, O Lord my God.

v16 When I said, 'Lest they rejoice over me, In the slipping of my foot against me they magnified themselves.

v17 For I am ready to halt, And my pain [is] before me continually.

v18 For mine iniquity I declare, I am sorry for my sin.

v19 And mine enemies [are] lively, They have been strong, and those hating me without cause, Have been multiplied.

v20 And those paying evil for good accuse me, Because of my pursuing good.

v21 Do not forsake me, O Jehovah, My God, be not far from me,

v22 Haste to help me, O Lord, my salvation!
