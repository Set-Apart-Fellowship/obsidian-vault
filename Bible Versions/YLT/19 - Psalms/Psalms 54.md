# Psalms 54 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 53|← Psalms 53]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 55|Psalms 55 →]]

***

v1 To the Overseer with stringed instruments. -- An instruction, by David, in the coming in of the Ziphim, and they say to Saul, 'Is not David hiding himself with us?' O God, by Thy name save me, and by Thy might judge me.

v2 O God, hear my prayer, Give ear to the sayings of my mouth,

v3 For strangers have risen up against me And terrible ones have sought my soul, They have not set God before them. Selah.

v4 Lo, God [is] a helper to me, The Lord [is] with those supporting my soul,

v5 Turn back doth the evil thing to mine enemies, In Thy truth cut them off.

v6 With a free will-offering I sacrifice to Thee, I thank Thy name, O Jehovah, for [it is] good,

v7 For, from all adversity He delivered me, And on mine enemies hath mine eye looked!
