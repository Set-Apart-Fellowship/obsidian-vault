# Psalms 48 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 47|← Psalms 47]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 49|Psalms 49 →]]

***

v1 A Song, a Psalm, by sons of Korah. Great [is] Jehovah, and praised greatly, In the city of our God -- His holy hill.

v2 Beautiful [for] elevation, A joy of all the land, [is] Mount Zion, The sides of the north, the city of a great king.

v3 God in her high places is known for a tower.

v4 For, lo, the kings met, they passed by together,

v5 They have seen -- so they have marvelled, They have been troubled, they were hastened away.

v6 Trembling hath seized them there, Pain, as of a travailing woman.

v7 By an east wind Thou shiverest ships of Tarshish.

v8 As we have heard, so we have seen, In the city of Jehovah of hosts, In the city of our God, God doth establish her -- to the age. Selah.

v9 We have thought, O God, of Thy kindness, In the midst of Thy temple,

v10 As [is] Thy name, O God, so [is] Thy praise, Over the ends of the earth, Righteousness hath filled Thy right hand.

v11 Rejoice doth Mount Zion, The daughters of Judah are joyful, For the sake of Thy judgments.

v12 Compass Zion, and go round her, count her towers,

v13 Set your heart to her bulwark, Consider her high places, So that ye recount to a later generation,

v14 That this God [is] our God -- To the age and for ever, He -- he doth lead us over death!
