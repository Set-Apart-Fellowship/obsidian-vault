# Psalms 133 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 132|← Psalms 132]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 134|Psalms 134 →]]

***

v1 A Song of the Ascents, by David. Lo, how good and how pleasant The dwelling of brethren -- even together!

v2 As the good oil on the head, Coming down on the beard, the beard of Aaron, That cometh down on the skirt of his robes,

v3 As dew of Hermon -- That cometh down on hills of Zion, For there Jehovah commanded the blessing -- Life unto the age!
