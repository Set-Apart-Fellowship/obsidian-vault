# Psalms 103 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 102|← Psalms 102]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 104|Psalms 104 →]]

***

v1 By David. Bless, O my soul, Jehovah, And all my inward parts -- His Holy Name.

v2 Bless, O my soul, Jehovah, And forget not all His benefits,

v3 Who is forgiving all thine iniquities, Who is healing all thy diseases,

v4 Who is redeeming from destruction thy life, Who is crowning thee -- kindness and mercies,

v5 Who is satisfying with good thy desire, Renew itself as an eagle doth thy youth.

v6 Jehovah is doing righteousness and judgments For all the oppressed.

v7 He maketh known His ways to Moses, To the sons of Israel His acts.

v8 Merciful and gracious [is] Jehovah, Slow to anger, and abundant in mercy.

v9 Not for ever doth He strive, Nor to the age doth He watch.

v10 Not according to our sins hath He done to us, Nor according to our iniquities Hath He conferred benefits upon us.

v11 For, as the height of the heavens [is] above the earth, His kindness hath been mighty over those fearing Him.

v12 As the distance of east from west He hath put far from us our transgressions.

v13 As a father hath mercy on sons, Jehovah hath mercy on those fearing Him.

v14 For He hath known our frame, Remembering that we [are] dust.

v15 Mortal man! as grass [are] his days, As a flower of the field so he flourisheth;

v16 For a wind hath passed over it, and it is not, And its place doth not discern it any more.

v17 And the kindness of Jehovah [Is] from age even unto age on those fearing Him, And His righteousness to sons' sons,

v18 To those keeping His covenant, And to those remembering His precepts to do them.

v19 Jehovah in the heavens Hath established His throne, And His kingdom over all hath ruled.

v20 Bless Jehovah, ye His messengers, Mighty in power -- doing His word, To hearken to the voice of His Word.

v21 Bless Jehovah, all ye His hosts, His ministers -- doing His pleasure.

v22 Bless Jehovah, all ye His works, In all places of His dominion. Bless, O my soul, Jehovah!
