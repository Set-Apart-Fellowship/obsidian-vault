# Psalms 14 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 13|← Psalms 13]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 15|Psalms 15 →]]

***

v1 To the Overseer. -- By David. A fool hath said in his heart, 'God is not;' They have done corruptly, They have done abominable actions, There is not a doer of good.

v2 Jehovah from the heavens Hath looked on the sons of men, To see if there is a wise one -- seeking God.

v3 The whole have turned aside, Together they have been filthy: There is not a doer of good, not even one.

v4 Have all working iniquity not known? Those consuming my people have eaten bread, Jehovah they have not called.

v5 There they have feared a fear, For God [is] in the generation of the righteous.

v6 The counsel of the poor ye cause to stink, Because Jehovah [is] his refuge.

v7 'Who doth give from Zion the salvation of Israel? When Jehovah doth turn back [To] a captivity of His people, Jacob doth rejoice -- Israel is glad!
