# Psalms 72 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 71|← Psalms 71]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 73|Psalms 73 →]]

***

v1 By Solomon. O God, Thy judgments to the king give, And Thy righteousness to the king's son.

v2 He judgeth Thy people with righteousness, And Thy poor with judgment.

v3 The mountains bear peace to the people, And the heights by righteousness.

v4 He judgeth the poor of the people, Giveth deliverance to the sons of the needy, And bruiseth the oppressor.

v5 They fear Thee with the sun, and before the moon, Generation -- generations.

v6 He cometh down as rain on mown grass, As showers -- sprinkling the earth.

v7 Flourish in his days doth the righteous, And abundance of peace till the moon is not.

v8 And he ruleth from sea unto sea, And from the river unto the ends of earth.

v9 Before him bow do the inhabitants of the dry places, And his enemies lick the dust.

v10 Kings of Tarshish and of the isles send back a present. Kings of Sheba and Seba a reward bring near.

v11 And all kings do bow themselves to him, All nations do serve him,

v12 For he delivereth the needy who crieth, And the poor when he hath no helper,

v13 He hath pity on the poor and needy, And the souls of the needy he saveth,

v14 From fraud and from violence he redeemeth their soul, And precious is their blood in his eyes.

v15 And he liveth, and giveth to him of the gold of Sheba, And prayeth for him continually, All the day he doth bless him.

v16 There is a handful of corn in the earth, On the top of mountains, Shake like Lebanon doth its fruit, And they flourish out of the city as the herb of the earth.

v17 His name is to the age, Before the sun is his name continued, And they bless themselves in him, All nations do pronounce him happy.

v18 Blessed is Jehovah God, God of Israel, He alone is doing wonders,

v19 And blessed [is] the Name of His honour to the age, And the whole earth is filled [with] His honour. Amen, and amen!

v20 The prayers of David son of Jesse have been ended.
