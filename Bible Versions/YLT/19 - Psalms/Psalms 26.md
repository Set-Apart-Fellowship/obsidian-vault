# Psalms 26 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 25|← Psalms 25]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 27|Psalms 27 →]]

***

v1 By David. Judge me, O Jehovah, for I in mine integrity have walked, And in Jehovah I have trusted, I slide not.

v2 Try me, O Jehovah, and prove me, Purified [are] my reins and my heart.

v3 For Thy kindness [is] before mine eyes, And I have walked habitually in Thy truth.

v4 I have not sat with vain men, And with dissemblers I enter not.

v5 I have hated the assembly of evil doers, And with the wicked I sit not.

v6 I wash in innocency my hands, And I compass Thine altar, O Jehovah.

v7 To sound with a voice of confession, And to recount all Thy wonders.

v8 Jehovah, I have loved the habitation of Thy house, And the place of the tabernacle of Thine honour.

v9 Do not gather with sinners my soul, And with men of blood my life,

v10 In whose hand [is] a wicked device, And their right hand [is] full of bribes.

v11 And I, in mine integrity I walk, Redeem me, and favour me.

v12 My foot hath stood in uprightness, In assemblies I bless Jehovah!
