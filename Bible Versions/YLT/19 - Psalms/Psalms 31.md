# Psalms 31 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 30|← Psalms 30]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 32|Psalms 32 →]]

***

v1 To the Overseer. -- A Psalm of David. In Thee, O Jehovah, I have trusted, Let me not be ashamed to the age, In Thy righteousness deliver me.

v2 Incline unto me Thine ear hastily, deliver me, Be to me for a strong rock, For a house of bulwarks to save me.

v3 For my rock and my bulwark [art] Thou, For Thy name's sake lead me and tend me.

v4 Bring me out from the net that they hid for me, For Thou [art] my strength.

v5 Into Thy hand I commit my spirit, Thou hast redeemed me, Jehovah God of truth.

v6 I have hated the observers of lying vanities, And I toward Jehovah have been confident.

v7 I rejoice, and am glad in Thy kindness, In that Thou hast seen mine affliction, Thou hast known in adversities my soul.

v8 And Thou hast not shut me up, Into the hand of an enemy, Thou hast caused my feet to stand in a broad place.

v9 Favour me, O Jehovah, for distress [is] to me, Mine eye, my soul, and my body Have become old by provocation.

v10 For my life hath been consumed in sorrow And my years in sighing. Feeble because of mine iniquity hath been my strength, And my bones have become old.

v11 Among all mine adversaries I have been a reproach, And to my neighbours exceedingly, And a fear to mine acquaintances, Those seeing me without -- fled from me.

v12 I have been forgotten as dead out of mind, I have been as a perishing vessel.

v13 For I have heard an evil account of many, Fear [is] round about. In their being united against me, To take my life they have devised,

v14 And I on Thee -- I have trusted, O Jehovah, I have said, 'Thou [art] my God.'

v15 In Thy hand [are] my times, Deliver me from the hand of my enemies, And from my pursuers.

v16 Cause Thy face to shine on Thy servant, Save me in Thy kindness.

v17 O Jehovah, let me not be ashamed, For I have called Thee, let the wicked be ashamed, Let them become silent to Sheol.

v18 Let lips of falsehood become dumb, That are speaking against the righteous, Ancient sayings, in pride and contempt.

v19 How abundant is Thy goodness, That Thou hast laid up for those fearing Thee,

v20 Thou hast wrought for those trusting in Thee, Before sons of men. Thou hidest them in the secret place of Thy presence, From artifices of man, Thou concealest them in a tabernacle, From the strife of tongues.

v21 Blessed [is] Jehovah, For He hath made marvellous His kindness To me in a city of bulwarks.

v22 And I -- I have said in my haste, 'I have been cut off from before Thine eyes,' But Thou hast heard the voice of my supplications, In my crying unto Thee.

v23 Love Jehovah, all ye His saints, Jehovah is keeping the faithful, And recompensing abundantly a proud doer.

v24 Be strong, and He strengtheneth your heart, All ye who are waiting for Jehovah!
