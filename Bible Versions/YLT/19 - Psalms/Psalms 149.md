# Psalms 149 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 148|← Psalms 148]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 150|Psalms 150 →]]

***

v1 Praise ye Jah! Sing ye to Jehovah a new song, His praise in an assembly of saints.

v2 Israel doth rejoice in his Maker, Sons of Zion do joy in their king.

v3 They praise His name in a dance, With timbrel and harp sing praise to Him.

v4 For Jehovah is pleased with His people, He beautifieth the humble with salvation.

v5 Exult do saints in honour, They sing aloud on their beds.

v6 The exaltation of God [is] in their throat, And a two-edged sword in their hand.

v7 To do vengeance among nations, Punishments among the peoples.

v8 To bind their kings with chains, And their honoured ones with fetters of iron,

v9 To do among them the judgment written, An honour it [is] for all his saints. Praise ye Jah!
