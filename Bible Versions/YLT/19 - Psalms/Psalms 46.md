# Psalms 46 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 45|← Psalms 45]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 47|Psalms 47 →]]

***

v1 To the Overseer. -- By sons of Korah. 'For the Virgins.' -- A song. God [is] to us a refuge and strength, A help in adversities found most surely.

v2 Therefore we fear not in the changing of earth, And in the slipping of mountains Into the heart of the seas.

v3 Roar -- troubled are its waters, Mountains they shake in its pride. Selah.

v4 A river -- its rivulets rejoice the city of God, Thy holy place of the tabernacles of the Most High.

v5 God [is] in her midst -- she is not moved, God doth help her at the turn of the morn!

v6 Troubled have been nations, Moved have been kingdoms, He hath given forth with His voice, earth melteth.

v7 Jehovah of Hosts [is] with us, A tower for us [is] the God of Jacob. Selah.

v8 Come ye, see the works of Jehovah, Who hath done astonishing things in the earth,

v9 Causing wars to cease, Unto the end of the earth, the bow he shivereth, And the spear He hath cut asunder, Chariots he doth burn with fire.

v10 Desist, and know that I [am] God, I am exalted among nations, I am exalted in the earth.

v11 Jehovah of hosts [is] with us, A tower for us [is] the God of Jacob! Selah.
