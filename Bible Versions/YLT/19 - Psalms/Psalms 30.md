# Psalms 30 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 29|← Psalms 29]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 31|Psalms 31 →]]

***

v1 A Psalm. -- A song of the dedication of the house of David. I exalt Thee, O Jehovah, For Thou hast drawn me up, and hast not let mine enemies rejoice over me.

v2 Jehovah my God, I have cried to Thee, And Thou dost heal me.

v3 Jehovah, Thou hast brought up from Sheol my soul, Thou hast kept me alive, From going down [to] the pit.

v4 Sing praise to Jehovah, ye His saints, And give thanks at the remembrance of His holiness,

v5 For -- a moment [is] in His anger, Life [is] in His good-will, At even remaineth weeping, and at morn singing.

v6 And I -- I have said in mine ease, 'I am not moved -- to the age.

v7 O Jehovah, in Thy good pleasure, Thou hast caused strength to remain for my mountain,' Thou hast hidden Thy face -- I have been troubled.

v8 Unto Thee, O Jehovah, I call, And unto Jehovah I make supplication.

v9 'What gain [is] in my blood? In my going down unto corruption? Doth dust thank Thee? doth it declare Thy truth?

v10 Hear, O Jehovah, and favour me, O Jehovah, be a helper to me.'

v11 Thou hast turned my mourning to dancing for me, Thou hast loosed my sackcloth, And girdest me [with] joy.

v12 So that honour doth praise Thee, and is not silent, O Jehovah, my God, to the age I thank Thee!
