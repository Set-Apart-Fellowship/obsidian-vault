# Psalms 50 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 49|← Psalms 49]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 51|Psalms 51 →]]

***

v1 A Psalm of Asaph. The God of gods -- Jehovah -- hath spoken, And He calleth to the earth From the rising of the sun unto its going in.

v2 From Zion, the perfection of beauty, God shone.

v3 Our God cometh, and is not silent, Fire before Him doth devour, And round about him it hath been very tempestuous.

v4 He doth call unto the heavens from above, And unto the earth, to judge His people.

v5 Gather ye to Me My saints, Making covenant with Me over a sacrifice.

v6 And the heavens declare His righteousness, For God Himself [is] judge. Selah.

v7 Hear, O My people, and I speak, O Israel, and I testify against thee, God, thy God [am] I.

v8 Not for thy sacrifices do I reprove thee, Yea, thy burnt-offerings [Are] before Me continually.

v9 I take not from thy house a bullock, From thy folds he goats.

v10 For Mine [is] every beast of the forest, The cattle on the hills of oxen.

v11 I have known every fowl of the mountains, And the wild beast of the field [is] with Me.

v12 If I am hungry I tell not to thee, For Mine [is] the world and its fulness.

v13 Do I eat the flesh of bulls, And drink the blood of he-goats?

v14 Sacrifice to God confession, And complete to the Most High thy vows.

v15 And call Me in a day of adversity, I deliver thee, and thou honourest Me.

v16 And to the wicked hath God said: What to thee -- to recount My statutes? That thou liftest up My covenant on thy mouth?

v17 Yea, thou hast hated instruction, And dost cast My words behind thee.

v18 If thou hast seen a thief, Then thou art pleased with him, And with adulterers [is] thy portion.

v19 Thy mouth thou hast sent forth with evil, And thy tongue joineth deceit together,

v20 Thou sittest, against thy brother thou speakest, Against a son of thy mother givest slander.

v21 These thou didst, and I kept silent, Thou hast thought that I am like thee, I reprove thee, and set in array before thine eyes.

v22 Understand this, I pray you, Ye who are forgetting God, Lest I tear, and there is no deliverer.

v23 He who is sacrificing praise honoureth Me, As to him who maketh a way, I cause him to look on the salvation of God!
