# Psalms 55 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 54|← Psalms 54]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 56|Psalms 56 →]]

***

v1 To the Overseer with stringed instruments. -- An instruction, by David. Give ear, O God, [to] my prayer, And hide not from my supplication.

v2 Attend to me, and answer me, I mourn in my meditation, and make a noise,

v3 Because of the voice of an enemy, Because of the oppression of the wicked, For they cause sorrow to move against me, And in anger they hate me.

v4 My heart is pained within me, And terrors of death have fallen on me.

v5 Fear and trembling come in to me, And horror doth cover me.

v6 And I say, 'Who doth give to me a pinion as a dove? I fly away and rest,

v7 Lo, I move far off, I lodge in a wilderness. Selah.

v8 I hasten escape for myself, From a rushing wind, from a whirlwind.

v9 Swallow up, O Lord, divide their tongue, For I saw violence and strife in a city.

v10 By day and by night they go round it, on its walls. Both iniquity and perverseness [are] in its midst,

v11 Mischiefs [are] in its midst. Fraud and deceit depart not from its street.

v12 For an enemy reproacheth me not, or I bear [it], He who is hating me Hath not magnified himself against me, Or I hide from him.

v13 But thou, a man -- as mine equal, My familiar friend, and mine acquaintance.

v14 When together we sweeten counsel, Into the house of God we walk in company.

v15 Desolations [are] upon them, They go down [to] Sheol -- alive, For wickedness [is] in their dwelling, in their midst.

v16 I -- to God I call, and Jehovah saveth me.

v17 Evening, and morning, and noon, I meditate, and make a noise, and He heareth my voice,

v18 He hath ransomed in peace my soul From him who is near to me, For with the multitude they were with me.

v19 God doth hear and afflict them, And He sitteth of old. Selah. Because they have no changes, and fear not God,

v20 He hath sent forth his hands against his well-wishers, He hath polluted his covenant.

v21 Sweeter than honey hath been his mouth, And his heart [is] war! Softer have been his words than oil, And they [are] drawn [swords].

v22 Cast on Jehovah that which He hath given thee, And He doth sustain thee, He doth not suffer for ever the moving of the righteous.

v23 And Thou, O God, dost bring them down To a pit of destruction, Men of blood and deceit reach not to half their days, And I -- I do trust in Thee!
