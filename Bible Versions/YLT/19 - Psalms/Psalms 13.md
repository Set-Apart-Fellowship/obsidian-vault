# Psalms 13 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 12|← Psalms 12]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 14|Psalms 14 →]]

***

v1 To the Overseer. -- A Psalm of David. Till when, O Jehovah, Dost Thou forget me? -- for ever? Till when dost Thou hide Thy face from me?

v2 Till when do I set counsels in my soul? Sorrow inn my heart daily? Till when is mine enemy exalted over me?

v3 Look attentively; Answer me, O Jehovah, my God, Enlighten mine eyes, lest I sleep in death,

v4 Lest mine enemy say, 'I overcame him,' Mine adversaries joy when I am moved.

v5 And I, in Thy kindness I have trusted, Rejoice doth my heart in Thy salvation.

v6 I do sing to Jehovah, For He hath conferred benefits upon me!
