# Psalms 104 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 103|← Psalms 103]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 105|Psalms 105 →]]

***

v1 Bless, O my soul, Jehovah! Jehovah, my God, Thou hast been very great, Honour and majesty Thou hast put on.

v2 Covering himself [with] light as a garment, Stretching out the heavens as a curtain,

v3 Who is laying the beam of His upper chambers in the waters, Who is making thick clouds His chariot, Who is walking on wings of wind,

v4 Making His messengers -- the winds, His ministers -- the flaming fire.

v5 He hath founded earth on its bases, It is not moved to the age and for ever.

v6 The abyss! as with clothing Thou hast covered it, Above hills do waters stand.

v7 From Thy rebuke they flee, From the voice of Thy thunder haste away.

v8 They go up hills -- they go down valleys, Unto a place Thou hast founded for them.

v9 A border Thou hast set, they pass not over, They turn not back to cover the earth.

v10 Who is sending forth fountains in valleys, Between hills they go on.

v11 They water every beast of the field, Wild asses break their thirst.

v12 By them the fowl of the heavens doth dwell, From between the branches They give forth the voice.

v13 Watering hills from His upper chambers, From the fruit of Thy works is the earth satisfied.

v14 Causing grass to spring up for cattle, And herb for the service of man, To bring forth bread from the earth,

v15 And wine -- it rejoiceth the heart of man, To cause the face to shine from oil, And bread -- the heart of man it supporteth.

v16 Satisfied [are] the trees of Jehovah, Cedars of Lebanon that He hath planted,

v17 Where birds do make nests, The stork -- the firs [are] her house.

v18 The high hills [are] for wild goats, Rocks [are] a refuge for conies,

v19 He made the moon for seasons, The sun hath known his place of entrance.

v20 Thou settest darkness, and it is night, In it doth every beast of the forest creep.

v21 The young lions are roaring for prey, And to seek from God their food.

v22 The sun riseth, they are gathered, And in their dens they crouch.

v23 Man goeth forth to his work, And to his service -- till evening.

v24 How many have been Thy works, O Jehovah, All of them in wisdom Thou hast made, Full is the earth of thy possessions.

v25 This, the sea, great and broad of sides, There [are] moving things -- innumerable, Living creatures -- small with great.

v26 There do ships go: leviathan, That Thou hast formed to play in it.

v27 All of them unto Thee do look, To give their food in its season.

v28 Thou dost give to them -- they gather, Thou dost open Thy hand -- they [are] satisfied [with] good.

v29 Thou hidest Thy face -- they are troubled, Thou gatherest their spirit -- they expire, And unto their dust they turn back.

v30 Thou sendest out Thy Spirit, they are created, And Thou renewest the face of the ground.

v31 The honour of Jehovah is to the age, Jehovah rejoiceth in His works,

v32 Who is looking to earth, and it trembleth, He cometh against hills, and they smoke.

v33 I sing to Jehovah during my life, I sing praise to my God while I exist.

v34 Sweet is my meditation on Him, I -- I do rejoice in Jehovah.

v35 Consumed are sinners from the earth, And the wicked are no more. Bless, O my soul, Jehovah. Praise ye Jehovah!
