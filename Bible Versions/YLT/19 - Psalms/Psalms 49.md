# Psalms 49 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 48|← Psalms 48]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 50|Psalms 50 →]]

***

v1 To the Overseer. -- By sons of Korah. A Psalm. Hear this, all ye peoples, Give ear, all ye inhabitants of the world.

v2 Both low and high, together rich and needy.

v3 My mouth speaketh wise things, And the meditations of my heart [are] things of understanding.

v4 I incline to a simile mine ear, I open with a harp my riddle:

v5 Why do I fear in days of evil? The iniquity of my supplanters doth compass me.

v6 Those trusting on their wealth, And in the multitude of their riches, Do shew themselves foolish.

v7 A brother doth no one at all ransom, He doth not give to God his atonement.

v8 And precious [is] the redemption of their soul, And it hath ceased -- to the age.

v9 And still he liveth for ever, He seeth not the pit.

v10 For he seeth wise men die, Together the foolish and brutish perish, And have left to others their wealth.

v11 Their heart [is]: Their houses [are] to the age, Their tabernacles to all generations. They proclaimed their names over the lands.

v12 And man in honour doth not remain, He hath been like the beasts, they have been cut off.

v13 This their way [is] folly for them, And their posterity with their sayings are pleased. Selah.

v14 As sheep for Sheol they have set themselves, Death doth afflict them, And the upright rule over them in the morning, And their form [is] for consumption. Sheol [is] a dwelling for him.

v15 Only, God doth ransom my soul from the hand of Sheol, For He doth receive me. Selah.

v16 Fear not, when one maketh wealth, When the honour of his house is abundant,

v17 For at his death he receiveth nothing, His honour goeth not down after him.

v18 For his soul in his life he blesseth, (And they praise thee when thou dost well for thyself.)

v19 It cometh to the generation of his fathers, For ever they see not the light.

v20 Man in honour, who understandest not, Hath been like the beasts, they have been cut off!
