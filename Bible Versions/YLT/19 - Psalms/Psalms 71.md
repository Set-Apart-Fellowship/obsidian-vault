# Psalms 71 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 70|← Psalms 70]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 72|Psalms 72 →]]

***

v1 In Thee, O Jehovah, I have trusted, Let me not be ashamed to the age.

v2 In Thy righteousness Thou dost deliver me, And dost cause me to escape, Incline unto me Thine ear, and save me.

v3 Be to me for a rock -- a habitation, To go in continually, Thou hast given command to save me, For my rock and my bulwark [art] Thou.

v4 O my God, cause me to escape From the hand of the wicked, From the hand of the perverse and violent.

v5 For Thou [art] my hope, O Lord Jehovah, My trust from my youth.

v6 By Thee I have been supported from the womb, From my mother's bowels Thou dost cut me out, In Thee [is] my praise continually.

v7 As a wonder I have been to many, And Thou [art] my strong refuge.

v8 Filled is my mouth [with] Thy praise, All the day [with] Thy beauty.

v9 Cast me not off at the time of old age, According to the consumption of my power forsake me not.

v10 For mine enemies have spoken against me, And those watching my soul have taken counsel together,

v11 Saying, 'God hath forsaken him, Pursue and catch him, for there is no deliverer.'

v12 O God, be not far from me, O my God, for my help make haste.

v13 They are ashamed, they are consumed, Who are opposing my soul, They are covered [with] reproach and blushing, Who are seeking my evil,

v14 And I continually do wait with hope, And have added unto all Thy praise.

v15 My mouth recounteth Thy righteousness, All the day Thy salvation, For I have not known the numbers.

v16 I come in the might of the Lord Jehovah, I mention Thy righteousness -- Thine only.

v17 God, Thou hast taught me from my youth, And hitherto I declare Thy wonders.

v18 And also unto old age and grey hairs, O God, forsake me not, Till I declare Thy strength to a generation, To every one that cometh Thy might.

v19 And Thy righteousness, O God, [is] unto the heights, Because Thou hast done great things, O God, who [is] like Thee?

v20 Because Thou hast showed me many and sad distresses, Thou turnest back -- Thou revivest me, And from the depths of the earth, Thou turnest back -- Thou bringest me up.

v21 Thou dost increase my greatness, And Thou surroundest -- Thou comfortest me,

v22 I also thank Thee with a vessel of psaltery, Thy truth, O my God, I sing to Thee with a harp, O Holy One of Israel,

v23 My lips cry aloud when I sing praise to Thee, And my soul that Thou hast redeemed,

v24 My tongue also all the day uttereth Thy righteousness, Because ashamed -- because confounded, Have been those seeking my evil!
