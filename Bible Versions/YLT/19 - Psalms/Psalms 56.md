# Psalms 56 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 55|← Psalms 55]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 57|Psalms 57 →]]

***

v1 To the Overseer. -- 'On the Dumb Dove far off.' -- A secret treasure of David, in the Philistines' taking hold of him in Gath. Favour me, O God, for man swallowed me up, All the day fighting he oppresseth me,

v2 Mine enemies have swallowed up all the day, For many [are] fighting against me, O most High,

v3 The day I am afraid I am confident toward Thee.

v4 In God I praise His word, in God I have trusted, I fear not what flesh doth to me.

v5 All the day they wrest my words, Concerning me all their thoughts [are] for evil,

v6 They assemble, they hide, they watch my heels, When they have expected my soul.

v7 By iniquity they escape, In anger the peoples put down, O God.

v8 My wandering Thou hast counted, Thou -- place Thou my tear in Thy bottle, Are they not in Thy book?

v9 Then turn back do mine enemies in the day I call. This I have known, that God [is] for me.

v10 In God I praise the word, In Jehovah I praise the word.

v11 In God I trusted, I fear not what man doth to me,

v12 On me, O God, [are] Thy vows, I repay thank-offerings to Thee.

v13 For Thou hast delivered my soul from death, Dost Thou not my feet from falling? To walk habitually before God in the light of the living!
