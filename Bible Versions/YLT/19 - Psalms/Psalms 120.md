# Psalms 120 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 119|← Psalms 119]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 121|Psalms 121 →]]

***

v1 A Song of the Ascents. Unto Jehovah in my distress I have called, And He answereth me.

v2 O Jehovah, deliver my soul from a lying lip, From a deceitful tongue!

v3 What doth He give to thee? And what doth He add to thee? O deceitful tongue!

v4 Sharp arrows of a mighty one, with broom-coals.

v5 Wo to me, for I have inhabited Mesech, I have dwelt with tents of Kedar.

v6 Too much hath my soul dwelt with him who is hating peace.

v7 I [am] peace, and when I speak they [are] for war!
