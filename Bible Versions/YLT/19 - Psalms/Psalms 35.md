# Psalms 35 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 34|← Psalms 34]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 36|Psalms 36 →]]

***

v1 By David. Strive, Jehovah, with my strivers, fight with my fighters,

v2 Take hold of shield and buckler, and rise for my help,

v3 And draw out spear and lance, To meet my pursuers. Say to my soul, 'Thy salvation I [am].'

v4 They are ashamed and blush, those seeking my soul, Turned backward and confounded, Those devising my evil.

v5 They are as chaff before wind, And a messenger of Jehovah driving away.

v6 Their way is darkness and slipperiness, And a messenger of Jehovah their pursuer.

v7 For without cause they hid for me their netpit, Without cause they digged for my soul.

v8 Meet him doth desolation -- he knoweth not, And his net that he hid catcheth him, For desolation he falleth into it.

v9 And my soul is joyful in Jehovah, It rejoiceth in His salvation.

v10 All my bones say, 'Jehovah, who is like Thee, Delivering the poor from the stronger than he, And the poor and needy from his plunderer.'

v11 Violent witnesses rise up, That which I have not known they ask me.

v12 They pay me evil for good, bereaving my soul,

v13 And I -- in their sickness my clothing [is] sackcloth, I have humbled with fastings my soul, And my prayer unto my bosom returneth.

v14 As [if] a friend, as [if] my brother, I walked habitually, As a mourner for a mother, Mourning I have bowed down.

v15 And -- in my halting they have rejoiced, And have been gathered together, Gathered against me were the smiters, And I have not known, They have rent, and they have not ceased;

v16 With profane ones, mockers in feasts, Gnashing against me their teeth.

v17 Lord, how long dost thou behold? Keep back my soul from their desolations, From young lions my only one.

v18 I thank Thee in a great assembly, Among a mighty people I praise Thee.

v19 Mine enemies rejoice not over me [with] falsehood, Those hating me without cause wink the eye.

v20 For they speak not peace, And against the quiet of the land, Deceitful words they devise,

v21 And they enlarge against me their mouth, They said, 'Aha, aha, our eye hath seen.'

v22 Thou hast seen, O Jehovah, Be not silent, O Lord -- be not far from me,

v23 Stir up, and wake to my judgment, My God, and my Lord, to my plea.

v24 Judge me according to Thy righteousness, O Jehovah my God, And they do not rejoice over me.

v25 They do not say in their heart, 'Aha, our desire.' They do not say, 'We swallowed him up.'

v26 They are ashamed and confounded together, Who are rejoicing at my evil. They put on shame and confusion, Who are magnifying themselves against me.

v27 They sing and rejoice, who are desiring my righteousness, And they say continually, 'Jehovah is magnified, Who is desiring the peace of His servant.'

v28 And my tongue uttereth Thy righteousness, All the day Thy praise!
