# Psalms 137 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 136|← Psalms 136]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 138|Psalms 138 →]]

***

v1 By rivers of Babylon -- There we did sit, Yea, we wept when we remembered Zion.

v2 On willows in its midst we hung our harps.

v3 For there our captors asked us the words of a song, And our spoilers -- joy: 'Sing ye to us of a song of Zion.'

v4 How do we sing the song of Jehovah, On the land of a stranger?

v5 If I forget thee, O Jerusalem, my right hand forgetteth!

v6 My tongue doth cleave to my palate, If I do not remember thee, If I do not exalt Jerusalem above my chief joy.

v7 Remember, Jehovah, for the sons of Edom, The day of Jerusalem, Those saying, 'Rase, rase to its foundation!'

v8 O daughter of Babylon, O destroyed one, O the happiness of him who repayeth to thee thy deed, That thou hast done to us.

v9 O the happiness of him who doth seize, And hath dashed thy sucklings on the rock!
