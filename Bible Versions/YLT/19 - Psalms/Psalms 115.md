# Psalms 115 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 114|← Psalms 114]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 116|Psalms 116 →]]

***

v1 Not to us, O Jehovah, not to us, But to Thy name give honour, For Thy kindness, for Thy truth.

v2 Why do the nations say, 'Where, pray, [is] their God.

v3 And our God [is] in the heavens, All that He hath pleased He hath done.

v4 Their idols [are] silver and gold, work of man's hands,

v5 A mouth they have, and they speak not, Eyes they have, and they see not,

v6 Ears they have, and they hear not, A nose they have, and they smell not,

v7 Their hands, but they handle not, Their feet, and they walk not;

v8 Nor do they mutter through their throat, Like them are their makers, Every one who is trusting in them.

v9 O Israel, trust in Jehovah, 'Their help and their shield [is] He.'

v10 O house of Aaron, trust in Jehovah, 'Their help and their shield [is] He.'

v11 Ye fearing Jehovah, trust in Jehovah, 'Their help and their shield [is] He.'

v12 Jehovah hath remembered us, He blesseth, He blesseth the house of Israel, He blesseth the house of Aaron,

v13 He blesseth those fearing Jehovah, The small with the great.

v14 Jehovah addeth to you, to you, and to your sons.

v15 Blessed [are] ye of Jehovah, maker of heaven and earth,

v16 The heavens -- the heavens [are] Jehovah's, And the earth He hath given to sons of men,

v17 The dead praise not Jah, Nor any going down to silence.

v18 And we, we bless Jah, From henceforth, and unto the age. Praise ye Jah!
