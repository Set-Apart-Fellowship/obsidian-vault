# Psalms 2 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 1|← Psalms 1]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 3|Psalms 3 →]]

***

v1 Why have nations tumultuously assembled? And do peoples meditate vanity?

v2 Station themselves do kings of the earth, And princes have been united together, Against Jehovah, and against His Messiah:

v3 'Let us draw off Their cords, And cast from us Their thick bands.'

v4 He who is sitting in the heavens doth laugh, The Lord doth mock at them.

v5 Then doth He speak unto them in His anger, And in His wrath He doth trouble them:

v6 'And I -- I have anointed My King, Upon Zion -- My holy hill.'

v7 I declare concerning a statute: Jehovah said unto me, 'My Son Thou [art], I to-day have brought thee forth.

v8 Ask of Me and I give nations -- thy inheritance, And thy possession -- the ends of earth.

v9 Thou dost rule them with a sceptre of iron, As a vessel of a potter Thou dost crush them.'

v10 And now, O kings, act wisely, Be instructed, O judges of earth,

v11 Serve ye Jehovah with fear, And rejoice with trembling.

v12 Kiss the Chosen One, lest He be angry, And ye lose the way, When His anger burneth but a little, O the happiness of all trusting in Him!
