# Psalms 105 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 104|← Psalms 104]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/19 - Psalms/Psalms 106|Psalms 106 →]]

***

v1 Give ye thanks to Jehovah -- call ye in His name, Make known among the peoples His acts.

v2 Sing ye to Him -- sing praise to Him, Meditate ye on all His wonders.

v3 Boast yourselves in His Holy Name, The heart of those seeking Jehovah rejoiceth.

v4 Seek ye Jehovah and His strength, Seek ye His face continually.

v5 Remember His wonders that He did, His signs and the judgments of His mouth.

v6 O seed of Abraham, His servant, O sons of Jacob, His chosen ones.

v7 He [is] Jehovah our God, In all the earth [are] His judgments.

v8 He hath remembered to the age His covenant, The word He commanded to a thousand generations,

v9 That He hath made with Abraham, And His oath to Isaac,

v10 And doth establish it to Jacob for a statute, To Israel -- a covenant age-during,

v11 Saying, 'To thee I give the land of Canaan, The portion of your inheritance,'

v12 In their being few in number, But a few, and sojourners in it.

v13 And they go up and down, from nation unto nation, From a kingdom unto another people.

v14 He hath not suffered any to oppress them And He reproveth for their sakes kings.

v15 'Strike not against Mine anointed, And to My prophets do not evil.'

v16 And He calleth a famine on the land, The whole staff of bread He hath broken.

v17 He hath sent before them a man, For a servant hath Joseph been sold.

v18 They have afflicted with fetters his feet, Iron hath entered his soul,

v19 Till the time of the coming of His word The saying of Jehovah hath tried him.

v20 The king hath sent, and looseth him, The ruler of the peoples, and draweth him out.

v21 He hath made him lord of his house, And ruler over all his possessions.

v22 To bind his chiefs at his pleasure, And his elders he maketh wise.

v23 And Israel cometh in to Egypt, And Jacob hath sojourned in the land of Ham.

v24 And He maketh His people very fruitful, And maketh it mightier than its adversaries.

v25 He turned their heart to hate His people, To conspire against His servants.

v26 He hath sent Moses His servant, Aaron whom He had fixed on.

v27 They have set among them the matters of His signs, And wonders in the land of Ham.

v28 He hath sent darkness, and it is dark, And they have not provoked His word.

v29 He hath turned their waters to blood, And putteth to death their fish.

v30 Teemed hath their land [with] frogs, In the inner chambers of their kings.

v31 He hath said, and the beetle cometh, Lice into all their border.

v32 He hath made their showers hail, A flaming fire [is] in their land.

v33 And He smiteth their vine and their fig, And shivereth the trees of their border.

v34 He hath said, and the locust cometh, And the cankerworm -- innumerable,

v35 And it consumeth every herb in their land, And it consumeth the fruit of their ground.

v36 And He smiteth every first-born in their land, The first-fruit of all their strength,

v37 And bringeth them out with silver and gold, And there is not in its tribes a feeble one.

v38 Rejoiced hath Egypt in their going forth, For their fear had fallen upon them.

v39 He hath spread a cloud for a covering, And fire to enlighten the night.

v40 They have asked, and He bringeth quails, And [with] bread of heaven satisfieth them.

v41 He hath opened a rock, and waters issue, They have gone on in dry places -- a river.

v42 For He hath remembered His holy word, With Abraham His servant,

v43 And He bringeth forth His people with joy, With singing His chosen ones.

v44 And He giveth to them the lands of nations, And the labour of peoples they possess,

v45 That they may observe His statutes, And His laws may keep. Praise ye Jehovah!
