# Lamentations 3 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 2|← Lamentations 2]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 4|Lamentations 4 →]]

***

v1 I [am] the man [who] hath seen affliction By the rod of His wrath.

v2 Me He hath led, and causeth to go [in] darkness, and without light.

v3 Surely against me He turneth back, He turneth His hand all the day.

v4 He hath worn out my flesh and my skin. He hath broken my bones.

v5 He hath built up against me, And setteth round poverty and weariness.

v6 In dark places He hath caused me to dwell, As the dead of old.

v7 He hath hedged me about, and I go not out, He hath made heavy my fetter.

v8 Also when I call and cry out, He hath shut out my prayer.

v9 He hath hedged my ways with hewn work, My paths He hath made crooked.

v10 A bear lying in wait He [is] to me, A lion in secret hiding-places.

v11 My ways He is turning aside, and He pulleth me in pieces, He hath made me a desolation.

v12 He hath trodden His bow, And setteth me up as a mark for an arrow.

v13 He hath caused to enter into my reins The sons of His quiver.

v14 I have been a derision to all my people, Their song all the day.

v15 He hath filled me with bitter things, He hath filled me [with] wormwood.

v16 And He breaketh with gravel my teeth, He hath covered me with ashes.

v17 And Thou castest off from peace my soul, I have forgotten prosperity.

v18 And I say, Perished hath my strength and my hope from Jehovah.

v19 Remember my affliction and my mourning, Wormwood and gall!

v20 Remember well, and bow down doth my soul in me.

v21 This I turn to my heart -- therefore I hope.

v22 The kindnesses of Jehovah! For we have not been consumed, For not ended have His mercies.

v23 New every morning, abundant [is] thy faithfulness.

v24 My portion [is] Jehovah, hath my soul said, Therefore I hope for Him.

v25 Good [is] Jehovah to those waiting for Him, To the soul [that] seeketh Him.

v26 Good! when one doth stay and stand still For the salvation of Jehovah.

v27 Good for a man that he beareth a yoke in his youth.

v28 He sitteth alone, and is silent, For He hath laid [it] upon him.

v29 He putteth in the dust his mouth, if so be there is hope.

v30 He giveth to his smiter the cheek, He is filled with reproach.

v31 For the Lord doth not cast off to the age.

v32 For though He afflicted, yet He hath pitied, According to the abundance of His kindness.

v33 For He hath not afflicted with His heart, Nor doth He grieve the sons of men.

v34 To bruise under one's feet any bound ones of earth,

v35 To turn aside the judgment of a man, Over-against the face of the Most High,

v36 To subvert a man in his cause, the Lord hath not approved.

v37 Who [is] this -- he hath said, and it is, [And] the Lord hath not commanded [it]?

v38 From the mouth of the Most High Go not forth the evils and the good.

v39 What -- sigh habitually doth a living man, A man for his sin?

v40 We search our ways, and investigate, And turn back unto Jehovah.

v41 We lift up our heart on the hands unto God in the heavens.

v42 We -- we have transgressed and rebelled, Thou -- Thou hast not forgiven.

v43 Thou hast covered Thyself with anger, And dost pursue us; Thou hast slain -- Thou hast not pitied.

v44 Thou hast covered Thyself with a cloud, So that prayer doth not pass through.

v45 Offscouring and refuse Thou dost make us In the midst of the peoples.

v46 Opened against us their mouth have all our enemies.

v47 Fear and a snare hath been for us, Desolation and destruction.

v48 Rivulets of water go down my eye, For the destruction of the daughter of my people.

v49 Mine eye is poured out, And doth not cease without intermission,

v50 Till Jehovah looketh and seeth from the heavens,

v51 My eye affecteth my soul, Because of all the daughters of my city.

v52 Hunted me sore as a bird have my enemies without cause.

v53 They have cut off in a pit my life, And they cast a stone against me.

v54 Flowed have waters over my head, I have said, I have been cut off.

v55 I called Thy name, O Jehovah, from the lower pit.

v56 My voice Thou hast heard, Hide not Thine ear at my breathing -- at my cry.

v57 Thou hast drawn near in the day I call Thee, Thou hast said, Fear not.

v58 Thou hast pleaded, O Lord, the pleadings of my soul, Thou hast redeemed my life.

v59 Thou hast seen, O Jehovah, my overthrow, Judge Thou my cause.

v60 Thou hast seen all their vengeance, All their thoughts of me.

v61 Thou hast heard their reproach, O Jehovah, All their thoughts against me,

v62 The lips of my withstanders, Even their meditation against me all the day.

v63 Their sitting down, and their rising up, Behold attentively, I [am] their song.

v64 Thou returnest to them the deed, O Jehovah, According to the work of their hands.

v65 Thou givest to them a covered heart, Thy curse to them.

v66 Thou pursuest in anger, and destroyest them, From under the heavens of Jehovah!
