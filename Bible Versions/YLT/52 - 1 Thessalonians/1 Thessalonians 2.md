# 1 Thessalonians 2 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/52 - 1 Thessalonians/1 Thessalonians 1|← 1 Thessalonians 1]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/52 - 1 Thessalonians/1 Thessalonians]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/52 - 1 Thessalonians/1 Thessalonians 3|1 Thessalonians 3 →]]

***

v1 For yourselves have known, brethren, our entrance in unto you, that it did not become vain,

v2 but having both suffered before, and having been injuriously treated (as ye have known) in Philippi, we were bold in our God to speak unto you the good news of God in much conflict,

v3 for our exhortation [is] not out of deceit, nor out of uncleanness, nor in guile,

v4 but as we have been approved by God to be entrusted with the good news, so we speak, not as pleasing men, but God, who is proving our hearts,

v5 for at no time did we come with speech of flattery, (as ye have known,) nor in a pretext for covetousness, (God [is] witness!)

v6 nor seeking of men glory, neither from you nor from others, being able to be burdensome, as Christ's apostles.

v7 But we became gentle in your midst, as a nurse may cherish her own children,

v8 so being desirous of you, we are well-pleased to impart to you not only the good news of God, but also our own souls, because beloved ye have become to us,

v9 for ye remember, brethren, our labour and travail, for, night and day working not to be a burden upon any of you, we did preach to you the good news of God;

v10 ye [are] witnesses -- God also -- how kindly and righteously, and blamelessly to you who believe we became,

v11 even as ye have known, how each one of you, as a father his own children, we are exhorting you, and comforting, and testifying,

v12 for your walking worthily of God, who is calling you to His own reign and glory.

v13 Because of this also, we -- we do give thanks to God continually, that, having received the word of hearing from us of God, ye accepted, not the word of man, but as it is truly, the word of God, who also doth work in you who believe;

v14 for ye became imitators, brethren, of the assemblies of God that are in Judea in Christ Jesus, because such things ye suffered, even ye, from your own countrymen, as also they from the Jews,

v15 who did both put to death the Lord Jesus and their own prophets, and did persecute us, and God they are not pleasing, and to all men [are] contrary,

v16 forbook_idding us to speak to the nations that they might be saved, to fill up their sins always, but the anger did come upon them -- to the end!

v17 And we, brethren, having been taken from you for the space of an hour -- in presence, not in heart -- did hasten the more abundantly to see your face in much desire,

v18 wherefore we wished to come unto you, (I indeed Paul,) both once and again, and the Adversary did hinder us;

v19 for what [is] our hope, or joy, or crown of rejoicing? are not even ye before our Lord Jesus Christ in his presence?

v20 for ye are our glory and joy.
