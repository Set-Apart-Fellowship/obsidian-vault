# Job 9 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/18 - Job/Job 8|← Job 8]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/18 - Job/Job 10|Job 10 →]]

***

v1 Then Job answered,

v2 "Truly I know that it is so,  But how can man be just with God?

v3 If he is pleased to contend with him,  He can't answer him one time in a thousand.

v4 God who is wise in heart, and mighty in strength:  Who has hardened himself against him, and prospered?

v5 Who removes the mountains, and they don't know it,  When he overturns them in his anger

v6 Who shakes the earth out of its place;  The pillars of it tremble;

v7 Who commands the sun, and it doesn't rise,  And seals up the stars;

v8 Who alone stretches out the heavens,  Treads on the waves of the sea;

v9 Who makes the Bear, Orion, and the Pleiades,  And the chambers of the south;

v10 Who does great things past finding out,  Yes, marvelous things without number.

v11 Behold, he goes by me, and I don't see him.  He passes on also, but I don't perceive him.

v12 Behold, he snatches away; who can hinder him?  Who will ask him, 'What are you doing?'

v13 "God will not withdraw his anger;  The helpers of Rahab stoop under him.

v14 How much less shall I answer him,  Choose my words to argue with him?

v15 Whom, though I were righteous, yet would I not answer.  I would make supplication to my judge.

v16 If I had called, and he had answered me,  Yet would I not believe that he listened to my voice.

v17 For he breaks me with a tempest,  Multiplies my wounds without cause.

v18 He will not allow me to take my breath,  But fills me with bitterness.

v19 If it is a matter of strength, behold, he is mighty!  If of justice, 'Who,' says he, 'will summon me?'

v20 Though I am righteous, my own mouth shall condemn me.  Though I am blameless, it shall prove me perverse.

v21 I am blameless. I don't regard myself.  I despise my life.

v22 "It is all the same. Therefore I say,  He destroys the blameless and the wicked.

v23 If the scourge kills suddenly,  He will mock at the trial of the innocent.

v24 The earth is given into the hand of the wicked.  He covers the faces of the judges of it.  If not he, then who is it?

v25 "Now my days are swifter than a runner.  They flee away, they see no good,

v26 They have passed away as the swift ships,  As the eagle that swoops on the prey.

v27 If I say, 'I will forget my complaint,  I will put off my sad face, and cheer up;'

v28 I am afraid of all my sorrows,  I know that you will not hold me innocent.

v29 I shall be condemned;  Why then do I labor in vain?

v30 If I wash myself with snow,  And cleanse my hands with lye,

v31 Yet you will plunge me in the ditch.  My own clothes shall abhor me.

v32 For he is not a man, as I am, that I should answer him,  That we should come together in judgment.

v33 There is no umpire between us,  That might lay his hand on us both.

v34 Let him take his rod away from me,  Let his terror not make me afraid:

v35 Then I would speak, and not fear him,  For I am not so in myself.
