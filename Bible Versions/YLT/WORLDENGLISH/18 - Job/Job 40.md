# Job 40 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/18 - Job/Job 39|← Job 39]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/18 - Job/Job 41|Job 41 →]]

***

v1 Moreover Yahweh answered Job,

v2 "Shall he who argues contend with the Almighty?  He who argues with God, let him answer it."

v3 Then Job answered Yahweh,

v4 "Behold, I am of small account. What shall I answer you?  I lay my hand on my mouth.

v5 I have spoken once, and I will not answer;  Yes, twice, but I will proceed no further."

v6 Then Yahweh answered Job out of the whirlwind,

v7 "Now brace yourself like a man.  I will question you, and you will answer me.

v8 Will you even annul my judgment?  Will you condemn me, that you may be justified?

v9 Or have you an arm like God?  Can you thunder with a voice like him?

v10 "Now deck yourself with excellency and dignity.  Array yourself with honor and majesty.

v11 Pour forth the fury of your anger.  Look on everyone who is proud, and bring him low.

v12 Look on everyone who is proud, and humble him.  Crush the wicked in their place.

v13 Hide them in the dust together.  Bind their faces in the hidden place.

v14 Then I will also admit to you  That your own right hand can save you.

v15 "See now, behemoth, which I made as well as you.  He eats grass as an ox.

v16 Look now, his strength is in his loins,  His force is in the muscles of his belly.

v17 He moves his tail like a cedar:  The sinews of his thighs are knit together.

v18 His bones are like tubes of brass.  His limbs are like bars of iron.

v19 He is the chief of the ways of God.  He who made him gives him his sword.

v20 Surely the mountains bring him forth food,  Where all the animals of the field do play.

v21 He lies under the lotus-trees,  In the covert of the reed, and the marsh.

v22 The lotuses cover him with their shade.  The willows of the brook surround him.

v23 Behold, if a river overflows, he doesn't tremble.  He is confident, though the Jordan swells even to his mouth.

v24 Shall any take him when he is on the watch,  Or pierce through his nose with a snare?
