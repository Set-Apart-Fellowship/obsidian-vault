# Job 26 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/18 - Job/Job 25|← Job 25]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/18 - Job/Job 27|Job 27 →]]

***

v1 Then Job answered,

v2 "How have you helped him who is without power!  How have you saved the arm that has no strength!

v3 How have you counseled him who has no wisdom,  And plentifully declared sound knowledge!

v4 To whom have you uttered words?  Whose spirit came forth from you?

v5 "Those who are deceased tremble,  Those beneath the waters and all that live in them.

v6 Sheol is naked before God,  And Abaddon has no covering.

v7 He stretches out the north over empty space,  And hangs the earth on nothing.

v8 He binds up the waters in his thick clouds,  And the cloud is not burst under them.

v9 He encloses the face of his throne,  And spreads his cloud on it.

v10 He has described a boundary on the surface of the waters,  And to the confines of light and darkness.

v11 The pillars of heaven tremble  And are astonished at his rebuke.

v12 He stirs up the sea with his power,  And by his understanding he strikes through Rahab.

v13 By his Spirit the heavens are garnished.  His hand has pierced the swift serpent.

v14 Behold, these are but the outskirts of his ways.  How small a whisper do we hear of him!  But the thunder of his power who can understand?"
