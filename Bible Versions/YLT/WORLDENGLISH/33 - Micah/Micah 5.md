# Micah 5 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/33 - Micah/Micah 4|← Micah 4]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/33 - Micah/Micah]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/33 - Micah/Micah 6|Micah 6 →]]

***

v1 Now shall you gather yourself in troops, daughter of troops: he    has laid siege against us; they shall strike the judge of Israel    with a rod on the cheek.

v2 But you, Beth-lehem Ephrathah,    which are little to be among the thousands of Judah, out of you    shall one come forth to me that is to be ruler in Israel; whose    goings forth are from of old, from everlasting.

v3 Therefore    will he give them up, until the time that she who travails has    brought forth: then the residue of his brothers shall return to    the children of Israel.

v4 He shall stand, and shall feed [his    flock] in the strength of Yahweh, in the majesty of the name of    Yahweh his God: and they shall abook_ide; for now shall he be great    to the ends of the earth.

v5 This [man] shall be [our] peace.    When the Assyrian shall come into our land, and when he shall    tread in our palaces, then shall we raise against him seven    shepherds, and eight principal men.

v6 They shall waste the    land of Assyria with the sword, and the land of Nimrod in the    entrances of it: and he shall deliver us from the Assyrian, when    he comes into our land, and when he treads within our border.

v7 The remnant of Jacob shall be in the midst of many peoples    as dew from Yahweh, as showers on the grass, that don't wait for    man, nor wait for the sons of men.

v8 The remnant of Jacob    shall be among the nations, in the midst of many peoples, as a    lion among the animals of the forest, as a young lion among the    flocks of sheep; who, if he go through, treads down and tears in    pieces, and there is none to deliver.

v9 Let your hand be    lifted up above your adversaries, and let all your enemies be cut    off.

v10 It shall happen in that day, says Yahweh, that I will    cut off your horses out of the midst of you, and will destroy    your chariots:

v11 and I will cut off the cities of your land,    and will throw down all your strongholds.

v12 I will cut off    witchcraft out of your hand; and you shall have no [more]    soothsayers:

v13 and I will cut off your engraved images and    your pillars out of the midst of you; and you shall no more    worship the work of your hands;

v14 and I will pluck up your    Asherim out of the midst of you; and I will destroy your cities.

v15 I will execute vengeance in anger and wrath on the nations    which didn't listen.
