# Genesis 7 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6|← Genesis 6]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/01 - Genesis/Genesis]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/01 - Genesis/Genesis 8|Genesis 8 →]]

***

v1 Yahweh said to Noah, "Come with all of your household into the ark, for I have seen your righteousness before me in this generation.

v2 You shall take seven pairs of every clean animal with you, the male and his female. Of the animals that are not clean, take two, the male and his female.

v3 Also of the birds of the sky, seven and seven, male and female, to keep seed alive on the surface of all the earth.

v4 In seven days, I will cause it to rain on the earth for forty days and forty nights. Every living thing that I have made, I will destroy from the surface of the ground."

v5 Noah did everything that Yahweh commanded him.

v6 Noah was six hundred years old when the flood of waters came on the earth.

v7 Noah went into the ark with his sons, his wife, and his sons' wives, because of the waters of the flood.

v8 Clean animals, animals that are not clean, birds, and everything that creeps on the ground

v9 went by pairs to Noah into the ark, male and female, as God commanded Noah.

v10 It happened after the seven days, that the waters of the flood came on the earth.

v11 In the six hundredth year of Noah's life, in the second month, on the seventeenth day of the month, on the same day all the fountains of the great deep were burst open, and the sky's windows were opened.

v12 The rain was on the earth forty days and forty nights.

v13 In the same day Noah, and Shem, Ham, and Japheth, the sons of Noah, and Noah's wife, and the three wives of his sons with them, entered into the ark;

v14 they, and every animal after its kind, all the cattle after their kind, every creeping thing that creeps on the earth after its kind, and every bird after its kind, every bird of every sort.

v15 They went to Noah into the ark, by pairs of all flesh with the breath of life in them.

v16 Those who went in, went in male and female of all flesh, as God commanded him; and Yahweh shut him in.

v17 The flood was forty days on the earth. The waters increased, and lifted up the ark, and it was lifted up above the earth.

v18 The waters prevailed, and increased greatly on the earth; and the ark floated on the surface of the waters.

v19 The waters prevailed exceedingly on the earth. All the high mountains that were under the whole sky were covered.

v20 The waters prevailed fifteen cubits upward, and the mountains were covered.

v21 All flesh died that moved on the earth, including birds, cattle, animals, every creeping thing that creeps on the earth, and every man.

v22 All in whose nostrils was the breath of the spirit of life, of all that was on the dry land, died.

v23 Every living thing was destroyed that was on the surface of the ground, including man, cattle, creeping things, and birds of the sky. They were destroyed from the earth. Only Noah was left, and those who were with him in the ark.

v24 The waters prevailed on the earth one hundred fifty days.
