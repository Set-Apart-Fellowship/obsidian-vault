# Psalms 84 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 83|← Psalms 83]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 85|Psalms 85 →]]

***

v1 How lovely are your dwellings,  Yahweh of Hosts!

v2 My soul longs, and even faints for the courts of Yahweh.  My heart and my flesh cry out for the living God.

v3 Yes, the sparrow has found a home,  And the swallow a nest for herself, where she may have her young,  Near your altars, Yahweh of Hosts,  My King, and my God.

v4 Blessed are those who dwell in your house.  They are always praising you. Selah.

v5 Blessed is the man whose strength is in you; Who have set their hearts on a pilgrimage.

v6 Passing through the valley of Weeping, they make it a place of    springs.  Yes, the autumn rain covers it with blessings.

v7 They go from strength to strength.  Everyone of them appears before God in Zion.

v8 Yahweh, God of hosts, hear my prayer.  Listen, God of Jacob. Selah.

v9 Behold, God our shield,  Look at the face of your anointed.

v10 For a day in your courts is better than a thousand.  I would rather be a doorkeeper in the house of my God,  Than to dwell in the tents of wickedness.

v11 For Yahweh God is a sun and a shield.  Yahweh will give grace and glory.  He withholds no good thing from those who walk blamelessly.

v12 Yahweh of Hosts,  Blessed is the man who trusts in you.   Psalm 85    For the Chief Musician. A Psalm by the sons of Korah.
