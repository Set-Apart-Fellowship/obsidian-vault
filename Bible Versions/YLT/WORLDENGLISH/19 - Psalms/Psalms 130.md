# Psalms 130 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 129|← Psalms 129]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 131|Psalms 131 →]]

***

v1 Out of the depths I have cried to you, Yahweh.

v2 Lord, hear my voice.  Let your ears be attentive to the voice of my petitions.

v3 If you, Yah, kept a record of sins,  Lord, who could stand?

v4 But there is forgiveness with you,  Therefore you are feared.

v5 I wait for Yahweh.  My soul waits.  I hope in his word.

v6 My soul longs for the Lord more than watchmen long for the    morning;  More than watchmen for the morning.

v7 Israel, hope in Yahweh,  For with Yahweh there is lovingkindness.  With him is abundant redemption.

v8 He will redeem Israel from all their sins.   Psalm 131 A Song of Ascents. By David.
