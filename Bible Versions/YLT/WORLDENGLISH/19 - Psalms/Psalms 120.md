# Psalms 120 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 119|← Psalms 119]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 121|Psalms 121 →]]

***

v1 In my distress, I cried to Yahweh.  He answered me.

v2 Deliver my soul, Yahweh, from lying lips,  From a deceitful tongue.

v3 What will be given to you, and what will be done more to you,  You deceitful tongue?

v4 Sharp arrows of the mighty,  With coals of juniper.

v5 Woe is me, that I live in Meshech,  That I dwell among the tents of Kedar!

v6 My soul has had her dwelling too long  With him who hates peace.

v7 I am for peace,  But when I speak, they are for war.   Psalm 121    A Song of Ascents.
