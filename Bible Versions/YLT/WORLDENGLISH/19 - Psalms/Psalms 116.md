# Psalms 116 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 115|← Psalms 115]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 117|Psalms 117 →]]

***

v1 I love Yahweh, because he listens to my voice,  And my cries for mercy.

v2 Because he has turned his ear to me,  Therefore I will call on him as long as I live.

v3 The cords of death surrounded me,  The pains of Sheol got a hold on me.  I found trouble and sorrow.

v4 Then called I on the name of Yahweh:  "Yahweh, I beg you, deliver my soul."

v5 Gracious is Yahweh, and righteous;  Yes, our God is merciful.

v6 Yahweh preserves the simple.  I was brought low, and he saved me.

v7 Return to your rest, my soul,  For Yahweh has dealt bountifully with you.

v8 For you have delivered my soul from death,  My eyes from tears,  And my feet from falling.

v9 I will walk before Yahweh in the land of the living.

v10 I believed, therefore I said,  "I was greatly afflicted."

v11 I said in my haste,  "All men are liars."

v12 What will I give to Yahweh for all his benefits toward me?

v13 I will take the cup of salvation, and call on the name of    Yahweh.

v14 I will pay my vows to Yahweh,  Yes, in the presence of all his people.

v15 Precious in the sight of Yahweh is the death of his saints.

v16 Yahweh, truly I am your servant.  I am your servant, the son of your handmaid.  You have freed me from my chains.

v17 I will offer to you the sacrifice of thanksgiving,  And will call on the name of Yahweh.

v18 I will pay my vows to Yahweh,  Yes, in the presence of all his people,

v19 In the courts of Yahweh's house,  In the midst of you, Jerusalem. Praise Yah!   Psalm 117
