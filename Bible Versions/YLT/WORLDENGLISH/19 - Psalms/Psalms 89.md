# Psalms 89 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 88|← Psalms 88]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 90|Psalms 90 →]]

***

v1 I will sing of the lovingkindness of Yahweh forever.  With my mouth, I will make known your faithfulness to all    generations.

v2 I indeed declare, "Love stands firm forever.  You established the heavens.  Your faithfulness is in them."

v3 "I have made a covenant with my chosen one,  I have sworn to David, my servant,

v4 'I will establish your seed forever,  And build up your throne to all generations.'" Selah.

v5 The heavens will praise your wonders, Yahweh;  Your faithfulness also in the assembly of the holy ones.

v6 For who in the skies can be compared to Yahweh?  Who among the sons of the heavenly beings is like Yahweh,

v7 A very awesome God in the council of the holy ones,  To be feared above all those who are around him?

v8 Yahweh, God of hosts, who is a mighty one, like you?  Yah, your faithfulness is around you.

v9 You rule the pride of the sea.  When its waves rise up, you calm them.

v10 You have broken Rahab in pieces, like one of the slain.  You have scattered your enemies with your mighty arm.

v11 The heavens are yours, the earth also is yours;  The world and its fullness. You have founded them.

v12 The north and the south, you have created them.  Tabor and Hermon rejoice in your name.

v13 You have a mighty arm.  Your hand is strong, and your right hand is exalted.

v14 Righteousness and justice are the foundation of your throne.  Lovingkindness and truth go before your face.

v15 Blessed are the people who learn to acclaim you.  They walk in the light of your presence, Yahweh.

v16 In your name do they rejoice all day.  In your righteousness, they are exalted.

v17 For you are the glory of their strength.  In your favor, our horn will be exalted.

v18 For our shield belongs to Yahweh;  Our king to the Holy One of Israel.

v19 Then you spoke in vision to your saints,  And said, "I have bestowed strength on the warrior.  I have exalted a young man from the people.

v20 I have found David, my servant.  I have anointed him with my holy oil,

v21 With whom my hand shall be established.  My arm will also strengthen him.

v22 No enemy will tax him.  No wicked man will oppress him.

v23 I will beat down his adversaries before him,  And strike those who hate him.

v24 But my faithfulness and my lovingkindness will be with him.  In my name, his horn will be exalted.

v25 I will set his hand also on the sea,  And his right hand on the rivers.

v26 He will call to me, 'You are my Father,  My God, and the rock of my salvation!'

v27 I will also appoint him my firstborn,  The highest of the kings of the earth.

v28 I will keep my lovingkindness for him forevermore.  My covenant will stand firm with him.

v29 I will also make his seed endure forever,  And his throne as the days of heaven.

v30 If his children forsake my law,  And don't walk in my ordinances;

v31 If they break my statutes,  And don't keep my commandments;

v32 Then I will punish their sin with the rod,  And their iniquity with stripes.

v33 But I will not completely take my lovingkindness from him,  Nor allow my faithfulness to fail.

v34 I will not break my covenant,  Nor alter what my lips have uttered.

v35 Once have I sworn by my holiness,  I will not lie to David.

v36 His seed will endure forever,  His throne like the sun before me.

v37 It will be established forever like the moon,  The faithful witness in the sky." Selah.

v38 But you have rejected and spurned.  You have been angry with your anointed.

v39 You have renounced the covenant of your servant.  You have defiled his crown in the dust.

v40 You have broken down all his hedges.  You have brought his strongholds to ruin.

v41 All who pass by the way rob him. He has become a reproach to his neighbors.

v42 You have exalted the right hand of his adversaries.  You have made all of his enemies rejoice.

v43 Yes, you turn back the edge of his sword,  And haven't supported him in battle.

v44 You have ended his splendor,  And thrown his throne down to the ground.

v45 You have shortened the days of his youth.  You have covered him with shame. Selah.

v46 How long, Yahweh?  Will you hide yourself forever?  Will your wrath burn like fire?

v47 Remember how short my time is!  For what vanity have you created all the children of men!

v48 What man is he who shall live and not see death,  Who shall deliver his soul from the power of Sheol? Selah.

v49 Lord, where are your former lovingkindnesses,  Which you swore to David in your faithfulness?

v50 Remember, Lord, the reproach of your servants,  How I bear in my heart the taunts of all the mighty peoples,

v51 With which your enemies have mocked, Yahweh,  With which they have mocked the footsteps of your anointed one.

v52 Blessed be Yahweh forevermore. Amen, and Amen.   BOOK IV   Psalm 90    A Prayer by Moses, the man of God.
