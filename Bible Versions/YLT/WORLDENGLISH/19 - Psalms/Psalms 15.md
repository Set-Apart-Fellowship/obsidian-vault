# Psalms 15 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 14|← Psalms 14]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 16|Psalms 16 →]]

***

v1 Yahweh, who shall dwell in your sanctuary?  Who shall live on your holy hill?

v2 He who walks blamelessly, does what is right,  And speaks truth in his heart;

v3 He who doesn't slander with his tongue,  Nor does evil to his friend,  Nor casts slurs against his fellow man;

v4 In whose eyes a vile man is despised,  But who honors those who fear Yahweh; He who keeps an oath even when it hurts, and doesn't change;

v5 He who doesn't lend out his money for usury,  Nor take a bribe against the innocent. He who does these things shall never be shaken.   Psalm 16 A Poem by David.
