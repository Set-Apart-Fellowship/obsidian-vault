# Psalms 148 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 147|← Psalms 147]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 149|Psalms 149 →]]

***

v1 Praise Yah!  Praise Yahweh from the heavens!  Praise him in the heights!

v2 Praise him, all his angels!  Praise him, all his host!

v3 Praise him, sun and moon!  Praise him, all you shining stars!

v4 Praise him, you heavens of heavens,  You waters that are above the heavens.

v5 Let them praise the name of Yahweh,  For he commanded, and they were created.

v6 He has also established them forever and ever.  He has made a decree which will not pass away.

v7 Praise Yahweh from the earth,  You great sea creatures, and all depths!

v8 Lightning and hail, snow and clouds;  Stormy wind, fulfilling his word;

v9 Mountains and all hills;  Fruit trees and all cedars;

v10 Wild animals and all cattle;  Small creatures and flying birds;

v11 Kings of the earth and all peoples;  Princes and all judges of the earth;

v12 Both young men and maidens;  Old men and children:

v13 Let them praise the name of Yahweh,  For his name alone is exalted.  His glory is above the earth and the heavens.

v14 He has lifted up the horn of his people,  The praise of all his saints;  Even of the children of Israel, a people near to him. Praise Yah!    Psalm 149
