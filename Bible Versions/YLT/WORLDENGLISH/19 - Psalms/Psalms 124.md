# Psalms 124 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 123|← Psalms 123]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 125|Psalms 125 →]]

***

v1 If it had not been Yahweh who was on our side,  Let Israel now say,

v2 If it had not been Yahweh who was on our side,  When men rose up against us;

v3 Then they would have swallowed us up alive,  When their wrath was kindled against us;

v4 Then the waters would have overwhelmed us,  The stream would have gone over our soul;

v5 Then the proud waters would have gone over our soul.

v6 Blessed be Yahweh,  Who has not given us as a prey to their teeth.

v7 Our soul has escaped like a bird out of the fowler's snare.  The snare is broken, and we have escaped.

v8 Our help is in the name of Yahweh,  Who made heaven and earth.   Psalm 125    A Song of Ascents.
