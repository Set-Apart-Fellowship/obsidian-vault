# Psalms 12 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 11|← Psalms 11]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 13|Psalms 13 →]]

***

v1 Help, Yahweh; for the godly man ceases.  For the faithful fail from among the children of men.

v2 Everyone lies to his neighbor.  With flattering lips, and with a double heart, do they speak.

v3 May Yahweh cut off all flattering lips,  And the tongue that boasts,

v4 Who have said, "With our tongue will we prevail.  Our lips are our own. Who is lord over us?"

v5 "Because of the oppression of the weak and because of the    groaning of the needy,  I will now arise," says Yahweh; "I will set him in safety from those who malign him."

v6 The words of Yahweh are flawless words,  As silver refined in a clay furnace, purified seven times.

v7 You will keep them, Yahweh,  You will preserve them from this generation forever.

v8 The wicked walk on every side,  When what is vile is exalted among the sons of men.   Psalm 13 For the Chief Musician. A Psalm by David.
