# Psalms 18 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 17|← Psalms 17]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 19|Psalms 19 →]]

***

v1 I love you, Yahweh, my strength.

v2 Yahweh is my rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer;  My God, my rock, in whom I will take refuge;  My shield, and the horn of my salvation, my high tower.

v3 I will call on Yahweh, who is worthy to be praised;  So shall I be saved from my enemies.

v4 The cords of death surrounded me.  The floods of ungodliness made me afraid.

v5 The cords of Sheol were round about me;  The snares of death came on me.

v6 In my distress I called on Yahweh,  And cried to my God. He heard my voice out of his temple,  My cry before him came into his ears.

v7 Then the earth shook and trembled.  The foundations also of the mountains quaked and were shaken,  Because he was angry.

v8 There went up a smoke out of his nostrils,  Fire out of his mouth devoured;  Coals were kindled by it.

v9 He bowed the heavens also, and came down.  Thick darkness was under his feet.

v10 He rode on a cherub, and flew.  Yes, he soared on the wings of the wind.

v11 He made darkness his hiding-place, his pavilion around him,  Darkness of waters, thick clouds of the skies.

v12 At the brightness before him his thick clouds passed,  Hailstones and coals of fire.

v13 Yahweh also thundered in the sky,  The Most High uttered his voice,  Hailstones and coals of fire.

v14 He sent out his arrows, and scattered them;  Yes, lightnings manifold, and routed them.

v15 Then the channels of waters appeared,  The foundations of the world were laid bare, At your rebuke, Yahweh,  At the blast of the breath of your nostrils.

v16 He sent from on high. He took me.  He drew me out of many waters.

v17 He delivered me from my strong enemy,  From those who hated me; for they were too mighty for me.

v18 They came on me in the day of my calamity,  But Yahweh was my support.

v19 He brought me forth also into a large place.  He delivered me, because he delighted in me.

v20 Yahweh has rewarded me according to my righteousness.  According to the cleanness of my hands has he recompensed me.

v21 For I have kept the ways of Yahweh,  And have not wickedly departed from my God.

v22 For all his ordinances were before me.  I didn't put away his statutes from me.

v23 I was also blameless with him.  I kept myself from my iniquity.

v24 Therefore has Yahweh recompensed me according to my    righteousness,  According to the cleanness of my hands in his eyesight.

v25 With the merciful you will show yourself merciful.  With the perfect man, you will show yourself perfect.

v26 With the pure, you will show yourself pure.  With the crooked you will show yourself shrewd.

v27 For you will save the afflicted people,  But the haughty eyes you will bring down.

v28 For you will light my lamp.  Yahweh, my God, will light up my darkness.

v29 For by you, I advance through a troop.  By my God, I leap over a wall.

v30 As for God, his way is perfect.  The word of Yahweh is tried.  He is a shield to all those who take refuge in him.

v31 For who is God, except Yahweh?  Who is a rock, besides our God,

v32 The God who arms me with strength, and makes my way perfect?

v33 He makes my feet like hinds' feet,  And sets me on my high places.

v34 He teaches my hands to war;  So that my arms bend a bow of bronze.

v35 You have also given me the shield of your salvation.  Your right hand sustains me.  Your gentleness has made me great.

v36 You have enlarged my steps under me,  My feet have not slipped.

v37 I will pursue my enemies, and overtake them.  Neither will I turn again until they are consumed.

v38 I will strike them through, so that they will not be able to    rise.  They shall fall under my feet.

v39 For you have girded me with strength to the battle.  You have subdued under me those who rose up against me.

v40 You have also made my enemies turn their backs to me,  That I might cut off those who hate me.

v41 They cried, but there was none to save;  Even to Yahweh, but he didn't answer them.

v42 Then I beat them small as the dust before the wind.  I cast them out as the mire of the streets.

v43 You have delivered me from the strivings of the people.  You have made me the head of the nations. A people whom I have not known shall serve me.

v44 As soon as they hear of me they shall obey me.  The foreigners shall submit themselves to me.

v45 The foreigners shall fade away,  And shall come trembling out of their close places.

v46 Yahweh lives; and blessed be my rock.  Exalted be the God of my salvation,

v47 Even the God who executes vengeance for me,  And subdues peoples under me.

v48 He rescues me from my enemies.  Yes, you lift me up above those who rise up against me.  You deliver me from the violent man.

v49 Therefore I will give thanks to you, Yahweh, among the nations,  And will sing praises to your name.

v50 He gives great deliverance to his king,  And shows lovingkindness to his anointed,  To David and to his seed, forevermore.   Psalm 19 For the Chief Musician. A Psalm by David.
