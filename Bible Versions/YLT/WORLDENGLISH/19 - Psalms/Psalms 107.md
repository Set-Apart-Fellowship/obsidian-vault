# Psalms 107 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 106|← Psalms 106]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 108|Psalms 108 →]]

***

v1 Give thanks to Yahweh,  For he is good, For his lovingkindness endures forever.

v2 Let the redeemed by Yahweh say so,  Whom he has redeemed from the hand of the adversary,

v3 Gathered out of the lands,  From the east and from the west,  From the north and from the south.

v4 They wandered in the wilderness in a desert way.  They found no city to live in.

v5 Hungry and thirsty,  Their soul fainted in them.

v6 Then they cried to Yahweh in their trouble,  And he delivered them out of their distresses,

v7 He led them also by a straight way,  That they might go to a city to live in.

v8 Let them praise Yahweh for his lovingkindness,  For his wonderful works to the children of men!

v9 For he satisfies the longing soul.  He fills the hungry soul with good.

v10 Some sat in darkness and in the shadow of death,  Being bound in affliction and iron,

v11 Because they rebelled against the words of God,  And condemned the counsel of the Most High.

v12 Therefore he brought down their heart with labor.  They fell down, and there was none to help.

v13 Then they cried to Yahweh in their trouble,  And he saved them out of their distresses.

v14 He brought them out of darkness and the shadow of death,  And broke their bonds in sunder.

v15 Let them praise Yahweh for his lovingkindness,  For his wonderful works to the children of men!

v16 For he has broken the gates of brass,  And cut through bars of iron.

v17 Fools are afflicted because of their disobedience,  And because of their iniquities.

v18 Their soul abhors all kinds of food.  They draw near to the gates of death.

v19 Then they cry to Yahweh in their trouble,  He saves them out of their distresses.

v20 He sends his word, and heals them,  And delivers them from their graves.

v21 Let them praise Yahweh for his lovingkindness,  For his wonderful works to the children of men!

v22 Let them offer the sacrifices of thanksgiving,  And declare his works with singing.

v23 Those who go down to the sea in ships,  Who do business in great waters;

v24 These see Yahweh's works,  And his wonders in the deep.

v25 For he commands, and raises the stormy wind,  Which lifts up its waves.

v26 They mount up to the sky; they go down again to the depths.  Their soul melts away because of trouble.

v27 They reel back and forth, and stagger like a drunken man,  And are at their wits' end.

v28 Then they cry to Yahweh in their trouble,  He brings them out of their distress.

v29 He makes the storm a calm,  So that its waves are still.

v30 Then are they glad because it is calm,  So he brings them to their desired haven.

v31 Let them praise Yahweh for his lovingkindness,  For his wonderful works to the children of men!

v32 Let them exalt him also in the assembly of the people,  And praise him in the seat of the elders.

v33 He turns rivers into a desert,  Water springs into a thirsty ground,

v34 And a fruitful land into a salt waste,  For the wickedness of those who dwell in it.

v35 He turns a desert into a pool of water,  And a dry land into water springs.

v36 There he makes the hungry to live,  That they may prepare a city to live in,

v37 Sow fields, plant vineyards,  And reap the fruits of increase.

v38 He blesses them also, so that they are multiplied greatly.  He doesn't allow their cattle to decrease.

v39 Again, they are diminished and bowed down  Through oppression, trouble, and sorrow.

v40 He pours contempt on princes,  And causes them to wander in a trackless waste.

v41 Yet he lifts the needy out of their affliction,  And increases their families like a flock.

v42 The upright will see it, and be glad.  All the wicked will shut their mouths.

v43 Whoever is wise will pay attention to these things.  They will consider the lovingkindnesses of Yahweh.   Psalm 108    A Song. A Psalm by David.
