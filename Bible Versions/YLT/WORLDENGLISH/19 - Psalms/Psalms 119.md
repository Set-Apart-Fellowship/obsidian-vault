# Psalms 119 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 118|← Psalms 118]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 120|Psalms 120 →]]

***

v1 Blessed are those whose ways are blameless,  Who walk according to the law of Yahweh.

v2 Blessed are those who keep his statutes,  Who seek him with their whole heart.

v3 Yes, they do nothing wrong.  They walk in his ways.

v4 You have commanded your precepts,  That we should fully obey them.

v5 Oh that my ways were steadfast  To obey your statutes!

v6 Then I wouldn't be put to shame,  When I consider all of your commandments.

v7 I will give thanks to you with uprightness of heart,  When I learn your righteous judgments.

v8 I will observe your statutes.  Don't utterly forsake me. BEIT

v9 How can a young man keep his way pure?  By living according to your word.

v10 With my whole heart, I have sought you.  Don't let me wander from your commandments.

v11 I have hidden your word in my heart,  That I might not sin against you.

v12 Blessed are you, Yahweh.  Teach me your statutes.

v13 With my lips,  I have declared all the ordinances of your mouth.

v14 I have rejoiced in the way of your testimonies,  As much as in all riches.

v15 I will meditate on your precepts,  And consider your ways.

v16 I will delight myself in your statutes.  I will not forget your word. GIMEL

v17 Do good to your servant.  I will live and I will obey your word.

v18 Open my eyes,  That I may see wondrous things out of your law.

v19 I am a stranger on the earth.  Don't hide your commandments from me.

v20 My soul is consumed with longing for your ordinances at all    times.

v21 You have rebuked the proud who are cursed,  Who wander from your commandments.

v22 Take reproach and contempt away from me,  For I have kept your statutes.

v23 Though princes sit and slander me,  Your servant will meditate on your statutes.

v24 Indeed your statutes are my delight,  And my counselors. DALED

v25 My soul is laid low in the dust.  Revive me according to your word!

v26 I declared my ways, and you answered me.  Teach me your statutes.

v27 Let me understand the teaching of your precepts!  Then I will meditate on your wondrous works.

v28 My soul is weary with sorrow.  Strengthen me according to your word.

v29 Keep me from the way of deceit.  Grant me your law graciously!

v30 I have chosen the way of truth.  I have set my heart on your law.

v31 I cling to your statutes, Yahweh.  Don't let me be put to shame.

v32 I run in the path of your commandments,  For you have set my heart free. HEY

v33 Teach me, Yahweh, the way of your statutes.  I will keep them to the end.

v34 Give me understanding, and I will keep your law.  Yes, I will obey it with my whole heart.

v35 Direct me in the path of your commandments,  For I delight in them.

v36 Turn my heart toward your statutes,  Not toward selfish gain.

v37 Turn my eyes away from looking at worthless things.  Revive me in your ways.

v38 Fulfill your promise to your servant,  That you may be feared.

v39 Take away my disgrace that I dread,  For your ordinances are good.

v40 Behold, I long for your precepts!  Revive me in your righteousness. WAW

v41 Let your lovingkindness also come to me, Yahweh,  Your salvation, according to your word.

v42 So I will have an answer for him who reproaches me,  For I trust in your word.

v43 Don't snatch the word of truth out of my mouth,  For I put my hope in your ordinances.

v44 So I will obey your law continually,  Forever and ever.

v45 I will walk in liberty,  For I have sought your precepts.

v46 I will also speak of your statutes before kings,  And will not be put to shame.

v47 I will delight myself in your commandments,  Because I love them.

v48 I reach out my hands for your commandments, which I love.  I will meditate on your statutes. ZAYIN

v49 Remember your word to your servant,  Because you gave me hope.

v50 This is my comfort in my affliction,  For your word has revived me.

v51 The arrogant mock me excessively,  But I don't swerve from your law.

v52 I remember your ordinances of old, Yahweh,  And have comforted myself.

v53 Indignation has taken hold on me,  Because of the wicked who forsake your law.

v54 Your statutes have been my songs,  In the house where I live.

v55 I have remembered your name, Yahweh, in the night,  And I obey your law.

v56 This is my way,  That I keep your precepts. CHET

v57 Yahweh is my portion.  I promised to obey your words.

v58 I sought your favor with my whole heart.  Be merciful to me according to your word.

v59 I considered my ways,  And turned my steps to your statutes.

v60 I will hurry, and not delay,  To obey your commandments.

v61 The ropes of the wicked bind me,  But I won't forget your law.

v62 At midnight I will rise to give thanks to you,  Because of your righteous ordinances.

v63 I am a friend of all those who fear you,  Of those who observe your precepts.

v64 The earth is full of your lovingkindness, Yahweh.  Teach me your statutes. TET

v65 Do good to your servant,  According to your word, Yahweh.

v66 Teach me good judgment and knowledge,  For I believe in your commandments.

v67 Before I was afflicted, I went astray;  But now I observe your word.

v68 You are good, and do good.  Teach me your statutes.

v69 The proud have smeared a lie upon me.  With my whole heart, I will keep your precepts.

v70 Their heart is as callous as the fat,  But I delight in your law.

v71 It is good for me that I have been afflicted,  That I may learn your statutes.

v72 The law of your mouth is better to me than thousands of pieces    of gold and silver. YUD

v73 Your hands have made me and formed me.  Give me understanding, that I may learn your commandments.

v74 Those who fear you will see me and be glad,  Because I have put my hope in your word.

v75 Yahweh, I know that your judgments are righteous,  That in faithfulness you have afflicted me.

v76 Please let your lovingkindness be for my comfort,  According to your word to your servant.

v77 Let your tender mercies come to me, that I may live;  For your law is my delight.

v78 Let the proud be put to shame, for they have overthrown me    wrongfully.  I will meditate on your precepts.

v79 Let those who fear you turn to me.  They will know your statutes.

v80 Let my heart be blameless toward your decrees,  That I may not be put to shame. KAF

v81 My soul faints for your salvation.  I hope in your word.

v82 My eyes fail for your word.  I say, "When will you comfort me?"

v83 For I have become like a wineskin in the smoke.  I don't forget your statutes.

v84 How many are the days of your servant?  When will you execute judgment on those who persecute me?

v85 The proud have dug pits for me,  Contrary to your law.

v86 All of your commandments are faithful.  They persecute me wrongfully. Help me!

v87 They had almost wiped me from the earth,  But I didn't forsake your precepts.

v88 Preserve my life according to your lovingkindness,  So I will obey the statutes of your mouth. LAMED

v89 Yahweh, your word is settled in heaven forever.

v90 Your faithfulness is to all generations.  You have established the earth, and it remains.

v91 Your laws remain to this day,  For all things serve you.

v92 Unless your law had been my delight,  I would have perished in my affliction.

v93 I will never forget your precepts,  For with them, you have revived me.

v94 I am yours. Save me,  For I have sought your precepts.

v95 The wicked have waited for me, to destroy me.  I will consider your statutes.

v96 I have seen a limit to all perfection,  But your commands are boundless. MEM

v97 How love I your law!  It is my meditation all day.

v98 Your commandments make me wiser than my enemies,  For your commandments are always with me.

v99 I have more understanding than all my teachers,  For your testimonies are my meditation.

v100 I understand more than the aged,  Because I have kept your precepts.

v101 I have kept my feet from every evil way,  That I might observe your word.

v102 I have not turned aside from your ordinances,  For you have taught me.

v103 How sweet are your promises to my taste,  More than honey to my mouth!

v104 Through your precepts, I get understanding;  Therefore I hate every false way. NUN

v105 Your word is a lamp to my feet,  And a light for my path.

v106 I have sworn, and have confirmed it,  That I will obey your righteous ordinances.

v107 I am afflicted very much.  Revive me, Yahweh, according to your word.

v108 Accept, I beg you, the willing offerings of my mouth.  Yahweh, teach me your ordinances.

v109 My soul is continually in my hand,  Yet I won't forget your law.

v110 The wicked have laid a snare for me,  Yet I haven't gone astray from your precepts.

v111 I have taken your testimonies as a heritage forever,  For they are the joy of my heart.

v112 I have set my heart to perform your statutes forever,  Even to the end. SAMEKH

v113 I hate double-minded men,  But I love your law.

v114 You are my hiding place and my shield.  I hope in your word.

v115 Depart from me, you evildoers,  That I may keep the commandments of my God.

v116 Uphold me according to your word, that I may live.  Let me not be ashamed of my hope.

v117 Hold me up, and I will be safe,  And will have respect for your statutes continually.

v118 You reject all those who stray from your statutes,  For their deceit is in vain.

v119 You put away all the wicked of the earth like dross.  Therefore I love your testimonies.

v120 My flesh trembles for fear of you.  I am afraid of your judgments. AYIN

v121 I have done what is just and righteous.  Don't leave me to my oppressors.

v122 Ensure your servant's well-being.  Don't let the proud oppress me.

v123 My eyes fail looking for your salvation,  For your righteous word.

v124 Deal with your servant according to your lovingkindness.  Teach me your statutes.

v125 I am your servant. Give me understanding,  That I may know your testimonies.

v126 It is time to act, Yahweh,  For they break your law.

v127 Therefore I love your commandments more than gold,  Yes, more than pure gold.

v128 Therefore I consider all of your precepts to be right.  I hate every false way. PEY

v129 Your testimonies are wonderful,  Therefore my soul keeps them.

v130 The entrance of your words gives light.  It gives understanding to the simple.

v131 I opened my mouth wide and panted,  For I longed for your commandments.

v132 Turn to me, and have mercy on me,  As you always do to those who love your name.

v133 Establish my footsteps in your word.  Don't let any iniquity have dominion over me.

v134 Redeem me from the oppression of man,  So I will observe your precepts.

v135 Make your face to shine on your servant.  Teach me your statutes.

v136 Streams of tears run down my eyes,  Because they don't observe your law. TZADI

v137 You are righteous, Yahweh.  your judgments are upright.

v138 You have commanded your statutes in righteousness.  They are fully trustworthy.

v139 My zeal wears me out,  Because my enemies ignore your words.

v140 Your promises have been thoroughly tested,  And your servant loves them.

v141 I am small and despised.  I don't forget your precepts.

v142 Your righteousness is an everlasting righteousness.  Your law is truth.

v143 Trouble and anguish have taken hold of me.  Your commandments are my delight.

v144 Your testimonies are righteous forever.  Give me understanding, that I may live. KUF

v145 I have called with my whole heart. Answer me, Yahweh!  I will keep your statutes.

v146 I have called to you. Save me!  I will obey your statutes.

v147 I rise before dawn and cry for help.  I put my hope in your words.

v148 My eyes stay open through the night watches,  That I might meditate on your word.

v149 Hear my voice according to your lovingkindness.  Revive me, Yahweh, according to your ordinances.

v150 They draw near who follow after wickedness.  They are far from your law.

v151 You are near, Yahweh.  All your commandments are truth.

v152 Of old I have known from your testimonies,  That you have founded them forever. RESH

v153 Consider my affliction, and deliver me,  For I don't forget your law.

v154 Plead my cause, and redeem me!  Revive me according to your promise.

v155 Salvation is far from the wicked,  For they don't seek your statutes.

v156 Great are your tender mercies, Yahweh.  Revive me according to your ordinances.

v157 Many are my persecutors and my adversaries.  I haven't swerved from your testimonies.

v158 I look at the faithless with loathing,  Because they don't observe your word.

v159 Consider how I love your precepts.  Revive me, Yahweh, according to your lovingkindness.

v160 All of your words are truth.  Every one of your righteous ordinances endures forever. SIN AND SHIN

v161 Princes have persecuted me without a cause,  But my heart stands in awe of your words.

v162 I rejoice at your word,  As one who finds great spoil.

v163 I hate and abhor falsehood.  I love your law.

v164 Seven times a day, I praise you,  Because of your righteous ordinances.

v165 Those who love your law have great peace.  Nothing causes them to stumble.

v166 I have hoped for your salvation, Yahweh.  I have done your commandments.

v167 My soul has observed your testimonies.  I love them exceedingly.

v168 I have obeyed your precepts and your testimonies,  For all my ways are before you. TAV

v169 Let my cry come before you, Yahweh.  Give me understanding according to your word.

v170 Let my supplication come before you.  Deliver me according to your word.

v171 Let my lips utter praise,  For you teach me your statutes.

v172 Let my tongue sing of your word,  For all your commandments are righteousness.

v173 Let your hand be ready to help me,  For I have chosen your precepts.

v174 I have longed for your salvation, Yahweh.  Your law is my delight.

v175 Let my soul live, that I may praise you.  Let your ordinances help me.

v176 I have gone astray like a lost sheep.  Seek your servant, for I don't forget your commandments.   Psalm 120    A Song of Ascents.
