# Psalms 38 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 37|← Psalms 37]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/19 - Psalms/Psalms 39|Psalms 39 →]]

***

v1 Yahweh, don't rebuke me in your wrath,  Neither chasten me in your hot displeasure.

v2 For your arrows have pierced me,  Your hand presses hard on me.

v3 There is no soundness in my flesh because of your indignation,  Neither is there any health in my bones because of my sin.

v4 For my iniquities have gone over my head.  As a heavy burden, they are too heavy for me.

v5 My wounds are loathsome and corrupt,  Because of my foolishness.

v6 I am pained and bowed down greatly.  I go mourning all the day long.

v7 For my loins are filled with burning.  There is no soundness in my flesh.

v8 I am faint and severely bruised.  I have groaned by reason of the anguish of my heart.

v9 Lord, all my desire is before you.  My groaning is not hidden from you.

v10 My heart throbs. My strength fails me.  As for the light of my eyes, it also is gone from me.

v11 My lovers and my friends stand aloof from my plague.  My kinsmen stand afar off.

v12 They also who seek after my life lay snares.  Those who seek my hurt speak mischievous things,  And meditate deceits all day long.

v13 But I, as a deaf man, don't hear.  I am as a mute man who doesn't open his mouth.

v14 Yes, I am as a man who doesn't hear,  In whose mouth are no reproofs.

v15 For in you, Yahweh, do I hope.  You will answer, Lord my God.

v16 For I said, "Don't let them gloat over me,  Or exalt themselves over me when my foot slips."

v17 For I am ready to fall.  My pain is continually before me.

v18 For I will declare my iniquity.  I will be sorry for my sin.

v19 But my enemies are vigorous and many.  Those who hate me without reason are numerous.

v20 They who also render evil for good are adversaries to me,  Because I follow what is good.

v21 Don't forsake me, Yahweh.  My God, don't be far from me.

v22 Hurry to help me,  Lord, my salvation.   Psalm 39 For the Chief Musician. For Jeduthun. A Psalm by David.
