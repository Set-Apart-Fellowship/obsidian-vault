# 2 Chronicles 8 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 7|← 2 Chronicles 7]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 9|2 Chronicles 9 →]]

***

v1 It happened at the end of twenty years, in which Solomon had built the house of Yahweh, and his own house,

v2 that the cities which Huram had given to Solomon, Solomon built them, and caused the children of Israel to dwell there.

v3 Solomon went to Hamath-zobah, and prevailed against it.

v4 He built Tadmor in the wilderness, and all the store-cities, which he built in Hamath.

v5 Also he built Beth-horon the upper, and Beth-horon the nether, fortified cities, with walls, gates, and bars;

v6 and Baalath, and all the store-cities that Solomon had, and all the cities for his chariots, and the cities for his horsemen, and all that Solomon desired to build for his pleasure in Jerusalem, and in Lebanon, and in all the land of his dominion.

v7 As for all the people who were left of the Hittites, and the Amorites, and the Perizzites, and the Hivites, and the Jebusites, who were not of Israel;

v8 of their children who were left after them in the land, whom the children of Israel didn't consume, of them did Solomon raise a levy [of bondservants] to this day.

v9 But of the children of Israel did Solomon make no servants for his work; but they were men of war, and chief of his captains, and rulers of his chariots and of his horsemen.

v10 These were the chief officers of king Solomon, even two hundred fifty, who ruled over the people.

v11 Solomon brought up the daughter of Pharaoh out of the city of David to the house that he had built for her; for he said, My wife shall not dwell in the house of David king of Israel, because the places are holy, whereunto the ark of Yahweh has come.

v12 Then Solomon offered burnt-offerings to Yahweh on the altar of Yahweh, which he had built before the porch,

v13 even as the duty of every day required, offering according to the commandment of Moses, on the Sabbaths, and on the new moons, and on the set feasts, three times in the year, [even] in the feast of unleavened bread, and in the feast of weeks, and in the feast of tents.

v14 He appointed, according to the ordinance of David his father, the courses of the priests to their service, and the Levites to their offices, to praise, and to minister before the priests, as the duty of every day required; the doorkeepers also by their courses at every gate: for so had David the man of God commanded.

v15 They didn't depart from the commandment of the king to the priests and Levites concerning any matter, or concerning the treasures.

v16 Now all the work of Solomon was prepared to the day of the foundation of the house of Yahweh, and until it was finished. [So] the house of Yahweh was completed.

v17 Then went Solomon to Ezion-geber, and to Eloth, on the seashore in the land of Edom.

v18 Huram sent him by the hands of his servants ships, and servants who had knowledge of the sea; and they came with the servants of Solomon to Ophir, and fetched from there four hundred fifty talents of gold, and brought them to king Solomon.
