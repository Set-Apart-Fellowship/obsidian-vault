# Leviticus 18 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 17|← Leviticus 17]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 19|Leviticus 19 →]]

***

v1 Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

v2 Speak to the children of Israel, and tell them, I am Yahweh your God.

v3 After the doings of the land of Egypt, in which you lived, you shall not do: and after the doings of the land of Canaan, where I bring you, you shall not do; neither shall you walk in their statutes.

v4 My ordinances shall you do, and my statutes shall you keep, to walk therein: I am Yahweh your God.

v5 You shall therefore keep my statutes, and my ordinances; which if a man do, he shall live in them: I am Yahweh.

v6 None of you shall approach to any who are close relatives to him, to uncover [their] nakedness: I am Yahweh.

v7 The nakedness of your father, even the nakedness of your mother, you shall not uncover: she is your mother; you shall not uncover her nakedness.

v8 The nakedness of your father's wife you shall not uncover: it is your father's nakedness.

v9 The nakedness of your sister, the daughter of your father, or the daughter of your mother, whether born at home, or born abroad, even their nakedness you shall not uncover.

v10 The nakedness of your son's daughter, or of your daughter's daughter, even their nakedness you shall not uncover: for theirs is your own nakedness.

v11 The nakedness of your father's wife's daughter, conceived by your father, she is your sister, you shall not uncover her nakedness.

v12 You shall not uncover the nakedness of your father's sister: she is your father's near kinswoman.

v13 You shall not uncover the nakedness of your mother's sister: for she is your mother's near kinswoman.

v14 You shall not uncover the nakedness of your father's brother, you shall not approach to his wife: she is your aunt.

v15 You shall not uncover the nakedness of your daughter-in-law: she is your son's wife; you shall not uncover her nakedness.

v16 You shall not uncover the nakedness of your brother's wife: it is your brother's nakedness.

v17 You shall not uncover the nakedness of a woman and her daughter; you shall not take her son's daughter, or her daughter's daughter, to uncover her nakedness; they are near kinswomen: it is wickedness.

v18 You shall not take a wife to her sister, to be a rival [to her], to uncover her nakedness, besides the other in her lifetime.

v19 You shall not approach to a woman to uncover her nakedness, as long as she is impure by her uncleanness.

v20 You shall not lie carnally with your neighbor's wife, to defile yourself with her.

v21 You shall not give any of your seed to make them pass through [the fire] to Molech; neither shall you profane the name of your God: I am Yahweh.

v22 You shall not lie with mankind, as with womankind: it is abomination.

v23 You shall not lie with any animal to defile yourself therewith; neither shall any woman stand before a animal, to lie down thereto: it is confusion.

v24 Don't defile yourselves in any of these things: for in all these the nations are defiled which I cast out from before you;

v25 The land is defiled: therefore I do visit the iniquity of it on it, and the land vomits out her inhabitants.

v26 You therefore shall keep my statutes and my ordinances, and shall not do any of these abominations; neither the home-born, nor the stranger who sojourns among you;

v27 (for all these abominations have the men of the land done, that were before you, and the land is defiled);

v28 that the land not vomit you out also, when you defile it, as it vomited out the nation that was before you.

v29 For whoever shall do any of these abominations, even the souls that do them shall be cut off from among their people.

v30 Therefore shall you keep my charge, that you not practice any of these abominable customs, which were practiced before you, and that you not defile yourselves therein: I am Yahweh your God.
