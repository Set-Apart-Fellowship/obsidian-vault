# Numbers 33 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/04 - Numbers/Numbers 32|← Numbers 32]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/04 - Numbers/Numbers]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/04 - Numbers/Numbers 34|Numbers 34 →]]

***

v1 These are the journeys of the children of Israel, when they went forth out of the land of Egypt by their hosts under the hand of Moses and Aaron.

v2 Moses wrote their goings out according to their journeys by the commandment of Yahweh: and these are their journeys according to their goings out.

v3 They journeyed from Rameses in the first month, on the fifteenth day of the first month; on the next day after the Passover the children of Israel went out with a high hand in the sight of all the Egyptians,

v4 while the Egyptians were burying all their firstborn, whom Yahweh had struck among them: on their gods also Yahweh executed judgments.

v5 The children of Israel journeyed from Rameses, and encamped in Succoth.

v6 They journeyed from Succoth, and encamped in Etham, which is in the edge of the wilderness.

v7 They journeyed from Etham, and turned back to Pihahiroth, which is before Baal-zephon: and they encamped before Migdol.

v8 They journeyed from before Hahiroth, and passed through the midst of the sea into the wilderness: and they went three days' journey in the wilderness of Etham, and encamped in Marah.

v9 They journeyed from Marah, and came to Elim: and in Elim were twelve springs of water, and seventy palm-trees; and they encamped there.

v10 They journeyed from Elim, and encamped by the Red Sea.

v11 They journeyed from the Red Sea, and encamped in the wilderness of Sin.

v12 They journeyed from the wilderness of Sin, and encamped in Dophkah.

v13 They journeyed from Dophkah, and encamped in Alush.

v14 They journeyed from Alush, and encamped in Rephidim, where was no water for the people to drink.

v15 They journeyed from Rephidim, and encamped in the wilderness of Sinai.

v16 They journeyed from the wilderness of Sinai, and encamped in Kibroth-hattaavah.

v17 They journeyed from Kibroth-hattaavah, and encamped in Hazeroth.

v18 They journeyed from Hazeroth, and encamped in Rithmah.

v19 They journeyed from Rithmah, and encamped in Rimmon-perez.

v20 They journeyed from Rimmon-perez, and encamped in Libnah.

v21 They journeyed from Libnah, and encamped in Rissah.

v22 They journeyed from Rissah, and encamped in Kehelathah.

v23 They journeyed from Kehelathah, and encamped in Mount Shepher.

v24 They journeyed from Mount Shepher, and encamped in Haradah.

v25 They journeyed from Haradah, and encamped in Makheloth.

v26 They journeyed from Makheloth, and encamped in Tahath.

v27 They journeyed from Tahath, and encamped in Terah.

v28 They journeyed from Terah, and encamped in Mithkah.

v29 They journeyed from Mithkah, and encamped in Hashmonah.

v30 They journeyed from Hashmonah, and encamped in Moseroth.

v31 They journeyed from Moseroth, and encamped in Bene-jaakan.

v32 They journeyed from Bene-jaakan, and encamped in Hor-haggidgad.

v33 They journeyed from Hor-haggidgad, and encamped in Jotbathah.

v34 They journeyed from Jotbathah, and encamped in Abronah.

v35 They journeyed from Abronah, and encamped in Ezion-geber.

v36 They journeyed from Ezion-geber, and encamped in the wilderness of Zin (the same is Kadesh).

v37 They journeyed from Kadesh, and encamped in Mount Hor, in the edge of the land of Edom.

v38 Aaron the priest went up into Mount Hor at the commandment of Yahweh, and died there, in the fortieth year after the children of Israel were come out of the land of Egypt, in the fifth month, on the first day of the month.

v39 Aaron was one hundred twenty-three years old when he died in Mount Hor.

v40 The Canaanite, the king of Arad, who lived in the South in the land of Canaan, heard of the coming of the children of Israel.

v41 They journeyed from Mount Hor, and encamped in Zalmonah.

v42 They journeyed from Zalmonah, and encamped in Punon.

v43 They journeyed from Punon, and encamped in Oboth.

v44 They journeyed from Oboth, and encamped in Iye-abarim, in the border of Moab.

v45 They journeyed from Iyim, and encamped in Dibon-gad.

v46 They journeyed from Dibon-gad, and encamped in Almon-diblathaim.

v47 They journeyed from Almon-diblathaim, and encamped in the mountains of Abarim, before Nebo.

v48 They journeyed from the mountains of Abarim, and encamped in the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho.

v49 They encamped by the Jordan, from Beth-jeshimoth even to Abel-shittim in the plains of Moab.

v50 Yahweh spoke to Moses in the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho, saying,

v51 Speak to the children of Israel, and tell them, When you pass over the Jordan into the land of Canaan,

v52 then you shall drive out all the inhabitants of the land from before you, and destroy all their figured [stones], and destroy all their molten images, and demolish all their high places:

v53 and you shall take possession of the land, and dwell therein; for to you have I given the land to possess it.

v54 You shall inherit the land by lot according to your families; to the more you shall give the more inheritance, and to the fewer you shall give the less inheritance: wherever the lot falls to any man, that shall be his; according to the tribes of your fathers shall you inherit.

v55 But if you will not drive out the inhabitants of the land from before you, then shall those who you let remain of them be as pricks in your eyes, and as thorns in your sides, and they shall vex you in the land in which you dwell.

v56 It shall happen that as I thought to do to them, so will I do to you.
