# 2 Samuel 22 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 21|← 2 Samuel 21]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 23|2 Samuel 23 →]]

***

v1 David spoke to Yahweh the words of this song in the day that Yahweh delivered him out of the hand of all his enemies, and out of the hand of Saul:

v2 and he said, Yahweh is my rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer, even mine;

v3 God, my rock, in him will I take refuge; My shield, and the horn of my salvation, my high tower, and my refuge; My savior, you save me from violence.

v4 I will call on Yahweh, who is worthy to be praised: So shall I be saved from my enemies.

v5 For the waves of death compassed me; The floods of ungodliness made me afraid:

v6 The cords of Sheol were round about me; The snares of death came on me.

v7 In my distress I called on Yahweh; Yes, I called to my God: He heard my voice out of his temple, My cry [came] into his ears.

v8 Then the earth shook and trembled, The foundations of heaven quaked Were shaken, because he was angry.

v9 There went up a smoke out of his nostrils, Fire out of his mouth devoured: Coals were kindled by it.

v10 He bowed the heavens also, and came down; Thick darkness was under his feet.

v11 He rode on a cherub, and did fly; Yes, he was seen on the wings of the wind.

v12 He made darkness pavilions round about him, Gathering of waters, thick clouds of the skies.

v13 At the brightness before him Coals of fire were kindled.

v14 Yahweh thundered from heaven, The Most High uttered his voice.

v15 He sent out arrows, and scattered them; Lightning, and confused them.

v16 Then the channels of the sea appeared, The foundations of the world were laid bare, By the rebuke of Yahweh, At the blast of the breath of his nostrils.

v17 He sent from on high, he took me; He drew me out of many waters;

v18 He delivered me from my strong enemy, From those who hated me; for they were too mighty for me.

v19 They came on me in the day of my calamity; But Yahweh was my stay.

v20 He brought me forth also into a large place; He delivered me, because he delighted in me.

v21 Yahweh rewarded me according to my righteousness; According to the cleanness of my hands has he recompensed me.

v22 For I have kept the ways of Yahweh, And have not wickedly departed from my God.

v23 For all his ordinances were before me; As for his statutes, I did not depart from them.

v24 I was also perfect toward him; I kept myself from my iniquity.

v25 Therefore has Yahweh recompensed me according to my    righteousness, According to my cleanness in his eyesight.

v26 With the merciful you will show yourself merciful; With the perfect man you will show yourself perfect;

v27 With the pure you will show yourself pure; With the crooked you will show yourself shrewd.

v28 The afflicted people you will save; But your eyes are on the haughty, that you may bring them down.

v29 For you are my lamp, Yahweh; Yahweh will lighten my darkness.

v30 For by you I run on a troop; By my God do I leap over a wall.

v31 As for God, his way is perfect: The word of Yahweh is tried; He is a shield to all those who take refuge in him.

v32 For who is God, save Yahweh? Who is a rock, save our God?

v33 God is my strong fortress; He guides the perfect in his way.

v34 He makes his feet like hinds' [feet], Sets me on my high places.

v35 He teaches my hands to war, So that my arms do bend a bow of brass.

v36 You have also given me the shield of your salvation; Your gentleness has made me great.

v37 You have enlarged my steps under me; My feet have not slipped.

v38 I have pursued my enemies, and destroyed them; Neither did I turn again until they were consumed.

v39 I have consumed them, and struck them through, so that they    can't arise: Yes, they are fallen under my feet.

v40 For you have girded me with strength to the battle; You have subdued under me those who rose up against me.

v41 You have also made my enemies turn their backs to me, That I might cut off those who hate me.

v42 They looked, but there was none to save; Even to Yahweh, but he didn't answer them.

v43 Then did I beat them small as the dust of the earth, I did crush them as the mire of the streets, and did spread them abroad.

v44 You also have delivered me from the strivings of my people; You have kept me to be the head of the nations: A people whom I have not known shall serve me.

v45 The foreigners shall submit themselves to me: As soon as they hear of me, they shall obey me.

v46 The foreigners shall fade away, Shall come trembling out of their close places.

v47 Yahweh lives; Blessed be my rock; Exalted be God, the rock of my salvation,

v48 Even the God who executes vengeance for me, Who brings down peoples under me,

v49 Who brings me forth from my enemies: Yes, you lift me up above those who rise up against me; You deliver me from the violent man.

v50 Therefore I will give thanks to you, Yahweh, among the nations, Will sing praises to your name.

v51 Great deliverance gives he to his king, Shows lovingkindness to his anointed, To David and to his seed, forevermore.
