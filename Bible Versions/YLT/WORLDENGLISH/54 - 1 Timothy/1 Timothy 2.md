# 1 Timothy 2 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 1|← 1 Timothy 1]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 3|1 Timothy 3 →]]

***

v1 I exhort therefore, first of all, that petitions, prayers, intercessions, and givings of thanks, be made for all men:

v2 for kings and all who are in high places; that we may lead a tranquil and quiet life in all godliness and reverence.

v3 For this is good and acceptable in the sight of God, our Savior;

v4 who desires to have all men to be saved, and come to full knowledge of the truth.

v5 For there is one God, and one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus,

v6 who gave himself as a ransom for all; the testimony in its own times;

v7 to which I was appointed a preacher and an apostle (I am telling the truth in Christ, not lying), a teacher of the Gentiles in faith and truth.

v8 I desire therefore that the men in every place pray, lifting up holy hands, without wrath and doubting.

v9 In the same way, that women also adorn themselves in decent clothing, with modesty and propriety; not just with braided hair, and gold or pearls or costly clothing;

v10 but (which becomes women professing godliness) through good works.

v11 Let a woman learn in quietness with all subjection.

v12 But I don't permit a woman to teach, nor to exercise authority over a man, but to be in quietness.

v13 For Adam was first formed, then Eve.

v14 Adam wasn't deceived, but the woman, being deceived, has fallen into disobedience;

v15 but she will be saved through her child-bearing, if they continue in faith, love, and sanctification with sobriety.
