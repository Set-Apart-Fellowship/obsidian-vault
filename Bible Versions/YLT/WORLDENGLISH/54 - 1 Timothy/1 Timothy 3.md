# 1 Timothy 3 (World English Bible)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 2|← 1 Timothy 2]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/WORLDENGLISH/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 4|1 Timothy 4 →]]

***

v1 This is a faithful saying: if a man seeks the office of an overseer, he desires a good work.

v2 The overseer therefore must be without reproach, the husband of one wife, temperate, sensible, modest, hospitable, good at teaching;

v3 not a drinker, not violent, not greedy for money, but gentle, not quarrelsome, not covetous;

v4 one who rules his own house well, having children in subjection with all reverence;

v5 (but if a man doesn't know how to rule his own house, how will he take care of the assembly of God?)

v6 not a novice, to avoid being puffed up and falling into the condemnation of the devil.

v7 Moreover he must have good testimony from those who are outside, to avoid falling into reproach and the snare of the devil.

v8 Deacons, in the same way, must be reverent, not double-tongued, not addicted to much wine, not greedy for money;

v9 holding the mystery of the faith in a pure conscience.

v10 Let them also first be tested; then let them serve as deacons, if they are blameless.

v11 Their wives in the same way must be reverent, not slanderers, temperate, faithful in all things.

v12 Let deacons be husbands of one wife, ruling their children and their own houses well.

v13 For those who have served well as deacons gain to themselves a good standing, and great boldness in the faith which is in Christ Jesus.

v14 These things I write to you, hoping to come to you shortly;

v15 but if I wait long, that you may know how men ought to behave themselves in the house of God, which is the assembly of the living God, the pillar and ground of the truth.

v16 Without controversy, the mystery of godliness is great: God was revealed in the flesh, Justified in the spirit, Seen by angels, Preached among the nations, Believed on in the world, And received up in glory.
