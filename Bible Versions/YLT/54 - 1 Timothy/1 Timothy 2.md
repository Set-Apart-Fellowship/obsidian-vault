# 1 Timothy 2 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 1|← 1 Timothy 1]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 3|1 Timothy 3 →]]

***

v1 I exhort, then, first of all, there be made supplications, prayers, intercessions, thanksgivings, for all men:

v2 for kings, and all who are in authority, that a quiet and peaceable life we may lead in all piety and gravity,

v3 for this [is] right and acceptable before God our Saviour,

v4 who doth will all men to be saved, and to come to the full knowledge of the truth;

v5 for one [is] God, one also [is] mediator of God and of men, the man Christ Jesus,

v6 who did give himself a ransom for all -- the testimony in its own times --

v7 in regard to which I was set a preacher and apostle -- truth I say in Christ, I do not lie -- a teacher of nations, in faith and truth.

v8 I wish, therefore, that men pray in every place, lifting up kind hands, apart from anger and reasoning;

v9 in like manner also the women, in becoming apparel, with modesty and sobriety to adorn themselves, not in braided hair, or gold, or pearls, or garments of great price,

v10 but -- which becometh women professing godly piety -- through good works.

v11 Let a woman in quietness learn in all subjection,

v12 and a woman I do not suffer to teach, nor to rule a husband, but to be in quietness,

v13 for Adam was first formed, then Eve,

v14 and Adam was not deceived, but the woman, having been deceived, into transgression came,

v15 and she shall be saved through the child-bearing, if they remain in faith, and love, and sanctification, with sobriety.
