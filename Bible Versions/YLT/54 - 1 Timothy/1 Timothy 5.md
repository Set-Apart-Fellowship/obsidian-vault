# 1 Timothy 5 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 4|← 1 Timothy 4]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 6|1 Timothy 6 →]]

***

v1 An aged person thou mayest not rebuke, but be entreating as a father; younger persons as brethren;

v2 aged women as mothers, younger ones as sisters -- in all purity;

v3 honour widows who are really widows;

v4 and if any widow have children or grandchildren, let them learn first to their own house to show piety, and to give back a recompense to the parents, for this is right and acceptable before God.

v5 And she who is really a widow and desolate, hath hoped upon God, and doth remain in the supplications and in the prayers night and day,

v6 and she who is given to luxury, living -- hath died;

v7 and these things charge, that they may be blameless;

v8 and if any one for his own -- and especially for those of the household -- doth not provide, the faith he hath denied, and than an unbeliever he is worse.

v9 A widow -- let her not be enrolled under sixty years of age, having been a wife of one husband,

v10 in good works being testified to: if she brought up children, if she entertained strangers, if saints' feet she washed, if those in tribulation she relieved, if every good work she followed after;

v11 and younger widows be refusing, for when they may revel against the Christ, they wish to marry,

v12 having judgment, because the first faith they did cast away,

v13 and at the same time also, they learn [to be] idle, going about the houses; and not only idle, but also tattlers and busybodies, speaking the things they ought not;

v14 I wish, therefore, younger ones to marry, to bear children, to be mistress of the house, to give no occasion to the opposer to reviling;

v15 for already certain did turn aside after the Adversary.

v16 If any believing man or believing woman have widows, let them relieve them, and let not the assembly be burdened, that those really widows it may relieve.

v17 The well-leading elders of double honour let them be counted worthy, especially those labouring in word and teaching,

v18 for the Writing saith, 'An ox treading out thou shalt not muzzle,' and 'Worthy [is] the workman of his reward.'

v19 Against an elder an accusation receive not, except upon two or three witnesses.

v20 Those sinning, reprove before all, that the others also may have fear;

v21 I testify fully, before God and the Lord Jesus Christ, and the choice messengers, that these things thou mayest keep, without forejudging, doing nothing by partiality.

v22 Be laying hands quickly on no one, nor be having fellowship with sins of others; be keeping thyself pure;

v23 no longer be drinking water, but a little wine be using, because of thy stomach and of thine often infirmities;

v24 of certain men the sins are manifest beforehand, leading before to judgment, and certain also they follow after;

v25 in like manner also the right works are manifest beforehand, and those that are otherwise are not able to be hid.
