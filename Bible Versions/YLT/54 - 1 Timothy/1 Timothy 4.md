# 1 Timothy 4 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 3|← 1 Timothy 3]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 5|1 Timothy 5 →]]

***

v1 And the Spirit expressly speaketh, that in latter times shall certain fall away from the faith, giving heed to seducing spirits and teachings of demons,

v2 in hypocrisy speaking lies, being seared in their own conscience,

v3 forbook_idding to marry -- to abstain from meats that God created to be received with thanksgiving by those believing and acknowledging the truth,

v4 because every creature of God [is] good, and nothing [is] to be rejected, with thanksgiving being received,

v5 for it is sanctified through the word of God and intercession.

v6 These things placing before the brethren, thou shalt be a good ministrant of Jesus Christ, being nourished by the words of the faith, and of the good teaching, which thou didst follow after,

v7 and the profane and old women's fables reject thou, and exercise thyself unto piety,

v8 for the bodily exercise is unto little profit, and the piety is to all things profitable, a promise having of the life that now is, and of that which is coming;

v9 stedfast [is] the word, and of all acceptation worthy;

v10 for for this we both labour and are reproached, because we hope on the living God, who is Saviour of all men -- especially of those believing.

v11 Charge these things, and teach;

v12 let no one despise thy youth, but a pattern become thou of those believing in word, in behaviour, in love, in spirit, in faith, in purity;

v13 till I come, give heed to the reading, to the exhortation, to the teaching;

v14 be not careless of the gift in thee, that was given thee through prophecy, with laying on of the hands of the eldership;

v15 of these things be careful; in these things be, that thy advancement may be manifest in all things;

v16 take heed to thyself, and to the teaching; remain in them, for this thing doing, both thyself thou shalt save, and those hearing thee.
