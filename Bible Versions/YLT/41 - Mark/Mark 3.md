# Mark 3 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/41 - Mark/Mark 2|← Mark 2]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/41 - Mark/Mark]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/41 - Mark/Mark 4|Mark 4 →]]

***

v1 And he entered again into the synagogue, and there was there a man having the hand withered,

v2 and they were watching him, whether on the sabbaths he will heal him, that they might accuse him.

v3 And he saith to the man having the hand withered, 'Rise up in the midst.'

v4 And he saith to them, 'Is it lawful on the sabbaths to do good, or to do evil? life to save, or to kill?' but they were silent.

v5 And having looked round upon them with anger, being grieved for the hardness of their heart, he saith to the man, 'Stretch forth thy hand;' and he stretched forth, and his hand was restored whole as the other;

v6 and the Pharisees having gone forth, immediately, with the Herodians, were taking counsel against him how they might destroy him.

v7 And Jesus withdrew with his disciples unto the sea, and a great multitude from Galilee followed him, and from Judea,

v8 and from Jerusalem, and from Idumea and beyond the Jordan; and they about Tyre and Sidon -- a great multitude -- having heard how great things he was doing, came unto him.

v9 And he said to his disciples that a little boat may wait on him, because of the multitude, that they may not press upon him,

v10 for he did heal many, so that they threw themselves on him, in order to touch him -- as many as had plagues;

v11 and the unclean spirits, when they were seeing him, were falling down before him, and were crying, saying -- 'Thou art the Son of God;'

v12 and many times he was charging them that they might not make him manifest.

v13 And he goeth up to the mountain, and doth call near whom he willed, and they went away to him;

v14 and he appointed twelve, that they may be with him, and that he may send them forth to preach,

v15 and to have power to heal the sicknesses, and to cast out the demons.

v16 And he put on Simon the name Peter;

v17 and James of Zebedee, and John the brother of James, and he put on them names -- Boanerges, that is, 'Sons of thunder;'

v18 and Andrew, and Philip, and Bartholomew, and Matthew, and Thomas, and James of Alpheus, and Thaddeus, and Simon the Cananite,

v19 and Judas Iscariot, who did also deliver him up; and they come into a house.

v20 And come together again doth a multitude, so that they are not able even to eat bread;

v21 and his friends having heard, went forth to lay hold on him, for they said that he was beside himself,

v22 and the scribes who [are] from Jerusalem having come down, said -- 'He hath Beelzeboul,' and -- 'By the ruler of the demons he doth cast out the demons.'

v23 And, having called them near, in similes he said to them, 'How is the Adversary able to cast out the Adversary?

v24 and if a kingdom against itself be divided, that kingdom cannot be made to stand;

v25 and if a house against itself be divided, that house cannot be made to stand;

v26 and if the Adversary did rise against himself, and hath been divided, he cannot be made to stand, but hath an end.

v27 'No one is able the vessels of the strong man -- having entered into his house -- to spoil, if first he may not bind the strong man, and then his house he will spoil.

v28 'Verily I say to you, that all the sins shall be forgiven to the sons of men, and evil speakings with which they might speak evil,

v29 but whoever may speak evil in regard to the Holy Spirit hath not forgiveness -- to the age, but is in danger of age-during judgment;'

v30 because they said, 'He hath an unclean spirit.'

v31 Then come do his brethren and mother, and standing without, they sent unto him, calling him,

v32 and a multitude was sitting about him, and they said to him, 'Lo, thy mother and thy brethren without do seek thee.'

v33 And he answered them, saying, 'Who is my mother, or my brethren?'

v34 And having looked round in a circle to those sitting about him, he saith, 'Lo, my mother and my brethren!

v35 for whoever may do the will of God, he is my brother, and my sister, and mother.'
