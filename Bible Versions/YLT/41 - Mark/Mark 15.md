# Mark 15 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/41 - Mark/Mark 14|← Mark 14]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/41 - Mark/Mark]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/41 - Mark/Mark 16|Mark 16 →]]

***

v1 And immediately, in the morning, the chief priests having made a consultation, with the elders, and scribes, and the whole sanhedrim, having bound Jesus, did lead away, and delivered [him] to Pilate;

v2 and Pilate questioned him, 'Art thou the king of the Jews?' and he answering said to him, 'Thou dost say [it].'

v3 And the chief priests were accusing him of many things, [but he answered nothing.]

v4 And Pilate again questioned him, saying, 'Thou dost not answer anything! lo, how many things they do testify against thee!'

v5 and Jesus did no more answer anything, so that Pilate wondered.

v6 And at every feast he was releasing to them one prisoner, whomsoever they were asking;

v7 and there was [one] named Barabbas, bound with those making insurrection with him, who had in the insurrection committed murder.

v8 And the multitude having cried out, began to ask for themselves as he was always doing to them,

v9 and Pilate answered them, saying, 'Will ye [that] I shall release to you the king of the Jews?'

v10 for he knew that because of envy the chief priests had delivered him up;

v11 and the chief priests did move the multitude, that he might rather release Barabbas to them.

v12 And Pilate answering, again said to them, 'What, then, will ye [that] I shall do to him whom ye call king of the Jews?'

v13 and they again cried out, 'Crucify him.'

v14 And Pilate said to them, 'Why -- what evil did he?' and they cried out the more vehemently, 'Crucify him;'

v15 and Pilate, wishing to content the multitude, released to them Barabbas, and delivered up Jesus -- having scourged [him] -- that he might be crucified.

v16 And the soldiers led him away into the hall, which is Praetorium, and call together the whole band,

v17 and clothe him with purple, and having plaited a crown of thorns, they put [it] on him,

v18 and began to salute him, 'Hail, King of the Jews.'

v19 And they were smiting him on the head with a reed, and were spitting on him, and having bent the knee, were bowing to him,

v20 and when they [had] mocked him, they took the purple from off him, and clothed him in his own garments, and they led him forth, that they may crucify him.

v21 And they impress a certain one passing by -- Simon, a Cyrenian, coming from the field, the father of Alexander and Rufus -- that he may bear his cross,

v22 and they bring him to the place Golgotha, which is, being interpreted, 'Place of a skull;'

v23 and they were giving him to drink wine mingled with myrrh, and he did not receive.

v24 And having crucified him, they were dividing his garments, casting a lot upon them, what each may take;

v25 and it was the third hour, and they crucified him;

v26 and the inscription of his accusation was written above -- 'The King of the Jews.'

v27 And with him they crucify two robbers, one on the right hand, and one on his left,

v28 and the Writing was fulfilled that is saying, 'And with lawless ones he was numbered.'

v29 And those passing by were speaking evil of him, shaking their heads, and saying, 'Ah, the thrower down of the sanctuary, and in three days the builder!

v30 save thyself, and come down from the cross!'

v31 And in like manner also the chief priests, mocking with one another, with the scribes, said, 'Others he saved; himself he is not able to save.

v32 The Christ! the king of Israel -- let him come down now from the cross, that we may see and believe;' and those crucified with him were reproaching him.

v33 And the sixth hour having come, darkness came over the whole land till the ninth hour,

v34 and at the ninth hour Jesus cried with a great voice, saying, 'Eloi, Eloi, lamma sabachthani?' which is, being interpreted, 'My God, my God, why didst Thou forsake me?'

v35 And certain of those standing by, having heard, said, 'Lo, Elijah he doth call;'

v36 and one having run, and having filled a spunge with vinegar, having put [it] also on a reed, was giving him to drink, saying, 'Let alone, let us see if Elijah doth come to take him down.'

v37 And Jesus having uttered a loud cry, yielded the spirit,

v38 and the veil of the sanctuary was rent in two, from top to bottom,

v39 and the centurion who was standing over-against him, having seen that, having so cried out, he yielded the spirit, said, 'Truly this man was Son of God.'

v40 And there were also women afar off beholding, among whom was also Mary the Magdalene, and Mary of James the less, and of Joses, and Salome,

v41 (who also, when he was in Galilee, were following him, and were ministering to him,) and many other women who came up with him to Jerusalem.

v42 And now evening having come, seeing it was the preparation, that is, the fore-sabbath,

v43 Joseph of Arimathea, an honourable counsellor, who also himself was waiting for the reign of God, came, boldly entered in unto Pilate, and asked the body of Jesus.

v44 And Pilate wondered if he were already dead, and having called near the centurion, did question him if he were long dead,

v45 and having known [it] from the centurion, he granted the body to Joseph.

v46 And he, having brought fine linen, and having taken him down, wrapped him in the linen, and laid him in a sepulchre that had been hewn out of a rock, and he rolled a stone unto the door of the sepulchre,

v47 and Mary the Magdalene, and Mary of Joses, were beholding where he is laid.
