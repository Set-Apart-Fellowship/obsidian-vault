# Mark 1 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/41 - Mark/Mark]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/41 - Mark/Mark 2|Mark 2 →]]

***

v1 A beginning of the good news of Jesus Christ, Son of God.

v2 As it hath been written in the prophets, 'Lo, I send My messenger before thy face, who shall prepare thy way before thee,' --

v3 'A voice of one calling in the wilderness, Prepare ye the way of the Lord, straight make ye his paths,' --

v4 John came baptizing in the wilderness, and proclaiming a baptism of reformation -- to remission of sins,

v5 and there were going forth to him all the region of Judea, and they of Jerusalem, and they were all baptized by him in the river Jordan, confessing their sins.

v6 And John was clothed with camel's hair, and a girdle of skin around his loins, and eating locusts and honey of the field,

v7 and he proclaimed, saying, 'He doth come -- who is mightier than I -- after me, of whom I am not worthy -- having stooped down -- to loose the latchet of his sandals;

v8 I indeed did baptize you with water, but he shall baptize you with the Holy Spirit.'

v9 And it came to pass in those days, Jesus came from Nazareth of Galilee, and was baptized by John at the Jordan;

v10 and immediately coming up from the water, he saw the heavens dividing, and the Spirit as a dove coming down upon him;

v11 and a voice came out of the heavens, 'Thou art My Son -- the Beloved, in whom I did delight.'

v12 And immediately doth the Spirit put him forth to the wilderness,

v13 and he was there in the wilderness forty days, being tempted by the Adversary, and he was with the beasts, and the messengers were ministering to him.

v14 And after the delivering up of John, Jesus came to Galilee, proclaiming the good news of the reign of God,

v15 and saying -- 'Fulfilled hath been the time, and the reign of God hath come nigh, reform ye, and believe in the good news.'

v16 And, walking by the sea of Galilee, he saw Simon, and Andrew his brother, casting a drag into the sea, for they were fishers,

v17 and Jesus said to them, 'Come ye after me, and I shall make you to become fishers of men;'

v18 and immediately, having left their nets, they followed him.

v19 And having gone on thence a little, he saw James of Zebedee, and John his brother, and they were in the boat refitting the nets,

v20 and immediately he called them, and, having left their father Zebedee in the boat with the hired servants, they went away after him.

v21 And they go on to Capernaum, and immediately, on the sabbaths, having gone into the synagogue, he was teaching,

v22 and they were astonished at his teaching, for he was teaching them as having authority, and not as the scribes.

v23 And there was in their synagogue a man with an unclean spirit, and he cried out,

v24 saying, 'Away! what -- to us and to thee, Jesus the Nazarene? thou didst come to destroy us; I have known thee who thou art -- the Holy One of God.'

v25 And Jesus rebuked him, saying, 'Be silenced, and come forth out of him,'

v26 and the unclean spirit having torn him, and having cried with a great voice, came forth out of him,

v27 and they were all amazed, so as to reason among themselves, saying, 'What is this? what new teaching [is] this? that with authority also the unclean spirits he commandeth, and they obey him!'

v28 And the fame of him went forth immediately to all the region, round about, of Galilee.

v29 And immediately, having come forth out of the synagogue, they went to the house of Simon and Andrew, with James and John,

v30 and the mother-in-law of Simon was lying fevered, and immediately they tell him about her,

v31 and having come near, he raised her up, having laid hold of her hand, and the fever left her immediately, and she was ministering to them.

v32 And evening having come, when the sun did set, they brought unto him all who were ill, and who were demoniacs,

v33 and the whole city was gathered together near the door,

v34 and he healed many who were ill of manifold diseases, and many demons he cast forth, and was not suffering the demons to speak, because they knew him.

v35 And very early, it being yet night, having risen, he went forth, and went away to a desert place, and was there praying;

v36 and Simon and those with him went in quest of him,

v37 and having found him, they say to him, -- 'All do seek thee;'

v38 and he saith to them, 'We may go to the next towns, that there also I may preach, for for this I came forth.'

v39 And he was preaching in their synagogues, in all Galilee, and is casting out the demons,

v40 and there doth come to him a leper, calling on him, and kneeling to him, and saying to him -- 'If thou mayest will, thou art able to cleanse me.'

v41 And Jesus having been moved with compassion, having stretched forth the hand, touched him, and saith to him, 'I will; be thou cleansed;'

v42 and he having spoken, immediately the leprosy went away from him, and he was cleansed.

v43 And having sternly charged him, immediately he put him forth,

v44 and saith to him, 'See thou mayest say nothing to any one, but go away, thyself shew to the priest, and bring near for thy cleansing the things Moses directed, for a testimony to them.'

v45 And he, having gone forth, began to proclaim much, and to spread abroad the thing, so that no more he was able openly to enter into the city, but he was without in desert places, and they were coming unto him from every quarter.
