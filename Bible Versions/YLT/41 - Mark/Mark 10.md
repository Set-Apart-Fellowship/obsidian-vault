# Mark 10 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/41 - Mark/Mark 9|← Mark 9]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/41 - Mark/Mark]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/41 - Mark/Mark 11|Mark 11 →]]

***

v1 And having risen thence, he doth come to the coasts of Judea, through the other side of the Jordan, and again do multitudes come together unto him, and, as he had been accustomed, again he was teaching them.

v2 And the Pharisees, having come near, questioned him, if it is lawful for a husband to put away a wife, tempting him,

v3 and he answering said to them, 'What did Moses command you?'

v4 and they said, 'Moses suffered to write a bill of divorce, and to put away.'

v5 And Jesus answering said to them, 'For the stiffness of your heart he wrote you this command,

v6 but from the beginning of the creation, a male and a female God did make them;

v7 on this account shall a man leave his father and mother, and shall cleave unto his wife,

v8 and they shall be -- the two -- for one flesh; so that they are no more two, but one flesh;

v9 what therefore God did join together, let not man put asunder.'

v10 And in the house again his disciples of the same thing questioned him,

v11 and he saith to them, 'Whoever may put away his wife, and may marry another, doth commit adultery against her;

v12 and if a woman may put away her husband, and is married to another, she committeth adultery.'

v13 And they were bringing to him children, that he might touch them, and the disciples were rebuking those bringing them,

v14 and Jesus having seen, was much displeased, and he said to them, 'Suffer the children to come unto me, and forbook_id them not, for of such is the reign of God;

v15 verily I say to you, whoever may not receive the reign of God, as a child -- he may not enter into it;'

v16 and having taken them in his arms, having put [his] hands upon them, he was blessing them.

v17 And as he is going forth into the way, one having run and having kneeled to him, was questioning him, 'Good teacher, what may I do, that life age-during I may inherit?'

v18 And Jesus said to him, 'Why me dost thou call good? no one [is] good except One -- God;

v19 the commands thou hast known: Thou mayest not commit adultery, Thou mayest do no murder, Thou mayest not steal, Thou mayest not bear false witness, Thou mayest not defraud, Honour thy father and mother.'

v20 And he answering said to him, 'Teacher, all these did I keep from my youth.'

v21 And Jesus having looked upon him, did love him, and said to him, 'One thing thou dost lack; go away, whatever thou hast -- sell, and give to the poor, and thou shalt have treasure in heaven, and come, be following me, having taken up the cross.'

v22 And he -- gloomy at the word -- went away sorrowing, for he was having many possessions.

v23 And Jesus having looked round, saith to his disciples, 'How hardly shall they who have riches enter into the reign of God!'

v24 And the disciples were astonished at his words, and Jesus again answering saith to them, 'Children, how hard is it to those trusting on the riches to enter into the reign of God!

v25 It is easier for a camel through the eye of the needle to enter, than for a rich man to enter into the reign of God.'

v26 And they were astonished beyond measure, saying unto themselves, 'And who is able to be saved?'

v27 And Jesus, having looked upon them, saith, 'With men it is impossible, but not with God; for all things are possible with God.'

v28 And Peter began to say to him, 'Lo, we left all, and we followed thee.'

v29 And Jesus answering said, 'Verily I say to you, there is no one who left house, or brothers, or sisters, or father, or mother, or wife, or children, or fields, for my sake, and for the good news',

v30 who may not receive an hundredfold now in this time, houses, and brothers, and sisters, and mothers, and children, and fields, with persecutions, and in the age that is coming, life age-during;

v31 and many first shall be last, and the last first.'

v32 And they were in the way going up to Jerusalem, and Jesus was going before them, and they were amazed, and following they were afraid. And having again taken the twelve, he began to tell them the things about to happen to him,

v33 -- 'Lo, we go up to Jerusalem, and the Son of Man shall be delivered to the chief priests, and to the scribes, and they shall condemn him to death, and shall deliver him to the nations,

v34 and they shall mock him, and scourge him, and spit on him, and kill him, and the third day he shall rise again.'

v35 And there come near to him James and John, the sons of Zebedee, saying, 'Teacher, we wish that whatever we may ask for ourselves, thou mayest do for us;'

v36 and he said to them, 'What do ye wish me to do for you?'

v37 and they said to him, 'Grant to us that, one on thy right hand and one on thy left, we may sit in thy glory;'

v38 and Jesus said to them, 'Ye have not known what ye ask; are ye able to drink of the cup that I drink of, and with the baptism that I am baptized with -- to be baptized?'

v39 And they said to him, 'We are able;' and Jesus said to them, 'Of the cup indeed that I drink of, ye shall drink, and with the baptism that I am baptized with, ye shall be baptized;

v40 but to sit on my right and on my left, is not mine to give, but -- to those for whom it hath been prepared.'

v41 And the ten having heard, began to be much displeased at James and John,

v42 but Jesus having called them near, saith to them, 'Ye have known that they who are considered to rule the nations do exercise lordship over them, and their great ones do exercise authority upon them;

v43 but not so shall it be among you; but whoever may will to become great among you, he shall be your minister,

v44 and whoever of you may will to become first, he shall be servant of all;

v45 for even the Son of Man came not to be ministered to, but to minister, and to give his life a ransom for many.'

v46 And they come to Jericho, and as he is going forth from Jericho, with his disciples and a great multitude, a son of Timaeus -- Bartimaeus the blind -- was sitting beside the way begging,

v47 and having heard that it is Jesus the Nazarene, he began to cry out, and to say, 'The Son of David -- Jesus! deal kindly with me;'

v48 and many were rebuking him, that he might keep silent, but the more abundantly he cried out, 'Son of David, deal kindly with me.'

v49 And Jesus having stood, he commanded him to be called, and they call the blind man, saying to him, 'Take courage, rise, he doth call thee;'

v50 and he, having cast away his garment, having risen, did come unto Jesus.

v51 And answering, Jesus saith to him, 'What wilt thou I may do to thee?' and the blind man said to him, 'Rabboni, that I may see again;'

v52 and Jesus said to him, 'Go, thy faith hath saved thee:' and immediately he saw again, and was following Jesus in the way.
