# Mark 9 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/41 - Mark/Mark 8|← Mark 8]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/41 - Mark/Mark]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/41 - Mark/Mark 10|Mark 10 →]]

***

v1 And he said to them, 'Verily I say to you, That there are certain of those standing here, who may not taste of death till they see the reign of God having come in power.'

v2 And after six days doth Jesus take Peter, and James, and John, and bringeth them up to a high mount by themselves, alone, and he was transfigured before them,

v3 and his garments became glittering, white exceedingly, as snow, so as a fuller upon the earth is not able to whiten [them].

v4 And there appeared to them Elijah with Moses, and they were talking with Jesus.

v5 And Peter answering saith to Jesus, 'Rabbi, it is good to us to be here; and we may make three booths, for thee one, and for Moses one, and for Elijah one:'

v6 for he was not knowing what he might say, for they were greatly afraid.

v7 And there came a cloud overshadowing them, and there came a voice out of the cloud, saying, 'This is My Son -- the Beloved, hear ye him;'

v8 and suddenly, having looked around, they saw no one any more, but Jesus only with themselves.

v9 And as they are coming down from the mount, he charged them that they may declare to no one the things that they saw, except when the Son of Man may rise out of the dead;

v10 and the thing they kept to themselves, questioning together what the rising out of the dead is.

v11 And they were questioning him, saying, that the scribes say that Elijah it behoveth to come first.

v12 And he answering said to them, 'Elijah indeed, having come first, doth restore all things; and how hath it been written concerning the Son of Man, that many things he may suffer, and be set at nought?

v13 But I say to you, That also Elijah hath come, and they did to him what they willed, as it hath been written of him.'

v14 And having come unto the disciples, he saw a great multitude about them, and scribes questioning with them,

v15 and immediately, all the multitude having seen him, were amazed, and running near, were saluting him.

v16 And he questioned the scribes, 'What dispute ye with them?'

v17 and one out of the multitude answering said, 'Teacher, I brought my son unto thee, having a dumb spirit;

v18 and wherever it doth seize him, it doth tear him, and he foameth, and gnasheth his teeth, and pineth away; and I spake to thy disciples that they may cast it out, and they were not able.'

v19 And he answering him, said, 'O generation unbelieving, till when shall I be with you? till when shall I suffer you? bring him unto me;'

v20 and they brought him unto him, and he having seen him, immediately the spirit tare him, and he, having fallen upon the earth, was wallowing -- foaming.

v21 And he questioned his father, 'How long time is it since this came to him?' and he said, 'From childhood,

v22 and many times also it cast him into fire, and into water, that it might destroy him; but if thou art able to do anything, help us, having compassion on us.'

v23 And Jesus said to him, 'If thou art able to believe! all things are possible to the one that is believing;'

v24 and immediately the father of the child, having cried out, with tears said, 'I believe, sir; be helping mine unbelief.'

v25 Jesus having seen that a multitude doth run together, rebuked the unclean spirit, saying to it, 'Spirit -- dumb and deaf -- I charge thee, come forth out of him, and no more thou mayest enter into him;'

v26 and having cried, and rent him much, it came forth, and he became as dead, so that many said that he was dead,

v27 but Jesus, having taken him by the hand, lifted him up, and he arose.

v28 And he having come into the house, his disciples were questioning him by himself -- 'Why were we not able to cast it forth?'

v29 And he said to them, 'This kind is able to come forth with nothing except with prayer and fasting.'

v30 And having gone forth thence, they were passing through Galilee, and he did not wish that any may know,

v31 for he was teaching his disciples, and he said to them, 'The Son of Man is being delivered to the hands of men, and they shall kill him, and having been killed the third day he shall rise,'

v32 but they were not understanding the saying, and they were afraid to question him.

v33 And he came to Capernaum, and being in the house, he was questioning them, 'What were ye reasoning in the way among yourselves?'

v34 and they were silent, for with one another they did reason in the way who is greater;

v35 and having sat down he called the twelve, and he saith to them, 'If any doth will to be first, he shall be last of all, and minister of all.'

v36 And having taken a child, he set him in the midst of them, and having taken him in his arms, said to them,

v37 'Whoever may receive one of such children in my name, doth receive me, and whoever may receive me, doth not receive me, but Him who sent me.'

v38 And John did answer him, saying, 'Teacher, we saw a certain one in thy name casting out demons, who doth not follow us, and we forbade him, because he doth not follow us.'

v39 And Jesus said, 'Forbook_id him not, for there is no one who shall do a mighty work in my name, and shall be able readily to speak evil of me:

v40 for he who is not against us is for us;

v41 for whoever may give you to drink a cup of water in my name, because ye are Christ's, verily I say to you, he may not lose his reward;

v42 and whoever may cause to stumble one of the little ones believing in me, better is it for him if a millstone is hanged about his neck, and he hath been cast into the sea.

v43 'And if thy hand may cause thee to stumble, cut it off; it is better for thee maimed to enter into the life, than having the two hands, to go away to the gehenna, to the fire -- the unquenchable --

v44 where there worm is not dying, and the fire is not being quenched.

v45 'And if thy foot may cause thee to stumble, cut it off; it is better for thee to enter into the life lame, than having the two feet to be cast to the gehenna, to the fire -- the unquenchable --

v46 where there worm is not dying, and the fire is not being quenched.

v47 And if thine eye may cause thee to stumble, cast it out; it is better for thee one-eyed to enter into the reign of God, than having two eyes, to be cast to the gehenna of the fire --

v48 where their worm is not dying, and the fire is not being quenched;

v49 for every one with fire shall be salted, and every sacrifice with salt shall be salted.

v50 The salt [is] good, but if the salt may become saltless, in what will ye season [it]? Have in yourselves salt, and have peace in one another.'
