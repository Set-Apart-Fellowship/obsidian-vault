# John 1 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/43 - John/John]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/43 - John/John 2|John 2 →]]

***

v1 In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God;

v2 this one was in the beginning with God;

v3 all things through him did happen, and without him happened not even one thing that hath happened.

v4 In him was life, and the life was the light of men,

v5 and the light in the darkness did shine, and the darkness did not perceive it.

v6 There came a man -- having been sent from God -- whose name [is] John,

v7 this one came for testimony, that he might testify about the Light, that all might believe through him;

v8 that one was not the Light, but -- that he might testify about the Light.

v9 He was the true Light, which doth enlighten every man, coming to the world;

v10 in the world he was, and the world through him was made, and the world did not know him:

v11 to his own things he came, and his own people did not receive him;

v12 but as many as did receive him to them he gave authority to become sons of God -- to those believing in his name,

v13 who -- not of blood nor of a will of flesh, nor of a will of man but -- of God were begotten.

v14 And the Word became flesh, and did tabernacle among us, and we beheld his glory, glory as of an only begotten of a father, full of grace and truth.

v15 John doth testify concerning him, and hath cried, saying, 'This was he of whom I said, He who after me is coming, hath come before me, for he was before me;'

v16 and out of his fulness did we all receive, and grace over-against grace;

v17 for the law through Moses was given, the grace and the truth through Jesus Christ did come;

v18 God no one hath ever seen; the only begotten Son, who is on the bosom of the Father -- he did declare.

v19 And this is the testimony of John, when the Jews sent out of Jerusalem priests and Levites, that they might question him, 'Who art thou?'

v20 and he confessed and did not deny, and confessed -- 'I am not the Christ.'

v21 And they questioned him, 'What then? Elijah art thou?' and he saith, 'I am not.' -- 'The prophet art thou?' and he answered, 'No.'

v22 They said then to him, 'Who art thou, that we may give an answer to those sending us? what dost thou say concerning thyself?'

v23 He said, 'I [am] a voice of one crying in the wilderness: Make straight the way of the Lord, as said Isaiah the prophet.'

v24 And those sent were of the Pharisees,

v25 and they questioned him and said to him, 'Why, then, dost thou baptize, if thou art not the Christ, nor Elijah, nor the prophet?'

v26 John answered them, saying, 'I baptize with water, but in midst of you he hath stood whom ye have not known, this one it is who is coming after me, who hath been before me,

v27 of whom I am not worthy that I may loose the cord of his sandal.'

v28 These things came to pass in Bethabara, beyond the Jordan, where John was baptizing,

v29 on the morrow John seeth Jesus coming unto him, and saith, 'Lo, the Lamb of God, who is taking away the sin of the world;

v30 this is he concerning whom I said, After me doth come a man, who hath come before me, because he was before me:

v31 and I knew him not, but, that he might be manifested to Israel, because of this I came with the water baptizing.

v32 And John testified, saying -- 'I have seen the Spirit coming down, as a dove, out of heaven, and it remained on him;

v33 and I did not know him, but he who sent me to baptize with water, He said to me, On whomsoever thou mayst see the Spirit coming down, and remaining on him, this is he who is baptizing with the Holy Spirit;

v34 and I have seen, and have testified, that this is the Son of God.'

v35 On the morrow, again, John was standing, and two of his disciples,

v36 and having looked on Jesus walking, he saith, 'Lo, the Lamb of God;'

v37 and the two disciples heard him speaking, and they followed Jesus.

v38 And Jesus having turned, and having beheld them following, saith to them, 'What seek ye?' and they said to them, 'Rabbi, (which is, being interpreted, Teacher,) where remainest thou?'

v39 He saith to them, 'Come and see;' they came, and saw where he doth remain, and with him they remained that day and the hour was about the tenth.

v40 Andrew, the brother of Simon Peter, was one of the two who heard from John, and followed him;

v41 this one doth first find his own brother Simon, and saith to him, 'We have found the Messiah,' (which is, being interpreted, The Anointed,)

v42 and he brought him unto Jesus: and having looked upon him, Jesus saith, 'Thou art Simon, the son of Jonas, thou shalt be called Cephas,' (which is interpreted, A rock.)

v43 On the morrow, he willed to go forth to Galilee, and he findeth Philip, and saith to him, 'Be following me.'

v44 And Philip was from Bethsaida, of the city of Andrew and Peter;

v45 Philip findeth Nathanael, and saith to him, 'Him of whom Moses wrote in the Law, and the prophets, we have found, Jesus the son of Joseph, who [is] from Nazareth;'

v46 and Nathanael said to him, 'Out of Nazareth is any good thing able to be?' Philip said to him, 'Come and see.'

v47 Jesus saw Nathanael coming unto him, and he saith concerning him, 'Lo, truly an Israelite, in whom guile is not;'

v48 Nathanael saith to him, 'Whence me dost thou know?' Jesus answered and said to him, 'Before Philip's calling thee -- thou being under the fig-tree -- I saw thee.'

v49 Nathanael answered and saith to him, 'Rabbi, thou art the Son of God, thou art the king of Israel.'

v50 Jesus answered and said to him, 'Because I said to thee, I saw thee under the fig-tree, thou dost believe; greater things than these thou shalt see;'

v51 and he saith to him, 'Verily, verily, I say to you, henceforth ye shall see the heaven opened, and the messengers of God going up and coming down upon the Son of Man.'
