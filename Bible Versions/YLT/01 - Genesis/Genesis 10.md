# Genesis 10 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9|← Genesis 9]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/01 - Genesis/Genesis]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/01 - Genesis/Genesis 11|Genesis 11 →]]

***

v1 And these [are] births of the sons of Noah, Shem, Ham, and Japheth; and born to them are sons after the deluge.

v2 'Sons of Japheth [are] Gomer, and Magog, and Madai, and Javan, and Tubal, and Meshech, and Tiras.

v3 And sons of Gomer [are] Ashkenaz, and Riphath, and Togarmah.

v4 And sons of Javan [are] Elishah, and Tarshish, Kittim, and Dodanim.

v5 By these have the isles of the nations been parted in their lands, each by his tongue, by their families, in their nations.

v6 And sons of Ham [are] Cush, and Mitzraim, and Phut, and Canaan.

v7 And sons of Cush [are] Seba, and Havilah, and Sabtah, and Raamah, and Sabtechah; and sons of Raamah [are] Sheba and Dedan.

v8 And Cush hath begotten Nimrod;

v9 he hath begun to be a hero in the land; he hath been a hero in hunting before Jehovah; therefore it is said, 'As Nimrod the hero [in] hunting before Jehovah.'

v10 And the first part of his kingdom is Babel, and Erech, and Accad, and Calneh, in the land of Shinar;

v11 from that land he hath gone out to Asshur, and buildeth Nineveh, even the broad places of the city, and Calah,

v12 and Resen, between Nineveh and Calah; it [is] the great city.

v13 And Mitzraim hath begotten the Ludim, and the Anamim, and the Lehabim, and the Naphtuhim,

v14 and the Pathrusim, and the Casluhim, (whence have come out Philistim,) and the Caphtorim.

v15 And Canaan hath begotten Sidon his first-born, and Heth,

v16 and the Jebusite, and the Amorite, and the Girgashite,

v17 and the Hivite, and the Arkite, and the Sinite,

v18 and the Arvadite, and the Zemarite, and the Hamathite; and afterwards have the families of the Canaanite been scattered.

v19 And the border of the Canaanite is from Sidon, [in] thy coming towards Gerar, unto Gaza; [in] thy coming towards Sodom, and Gomorrah, and Admah, and Zeboim, unto Lasha.

v20 These [are] sons of Ham, by their families, by their tongues, in their lands, in their nations.

v21 As to Shem, father of all sons of Eber, brother of Japheth the elder, he hath also begotten:

v22 Sons of Shem [are] Elam, and Asshur, and Arphaxad, and Lud, and Aram.

v23 And sons of Aram [are] Uz, and Hul, and Gether, and Mash.

v24 And Arphaxad hath begotten Salah, and Salah hath begotten Eber.

v25 And to Eber have two sons been born; the name of the one [is] Peleg (for in his days hath the earth been divided,) and his brother's name [is] Joktan.

v26 And Joktan hath begotten Almodad, and Sheleph, and Hazarmaveth, and Jerah,

v27 and Hadoram, and Uzal, and Diklah,

v28 and Obal, and Abimael, and Sheba,

v29 and Ophir, and Havilah, and Jobab; all these [are] sons of Joktan;

v30 and their dwelling is from Mesha, [in] thy coming towards Sephar, a mount of the east.

v31 These [are] sons of Shem, by their families, by their tongues, in their lands, by their nations.

v32 These [are] families of the sons of Noah, by their births, in their nations, and by these have the nations been parted in the earth after the deluge.
