# Genesis 15 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/01 - Genesis/Genesis 14|← Genesis 14]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/01 - Genesis/Genesis]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/01 - Genesis/Genesis 16|Genesis 16 →]]

***

v1 After these things hath the word of Jehovah been unto Abram in a vision, saying, 'Fear not, Abram, I [am] a shield to thee, thy reward [is] exceeding great.'

v2 And Abram saith, 'Lord Jehovah, what dost Thou give to me, and I am going childless? and an acquired son in my house is Demmesek Eliezer.'

v3 And Abram saith, 'Lo, to me Thou hast not given seed, and lo, a domestic doth heir me.'

v4 And lo, the word of Jehovah [is] unto him, saying, 'This [one] doth not heir thee; but he who cometh out from thy bowels, he doth heir thee;'

v5 and He bringeth him out without, and saith, 'Look attentively, I pray thee, towards the heavens, and count the stars, if thou art able to count them;' and He saith to him, 'Thus is thy seed.'

v6 And he hath believed in Jehovah, and He reckoneth it to him -- righteousness.

v7 And He saith unto him, 'I [am] Jehovah who brought thee out from Ur of the Chaldees, to give to thee this land to possess it;'

v8 and he saith, 'Lord Jehovah, whereby do I know that I possess it?'

v9 And He saith unto him, 'Take for Me a heifer of three years, and a she-goat of three years, and a ram of three years, and a turtle-dove, and a young bird;'

v10 and he taketh to him all these, and separateth them in the midst, and putteth each piece over against its fellow, but the bird he hath not divided;

v11 and the ravenous birds come down upon the carcases, and Abram causeth them to turn back.

v12 And the sun is about to go in, and deep sleep hath fallen upon Abram, and lo, a terror of great darkness is falling upon him;

v13 and He saith to Abram, 'knowing -- know that thy seed is a sojourner in a land not theirs, and they have served them, and they have afflicted them four hundred years,

v14 and the nation also whom they serve I judge, and after this they go out with great substance;

v15 and thou -- thou comest in unto thy fathers in peace; thou art buried in a good old age;

v16 and the fourth generation doth turn back hither, for the iniquity of the Amorite is not yet complete.'

v17 And it cometh to pass -- the sun hath gone in, and thick darkness hath been -- and lo, a furnace of smoke, and a lamp of fire, which hath passed over between those pieces.

v18 In that day hath Jehovah made with Abram a covenant, saying, 'To thy seed I have given this land, from the river of Egypt unto the great river, the river Phrat,

v19 with the Kenite, and the Kenizzite, and the Kadmonite,

v20 and the Hittite, and the Perizzite, and the Rephaim,

v21 and the Amorite, and the Canaanite, and the Girgashite, and the Jebusite.'
