# Genesis 49 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/01 - Genesis/Genesis 48|← Genesis 48]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/01 - Genesis/Genesis]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/01 - Genesis/Genesis 50|Genesis 50 →]]

***

v1 And Jacob calleth unto his sons and saith, 'Be gathered together, and I declare to you that which doth happen with you in the latter end of the days.

v2 'Be assembled, and hear, sons of Jacob, And hearken unto Israel your father.

v3 Reuben! my first-born thou, My power, and beginning of my strength, The abundance of exaltation, And the abundance of strength;

v4 Unstable as water, thou art not abundant; For thou hast gone up thy father's bed; Then thou hast polluted: My couch he went up!

v5 Simeon and Levi [are] brethren! Instruments of violence -- their espousals!

v6 Into their secret, come not, O my soul! Unto their assembly be not united, O mine honour; For in their anger they slew a man, And in their self-will eradicated a prince.

v7 Cursed [is] their anger, for [it is] fierce, And their wrath, for [it is] sharp; I divide them in Jacob, And I scatter them in Israel.

v8 Judah! thou -- thy brethren praise thee! Thy hand [is] on the neck of thine enemies, Sons of thy father bow themselves to thee.

v9 A lion's whelp [is] Judah, For prey, my son, thou hast gone up; He hath bent, he hath crouched as a lion, And as a lioness; who causeth him to arise?

v10 The sceptre turneth not aside from Judah, And a lawgiver from between his feet, Till his Seed come; And his [is] the obedience of peoples.

v11 Binding to the vine his ass, And to the choice vine the colt of his ass, He hath washed in wine his clothing, And in the blood of grapes his covering;

v12 Red [are] eyes with wine, And white [are] teeth with milk!

v13 Zebulun at a haven of the seas doth dwell, And he [is] for a haven of ships; And his side [is] unto Zidon.

v14 Issacher [is] a strong ass, Crouching between the two folds;

v15 And he seeth rest that [it is] good, And the land that [it is] pleasant, And he inclineth his shoulder to bear, And is to tribute a servant.

v16 Dan doth judge his people, As one of the tribes of Israel;

v17 Dan is a serpent by the way, An adder by the path, Which is biting the horse's heels, And its rider falleth backward.

v18 For Thy salvation I have waited, Jehovah!

v19 Gad! a troop assaulteth him, But he assaulteth last.

v20 Out of Asher his bread [is] fat; And he giveth dainties of a king.

v21 Naphtali [is] a hind sent away, Who is giving beauteous young ones.

v22 Joseph [is] a fruitful son; A fruitful son by a fountain, Daughters step over the wall;

v23 And embitter him -- yea, they have striven, Yea, hate him do archers;

v24 And his bow abook_ideth in strength, And strengthened are the arms of his hands By the hands of the Mighty One of Jacob, Whence is a shepherd, a son of Israel.

v25 By the God of thy father who helpeth thee, And the Mighty One who blesseth thee, Blessings of the heavens from above, Blessings of the deep lying under, Blessings of breasts and womb; --

v26 Thy father's blessings have been mighty Above the blessings of my progenitors, Unto the limit of the heights age-during They are for the head of Joseph, And for the crown of the one Separate [from] his brethren.

v27 Benjamin! a wolf teareth; In the morning he eateth prey, And at evening he apportioneth spoil.'

v28 All these [are] the twelve tribes of Israel, and this [is] that which their father hath spoken unto them, and he blesseth them; each according to his blessing he hath blessed them.

v29 And he commandeth them, and saith unto them, 'I am being gathered unto my people; bury me by my fathers, at the cave which [is] in the field of Ephron the Hittite;

v30 in the cave which [is] in the field of Machpelah, which [is] on the front of Mamre, in the land of Canaan, which Abraham bought with the field from Ephron the Hittite for a possession of a burying-place;

v31 (there they buried Abraham and Sarah his wife; there they buried Isaac and Rebekah his wife; and there I buried Leah);

v32 the purchase of the field and of the cave which [is] in it, [is] from Sons of Heth.'

v33 And Jacob finisheth commanding his sons, and gathereth up his feet unto the bed, and expireth, and is gathered unto his people.
