# Genesis 4 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3|← Genesis 3]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/01 - Genesis/Genesis]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/01 - Genesis/Genesis 5|Genesis 5 →]]

***

v1 And the man knew Eve his wife, and she conceiveth and beareth Cain, and saith, 'I have gotten a man by Jehovah;'

v2 and she addeth to bear his brother, even Abel. And Abel is feeding a flock, and Cain hath been servant of the ground.

v3 And it cometh to pass at the end of days that Cain bringeth from the fruit of the ground a present to Jehovah;

v4 and Abel, he hath brought, he also, from the female firstlings of his flock, even from their fat ones; and Jehovah looketh unto Abel and unto his present,

v5 and unto Cain and unto his present He hath not looked; and it is very displeasing to Cain, and his countenance is fallen.

v6 And Jehovah saith unto Cain, 'Why hast thou displeasure? and why hath thy countenance fallen?

v7 Is there not, if thou dost well, acceptance? and if thou dost not well, at the opening a sin-offering is crouching, and unto thee its desire, and thou rulest over it.'

v8 And Cain saith unto Abel his brother, ['Let us go into the field;'] and it cometh to pass in their being in the field, that Cain riseth up against Abel his brother, and slayeth him.

v9 And Jehovah saith unto Cain, 'Where [is] Abel thy brother?' and he saith, 'I have not known; my brother's keeper -- I?'

v10 And He saith, 'What hast thou done? the voice of thy brother's blood is crying unto Me from the ground;

v11 and now, cursed [art] thou from the ground, which hath opened her mouth to receive the blood of thy brother from thy hand;

v12 when thou tillest the ground, it doth not add to give its strength to thee -- a wanderer, even a trembling one, thou art in the earth.'

v13 And Cain saith unto Jehovah, 'Greater is my punishment than to be borne;

v14 lo, Thou hast driven me to-day from off the face of the ground, and from Thy face I am hid; and I have been a wanderer, even a trembling one, in the earth, and it hath been -- every one finding me doth slay me.'

v15 And Jehovah saith to him, 'Therefore -- of any slayer of Cain sevenfold it is required;' and Jehovah setteth to Cain a token that none finding him doth slay him.

v16 And Cain goeth out from before Jehovah, and dwelleth in the land, moving about east of Eden;

v17 and Cain knoweth his wife, and she conceiveth, and beareth Enoch; and he is building a city, and he calleth the name of the city, according to the name of his son -- Enoch.

v18 And born to Enoch is Irad; and Irad hath begotten Mehujael; and Mehujael hath begotten Methusael; and Methusael hath begotten Lamech.

v19 And Lamech taketh to himself two wives, the name of the one Adah, and the name of the second Zillah.

v20 And Adah beareth Jabal, he hath been father of those inhabiting tents and purchased possessions;

v21 and the name of his brother [is] Jubal, he hath been father of every one handling harp and organ.

v22 And Zillah she also bare Tubal-Cain, an instructor of every artificer in brass and iron; and a sister of Tubal-Cain [is] Naamah.

v23 And Lamech saith to his wives: -- 'Adah and Zillah, hear my voice; Wives of Lamech, give ear [to] my saying: For a man I have slain for my wound, Even a young man for my hurt;

v24 For sevenfold is required for Cain, And for Lamech seventy and sevenfold.'

v25 And Adam again knoweth his wife, and she beareth a son, and calleth his name Seth, 'for God hath appointed for me another seed instead of Abel:' for Cain had slain him.

v26 And to Seth, to him also a son hath been born, and he calleth his name Enos; then a beginning was made of preaching in the name of Jehovah.
