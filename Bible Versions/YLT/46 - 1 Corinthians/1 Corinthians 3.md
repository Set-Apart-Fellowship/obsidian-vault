# 1 Corinthians 3 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 2|← 1 Corinthians 2]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 4|1 Corinthians 4 →]]

***

v1 And I, brethren, was not able to speak to you as to spiritual, but as to fleshly -- as to babes in Christ;

v2 with milk I fed you, and not with meat, for ye were not yet able, but not even yet are ye now able,

v3 for yet ye are fleshly, for where [there is] among you envying, and strife, and divisions, are ye not fleshly, and in the manner of men do walk?

v4 for when one may say, 'I, indeed, am of Paul;' and another, 'I -- of Apollos;' are ye not fleshly?

v5 Who, then, is Paul, and who Apollos, but ministrants through whom ye did believe, and to each as the Lord gave?

v6 I planted, Apollos watered, but God was giving growth;

v7 so that neither is he who is planting anything, nor he who is watering, but He who is giving growth -- God;

v8 and he who is planting and he who is watering are one, and each his own reward shall receive, according to his own labour,

v9 for of God we are fellow-workmen; God's tillage, God's building ye are.

v10 According to the grace of God that was given to me, as a wise master-builder, a foundation I have laid, and another doth build on [it],

v11 for other foundation no one is able to lay except that which is laid, which is Jesus the Christ;

v12 and if any one doth build upon this foundation gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, straw --

v13 of each the work shall become manifest, for the day shall declare [it], because in fire it is revealed, and the work of each, what kind it is, the fire shall prove;

v14 if of any one the work doth remain that he built on [it], a wage he shall receive;

v15 if of any the work is burned up, he shall suffer loss; and himself shall be saved, but so as through fire.

v16 have ye not known that ye are a sanctuary of God, and the Spirit of God doth dwell in you?

v17 if any one the sanctuary of God doth waste, him shall God waste; for the sanctuary of God is holy, the which ye are.

v18 Let no one deceive himself; if any one doth seem to be wise among you in this age -- let him become a fool, that he may become wise,

v19 for the wisdom of this world is foolishness with God, for it hath been written, 'Who is taking the wise in their craftiness;'

v20 and again, 'The Lord doth know the reasonings of the wise, that they are vain.'

v21 So then, let no one glory in men, for all things are yours,

v22 whether Paul, or Apollos, or Cephas, or the world, or life, or death, or things present, or things about to be -- all are yours,

v23 and ye [are] Christ's, and Christ [is] God's.
