# 1 Corinthians 14 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 13|← 1 Corinthians 13]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15|1 Corinthians 15 →]]

***

v1 Pursue the love, and seek earnestly the spiritual things, and rather that ye may prophecy,

v2 for he who is speaking in an [unknown] tongue -- to men he doth not speak, but to God, for no one doth hearken, and in spirit he doth speak secrets;

v3 and he who is prophesying to men doth speak edification, and exhortation, and comfort;

v4 he who is speaking in an [unknown] tongue, himself doth edify, and he who is prophesying, an assembly doth edify;

v5 and I wish you all to speak with tongues, and more that ye may prophecy, for greater is he who is prophesying than he who is speaking with tongues, except one may interpret, that the assembly may receive edification.

v6 And now, brethren, if I may come unto you speaking tongues, what shall I profit you, except I shall speak to you either in revelation, or in knowledge, or in prophesying, or in teaching?

v7 yet the things without life giving sound -- whether pipe or harp -- if a difference in the sounds they may not give, how shall be known that which is piped or that which is harped?

v8 for if also an uncertain sound a trumpet may give, who shall prepare himself for battle?

v9 so also ye, if through the tongue, speech easily understood ye may not give -- how shall that which is spoken be known? for ye shall be speaking to air.

v10 There are, it may be, so many kinds of voices in the world, and none of them is unmeaning,

v11 if, then, I do not know the power of the voice, I shall be to him who is speaking a foreigner, and he who is speaking, is to me a foreigner;

v12 so also ye, since ye are earnestly desirous of spiritual gifts, for the building up of the assembly seek that ye may abound;

v13 wherefore he who is speaking in an [unknown] tongue -- let him pray that he may interpret;

v14 for if I pray in an [unknown] tongue, my spirit doth pray, and my understanding is unfruitful.

v15 What then is it? I will pray with the spirit, and I will pray also with the understanding; I will sing psalms with the spirit, and I will sing psalms also with the understanding;

v16 since, if thou mayest bless with the spirit, he who is filling the place of the unlearned, how shall he say the Amen at thy giving of thanks, since what thou dost say he hath not known?

v17 for thou, indeed, dost give thanks well, but the other is not built up!

v18 I give thanks to my God -- more than you all with tongues speaking --

v19 but in an assembly I wish to speak five words through my understanding, that others also I may instruct, rather than myriads of words in an [unknown] tongue.

v20 Brethren, become not children in the understanding, but in the evil be ye babes, and in the understanding become ye perfect;

v21 in the law it hath been written, that, 'With other tongues and with other lips I will speak to this people, and not even so will they hear Me, saith the Lord;'

v22 so that the tongues are for a sign, not to the believing, but to the unbelieving; and the prophesy [is] not for the unbelieving, but for the believing,

v23 If, therefore, the whole assembly may come together, to the same place, and all may speak with tongues, and there may come in unlearned or unbelievers, will they not say that ye are mad?

v24 and if all may prophecy, and any one may come in, an unbeliever or unlearned, he is convicted by all, he is discerned by all,

v25 and so the secrets of his heart become manifest, and so having fallen upon [his] face, he will bow before God, declaring that God really is among you.

v26 What then is it, brethren? whenever ye may come together, each of you hath a psalm, hath a teaching, hath a tongue, hath a revelation, hath an interpretation? let all things be for building up;

v27 if an [unknown] tongue any one do speak, by two, or at the most, by three, and in turn, and let one interpret;

v28 and if there may be no interpreter, let him be silent in an assembly, and to himself let him speak, and to God.

v29 And prophets -- let two or three speak, and let the others discern,

v30 and if to another sitting [anything] may be revealed, let the first be silent;

v31 for ye are able, one by one, all to prophesy, that all may learn, and all may be exhorted,

v32 and the spiritual gift of prophets to prophets are subject,

v33 for God is not [a God] of tumult, but of peace, as in all the assemblies of the saints.

v34 Your women in the assemblies let them be silent, for it hath not been permitted to them to speak, but to be subject, as also the law saith;

v35 and if they wish to learn anything, at home their own husbands let them question, for it is a shame to women to speak in an assembly.

v36 From you did the word of God come forth? or to you alone did it come?

v37 if any one doth think to be a prophet, or spiritual, let him acknowledge the things that I write to you -- that of the Lord they are commands;

v38 and if any one is ignorant -- let him be ignorant;

v39 so that, brethren, earnestly desire to prophesy, and to speak with tongues do not forbook_id;

v40 let all things be done decently and in order.
