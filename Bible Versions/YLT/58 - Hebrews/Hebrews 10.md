# Hebrews 10 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9|← Hebrews 9]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11|Hebrews 11 →]]

***

v1 For the law having a shadow of the coming good things -- not the very image of the matters, every year, by the same sacrifices that they offer continually, is never able to make perfect those coming near,

v2 since, would they not have ceased to be offered, because of those serving having no more conscience of sins, having once been purified?

v3 but in those [sacrifices] is a remembrance of sins every year,

v4 for it is impossible for blood of bulls and goats to take away sins.

v5 Wherefore, coming into the world, he saith, 'Sacrifice and offering Thou didst not will, and a body Thou didst prepare for me,

v6 in burnt-offerings, and concerning sin-offerings, Thou didst not delight,

v7 then I said, Lo, I come, (in a volume of the book it hath been written concerning me,) to do, O God, Thy will;'

v8 saying above -- 'Sacrifice, and offering, and burnt-offerings, and concerning sin-offering Thou didst not will, nor delight in,' -- which according to the law are offered --

v9 then he said, 'Lo, I come to do, O God, Thy will;' he doth take away the first that the second he may establish;

v10 in the which will we are having been sanctified through the offering of the body of Jesus Christ once,

v11 and every priest, indeed, hath stood daily serving, and the same sacrifices many times offering, that are never able to take away sins.

v12 And He, for sin one sacrifice having offered -- to the end, did sit down on the right hand of God, --

v13 as to the rest, expecting till He may place his enemies [as] his footstool,

v14 for by one offering he hath perfected to the end those sanctified;

v15 and testify to us also doth the Holy Spirit, for after that He hath said before,

v16 'This [is] the covenant that I will make with them after those days, saith the Lord, giving My laws on their hearts, and upon their minds I will write them,'

v17 and 'their sins and their lawlessness I will remember no more;'

v18 and where forgiveness of these [is], there is no more offering for sin.

v19 Having, therefore, brethren, boldness for the entrance into the holy places, in the blood of Jesus,

v20 which way he did initiate for us -- new and living, through the vail, that is, his flesh --

v21 and a high priest over the house of God,

v22 may we draw near with a true heart, in full assurance of faith, having the hearts sprinkled from an evil conscience, and having the body bathed with pure water;

v23 may we hold fast the unwavering profession of the hope, (for faithful [is] He who did promise),

v24 and may we consider one another to provoke to love and to good works,

v25 not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together, as a custom of certain [is], but exhorting, and so much the more as ye see the day coming nigh.

v26 For we -- willfully sinning after the receiving the full knowledge of the truth -- no more for sins doth there remain a sacrifice,

v27 but a certain fearful looking for of judgment, and fiery zeal, about to devour the opposers;

v28 any one who did set at nought a law of Moses, apart from mercies, by two or three witnesses, doth die,

v29 of how much sorer punishment shall he be counted worthy who the Son of God did trample on, and the blood of the covenant did count a common thing, in which he was sanctified, and to the Spirit of the grace did despite?

v30 for we have known Him who is saying, 'Vengeance [is] Mine, I will recompense, saith the Lord;' and again, 'The Lord shall judge His people;' --

v31 fearful [is] the falling into the hands of a living God.

v32 And call to your remembrance the former days, in which, having been enlightened, ye did endure much conflict of sufferings,

v33 partly both with reproaches and tribulations being made spectacles, and partly having become partners of those so living,

v34 for also with my bonds ye sympathised, and the robbery of your goods with joy ye did receive, knowing that ye have in yourselves a better substance in the heavens, and an enduring one.

v35 Ye may not cast away, then, your boldness, which hath great recompense of reward,

v36 for of patience ye have need, that the will of God having done, ye may receive the promise,

v37 for yet a very very little, He who is coming will come, and will not tarry;

v38 and 'the righteous by faith shall live,' and 'if he may draw back, My soul hath no pleasure in him,'

v39 and we are not of those drawing back to destruction, but of those believing to a preserving of soul.
