# Proverbs 13 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 12|← Proverbs 12]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 14|Proverbs 14 →]]

***

v1 A wise son -- the instruction of a father, And a scorner -- he hath not heard rebuke.

v2 From the fruit of the mouth a man eateth good, And the soul of the treacherous -- violence.

v3 Whoso is keeping his mouth, is keeping his soul, Whoso is opening wide his lips -- ruin to him!

v4 The soul of the slothful is desiring, and hath not. And the soul of the diligent is made fat.

v5 A false word the righteous hateth, And the wicked causeth abhorrence, and is confounded.

v6 Righteousness keepeth him who is perfect in the way, And wickedness overthroweth a sin offering.

v7 There is who is making himself rich, and hath nothing, Who is making himself poor, and wealth [is] abundant.

v8 The ransom of a man's life [are] his riches, And the poor hath not heard rebuke.

v9 The light of the righteous rejoiceth, And the lamp of the wicked is extinguished.

v10 A vain man through pride causeth debate, And with the counselled [is] wisdom.

v11 Wealth from vanity becometh little, And whoso is gathering by the hand becometh great.

v12 Hope prolonged is making the heart sick, And a tree of life [is] the coming desire.

v13 Whoso is despising the Word is destroyed for it, And whoso is fearing the Command is repayed.

v14 The law of the wise [is] a fountain of life, To turn aside from snares of death.

v15 Good understanding giveth grace, And the way of the treacherous [is] hard.

v16 Every prudent one dealeth with knowledge, And a fool spreadeth out folly.

v17 A wicked messenger falleth into evil, And a faithful ambassador is healing.

v18 Whoso is refusing instruction -- poverty and shame, And whoso is observing reproof is honoured.

v19 A desire accomplished is sweet to the soul, And an abomination to fools [is]: Turn from evil.

v20 Whoso is walking with wise men is wise, And a companion of fools suffereth evil.

v21 Evil pursueth sinners, And good recompenseth the righteous.

v22 A good man causeth sons' sons to inherit, And laid up for the righteous [is] the sinner's wealth.

v23 Abundance of food -- the tillage of the poor, And substance is consumed without judgment.

v24 Whoso is sparing his rod is hating his son, And whoso is loving him hath hastened him chastisement.

v25 The righteous is eating to the satiety of his soul, And the belly of the wicked lacketh!
