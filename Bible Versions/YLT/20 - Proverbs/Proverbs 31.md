# Proverbs 31 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 30|← Proverbs 30]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]]

***

v1 Words of Lemuel a king, a declaration that his mother taught him:

v2 'What, my son? and what, son of my womb? And what, son of my vows?

v3 Give not to women thy strength, And thy ways to wiping away of kings.

v4 Not for kings, O Lemuel, Not for kings, to drink wine, And for princes a desire of strong drink.

v5 Lest he drink, and forget the decree, And change the judgment of any of the sons of affliction.

v6 Give strong drink to the perishing, And wine to the bitter in soul,

v7 He drinketh, and forgetteth his poverty, And his misery he remembereth not again.

v8 Open thy mouth for the dumb, For the right of all sons of change.

v9 Open thy mouth, judge righteously, Both the cause of the poor and needy!'

v10 A woman of worth who doth find? Yea, far above rubies [is] her price.

v11 The heart of her husband hath trusted in her, And spoil he lacketh not.

v12 She hath done him good, and not evil, All days of her life.

v13 She hath sought wool and flax, And with delight she worketh [with] her hands.

v14 She hath been as ships of the merchant, From afar she bringeth in her bread.

v15 Yea, she riseth while yet night, And giveth food to her household, And a portion to her damsels.

v16 She hath considered a field, and taketh it, From the fruit of her hands she hath planted a vineyard.

v17 She hath girded with might her loins, And doth strengthen her arms.

v18 She hath perceived when her merchandise [is] good, Her lamp is not extinguished in the night.

v19 Her hands she hath sent forth on a spindle, And her hands have held a distaff.

v20 Her hand she hath spread forth to the poor, Yea, her hands she sent forth to the needy.

v21 She is not afraid of her household from snow, For all her household are clothed [with] scarlet.

v22 Ornamental coverings she hath made for herself, Silk and purple [are] her clothing.

v23 Known in the gates is her husband, In his sitting with elders of the land.

v24 Linen garments she hath made, and selleth, And a girdle she hath given to the merchant.

v25 Strength and honour [are] her clothing, And she rejoiceth at a latter day.

v26 Her mouth she hath opened in wisdom, And the law of kindness [is] on her tongue.

v27 She [is] watching the ways of her household, And bread of sloth she eateth not.

v28 Her sons have risen up, and pronounce her happy, Her husband, and he praiseth her,

v29 'Many [are] the daughters who have done worthily, Thou hast gone up above them all.'

v30 The grace [is] false, and the beauty [is] vain, A woman fearing Jehovah, she may boast herself.

v31 Give ye to her of the fruit of her hands, And her works do praise her in the gates!
