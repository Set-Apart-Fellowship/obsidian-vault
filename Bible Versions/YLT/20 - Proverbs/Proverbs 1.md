# Proverbs 1 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 2|Proverbs 2 →]]

***

v1 Proverbs of Solomon, son of David, king of Israel:

v2 For knowing wisdom and instruction, For understanding sayings of intelligence,

v3 For receiving the instruction of wisdom, Righteousness, judgment, and uprightness,

v4 For giving to simple ones -- prudence, To a youth -- knowledge and discretion.

v5 (The wise doth hear and increaseth learning, And the intelligent doth obtain counsels.)

v6 For understanding a proverb and its sweetness, Words of the wise and their acute sayings.

v7 Fear of Jehovah [is] a beginning of knowledge, Wisdom and instruction fools have despised!

v8 Hear, my son, the instruction of thy father, And leave not the law of thy mother,

v9 For a graceful wreath [are] they to thy head, And chains to thy neck.

v10 My son, if sinners entice thee be not willing.

v11 If they say, 'Come with us, we lay wait for blood, We watch secretly for the innocent without cause,

v12 We swallow them as Sheol -- alive, And whole -- as those going down [to] the pit,

v13 Every precious substance we find, We fill our houses [with] spoil,

v14 Thy lot thou dost cast among us, One purse is -- to all of us.'

v15 My son! go not in the way with them, Withhold thy foot from their path,

v16 For their feet to evil do run, And they haste to shed blood.

v17 Surely in vain is the net spread out before the eyes of any bird.

v18 And they for their own blood lay wait, They watch secretly for their own lives.

v19 So [are] the paths of every gainer of dishonest gain, The life of its owners it taketh.

v20 Wisdom in an out-place crieth aloud, In broad places she giveth forth her voice,

v21 At the head of the multitudes she calleth, In the openings of the gates, In the city her sayings she saith:

v22 'Till when, ye simple, do ye love simplicity? And have scorners their scorning desired? And do fools hate knowledge?

v23 Turn back at my reproof, lo, I pour forth to you my spirit, I make known my words with you.

v24 Because I have called, and ye refuse, I stretched out my hand, and none is attending,

v25 And ye slight all my counsel, And my reproof ye have not desired.

v26 I also in your calamity do laugh, I deride when your fear cometh,

v27 When your fear cometh as destruction, And your calamity as a hurricane doth come, When on you come adversity and distress.

v28 Then they call me, and I do not answer, They seek me earnestly, and find me not.

v29 Because that they have hated knowledge, And the fear of Jehovah have not chosen.

v30 They have not consented to my counsel, They have despised all my reproof,

v31 And they eat of the fruit of their way, And from their own counsels they are filled.

v32 For the turning of the simple slayeth them, And the security of the foolish destroyeth them.

v33 And whoso is hearkening to me dwelleth confidently, And [is] quiet from fear of evil!'
