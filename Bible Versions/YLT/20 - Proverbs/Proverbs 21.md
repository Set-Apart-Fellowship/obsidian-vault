# Proverbs 21 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 20|← Proverbs 20]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 22|Proverbs 22 →]]

***

v1 Rivulets of waters [is] the heart of a king in the hand of Jehovah, Wherever He pleaseth He inclineth it.

v2 Every way of a man [is] right in his own eyes, And Jehovah is pondering hearts.

v3 To do righteousness and judgment, Is chosen of Jehovah rather than sacrifice.

v4 Loftiness of eyes, and breadth of heart, Tillage of the wicked [is] sin.

v5 The purposes of the diligent [are] only to advantage, And of every hasty one, only to want.

v6 The making of treasures by a lying tongue, [Is] a vanity driven away of those seeking death.

v7 The spoil of the wicked catcheth them, Because they have refused to do judgment.

v8 Froward [is] the way of a man who is vile, And the pure -- upright [is] his work.

v9 Better to sit on a corner of the roof, Than [with] a woman of contentions and a house of company.

v10 The soul of the wicked hath desired evil, Not gracious in his eyes is his neighbour.

v11 When the scorner is punished, the simple becometh wise, And in giving understanding to the wise He receiveth knowledge.

v12 The Righteous One is acting wisely Towards the house of the wicked, He is overthrowing the wicked for wickedness.

v13 Whoso is shutting his ear from the cry of the poor, He also doth cry, and is not answered.

v14 A gift in secret pacifieth anger, And a bribe in the bosom strong fury.

v15 To do justice [is] joy to the righteous, But ruin to workers of iniquity.

v16 A man who is wandering from the way of understanding, In an assembly of Rephaim resteth.

v17 Whoso [is] loving mirth [is] a poor man, Whoso is loving wine and oil maketh no wealth.

v18 The wicked [is] an atonement for the righteous, And for the upright the treacherous dealer.

v19 Better to dwell in a wilderness land, Than [with] a woman of contentions and anger.

v20 A treasure to be desired, and oil, [Is] in the habitation of the wise, And a foolish man swalloweth it up.

v21 Whoso is pursuing righteousness and kindness, Findeth life, righteousness, and honour.

v22 A city of the mighty hath the wise gone up, And bringeth down the strength of its confidence.

v23 Whoso is keeping his mouth and his tongue, Is keeping from adversities his soul.

v24 Proud, haughty, scorner [is] his name, Who is working in the wrath of pride.

v25 The desire of the slothful slayeth him, For his hands have refused to work.

v26 All the day desiring he hath desired, And the righteous giveth and withholdeth not.

v27 The sacrifice of the wicked [is] abomination, Much more when in wickedness he bringeth it.

v28 A false witness doth perish, And an attentive man for ever speaketh.

v29 A wicked man hath hardened by his face, And the upright -- he prepareth his way.

v30 There is no wisdom, nor understanding, Nor counsel, over-against Jehovah.

v31 A horse is prepared for a day of battle, And the deliverance [is] of Jehovah!
