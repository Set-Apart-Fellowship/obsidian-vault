# Proverbs 23 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 22|← Proverbs 22]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 24|Proverbs 24 →]]

***

v1 When thou sittest to eat with a ruler, Thou considerest diligently that which [is] before thee,

v2 And thou hast put a knife to thy throat, If thou [art] a man of appetite.

v3 Have no desire to his dainties, seeing it [is] lying food.

v4 Labour not to make wealth, From thine own understanding cease, Dost thou cause thine eyes to fly upon it? Then it is not.

v5 For wealth maketh to itself wings, As an eagle it flieth to the heavens.

v6 Eat not the bread of an evil eye, And have no desire to his dainties,

v7 For as he hath thought in his soul, so [is] he, 'Eat and drink,' saith he to thee, And his heart [is] not with thee.

v8 Thy morsel thou hast eaten thou dost vomit up, And hast marred thy words that [are] sweet.

v9 In the ears of a fool speak not, For he treadeth on the wisdom of thy words.

v10 Remove not a border of olden times, And into fields of the fatherless enter not,

v11 For their Redeemer [is] strong, He doth plead their cause with thee.

v12 Bring in to instruction thy heart, And thine ear to sayings of knowledge.

v13 Withhold not from a youth chastisement, When thou smitest him with a rod he dieth not.

v14 Thou with a rod smitest him, And his soul from Sheol thou deliverest.

v15 My son, if thy heart hath been wise, My heart rejoiceth, even mine,

v16 And my reins exult when thy lips speak uprightly.

v17 Let not thy heart be envious at sinners, But -- in the fear of Jehovah all the day.

v18 For, is there a posterity? Then thy hope is not cut off.

v19 Hear thou, my son, and be wise, And make happy in the way thy heart,

v20 Be not thou among quaffers of wine, Among gluttonous ones of flesh,

v21 For the quaffer and glutton become poor, And drowsiness clotheth with rags.

v22 Hearken to thy father, who begat thee, And despise not thy mother when she hath become old.

v23 Truth buy, and sell not, Wisdom, and instruction, and understanding,

v24 The father of the righteous rejoiceth greatly, The begetter of the wise rejoiceth in him.

v25 Rejoice doth thy father and thy mother, Yea, she that bare thee is joyful.

v26 Give, my son, thy heart to me, And let thine eyes watch my ways.

v27 For a harlot [is] a deep ditch, And a strange woman [is] a strait pit.

v28 She also, as catching prey, lieth in wait, And the treacherous among men she increaseth.

v29 Who hath wo? who hath sorrow? Who hath contentions? who hath plaint? Who hath wounds without cause? Who hath redness of eyes?

v30 Those tarrying by the wine, Those going in to search out mixed wine.

v31 See not wine when it showeth itself red, When it giveth in the cup its colour, It goeth up and down through the upright.

v32 Its latter end -- as a serpent it biteth, And as a basilisk it stingeth.

v33 Thine eyes see strange women, And thy heart speaketh perverse things.

v34 And thou hast been as one lying down in the heart of the sea, And as one lying down on the top of a mast.

v35 'They smote me, I have not been sick, They beat me, I have not known. When I awake -- I seek it yet again!'
