# Proverbs 15 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 14|← Proverbs 14]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 16|Proverbs 16 →]]

***

v1 A soft answer turneth back fury, And a grievous word raiseth up anger.

v2 The tongue of the wise maketh knowledge good, And the mouth of fools uttereth folly.

v3 In every place are the eyes of Jehovah, Watching the evil and the good.

v4 A healed tongue [is] a tree of life, And perverseness in it -- a breach in the spirit.

v5 A fool despiseth the instruction of his father, And whoso is regarding reproof is prudent.

v6 [In] the house of the righteous [is] abundant strength, And in the increase of the wicked -- trouble.

v7 The lips of the wise scatter knowledge, And the heart of fools [is] not right.

v8 The sacrifice of the wicked [is] an abomination to Jehovah, And the prayer of the upright [is] His delight.

v9 An abomination to Jehovah [is] the way of the wicked, And whoso is pursuing righteousness He loveth.

v10 Chastisement [is] grievous to him who is forsaking the path, Whoso is hating reproof dieth.

v11 Sheol and destruction [are] before Jehovah, Surely also the hearts of the sons of men.

v12 A scorner loveth not his reprover, Unto the wise he goeth not.

v13 A joyful heart maketh glad the face, And by grief of heart is the spirit smitten.

v14 The heart of the intelligent seeketh knowledge, And the mouth of fools enjoyeth folly.

v15 All the days of the afflicted [are] evil, And gladness of heart [is] a perpetual banquet.

v16 Better [is] a little with the fear of Jehovah, Than much treasure, and tumult with it.

v17 Better [is] an allowance of green herbs and love there, Than a fatted ox, and hatred with it.

v18 A man of fury stirreth up contention, And the slow to anger appeaseth strife.

v19 The way of the slothful [is] as a hedge of briers, And the path of the upright is raised up.

v20 A wise son rejoiceth a father. And a foolish man is despising his mother.

v21 Folly is joy to one lacking heart, And a man of intelligence directeth [his] going.

v22 Without counsel [is] the making void of purposes, And in a multitude of counsellors it is established.

v23 Joy [is] to a man in the answer of his mouth, And a word in its season -- how good!

v24 A path of life [is] on high for the wise, To turn aside from Sheol beneath.

v25 The house of the proud Jehovah pulleth down, And He setteth up the border of the widow.

v26 An abomination to Jehovah [are] thoughts of wickedness, And pure [are] sayings of pleasantness.

v27 A dishonest gainer is troubling his house, And whoso is hating gifts liveth.

v28 The heart of the righteous meditateth to answer, And the mouth of the wicked uttereth evil things.

v29 Far [is] Jehovah from the wicked, And the prayer of the righteous He heareth.

v30 The light of the eyes rejoiceth the heart, A good report maketh fat the bone.

v31 An ear that is hearing the reproof of life Doth lodge among the wise.

v32 Whoso is refusing instruction is despising his soul, And whoso is hearing reproof Is getting understanding.

v33 The fear of Jehovah [is] the instruction of wisdom, And before honour [is] humility!
