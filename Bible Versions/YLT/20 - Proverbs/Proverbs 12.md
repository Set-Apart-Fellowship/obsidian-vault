# Proverbs 12 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 11|← Proverbs 11]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 13|Proverbs 13 →]]

***

v1 Whoso is loving instruction, is loving knowledge, And whoso is hating reproof [is] brutish.

v2 The good bringeth forth favour from Jehovah, And the man of wicked devices He condemneth.

v3 A man is not established by wickedness, And the root of the righteous is not moved.

v4 A virtuous woman [is] a crown to her husband, And as rottenness in his bones [is] one causing shame.

v5 The thoughts of the righteous [are] justice, The counsels of the wicked -- deceit.

v6 The words of the wicked [are]: 'Lay wait for blood,' And the mouth of the upright delivereth them.

v7 Overthrow the wicked, and they are not, And the house of the righteous standeth.

v8 According to his wisdom is a man praised, And the perverted of heart becometh despised.

v9 Better [is] the lightly esteemed who hath a servant, Than the self-honoured who lacketh bread.

v10 The righteous knoweth the life of his beast, And the mercies of the wicked [are] cruel.

v11 Whoso is tilling the ground is satisfied [with] bread, And whoso is pursuing vanities is lacking heart,

v12 The wicked hath desired the net of evil doers, And the root of the righteous giveth.

v13 In transgression of the lips [is] the snare of the wicked, And the righteous goeth out from distress.

v14 From the fruit of the mouth [is] one satisfied [with] good, And the deed of man's hands returneth to him.

v15 The way of a fool [is] right in his own eyes, And whoso is hearkening to counsel [is] wise.

v16 The fool -- in a day is his anger known, And the prudent is covering shame.

v17 Whoso uttereth faithfulness declareth righteousness, And a false witness -- deceit.

v18 A rash speaker is like piercings of a sword, And the tongue of the wise is healing.

v19 The lip of truth is established for ever, And for a moment -- a tongue of falsehood.

v20 Deceit [is] in the heart of those devising evil, And to those counselling peace [is] joy.

v21 No iniquity is desired by the righteous, And the wicked have been full of evil.

v22 An abomination to Jehovah [are] lying lips, And stedfast doers [are] his delight.

v23 A prudent man is concealing knowledge, And the heart of fools proclaimeth folly.

v24 The hand of the diligent ruleth, And slothfulness becometh tributary.

v25 Sorrow in the heart of a man boweth down, And a good word maketh him glad.

v26 The righteous searcheth his companion, And the way of the wicked causeth them to err.

v27 The slothful roasteth not his hunting, And the wealth of a diligent man is precious.

v28 In the path of righteousness [is] life, And in the way of [that] path [is] no death!
