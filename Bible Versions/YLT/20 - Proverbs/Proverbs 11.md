# Proverbs 11 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 10|← Proverbs 10]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 12|Proverbs 12 →]]

***

v1 Balances of deceit [are] an abomination to Jehovah, And a perfect weight [is] His delight.

v2 Pride hath come, and shame cometh, And with the lowly [is] wisdom.

v3 The integrity of the upright leadeth them, And the perverseness of the treacherous destroyeth them.

v4 Wealth profiteth not in a day of wrath, And righteousness delivereth from death.

v5 The righteousness of the perfect maketh right his way, And by his wickedness doth the wicked fall.

v6 The righteousness of the upright delivereth them, And in mischief the treacherous are captured.

v7 In the death of a wicked man, hope perisheth, And the expectation of the iniquitous hath been lost.

v8 The righteous from distress is drawn out, And the wicked goeth in instead of him.

v9 With the mouth a hypocrite corrupteth his friend, And by knowledge the righteous are drawn out.

v10 In the good of the righteous a city exulteth, And in the destruction of the wicked [is] singing.

v11 By the blessing of the upright is a city exalted, And by the mouth of the wicked thrown down.

v12 Whoso is despising his neighbour lacketh heart, And a man of understanding keepeth silence.

v13 A busybody is revealing secret counsel, And the faithful of spirit is covering the matter.

v14 Without counsels do a people fall, And deliverance [is] in a multitude of counsellors.

v15 Evil [one] suffereth when he hath been surety [for] a stranger, And whoso is hating suretyship is confident.

v16 A gracious woman retaineth honour, And terrible [men] retain riches.

v17 A kind man is rewarding his own soul, And the fierce is troubling his own flesh.

v18 The wicked is getting a lying wage, And whoso is sowing righteousness -- a true reward.

v19 Rightly [is] righteousness for life, And whoso is pursuing evil -- for his own death.

v20 An abomination to Jehovah [are] the perverse of heart, And the perfect of the way [are] His delight.

v21 Hand to hand, the wicked is not acquitted, And the seed of the righteous hath escaped.

v22 A ring of gold in the nose of a sow -- A fair woman and stubborn of behaviour.

v23 The desire of the righteous [is] only good, The hope of the wicked [is] transgression.

v24 There is who is scattering, and yet is increased, And who is keeping back from uprightness, only to want.

v25 A liberal soul is made fat, And whoso is watering, he also is watered.

v26 Whoso is withholding corn, the people execrate him, And a blessing [is] for the head of him who is selling.

v27 Whoso is earnestly seeking good Seeketh a pleasing thing, And whoso is seeking evil -- it meeteth him.

v28 Whoso is confident in his wealth he falleth, And as a leaf, the righteous flourish.

v29 Whoso is troubling his own house inheriteth wind, And a servant [is] the fool to the wise of heart.

v30 The fruit of the righteous [is] a tree of life, And whoso is taking souls [is] wise.

v31 Lo, the righteous in the earth is recompensed, Surely also the wicked and the sinner!
