# Proverbs 10 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 9|← Proverbs 9]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 11|Proverbs 11 →]]

***

v1 Proverbs of Solomon. A wise son causeth a father to rejoice, And a foolish son [is] an affliction to his mother.

v2 Treasures of wickedness profit not, And righteousness delivereth from death.

v3 Jehovah causeth not the soul of the righteous to hunger, And the desire of the wicked He thrusteth away.

v4 Poor [is] he who is working -- a slothful hand, And the hand of the diligent maketh rich.

v5 Whoso is gathering in summer [is] a wise son, Whoso is sleeping in harvest [is] a son causing shame.

v6 Blessings [are] for the head of the righteous, And the mouth of the wicked cover doth violence.

v7 The remembrance of the righteous [is] for a blessing, And the name of the wicked doth rot.

v8 The wise in heart accepteth commands, And a talkative fool kicketh.

v9 Whoso is walking in integrity walketh confidently, And whoso is perverting his ways is known.

v10 Whoso is winking the eye giveth grief, And a talkative fool kicketh.

v11 A fountain of life [is] the mouth of the righteous, And the mouth of the wicked cover doth violence.

v12 Hatred awaketh contentions, And over all transgressions love covereth.

v13 In the lips of the intelligent is wisdom found, And a rod [is] for the back of him who is lacking understanding.

v14 The wise lay up knowledge, and the mouth of a fool [is] near ruin.

v15 The wealth of the rich [is] his strong city, The ruin of the poor [is] their poverty.

v16 The wage of the righteous [is] for life, The increase of the wicked for sin.

v17 A traveller to life [is] he who is keeping instruction, And whoso is forsaking rebuke is erring.

v18 Whoso is covering hatred with lying lips, And whoso is bringing out an evil report is a fool.

v19 In the abundance of words transgression ceaseth not, And whoso is restraining his lips [is] wise.

v20 The tongue of the righteous [is] chosen silver, The heart of the wicked -- as a little thing.

v21 The lips of the righteous delight many, And fools for lack of heart die.

v22 The blessing of Jehovah -- it maketh rich, And He addeth no grief with it.

v23 To execute inventions [is] as play to a fool, And wisdom to a man of understanding.

v24 The feared thing of the wicked it meeteth him, And the desire of the righteous is given.

v25 As the passing by of a hurricane, So the wicked is not, And the righteous is a foundation age-during.

v26 As vinegar to the teeth, And as smoke to the eyes, So [is] the slothful to those sending him.

v27 The fear of Jehovah addeth days, And the years of the wicked are shortened.

v28 The hope of the righteous [is] joyful, And the expectation of the wicked perisheth.

v29 The way of Jehovah [is] strength to the perfect, And ruin to workers of iniquity.

v30 The righteous to the age is not moved, And the wicked inhabit not the earth.

v31 The mouth of the righteous uttereth wisdom, And the tongue of frowardness is cut out.

v32 The lips of the righteous know a pleasing thing, And the mouth of the wicked perverseness!
