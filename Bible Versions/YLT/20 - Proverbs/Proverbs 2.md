# Proverbs 2 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 1|← Proverbs 1]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 3|Proverbs 3 →]]

***

v1 My son, if thou dost accept my sayings, And my commands dost lay up with thee,

v2 To cause thine ear to attend to wisdom, Thou inclinest thy heart to understanding,

v3 For, if for intelligence thou callest, For understanding givest forth thy voice,

v4 If thou dost seek her as silver, And as hid treasures searchest for her,

v5 Then understandest thou fear of Jehovah, And knowledge of God thou findest.

v6 For Jehovah giveth wisdom, From His mouth knowledge and understanding.

v7 Even to lay up for the upright substance, A shield for those walking uprightly.

v8 To keep the paths of judgment, And the way of His saints He preserveth.

v9 Then understandest thou righteousness, And judgment, and uprightness -- every good path.

v10 For wisdom cometh into thy heart, And knowledge to thy soul is pleasant,

v11 Thoughtfulness doth watch over thee, Understanding doth keep thee,

v12 To deliver thee from an evil way, From any speaking froward things,

v13 Who are forsaking paths of uprightness, To walk in ways of darkness,

v14 Who are rejoicing to do evil, They delight in frowardness of the wicked,

v15 Whose paths [are] crooked, Yea, they are perverted in their ways.

v16 To deliver thee from the strange woman, From the stranger who hath made smooth her sayings,

v17 Who is forsaking the guide of her youth, And the covenant of her God hath forgotten.

v18 For her house hath inclined unto death, And unto Rephaim her paths.

v19 None going in unto her turn back, Nor do they reach the paths of life.

v20 That thou dost go in the way of the good, And the paths of the righteous dost keep.

v21 For the upright do inhabit the earth, And the perfect are left in it,

v22 And the wicked from the earth are cut off, And treacherous dealers plucked out of it!
