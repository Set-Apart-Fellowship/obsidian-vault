# Proverbs 26 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 25|← Proverbs 25]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 27|Proverbs 27 →]]

***

v1 As snow in summer, and as rain in harvest, So honour [is] not comely for a fool.

v2 As a bird by wandering, as a swallow by flying, So reviling without cause doth not come.

v3 A whip is for a horse, a bridle for an ass, And a rod for the back of fools.

v4 Answer not a fool according to his folly, Lest thou be like to him -- even thou.

v5 Answer a fool according to his folly, Lest he be wise in his own eyes.

v6 He is cutting off feet, he is drinking injury, Who is sending things by the hand of a fool.

v7 Weak have been the two legs of the lame, And a parable in the mouth of fools.

v8 As one who is binding a stone in a sling, So [is] he who is giving honour to a fool.

v9 A thorn hath gone up into the hand of a drunkard, And a parable in the mouth of fools.

v10 Great [is] the Former of all, And He is rewarding a fool, And is rewarding transgressors.

v11 As a dog hath returned to its vomit, A fool is repeating his folly.

v12 Thou hast seen a man wise in his own eyes, More hope of a fool than of him!

v13 The slothful hath said, 'A lion [is] in the way, A lion [is] in the broad places.'

v14 The door turneth round on its hinge, And the slothful on his bed.

v15 The slothful hath hid his hand in a dish, He is weary of bringing it back to his mouth.

v16 Wiser [is] the slothful in his own eyes, Than seven [men] returning a reason.

v17 Laying hold on the ears of a dog, [Is] a passer-by making himself wrath for strife not his own.

v18 As [one] pretending to be feeble, Who is casting sparks, arrows, and death,

v19 So hath a man deceived his neighbour, And hath said, 'Am not I playing?'

v20 Without wood is fire going out, And without a tale-bearer, contention ceaseth,

v21 Coal to burning coals, and wood to fire, And a man of contentions to kindle strife.

v22 The words of a tale-bearer [are] as self-inflicted wounds, And they have gone down [to] the inner parts of the heart.

v23 Silver of dross spread over potsherd, [Are] burning lips and an evil heart.

v24 By his lips doth a hater dissemble, And in his heart he placeth deceit,

v25 When his voice is gracious trust not in him, For seven abominations [are] in his heart.

v26 Hatred is covered by deceit, Revealed is its wickedness in an assembly.

v27 Whoso is digging a pit falleth into it, And the roller of a stone, to him it turneth.

v28 A lying tongue hateth its bruised ones, And a flattering mouth worketh an overthrow!
