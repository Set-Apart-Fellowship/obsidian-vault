# Proverbs 22 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 21|← Proverbs 21]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 23|Proverbs 23 →]]

***

v1 A name is chosen rather than much wealth, Than silver and than gold -- good grace.

v2 Rich and poor have met together, The Maker of them all [is] Jehovah.

v3 The prudent hath seen the evil, and is hidden, And the simple have passed on, and are punished.

v4 The end of humility [is] the fear of Jehovah, Riches, and honour, and life.

v5 Thorns -- snares [are] in the way of the perverse, Whoso is keeping his soul is far from them.

v6 Give instruction to a youth about his way, Even when he is old he turneth not from it.

v7 The rich over the poor ruleth, And a servant [is] the borrower to the lender.

v8 Whoso is sowing perverseness reapeth sorrow, And the rod of his anger weareth out.

v9 The good of eye -- he is blessed, For he hath given of his bread to the poor.

v10 Cast out a scorner -- and contention goeth out, And strife and shame cease.

v11 Whoso is loving cleanness of heart, Grace [are] his lips, a king [is] his friend.

v12 The eyes of Jehovah have kept knowledge, And He overthroweth the words of the treacherous.

v13 The slothful hath said, 'A lion [is] without, In the midst of the broad places I am slain.'

v14 A deep pit [is] the mouth of strange women, The abhorred of Jehovah falleth there.

v15 Folly is bound up in the heart of a youth, The rod of chastisement putteth it far from him.

v16 He is oppressing the poor to multiply to him, He is giving to the rich -- only to want.

v17 Incline thine ear, and hear words of the wise, And thy heart set to my knowledge,

v18 For they are pleasant when thou dost keep them in thy heart, They are prepared together for thy lips.

v19 That thy trust may be in Jehovah, I caused thee to know to-day, even thou.

v20 Have I not written to thee three times With counsels and knowledge?

v21 To cause thee to know the certainty of sayings of truth, To return sayings of truth to those sending thee.

v22 Rob not the poor because he [is] poor, And bruise not the afflicted in the gate.

v23 For Jehovah pleadeth their cause, And hath spoiled the soul of their spoilers.

v24 Shew not thyself friendly with an angry man, And with a man of fury go not in,

v25 Lest thou learn his paths, And have received a snare to thy soul.

v26 Be not thou among those striking hands, Among sureties [for] burdens.

v27 If thou hast nothing to pay, Why doth he take thy bed from under thee?

v28 Remove not a border of olden times, That thy fathers have made.

v29 Hast thou seen a man speedy in his business? Before kings he doth station himself, He stations not himself before obscure men!
