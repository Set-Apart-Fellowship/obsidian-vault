# Proverbs 18 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 17|← Proverbs 17]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 19|Proverbs 19 →]]

***

v1 For [an object of] desire he who is separated doth seek, With all wisdom he intermeddleth.

v2 A fool delighteth not in understanding, But -- in uncovering his heart.

v3 With the coming of the wicked come also hath contempt, And with shame -- reproach.

v4 Deep waters [are] the words of a man's mouth, The fountain of wisdom [is] a flowing brook.

v5 Acceptance of the face of the wicked [is] not good, To turn aside the righteous in judgment.

v6 The lips of a fool enter into strife, And his mouth for stripes calleth.

v7 The mouth of a fool [is] ruin to him, And his lips [are] the snare of his soul.

v8 The words of a tale-bearer [are] as self-inflicted wounds, And they have gone down [to] the inner parts of the heart.

v9 He also that is remiss in his work, A brother he [is] to a destroyer.

v10 A tower of strength [is] the name of Jehovah, Into it the righteous runneth, and is set on high.

v11 The wealth of the rich [is] the city of his strength, And as a wall set on high in his own imagination.

v12 Before destruction the heart of man is high, And before honour [is] humility.

v13 Whoso is answering a matter before he heareth, Folly it is to him and shame.

v14 The spirit of a man sustaineth his sickness, And a smitten spirit who doth bear?

v15 The heart of the intelligent getteth knowledge, And the ear of the wise seeketh knowledge.

v16 The gift of a man maketh room for him, And before the great it leadeth him.

v17 Righteous [is] the first in his own cause, His neighbour cometh and hath searched him.

v18 The lot causeth contentions to cease, And between the mighty it separateth.

v19 A brother transgressed against is as a strong city, And contentions as the bar of a palace.

v20 From the fruit of a man's mouth is his belly satisfied, [From the] increase of his lips he is satisfied.

v21 Death and life [are] in the power of the tongue, And those loving it eat its fruit.

v22 [Whoso] hath found a wife hath found good, And bringeth out good-will from Jehovah.

v23 [With] supplications doth the poor speak, And the rich answereth fierce things.

v24 A man with friends [is] to show himself friendly, And there is a lover adhering more than a brother!
