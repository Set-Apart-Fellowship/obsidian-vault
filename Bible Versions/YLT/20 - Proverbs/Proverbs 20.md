# Proverbs 20 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 19|← Proverbs 19]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 21|Proverbs 21 →]]

***

v1 Wine [is] a scorner -- strong drink [is] noisy, And any going astray in it is not wise.

v2 The fear of a king [is] a growl as of a young lion, He who is causing him to be wroth is wronging his soul.

v3 An honour to a man is cessation from strife, And every fool intermeddleth.

v4 Because of winter the slothful plougheth not, He asketh in harvest, and there is nothing.

v5 Counsel in the heart of a man [is] deep water, And a man of understanding draweth it up.

v6 A multitude of men proclaim each his kindness, And a man of stedfastness who doth find?

v7 The righteous is walking habitually in his integrity, O the happiness of his sons after him!

v8 A king sitting on a throne of judgment, Is scattering with his eyes all evil,

v9 Who saith, 'I have purified my heart, I have been cleansed from my sin?'

v10 A stone and a stone, an ephah and an ephah, Even both of them [are] an abomination to Jehovah.

v11 Even by his actions a youth maketh himself known, Whether his work be pure or upright.

v12 A hearing ear, and a seeing eye, Jehovah hath made even both of them.

v13 Love not sleep, lest thou become poor, Open thine eyes -- be satisfied [with] bread.

v14 'Bad, bad,' saith the buyer, And going his way then he boasteth himself.

v15 Substance, gold, and a multitude of rubies, Yea, a precious vessel, [are] lips of knowledge.

v16 Take his garment when a stranger hath been surety, And for strangers pledge it.

v17 Sweet to a man [is] the bread of falsehood, And afterwards is his mouth filled [with] gravel.

v18 Purposes by counsel thou dost establish, And with plans make thou war.

v19 A revealer of secret counsels is the busybody, And for a deceiver [with] his lips make not thyself surety.

v20 Whoso is vilifying his father and his mother, Extinguished is his lamp in blackness of darkness.

v21 An inheritance gotten wrongly at first, Even its latter end is not blessed.

v22 Do not say, 'I recompense evil,' Wait for Jehovah, and He delivereth thee.

v23 An abomination to Jehovah [are] a stone and a stone, And balances of deceit [are] not good.

v24 From Jehovah [are] the steps of a man, And man -- how understandeth he his way?

v25 A snare to a man [is] he hath swallowed a holy thing, And after vows to make inquiry.

v26 A wise king is scattering the wicked, And turneth back on them the wheel.

v27 The breath of man [is] a lamp of Jehovah, Searching all the inner parts of the heart.

v28 Kindness and truth keep a king, And he hath supported by kindness his throne.

v29 The beauty of young men is their strength, And the honour of old men is grey hairs.

v30 The bandages of a wound thou removest with the evil, Also the plagues of the inner parts of the heart!
