# Proverbs 8 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 7|← Proverbs 7]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 9|Proverbs 9 →]]

***

v1 Doth not wisdom call? And understanding give forth her voice?

v2 At the head of high places by the way, Between the paths she hath stood,

v3 At the side of the gates, at the mouth of the city, The entrance of the openings, she crieth aloud,

v4 'Unto you, O men, I call, And my voice [is] unto the sons of men.

v5 Understand, ye simple ones, prudence, And ye fools, understand the heart,

v6 Hearken, for noble things I speak, And the opening of my lips [is] uprightness.

v7 For truth doth my mouth utter, And an abomination to my lips [is] wickedness.

v8 In righteousness [are] all the sayings of my mouth, Nothing in them is froward and perverse.

v9 All of them [are] plain to the intelligent, And upright to those finding knowledge.

v10 Receive my instruction, and not silver, And knowledge rather than choice gold.

v11 For better [is] wisdom than rubies, Yea, all delights are not comparable with it.

v12 I, wisdom, have dwelt with prudence, And a knowledge of devices I find out.

v13 The fear of Jehovah [is] to hate evil; Pride, and arrogance, and an evil way, And a froward mouth, I have hated.

v14 Mine [is] counsel and substance, I [am] understanding, I have might.

v15 By me kings reign, and princes decree righteousness,

v16 By me do chiefs rule, and nobles, All judges of the earth.

v17 I love those loving me, And those seeking me earnestly do find me.

v18 Wealth and honour [are] with me, Lasting substance and righteousness.

v19 Better [is] my fruit than gold, even fine gold, And mine increase than choice silver.

v20 In a path of righteousness I cause to walk, In midst of paths of judgment,

v21 To cause my lovers to inherit substance, Yea, their treasures I fill.

v22 Jehovah possessed me -- the beginning of His way, Before His works since then.

v23 From the age I was anointed, from the first, From former states of the earth.

v24 In there being no depths, I was brought forth, In there being no fountains heavy [with] waters,

v25 Before mountains were sunk, Before heights, I was brought forth.

v26 While He had not made the earth, and out-places, And the top of the dusts of the world.

v27 In His preparing the heavens I [am] there, In His decreeing a circle on the face of the deep,

v28 In His strengthening clouds above, In His making strong fountains of the deep,

v29 In His setting for the sea its limit, And the waters transgress not His command, In His decreeing the foundations of earth,

v30 Then I am near Him, a workman, And I am a delight -- day by day. Rejoicing before Him at all times,

v31 Rejoicing in the habitable part of His earth, And my delights [are] with the sons of men.

v32 And now, ye sons, hearken to me, Yea, happy are they who keep my ways.

v33 Hear instruction, and be wise, and slight not.

v34 O the happiness of the man hearkening to me, To watch at my doors day by day, To watch at the door-posts of my entrance.

v35 For whoso is finding me, hath found life, And bringeth out good-will from Jehovah.

v36 And whoso is missing me, is wronging his soul, All hating me have loved death!
