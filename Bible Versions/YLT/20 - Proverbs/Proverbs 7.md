# Proverbs 7 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 6|← Proverbs 6]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 8|Proverbs 8 →]]

***

v1 My son! keep my sayings, And my commands lay up with thee.

v2 Keep my commands, and live, And my law as the pupil of thine eye.

v3 Bind them on thy fingers, Write them on the tablet of thy heart.

v4 Say to wisdom, 'My sister Thou [art].' And cry to understanding, 'Kinswoman!'

v5 To preserve thee from a strange woman, From a stranger who hath made smooth her sayings.

v6 For, at a window of my house, Through my casement I have looked out,

v7 And I do see among the simple ones, I discern among the sons, A young man lacking understanding,

v8 Passing on in the street, near her corner, And the way [to] her house he doth step,

v9 In the twilight -- in the evening of day, In the darkness of night and blackness.

v10 And, lo, a woman to meet him -- (A harlot's dress, and watchful of heart,

v11 Noisy she [is], and stubborn, In her house her feet rest not.

v12 Now in an out-place, now in broad places, And near every corner she lieth in wait) --

v13 And she laid hold on him, and kissed him, She hath hardened her face, and saith to him,

v14 'Sacrifices of peace-offerings [are] by me, To-day I have completed my vows.

v15 Therefore I have come forth to meet thee, To seek earnestly thy face, and I find thee.

v16 [With] ornamental coverings I decked my couch, Carved works -- cotton of Egypt.

v17 I sprinkled my bed -- myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon.

v18 Come, we are filled [with] loves till the morning, We delight ourselves in loves.

v19 For the man is not in his house, He hath gone on a long journey.

v20 A bag of money he hath taken in his hand, At the day of the new moon he cometh to his house.'

v21 She turneth him aside with the abundance of her speech, With the flattery of her lips she forceth him.

v22 He is going after her straightway, As an ox unto the slaughter he cometh, And as a fetter unto the chastisement of a fool,

v23 Till an arrow doth split his liver, As a bird hath hastened unto a snare, And hath not known that it [is] for its life.

v24 And now, ye sons, hearken to me, And give attention to sayings of my mouth.

v25 Let not thy heart turn unto her ways, Do not wander in her paths,

v26 For many [are] the wounded she caused to fall, And mighty [are] all her slain ones.

v27 The ways of Sheol -- her house, Going down unto inner chambers of death!
