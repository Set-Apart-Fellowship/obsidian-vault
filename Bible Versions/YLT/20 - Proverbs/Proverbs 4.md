# Proverbs 4 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 3|← Proverbs 3]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 5|Proverbs 5 →]]

***

v1 Hear, ye sons, the instruction of a father, And give attention to know understanding.

v2 For good learning I have given to you, My law forsake not.

v3 For, a son I have been to my father -- tender, And an only one before my mother.

v4 And he directeth me, and he saith to me: 'Let thy heart retain my words, Keep my commands, and live.

v5 Get wisdom, get understanding, Do not forget, nor turn away From the sayings of my mouth.

v6 Forsake her not, and she doth preserve thee, Love her, and she doth keep thee.

v7 The first thing [is] wisdom -- get wisdom, And with all thy getting get understanding.

v8 Exalt her, and she doth lift thee up, She honoureth thee, when thou dost embrace her.

v9 She giveth to thy head a wreath of grace, A crown of beauty she doth give thee freely.

v10 Hear, my son, and receive my sayings, And years of life [are] multiplied to thee.

v11 In a way of wisdom I have directed thee, I have caused thee to tread in paths of uprightness.

v12 In thy walking thy step is not straitened, And if thou runnest, thou stumblest not.

v13 Lay hold on instruction, do not desist, Keep her, for she [is] thy life.

v14 Into the path of the wicked enter not, And be not happy in a way of evil doers.

v15 Avoid it, pass not over into it, Turn aside from it, and pass on.

v16 For they sleep not if they do not evil, And their sleep hath been taken violently away, If they cause not [some] to stumble.

v17 For they have eaten bread of wickedness, And wine of violence they drink.

v18 And the path of the righteous [is] as a shining light, Going and brightening till the day is established,

v19 The way of the wicked [is] as darkness, They have not known at what they stumble.

v20 My son, to my words give attention, To my sayings incline thine ear,

v21 Let them not turn aside from thine eyes, Preserve them in the midst of thy heart.

v22 For life they [are] to those finding them, And to all their flesh healing.

v23 Above every charge keep thy heart, For out of it [are] the outgoings of life.

v24 Turn aside from thee a froward mouth, And perverse lips put far from thee,

v25 Thine eyes do look straightforward, And thine eyelids look straight before thee.

v26 Ponder thou the path of thy feet, And all thy ways [are] established.

v27 Incline not [to] the right or to the left, Turn aside thy foot from evil!
