# Proverbs 30 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 29|← Proverbs 29]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 31|Proverbs 31 →]]

***

v1 Words of a Gatherer, son of an obedient one, the declaration, an affirmation of the man: -- I have wearied myself [for] God, I have wearied myself [for] God, and am consumed.

v2 For I am more brutish than any one, And have not the understanding of a man.

v3 Nor have I learned wisdom, Yet the knowledge of Holy Ones I know.

v4 Who went up to heaven, and cometh down? Who hath gathered the wind in his fists? Who hath bound waters in a garment? Who established all ends of the earth? What [is] His name? and what His son's name? Surely thou knowest!

v5 Every saying of God [is] tried, A shield He [is] to those trusting in Him.

v6 Add not to His words, lest He reason with thee, And thou hast been found false.

v7 Two things I have asked from Thee, Withhold not from me before I die.

v8 Vanity and a lying word put far from me, Poverty or wealth give not to me, Cause me to eat the bread of my portion,

v9 Lest I become satiated, and have denied, And have said, 'Who [is] Jehovah?' And lest I be poor, and have stolen, And have laid hold of the name of my God.

v10 Accuse not a servant unto his lord, Lest he disesteem thee, and thou be found guilty.

v11 A generation [is], that lightly esteemeth their father, And their mother doth not bless.

v12 A generation -- pure in their own eyes, But from their own filth not washed.

v13 A generation -- how high are their eyes, Yea, their eyelids are lifted up.

v14 A generation -- swords [are] their teeth, And knives -- their jaw-teeth, To consume the poor from earth, And the needy from [among] men.

v15 To the leech [are] two daughters, 'Give, give, Lo, three things are not satisfied, Four have not said 'Sufficiency;'

v16 Sheol, and a restrained womb, Earth -- it [is] not satisfied [with] water, And fire -- it hath not said, 'Sufficiency,'

v17 An eye that mocketh at a father, And despiseth to obey a mother, Dig it out do ravens of the valley, And eat it do young eagles.

v18 Three things have been too wonderful for me, Yea, four that I have not known:

v19 The way of the eagle in the heavens, The way of a serpent on a rock, The way of a ship in the heart of the sea, And the way of a man in youth.

v20 So -- the way of an adulterous woman, She hath eaten and hath wiped her mouth, And hath said, 'I have not done iniquity.'

v21 For three things hath earth been troubled, And for four -- it is not able to bear:

v22 For a servant when he reigneth, And a fool when he is satisfied with bread,

v23 For a hated one when she ruleth, And a maid-servant when she succeedeth her mistress.

v24 Four [are] little ones of earth, And they are made wiser than the wise:

v25 The ants [are] a people not strong, And they prepare in summer their food,

v26 Conies [are] a people not strong, And they place in a rock their house,

v27 A king there is not to the locust, And it goeth out -- each one shouting,

v28 A spider with two hands taketh hold, And is in the palaces of a king.

v29 Three there are going well, Yea, four are good in going:

v30 An old lion -- mighty among beasts, That turneth not back from the face of any,

v31 A girt one of the loins, or a he-goat, And a king -- no rising up with him.

v32 If thou hast been foolish in lifting up thyself, And if thou hast devised evil -- hand to mouth!

v33 For the churning of milk bringeth out butter, And the wringing of the nose bringeth out blood, And the forcing of anger bringeth out strife!
