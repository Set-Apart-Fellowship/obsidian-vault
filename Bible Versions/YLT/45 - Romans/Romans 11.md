# Romans 11 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/45 - Romans/Romans 10|← Romans 10]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/45 - Romans/Romans]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/45 - Romans/Romans 12|Romans 12 →]]

***

v1 I say, then, Did God cast away His people? let it not be! for I also am an Israelite, of the seed of Abraham, of the tribe of Benjamin:

v2 God did not cast away His people whom He knew before; have ye not known -- in Elijah -- what the Writing saith? how he doth plead with God concerning Israel, saying,

v3 'Lord, Thy prophets they did kill, and Thy altars they dug down, and I was left alone, and they seek my life;'

v4 but what saith the divine answer to him? 'I left to Myself seven thousand men, who did not bow a knee to Baal.'

v5 So then also in the present time a remnant according to the choice of grace there hath been;

v6 and if by grace, no more of works, otherwise the grace becometh no more grace; and if of works, it is no more grace, otherwise the work is no more work.

v7 What then? What Israel doth seek after, this it did not obtain, and the chosen did obtain, and the rest were hardened,

v8 according as it hath been written, 'God gave to them a spirit of deep sleep, eyes not to see, and ears not to hear,' -- unto this very day,

v9 and David saith, 'Let their table become for a snare, and for a trap, and for a stumbling-block, and for a recompense to them;

v10 let their eyes be darkened -- not to behold, and their back do Thou always bow down.'

v11 I say, then, Did they stumble that they might fall? let it not be! but by their fall the salvation [is] to the nations, to arouse them to jealousy;

v12 and if the fall of them [is] the riches of a world, and the diminution of them the riches of nations, how much more the fulness of them?

v13 For to you I speak -- to the nations -- inasmuch as I am indeed an apostle of nations, my ministration I do glorify;

v14 if by any means I shall arouse to jealousy mine own flesh, and shall save some of them,

v15 for if the casting away of them [is] a reconciliation of the world, what the reception -- if not life out of the dead?

v16 and if the first-fruit [is] holy, the lump also; and if the root [is] holy, the branches also.

v17 And if certain of the branches were broken off, and thou, being a wild olive tree, wast graffed in among them, and a fellow-partaker of the root and of the fatness of the olive tree didst become --

v18 do not boast against the branches; and if thou dost boast, thou dost not bear the root, but the root thee!

v19 Thou wilt say, then, 'The branches were broken off, that I might be graffed in;' right!

v20 by unbelief they were broken off, and thou hast stood by faith; be not high-minded, but be fearing;

v21 for if God the natural branches did not spare -- lest perhaps He also shall not spare thee.

v22 Lo, then, goodness and severity of God -- upon those indeed who fell, severity; and upon thee, goodness, if thou mayest remain in the goodness, otherwise, thou also shalt be cut off.

v23 And those also, if they may not remain in unbelief, shall be graffed in, for God is able again to graff them in;

v24 for if thou, out of the olive tree, wild by nature, wast cut out, and, contrary to nature, wast graffed into a good olive tree, how much rather shall they, who [are] according to nature, be graffed into their own olive tree?

v25 For I do not wish you to be ignorant, brethren, of this secret -- that ye may not be wise in your own conceits -- that hardness in part to Israel hath happened till the fulness of the nations may come in;

v26 and so all Israel shall be saved, according as it hath been written, 'There shall come forth out of Sion he who is delivering, and he shall turn away impiety from Jacob,

v27 and this to them [is] the covenant from Me, when I may take away their sins.'

v28 As regards, indeed, the good tidings, [they are] enemies on your account; and as regards the choice -- beloved on account of the fathers;

v29 for unrepented of [are] the gifts and the calling of God;

v30 for as ye also once did not believe in God, and now did find kindness by the unbelief of these:

v31 so also these now did not believe, that in your kindness they also may find kindness;

v32 for God did shut up together the whole to unbelief, that to the whole He might do kindness.

v33 O depth of riches, and wisdom and knowledge of God! how unsearchable His judgments, and untraceable His ways!

v34 for who did know the mind of the Lord? or who did become His counsellor?

v35 or who did first give to Him, and it shall be given back to him again?

v36 because of Him, and through Him, and to Him [are] the all things; to Him [is] the glory -- to the ages. Amen.
