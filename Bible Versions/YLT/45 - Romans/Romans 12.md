# Romans 12 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/45 - Romans/Romans 11|← Romans 11]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/45 - Romans/Romans]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/45 - Romans/Romans 13|Romans 13 →]]

***

v1 I call upon you, therefore, brethren, through the compassions of God, to present your bodies a sacrifice -- living, sanctified, acceptable to God -- your intelligent service;

v2 and be not conformed to this age, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind, for your proving what [is] the will of God -- the good, and acceptable, and perfect.

v3 For I say, through the grace that was given to me, to every one who is among you, not to think above what it behoveth to think; but to think so as to think wisely, as to each God did deal a measure of faith,

v4 for as in one body we have many members, and all the members have not the same office,

v5 so we, the many, one body are in Christ, and members each one of one another.

v6 And having gifts, different according to the grace that was given to us; whether prophecy -- 'According to the proportion of faith!'

v7 or ministration -- 'In the ministration!' or he who is teaching -- 'In the teaching!'

v8 or he who is exhorting -- 'In the exhortation!' he who is sharing -- 'In simplicity!' he who is leading -- 'In diligence?' he who is doing kindness -- 'In cheerfulness.'

v9 The love unfeigned: abhorring the evil; cleaving to the good;

v10 in the love of brethren, to one another kindly affectioned: in the honour going before one another;

v11 in the diligence not slothful; in the spirit fervent; the Lord serving;

v12 in the hope rejoicing; in the tribulation enduring; in the prayer persevering;

v13 to the necessities of the saints communicating; the hospitality pursuing.

v14 Bless those persecuting you; bless, and curse not;

v15 to rejoice with the rejoicing, and to weep with the weeping,

v16 of the same mind one toward another, not minding the high things, but with the lowly going along; become not wise in your own conceit;

v17 giving back to no one evil for evil; providing right things before all men.

v18 If possible -- so far as in you -- with all men being in peace;

v19 not avenging yourselves, beloved, but give place to the wrath, for it hath been written, 'Vengeance [is] Mine,

v20 I will recompense again, saith the Lord;' if, then, thine enemy doth hunger, feed him; if he doth thirst, give him drink; for this doing, coals of fire thou shalt heap upon his head;

v21 Be not overcome by the evil, but overcome, in the good, the evil.
