# Romans 3 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/45 - Romans/Romans 2|← Romans 2]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/45 - Romans/Romans]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/45 - Romans/Romans 4|Romans 4 →]]

***

v1 What, then, [is] the superiority of the Jew? or what the profit of the circumcision?

v2 much in every way; for first, indeed, that they were intrusted with the oracles of God;

v3 for what, if certain were faithless? shall their faithlessness the faithfulness of god make useless?

v4 let it not be! and let God become true, and every man false, according as it hath been written, 'That Thou mayest be declared righteous in Thy words, and mayest overcome in Thy being judged.'

v5 And, if our unrighteousness God's righteousness doth establish, what shall we say? is God unrighteous who is inflicting the wrath? (after the manner of a man I speak)

v6 let it not be! since how shall God judge the world?

v7 for if the truth of God in my falsehood did more abound to His glory, why yet am I also as a sinner judged?

v8 and not, as we are evil spoken of, and as certain affirm us to say -- 'We may do the evil things, that the good ones may come?' whose judgment is righteous.

v9 What, then? are we better? not at all! for we did before charge both Jews and Greeks with being all under sin,

v10 according as it hath been written -- 'There is none righteous, not even one;

v11 There is none who is understanding, there is none who is seeking after God.

v12 All did go out of the way, together they became unprofitable, there is none doing good, there is not even one.

v13 A sepulchre opened [is] their throat; with their tongues they used deceit; poison of asps [is] under their lips.

v14 Whose mouth is full of cursing and bitterness.

v15 Swift [are] their feet to shed blood.

v16 Ruin and misery [are] in their ways.

v17 And a way of peace they did not know.

v18 There is no fear of God before their eyes.'

v19 And we have known that as many things as the law saith, to those in the law it doth speak, that every mouth may be stopped, and all the world may come under judgment to God;

v20 wherefore by works of law shall no flesh be declared righteous before Him, for through law is a knowledge of sin.

v21 And now apart from law hath the righteousness of God been manifested, testified to by the law and the prophets,

v22 and the righteousness of God [is] through the faith of Jesus Christ to all, and upon all those believing, -- for there is no difference,

v23 for all did sin, and are come short of the glory of God --

v24 being declared righteous freely by His grace through the redemption that [is] in Christ Jesus,

v25 whom God did set forth a mercy seat, through the faith in his blood, for the shewing forth of His righteousness, because of the passing over of the bygone sins in the forbearance of God --

v26 for the shewing forth of His righteousness in the present time, for His being righteous, and declaring him righteous who [is] of the faith of Jesus.

v27 Where then [is] the boasting? it was excluded; by what law? of works? no, but by a law of faith:

v28 therefore do we reckon a man to be declared righteous by faith, apart from works of law.

v29 The God of Jews only [is He], and not also of nations?

v30 yes, also of nations; since one [is] God who shall declare righteous the circumcision by faith, and the uncircumcision through the faith.

v31 Law then do we make useless through the faith? let it not be! yea, we do establish law.
