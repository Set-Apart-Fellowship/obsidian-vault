# Nehemiah 7 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 6|← Nehemiah 6]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 8|Nehemiah 8 →]]

***

v1 And it cometh to pass, when the wall hath been built, that I set up the doors, and the gatekeepers are appointed, and the singers, and the Levites,

v2 and I charge Hanani my brother, and Hananiah head of the palace, concerning Jerusalem -- for he [is] as a man of truth, and fearing God above many --

v3 and I say to them, 'Let not the gates of Jerusalem be opened till the heat of the sun, and while they are standing by let them shut the doors, and fasten, and appoint guards of the inhabitants of Jerusalem, each in his guard, and each over-against his house.'

v4 And the city [is] broad on both sides, and great, and the people [are] few in its midst, and there are no houses builded;

v5 and my God putteth it unto my heart, and I gather the freeman, and the prefects, and the people, for the genealogy, and I find a book of the genealogy of those coming up at the beginning, and I find written in it: --

v6 These [are] sons of the province, those coming up of the captives of the removal that Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon removed -- and they turn back to Jerusalem and to Judah, each to his city --

v7 who are coming in with Zerubbabel, Jeshua, Nehemiah, Azariah, Raamiah, Nahamani, Mordecai, Bilshan, Mispereth, Bigvai, Nehum, Baanah. Number of the men of the people of Israel:

v8 Sons of Parosh: two thousand a hundred and seventy and two.

v9 Sons of Shephatiah: three hundred seventy and two.

v10 Sons of Arah: six hundred fifty and two.

v11 Sons of Pahath-Moab, of the sons of Jeshua and Joab: two thousand and eight hundred [and] eighteen.

v12 Sons of Elam: a thousand two hundred fifty and four.

v13 Sons of Zattu: eight hundred forty and five.

v14 Sons of Zaccai: seven hundred and sixty.

v15 Sons of Binnui: six hundred forty and eight.

v16 Sons of Bebai: six hundred twenty and eight.

v17 Sons of Azgad: two thousand three hundred twenty and two.

v18 Sons of Adonikam: six hundred sixty and seven.

v19 Sons of Bigvai: two thousand sixty and seven.

v20 Sons of Adin: six hundred fifty and five.

v21 Sons of Ater of Hezekiah: ninety and eight.

v22 Sons of Hashum: three hundred twenty and eight.

v23 Sons of Bezai: three hundred twenty and four.

v24 Sons of Hariph: a hundred [and] twelve.

v25 Sons of Gibeon: ninety and five.

v26 Men of Beth-Lehem and Netophah: a hundred eighty and eight.

v27 Men of Anathoth: a hundred twenty and eight.

v28 Men of Beth-Azmaveth: forty and two.

v29 Men of Kirjath-Jearim, Chephirah, and Beeroth: seven hundred forty and three.

v30 Men of Ramah and Gaba: six hundred twenty and one.

v31 Men of Michmas: a hundred and twenty and two.

v32 Men of Bethel and Ai: a hundred twenty and three.

v33 Men of the other Nebo: fifty and two.

v34 Sons of the other Elam: a thousand two hundred fifty and four.

v35 Sons of Harim: three hundred and twenty.

v36 Sons of Jericho: three hundred forty and five.

v37 Sons of Lod, Hadid, and Ono: seven hundred and twenty and one.

v38 Sons of Senaah: three thousand nine hundred and thirty.

v39 The priests: sons of Jedaiah: of the house of Jeshua: nine hundred seventy and three;

v40 sons of Immer: a thousand fifty and two;

v41 sons of Pashur: a thousand two hundred forty and seven;

v42 sons of Harim: a thousand and seventeen.

v43 The Levites: sons of Jeshua, of Kadmiel: of sons of Hodevah: seventy and four.

v44 The singers: sons of Asaph: a hundred forty and eight.

v45 The gatekeepers: sons of Shallum, sons of Ater, sons of Talmon, sons of Akkub, sons of Hatita, sons of Shobai: a hundred thirty and eight.

v46 The Nethinim: sons of Ziha, sons of Hasupha, sons of Tabbaoth,

v47 sons of Keros, sons of Sia, sons of Padon,

v48 sons of Lebanah, sons of Hagaba, sons of Shalmai,

v49 sons of Hanan, sons of Giddel, sons of Gahar,

v50 sons of Reaiah, sons of Rezin, sons of Nekoda,

v51 sons of Gazzam, sons of Uzza, sons of Phaseah,

v52 sons of Bezai, sons of Meunim, sons of Nephishesim,

v53 sons of Bakbuk, sons of Hakupha, sons of Harhur,

v54 sons of Bazlith, sons of Mehida, sons of Harsha,

v55 sons of Barkos, sons of Sisera, sons of Tamah,

v56 sons of Neziah, sons of Hatipha.

v57 Sons of the servants of Solomon: sons of Sotai, sons of Sophereth, sons of Perida,

v58 sons of Jaala, sons of Darkon, sons of Giddel,

v59 sons of Shephatiah, sons of Hattil, sons of Pochereth of Zebaim, sons of Amon.

v60 All the Nethinim and the sons of the servants of Solomon [are] three hundred ninety and two.

v61 And these [are] those coming up from Tel-Melah, Tel-Harsha, Cherub, Addon, and Immer -- and they have not been able to declare the house of their fathers, and their seed, whether they [are] of Israel --

v62 sons of Delaiah, sons of Tobiah, sons of Nekoda, six hundred forty and two.

v63 And of the priests: sons of Habaiah sons of Koz, sons of Barzillai, who hath taken from the daughters of Barzillai the Gileadite a wife, and is called by their name.

v64 These have sought their register among those reckoning themselves by genealogy, and it hath not been found, and they are redeemed from the priesthood,

v65 and the Tirshatha saith to them that they eat not of the most holy things till the standing up of the priest with Urim and Thummim.

v66 All the assembly together [is] four myriads two thousand three hundred and sixty,

v67 apart from their servants and their handmaids -- these [are] seven thousand three hundred thirty and seven; and of them [are] singers and songstresses, two hundred forty and five.

v68 Their horses [are] seven hundred thirty and six; their mules, two hundred [and] forty and five;

v69 camels, four hundred thirty and five; asses, six thousand seven hundred and twenty.

v70 And from the extremity of the heads of the fathers they have given to the work; the Tirshatha hath given to the treasure, of gold, drams a thousand, bowls fifty, priests' coats thirty and five hundred.

v71 And of the heads of the fathers they have given to the treasure of the work, of gold, drams two myriads, and of silver, pounds two thousand and two hundred.

v72 And that which the rest of the people have given [is] of gold, drams two myriads, and of silver, pounds two thousand, and of priests coats, sixty and seven.

v73 And they dwell -- the priests, and the Levites, and the gatekeepers, and the singers, and [some] of the people, and the Nethinim, and all Israel -- in their cities, and the seventh month cometh, and the sons of Israel [are] in their cities.
