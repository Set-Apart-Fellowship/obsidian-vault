# Nehemiah 11 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 10|← Nehemiah 10]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 12|Nehemiah 12 →]]

***

v1 And the heads of the people dwell in Jerusalem, and the rest of the people have caused to fall lots to bring in one out of ten to dwell in Jerusalem the holy city, and nine parts in the cities,

v2 and the people give a blessing to all the men who are offering themselves willingly to dwell in Jerusalem.

v3 And these [are] heads of the province who have dwelt in Jerusalem, and in cities of Judah, they have dwelt each in his possession in their cities; Israel, the priests, and the Levites, and the Nethinim, and the sons of the servants of Solomon.

v4 And in Jerusalem have dwelt of the sons of Judah, and of the sons of Benjamin. Of the sons of Judah: Athaiah son of Uzziah, son of Zechariah, son of Amariah, son of Shephatiah, son of Mahalaleel, of the sons of Perez;

v5 and Masseiah son of Baruch, son of Col-Hozeh, son of Hazaiah, son of Adaiah, son of Joiarib, son of Zechariah, son of Shiloni;

v6 all the sons of Perez who are dwelling in Jerusalem [are] four hundred sixty and eight, men of valour.

v7 And these [are] sons of Benjamin: Sallu son of Meshullam, son of Joed, son of Pedaiah, son of Kolaiah, son of Maaseiah, son of Ithiel, son of Jesaiah;

v8 and after him Gabbai, Sallai, nine hundred twenty and eight.

v9 And Joel son of Zichri [is] inspector over them, and Judah son of Senuah [is] over the city -- second.

v10 Of the priests: Jedaiah son of Joiarib, Jachin,

v11 Seraiah son of Hilkiah, son of Meshullam, son of Zadok, son of Meraioth, son of Ahitub, leader of the house of God,

v12 and their brethren doing the work of the house [are] eight hundred twenty and two; and Adaiah son of Jeroham, son of Pelaliah, son of Amzi, son of Zechariah, son of Pashhur, son of Malchiah,

v13 and his brethren, heads of fathers, two hundred forty and two; and Amashsai son of Azareel, son of Ahazai, son of Meshillemoth, son of Immer,

v14 and their brethren, mighty of valour, a hundred twenty and eight; and an inspector over them [is] Zabdiel, son of [one of] the great men.

v15 And of the Levites: Shemaiah son of Hashub, son of Azrikam, son of Hashabiah, son of Bunni,

v16 and Shabbethai, and Jozabad, [are] over the outward work of the house of God, of the heads of the Levites,

v17 and Mattaniah son of Micha, son of Zabdi, son of Asaph, [is] head -- at the commencement he giveth thanks in prayer; and Bakbukiah [is] second among his brethren, and Abda son of Shammua, son of Galal, son of Jeduthun.

v18 All the Levites, in the holy city, [are] two hundred eighty and four.

v19 And the gatekeepers, Akkub, Talmon, and their brethren, those watching at the gates, [are] a hundred seventy and two.

v20 And the rest of Israel, of the priests, of the Levites, [are] in all cities of Judah, each in his inheritance;

v21 and the Nethinim are dwelling in Ophel, and Ziha and Gishpa [are] over the Nethinim.

v22 And the overseer of the Levites in Jerusalem [is] Uzzi son of Bani, son of Hashabiah, son of Mattaniah, son of Micha: of the sons of Asaph, the singers [are] over-against the work of the house of God,

v23 for the command of the king [is] upon them, and support [is] for the singers, a matter of a day in its day.

v24 And Pethahiah son of Meshezabeel, of the sons of Zerah, son of Judah, [is] by the hand of the king, for every matter of the people.

v25 And at the villages with their fields, of the sons of Judah there have dwelt, in Kirjath-Arba and its small towns, and in Dibon and its small towns, and in Jekabzeel and its villages,

v26 and in Jeshua, and in Moladah, and in Beth-Phelet,

v27 and in Hazar-Shaul, and in Beer-Sheba and its small towns,

v28 and in Ziklag, and in Mekonah and in its small towns,

v29 and En-Rimmon, and in Zareah, and in Jarmuth,

v30 Zanoah, Adullam, and their villages, Lachish and its fields, Azekah and its small towns; and they encamp from Beer-Sheba unto the valley of Hinnom.

v31 And sons of Benjamin [are] at Geba, Michmash, and Aija, and Beth-El, and its small towns,

v32 Anathoth, Nob, Ananiah,

v33 Hazor, Ramah, Gittaim,

v34 Hadid, Zeboim, Neballat,

v35 Lod, and Ono, the valley of the artificers.

v36 And of the Levites, the courses of Judah [are] for Benjamin.
