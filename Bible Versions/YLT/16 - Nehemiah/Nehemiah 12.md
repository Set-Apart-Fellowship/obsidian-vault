# Nehemiah 12 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 11|← Nehemiah 11]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 13|Nehemiah 13 →]]

***

v1 And these [are] the priests and the Levites who came up with Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, and Jeshua; Seraiah, Jeremiah, Ezra,

v2 Amariah, Malluch, Hattush,

v3 Shechaniah, Rehum, Meremoth,

v4 Iddo, Ginnethoi, Abijah,

v5 Miamin, Maadiah, Bilgah,

v6 Shemaiah, and Joiarib, Jedaiah,

v7 Sallu, Amok, Hilkiah, Jedaiah; these [are] heads of the priests and of their brethren in the days of Jeshua.

v8 And the Levites [are] Jeshua, Binnui, Kadmiel, Sherebiah, Judah, Mattaniah, he [is] over the thanksgiving, and his brethren,

v9 and Bakbukiah and Unni, their brethren, [are] over-against them in charges.

v10 And Jeshua hath begotten Joiakim, and Joiakim hath begotten Eliashib, and Eliashib hath begotten Joiada,

v11 and Joiada hath begotten Jonathan, and Jonathan hath begotten Jaddua.

v12 And in the days of Joiakim have been priests, heads of the fathers; of Seraiah, Meraiah; of Jeremiah, Hananiah;

v13 of Ezra, Meshullam; of Amariah, Jehohanan;

v14 of Melicu, Jonathan; of Shebaniah, Joseph;

v15 of Harim, Adna; of Meraioth, Helkai;

v16 of Iddo, Zechariah; of Ginnethon, Meshullam;

v17 of Abijah, Zichri; of Miniamin; of Moadiah, Piltai;

v18 of Bilgah, Shammua; of Shemaiah, Jehonathan;

v19 and of Joiarib, Mattenai; of Jedaiah, Uzzi;

v20 of Sallai, Kallai; of Amok, Eber;

v21 of Hilkiah, Hashabiah; of Jedaiah, Nethaneel.

v22 The Levites, in the days of Eliashib, Joiada, and Johanan, and Jaddua, are written, heads of fathers, and of the priests, in the kingdom of Darius the Persian.

v23 Sons of Levi, heads of the fathers, are written on the book of the Chronicles even till the days of Johanan son of Eliashib;

v24 and heads of the Levites: Hashabiah, Sherebiah, and Jeshua son of Kadmiel, and their brethren, [are] over-against them, to give praise, to give thanks, by command of David the man of God, charge over-against charge.

v25 Mattaniah, and Bakbukiah, Obadiah, Meshullam, Talmon, Akkub, [are] gatekeepers, keeping charge in the gatherings of the gates.

v26 These [are] in the days of Joiakim son of Jeshua, son of Jozadak, and in the days of Nehemiah the governor, and of Ezra the priest, the scribe.

v27 And at the dedication of the wall of Jerusalem they sought the Levites out of all their places, to bring them in to Jerusalem, to make the dedication even with gladness, and with thanksgivings, and with singing, [with] cymbals, psalteries, and with harps;

v28 and sons of the singers are gathered together even from the circuit round about Jerusalem, and from the villages of Netophathi,

v29 and from the house of Gilgal, and from fields of Geba and Azmaveth, for villages have the singers built for themselves round about Jerusalem;

v30 and the priests and the Levites are cleansed, and they cleanse the people, and the gates, and the wall.

v31 And I bring up the heads of Judah upon the wall, and appoint two great thanksgiving companies and processions. At the right, on the wall, to the dung-gate;

v32 and after them goeth Hoshaiah, and half of the heads of Judah,

v33 and Azariah, Ezra, and Meshullam,

v34 Judah, and Benjamin, and Shemaiah, and Jeremiah;

v35 and of the sons of the priests with trumpets, Zechariah son of Jonathan, son of Shemaiah, son of Mattaniah, son of Michaiah, son of Zaccur, son of Asaph,

v36 and his brethren Shemaiah, and Azarael, Milalai, Gilalai, Maai, Nethaneel, and Judah, Hanani, with instruments of song of David the man of God, and Ezra the scribe [is] before them;

v37 and by the gate of the fountain and over-against them, they have gone up by the steps of the city of David, at the going up of the wall beyond the house of David, and unto the water-gate eastward.

v38 And the second thanksgiving company that is going over-against, and I after it, and half of the people on the wall from beyond the tower of the furnaces and unto the broad wall,

v39 and from beyond the gate of Ephraim, and by the old-gate, and by the fish-gate, and the tower of Hananeel, and the tower of Meah, and unto the sheep-gate -- and they have stood at the prison-gate.

v40 And the two thanksgiving companies stand in the house of God, and I and half of the prefects with me,

v41 and the priests, Eliakim, Maaseiah, Miniamin, Michaiah, Elioenai, Zechariah, Hananiah, with trumpets,

v42 and Masseiah, and Shemaiah, and Eleazar, and Uzzi, and Jehohanan, and Malchijah, and Elam, and Ezer, and the singers sound, and Jezrahiah the inspector;

v43 and they sacrifice on that day great sacrifices and rejoice, for God hath made them rejoice [with] great joy, and also, the women and the children have rejoiced, and the joy of Jerusalem is heard -- unto a distance.

v44 And certain are appointed on that day over the chambers for treasures, for heave-offerings, for first-fruits, and for tithes, to gather into them out of the fields of the cities the portions of the law for priests, and for Levites, for the joy of Judah [is] over the priests, and over the Levites, who are standing up.

v45 And the singers and the gatekeepers keep the charge of their God, even the charge of the cleansing -- according to the command of David [and] Solomon his son,

v46 for in the days of David and Asaph of old [were] heads of the singers, and a song of praise and thanksgiving to God.

v47 And all Israel in the days of Zerubbabel, and in the days of Nehemiah, are giving the portions of the singers, and of the gatekeepers, the matter of a day in its day, and are sanctifying to the Levites, and the Levites are sanctifying to the sons of Aaron.
