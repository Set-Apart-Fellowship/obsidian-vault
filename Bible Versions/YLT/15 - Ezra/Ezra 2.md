# Ezra 2 (Young's Literal Translation)

[[Bible Versions/YLT/15 - Ezra/Ezra 1|← Ezra 1]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/15 - Ezra/Ezra]] | [[Bible Versions/YLT/15 - Ezra/Ezra 3|Ezra 3 →]]

***

v1 And these [are] sons of the province who are going up -- of the captives of the removal that Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon removed to Babylon, and they turn back to Jerusalem and Judah, each to his city --

v2 who have come in with Zerubbabel, Jeshua, Nehemiah, Seraiah, Reelaiah, Mordecai, Bilshan, Mispar, Bigvai, Rehum, Baanah: The number of the men of the people of Israel:

v3 Sons of Parosh, two thousand a hundred seventy and two.

v4 Sons of Shephatiah, three hundred seventy and two.

v5 Sons of Arah, seven hundred five and seventy.

v6 Sons of Pahath-Moab, of the sons of Jeshua, Joab, two thousand eight hundred and twelve.

v7 Sons of Elam, a thousand two hundred fifty and four.

v8 Sons of Zattu, nine hundred and forty and five.

v9 Sons of Zaccai, seven hundred and sixty.

v10 Sons of Bani, six hundred forty and two.

v11 Sons of Bebai, six hundred twenty and three.

v12 Sons of Azgad, a thousand two hundred twenty and two.

v13 Sons of Adonikam, six hundred sixty and six.

v14 Sons of Bigvai, two thousand fifty and six.

v15 Sons of Adin, four hundred fifty and four.

v16 Sons of Ater of Hezekiah, ninety and eight.

v17 Sons of Bezai, three hundred twenty and three.

v18 Sons of Jorah, a hundred and twelve.

v19 Sons of Hashum, two hundred twenty and three.

v20 Sons of Gibbar, ninety and five.

v21 Sons of Beth-Lehem, a hundred twenty and three.

v22 Men of Netophah, fifty and six.

v23 Men of Anathoth, a hundred twenty and eight.

v24 Sons of Azmaveth, forty and two.

v25 Sons of Kirjath-Arim, Chephirah, and Beeroth, seven hundred and forty and three.

v26 Sons of Ramah and Gaba, six hundred twenty and one.

v27 Men of Michmas, a hundred twenty and two.

v28 Men of Beth-El and Ai, two hundred twenty and three.

v29 Sons of Nebo, fifty and two.

v30 Sons of Magbish, a hundred fifty and six.

v31 Sons of another Elam, a thousand two hundred fifty and four.

v32 Sons of Harim, three hundred and twenty.

v33 Sons of Lod, Hadid, and Ono, seven hundred twenty and five.

v34 Sons of Jericho, three hundred forty and five.

v35 Sons of Senaah, three thousand and six hundred and thirty.

v36 The Priests: sons of Jedaiah, of the house of Jeshua, nine hundred seventy and three.

v37 Sons of Imner, a thousand fifty and two.

v38 Sons of Pashhur, a thousand two hundred forty and seven.

v39 Sons of Harim, a thousand and seventeen.

v40 The Levites; sons of Jeshua and Kadmiel, of the sons of Hodaviah, seventy and four.

v41 The singers: sons of Asaph, a hundred twenty and eight.

v42 Sons of the gatekeepers; sons of Shallum, sons of Ater, sons of Talmon, sons of Akkub, sons of Hatita, sons of Shobai, the whole [are] a hundred thirty and nine.

v43 The Nethinim: sons of Ziha, sons of Hasupha, sons of Tabbaoth,

v44 Sons of Keros, sons of Siaha, sons of Padon,

v45 Sons of Lebanah, sons of Hagabah, sons of Akkub,

v46 Sons of Hagab, sons of Shalmai, sons of Hanan,

v47 Sons of Giddel, sons of Gahar, sons of Reaiah,

v48 Sons of Rezin, sons of Nekoda, sons of Gazzam,

v49 Sons of Uzza, sons of Paseah, sons of Besai,

v50 Sons of Asnah, sons of Mehunim, sons of Nephusim,

v51 Sons of Bakbuk, sons of Hakupha, sons of Harhur,

v52 Sons of Bazluth, sons of Mehida, sons of Harsha,

v53 Sons of Barkos, sons of Sisera, sons of Thamah,

v54 Sons of Neziah, sons of Hatipha.

v55 Sons of the servants of Solomon: sons of Sotai, sons of Sophereth, sons of Peruda,

v56 Sons of Jaalah, sons of Darkon, sons of Giddel,

v57 Sons of Shephatiah, sons of Hattil, sons of Pochereth of Zebaim, sons of Ami.

v58 All the Nethinim, and the sons of the servants of Solomon [are] three hundred ninety and two.

v59 And these [are] those going up from Tel-Melah, Tel-Harsa, Cherub, Addan, Immer, and they have not been able to declare the house of their fathers, and their seed, whether they [are] of Israel:

v60 sons of Delaiah, sons of Tobiah, sons of Nekoda, six hundred fifty and two.

v61 And of the sons of the priests: sons of Habaiah, sons of Koz, sons of Barzillai (who took from the daughters of Barzillai the Gileadite a wife, and is called by their name;)

v62 these have sought their register among those reckoning themselves by genealogy, and they have not been found, and they are redeemed from the priesthood,

v63 and the Tirshatha saith to them, that they eat not of the most holy things till the standing up of a priest with Urim and with Thummim.

v64 All the assembly together [is] four myriad two thousand three hundred sixty,

v65 apart from their servants and their handmaids; these [are] seven thousand three hundred thirty and seven: and of them [are] singers and songstresses two hundred.

v66 Their horses [are] seven hundred thirty and six, their mules, two hundred forty and five,

v67 their camels, four hundred thirty and five, asses, six thousand seven hundred and twenty.

v68 And some of the heads of the fathers in their coming in to the house of Jehovah that [is] in Jerusalem, have offered willingly for the house of God, to establish it on its base;

v69 according to their power they have given to the treasure of the work; of gold, drams six myriads and a thousand, and of silver, pounds five thousand, and of priests' coats, a hundred.

v70 And the priests dwell, and the Levites, and of the people, and the singers, and the gatekeepers, and the Nethinim, in their cities; even all Israel in their cities.
