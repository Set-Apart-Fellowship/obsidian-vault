# Song of Solomon 1 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/22 - Song of Solomon/Song of Solomon]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/22 - Song of Solomon/Song of Solomon 2|Song of Solomon 2 →]]

***

v1 The Song of songs, which is Solomon's.

v2 Let him kiss me with the kisses of his mouth; For thy love is better than wine.

v3 Thine oils have a goodly fragrance; Thy name is [as] oil poured forth; Therefore do the virgins love thee.

v4 Draw me; we will run after thee: The king hath brought me into his chambers; We will be glad and rejoice in thee; We will make mention of thy love more than of wine: Rightly do they love thee.

v5 I am black, but comely, Oh ye daughters of Jerusalem, As the tents of Kedar, As the curtains of Solomon.

v6 Look not upon me, because I am swarthy, Because the sun hath scorched me. My mother's sons were incensed against me; They made me keeper of the vineyards; [But] mine own vineyard have I not kept.

v7 Tell me, O thou whom my soul loveth, Where thou feedest [thy flock], Where thou makest [it] to rest at noon: For why should I be as one that is veiled Beside the flocks of thy companions?

v8 If thou know not, O thou fairest among women, Go thy way forth by the footsteps of the flock, And feed thy kids beside the shepherds' tents.

v9 I have compared thee, O my love, To a steed in Pharaoh's chariots.

v10 Thy cheeks are comely with plaits [of hair], Thy neck with strings of jewels.

v11 We will make thee plaits of gold With studs of silver.

v12 While the king sat at his table, My spikenard sent forth its fragrance.

v13 My beloved is unto me [as] a bundle of myrrh, That lieth betwixt my breasts.

v14 My beloved is unto me [as] a cluster of henna-flowers In the vineyards of En-gedi.

v15 Behold, thou art fair, my love; Behold thou art fair; Thine eyes are [as] doves.

v16 Behold, thou art fair, my beloved, yea, pleasant: Also our couch is green.

v17 The beams of our house are cedars, [And] our rafters are firs.
