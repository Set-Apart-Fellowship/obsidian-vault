# Psalms 135 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 134|← Psalms 134]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 136|Psalms 136 →]]

***

v1 Praise ye Jehovah. Praise ye the name of Jehovah; Praise [him], O ye servants of Jehovah,

v2 Ye that stand in the house of Jehovah, In the courts of the house of our God.

v3 Praise ye Jehovah; for Jehovah is good: Sing praises unto his name; for it is pleasant.

v4 For Jehovah hath chosen Jacob unto himself, [And] Israel for his own possession.

v5 For I know that Jehovah is great, And that our Lord is above all gods.

v6 Whatsoever Jehovah pleased, that hath he done, In heaven and in earth, in the seas and in all deeps;

v7 Who causeth the vapors to ascend from the ends of the    earth; Who maketh lightnings for the rain; Who bringeth forth the wind out of his treasuries;

v8 Who smote the first-born of Egypt, Both of man and beast;

v9 Who sent signs and wonders into the midst of thee, O    Egypt, Upon Pharaoh, and upon all his servants;

v10 Who smote many nations, And slew mighty kings,

v11 Sihon king of the Amorites, And Og king of Bashan, And all the kingdoms of Canaan,

v12 And gave their land for a heritage, A heritage unto Israel his people.

v13 Thy name, O Jehovah, [endureth] for ever; Thy memorial [name], O Jehovah, throughout all generations.

v14 For Jehovah will judge his people, And repent himself concerning his servants.

v15 The idols of the nations are silver and gold, The work of men's hands.

v16 They have mouths, but they speak not; Eyes have they, but they see not;

v17 They have ears, but they hear not; Neither is there any breath in their mouths.

v18 They that make them shall be like unto them; Yea, every one that trusteth in them.

v19 O house of Israel, bless ye Jehovah: O house of Aaron, bless ye Jehovah:

v20 O house of Levi, bless ye Jehovah: Ye that fear Jehovah, bless ye Jehovah.

v21 Blessed be Jehovah out of Zion, Who dwelleth at Jerusalem. Praise ye Jehovah.      Psalm 136
