# Psalms 90 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 89|← Psalms 89]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 91|Psalms 91 →]]

***

v1 Lord, thou hast been our dwelling-place In all generations.

v2 Before the mountains were brought forth, Or ever thou hadst formed the earth and the world, Even from everlasting to everlasting, thou art God.

v3 Thou turnest man to destruction, And sayest, Return, ye children of men.

v4 For a thousand years in thy sight Are but as yesterday when it is past, And as a watch in the night.

v5 Thou carriest them away as with a flood; they are as a    sleep: In the morning they are like grass which groweth up.

v6 In the morning it flourisheth, and groweth up; In the evening it is cut down, and withereth.

v7 For we are consumed in thine anger, And in thy wrath are we troubled.

v8 Thou hast set our iniquities before thee, Our secret sins in the light of thy countenance.

v9 For all our days are passed away in thy wrath: We bring our years to an end as a sigh.

v10 The days of our years are threescore years and ten, Or even by reason of strength fourscore years; Yet is their pride but labor and sorrow; For it is soon gone, and we fly away.

v11 Who knoweth the power of thine anger, And thy wrath according to the fear that is due unto thee?

v12 So teach us to number our days, That we may get us a heart of wisdom.

v13 Return, O Jehovah; how long? And let it repent thee concerning thy servants.

v14 Oh satisfy us in the morning with thy lovingkindness, That we may rejoice and be glad all our days.

v15 Make us glad according to the days wherein thou hast    afflicted us, And the years wherein we have seen evil.

v16 Let thy work appear unto thy servants, And thy glory upon their children.

v17 And let the favor of the Lord our God be upon us; And establish thou the work of our hands upon us; Yea, the work of our hands establish thou it.      Psalm 91
