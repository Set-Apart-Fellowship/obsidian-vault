# Psalms 26 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 25|← Psalms 25]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 27|Psalms 27 →]]

***

v1 Judge me, O Jehovah, for I have walked in mine integrity: I have trusted also in Jehovah without wavering.

v2 Examine me, O Jehovah, and prove me; Try my heart and my mind.

v3 For thy lovingkindness is before mine eyes; And I have walked in thy truth.

v4 I have not sat with men of falsehood; Neither will I go in with dissemblers.

v5 I hate the assembly of evil-doers, And will not sit with the wicked.

v6 I will wash my hands in innocency: So will I compass thine altar, O Jehovah;

v7 That I may make the voice of thanksgiving to be heard, And tell of all thy wondrous works.

v8 Jehovah, I love the habitation of thy house, And the place where thy glory dwelleth.

v9 Gather not my soul with sinners, Nor my life with men of blood;

v10 In whose hands is wickedness, And their right hand is full of bribes.

v11 But as for me, I will walk in mine integrity: Redeem me, and be merciful unto me.

v12 My foot standeth in an even place: In the congregations will I bless Jehovah.      Psalm 27 [A Psalm] of David.
