# Psalms 88 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 87|← Psalms 87]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 89|Psalms 89 →]]

***

v1 O Jehovah, the God of my salvation, I have cried day and night before thee.

v2 Let my prayer enter into thy presence; Incline thine ear unto my cry.

v3 For my soul is full of troubles, And my life draweth nigh unto Sheol.

v4 I am reckoned with them that go down into the pit; I am as a man that hath no help,

v5 Cast off among the dead, Like the slain that lie in the grave, Whom thou rememberest no more, And they are cut off from thy hand.

v6 Thou hast laid me in the lowest pit, In dark places, in the deeps.

v7 Thy wrath lieth hard upon me, And thou hast afflicted me with all thy waves.   Selah

v8 Thou hast put mine acquaintance far from me; Thou hast made me an abomination unto them: I am shut up, and I cannot come forth.

v9 Mine eye wasteth away by reason of affliction: I have called daily upon thee, O Jehovah; I have spread forth my hands unto thee.

v10 Wilt thou show wonders to the dead? Shall they that are decreased arise and praise thee?   Selah

v11 Shall thy lovingkindness be declared in the grave? Or thy faithfulness in Destruction?

v12 Shall thy wonders be known in the dark? And thy righteousness in the land of forgetfulness?

v13 But unto thee, O Jehovah, have I cried; And in the morning shall my prayer come before thee.

v14 Jehovah, why castest thou off my soul? Why hidest thou thy face from me?

v15 I am afflicted and ready to die from my youth up: While I suffer thy terrors I am distracted.

v16 Thy fierce wrath is gone over me; Thy terrors have cut me off.

v17 They came round about me like water all the day long; They compassed me about together.

v18 Lover and friend hast thou put far from me, And mine acquaintance into darkness.      Psalm 89 Maschil of Ethan the Ezrahite.
