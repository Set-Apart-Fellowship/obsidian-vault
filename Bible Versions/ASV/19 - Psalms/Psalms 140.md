# Psalms 140 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 139|← Psalms 139]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 141|Psalms 141 →]]

***

v1 Deliver me, O Jehovah, from the evil man; Preserve me from the violent man:

v2 Who devise mischiefs in their heart; Continually do they gather themselves together for war.

v3 They have sharpened their tongue like a serpent; Adders' poison is under their lips.   Selah

v4 Keep me, O Jehovah, from the hands of the wicked; Preserve me from the violent man: Who have purposed to thrust aside my steps.

v5 The proud have hid a snare for me, and cords; They have spread a net by the wayside; They have set gins for me.   Selah

v6 I said unto Jehovah, Thou art my God: Give ear unto the voice of my supplications, O Jehovah.

v7 O Jehovah the Lord, the strength of my salvation, Thou hast covered my head in the day of battle.

v8 Grant not, O Jehovah, the desires of the wicked; Further not his evil device, [lest] they exalt themselves.   Selah

v9 As for the head of those that compass me about, Let the mischief of their own lips cover them.

v10 Let burning coals fall upon them: Let them be cast into the fire, Into deep pits, whence they shall not rise.

v11 An evil speaker shall not be established in the earth: Evil shall hunt the violent man to overthrow him.

v12 I know that Jehovah will maintain the cause of the    afflicted, And justice for the needy.

v13 Surely the righteous shall give thanks unto thy name: The upright shall dwell in thy presence.      Psalm 141    A Psalm of David.
