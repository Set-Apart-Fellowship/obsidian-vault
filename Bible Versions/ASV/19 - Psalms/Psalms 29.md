# Psalms 29 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 28|← Psalms 28]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 30|Psalms 30 →]]

***

v1 Ascribe unto Jehovah, O ye sons of the mighty, Ascribe unto Jehovah glory and strength.

v2 Ascribe unto Jehovah the glory due unto his name; Worship Jehovah in holy array.

v3 The voice of Jehovah is upon the waters: The God of glory thundereth, Even Jehovah upon many waters.

v4 The voice of Jehovah is powerful; The voice of Jehovah is full of majesty.

v5 The voice of Jehovah breaketh the cedars; Yea, Jehovah breaketh in pieces the cedars of Lebanon.

v6 He maketh them also to skip like a calf; Lebanon and Sirion like a young wild-ox.

v7 The voice of Jehovah cleaveth the flames of fire.

v8 The voice of Jehovah shaketh the wilderness; Jehovah shaketh the wilderness of Kadesh.

v9 The voice of Jehovah maketh the hinds to calve, And strippeth the forests bare: And in his temple everything saith, Glory.

v10 Jehovah sat [as King] at the Flood; Yea, Jehovah sitteth as King for ever.

v11 Jehovah will give strength unto his people; Jehovah will bless his people with peace.      Psalm 30 A Psalm; a Song at the Dedication of the House. [A Psalm] of David.
