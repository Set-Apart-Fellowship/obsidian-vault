# Psalms 10 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 9|← Psalms 9]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 11|Psalms 11 →]]

***

v1 Why standest thou afar off, O Jehovah? Why hidest thou thyself in times of trouble?

v2 In the pride of the wicked the poor is hotly pursued; Let them be taken in the devices that they have conceived.

v3 For the wicked boasteth of his heart's desire, And the covetous renounceth, [yea], contemneth Jehovah.

v4 The wicked, in the pride of his countenance, [saith], He    will not require [it]. All his thoughts are, There is no God.

v5 His ways are firm at all times; Thy judgments are far above out of his sight: As for all his adversaries, he puffeth at them.

v6 He saith in his heart, I shall not be moved; To all generations I shall not be in adversity.

v7 His mouth is full of cursing and deceit and oppression: Under his tongue is mischief and iniquity.

v8 He sitteth in the lurking-places of the villages; In the secret places doth he murder the innocent; His eyes are privily set against the helpless.

v9 He lurketh in secret as a lion in his covert; He lieth in wait to catch the poor: He doth catch the poor, when he draweth him in his net.

v10 He croucheth, he boweth down, And the helpless fall by his strong ones.

v11 He saith in his heart, God hath forgotten; He hideth his face; he will never see it.

v12 Arise, O Jehovah; O God, lift up thy hand: Forget not the poor.

v13 Wherefore doth the wicked contemn God, And say in his heart, Thou wilt not require [it]?

v14 Thou hast seen [it]; for thou beholdest mischief and    spite, to requite it with thy hand: The helpless committeth [himself] unto thee; Thou hast been the helper of the fatherless.

v15 Break thou the arm of the wicked; And as for the evil man, seek out his wickedness till thou find    none.

v16 Jehovah is King for ever and ever: The nations are perished out of his land.

v17 Jehovah, thou hast heard the desire of the meek: Thou wilt prepare their heart, thou wilt cause thine ear to hear;

v18 To judge the fatherless and the oppressed, That man who is of the earth may be terrible no more.      Psalm 11 For the Chief Musician. [A Psalm] of David.
