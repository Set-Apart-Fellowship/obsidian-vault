# Psalms 94 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 93|← Psalms 93]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 95|Psalms 95 →]]

***

v1 O Jehovah, thou God to whom vengeance belongeth, Thou God to whom vengeance belongeth, shine forth.

v2 Lift up thyself, thou judge of the earth: Render to the proud [their] desert.

v3 Jehovah, how long shall the wicked, How long shall the wicked triumph?

v4 They prate, they speak arrogantly: All the workers of iniquity boast themselves.

v5 They break in pieces thy people, O Jehovah, And afflict thy heritage.

v6 They slay the widow and the sojourner, And murder the fatherless.

v7 And they say, Jehovah will not see, Neither will the God of Jacob consider.

v8 Consider, ye brutish among the people; And ye fools, when will ye be wise?

v9 He that planted the ear, shall he not hear? He that formed the eye, shall he not see?

v10 He that chastiseth the nations, shall not he correct, [Even] he that teacheth man knowledge?

v11 Jehovah knoweth the thoughts of man, That they are vanity.

v12 Blessed is the man whom thou chastenest, O Jehovah, And teachest out of thy law;

v13 That thou mayest give him rest from the days of    adversity, Until the pit be digged for the wicked.

v14 For Jehovah will not cast off his people, Neither will he forsake his inheritance.

v15 For judgment shall return unto righteousness; And all the upright in heart shall follow it.

v16 Who will rise up for me against the evil-doers? Who will stand up for me against the workers of iniquity?

v17 Unless Jehovah had been my help, My soul had soon dwelt in silence.

v18 When I said, My foot slippeth; Thy lovingkindness, O Jehovah, held me up.

v19 In the multitude of my thoughts within me Thy comforts    delight my soul.

v20 Shall the throne of wickedness have fellowship with thee, Which frameth mischief by statute?

v21 They gather themselves together against the soul of the    righteous, And condemn the innocent blood.

v22 But Jehovah hath been my high tower, And my God the rock of my refuge.

v23 And he hath brought upon them their own iniquity, And will cut them off in their own wickedness; Jehovah our God will cut them off.      Psalm 95
