# Psalms 108 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 107|← Psalms 107]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 109|Psalms 109 →]]

***

v1 My heart is fixed, O God; I will sing, yea, I will sing praises, even with my glory.

v2 Awake, psaltery and harp: I myself will awake right early.

v3 I will give thanks unto thee, O Jehovah, among the    peoples; And I will sing praises unto thee among the nations.

v4 For thy lovingkindness is great above the heavens; And thy truth [reacheth] unto the skies.

v5 Be thou exalted, O God, above the heavens, And thy glory above all the earth.

v6 That thy beloved may be delivered, Save with thy right hand, and answer us.

v7 God hath spoken in his holiness: I will exult; I will divide Shechem, and mete out the valley of Succoth.

v8 Gilead is mine; Manasseh is mine; Ephraim also is the defence of my head; Judah is my sceptre.

v9 Moab is my washpot; Upon Edom will I cast my shoe; Over Philistia will I shout.

v10 Who will bring me into the fortified city? Who hath led me unto Edom?

v11 Hast not thou cast us off, O God? And thou goest not forth, O God, with our hosts.

v12 Give us help against the adversary; For vain is the help of man.

v13 Through God we shall do valiantly: For he it is that will tread down our adversaries.      Psalm 109    For the Chief Musicion. A Psalm of David.
