# Psalms 32 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 31|← Psalms 31]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 33|Psalms 33 →]]

***

v1 Blessed is he whose transgression is forgiven, Whose sin is covered.

v2 Blessed is the man unto whom Jehovah imputeth not    iniquity, And in whose spirit there is no guile.

v3 When I kept silence, my bones wasted away Through my groaning all the day long.

v4 For day and night thy hand was heavy upon me: My moisture was changed [as] with the drought of summer.   Selah

v5 I acknowledged my sin unto thee, And mine iniquity did I not hide: I said, I will confess my transgressions unto Jehovah; And thou forgavest the iniquity of my sin.   Selah

v6 For this let every one that is godly pray unto thee in a    time when thou mayest be found: Surely when the great waters overflow they shall not reach unto    him.

v7 Thou art my hiding-place; thou wilt preserve me from    trouble; Thou wilt compass me about with songs of deliverance.   Selah

v8 I will instruct thee and teach thee in the way which thou    shalt go: I will counsel thee with mine eye upon thee.

v9 Be ye not as the horse, or as the mule, which have no    understanding; Whose trappings must be bit and bridle to hold them in, [Else] they will not come near unto thee.

v10 Many sorrows shall be to the wicked; But he that trusteth in Jehovah, lovingkindness shall compass    him about.

v11 Be glad in Jehovah, and rejoice, ye righteous; And shout for joy, all ye that are upright in heart.      Psalm 33
