# Psalms 62 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 61|← Psalms 61]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 63|Psalms 63 →]]

***

v1 My soul waiteth in silence for God only: From him [cometh] my salvation.

v2 He only is my rock and my salvation: [He is] my high tower; I shall not be greatly moved.

v3 How long will ye set upon a man, That ye may slay [him], all of you, Like a leaning wall, like a tottering fence?

v4 They only consult to thrust him down from his dignity; They delight in lies; They bless with their mouth, but they curse inwardly.   Selah

v5 My soul, wait thou in silence for God only; For my expectation is from him.

v6 He only is my rock and my salvation: [He is] my high tower; I shall not be moved.

v7 With God is my salvation and my glory: The rock of my strength, and my refuge, is in God.

v8 Trust in him at all times, ye people; Pour out your heart before him: God is a refuge for us.   Selah

v9 Surely men of low degree are vanity, and men of high    degree are a lie: In the balances they will go up; They are together lighter than vanity.

v10 Trust not in oppression, And become not vain in robbery: If riches increase, set not your heart [thereon].

v11 God hath spoken once, Twice have I heard this, That power belongeth unto God.

v12 Also unto thee, O Lord, belongeth lovingkindness; For thou renderest to every man according to his work.      Psalm 63 A Psalm of David when he was in the wilderness of Judah.
