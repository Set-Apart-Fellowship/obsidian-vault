# Psalms 3 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 2|← Psalms 2]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 4|Psalms 4 →]]

***

v1 Jehovah, how are mine adversaries increased! Many are they that rise up against me.

v2 Many there are that say of my soul, There is no help for him in God.   Selah

v3 But thou, O Jehovah, art a shield about me; My glory and the lifter up of my head.

v4 I cry unto Jehovah with my voice, And he answereth me out of his holy hill.   Selah

v5 I laid me down and slept; I awaked; for Jehovah sustaineth me.

v6 I will not be afraid of ten thousands of the people That have set themselves against me round about.

v7 Arise, O Jehovah; save me, O my God: For thou hast smitten all mine enemies upon the cheek bone; Thou hast broken the teeth of the wicked.

v8 Salvation belongeth unto Jehovah: Thy blessing be upon thy people.   Selah      Psalm 4 For the Chief Musician; on stringed instruments. A Psalm of David.
