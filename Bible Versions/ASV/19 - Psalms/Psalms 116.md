# Psalms 116 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 115|← Psalms 115]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 117|Psalms 117 →]]

***

v1 I love Jehovah, because he heareth My voice and my supplications.

v2 Because he hath inclined his ear unto me, Therefore will I call [upon him] as long as I live.

v3 The cords of death compassed me, And the pains of Sheol gat hold upon me: I found trouble and sorrow.

v4 Then called I upon the name of Jehovah: O Jehovah, I beseech thee, deliver my soul.

v5 Gracious is Jehovah, and righteous; Yea, our God is merciful.

v6 Jehovah preserveth the simple: I was brought low, and he saved me.

v7 Return unto thy rest, O my soul; For Jehovah hath dealt bountifully with thee.

v8 For thou hast delivered my soul from death, Mine eyes from tears, [And] my feet from falling.

v9 I will walk before Jehovah In the land of the living.

v10 I believe, for I will speak: I was greatly afflicted:

v11 I said in my haste, All men are liars.

v12 What shall I render unto Jehovah For all his benefits toward me?

v13 I will take the cup of salvation, And call upon the name of Jehovah.

v14 I will pay my vows unto Jehovah, Yea, in the presence of all his people.

v15 Precious in the sight of Jehovah Is the death of his saints.

v16 O Jehovah, truly I am thy servant: I am thy servant, the son of thy handmaid; Thou hast loosed my bonds.

v17 I will offer to thee the sacrifice of thanksgiving, And will call upon the name of Jehovah.

v18 I will pay my vows unto Jehovah, Yea, in the presence of all his people,

v19 In the courts of Jehovah's house, In the midst of thee, O Jerusalem. Praise ye Jehovah.      Psalm 117
