# Psalms 130 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 129|← Psalms 129]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 131|Psalms 131 →]]

***

v1 Out of the depths have I cried unto thee, O Jehovah.

v2 Lord, hear my voice: Let thine ears be attentive To the voice of my supplications.

v3 If thou, Jehovah, shouldest mark iniquities, O Lord, who could stand?

v4 But there is forgiveness with thee, That thou mayest be feared.

v5 I wait for Jehovah, my soul doth wait, And in his word do I hope.

v6 My soul [waiteth] for the Lord More than watchmen [wait] for the morning; [Yea, more than] watchmen for the morning.

v7 O Israel, hope in Jehovah; For with Jehovah there is lovingkindness, And with him is plenteous redemption.

v8 And he will redeem Israel From all his iniquities.      Psalm 131    A Song of Ascents; of David.
