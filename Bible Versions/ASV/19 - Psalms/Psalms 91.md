# Psalms 91 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 90|← Psalms 90]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 92|Psalms 92 →]]

***

v1 He that dwelleth in the secret place of the Most High Shall abook_ide under the shadow of the Almighty.

v2 I will say of Jehovah, He is my refuge and my fortress; My God, in whom I trust.

v3 For he will deliver thee from the snare of the fowler, And from the deadly pestilence.

v4 He will cover thee with his pinions, And under his wings shalt thou take refuge: His truth is a shield and a buckler.

v5 Thou shalt not be afraid for the terror by night, Nor for the arrow that flieth by day;

v6 For the pestilence that walketh in darkness, Nor for the destruction that wasteth at noonday.

v7 A thousand shall fall at thy side, And ten thousand at thy right hand; [But] it shall not come nigh thee.

v8 Only with thine eyes shalt thou behold, And see the reward of the wicked.

v9 For thou, O Jehovah, art my refuge! Thou hast made the Most High thy habitation;

v10 There shall no evil befall thee, Neither shall any plague come nigh thy tent.

v11 For he will give his angels charge over thee, To keep thee in all thy ways.

v12 They shall bear thee up in their hands, Lest thou dash thy foot against a stone.

v13 Thou shalt tread upon the lion and adder: The young lion and the serpent shalt thou trample under foot.

v14 Because he hath set his love upon me, therefore will I    deliver him: I will set him on high, because he hath known my name.

v15 He shall call upon me, and I will answer him; I will be with him in trouble: I will deliver him, and honor him.

v16 With long life will I satisfy him, And show him my salvation.      Psalm 92 A Psalm, a Song for the sabbath day.
