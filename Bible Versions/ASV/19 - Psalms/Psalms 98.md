# Psalms 98 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 97|← Psalms 97]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 99|Psalms 99 →]]

***

v1 Oh sing unto Jehovah a new song; For he hath done marvellous things: His right hand, and his holy arm, hath wrought salvation for him.

v2 Jehovah hath made known his salvation: His righteousness hath he openly showed in the sight of the    nations.

v3 He hath remembered his lovingkindness and his    faithfulness toward the house of Israel: All the ends of the earth have seen the salvation of our God.

v4 Make a joyful noise unto Jehovah, all the earth: Break forth and sing for joy, yea, sing praises.

v5 Sing praises unto Jehovah with the harp; With the harp and the voice of melody.

v6 With trumpets and sound of cornet Make a joyful noise before the King, Jehovah.

v7 Let the sea roar, and the fulness thereof; The world, and they that dwell therein;

v8 Let the floods clap their hands; Let the hills sing for joy together

v9 Before Jehovah; for he cometh to judge the earth: He will judge the world with righteousness, And the peoples with equity.      Psalm 99
