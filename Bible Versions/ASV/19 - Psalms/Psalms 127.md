# Psalms 127 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 126|← Psalms 126]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 128|Psalms 128 →]]

***

v1 Except Jehovah build the house, They labor in vain that build it: Except Jehovah keep the city, The watchman waketh but in vain.

v2 It is vain for you to rise up early, To take rest late, To eat the bread of toil; [For] so he giveth unto his beloved sleep.

v3 Lo, children are a heritage of Jehovah; [And] the fruit of the womb is [his] reward.

v4 As arrows in the hand of a mighty man, So are the children of youth.

v5 Happy is the man that hath his quiver full of them: They shall not be put to shame, When they speak with their enemies in the gate.      Psalm 128    A Song of Ascents.
