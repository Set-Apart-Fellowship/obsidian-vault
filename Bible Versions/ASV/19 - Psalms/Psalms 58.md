# Psalms 58 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 57|← Psalms 57]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 59|Psalms 59 →]]

***

v1 Do ye indeed in silence speak righteousness? Do ye judge uprightly, O ye sons of men?

v2 Nay, in heart ye work wickedness; Ye weigh out the violence of your hands in the earth.

v3 The wicked are estranged from the womb: They go astray as soon as they are born, speaking lies.

v4 Their poison is like the poison of a serpent: [They are] like the deaf adder that stoppeth her ear,

v5 Which hearkeneth not to the voice of charmers, Charming never so wisely.

v6 Break their teeth, O God, in their mouth: Break out the great teeth of the young lions, O Jehovah.

v7 Let them melt away as water that runneth apace: When he aimeth his arrows, let them be as though they were cut    off.

v8 [Let them be] as a snail which melteth and passeth away, [Like] the untimely birth of a woman, that hath not seen the sun.

v9 Before your pots can feel the thorns, He will take them away with a whirlwind, the green and the    burning alike.

v10 The righteous shall rejoice when he seeth the vengeance: He shall wash his feet in the blood of the wicked;

v11 So that men shall say, Verily there is a reward for the    righteous: Verily there is a God that judgeth in the earth.      Psalm 59 For the Chief Musician; [set to] Al-tashheth. [A Psalm] of David. Michtam; when Saul sent, and they watched the house to kill him.
