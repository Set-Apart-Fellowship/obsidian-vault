# Psalms 61 (American Standard Version)

***

v1 Hear my cry, O God; Attend unto my prayer.

v2 From the end of the earth will I call unto thee, when my    heart is overwhelmed: Lead me to the rock that is higher than I.

v3 For thou hast been a refuge for me, A strong tower from the enemy.

v4 I will dwell in thy tabernacle for ever: I will take refuge in the covert of thy wings.   Selah

v5 For thou, O God, hast heard my vows: Thou hast given [me] the heritage of those that fear thy name.

v6 Thou wilt prolong the king's life; His years shall be as many generations.

v7 He shall abook_ide before God for ever: Oh prepare lovingkindness and truth, that they may preserve him.

v8 So will I sing praise unto thy name for ever, That I may daily perform my vows.      Psalm 62 For the Chief Musician; after the manner of Jeduthan. A Psalm of David.
