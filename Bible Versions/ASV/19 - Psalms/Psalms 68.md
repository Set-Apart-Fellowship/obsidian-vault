# Psalms 68 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 67|← Psalms 67]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 69|Psalms 69 →]]

***

v1 Let God arise, let his enemies be scattered; Let them also that hate him flee before him.

v2 As smoke is driven away, so drive them away: As wax melteth before the fire, So let the wicked perish at the presence of God.

v3 But let the righteous be glad; let them exult before God: Yea, let them rejoice with gladness.

v4 Sing unto God, sing praises to his name: Cast up a highway for him that rideth through the deserts; His name is Jehovah; and exult ye before him.

v5 A father of the fatherless, and a judge of the widows, Is God in his holy habitation.

v6 God setteth the solitary in families: He bringeth out the prisoners into prosperity; But the rebellious dwell in a parched land.

v7 O God, when thou wentest forth before thy people, When thou didst march through the wilderness;   Selah

v8 The earth trembled, The heavens also dropped [rain] at the presence of God: Yon Sinai [trembled] at the presence of God, the God of Israel.

v9 Thou, O God, didst send a plentiful rain, Thou didst confirm thine inheritance, when it was weary.

v10 Thy congregation dwelt therein: Thou, O God, didst prepare of thy goodness for the poor.

v11 The Lord giveth the word: The women that publish the tidings are a great host.

v12 Kings of armies flee, they flee; And she that tarrieth at home divideth the spoil.

v13 When ye lie among the sheepfolds, [It is as] the wings of a dove covered with silver, And her pinions with yellow gold.

v14 When the Almighty scattered kings therein, [It was as when] it snoweth in Zalmon.

v15 A mountain of God is the mountain of Bashan; A high mountain is the mountain of Bashan.

v16 Why look ye askance, ye high mountains, At the mountain which God hath desired for his abode? Yea, Jehovah will dwell [in it] for ever.

v17 The chariots of God are twenty thousand, even thousands    upon thousands; The Lord is among them, [as in] Sinai, in the sanctuary.

v18 Thou hast ascended on high, thou hast led away captives; Thou hast received gifts among men, Yea, [among] the rebellious also, that Jehovah God might dwell    [with them].

v19 Blessed be the Lord, who daily beareth our burden, Even the God who is our salvation.   Selah

v20 God is unto us a God of deliverances; And unto Jehovah the Lord belongeth escape from death.

v21 But God will smite through the head of his enemies, The hairy scalp of such a one as goeth on still in his    guiltiness.

v22 The Lord said, I will bring again from Bashan, I will bring [them] again from the depths of the sea;

v23 That thou mayest crush [them], [dipping] thy foot in    blood, That the tongue of thy dogs may have its portion from [thine]    enemies.

v24 They have seen thy goings, O God, Even the goings of my God, my King, into the sanctuary.

v25 The singers went before, the minstrels followed after, In the midst of the damsels playing with timbrels.

v26 Bless ye God in the congregations, Even the Lord, [ye that are] of the fountain of Israel.

v27 There is little Benjamin their ruler, The princes of Judah [and] their council, The princes of Zebulun, the princes of Naphtali.

v28 Thy God hath commanded thy strength: Strengthen, O God, that which thou hast wrought for us.

v29 Because of thy temple at Jerusalem Kings shall bring presents unto thee.

v30 Rebuke the wild beast of the reeds, The multitude of the bulls, with the calves of the peoples, Trampling under foot the pieces of silver: He hath scattered the peoples that delight in war.

v31 Princes shall come out of Egypt; Ethiopia shall haste to stretch out her hands unto God.

v32 Sing unto God, ye kingdoms of the earth; Oh sing praises unto the Lord;   Selah

v33 To him that rideth upon the heaven of heavens, which are    of old; Lo, he uttereth his voice, a mighty voice.

v34 Ascribe ye strength unto God: His excellency is over Israel, And his strength is in the skies.

v35 O God, [thou art] terrible out of thy holy places: The God of Israel, he giveth strength and power unto [his]    people. Blessed be God.      Psalm 69 For the Chief Musician; set to Shoshanim. [A Psalm] of David.
