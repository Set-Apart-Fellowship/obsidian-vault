# Psalms 104 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 103|← Psalms 103]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 105|Psalms 105 →]]

***

v1 Bless Jehovah, O my soul. O Jehovah my God, thou art very great; Thou art clothed with honor and majesty:

v2 Who coverest thyself with light as with a garment; Who stretchest out the heavens like a curtain;

v3 Who layeth the beams of his chambers in the waters; Who maketh the clouds his chariot; Who walketh upon the wings of the wind;

v4 Who maketh winds his messengers; Flames of fire his ministers;

v5 Who laid the foundations of the earth, That it should not be moved for ever.

v6 Thou coveredst it with the deep as with a vesture; The waters stood above the mountains.

v7 At thy rebuke they fled; At the voice of thy thunder they hasted away

v8 (The mountains rose, the valleys sank down) Unto the place which thou hadst founded for them.

v9 Thou hast set a bound that they may not pass over; That they turn not again to cover the earth.

v10 He sendeth forth springs into the valleys; They run among the mountains;

v11 They give drink to every beast of the field; The wild asses quench their thirst.

v12 By them the birds of the heavens have their habitation; They sing among the branches.

v13 He watereth the mountains from his chambers: The earth is filled with the fruit of thy works.

v14 He causeth the grass to grow for the cattle, And herb for the service of man; That he may bring forth food out of the earth,

v15 And wine that maketh glad the heart of man, [And] oil to make his face to shine, And bread that strengtheneth man's heart.

v16 The trees of Jehovah are filled [with moisture], The cedars of Lebanon, which he hath planted;

v17 Where the birds make their nests: As for the stork, the fir-trees are her house.

v18 The high mountains are for the wild goats; The rocks are a refuge for the conies.

v19 He appointed the moon for seasons: The sun knoweth his going down.

v20 Thou makest darkness, and it is night, Wherein all the beasts of the forest creep forth.

v21 The young lions roar after their prey, And seek their food from God.

v22 The sun ariseth, they get them away, And lay them down in their dens.

v23 Man goeth forth unto his work And to his labor until the evening.

v24 O Jehovah, how manifold are thy works! In wisdom hast thou made them all: The earth is full of thy riches.

v25 Yonder is the sea, great and wide, Wherein are things creeping innumerable, Both small and great beasts.

v26 There go the ships; There is leviathan, whom thou hast formed to play therein.

v27 These wait all for thee, That thou mayest give them their food in due season.

v28 Thou givest unto them, they gather; Thou openest thy hand, they are satisfied with good.

v29 Thou hidest thy face, they are troubled; Thou takest away their breath, they die, And return to their dust.

v30 Thou sendest forth thy Spirit, they are created; And thou renewest the face of the ground.

v31 Let the glory of Jehovah endure for ever; Let Jehovah rejoice in his works:

v32 Who looketh on the earth, and it trembleth; He toucheth the mountains, and they smoke.

v33 I will sing unto Jehovah as long as I live: I will sing praise to my God while I have any being.

v34 Let thy meditation be sweet unto him: I will rejoice in Jehovah.

v35 Let sinners be consumed out of the earth. And let the wicked be no more. Bless Jehovah, O my soul. Praise ye Jehovah.      Psalm 105
