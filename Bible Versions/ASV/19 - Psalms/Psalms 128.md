# Psalms 128 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 127|← Psalms 127]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 129|Psalms 129 →]]

***

v1 Blessed is every one that feareth Jehovah, That walketh in his ways.

v2 For thou shalt eat the labor of thy hands: Happy shalt thou be, and it shall be well with thee.

v3 Thy wife shall be as a fruitful vine, In the innermost parts of thy house; Thy children like olive plants, Round about thy table.

v4 Behold, thus shall the man be blessed That feareth Jehovah.

v5 Jehovah bless thee out of Zion: And see thou the good of Jerusalem all the days of thy life.

v6 Yea, see thou thy children's children. Peace be upon Israel.      Psalm 129    A Song of Ascents.
