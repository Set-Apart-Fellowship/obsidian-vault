# Psalms 149 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 148|← Psalms 148]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 150|Psalms 150 →]]

***

v1 Praise ye Jehovah. Sing unto Jehovah a new song, And his praise in the assembly of the saints.

v2 Let Israel rejoice in him that made him: Let the children of Zion be joyful in their King.

v3 Let them praise his name in the dance: Let them sing praises unto him with timbrel and harp.

v4 For Jehovah taketh pleasure in his people: He will beautify the meek with salvation.

v5 Let the saints exult in glory: Let them sing for joy upon their beds.

v6 [Let] the high praises of God [be] in their mouth, And a two-edged sword in their hand;

v7 To execute vengeance upon the nations, And punishments upon the peoples;

v8 To bind their kings with chains, And their nobles with fetters of iron;

v9 To execute upon them the judgment written: This honor have all his saints. Praise ye Jehovah.      Psalm 150
