# Psalms 9 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 8|← Psalms 8]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 10|Psalms 10 →]]

***

v1 I will give thanks unto Jehovah with my whole heart; I will show forth all thy marvellous works.

v2 I will be glad and exult in thee; I will sing praise to thy name, O thou Most High.

v3 When mine enemies turn back, They stumble and perish at thy presence.

v4 For thou hast maintained my right and my cause; Thou sittest in the throne judging righteously.

v5 Thou hast rebuked the nations, thou hast destroyed the    wicked; Thou hast blotted out their name for ever and ever.

v6 The enemy are come to an end, they are desolate for ever; And the cities which thou hast overthrown, The very remembrance of them is perished.

v7 But Jehovah sitteth [as king] for ever: He hath prepared his throne for judgment;

v8 And he will judge the world in righteousness, He will minister judgment to the peoples in uprightness.

v9 Jehovah also will be a high tower for the oppressed, A high tower in times of trouble;

v10 And they that know thy name will put their trust in thee; For thou, Jehovah, hast not forsaken them that seek thee.

v11 Sing praises to Jehovah, who dwelleth in Zion: Declare among the people his doings.

v12 For he that maketh inquisition for blood remembereth them; He forgetteth not the cry of the poor.

v13 Have mercy upon me, O Jehovah; Behold my affliction [which I suffer] of them that hate me, Thou that liftest me up from the gates of death;

v14 That I may show forth all thy praise. In the gates of the daughter of Zion I will rejoice in thy salvation.

v15 The nations are sunk down in the pit that they made: In the net which they hid is their own foot taken.

v16 Jehovah hath made himself known, he hath executed    judgment: The wicked is snared in the work of his own hands.   Higgaion. Selah

v17 The wicked shall be turned back unto Sheol, Even all the nations that forget God.

v18 For the needy shall not alway be forgotten, Nor the expectation of the poor perish for ever.

v19 Arise, O Jehovah; let not man prevail: Let the nations be judged in thy sight.

v20 Put them in fear, O Jehovah: Let the nations know themselves to be but men.   Selah      Psalm 10
