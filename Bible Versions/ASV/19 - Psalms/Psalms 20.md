# Psalms 20 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 19|← Psalms 19]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 21|Psalms 21 →]]

***

v1 Jehovah answer thee in the day of trouble; The name of the God of Jacob set thee up on high;

v2 Send thee help from the sanctuary, And strengthen thee out of Zion;

v3 Remember all thy offerings, And accept thy burnt-sacrifice;   Selah

v4 Grant thee thy heart's desire, And fulfil all thy counsel.

v5 We will triumph in thy salvation, And in the name of our God we will set up our banners: Jehovah fulfil all thy petitions.

v6 Now know I that Jehovah saveth his anointed; He will answer him from his holy heaven With the saving strength of his right hand.

v7 Some [trust] in chariots, and some in horses; But we will make mention of the name of Jehovah our God.

v8 They are bowed down and fallen; But we are risen, and stand upright.

v9 Save, Jehovah: Let the King answer us when we call.      Psalm 21 For the Chief Musician. A Psalm of David.
