# Psalms 136 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 135|← Psalms 135]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 137|Psalms 137 →]]

***

v1 Oh give thanks unto Jehovah; for he is good; For his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever.

v2 Oh give thanks unto the God of gods; For his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever.

v3 Oh give thanks unto the Lord of lords; For his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever:

v4 To him who alone doeth great wonders; For his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever:

v5 To him that by understanding made the heavens; For his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever:

v6 To him that spread forth the earth above the waters; For his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever:

v7 To him that made great lights; For his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever:

v8 The sun to rule by day; For his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever;

v9 The moon and stars to rule by night; For his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever:

v10 To him that smote Egypt in their first-born; For his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever;

v11 And brought out Israel from among them; For his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever;

v12 With a strong hand, and with an outstretched arm; For his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever:

v13 To him that divided the Red Sea in sunder; For his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever;

v14 And made Israel to pass through the midst of it; For his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever;

v15 But overthrew Pharaoh and his host in the Red Sea; For his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever:

v16 To him that led his people through the wilderness; For his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever:

v17 To him that smote great kings; For his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever;

v18 And slew famous kings; For his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever:

v19 Sihon king of the Amorites; For his lovingkindness [endureth] forever;

v20 And Og king of Bashan; For his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever;

v21 And gave their land for a heritage; For his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever;

v22 Even a heritage unto Israel his servant; For his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever:

v23 Who remembered us in our low estate; For his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever;

v24 And hath delivered us from our adversaries; For his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever:

v25 Who giveth food to all flesh; For his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever.

v26 Oh give thanks unto the God of heaven; For his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever.      Psalm 137
