# Psalms 87 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 86|← Psalms 86]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 88|Psalms 88 →]]

***

v1 His foundation is in the holy mountains.

v2 Jehovah loveth the gates of Zion More than all the dwellings of Jacob.

v3 Glorious things are spoken of thee, O city of God.   Selah

v4 I will make mention of Rahab and Babylon as among them    that know me: Behold, Philistia, and Tyre, with Ethiopia: This one was born there.

v5 Yea, of Zion it shall be said, This one and that one was    born in her; And the Most High himself will establish her.

v6 Jehovah will count, when he writeth up the peoples, This one was born there.   Selah

v7 They that sing as well as they that dance [shall say], All my fountains are in thee.      Psalm 88 A Song, a Psalm of the sons of Korah; for the Chief Musician; set to Mahalath Leannoth. Maschil of Heman the Ezrahite.
