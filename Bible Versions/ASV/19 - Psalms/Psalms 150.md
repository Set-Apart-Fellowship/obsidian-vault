# Psalms 150 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 149|← Psalms 149]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]]

***

v1 Praise ye Jehovah. Praise God in his sanctuary: Praise him in the firmament of his power.

v2 Praise him for his mighty acts: Praise him according to his excellent greatness.

v3 Praise him with trumpet sound: Praise him with psaltery and harp.

v4 Praise him with timbrel and dance: Praise him with stringed instruments and pipe.

v5 Praise him with loud cymbals: Praise him with high sounding cymbals.

v6 Let everything that hath breath praise Jehovah. Praise ye Jehovah.
