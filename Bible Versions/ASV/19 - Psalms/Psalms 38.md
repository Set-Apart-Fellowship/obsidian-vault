# Psalms 38 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 37|← Psalms 37]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 39|Psalms 39 →]]

***

v1 O Jehovah, rebuke me not in thy wrath; Neither chasten me in thy hot displeasure.

v2 For thine arrows stick fast in me, And thy hand presseth me sore.

v3 There is no soundness in my flesh because of thine    indignation; Neither is there any health in my bones because of my sin.

v4 For mine iniquities are gone over my head: As a heavy burden they are too heavy for me.

v5 My wounds are loathsome and corrupt, Because of my foolishness.

v6 I am pained and bowed down greatly; I go mourning all the day long.

v7 For my loins are filled with burning; And there is no soundness in my flesh.

v8 I am faint and sore bruised: I have groaned by reason of the disquietness of my heart.

v9 Lord, all my desire is before thee; And my groaning is not hid from thee.

v10 My heart throbbeth, my strength faileth me: As for the light of mine eyes, it also is gone from me.

v11 My lovers and my friends stand aloof from my plague; And my kinsmen stand afar off.

v12 They also that seek after my life lay snares [for me]; And they that seek my hurt speak mischievous things, And meditate deceits all the day long.

v13 But I, as a deaf man, hear not; And I am as a dumb man that openeth not his mouth.

v14 Yea, I am as a man that heareth not, And in whose mouth are no reproofs.

v15 For in thee, O Jehovah, do I hope: Thou wilt answer, O Lord my God.

v16 For I said, Lest they rejoice over me: When my foot slippeth, they magnify themselves against me.

v17 For I am ready to fall, And my sorrow is continually before me.

v18 For I will declare mine iniquity; I will be sorry for my    sin.

v19 But mine enemies are lively, [and] are strong; And they that hate me wrongfully are multiplied.

v20 They also that render evil for good Are adversaries unto me, because I follow the thing that is good.

v21 Forsake me not, O Jehovah: O my God, be not far from me.

v22 Make haste to help me, O Lord, my salvation.      Psalm 39 For the Chief Musician, Jeduthun. A Psalm of David.
