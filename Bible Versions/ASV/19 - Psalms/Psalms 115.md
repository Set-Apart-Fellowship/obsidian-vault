# Psalms 115 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 114|← Psalms 114]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 116|Psalms 116 →]]

***

v1 Not unto us, O Jehovah, not unto us, But unto thy name give glory, For thy lovingkindness, and for thy truth's sake.

v2 Wherefore should the nations say, Where is now their God?

v3 But our God is in the heavens: He hath done whatsoever he pleased.

v4 Their idols are silver and gold, The work of men's hands.

v5 They have mouths, but they speak not; Eyes have they, but they see not;

v6 They have ears, but they hear not; Noses have they, but they smell not;

v7 They have hands, but they handle not; Feet have they, but they walk not; Neither speak they through their throat.

v8 They that make them shall be like unto them; Yea, every one that trusteth in them.

v9 O Israel, trust thou in Jehovah: He is their help and their shield.

v10 O house of Aaron, trust ye in Jehovah: He is their help and their shield.

v11 Ye that fear Jehovah, trust in Jehovah: He is their help and their shield.

v12 Jehovah hath been mindful of us; he will bless [us]: He will bless the house of Israel; He will bless the house of Aaron.

v13 He will bless them that fear Jehovah, Both small and great.

v14 Jehovah increase you more and more, You and your children.

v15 Blessed are ye of Jehovah, Who made heaven and earth.

v16 The heavens are the heavens of Jehovah; But the earth hath he given to the children of men.

v17 The dead praise not Jehovah, Neither any that go down into silence;

v18 But we will bless Jehovah From this time forth and for evermore. Praise ye Jehovah.      Psalm 116
