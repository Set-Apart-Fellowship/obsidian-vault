# Psalms 105 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 104|← Psalms 104]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 106|Psalms 106 →]]

***

v1 Oh give thanks unto Jehovah, call upon his name; Make known among the peoples his doings.

v2 Sing unto him, sing praises unto him; Talk ye of all his marvelous works.

v3 Glory ye in his holy name: Let the heart of them rejoice that seek Jehovah.

v4 Seek ye Jehovah and his strength; Seek his face evermore.

v5 Remember his marvellous works that he hath done, His wonders, and the judgments of his mouth,

v6 O ye seed of Abraham his servant, Ye children of Jacob, his chosen ones.

v7 He is Jehovah our God: His judgments are in all the earth.

v8 He hath remembered his covenant for ever, The word which he commanded to a thousand generations,

v9 [The covenant] which he made with Abraham, And his oath unto Isaac,

v10 And confirmed the same unto Jacob for a statute, To Israel for an everlasting covenant,

v11 Saying, Unto thee will I give the land of Canaan, The lot of your inheritance;

v12 When they were but a few men in number, Yea, very few, and sojourners in it.

v13 And they went about from nation to nation, From one kingdom to another people.

v14 He suffered no man to do them wrong; Yea, he reproved kings for their sakes,

v15 [Saying], Touch not mine anointed ones, And do my prophets no harm.

v16 And he called for a famine upon the land; He brake the whole staff of bread.

v17 He sent a man before them; Joseph was sold for a servant:

v18 His feet they hurt with fetters: He was laid in [chains of] iron,

v19 Until the time that his word came to pass, The word of Jehovah tried him.

v20 The king sent and loosed him; Even the ruler of peoples, and let him go free.

v21 He made him lord of his house, And ruler of all his substance;

v22 To bind his princes at his pleasure, And teach his elders wisdom.

v23 Israel also came into Egypt; And Jacob sojourned in the land of Ham.

v24 And he increased his people greatly, And made them stronger than their adversaries.

v25 He turned their heart to hate his people, To deal subtly with his servants.

v26 He sent Moses his servant, [And] Aaron whom he had chosen.

v27 They set among them his signs, And wonders in the land of Ham.

v28 He sent darkness, and made it dark; And they rebelled not against his words.

v29 He turned their waters into blood, And slew their fish.

v30 Their land swarmed with frogs In the chambers of their    kings.

v31 He spake, and there came swarms of flies, And lice in all their borders.

v32 He gave them hail for rain, [And] flaming fire in their land.

v33 He smote their vines also and their fig-trees, And brake the trees of their borders.

v34 He spake, and the locust came, And the grasshopper, and that without number,

v35 And did eat up every herb in their land, And did eat up the fruit of their ground.

v36 He smote also all the first-born in their land, The chief of all their strength.

v37 And he brought them forth with silver and gold; And there was not one feeble person among his tribes.

v38 Egypt was glad when they departed; For the fear of them had fallen upon them.

v39 He spread a cloud for a covering, And fire to give light in the night.

v40 They asked, and he brought quails, And satisfied them with the bread of heaven.

v41 He opened the rock, and waters gushed out; They ran in the dry places [like] a river.

v42 For he remembered his holy word, [And] Abraham his servant.

v43 And he brought forth his people with joy, [And] his chosen with singing.

v44 And he gave them the lands of the nations; And they took the labor of the peoples in possession:

v45 That they might keep his statutes, And observe his laws. Praise ye Jehovah.      Psalm 106
