# Psalms 81 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 80|← Psalms 80]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 82|Psalms 82 →]]

***

v1 Sing aloud unto God our strength: Make a joyful noise unto the God of Jacob.

v2 Raise a song, and bring hither the timbrel, The pleasant harp with the psaltery.

v3 Blow the trumpet at the new moon, At the full moon, on our feast-day.

v4 For it is a statute for Israel, An ordinance of the God of Jacob.

v5 He appointed it in Joseph for a testimony, When he went out over the land of Egypt, [Where] I heard a language that I knew not.

v6 I removed his shoulder from the burden: His hands were freed from the basket.

v7 Thou calledst in trouble, and I delivered thee; I answered thee in the secret place of thunder; I proved thee at the waters of Meribah.   Selah

v8 Hear, O my people, and I will testify unto thee: O Israel, if thou wouldest hearken unto me!

v9 There shall no strange god be in thee; Neither shalt thou worship any foreign god.

v10 I am Jehovah thy God, Who brought thee up out of the land of Egypt: Open thy mouth wide, and I will fill it.

v11 But my people hearkened not to my voice; And Israel would none of me.

v12 So I let them go after the stubbornness of their heart, That they might walk in their own counsels.

v13 Oh that my people would hearken unto me, That Israel would walk in my ways!

v14 I would soon subdue their enemies, And turn my hand against their adversaries.

v15 The haters of Jehovah should submit themselves unto him: But their time should endure for ever.

v16 He would feed them also with the finest of the wheat; And with honey out of the rock would I satisfy thee.      Psalm 82 A Psalm of Asaph.
