# Psalms 99 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 98|← Psalms 98]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 100|Psalms 100 →]]

***

v1 Jehovah reigneth; let the peoples tremble: He sitteth [above] the cherubim; let the earth be moved.

v2 Jehovah is great in Zion; And he is high above all the peoples.

v3 Let them praise thy great and terrible name: Holy is he.

v4 The king's strength also loveth justice; Thou dost establish equity; Thou executest justice and righteousness in Jacob.

v5 Exalt ye Jehovah our God, And worship at his footstool: Holy is he.

v6 Moses and Aaron among his priests, And Samuel among them that call upon his name; They called upon Jehovah, and he answered them.

v7 He spake unto them in the pillar of cloud: They kept his testimonies, And the statute that he gave them.

v8 Thou answeredst them, O Jehovah our God: Thou wast a God that forgavest them, Though thou tookest vengeance of their doings.

v9 Exalt ye Jehovah our God, And worship at his holy hill; For Jehovah our God is holy.      Psalm 100    A Psalm of thanksgiving.
