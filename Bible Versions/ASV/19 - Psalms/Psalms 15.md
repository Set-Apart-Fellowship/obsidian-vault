# Psalms 15 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 14|← Psalms 14]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 16|Psalms 16 →]]

***

v1 Jehovah, who shall sojourn in thy tabernacle? Who shall dwell in thy holy hill?

v2 He that walketh uprightly, and worketh righteousness, And speaketh truth in his heart;

v3 He that slandereth not with his tongue, Nor doeth evil to his friend, Nor taketh up a reproach against his neighbor;

v4 In whose eyes a reprobate is despised, But who honoreth them that fear Jehovah; He that sweareth to his own hurt, and changeth not;

v5 He that putteth not out his money to interest, Nor taketh reward against the innocent. He that doeth these things shall never be moved.      Psalm 16 Michtam of David.
