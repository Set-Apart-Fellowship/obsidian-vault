# Psalms 131 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 130|← Psalms 130]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 132|Psalms 132 →]]

***

v1 Jehovah, my heart is not haughty, nor mine eyes lofty; Neither do I exercise myself in great matters, Or in things too wonderful for me.

v2 Surely I have stilled and quieted my soul; Like a weaned child with his mother, Like a weaned child is my soul within me.

v3 O Israel, hope in Jehovah From this time forth and for evermore.      Psalm 132    A Song of Ascents.
