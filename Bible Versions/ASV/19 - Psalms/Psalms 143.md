# Psalms 143 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 142|← Psalms 142]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 144|Psalms 144 →]]

***

v1 Hear my prayer, O Jehovah; give ear to my supplications: In thy faithfulness answer me, [and] in thy righteousness.

v2 And enter not into judgment with thy servant; For in thy sight no man living is righteous.

v3 For the enemy hath persecuted my soul; He hath smitten my life down to the ground: He hath made me to dwell in dark places, as those that have been    long dead.

v4 Therefore is my spirit overwhelmed within me; My heart within me is desolate.

v5 I remember the days of old; I meditate on all thy doings; I muse on the work of thy hands.

v6 I spread forth my hands unto thee: My soul [thirsteth] after thee, as a weary land.   Selah

v7 Make haste to answer me, O Jehovah; my spirit faileth: Hide not thy face from me, Lest I become like them that go down into the pit.

v8 Cause me to hear thy lovingkindness in the morning; For in thee do I trust: Cause me to know the way wherein I should walk; For I lift up my soul unto thee.

v9 Deliver me, O Jehovah, from mine enemies: I flee unto thee to hide me.

v10 Teach me to do thy will; For thou art my God: Thy Spirit is good; Lead me in the land of uprightness.

v11 Quicken me, O Jehovah, for thy name's sake: In thy righteousness bring my soul out of trouble.

v12 And in thy lovingkindness cut off mine enemies, And destroy all them that afflict my soul; For I am thy servant.      Psalm 144    [A Psalm] of David.
