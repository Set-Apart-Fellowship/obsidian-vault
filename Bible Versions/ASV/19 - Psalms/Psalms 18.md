# Psalms 18 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 17|← Psalms 17]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 19|Psalms 19 →]]

***

v1 I love thee, O Jehovah, my strength.

v2 Jehovah is my rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer; My God, my rock, in whom I will take refuge; My shield, and the horn of my salvation, my high tower.

v3 I will call upon Jehovah, who is worthy to be praised: So shall I be saved from mine enemies.

v4 The cords of death compassed me, And the floods of ungodliness made me afraid.

v5 The cords of Sheol were round about me; The snares of death came upon me.

v6 In my distress I called upon Jehovah, And cried unto my God: He heard my voice out of his temple, And my cry before him came into his ears.

v7 Then the earth shook and trembled; The foundations also of the mountains quaked And were shaken, because he was wroth.

v8 There went up a smoke out of his nostrils, And fire out of his mouth devoured: Coals were kindled by it.

v9 He bowed the heavens also, and came down; And thick darkness was under his feet.

v10 And he rode upon a cherub, and did fly; Yea, he soared upon the wings of the wind.

v11 He made darkness his hiding-place, his pavilion round    about him, Darkness of waters, thick clouds of the skies.

v12 At the brightness before him his thick clouds passed, Hailstones and coals of fire.

v13 Jehovah also thundered in the heavens, And the Most High uttered his voice, Hailstones and coals of fire.

v14 And he sent out his arrows, and scattered them; Yea, lightnings manifold, and discomfited them.

v15 Then the channels of waters appeared, And the foundations of the world were laid bare, At thy rebuke, O Jehovah, At the blast of the breath of thy nostrils.

v16 He sent from on high, he took me; He drew me out of many waters.

v17 He delivered me from my strong enemy, And from them that hated me; for they were too mighty for me.

v18 They came upon me in the day of my calamity; But Jehovah was my stay.

v19 He brought me forth also into a large place; He delivered me, because he delighted in me.

v20 Jehovah hath rewarded me according to my righteousness; According to the cleanness of my hands hath he recompensed me.

v21 For I have kept the ways of Jehovah, And have not wickedly departed from my God.

v22 For all his ordinances were before me, And I put not away his statutes from me.

v23 I was also perfect with him, And I kept myself from mine iniquity.

v24 Therefore hath Jehovah recompensed me according to my    righteousness, According to the cleanness of my hands in his eyesight.

v25 With the merciful thou wilt show thyself merciful; With the perfect man thou wilt show thyself perfect;

v26 With the pure thou wilt show thyself pure; And with the perverse thou wilt show thyself froward.

v27 For thou wilt save the afflicted people; But the haughty eyes thou wilt bring down.

v28 For thou wilt light my lamp: Jehovah my God will lighten my darkness.

v29 For by thee I run upon a troop; And by my God do I leap over a wall.

v30 As for God, his way is perfect: The word of Jehovah is tried; He is a shield unto all them that take refuge in him.

v31 For who is God, save Jehovah? And who is a rock, besides our God,

v32 The God that girdeth me with strength, And maketh my way perfect?

v33 He maketh my feet like hinds' [feet]: And setteth me upon my high places.

v34 He teacheth my hands to war; So that mine arms do bend a bow of brass.

v35 Thou hast also given me the shield of thy salvation; And thy right hand hath holden me up, And thy gentleness hath made me great.

v36 Thou hast enlarged my steps under me, And my feet have not slipped.

v37 I will pursue mine enemies, and overtake them; Neither will I turn again till they are consumed.

v38 I will smite them through, so that they shall not be    able to rise: They shall fall under my feet.

v39 For thou hast girded me with strength unto the battle: Thou hast subdued under me those that rose up against me.

v40 Thou hast also made mine enemies turn their backs unto    me, That I might cut off them that hate me.

v41 They cried, but there was none to save; Even unto Jehovah, but he answered them not.

v42 Then did I beat them small as the dust before the wind; I did cast them out as the mire of the streets.

v43 Thou hast delivered me from the strivings of the people; Thou hast made me the head of the nations: A people whom I have not known shall serve me.

v44 As soon as they hear of me they shall obey me; The foreigners shall submit themselves unto me.

v45 The foreigners shall fade away, And shall come trembling out of their close places.

v46 Jehovah liveth; and blessed be my rock; And exalted be the God of my salvation,

v47 Even the God that executeth vengeance for me, And subdueth peoples under me.

v48 He rescueth me from mine enemies; Yea, thou liftest me up above them that rise up against me; Thou deliverest me from the violent man.

v49 Therefore I will give thanks unto thee, O Jehovah, among    the nations, And will sing praises unto thy name.

v50 Great deliverance giveth he to his king, And showeth lovingkindness to his anointed, To David and to his seed, for evermore.      Psalm 19 For the Chief Musician. A Psalm of David.
