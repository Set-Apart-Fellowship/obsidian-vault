# Psalms 11 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 10|← Psalms 10]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 12|Psalms 12 →]]

***

v1 In Jehovah do I take refuge: How say ye to my soul, Flee [as] a bird to your mountain;

v2 For, lo, the wicked bend the bow, They make ready their arrow upon the string, That they may shoot in darkness at the upright in heart;

v3 If the foundations be destroyed, What can the righteous do?

v4 Jehovah is in his holy temple; Jehovah, his throne is in heaven; His eyes behold, his eyelids try, the children of men.

v5 Jehovah trieth the righteous; But the wicked and him that loveth violence his soul hateth.

v6 Upon the wicked he will rain snares; Fire and brimstone and burning wind shall be the portion of    their cup.

v7 For Jehovah is righteous; he loveth righteousness: The upright shall behold his face.      Psalm 12 For the Chief Musician; set to the Sheminith. A Psalm of David.
