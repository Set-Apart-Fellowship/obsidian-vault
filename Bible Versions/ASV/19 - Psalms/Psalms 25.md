# Psalms 25 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 24|← Psalms 24]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 26|Psalms 26 →]]

***

v1 Unto thee, O Jehovah, do I lift up my soul.

v2 O my God, in thee have I trusted, Let me not be put to shame; Let not mine enemies triumph over me.

v3 Yea, none that wait for thee shall be put to shame: They shall be put to shame that deal treacherously without cause.

v4 Show me thy ways, O Jehovah; Teach me thy paths.

v5 Guide me in thy truth, and teach me; For thou art the God of my salvation; For thee do I wait all the day.

v6 Remember, O Jehovah, thy tender mercies and thy    lovingkindness; For they have been ever of old.

v7 Remember not the sins of my youth, nor my transgressions: According to thy lovingkindness remember thou me, For thy goodness' sake, O Jehovah.

v8 Good and upright is Jehovah: Therefore will he instruct sinners in the way.

v9 The meek will he guide in justice; And the meek will he teach his way.

v10 All the paths of Jehovah are lovingkindness and truth Unto such as keep his covenant and his testimonies.

v11 For thy name's sake, O Jehovah, Pardon mine iniquity, for it is great.

v12 What man is he that feareth Jehovah? Him shall he instruct in the way that he shall choose.

v13 His soul shall dwell at ease; And his seed shall inherit the land.

v14 The friendship of Jehovah is with them that fear him; And he will show them his covenant.

v15 Mine eyes are ever toward Jehovah; For he will pluck my feet out of the net.

v16 Turn thee unto me, and have mercy upon me; For I am desolate and afflicted.

v17 The troubles of my heart are enlarged: Oh bring thou me out of my distresses.

v18 Consider mine affliction and my travail; And forgive all my sins.

v19 Consider mine enemies, for they are many; And they hate me with cruel hatred.

v20 Oh keep my soul, and deliver me: Let me not be put to shame, for I take refuge in thee.

v21 Let integrity and uprightness preserve me, For I wait for thee.

v22 Redeem Israel, O God, Out all of his troubles.      Psalm 26 [A Psalm] of David.
