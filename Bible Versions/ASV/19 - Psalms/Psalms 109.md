# Psalms 109 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 108|← Psalms 108]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 110|Psalms 110 →]]

***

v1 Hold not thy peace, O God of my praise;

v2 For the mouth of the wicked and the mouth of deceit have    they opened against me: They have spoken unto me with a lying tongue.

v3 They have compassed me about also with words of hatred, And fought against me without a cause.

v4 For my love they are my adversaries: But I [give myself unto] prayer.

v5 And they have rewarded me evil for good, And hatred for my love.

v6 Set thou a wicked man over him; And let an adversary stand at his right hand.

v7 When he is judged, let him come forth guilty; And let his prayer be turned into sin.

v8 Let his days be few; [And] let another take his office.

v9 Let his children be fatherless, And his wife a widow.

v10 Let his children be vagabonds, and beg; And let them seek [their bread] out of their desolate places.

v11 Let the extortioner catch all that he hath; And let strangers make spoil of his labor.

v12 Let there be none to extend kindness unto him; Neither let there be any to have pity on his fatherless children.

v13 Let his posterity be cut off; In the generation following let their name be blotted out.

v14 Let the iniquity of his fathers be remembered with    Jehovah; And let not the sin of his mother be blotted out.

v15 Let them be before Jehovah continually, That he may cut off the memory of them from the earth;

v16 Because he remembered not to show kindness, But persecuted the poor and needy man, And the broken in heart, to slay [them].

v17 Yea, he loved cursing, and it came unto him; And he delighted not in blessing, and it was far from him.

v18 He clothed himself also with cursing as with his    garment, And it came into his inward parts like water, And like oil into his bones.

v19 Let it be unto him as the raiment wherewith he covereth    himself, And for the girdle wherewith he is girded continually.

v20 This is the reward of mine adversaries from Jehovah, And of them that speak evil against my soul.

v21 But deal thou with me, O Jehovah the Lord, for thy    name's sake: Because thy lovingkindness is good, deliver thou me;

v22 For I am poor and needy, And my heart is wounded within me.

v23 I am gone like the shadow when it declineth: I am tossed up and down as the locust.

v24 My knees are weak through fasting; And my flesh faileth of fatness.

v25 I am become also a reproach unto them: When they see me, they shake their head.

v26 Help me, O Jehovah my God; Oh save me according to thy lovingkindness:

v27 That they may know that this is thy hand; [That] thou, Jehovah, hast done it.

v28 Let them curse, but bless thou: When they arise, they shall be put to shame, But thy servant shall rejoice.

v29 Let mine adversaries be clothed with dishonor, And let them cover themselves with their own shame as with a    robe.

v30 I will give great thanks unto Jehovah with my mouth; Yea, I will praise him among the multitude.

v31 For he will stand at the right hand of the needy, To save him from them that judge his soul.      Psalm 110    A Psalm of David.
