# Psalms 142 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 141|← Psalms 141]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 143|Psalms 143 →]]

***

v1 I cry with my voice unto Jehovah; With my voice unto Jehovah do I make supplication.

v2 I pour out my complaint before him; I show before him my trouble.

v3 When my spirit was overwhelmed within me, Thou knewest my path. In the way wherein I walk Have they hidden a snare for me.

v4 Look on [my] right hand, and see; For there is no man that knoweth me: Refuge hath failed me; No man careth for my soul.

v5 I cried unto thee, O Jehovah; I said, Thou art my refuge, My portion in the land of the living.

v6 Attend unto my cry; For I am brought very low: Deliver me from my persecutors; For they are stronger than I.

v7 Bring my soul out of prison, That I may give thanks unto thy name: The righteous shall compass me about; For thou wilt deal bountifully with me.      Psalm 143    A Psalm of David.
