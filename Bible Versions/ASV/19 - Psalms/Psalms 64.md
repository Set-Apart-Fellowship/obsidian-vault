# Psalms 64 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 63|← Psalms 63]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 65|Psalms 65 →]]

***

v1 Hear my voice, O God, in my complaint: Preserve my life from fear of the enemy.

v2 Hide me from the secret counsel of evil-doers, From the tumult of the workers of iniquity;

v3 Who have whet their tongue like a sword, And have aimed their arrows, even bitter words,

v4 That they may shoot in secret places at the perfect: Suddenly do they shoot at him, and fear not.

v5 They encourage themselves in an evil purpose; They commune of laying snares privily; They say, Who will see them?

v6 They search out iniquities; We have accomplished, [say they], a diligent search: And the inward thought and the heart of every one is deep.

v7 But God will shoot at them; With an arrow suddenly shall they be wounded.

v8 So they shall be made to stumble, their own tongue being    against them: All that see them shall wag the head.

v9 And all men shall fear; And they shall declare the work of God, And shall wisely consider of his doing.

v10 The righteous shall be glad in Jehovah, and shall take    refuge in him; And all the upright in heart shall glory.      Psalm 65 For the Chief Musician. A Psalm. A song of David.
