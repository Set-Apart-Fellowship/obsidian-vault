# Psalms 147 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 146|← Psalms 146]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 148|Psalms 148 →]]

***

v1 Praise ye Jehovah; For it is good to sing praises unto our God; For it is pleasant, [and] praise is comely.

v2 Jehovah doth build up Jerusalem; He gathereth together the outcasts of Israel.

v3 He healeth the broken in heart, And bindeth up their wounds.

v4 He counteth the number of the stars; He calleth them all by [their] names.

v5 Great is our Lord, and mighty in power; His understanding is infinite.

v6 Jehovah upholdeth the meek: He bringeth the wicked down to the ground.

v7 Sing unto Jehovah with thanksgiving; Sing praises upon the harp unto our God,

v8 Who covereth the heavens with clouds, Who prepareth rain for the earth, Who maketh grass to grow upon the mountains.

v9 He giveth to the beast his food, [And] to the young ravens which cry.

v10 He delighteth not in the strength of the horse: He taketh no pleasure in the legs of a man.

v11 Jehovah taketh pleasure in them that fear him, In those that hope in his lovingkindness.

v12 Praise Jehovah, O Jerusalem; Praise thy God, O Zion.

v13 For he hath strengthened the bars of thy gates; He hath blessed thy children within thee.

v14 He maketh peace in thy borders; He filleth thee with the finest of the wheat.

v15 He sendeth out his commandment upon earth; His word runneth very swiftly.

v16 He giveth snow like wool; He scattereth the hoar-frost like ashes.

v17 He casteth forth his ice like morsels: Who can stand before his cold?

v18 He sendeth out his word, and melteth them: He causeth his wind to blow, and the waters flow.

v19 He showeth his word unto Jacob, His statutes and his ordinances unto Israel.

v20 He hath not dealt so with any nation; And as for his ordinances, they have not known them. Praise ye Jehovah.      Psalm 148
