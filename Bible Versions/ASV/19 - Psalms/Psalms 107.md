# Psalms 107 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 106|← Psalms 106]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 108|Psalms 108 →]]

***

v1 O give thanks unto Jehovah; For he is good; For his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever.

v2 Let the redeemed of Jehovah say [so], Whom he hath redeemed from the hand of the adversary,

v3 And gathered out of the lands, From the east and from the west, From the north and from the south.

v4 They wandered in the wilderness in a desert way; They found no city of habitation.

v5 Hungry and thirsty, Their soul fainted in them.

v6 Then they cried unto Jehovah in their trouble, And he delivered them out of their distresses,

v7 He led them also by a straight way, That they might go to a city of habitation.

v8 Oh that men would praise Jehovah for his lovingkindness, And for his wonderful works to the children of men!

v9 For he satisfieth the longing soul, And the hungry soul he filleth with good.

v10 Such as sat in darkness and in the shadow of death, Being bound in affliction and iron,

v11 Because they rebelled against the words of God, And contemned the counsel of the Most High:

v12 Therefore he brought down their heart with labor; They fell down, and there was none to help.

v13 Then they cried unto Jehovah in their trouble, And he saved them out of their distresses.

v14 He brought them out of darkness and the shadow of death, And brake their bonds in sunder.

v15 Oh that men would praise Jehovah for his lovingkindness, And for his wonderful works to the children of men!

v16 For he hath broken the gates of brass, And cut the bars of iron in sunder.

v17 Fools because of their transgression, And because of their iniquities, are afflicted.

v18 Their soul abhorreth all manner of food; And they draw near unto the gates of death.

v19 Then they cry unto Jehovah in their trouble, And he saveth them out of their distresses.

v20 He sendeth his word, and healeth them, And delivereth [them] from their destructions.

v21 Oh that men would praise Jehovah for his lovingkindness, And for his wonderful works to the children of men!

v22 And let them offer the sacrifices of thanksgiving, And declare his works with singing.

v23 They that go down to the sea in ships, That do business in great waters;

v24 These see the works of Jehovah, And his wonders in the deep.

v25 For he commandeth, and raiseth the stormy wind, Which lifteth up the waves thereof.

v26 They mount up to the heavens, they go down again to the    depths: Their soul melteth away because of trouble.

v27 They reel to and fro, and stagger like a drunken man, And are at their wits' end.

v28 Then they cry unto Jehovah in their trouble, And he bringeth them out of their distresses.

v29 He maketh the storm a calm, So that the waves thereof are still.

v30 Then are they glad because they are quiet; So he bringeth them unto their desired haven.

v31 Oh that men would praise Jehovah for his lovingkindness, And for his wonderful works to the children of men!

v32 Let them exalt him also in the assembly of the people, And praise him in the seat of the elders.

v33 He turneth rivers into a wilderness, And watersprings into a thirsty ground;

v34 A fruitful land into a salt desert, For the wickedness of them that dwell therein.

v35 He turneth a wilderness into a pool of water, And a dry land into watersprings.

v36 And there he maketh the hungry to dwell, That they may prepare a city of habitation,

v37 And sow fields, and plant vineyards, And get them fruits of increase.

v38 He blesseth them also, so that they are multiplied    greatly; And he suffereth not their cattle to decrease.

v39 Again, they are diminished and bowed down Through oppression, trouble, and sorrow.

v40 He poureth contempt upon princes, And causeth them to wander in the waste, where there is no way.

v41 Yet setteth he the needy on high from affliction, And maketh [him] families like a flock.

v42 The upright shall see it, and be glad; And all iniquity shall stop her mouth.

v43 Whoso is wise will give heed to these things; And they will consider the lovingkindnesses of Jehovah.      Psalm 108    A Song, A Psalm of David.
