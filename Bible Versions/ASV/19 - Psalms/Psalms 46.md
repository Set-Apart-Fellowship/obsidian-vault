# Psalms 46 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 45|← Psalms 45]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 47|Psalms 47 →]]

***

v1 God is our refuge and strength, A very present help in trouble.

v2 Therefore will we not fear, though the earth do change, And though the mountains be shaken into the heart of the seas;

v3 Though the waters thereof roar and be troubled, Though the mountains tremble with the swelling thereof.   Selah

v4 There is a river, the streams whereof make glad the city    of God, The holy place of the tabernacles of the Most High.

v5 God is in the midst of her; she shall not be moved: God will help her, and that right early.

v6 The nations raged, the kingdoms were moved: He uttered his voice, the earth melted.

v7 Jehovah of hosts is with us; The God of Jacob is our refuge.   Selah

v8 Come, behold the works of Jehovah, What desolations he hath made in the earth.

v9 He maketh wars to cease unto the end of the earth; He breaketh the bow, and cutteth the spear in sunder; He burneth the chariots in the fire.

v10 Be still, and know that I am God: I will be exalted among the nations, I will be exalted in the    earth.

v11 Jehovah of hosts is with us; The God of Jacob is our refuge.   Selah      Psalm 47 For the Chief Musician. A Psalm of the sons of Korah.
