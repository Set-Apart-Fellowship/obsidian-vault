# Psalms 89 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 88|← Psalms 88]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 90|Psalms 90 →]]

***

v1 I will sing of the lovingkindness of Jehovah for ever: With my mouth will I make known thy faithfulness to all    generations.

v2 For I have said, Mercy shall be built up for ever; Thy faithfulness wilt thou establish in the very heavens.

v3 I have made a covenant with my chosen, I have sworn unto David my servant:

v4 Thy seed will I establish for ever, And build up thy throne to all generations.   Selah

v5 And the heavens shall praise thy wonders, O Jehovah; Thy faithfulness also in the assembly of the holy ones.

v6 For who in the skies can be compared unto Jehovah? Who among the sons of the mighty is like unto Jehovah,

v7 A God very terrible in the council of the holy ones, And to be feared above all them that are round about him?

v8 O Jehovah God of hosts, Who is a mighty one, like unto thee, O Jehovah? And thy faithfulness is round about thee.

v9 Thou rulest the pride of the sea: When the waves thereof arise, thou stillest them.

v10 Thou hast broken Rahab in pieces, as one that is slain; Thou hast scattered thine enemies with the arm of thy strength.

v11 The heavens are thine, the earth also is thine: The world and the fulness thereof, thou hast founded them.

v12 The north and the south, thou hast created them: Tabor and Hermon rejoice in thy name.

v13 Thou hast a mighty arm; Strong is thy hand, and high is thy right hand.

v14 Righteousness and justice are the foundation of thy    throne: Lovingkindness and truth go before thy face.

v15 Blessed is the people that know the joyful sound: They walk, O Jehovah, in the light of thy countenance.

v16 In thy name do they rejoice all the day; And in thy righteousness are they exalted.

v17 For thou art the glory of their strength; And in thy favor our horn shall be exalted.

v18 For our shield belongeth unto Jehovah; And our king to the Holy One of Israel.

v19 Then thou spakest in vision to thy saints, And saidst, I have laid help upon one that is mighty; I have exalted one chosen out of the people.

v20 I have found David my servant; With my holy oil have I anointed him:

v21 With whom my hand shall be established; Mine arm also shall strengthen him.

v22 The enemy shall not exact from him, Nor the son of wickedness afflict him.

v23 And I will beat down his adversaries before him, And smite them that hate him.

v24 But my faithfulness and my lovingkindness shall be with    him; And in my name shall his horn be exalted.

v25 I will set his hand also on the sea, And his right hand on the rivers.

v26 He shall cry unto me, Thou art my Father, My God, and the rock of my salvation.

v27 I also will make him [my] first-born, The highest of the kings of the earth.

v28 My lovingkindness will I keep for him for evermore; And my covenant shall stand fast with him.

v29 His seed also will I make to endure for ever, And his throne as the days of heaven.

v30 If his children forsake my law, And walk not in mine ordinances;

v31 If they break my statutes, And keep not my commandments;

v32 Then will I visit their transgression with the rod, And their iniquity with stripes.

v33 But my lovingkindness will I not utterly take from him, Nor suffer my faithfulness to fail.

v34 My covenant will I not break, Nor alter the thing that is gone out of my lips.

v35 Once have I sworn by my holiness: I will not lie unto David:

v36 His seed shall endure for ever, And his throne as the sun before me.

v37 It shall be established for ever as the moon, And [as] the faithful witness in the sky.   Selah

v38 But thou hast cast off and rejected, Thou hast been wroth with thine anointed.

v39 Thou hast abhorred the covenant of thy servant: Thou hast profaned his crown [by casting it] to the ground.

v40 Thou hast broken down all his hedges; Thou hast brought his strongholds to ruin.

v41 All that pass by the way rob him: He is become a reproach to his neighbors.

v42 Thou hast exalted the right hand of his adversaries; Thou hast made all his enemies to rejoice.

v43 Yea, thou turnest back the edge of his sword, And hast not made him to stand in the battle.

v44 Thou hast made his brightness to cease, And cast his throne down to the ground.

v45 The days of his youth hast thou shortened: Thou hast covered him with shame.   Selah

v46 How long, O Jehovah? wilt thou hide thyself for ever? [How long] shall thy wrath burn like fire?

v47 Oh remember how short my time is: For what vanity hast thou created all the children of men!

v48 What man is he that shall live and not see death, That shall deliver his soul from the power of Sheol?   Selah

v49 Lord, where are thy former lovingkindnesses, Which thou swarest unto David in thy faithfulness?

v50 Remember, Lord, the reproach of thy servants; How I do bear in my bosom [the reproach of] all the mighty    peoples,

v51 Wherewith thine enemies have reproached, O Jehovah, Wherewith they have reproached the footsteps of thine anointed.

v52 Blessed be Jehovah for evermore. Amen, and Amen.     BOOK IV   Psalm 90 A Prayer of Moses the man of God.
