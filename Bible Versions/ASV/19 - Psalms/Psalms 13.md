# Psalms 13 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 12|← Psalms 12]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 14|Psalms 14 →]]

***

v1 How long, O Jehovah? wilt thou forget me for ever? How long wilt thou hide thy face from me?

v2 How long shall I take counsel in my soul, Having sorrow in my heart all the day? How long shall mine enemy be exalted over me?

v3 Consider [and] answer me, O Jehovah my God: Lighten mine eyes, lest I sleep the [sleep of] death;

v4 Lest mine enemy say, I have prevailed against him; [Lest] mine adversaries rejoice when I am moved.

v5 But I have trusted in thy lovingkindness; My heart shall rejoice in thy salvation.

v6 I will sing unto Jehovah, Because he hath dealt bountifully with me.      Psalm 14 For the Chief Musician. [A Psalm] of David.
