# Psalms 82 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 81|← Psalms 81]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 83|Psalms 83 →]]

***

v1 God standeth in the congregation of God; He judgeth among the gods.

v2 How long will ye judge unjustly, And respect the persons of the wicked?   Selah

v3 Judge the poor and fatherless: Do justice to the afflicted and destitute.

v4 Rescue the poor and needy: Deliver them out of the hand of the wicked.

v5 They know not, neither do they understand; They walk to and fro in darkness: All the foundations of the earth are shaken.

v6 I said, Ye are gods, And all of you sons of the Most High.

v7 Nevertheless ye shall die like men, And fall like one of the princes.

v8 Arise, O God, judge the earth; For thou shalt inherit all the nations.      Psalm 83 A song. A Psalm of Asaph.
