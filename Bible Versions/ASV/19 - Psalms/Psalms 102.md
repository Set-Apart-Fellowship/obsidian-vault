# Psalms 102 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 101|← Psalms 101]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 103|Psalms 103 →]]

***

v1 Hear my prayer, O Jehovah, And let my cry come unto thee.

v2 Hide not thy face from me in the day of my distress: Incline thine ear unto me; In the day when I call answer me speedily.

v3 For my days consume away like smoke, And my bones are burned as a firebrand.

v4 My heart is smitten like grass, and withered; For I forget to eat my bread.

v5 By reason of the voice of my groaning My bones cleave to my flesh.

v6 I am like a pelican of the wilderness; I am become as an owl of the waste places.

v7 I watch, and am become like a sparrow That is alone upon the house-top.

v8 Mine enemies reproach me all the day; They that are mad against me do curse by me.

v9 For I have eaten ashes like bread, And mingled my drink with weeping,

v10 Because of thine indignation and thy wrath: For thou hast taken me up, and cast me away.

v11 My days are like a shadow that declineth; And I am withered like grass.

v12 But thou, O Jehovah, wilt abook_ide for ever; And thy memorial [name] unto all generations.

v13 Thou wilt arise, and have mercy upon Zion; For it is time to have pity upon her, Yea, the set time is come.

v14 For thy servants take pleasure in her stones, And have pity upon her dust.

v15 So the nations shall fear the name of Jehovah, And all the kings of the earth thy glory.

v16 For Jehovah hath built up Zion; He hath appeared in his glory.

v17 He hath regarded the prayer of the destitute, And hath not despised their prayer.

v18 This shall be written for the generation to come; And a people which shall be created shall praise Jehovah.

v19 For he hath looked down from the height of his    sanctuary; From heaven did Jehovah behold the earth;

v20 To hear the sighing of the prisoner; To loose those that are appointed to death;

v21 That men may declare the name of Jehovah in Zion, And his praise in Jerusalem;

v22 When the peoples are gathered together, And the kingdoms, to serve Jehovah.

v23 He weakened my strength in the way; He shortened my days.

v24 I said, O my God, take me not away in the midst of my    days: Thy years are throughout all generations.

v25 Of old didst thou lay the foundation of the earth; And the heavens are the work of thy hands.

v26 They shall perish, but thou shalt endure; Yea, all of them shall wax old like a garment; As a vesture shalt thou change them, and they shall be changed:

v27 But thou art the same, And thy years shall have no end.

v28 The children of thy servants shall continue, And their seed shall be established before thee.      Psalm 103    [A Psalm] of David.
