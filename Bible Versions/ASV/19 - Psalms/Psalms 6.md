# Psalms 6 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 5|← Psalms 5]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 7|Psalms 7 →]]

***

v1 O Jehovah, rebuke me not in thine anger, Neither chasten me in thy hot displeasure.

v2 Have mercy upon me, O Jehovah; for I am withered away: O Jehovah, heal me; for my bones are troubled.

v3 My soul also is sore troubled: And thou, O Jehovah, how long?

v4 Return, O Jehovah, deliver my soul: Save me for thy lovingkindness' sake.

v5 For in death there is no remembrance of thee: In Sheol who shall give thee thanks?

v6 I am weary with my groaning; Every night make I my bed to swim; I water my couch with my tears.

v7 Mine eye wasteth away because of grief; It waxeth old because of all mine adversaries.

v8 Depart from me, all ye workers of iniquity; For Jehovah hath heard the voice of my weeping.

v9 Jehovah hath heard my supplication; Jehovah will receive my prayer.

v10 All mine enemies shall be put to shame and sore troubled: They shall turn back, they shall be put to shame suddenly.      Psalm 7 Shiggaion of David, which he sang unto Jehova, concerning the words of Cush a Benjamite.
