# Psalms 76 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 75|← Psalms 75]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 77|Psalms 77 →]]

***

v1 In Judah is God known: His name is great in Israel.

v2 In Salem also is his tabernacle, And his dwelling-place in Zion.

v3 There he brake the arrows of the bow; The shield, and the sword, and the battle.   Selah

v4 Glorious art thou [and] excellent, From the mountains of prey.

v5 The stouthearted are made a spoil, They have slept their sleep; And none of the men of might have found their hands.

v6 At thy rebuke, O God of Jacob, Both chariot and horse are cast into a deep sleep.

v7 Thou, even thou, art to be feared; And who may stand in thy sight when once thou art angry?

v8 Thou didst cause sentence to be heard from heaven; The earth feared, and was still,

v9 When God arose to judgment, To save all the meek of the earth.   Selah

v10 Surely the wrath of man shall praise thee: The residue of wrath shalt thou gird upon thee.

v11 Vow, and pay unto Jehovah your God: Let all that are round about him bring presents unto him that    ought to be feared.

v12 He will cut off the spirit of princes: He is terrible to the kings of the earth.      Psalm 77 For the Chief Musician; after the manner of Jeduthan. A Psalm of Asaph.
