# Psalms 67 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 66|← Psalms 66]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 68|Psalms 68 →]]

***

v1 God be merciful unto us, and bless us, [And] cause his face to shine upon us;   Selah

v2 That thy way may be known upon earth, Thy salvation among all nations.

v3 Let the peoples praise thee, O God; Let all the peoples praise thee.

v4 Oh let the nations be glad and sing for joy; For thou wilt judge the peoples with equity, And govern the nations upon earth.   Selah

v5 Let the peoples praise thee, O God; Let all the peoples praise thee.

v6 The earth hath yielded its increase: God, even our own God, will bless us.

v7 God will bless us; And all the ends of the earth shall fear him.      Psalm 68 For the Chief Musician; A Psalm of David, a song.
