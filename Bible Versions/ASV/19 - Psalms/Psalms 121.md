# Psalms 121 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 120|← Psalms 120]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 122|Psalms 122 →]]

***

v1 I will lift up mine eyes unto the mountains: From whence shall my help come?

v2 My help [cometh] from Jehovah, Who made heaven and earth.

v3 He will not suffer thy foot to be moved: He that keepeth thee will not slumber.

v4 Behold, he that keepeth Israel Will neither slumber nor sleep.

v5 Jehovah is thy keeper: Jehovah is thy shade upon thy right hand.

v6 The sun shall not smite thee by day, Nor the moon by night.

v7 Jehovah will keep thee from all evil; He will keep thy soul.

v8 Jehovah will keep thy going out and thy coming in From this time forth and for evermore.      Psalm 122    A Song of Ascents; of David.
