# Psalms 95 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 94|← Psalms 94]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 96|Psalms 96 →]]

***

v1 Oh come, let us sing unto Jehovah; Let us make a joyful noise to the rock of our salvation.

v2 Let us come before his presence with thanksgiving; Let us make a joyful noise unto him with psalms.

v3 For Jehovah is a great God, And a great King above all gods.

v4 In his hand are the deep places of the earth; The heights of the mountains are his also.

v5 The sea is his, and he made it; And his hands formed the dry land.

v6 Oh come, let us worship and bow down; Let us kneel before Jehovah our Maker:

v7 For he is our God, And we are the people of his pasture, and the sheep of his hand. To-day, oh that ye would hear his voice!

v8 Harden not your heart, as at Meribah, As in the day of Massah in the wilderness;

v9 When your fathers tempted me, Proved me, and saw my work.

v10 Forty years long was I grieved with [that] generation, And said, It is a people that do err in their heart, And they have not known my ways:

v11 Wherefore I sware in my wrath, That they should not enter into my rest.      Psalm 96
