# Psalms 7 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 6|← Psalms 6]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 8|Psalms 8 →]]

***

v1 O Jehovah my God, in thee do I take refuge: Save me from all them that pursue me, and deliver me,

v2 Lest they tear my soul like a lion, Rending it in pieces, while there is none to deliver.

v3 O Jehovah my God, if I have done this; If there be iniquity in my hands;

v4 If I have rewarded evil unto him that was at peace with me; (Yea, I have delivered him that without cause was mine    adversary;)

v5 Let the enemy pursue my soul, and overtake it; Yea, let him tread my life down to the earth, And lay my glory in the dust.   Selah

v6 Arise, O Jehovah, in thine anger; Lift up thyself against the rage of mine adversaries, And awake for me; thou hast commanded judgment.

v7 And let the congregation of the peoples compass thee about; And over them return thou on high.

v8 Jehovah ministereth judgment to the peoples: Judge me, O Jehovah, according to my righteousness, and to mine    integrity that is in me.

v9 O let the wickedness of the wicked come to an end, but    establish thou the righteous: For the righteous God trieth the minds and hearts.

v10 My shield is with God, Who saveth the upright in heart.

v11 God is a righteous judge, Yea, a God that hath indignation every day.

v12 If a man turn not, he will whet his sword; He hath bent his bow, and made it ready.

v13 He hath also prepared for him the instruments of death; He maketh his arrows fiery [shafts].

v14 Behold, he travaileth with iniquity; Yea, he hath conceived mischief, and brought forth falsehood.

v15 He hath made a pit, and digged it, And is fallen into the ditch which he made.

v16 His mischief shall return upon his own head, And his violence shall come down upon his own pate.

v17 I will give thanks unto Jehovah according to his    righteousness, And will sing praise to the name of Jehovah Most High.      Psalm 8 For the Chief Musician; set to the Gittith. A Psalm of David.
