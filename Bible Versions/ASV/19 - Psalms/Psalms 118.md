# Psalms 118 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 117|← Psalms 117]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 119|Psalms 119 →]]

***

v1 Oh give thanks unto Jehovah; for he is good; For his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever.

v2 Let Israel now say, That his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever.

v3 Let the house of Aaron now say, That his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever.

v4 Let them now that fear Jehovah say, That his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever.

v5 Out of my distress I called upon Jehovah: Jehovah answered me [and set me] in a large place.

v6 Jehovah is on my side; I will not fear: What can man do unto me?

v7 Jehovah is on my side among them that help me: Therefore shall I see [my desire] upon them that hate me.

v8 It is better to take refuge in Jehovah Than to put confidence in man.

v9 It is better to take refuge in Jehovah Than to put confidence in princes.

v10 All nations compassed me about: In the name of Jehovah I will cut them off.

v11 They compassed me about; yea, they compassed me about: In the name of Jehovah I will cut them off.

v12 They compassed me about like bees; They are quenched as the fire of thorns: In the name of Jehovah I will cut them off.

v13 Thou didst thrust sore at me that I might fall; But Jehovah helped me.

v14 Jehovah is my strength and song; And he is become my salvation.

v15 The voice of rejoicing and salvation is in the tents of    the righteous: The right hand of Jehovah doeth valiantly.

v16 The right hand of Jehovah is exalted: The right hand of Jehovah doeth valiantly.

v17 I shall not die, but live, And declare the works of Jehovah.

v18 Jehovah hath chastened me sore; But he hath not given me over unto death.

v19 Open to me the gates of righteousness: I will enter into them, I will give thanks unto Jehovah.

v20 This is the gate of Jehovah; The righteous shall enter into it.

v21 I will give thanks unto thee; for thou hast answered me, And art become my salvation.

v22 The stone which the builders rejected Is become the    head of the corner.

v23 This is Jehovah's doing; It is marvellous in our eyes.

v24 This is the day which Jehovah hath made; We will rejoice and be glad in it.

v25 Save now, we beseech thee, O Jehovah: O Jehovah, we beseech thee, send now prosperity.

v26 Blessed be he that cometh in the name of Jehovah: We have blessed you out of the house of Jehovah.

v27 Jehovah is God, and he hath given us light: Bind the sacrifice with cords, even unto the horns of the altar.

v28 Thou art my God, and I will give thanks unto thee: Thou art my God, I will exalt thee.

v29 Oh give thanks unto Jehovah; for he is good; For his lovingkindness [endureth] for ever.      Psalm 119      ALEPH.
