# Psalms 17 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 16|← Psalms 16]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 18|Psalms 18 →]]

***

v1 Hear the right, O Jehovah, attend unto my cry; Give ear unto my prayer, that goeth not out of feigned lips.

v2 Let my sentence come forth from thy presence; Let thine eyes look upon equity.

v3 Thou hast proved my heart; thou hast visited me in the    night; Thou hast tried me, and findest nothing; I am purposed that my mouth shall not transgress.

v4 As for the works of men, by the word of thy lips I have kept me from the ways of the violent.

v5 My steps have held fast to thy paths, My feet have not slipped.

v6 I have called upon thee, for thou wilt answer me, O God: Incline thine ear unto me, [and] hear my speech.

v7 Show thy marvellous lovingkindness, O thou that savest by thy right hand them that take refuge [in    thee ]From those that rise up [against them].

v8 Keep me as the apple of the eye; Hide me under the shadow of thy wings,

v9 From the wicked that oppress me, My deadly enemies, that compass me about.

v10 They are inclosed in their own fat: With their mouth they speak proudly.

v11 They have now compassed us in our steps; They set their eyes to cast [us] down to the earth.

v12 He is like a lion that is greedy of his prey, And as it were a young lion lurking in secret places.

v13 Arise, O Jehovah, Confront him, cast him down: Deliver my soul from the wicked by thy sword;

v14 From men by thy hand, O Jehovah, From men of the world, whose portion is in [this] life, And whose belly thou fillest with thy treasure: They are satisfied with children, And leave the rest of their substance to their babes.
