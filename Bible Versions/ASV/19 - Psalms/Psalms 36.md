# Psalms 36 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 35|← Psalms 35]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 37|Psalms 37 →]]

***

v1 The transgression of the wicked saith within my heart, There is no fear of God before his eyes.

v2 For he flattereth himself in his own eyes, That his iniquity will not be found out and be hated.

v3 The words of his mouth are iniquity and deceit: He hath ceased to be wise [and] to do good.

v4 He deviseth iniquity upon his bed; He setteth himself in a way that is not good; He abhorreth not evil.

v5 Thy lovingkindness, O Jehovah, is in the heavens; Thy faithfulness [reacheth] unto the skies.

v6 Thy righteousness is like the mountains of God; Thy judgments are a great deep: O Jehovah, thou preservest man and beast.

v7 How precious is thy lovingkindness, O God! And the children of men take refuge under the shadow of thy    wings.

v8 They shall be abundantly satisfied with the fatness of    thy house; And thou wilt make them drink of the river of thy pleasures.

v9 For with thee is the fountain of life: In thy light shall we see light.

v10 Oh continue thy lovingkindness unto them that know thee, And thy righteousness to the upright in heart.

v11 Let not the foot of pride come against me, And let not the hand of the wicked drive me away.

v12 There are the workers of iniquity fallen: They are thrust down, and shall not be able to rise.      Psalm 37 [A Psalm] of David.
