# Psalms 84 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 83|← Psalms 83]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 85|Psalms 85 →]]

***

v1 How amiable are thy tabernacles, O Jehovah of hosts!

v2 My soul longeth, yea, even fainteth for the courts of    Jehovah; My heart and my flesh cry out unto the living God.

v3 Yea, the sparrow hath found her a house, And the swallow a nest for herself, where she may lay her young, Even thine altars, O Jehovah of hosts, My King, and my God.

v4 Blessed are they that dwell in thy house: They will be still praising thee.   Selah

v5 Blessed is the man whose strength is in thee; In whose heart are the highways [to Zion].

v6 Passing through the valley of Weeping they make it a    place of springs; Yea, the early rain covereth it with blessings.

v7 They go from strength to strength; Every one of them appeareth before God in Zion.

v8 O Jehovah God of hosts, hear my prayer; Give ear, O God of Jacob.   Selah

v9 Behold, O God our shield, And look upon the face of thine anointed.

v10 For a day in thy courts is better than a thousand. I had rather be a doorkeeper in the house of my God, Than to dwell in the tents of wickedness.

v11 For Jehovah God is a sun and a shield: Jehovah will give grace and glory; No good thing will he withhold from them that walk uprightly.

v12 O Jehovah of hosts, Blessed is the man that trusteth in thee.      Psalm 85 For the Chief Musician. A Psalm of the sons of Korah.
