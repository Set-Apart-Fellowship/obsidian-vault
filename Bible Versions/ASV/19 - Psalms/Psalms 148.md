# Psalms 148 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 147|← Psalms 147]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 149|Psalms 149 →]]

***

v1 Praise ye Jehovah. Praise ye Jehovah from the heavens: Praise him in the heights.

v2 Praise ye him, all his angels: Praise ye him, all his host.

v3 Praise ye him, sun and moon: Praise him, all ye stars of light.

v4 Praise him, ye heavens of heavens, And ye waters that are above the heavens.

v5 Let them praise the name of Jehovah; For he commanded, and they were created.

v6 He hath also established them for ever and ever: He hath made a decree which shall not pass away.

v7 Praise Jehovah from the earth, Ye sea-monsters, and all deeps.

v8 Fire and hail, snow and vapor; Stormy wind, fulfilling his word;

v9 Mountains and all hills; Fruitful trees and all cedars;

v10 Beasts and all cattle; Creeping things and flying birds;

v11 Kings of the earth and all peoples; Princes and all judges of the earth;

v12 Both young men and virgins; Old men and children:

v13 Let them praise the name of Jehovah; For his name alone is exalted; His glory is above the earth and the heavens.

v14 And he hath lifted up the horn of his people, The praise of all his saints; Even of the children of Israel, a people near unto him. Praise ye Jehovah.      Psalm 149
