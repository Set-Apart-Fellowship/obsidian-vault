# Psalms 22 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 21|← Psalms 21]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 23|Psalms 23 →]]

***

v1 My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me? [Why art thou so] far from helping me, [and from] the words of    my groaning?

v2 O my God, I cry in the daytime, but thou answerest not; And in the night season, and am not silent.

v3 But thou art holy, O thou that inhabitest the praises of Israel.

v4 Our fathers trusted in thee: They trusted, and thou didst deliver them.

v5 They cried unto thee, and were delivered: They trusted in thee, and were not put to shame.

v6 But I am a worm, and no man; A reproach of men, and despised of the people.

v7 All they that see me laugh me to scorn: They shoot out the lip, they shake the head, [saying],

v8 Commit [thyself] unto Jehovah; Let him deliver him: Let him rescue him, seeing he delighteth in him.

v9 But thou art he that took me out of the womb; Thou didst make me trust [when I was] upon my mother's breasts.

v10 I was cast upon thee from the womb; Thou art my God since my mother bare me.

v11 Be not far from me; for trouble is near; For there is none to help.

v12 Many bulls have compassed me; Strong bulls of Bashan have beset me round.

v13 They gape upon me with their mouth, [As] a ravening and a roaring lion.

v14 I am poured out like water, And all my bones are out of joint: My heart is like wax; It is melted within me.

v15 My strength is dried up like a potsherd; And my tongue cleaveth to my jaws; And thou hast brought me into the dust of death.

v16 For dogs have compassed me: A company of evil-doers have inclosed me; They pierced my hands and my feet.

v17 I may count all my bones; They look and stare upon me.

v18 They part my garments among them, And upon my vesture do they cast lots.

v19 But be not thou far off, O Jehovah: O thou my succor, haste thee to help me.

v20 Deliver my soul from the sword, My darling from the power of the dog.

v21 Save me from the lion's mouth; Yea, from the horns of the wild-oxen thou hast answered me.

v22 I will declare thy name unto my brethren: In the midst of the assembly will I praise thee.

v23 Ye that fear Jehovah, praise him; All ye the seed of Jacob, glorify him; And stand in awe of him, all ye the seed of Israel.

v24 For he hath not despised nor abhorred the affliction of    the afflicted; Neither hath he hid his face from him; But when he cried unto him, he heard.

v25 Of thee cometh my praise in the great assembly: I will pay my vows before them that fear him.

v26 The meek shall eat and be satisfied; They shall praise Jehovah that seek after him: Let your heart live for ever.

v27 All the ends of the earth shall remember and turn unto    Jehovah; And all the kindreds of the nations shall worship before thee.

v28 For the kingdom is Jehovah's; And he is the ruler over the nations.

v29 All the fat ones of the earth shall eat and worship: All they that go down to the dust shall bow before him, Even he that cannot keep his soul alive.

v30 A seed shall serve him; It shall be told of the Lord unto the [next] generation.

v31 They shall come and shall declare his righteousness Unto a people that shall be born, that he hath done it.      Psalm 23 A Psalm of David.
