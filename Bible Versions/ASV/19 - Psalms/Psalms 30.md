# Psalms 30 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 29|← Psalms 29]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 31|Psalms 31 →]]

***

v1 I will extol thee, O Jehovah; for thou hast raised me up, And hast not made my foes to rejoice over me.

v2 O Jehovah my God, I cried unto thee, and thou hast healed me.

v3 O Jehovah, thou hast brought up my soul from Sheol; Thou hast kept me alive, that I should not go down to the pit.

v4 Sing praise unto Jehovah, O ye saints of his, And give thanks to his holy memorial [name].

v5 For his anger is but for a moment; His favor is for a life-time: Weeping may tarry for the night, But joy [cometh] in the morning.

v6 As for me, I said in my prosperity, I shall never be moved.

v7 Thou, Jehovah, of thy favor hadst made my mountain to    stand strong: Thou didst hide thy face; I was troubled.

v8 I cried to thee, O Jehovah; And unto Jehovah I made supplication:

v9 What profit is there in my blood, when I go down to the    pit? Shall the dust praise thee? shall it declare thy truth?

v10 Hear, O Jehovah, and have mercy upon me: Jehovah, be thou my helper.

v11 Thou hast turned for me my mourning into dancing; Thou hast loosed my sackcloth, and girded me with gladness;

v12 To the end that [my] glory may sing praise to thee, and    not be silent. O Jehovah my God, I will give thanks unto thee for ever.      Psalm 31 For the Chief Musician. A Psalm of David.
