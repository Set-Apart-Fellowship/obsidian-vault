# Psalms 59 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 58|← Psalms 58]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/19 - Psalms/Psalms 60|Psalms 60 →]]

***

v1 Deliver me from mine enemies, O my God: Set me on high from them that rise up against me.

v2 Deliver me from the workers of iniquity, And save me from the bloodthirsty men.

v3 For, lo, they lie in wait for my soul; The mighty gather themselves together against me: Not for my transgression, nor for my sin, O Jehovah.

v4 They run and prepare themselves without [my] fault: Awake thou to help me, and behold.

v5 Even thou, O Jehovah God of hosts, the God of Israel, Arise to visit all the nations: Be not merciful to any wicked transgressors.   Selah

v6 They return at evening, they howl like a dog, And go round about the city.

v7 Behold, they belch out with their mouth; Swords are in their lips: For who, [say they], doth hear?

v8 But thou, O Jehovah, wilt laugh at them; Thou wilt have all the nations in derision.

v9 [Because of] his strength I will give heed unto thee; For God is my high tower.

v10 My God with his lovingkindness will meet me: God will let me see [my desire] upon mine enemies.

v11 Slay them not, lest my people forget: Scatter them by thy power, and bring them down, O Lord our shield.

v12 [For] the sin of their mouth, [and] the words of their    lips, Let them even be taken in their pride, And for cursing and lying which they speak.

v13 Consume them in wrath, consume them, so that they shall    be no more: And let them know that God ruleth in Jacob, Unto the ends of the earth.   Selah

v14 And at evening let them return, let them howl like a dog, And go round about the city.

v15 They shall wander up and down for food, And tarry all night if they be not satisfied.

v16 But I will sing of thy strength; Yea, I will sing aloud of thy lovingkindness in the morning: For thou hast been my high tower, And a refuge in the day of my distress.

v17 Unto thee, O my strength, will I sing praises: For God is my high tower, the God of my mercy.      Psalm 60 For the Chief Musician; [set to] Shushan Eduth. Michtam of David, to teach; and when he strove with Aram-naharaim and with Aram-zobah, and Joab returned, and smote of Edom in the Valley of Salt twelve thousand.
