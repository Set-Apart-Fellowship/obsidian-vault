# Genesis 11 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/01 - Genesis/Genesis 10|← Genesis 10]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/01 - Genesis/Genesis]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12|Genesis 12 →]]

***

v1 And the whole earth was of one language and of one speech.

v2 And it came to pass, as they journeyed east, that they found a plain in the land of Shinar; and they dwelt there.

v3 And they said one to another, Come, let us make brick, and burn them thoroughly. And they had brick for stone, and slime had they for mortar.

v4 And they said, Come, let us build us a city, and a tower, whose top [may reach] unto heaven, and let us make us a name; lest we be scattered abroad upon the face of the whole earth.

v5 And Jehovah came down to see the city and the tower, which the children of men builded.

v6 And Jehovah said, Behold, they are one people, and they have all one language; and this is what they begin to do: and now nothing will be withholden from them, which they purpose to do.

v7 Come, let us go down, and there confound their language, that they may not understand one another's speech.

v8 So Jehovah scattered them abroad from thence upon the face of all the earth: and they left off building the city.

v9 Therefore was the name of it called Babel; because Jehovah did there confound the language of all the earth: and from thence did Jehovah scatter them abroad upon the face of all the earth.

v10 These are the generations of Shem. Shem was a hundred years old, and begat Arpachshad two years after the flood.

v11 and Shem lived after he begat Arpachshad five hundred years, and begat sons and daughters.

v12 And Arpachshad lived five and thirty years, and begat Shelah.

v13 and Arpachshad lived after he begat Shelah four hundred and three years, and begat sons and daughters.

v14 And Shelah lived thirty years, and begat Eber:

v15 and Shelah lived after he begat Eber four hundred and three years, and begat sons and daughters.

v16 And Eber lived four and thirty years, and begat Peleg:

v17 and Eber lived after he begat Peleg four hundred and thirty years, and begat sons and daughters.

v18 And Peleg lived thirty years, and begat Reu:

v19 and Peleg lived after he begat Reu two hundred and nine years, and begat sons and daughters.

v20 And Reu lived two and thirty years, and begat Serug:

v21 and Reu lived after he begat Serug two hundred and seven years, and begat sons and daughters.

v22 And Serug lived thirty years, and begat Nahor:

v23 and Serug lived after he begat Nahor two hundred years, and begat sons and daughters.

v24 And Nahor lived nine and twenty years, and begat Terah:

v25 and Nahor lived after he begat Terah a hundred and nineteen years, and begat sons and daughters.

v26 And Terah lived seventy years, and begat Abram, Nahor, and Haran.

v27 Now these are the generations of Terah. Terah begat Abram, Nahor, and Haran. And Haran begat Lot.

v28 And Haran died before his father Terah in the land of his nativity, in Ur of the Chaldees.

v29 And Abram and Nahor took them wives: The name of Abram's wife was Sarai; and the name of Nahor's wife, Milcah, the daughter of Haran, the father of Milcah, and the father of Iscah.

v30 And Sarai was barren; She had no child.

v31 And Terah took Abram his son, and Lot the son of Haran, his son's son, and Sarai his daughter-in-law, his son Abram's wife; and they went forth with them from Ur of the Chaldees, to go into the land of Canaan; and they came unto Haran, and dwelt there.

v32 And the days of Terah were two hundred and five years: and Terah died in Haran.
