# Luke 2 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/42 - Luke/Luke 1|← Luke 1]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/42 - Luke/Luke]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/42 - Luke/Luke 3|Luke 3 →]]

***

v1 Now it came to pass in those days, there went out a decree from Caesar Augustus, that all the world should be enrolled.

v2 This was the first enrolment made when Quirinius was governor of Syria.

v3 And all went to enrol themselves, every one to his own city.

v4 And Joseph also went up from Galilee, out of the city of Nazareth, into Judaea, to the city of David, which is called Bethlehem, because he was of the house and family of David;

v5 to enrol himself with Mary, who was betrothed to him, being great with child.

v6 And it came to pass, while they were there, the days were fulfilled that she should be delivered.

v7 And she brought forth her firstborn son; and she wrapped him in swaddling clothes, and laid him in a manger, because there was no room for them in the inn.

v8 And there were shepherds in the same country abook_iding in the field, and keeping watch by night over their flock.

v9 And an angel of the Lord stood by them, and the glory of the Lord shone round about them: and they were sore afraid.

v10 And the angel said unto them, Be not afraid; for behold, I bring you good tidings of great joy which shall be to all the people:

v11 for there is born to you this day in the city of David a Saviour, who is Christ the Lord.

v12 And this [is] the sign unto you: Ye shall find a babe wrapped in swaddling clothes, and lying in a manger.

v13 And suddenly there was with the angel a multitude of the heavenly host praising God, and saying,

v14 Glory to God in the highest, And on earth peace among men in whom he is well pleased.

v15 And it came to pass, when the angels went away from them into heaven, the shepherds said one to another, Let us now go even unto Bethlehem, and see this thing that is come to pass, which the Lord hath made known unto us.

v16 And they came with haste, and found both Mary and Joseph, and the babe lying in the manger.

v17 And when they saw it, they made known concerning the saying which was spoken to them about this child.

v18 And all that heard it wondered at the things which were spoken unto them by the shepherds.

v19 But Mary kept all these sayings, pondering them in her heart.

v20 And the shepherds returned, glorifying and praising God for all the things that they had heard and seen, even as it was spoken unto them.

v21 And when eight days were fulfilled for circumcising him, his name was called JESUS, which was so called by the angel before he was conceived in the womb.

v22 And when the days of their purification according to the law of Moses were fulfilled, they brought him up to Jerusalem, to present him to the Lord

v23 (as it is written in the law of the Lord, Every male that openeth the womb shall be called holy to the Lord),

v24 and to offer a sacrifice according to that which is said in the law of the Lord, A pair of turtledoves, or two young pigeons.

v25 And behold, there was a man in Jerusalem whose name was Simeon; and this man was righteous and devout, looking for the consolation of Israel: and the Holy Spirit was upon him.

v26 And it had been revealed unto him by the Holy Spirit, that he should not see death, before he had seen the Lord's Christ.

v27 And he came in the Spirit into the temple: and when the parents brought in the child Jesus, that they might do concerning him after the custom of the law,

v28 then he received him into his arms, and blessed God, and said,

v29 Now lettest thou thy servant depart, Lord, According to thy word, in peace;

v30 For mine eyes have seen thy salvation,

v31 Which thou hast prepared before the face of all peoples;

v32 A light for revelation to the Gentiles, And the glory of thy people Israel.

v33 And his father and his mother were marvelling at the things which were spoken concerning him;

v34 and Simeon blessed them, and said unto Mary his mother, Behold, this [child] is set for the falling and the rising of many in Israel; and for a sign which is spoken against;

v35 yea and a sword shall pierce through thine own soul; that thoughts out of many hearts may be revealed.

v36 And there was one Anna, a prophetess, the daughter of Phanuel, of the tribe of Asher (she was of a great age, having lived with a husband seven years from her virginity,

v37 and she had been a widow even unto fourscore and four years), who departed not from the temple, worshipping with fastings and supplications night and day.

v38 And coming up at that very hour she gave thanks unto God, and spake of him to all them that were looking for the redemption of Jerusalem.

v39 And when they had accomplished all things that were according to the law of the Lord, they returned into Galilee, to their own city Nazareth.

v40 And the child grew, and waxed strong, filled with wisdom: and the grace of God was upon him.

v41 And his parents went every year to Jerusalem at the feast of the passover.

v42 And when he was twelve years old, they went up after the custom of the feast;

v43 and when they had fulfilled the days, as they were returning, the boy Jesus tarried behind in Jerusalem; and his parents knew it not;

v44 but supposing him to be in the company, they went a day's journey; and they sought for him among their kinsfolk and acquaintance:

v45 and when they found him not, they returned to Jerusalem, seeking for him.

v46 And it came to pass, after three days they found him in the temple, sitting in the midst of the teachers, both hearing them, and asking them questions:

v47 and all that heard him were amazed at his understanding and his answers.

v48 And when they saw him, they were astonished; and his mother said unto him, Son, why hast thou thus dealt with us? behold, thy father and I sought thee sorrowing.

v49 And he said unto them, How is it that ye sought me? knew ye not that I must be in my Father's house?

v50 And they understood not the saying which he spake unto them.

v51 And he went down with them, and came to Nazareth; and he was subject unto them: and his mother kept all [these] sayings in her heart.

v52 And Jesus advanced in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and men.
