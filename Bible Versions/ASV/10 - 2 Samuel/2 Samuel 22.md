# 2 Samuel 22 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 21|← 2 Samuel 21]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 23|2 Samuel 23 →]]

***

v1 And David spake unto Jehovah the words of this song in the day that Jehovah delivered him out of the hand of all his enemies, and out of the hand of Saul:

v2 and he said, Jehovah is my rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer, even mine;

v3 God, my rock, in him will I take refuge; My shield, and the horn of my salvation, my high tower, and my    refuge; My saviour, thou savest me from violence.

v4 I will call upon Jehovah, who is worthy to be praised: So shall I be saved from mine enemies.

v5 For the waves of death compassed me; The floods of ungodliness made me afraid:

v6 The cords of Sheol were round about me; The snares of death came upon me.

v7 In my distress I called upon Jehovah; Yea, I called unto my God: And he heard my voice out of his temple, And my cry [came] into his ears.

v8 Then the earth shook and trembled, The foundations of heaven quaked And were shaken, because he was wroth.

v9 There went up a smoke out of his nostrils, And fire out of his mouth devoured: Coals were kindled by it.

v10 He bowed the heavens also, and came down; And thick darkness was under his feet.

v11 And he rode upon a cherub, and did fly; Yea, he was seen upon the wings of the wind.

v12 And he made darkness pavilions round about him, Gathering of waters, thick clouds of the skies.

v13 At the brightness before him Coals of fire were kindled.

v14 Jehovah thundered from heaven, And the Most High uttered his voice.

v15 And he sent out arrows, and scattered them; Lightning, and discomfited them.

v16 Then the channels of the sea appeared, The foundations of the world were laid bare, By the rebuke of Jehovah, At the blast of the breath of his nostrils.

v17 He sent from on high, he took me; He drew me out of many waters;

v18 He delivered me from my strong enemy, From them that hated me; for they were too mighty for me.

v19 They came upon me in the day of my calamity; But Jehovah was my stay.

v20 He brought me forth also into a large place; He delivered me, because he delighted in me.

v21 Jehovah rewarded me according to my righteousness; According to the cleanness of my hands hath he recompensed me.

v22 For I have kept the ways of Jehovah, And have not wickedly departed from my God.

v23 For all his ordinances were before me; And as for his statutes, I did not depart from them.

v24 I was also perfect toward him; And I kept myself from mine iniquity.

v25 Therefore hath Jehovah recompensed me according to my    righteousness, According to my cleanness in his eyesight.

v26 With the merciful thou wilt show thyself merciful; With the perfect man thou wilt show thyself perfect;

v27 With the pure thou wilt show thyself pure; And with the perverse thou wilt show thyself froward.

v28 And the afflicted people thou wilt save; But thine eyes are upon the haughty, that thou mayest bring them    down.

v29 For thou art my lamp, O Jehovah; And Jehovah will lighten my darkness.

v30 For by thee I run upon a troop; By my God do I leap over a wall.

v31 As for God, his way is perfect: The word of Jehovah is tried; He is a shield unto all them that take refuge in him.

v32 For who is God, save Jehovah? And who is a rock, save our God?

v33 God is my strong fortress; And he guideth the perfect in his way.

v34 He maketh his feet like hinds' [feet], And setteth me upon my high places.

v35 He teacheth my hands to war, So that mine arms do bend a bow of brass.

v36 Thou hast also given me the shield of thy salvation; And thy gentleness hath made me great.

v37 Thou hast enlarged my steps under me; And my feet have not slipped.

v38 I have pursued mine enemies, and destroyed them; Neither did I turn again till they were consumed.

v39 And I have consumed them, and smitten them through, so    that they cannot arise: Yea, they are fallen under my feet.

v40 For thou hast girded me with strength unto the battle; Thou hast subdued under me those that rose up against me.

v41 Thou hast also made mine enemies turn their backs unto    me, That I might cut off them that hate me.

v42 They looked, but there was none to save; Even unto Jehovah, but he answered them not.

v43 Then did I beat them small as the dust of the earth, I did crush them as the mire of the streets, and did spread them    abroad.

v44 Thou also hast delivered me from the strivings of my    people; Thou hast kept me to be the head of the nations: A people whom I have not known shall serve me.

v45 The foreigners shall submit themselves unto me: As soon as they hear of me, they shall obey me.

v46 The foreigners shall fade away, And shall come trembling out of their close places.

v47 Jehovah liveth; And blessed be my rock; And exalted be God, the rock of my salvation,

v48 Even the God that executeth vengeance for me, And that bringeth down peoples under me,

v49 And that bringeth me forth from mine enemies: Yea, thou liftest me up above them that rise up against me; Thou deliverest me from the violent man.

v50 Therefore I will give thanks unto thee, O Jehovah, among    the nations, And will sing praises unto thy name.

v51 Great deliverance giveth he to his king, And showeth lovingkindness to his anointed, To David and to his seed, for evermore.
