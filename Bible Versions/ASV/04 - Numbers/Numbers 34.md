# Numbers 34 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/04 - Numbers/Numbers 33|← Numbers 33]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/04 - Numbers/Numbers]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/04 - Numbers/Numbers 35|Numbers 35 →]]

***

v1 And Jehovah spake unto Moses, saying,

v2 Command the children of Israel, and say unto them, When ye come into the land of Canaan (this is the land that shall fall unto you for an inheritance, even the land of Canaan according to the borders thereof),

v3 then your south quarter shall be from the wilderness of Zin along by the side of Edom, and your south border shall be from the end of the Salt Sea eastward;

v4 and your border shall turn about southward of the ascent of Akrabbim, and pass along to Zin; and the goings out thereof shall be southward of Kadesh-barnea; and it shall go forth to Hazar-addar, and pass along to Azmon;

v5 and the border shall turn about from Azmon unto the brook of Egypt, and the goings out thereof shall be at the sea.

v6 And for the western border, ye shall have the great sea and the border [thereof]: this shall be your west border.

v7 And this shall be your north border: from the great sea ye shall mark out for you mount Hor;

v8 from mount Hor ye shall mark out unto the entrance of Hamath; and the goings out of the border shall be at Zedad;

v9 and the border shall go forth to Ziphron, and the goings out thereof shall be at Hazar-enan: this shall be your north border.

v10 And ye shall mark out your east border from Hazar-enan to Shepham;

v11 and the border shall go down from Shepham to Riblah, on the east side of Ain; and the border shall go down, and shall reach unto the side of the sea of Chinnereth eastward;

v12 and the border shall go down to the Jordan, and the goings out thereof shall be at the Salt Sea. This shall be your land according to the borders thereof round about.

v13 And Moses commanded the children of Israel, saying, This is the land which ye shall inherit by lot, which Jehovah hath commanded to give unto the nine tribes, and to the half-tribe;

v14 for the tribe of the children of Reuben according to their fathers' houses, and the tribe of the children of Gad according to their fathers' houses, have received, and the half-tribe of Manasseh have received, their inheritance:

v15 the two tribes and the half-tribe have received their inheritance beyond the Jordan at Jericho eastward, toward the sunrising.

v16 And Jehovah spake unto Moses, saying,

v17 These are the names of the men that shall divide the land unto you for inheritance: Eleazar the priest, and Joshua the son of Nun.

v18 And ye shall take one prince of every tribe, to divide the land for inheritance.

v19 And these are the names of the men: Of the tribe of Judah, Caleb the son of Jephunneh.

v20 And of the tribe of the children of Simeon, Shemuel the son of Ammihud.

v21 Of the tribe of Benjamin, Elidad the son of Chislon.

v22 And of the tribe of the children of Dan a prince, Bukki the son of Jogli.

v23 Of the children of Joseph: of the tribe of the children of Manasseh a prince, Hanniel the son of Ephod.

v24 And of the tribe of the children of Ephraim a prince, Kemuel the son of Shiphtan.

v25 And of the tribe of the children of Zebulun a prince, Elizaphan the son of Parnach.

v26 And of the tribe of the children of Issachar a prince, Paltiel the son of Azzan.

v27 And of the tribe of the children of Asher a prince, Ahihud the son of Shelomi.

v28 And of the tribe of the children of Naphtali a prince, Pedahel the son of Ammihud.

v29 These are they whom Jehovah commanded to divide the inheritance unto the children of Israel in the land of Canaan.
