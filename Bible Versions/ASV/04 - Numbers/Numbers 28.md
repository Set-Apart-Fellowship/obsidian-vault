# Numbers 28 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/04 - Numbers/Numbers 27|← Numbers 27]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/04 - Numbers/Numbers]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/04 - Numbers/Numbers 29|Numbers 29 →]]

***

v1 And Jehovah spake unto Moses, saying,

v2 Command the children of Israel, and say unto them, My oblation, my food for my offerings made by fire, of a sweet savor unto me, shall ye observe to offer unto me in their due season.

v3 And thou shalt say unto them, This is the offering made by fire which ye shall offer unto Jehovah: he-lambs a year old without blemish, two day by day, for a continual burnt-offering.

v4 The one lamb shalt thou offer in the morning, and the other lamb shalt thou offer at even;

v5 and the tenth part of an ephah of fine flour for a meal-offering, mingled with the fourth part of a hin of beaten oil.

v6 It is a continual burnt-offering, which was ordained in mount Sinai for a sweet savor, an offering made by fire unto Jehovah.

v7 And the drink-offering thereof shall be the fourth part of a hin for the one lamb: in the holy place shalt thou pour out a drink-offering of strong drink unto Jehovah.

v8 And the other lamb shalt thou offer at even: as the meal-offering of the morning, and as the drink-offering thereof, thou shalt offer it, an offering made by fire, of a sweet savor unto Jehovah.

v9 And on the sabbath day two he-lambs a year old without blemish, and two tenth parts [of an ephah] of fine flour for a meal-offering, mingled with oil, and the drink-offering thereof:

v10 this is the burnt-offering of every sabbath, besides the continual burnt-offering, and the drink-offering thereof.

v11 And in the beginnings of your months ye shall offer a burnt-offering unto Jehovah: two young bullocks, and one ram, seven he-lambs a year old without blemish;

v12 and three tenth parts [of an ephah] of fine flour for a meal-offering, mingled with oil, for each bullock; and two tenth parts of fine flour for a meal-offering, mingled with oil, for the one ram;

v13 and a tenth part of fine flour mingled with oil for a meal-offering unto every lamb; for a burnt-offering of a sweet savor, an offering made by fire unto Jehovah.

v14 And their drink-offerings shall be half a hin of wine for a bullock, and the third part of a hin for the ram, and the fourth part of a hin for a lamb: this is the burnt-offering of every month throughout the months of the year.

v15 And one he-goat for a sin-offering unto Jehovah; it shall be offered besides the continual burnt-offering, and the drink-offering thereof.

v16 And in the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, is Jehovah's passover.

v17 And on the fifteenth day of this month shall be a feast: seven days shall unleavened bread be eaten.

v18 In the first day shall be a holy convocation: ye shall do no servile work;

v19 but ye shall offer an offering made by fire, a burnt-offering unto Jehovah: two young bullocks, and one ram, and seven he-lambs a year old; they shall be unto you without blemish;

v20 and their meal-offering, fine flour mingled with oil: three tenth parts shall ye offer for a bullock, and two tenth parts for the ram;

v21 a tenth part shalt thou offer for every lamb of the seven lambs;

v22 and one he-goat for a sin-offering, to make atonement for you.

v23 Ye shall offer these besides the burnt-offering of the morning, which is for a continual burnt-offering.

v24 After this manner ye shall offer daily, for seven days, the food of the offering made by fire, of a sweet savor unto Jehovah: it shall be offered besides the continual burnt-offering, and the drink-offering thereof.

v25 And on the seventh day ye shall have a holy convocation: ye shall do no servile work.

v26 Also in the day of the first-fruits, when ye offer a new meal-offering unto Jehovah in your [feast of] weeks, ye shall have a holy convocation; ye shall do no servile work;

v27 but ye shall offer a burnt-offering for a sweet savor unto Jehovah: two young bullocks, one ram, seven he-lambs a year old;

v28 and their meal-offering, fine flour mingled with oil, three tenth parts for each bullock, two tenth parts for the one ram,

v29 a tenth part for every lamb of the seven lambs;

v30 one he-goat, to make atonement for you.

v31 Besides the continual burnt-offering, and the meal-offering thereof, ye shall offer them (they shall be unto you without blemish), and their drink-offerings.
