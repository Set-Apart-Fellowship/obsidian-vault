# Job 10 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 9|← Job 9]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 11|Job 11 →]]

***

v1 My soul is weary of my life; I will give free course to my complaint; I will speak in the bitterness of my soul.

v2 I will say unto God, Do not condemn me; Show me wherefore thou contendest with me.

v3 Is it good unto thee that thou shouldest oppress, That thou shouldest despise the work of thy hands, And shine upon the counsel of the wicked?

v4 Hast thou eyes of flesh? Or seest thou as man seeth?

v5 Are thy days as the days of man, Or thy years as man's days,

v6 That thou inquirest after mine iniquity, And searchest after my sin,

v7 Although thou knowest that I am not wicked, And there is none that can deliver out of thy hand?

v8 Thy hands have framed me and fashioned me Together round about; yet thou dost destroy me.

v9 Remember, I beseech thee, that thou hast fashioned me as    clay; And wilt thou bring me into dust again?

v10 Hast thou not poured me out as milk, And curdled me like cheese?

v11 Thou hast clothed me with skin and flesh, And knit me together with bones and sinews.

v12 Thou hast granted me life and lovingkindness; And thy visitation hath preserved my spirit.

v13 Yet these things thou didst hide in thy heart; I know that this is with thee:

v14 If I sin, then thou markest me, And thou wilt not acquit me from mine iniquity.

v15 If I be wicked, woe unto me; And if I be righteous, yet shall I not lift up my head; Being filled with ignominy, And looking upon mine affliction.

v16 And if [my head] exalt itself, thou huntest me as a lion; And again thou showest thyself marvellous upon me.

v17 Thou renewest thy witnesses against me, And increasest thine indignation upon me: Changes and warfare are with me.

v18 Wherefore then hast thou brought me forth out of the    womb? I had given up the ghost, and no eye had seen me.

v19 I should have been as though I had not been; I should have been carried from the womb to the grave.

v20 Are not my days few? cease then, And let me alone, that I may take comfort a little,

v21 Before I go whence I shall not return, [Even] to the land of darkness and of the shadow of death;

v22 The land dark as midnight, [The land] of the shadow of death, without any order, And where the light is as midnight.
