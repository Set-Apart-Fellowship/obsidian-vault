# Job 30 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 29|← Job 29]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 31|Job 31 →]]

***

v1 But now they that are younger than I have me in derision, Whose fathers I disdained to set with the dogs of my flock.

v2 Yea, the strength of their hands, whereto should it    profit me? Men in whom ripe age is perished.

v3 They are gaunt with want and famine; They gnaw the dry ground, in the gloom of wasteness and    desolation.

v4 They pluck salt-wort by the bushes; And the roots of the broom are their food.

v5 They are driven forth from the midst [of men]; They cry after them as after a thief;

v6 So that they dwell in frightful valleys, In holes of the earth and of the rocks.

v7 Among the bushes they bray; Under the nettles they are gathered together.

v8 [They are] children of fools, yea, children of base men; They were scourged out of the land.

v9 And now I am become their song, Yea, I am a byword unto them.

v10 They abhor me, they stand aloof from me, And spare not to spit in my face.

v11 For he hath loosed his cord, and afflicted me; And they have cast off the bridle before me.

v12 Upon my right hand rise the rabble; They thrust aside my feet, And they cast up against me their ways of destruction.

v13 They mar my path, They set forward my calamity, [Even] men that have no helper.

v14 As through a wide breach they come: In the midst of the ruin they roll themselves [upon me].

v15 Terrors are turned upon me; They chase mine honor as the wind; And my welfare is passed away as a cloud.

v16 And now my soul is poured out within me; Days of affliction have taken hold upon me.

v17 In the night season my bones are pierced in me, And the [pains] that gnaw me take no rest.

v18 By [God's] great force is my garment disfigured; It bindeth me about as the collar of my coat.

v19 He hath cast me into the mire, And I am become like dust and ashes.

v20 I cry unto thee, and thou dost not answer me: I stand up, and thou gazest at me.

v21 Thou art turned to be cruel to me; With the might of thy hand thou persecutest me.

v22 Thou liftest me up to the wind, thou causest me to ride    [upon it]; And thou dissolvest me in the storm.

v23 For I know that thou wilt bring me to death, And to the house appointed for all living.

v24 Howbeit doth not one stretch out the hand in his fall? Or in his calamity therefore cry for help?

v25 Did not I weep for him that was in trouble? Was not my soul grieved for the needy?

v26 When I looked for good, then evil came; And when I waited for light, there came darkness.

v27 My heart is troubled, and resteth not; Days of affliction are come upon me.

v28 I go mourning without the sun: I stand up in the assembly, and cry for help.

v29 I am a brother to jackals, And a companion to ostriches.

v30 My skin is black, [and falleth] from me, And my bones are burned with heat.

v31 Therefore is my harp [turned] to mourning, And my pipe into the voice of them that weep.
