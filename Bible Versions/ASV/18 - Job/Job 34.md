# Job 34 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 33|← Job 33]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 35|Job 35 →]]

***

v1 Moreover Elihu answered and said,

v2 Hear my words, ye wise men; And give ear unto me, ye that have knowledge.

v3 For the ear trieth words, As the palate tasteth food.

v4 Let us choose for us that which is right: Let us know among ourselves what is good.

v5 For Job hath said, I am righteous, And God hath taken away my right:

v6 Notwithstanding my right I am [accounted] a liar; My wound is incurable, [though I am] without transgression.

v7 What man is like Job, Who drinketh up scoffing like water,

v8 Who goeth in company with the workers of iniquity, And walketh with wicked men?

v9 For he hath said, It profiteth a man nothing That he should delight himself with God.

v10 Therefore hearken unto me, ye men of understanding: Far be it from God, that he should do wickedness, And from the Almighty, that he should commit iniquity.

v11 For the work of a man will he render unto him, And cause every man to find according to his ways.

v12 Yea, of a surety, God will not do wickedly, Neither will the Almighty pervert justice.

v13 Who gave him a charge over the earth? Or who hath disposed the whole world?

v14 If he set his heart upon himself, [If] he gather unto himself his spirit and his breath;

v15 All flesh shall perish together, And man shall turn again unto dust.

v16 If now [thou hast] understanding, hear this: Hearken to the voice of my words.

v17 Shall even one that hateth justice govern? And wilt thou condemn him that is righteous [and] mighty?-

v18 [Him] that saith to a king, [Thou art] vile, [Or] to nobles, [Ye are] wicked;

v19 That respecteth not the persons of princes, Nor regardeth the rich more than the poor; For they all are the work of his hands.

v20 In a moment they die, even at midnight; The people are shaken and pass away, And the mighty are taken away without hand.

v21 For his eyes are upon the ways of a man, And he seeth all his goings.

v22 There is no darkness, nor thick gloom, Where the workers of iniquity may hide themselves.

v23 For he needeth not further to consider a man, That he should go before God in judgment.

v24 He breaketh in pieces mighty men [in ways] past finding    out, And setteth others in their stead.

v25 Therefore he taketh knowledge of their works; And he overturneth them in the night, so that they are destroyed.

v26 He striketh them as wicked men In the open sight of others;

v27 Because they turned aside from following him, And would not have regard in any of his ways:

v28 So that they caused the cry of the poor to come unto him, And he heard the cry of the afflicted.

v29 When he giveth quietness, who then can condemn? And when he hideth his face, who then can behold him? Alike whether [it be done] unto a nation, or unto a man:

v30 That the godless man reign not, That there be none to ensnare the people.

v31 For hath any said unto God, I have borne [chastisement], I will not offend [any more]:

v32 That which I see not teach thou me: If I have done iniquity, I will do it no more?

v33 Shall his recompense be as thou wilt, that thou refusest    it? For thou must choose, and not I: Therefore speak what thou knowest.

v34 Men of understanding will say unto me, Yea, every wise man that heareth me:

v35 Job speaketh without knowledge, And his words are without wisdom.

v36 Would that Job were tried unto the end, Because of his answering like wicked men.

v37 For he addeth rebellion unto his sin; He clappeth his hands among us, And multiplieth his words against God.
