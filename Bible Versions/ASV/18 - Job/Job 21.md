# Job 21 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 20|← Job 20]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 22|Job 22 →]]

***

v1 Then Job answered and said,

v2 Hear diligently my speech; And let this be your consolations.

v3 Suffer me, and I also will speak; And after that I have spoken, mock on.

v4 As for me, is my complaint to man? And why should I not be impatient?

v5 Mark me, and be astonished, And lay your hand upon your mouth.

v6 Even when I remember I am troubled, And horror taketh hold on my flesh.

v7 Wherefore do the wicked live, Become old, yea, wax mighty in power?

v8 Their seed is established with them in their sight, And their offspring before their eyes.

v9 Their houses are safe from fear, Neither is the rod of God upon them.

v10 Their bull gendereth, and faileth not; Their cow calveth, and casteth not her calf.

v11 They send forth their little ones like a flock, And their children dance.

v12 They sing to the timbrel and harp, And rejoice at the sound of the pipe.

v13 They spend their days in prosperity, And in a moment they go down to Sheol.

v14 And they say unto God, Depart from us; For we desire not the knowledge of thy ways.

v15 What is the Almighty, that we should serve him? And what profit should we have, if we pray unto him?

v16 Lo, their prosperity is not in their hand: The counsel of the wicked is far from me.

v17 How oft is it that the lamp of the wicked is put out? That their calamity cometh upon them? That [God] distributeth sorrows in his anger?

v18 That they are as stubble before the wind, And as chaff that the storm carrieth away?

v19 [Ye say], God layeth up his iniquity for his children. Let him recompense it unto himself, that he may know it:

v20 Let his own eyes see his destruction, And let him drink of the wrath of the Almighty.

v21 For what careth he for his house after him, When the number of his months is cut off?

v22 Shall any teach God knowledge, Seeing he judgeth those that are high?

v23 One dieth in his full strength, Being wholly at ease and quiet:

v24 His pails are full of milk, And the marrow of his bones is moistened.

v25 And another dieth in bitterness of soul, And never tasteth of good.

v26 They lie down alike in the dust, And the worm covereth them.

v27 Behold, I know your thoughts, And the devices wherewith ye would wrong me.

v28 For ye say, Where is the house of the prince? And where is the tent wherein the wicked dwelt?

v29 Have ye not asked wayfaring men? And do ye not know their evidences,

v30 That the evil man is reserved to the day of calamity? That they are led forth to the day of wrath?

v31 Who shall declare his way to his face? And who shall repay him what he hath done?

v32 Yet shall he be borne to the grave, And men shall keep watch over the tomb.

v33 The clods of the valley shall be sweet unto him, And all men shall draw after him, As there were innumerable before him.

v34 How then comfort ye me in vain, Seeing in your answers there remaineth [only] falsehood?
