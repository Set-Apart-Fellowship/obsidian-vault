# Job 27 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 26|← Job 26]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 28|Job 28 →]]

***

v1 And Job again took up his parable, and said,

v2 As God liveth, who hath taken away my right, And the Almighty, who hath vexed my soul:

v3 (For my life is yet whole in me, And the spirit of God is in my nostrils);

v4 Surely my lips shall not speak unrighteousness, Neither shall my tongue utter deceit.

v5 Far be it from me that I should justify you: Till I die I will not put away mine integrity from me.

v6 My righteousness I hold fast, and will not let it go: My heart shall not reproach [me] so long as I live.

v7 Let mine enemy be as the wicked, And let him that riseth up against me be as the unrighteous.

v8 For what is the hope of the godless, though he get him    gain, When God taketh away his soul?

v9 Will God hear his cry, When trouble cometh upon him?

v10 Will he delight himself in the Almighty, And call upon God at all times?

v11 I will teach you concerning the hand of God; That which is with the Almighty will I not conceal.

v12 Behold, all ye yourselves have seen it; Why then are ye become altogether vain?

v13 This is the portion of a wicked man with God, And the heritage of oppressors, which they receive from the    Almighty:

v14 If his children be multiplied, it is for the sword; And his offspring shall not be satisfied with bread.

v15 Those that remain of him shall be buried in death, And his widows shall make no lamentation.

v16 Though he heap up silver as the dust, And prepare raiment as the clay;

v17 He may prepare it, but the just shall put it on, And the innocent shall divide the silver.

v18 He buildeth his house as the moth, And as a booth which the keeper maketh.

v19 He lieth down rich, but he shall not be gathered [to his    fathers]; He openeth his eyes, and he is not.

v20 Terrors overtake him like waters; A tempest stealeth him away in the night.

v21 The east wind carrieth him away, and he departeth; And it sweepeth him out of his place.

v22 For [God] shall hurl at him, and not spare: He would fain flee out of his hand.

v23 Men shall clap their hands at him, And shall hiss him out of his place.
