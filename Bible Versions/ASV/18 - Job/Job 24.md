# Job 24 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 23|← Job 23]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 25|Job 25 →]]

***

v1 Why are times not laid up by the Almighty? And why do not they that know him see his days?

v2 There are that remove the landmarks; They violently take away flocks, and feed them.

v3 They drive away the ass of the fatherless; They take the widow's ox for a pledge.

v4 They turn the needy out of the way: The poor of the earth all hide themselves.

v5 Behold, as wild asses in the desert They go forth to their work, seeking diligently for food; The wilderness [yieldeth] them bread for their children.

v6 They cut their provender in the field; And they glean the vintage of the wicked.

v7 They lie all night naked without clothing, And have no covering in the cold.

v8 They are wet with the showers of the mountains, And embrace the rock for want of a shelter.

v9 There are that pluck the fatherless from the breast, And take a pledge of the poor;

v10 [So that] they go about naked without clothing, And being hungry they carry the sheaves.

v11 They make oil within the walls of these men; They tread [their] winepresses, and suffer thirst.

v12 From out of the populous city men groan, And the soul of the wounded crieth out: Yet God regardeth not the folly.

v13 These are of them that rebel against the light; They know not the ways thereof, Nor abook_ide in the paths thereof.

v14 The murderer riseth with the light; He killeth the poor and needy; And in the night he is as a thief.

v15 The eye also of the adulterer waiteth for the twilight, Saying, No eye shall see me: And he disguiseth his face.

v16 In the dark they dig through houses: They shut themselves up in the day-time; They know not the light.

v17 For the morning is to all of them as thick darkness; For they know the terrors of the thick darkness.

v18 Swiftly they [pass away] upon the face of the waters; Their portion is cursed in the earth: They turn not into the way of the vineyards.

v19 Drought and heat consume the snow waters: [So doth] Sheol [those that] have sinned.

v20 The womb shall forget him; The worm shall feed sweetly on him; He shall be no more remembered; And unrighteousness shall be broken as a tree.

v21 He devoureth the barren that beareth not, And doeth not good to the widow.

v22 Yet [God] preserveth the mighty by his power: He riseth up that hath no assurance of life.

v23 [God] giveth them to be in security, and they rest    thereon; And his eyes are upon their ways.

v24 They are exalted; yet a little while, and they are gone; Yea, they are brought low, they are taken out of the way as all    others, And are cut off as the tops of the ears of grain.

v25 And if it be not so now, who will prove me a liar, And make my speech nothing worth?
