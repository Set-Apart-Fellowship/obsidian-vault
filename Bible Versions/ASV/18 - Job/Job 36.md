# Job 36 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 35|← Job 35]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 37|Job 37 →]]

***

v1 Elihu also proceeded, and said,

v2 Suffer me a little, and I will show thee; For I have yet somewhat to say on God's behalf.

v3 I will fetch my knowledge from afar, And will ascribe righteousness to my Maker.

v4 For truly my words are not false: One that is perfect in knowledge is with thee.

v5 Behold, God is mighty, and despiseth not any: He is mighty in strength of understanding.

v6 He preserveth not the life of the wicked, But giveth to the afflicted [their] right.

v7 He withdraweth not his eyes from the righteous: But with kings upon the throne He setteth them for ever, and they are exalted.

v8 And if they be bound in fetters, And be taken in the cords of afflictions;

v9 Then he showeth them their work, And their transgressions, that they have behaved themselves    proudly.

v10 He openeth also their ear to instruction, And commandeth that they return from iniquity.

v11 If they hearken and serve [him], They shall spend their days in prosperity, And their years in pleasures.

v12 But if they hearken not, they shall perish by the sword, And they shall die without knowledge.

v13 But they that are godless in heart lay up anger: They cry not for help when he bindeth them.

v14 They die in youth, And their life [perisheth] among the unclean.

v15 He delivereth the afflicted by their affliction, And openeth their ear in oppression.

v16 Yea, he would have allured thee out of distress Into a broad place, where there is no straitness; And that which is set on thy table would be full of fatness.

v17 But thou art full of the judgment of the wicked: Judgment and justice take hold [on thee].

v18 For let not wrath stir thee up against chastisements; Neither let the greatness of the ransom turn thee aside.

v19 Will thy cry avail, [that thou be] not in distress, Or all the forces of [thy] strength?

v20 Desire not the night, When peoples are cut off in their place.

v21 Take heed, regard not iniquity: For this hast thou chosen rather than affliction.

v22 Behold, God doeth loftily in his power: Who is a teacher like unto him?

v23 Who hath enjoined him his way? Or who can say, Thou hast wrought unrighteousness?

v24 Remember that thou magnify his work, Whereof men have sung.

v25 All men have looked thereon; Man beholdeth it afar off.

v26 Behold, God is great, and we know him not; The number of his years is unsearchable.

v27 For he draweth up the drops of water, Which distil in rain from his vapor,

v28 Which the skies pour down And drop upon man abundantly.

v29 Yea, can any understand the spreadings of the clouds, The thunderings of his pavilion?

v30 Behold, he spreadeth his light around him; And he covereth the bottom of the sea.

v31 For by these he judgeth the peoples; He giveth food in abundance.

v32 He covereth his hands with the lightning, And giveth it a charge that it strike the mark.

v33 The noise thereof telleth concerning him, The cattle also concerning [the storm] that cometh up.
