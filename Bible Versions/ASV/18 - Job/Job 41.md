# Job 41 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 40|← Job 40]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 42|Job 42 →]]

***

v1 Canst thou draw out leviathan with a fishhook? Or press down his tongue with a cord?

v2 Canst thou put a rope into his nose? Or pierce his jaw through with a hook?

v3 Will he make many supplications unto thee? Or will he speak soft words unto thee?

v4 Will he make a covenant with thee, That thou shouldest take him for a servant for ever?

v5 Wilt thou play with him as with a bird? Or wilt thou bind him for thy maidens?

v6 Will the bands [of fishermen] make traffic of him? Will they part him among the merchants?

v7 Canst thou fill his skin with barbed irons, Or his head with fish-spears?

v8 Lay thy hand upon him; Remember the battle, and do so no more.

v9 Behold, the hope of him is in vain: Will not one be cast down even at the sight of him?

v10 None is so fierce that he dare stir him up; Who then is he that can stand before me?

v11 Who hath first given unto me, that I should repay him? [Whatsoever is] under the whole heaven is mine.

v12 I will not keep silence concerning his limbs, Nor his mighty strength, nor his goodly frame.

v13 Who can strip off his outer garment? Who shall come within his jaws?

v14 Who can open the doors of his face? Round about his teeth is terror.

v15 [His] strong scales are [his] pride, Shut up together [as with] a close seal.

v16 One is so near to another, That no air can come between them.

v17 They are joined one to another; They stick together, so that they cannot be sundered.

v18 His sneezings flash forth light, And his eyes are like the eyelids of the morning.

v19 Out of his mouth go burning torches, And sparks of fire leap forth.

v20 Out of his nostrils a smoke goeth, As of a boiling pot and [burning] rushes.

v21 His breath kindleth coals, And a flame goeth forth from his mouth.

v22 In his neck abook_ideth strength, And terror danceth before him.

v23 The flakes of his flesh are joined together: They are firm upon him; they cannot be moved.

v24 His heart is as firm as a stone; Yea, firm as the nether millstone.

v25 When he raiseth himself up, the mighty are afraid: By reason of consternation they are beside themselves.

v26 If one lay at him with the sword, it cannot avail; Nor the spear, the dart, nor the pointed shaft.

v27 He counteth iron as straw, [And] brass as rotten wood.

v28 The arrow cannot make him flee: Sling-stones are turned with him into stubble.

v29 Clubs are counted as stubble: He laugheth at the rushing of the javelin.

v30 His underparts are [like] sharp potsherds: He spreadeth [as it were] a threshing-wain upon the mire.

v31 He maketh the deep to boil like a pot: He maketh the sea like a pot of ointment.

v32 He maketh a path to shine after him; One would think the deep to be hoary.

v33 Upon earth there is not his like, That is made without fear.

v34 He beholdeth everything that is high: He is king over all the sons of pride.
