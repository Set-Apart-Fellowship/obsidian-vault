# Job 28 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 27|← Job 27]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 29|Job 29 →]]

***

v1 Surely there is a mine for silver, And a place for gold which they refine.

v2 Iron is taken out of the earth, And copper is molten out of the stone.

v3 [Man] setteth an end to darkness, And searcheth out, to the furthest bound, The stones of obscurity and of thick darkness.

v4 He breaketh open a shaft away from where men sojourn; They are forgotten of the foot; They hang afar from men, they swing to and fro.

v5 As for the earth, out of it cometh bread; And underneath it is turned up as it were by fire.

v6 The stones thereof are the place of sapphires, And it hath dust of gold.

v7 That path no bird of prey knoweth, Neither hath the falcon's eye seen it:

v8 The proud beasts have not trodden it, Nor hath the fierce lion passed thereby.

v9 He putteth forth his hand upon the flinty rock; He overturneth the mountains by the roots.

v10 He cutteth out channels among the rocks; And his eye seeth every precious thing.

v11 He bindeth the streams that they trickle not; And the thing that is hid bringeth he forth to light.

v12 But where shall wisdom be found? And where is the place of understanding?

v13 Man knoweth not the price thereof; Neither is it found in the land of the living.

v14 The deep saith, It is not in me; And the sea saith, It is not with me.

v15 It cannot be gotten for gold, Neither shall silver be weighed for the price thereof.

v16 It cannot be valued with the gold of Ophir, With the precious onyx, or the sapphire.

v17 Gold and glass cannot equal it, Neither shall it be exchanged for jewels of fine gold.

v18 No mention shall be made of coral or of crystal: Yea, the price of wisdom is above rubies.

v19 The topaz of Ethiopia shall not equal it, Neither shall it be valued with pure gold.

v20 Whence then cometh wisdom? And where is the place of understanding?

v21 Seeing it is hid from the eyes of all living, And kept close from the birds of the heavens.

v22 Destruction and Death say, We have heard a rumor thereof with our ears.

v23 God understandeth the way thereof, And he knoweth the place thereof.

v24 For he looketh to the ends of the earth, And seeth under the whole heaven;

v25 To make a weight for the wind: Yea, he meteth out the waters by measure.

v26 When he made a decree for the rain, And a way for the lightning of the thunder;

v27 Then did he see it, and declare it; He established it, yea, and searched it out.

v28 And unto man he said, Behold, the fear of the Lord, that is wisdom; And to depart from evil is understanding.
