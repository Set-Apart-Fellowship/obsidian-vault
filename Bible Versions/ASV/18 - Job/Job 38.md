# Job 38 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 37|← Job 37]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 39|Job 39 →]]

***

v1 Then Jehovah answered Job out of the whirlwind, and said,

v2 Who is this that darkeneth counsel By words without knowledge?

v3 Gird up now thy loins like a man; For I will demand of thee, and declare thou unto me.

v4 Where wast thou when I laid the foundations of the earth? Declare, if thou hast understanding.

v5 Who determined the measures thereof, if thou knowest? Or who stretched the line upon it?

v6 Whereupon were the foundations thereof fastened? Or who laid the corner-stone thereof,

v7 When the morning stars sang together, And all the sons of God shouted for joy?

v8 Or [who] shut up the sea with doors, When it brake forth, [as if] it had issued out of the womb;

v9 When I made clouds the garment thereof, And thick darkness a swaddling-band for it,

v10 And marked out for it my bound, And set bars and doors,

v11 And said, Hitherto shalt thou come, but no further; And here shall thy proud waves be stayed?

v12 Hast thou commanded the morning since thy days [began], [And] caused the dayspring to know its place;

v13 That it might take hold of the ends of the earth, And the wicked be shaken out of it?

v14 It is changed as clay under the seal; And [all things] stand forth as a garment:

v15 And from the wicked their light is withholden, And the high arm is broken.

v16 Hast thou entered into the springs of the sea? Or hast thou walked in the recesses of the deep?

v17 Have the gates of death been revealed unto thee? Or hast thou seen the gates of the shadow of death?

v18 Hast thou comprehended the earth in its breadth? Declare, if thou knowest it all.

v19 Where is the way to the dwelling of light? And as for darkness, where is the place thereof,

v20 That thou shouldest take it to the bound thereof, And that thou shouldest discern the paths to the house thereof?

v21 [Doubtless], thou knowest, for thou wast then born, And the number of thy days is great!

v22 Hast thou entered the treasuries of the snow, Or hast thou seen the treasures of the hail,

v23 Which I have reserved against the time of trouble, Against the day of battle and war?

v24 By what way is the light parted, Or the east wind scattered upon the earth?

v25 Who hath cleft a channel for the waterflood, Or the way for the lightning of the thunder;

v26 To cause it to rain on a land where no man is; On the wilderness, wherein there is no man;

v27 To satisfy the waste and desolate [ground], And to cause the tender grass to spring forth?

v28 Hath the rain a father? Or who hath begotten the drops of dew?

v29 Out of whose womb came the ice? And the hoary frost of heaven, who hath gendered it?

v30 The waters hide themselves [and become] like stone, And the face of the deep is frozen.

v31 Canst thou bind the cluster of the Pleiades, Or loose the bands of Orion?

v32 Canst thou lead forth the Mazzaroth in their season? Or canst thou guide the Bear with her train?

v33 Knowest thou the ordinances of the heavens? Canst thou establish the dominion thereof in the earth?

v34 Canst thou lift up thy voice to the clouds, That abundance of waters may cover thee?

v35 Canst thou send forth lightnings, that they may go, And say unto thee, Here we are?

v36 Who hath put wisdom in the inward parts? Or who hath given understanding to the mind?

v37 Who can number the clouds by wisdom? Or who can pour out the bottles of heaven,

v38 When the dust runneth into a mass, And the clods cleave fast together?

v39 Canst thou hunt the prey for the lioness, Or satisfy the appetite of the young lions,

v40 When they couch in their dens, [And] abook_ide in the covert to lie in wait?

v41 Who provideth for the raven his prey, When his young ones cry unto God, [And] wander for lack of food?
