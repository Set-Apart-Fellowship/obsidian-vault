# Job 9 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 8|← Job 8]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 10|Job 10 →]]

***

v1 Then Job answered and said,

v2 Of a truth I know that it is so: But how can man be just with God?

v3 If he be pleased to contend with him, He cannot answer him one of a thousand.

v4 [He is] wise in heart, and mighty in strength: Who hath hardened himself against him, and prospered?-

v5 [Him] that removeth the mountains, and they know it not, When he overturneth them in his anger;

v6 That shaketh the earth out of its place, And the pillars thereof tremble;

v7 That commandeth the sun, and it riseth not, And sealeth up the stars;

v8 That alone stretcheth out the heavens, And treadeth upon the waves of the sea;

v9 That maketh the Bear, Orion, and the Pleiades, And the chambers of the south;

v10 That doeth great things past finding out, Yea, marvellous things without number.

v11 Lo, he goeth by me, and I see him not: He passeth on also, but I perceive him not.

v12 Behold, he seizeth [the prey], who can hinder him? Who will say unto him, What doest thou?

v13 God will not withdraw his anger; The helpers of Rahab do stoop under him.

v14 How much less shall I answer him, And choose out my words [to reason] with him?

v15 Whom, though I were righteous, yet would I not answer; I would make supplication to my judge.

v16 If I had called, and he had answered me, Yet would I not believe that he hearkened unto my voice.

v17 For he breaketh me with a tempest, And multiplieth my wounds without cause.

v18 He will not suffer me to take my breath, But filleth me with bitterness.

v19 If [we speak] of strength, lo, [he is] mighty! And if of justice, Who, [saith he], will summon me?

v20 Though I be righteous, mine own mouth shall condemn me: Though I be perfect, it shall prove me perverse.

v21 I am perfect; I regard not myself; I despise my life.

v22 It is all one; therefore I say, He destroyeth the perfect and the wicked.

v23 If the scourge slay suddenly, He will mock at the trial of the innocent.

v24 The earth is given into the hand of the wicked; He covereth the faces of the judges thereof: If [it be] not [he], who then is it?

v25 Now my days are swifter than a post: They flee away, they see no good,

v26 They are passed away as the swift ships; As the eagle that swoopeth on the prey.

v27 If I say, I will forget my complaint, I will put off my [sad] countenance, and be of good cheer;

v28 I am afraid of all my sorrows, I know that thou wilt not hold me innocent.

v29 I shall be condemned; Why then do I labor in vain?

v30 If I wash myself with snow water, And make my hands never so clean;

v31 Yet wilt thou plunge me in the ditch, And mine own clothes shall abhor me.

v32 For he is not a man, as I am, that I should answer him, That we should come together in judgment.

v33 There is no umpire betwixt us, That might lay his hand upon us both.

v34 Let him take his rod away from me, And let not his terror make me afraid:

v35 Then would I speak, and not fear him; For I am not so in myself.
