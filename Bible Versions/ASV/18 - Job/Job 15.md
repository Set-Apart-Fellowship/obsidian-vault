# Job 15 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 14|← Job 14]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 16|Job 16 →]]

***

v1 Then answered Eliphaz the Temanite, and said,

v2 Should a wise man make answer with vain knowledge, And fill himself with the east wind?

v3 Should he reason with unprofitable talk, Or with speeches wherewith he can do no good?

v4 Yea, thou doest away with fear, And hinderest devotion before God.

v5 For thine iniquity teacheth thy mouth, And thou choosest the tongue of the crafty.

v6 Thine own mouth condemneth thee, and not I; Yea, thine own lips testify against thee.

v7 Art thou the first man that was born? Or wast thou brought forth before the hills?

v8 Hast thou heard the secret counsel of God? And dost thou limit wisdom to thyself?

v9 What knowest thou, that we know not? What understandest thou, which is not in us?

v10 With us are both the gray-headed and the very aged men, Much elder than thy father.

v11 Are the consolations of God too small for thee, Even the word that is gentle toward thee?

v12 Why doth thy heart carry thee away? And why do thine eyes flash,

v13 That against God thou turnest thy spirit, And lettest words go out of thy mouth?

v14 What is man, that he should be clean? And he that is born of a woman, that he should be righteous?

v15 Behold, he putteth no trust in his holy ones; Yea, the heavens are not clean in his sight:

v16 How much less one that is abominable and corrupt, A man that drinketh iniquity like water!

v17 I will show thee, hear thou me; And that which I have seen I will declare:

v18 (Which wise men have told From their fathers, and have not hid it;

v19 Unto whom alone the land was given, And no stranger passed among them):

v20 The wicked man travaileth with pain all his days, Even the number of years that are laid up for the oppressor.

v21 A sound of terrors is in his ears; In prosperity the destroyer shall come upon him.

v22 He believeth not that he shall return out of darkness, And he is waited for of the sword.

v23 He wandereth abroad for bread, [saying], Where is it? He knoweth that the day of darkness is ready at his hand.

v24 Distress and anguish make him afraid; They prevail against him, as a king ready to the battle.

v25 Because he hath stretched out his hand against God, And behaveth himself proudly against the Almighty;

v26 He runneth upon him with a [stiff] neck, With the thick bosses of his bucklers;

v27 Because he hath covered his face with his fatness, And gathered fat upon his loins;

v28 And he hath dwelt in desolate cities, In houses which no man inhabited, Which were ready to become heaps;

v29 He shall not be rich, neither shall his substance    continue, Neither shall their possessions be extended on the earth.

v30 He shall not depart out of darkness; The flame shall dry up his branches, And by the breath of [God's] mouth shall he go away.

v31 Let him not trust in vanity, deceiving himself; For vanity shall be his recompense.

v32 It shall be accomplished before his time, And his branch shall not be green.

v33 He shall shake off his unripe grape as the vine, And shall cast off his flower as the olive-tree.

v34 For the company of the godless shall be barren, And fire shall consume the tents of bribery.

v35 They conceive mischief, and bring forth iniquity, And their heart prepareth deceit.
