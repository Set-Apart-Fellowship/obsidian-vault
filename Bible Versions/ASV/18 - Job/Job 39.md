# Job 39 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 38|← Job 38]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 40|Job 40 →]]

***

v1 Knowest thou the time when the wild goats of the rock    bring forth? [Or] canst thou mark when the hinds do calve?

v2 Canst thou number the months that they fulfil? Or knowest thou the time when they bring forth?

v3 They bow themselves, they bring forth their young, They cast out their pains.

v4 Their young ones become strong, they grow up in the open    field; They go forth, and return not again.

v5 Who hath sent out the wild ass free? Or who hath loosed the bonds of the swift ass,

v6 Whose home I have made the wilderness, And the salt land his dwelling-place?

v7 He scorneth the tumult of the city, Neither heareth he the shoutings of the driver.

v8 The range of the mountains is his pasture, And he searcheth after every green thing.

v9 Will the wild-ox be content to serve thee? Or will he abook_ide by thy crib?

v10 Canst thou bind the wild-ox with his band in the furrow? Or will he harrow the valleys after thee?

v11 Wilt thou trust him, because his strength is great? Or wilt thou leave to him thy labor?

v12 Wilt thou confide in him, that he will bring home thy    seed, And gather [the grain] of thy threshing-floor?

v13 The wings of the ostrich wave proudly; [But] are they the pinions and plumage of love?

v14 For she leaveth her eggs on the earth, And warmeth them in the dust,

v15 And forgetteth that the foot may crush them, Or that the wild beast may trample them.

v16 She dealeth hardly with her young ones, as if they were    not hers: Though her labor be in vain, [she is] without fear;

v17 Because God hath deprived her of wisdom, Neither hath he imparted to her understanding.

v18 What time she lifteth up herself on high, She scorneth the horse and his rider.

v19 Hast thou given the horse [his] might? Hast thou clothed his neck with the quivering mane?

v20 Hast thou made him to leap as a locust? The glory of his snorting is terrible.

v21 He paweth in the valley, and rejoiceth in his strength: He goeth out to meet the armed men.

v22 He mocketh at fear, and is not dismayed; Neither turneth he back from the sword.

v23 The quiver rattleth against him, The flashing spear and the javelin.

v24 He swalloweth the ground with fierceness and rage; Neither believeth he that it is the voice of the trumpet.

v25 As oft as the trumpet [soundeth] he saith, Aha! And he smelleth the battle afar off, The thunder of the captains, and the shouting.

v26 Is it by thy wisdom that the hawk soareth, (And) stretcheth her wings toward the south?

v27 Is it at thy command that the eagle mounteth up, And maketh her nest on high?

v28 On the cliff she dwelleth, and maketh her home, Upon the point of the cliff, and the stronghold.

v29 From thence she spieth out the prey; Her eyes behold it afar off.

v30 Her young ones also suck up blood: And where the slain are, there is she.
