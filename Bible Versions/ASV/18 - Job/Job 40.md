# Job 40 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 39|← Job 39]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 41|Job 41 →]]

***

v1 Moreover Jehovah answered Job, and said,

v2 Shall he that cavilleth contend with the Almighty? He that argueth with God, let him answer it.

v3 Then Job answered Jehovah, and said,

v4 Behold, I am of small account; What shall I answer thee? I lay my hand upon my mouth.

v5 Once have I spoken, and I will not answer; Yea, twice, but I will proceed no further.

v6 Then Jehovah answered Job out of the whirlwind, and said,

v7 Gird up thy loins now like a man: I will demand of thee, and declare thou unto me.

v8 Wilt thou even annul my judgment? Wilt thou condemn me, that thou mayest be justified?

v9 Or hast thou an arm like God? And canst thou thunder with a voice like him?

v10 Deck thyself now with excellency and dignity; And array thyself with honor and majesty.

v11 Pour forth the overflowings of thine anger; And look upon every one that is proud, and abase him.

v12 Look on every one that is proud, [and] bring him low; And tread down the wicked where they stand.

v13 Hide them in the dust together; Bind their faces in the hidden [place].

v14 Then will I also confess of thee That thine own right hand can save thee.

v15 Behold now, behemoth, which I made as well as thee; He eateth grass as an ox.

v16 Lo now, his strength is in his loins, And his force is in the muscles of his belly.

v17 He moveth his tail like a cedar: The sinews of his thighs are knit together.

v18 His bones are [as] tubes of brass; His limbs are like bars of iron.

v19 He is the chief of the ways of God: He [only] that made him giveth him his sword.

v20 Surely the mountains bring him forth food, Where all the beasts of the field do play.

v21 He lieth under the lotus-trees, In the covert of the reed, and the fen.

v22 The lotus-trees cover him with their shade; The willows of the brook compass him about.

v23 Behold, if a river overflow, he trembleth not; He is confident, though a Jordan swell even to his mouth.

v24 Shall any take him when he is on the watch, Or pierce through his nose with a snare?
