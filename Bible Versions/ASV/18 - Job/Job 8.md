# Job 8 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 7|← Job 7]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 9|Job 9 →]]

***

v1 Then answered Bildad the Shuhite, and said,

v2 How long wilt thou speak these things? And [how long] shall the words of thy mouth be [like] a mighty    wind?

v3 Doth God pervert justice? Or doth the Almighty pervert righteousness?

v4 If thy children have sinned against him, And he hath delivered them into the hand of their transgression;

v5 If thou wouldest seek diligently unto God, And make thy supplication to the Almighty;

v6 If thou wert pure and upright: Surely now he would awake for thee, And make the habitation of thy righteousness prosperous.

v7 And though thy beginning was small, Yet thy latter end would greatly increase.

v8 For inquire, I pray thee, of the former age, And apply thyself to that which their fathers have searched out:

v9 (For we are but of yesterday, and know nothing, Because our days upon earth are a shadow);

v10 Shall not they teach thee, and tell thee, And utter words out of their heart?

v11 Can the rush grow up without mire? Can the flag grow without water?

v12 Whilst it is yet in its greenness, [and] not cut down, It withereth before any [other] herb.

v13 So are the paths of all that forget God; And the hope of the godless man shall perish:

v14 Whose confidence shall break in sunder, And whose trust is a spider's web.

v15 He shall lean upon his house, but it shall not stand: He shall hold fast thereby, but it shall not endure.

v16 He is green before the sun, And his shoots go forth over his garden.

v17 His roots are wrapped about the [stone] -heap, He beholdeth the place of stones.

v18 If he be destroyed from his place, Then it shall deny him, [saying], I have not seen thee.

v19 Behold, this is the joy of his way; And out of the earth shall others spring.

v20 Behold, God will not cast away a perfect man, Neither will he uphold the evil-doers.

v21 He will yet fill thy mouth with laughter, And thy lips with shouting.

v22 They that hate thee shall be clothed with shame; And the tent of the wicked shall be no more.
