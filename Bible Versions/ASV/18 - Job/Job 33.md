# Job 33 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 32|← Job 32]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/18 - Job/Job 34|Job 34 →]]

***

v1 Howbeit, Job, I pray thee, hear my speech, And hearken to all my words.

v2 Behold now, I have opened my mouth; My tongue hath spoken in my mouth.

v3 My words [shall utter] the uprightness of my heart; And that which my lips know they shall speak sincerely.

v4 The Spirit of God hath made me, And the breath of the Almighty giveth me life.

v5 If thou canst, answer thou me; Set [thy words] in order before me, stand forth.

v6 Behold, I am toward God even as thou art: I also am formed out of the clay.

v7 Behold, my terror shall not make thee afraid, Neither shall my pressure be heavy upon thee.

v8 Surely thou hast spoken in my hearing, And I have heard the voice of [thy] words, [saying],

v9 I am clean, without transgression; I am innocent, neither is there iniquity in me:

v10 Behold, he findeth occasions against me, He counteth me for his enemy:

v11 He putteth my feet in the stocks, He marketh all my paths.

v12 Behold, I will answer thee, in this thou art not just; For God is greater than man.

v13 Why dost thou strive against him, For that he giveth not account of any of his matters?

v14 For God speaketh once, Yea twice, [though man] regardeth it not.

v15 In a dream, in a vision of the night, When deep sleep falleth upon men, In slumberings upon the bed;

v16 Then he openeth the ears of men, And sealeth their instruction,

v17 That he may withdraw man [from his] purpose, And hide pride from man;

v18 He keepeth back his soul from the pit, And his life from perishing by the sword.

v19 He is chastened also with pain upon his bed, And with continual strife in his bones;

v20 So that his life abhorreth bread, And his soul dainty food.

v21 His flesh is consumed away, that it cannot be seen; And his bones that were not seen stick out.

v22 Yea, his soul draweth near unto the pit, And his life to the destroyers.

v23 If there be with him an angel, An interpreter, one among a thousand, To show unto man what is right for him;

v24 Then [God] is gracious unto him, and saith, Deliver him from going down to the pit, I have found a ransom.

v25 His flesh shall be fresher than a child's; He returneth to the days of his youth.

v26 He prayeth unto God, and he is favorable unto him, So that he seeth his face with joy: And he restoreth unto man his righteousness.

v27 He singeth before men, and saith, I have sinned, and perverted that which was right, And it profited me not:

v28 He hath redeemed my soul from going into the pit, And my life shall behold the light.

v29 Lo, all these things doth God work, Twice, [yea] thrice, with a man,

v30 To bring back his soul from the pit, That he may be enlightened with the light of the living.

v31 Mark well, O Job, hearken unto me: Hold thy peace, and I will speak.

v32 If thou hast anything to say, answer me: Speak, for I desire to justify thee.

v33 If not, hearken thou unto me: Hold thy peace, and I will teach thee wisdom.
