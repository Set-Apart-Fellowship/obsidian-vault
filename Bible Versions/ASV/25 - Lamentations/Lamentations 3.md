# Lamentations 3 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 2|← Lamentations 2]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 4|Lamentations 4 →]]

***

v1 I am the man that hath seen affliction by the rod of his    wrath.

v2 He hath led me and caused me to walk in darkness, and not    in light.

v3 Surely against me he turneth his hand again and again all    the day.

v4 My flesh and my skin hath he made old; he hath broken my    bones.

v5 He hath builded against me, and compassed me with gall and    travail.

v6 He hath made me to dwell in dark places, as those that    have been long dead.

v7 He hath walled me about, that I cannot go forth; he hath    made my chain heavy.

v8 Yea, when I cry, and call for help, he shutteth out my    prayer.

v9 He hath walled up my ways with hewn stone; he hath made my    paths crooked.

v10 He is unto me as a bear lying in wait, as a lion in    secret places.

v11 He hath turned aside my ways, and pulled me in pieces; he    hath made me desolate.

v12 He hath bent his bow, and set me as a mark for the arrow.

v13 He hath caused the shafts of his quiver to enter into my    reins.

v14 I am become a derision to all my people, and their song    all the day.

v15 He hath filled me with bitterness, he hath sated me with    wormwood.

v16 He hath also broken my teeth with gravel stones; he hath    covered me with ashes.

v17 And thou hast removed my soul far off from peace; I    forgat prosperity.

v18 And I said, My strength is perished, and mine expectation    from Jehovah.

v19 Remember mine affliction and my misery, the wormwood and    the gall.

v20 My soul hath them still in remembrance, and is bowed down    within me.

v21 This I recall to my mind; therefore have I hope.

v22 [It is of] Jehovah's lovingkindnesses that we are not    consumed, because his compassions fail not.

v23 They are new every morning; great is thy faithfulness.

v24 Jehovah is my portion, saith my soul; therefore will I    hope in him.

v25 Jehovah is good unto them that wait for him, to the soul    that seeketh him.

v26 It is good that a man should hope and quietly wait for    the salvation of Jehovah.

v27 It is good for a man that he bear the yoke in his youth.

v28 Let him sit alone and keep silence, because he hath laid    it upon him.

v29 Let him put his mouth in the dust, if so be there may be    hope.

v30 Let him give his cheek to him that smiteth him; let him    be filled full with reproach.

v31 For the Lord will not cast off for ever.

v32 For though he cause grief, yet will he have compassion    according to the multitude of his lovingkindnesses.

v33 For he doth not afflict willingly, nor grieve the    children of men.

v34 To crush under foot all the prisoners of the earth,

v35 To turn aside the right of a man before the face of the    Most High,

v36 To subvert a man in his cause, the Lord approveth not.

v37 Who is he that saith, and it cometh to pass, when the    Lord commandeth it not?

v38 Out of the mouth of the Most High cometh there not evil    and good?

v39 Wherefore doth a living man complain, a man for the    punishment of his sins?

v40 Let us search and try our ways, and turn again to Jehovah.

v41 Let us lift up our heart with our hands unto God in the    heavens.

v42 We have transgressed and have rebelled; thou hast not    pardoned.

v43 Thou hast covered with anger and pursued us; thou hast    slain, thou hast not pitied.

v44 Thou hast covered thyself with a cloud, so that no prayer    can pass through.

v45 Thou hast made us an off-scouring and refuse in the midst    of the peoples.

v46 All our enemies have opened their mouth wide against us.

v47 Fear and the pit are come upon us, devastation and    destruction.

v48 Mine eye runneth down with streams of water, for the    destruction of the daughter of my people.

v49 Mine eye poureth down, and ceaseth not, without any    intermission,

v50 Till Jehovah look down, and behold from heaven.

v51 Mine eye affecteth my soul, because of all the daughters    of my city.

v52 They have chased me sore like a bird, they that are mine    enemies without cause.

v53 They have cut off my life in the dungeon, and have cast a    stone upon me.

v54 Waters flowed over my head; I said, I am cut off.

v55 I called upon thy name, O Jehovah, out of the lowest    dungeon.

v56 Thou heardest my voice; hide not thine ear at my    breathing, at my cry.

v57 Thou drewest near in the day that I called upon thee;    thou saidst, Fear not.

v58 O Lord, thou hast pleaded the causes of my soul; thou    hast redeemed my life.

v59 O Jehovah, thou hast seen my wrong; judge thou my cause.

v60 Thou hast seen all their vengeance and all their devices    against me.

v61 Thou hast heard their reproach, O Jehovah, and all their    devices against me,

v62 The lips of those that rose up against me, and their    device against me all the day.

v63 Behold thou their sitting down, and their rising up; I am    their song.

v64 Thou wilt render unto them a recompense, O Jehovah,    according to the work of their hands.

v65 Thou wilt give them hardness of heart, thy curse unto    them.

v66 Thou wilt pursue them in anger, and destroy them from    under the heavens of Jehovah.
