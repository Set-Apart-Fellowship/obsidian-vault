# Lamentations 5 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 4|← Lamentations 4]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations]]

***

v1 Remember, O Jehovah, what is come upon us: Behold, and see our reproach.

v2 Our inheritance is turned unto strangers, Our houses unto aliens.

v3 We are orphans and fatherless; Our mothers are as widows.

v4 We have drunken our water for money; Our wood is sold unto us.

v5 Our pursuers are upon our necks: We are weary, and have no rest.

v6 We have given the hand to the Egyptians, And to the Assyrians, to be satisfied with bread.

v7 Our fathers sinned, and are not; And we have borne their iniquities.

v8 Servants rule over us: There is none to deliver us out of their hand.

v9 We get our bread at the peril of our lives, Because of the sword of the wilderness.

v10 Our skin is black like an oven, Because of the burning heat of famine.

v11 They ravished the women in Zion, The virgins in the cities of Judah.

v12 Princes were hanged up by their hand: The faces of elders were not honored.

v13 The young men bare the mill; And the children stumbled under the wood.

v14 The elders have ceased from the gate, The young men from their music.

v15 The joy of our heart is ceased; Our dance is turned into mourning.

v16 The crown is fallen from our head: Woe unto us! for we have sinned.

v17 For this our heart is faint; For these things our eyes are dim;

v18 For the mountain of Zion, which is desolate: The foxes walk upon it.

v19 Thou, O Jehovah, abook_idest for ever; Thy throne is from generation to generation.

v20 Wherefore dost thou forget us for ever, [And] forsake us so long time?

v21 Turn thou us unto thee, O Jehovah, and we shall be turned; Renew our days as of old.

v22 But thou hast utterly rejected us; Thou art very wroth against us.
