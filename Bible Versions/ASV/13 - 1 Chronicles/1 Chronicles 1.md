# 1 Chronicles 1 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 2|1 Chronicles 2 →]]

***

v1 Adam, Seth, Enosh,

v2 Kenan, Mahalalel, Jared,

v3 Enoch, Methuselah, Lamech,

v4 Noah, Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

v5 The sons of Japheth: Gomer, and Magog, and Madai, and Javan, and Tubal, and Meshech, and Tiras.

v6 And the sons of Gomer: Ashkenaz, and Diphath, and Togarmah.

v7 And the sons of Javan: Elishah, and Tarshish, Kittim, and Rodanim.

v8 The sons of Ham: Cush, and Mizraim, Put, and Canaan.

v9 And the sons of Cush: Seba, and Havilah, and Sabta, and Raama, and Sabteca. And the sons of Raamah: Sheba, and Dedan.

v10 And Cush begat Nimrod; he began to be a mighty one in the earth.

v11 And Mizraim begat Ludim, and Anamim, and Lehabim, and Naphtuhim,

v12 and Pathrusim, and Casluhim (from whence came the Philistines), and Caphtorim.

v13 And Canaan begat Sidon his first-born, and Heth,

v14 and the Jebusite, and the Amorite, and the Girgashite,

v15 and the Hivite, and the Arkite, and the Sinite,

v16 and the Arvadite, and the Zemarite, and the Hamathite.

v17 The sons of Shem: Elam, and Asshur, and Arpachshad, and Lud, and Aram, and Uz, and Hul, and Gether, and Meshech.

v18 And Arpachshad begat Shelah, and Shelah begat Eber.

v19 And unto Eber were born two sons: the name of the one was Peleg; for in his days the earth was divided; and his brother's name was Joktan.

v20 And Joktan begat Almodad, and Sheleph, and Hazarmaveth, and Jerah,

v21 and Hadoram, and Uzal, and Diklah,

v22 and Ebal, and Abimael, and Sheba,

v23 and Ophir, and Havilah, and Jobab. All these were the sons of Joktan.

v24 Shem, Arpachshad, Shelah,

v25 Eber, Peleg, Reu,

v26 Serug, Nahor, Terah,

v27 Abram (the same is Abraham).

v28 The sons of Abraham: Isaac, and Ishmael.

v29 These are their generations: the first-born of Ishmael, Nebaioth; then Kedar, and Adbeel, and Mibsam,

v30 Mishma, and Dumah, Massa, Hadad, and Tema,

v31 Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah. These are the sons of Ishmael.

v32 And the sons of Keturah, Abraham's concubine: she bare Zimran, and Jokshan, and Medan, and Midian, and Ishbak, and Shuah. And the sons of Jokshan: Sheba, and Dedan.

v33 And the sons of Midian: Ephah, and Epher, and Hanoch, and Abook_ida, and Eldaah. All these were the sons of Keturah.

v34 And Abraham begat Isaac. The sons of Isaac: Esau, and Israel.

v35 The sons of Esau: Eliphaz, Reuel, and Jeush, and Jalam, and Korah.

v36 The sons of Eliphaz: Teman, and Omar, Zephi, and Gatam, Kenaz, and Timna, and Amalek.

v37 The sons of Reuel: Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah.

v38 And the sons of Seir: Lotan, and Shobal, and Zibeon, and Anah, and Dishon, and Ezer, and Dishan.

v39 And the sons of Lotan: Hori, and Homam; and Timna was Lotan's sister.

v40 The sons of Shobal: Alian, and Manahath, and Ebal, Shephi, and Onam. And the sons of Zibeon: Aiah, and Anah.

v41 The sons of Anah: Dishon. And the sons of Dishon: Hamran, and Eshban, and Ithran, and Cheran.

v42 The sons of Ezer: Bilhan, and Zaavan, Jaakan. The sons of Dishan: Uz, and Aran.

v43 Now these are the kings that reigned in the land of Edom, before there resigned any king over the children of Israel: Bela the son of Beor; and the name of his city was Dinhabah.

v44 And Bela died, and Jobab the son of Zerah of Bozrah reigned in his stead.

v45 And Jobab died, and Husham of the land of the Temanites reigned in his stead.

v46 And Husham died, and Hadad the son of Bedad, who smote Midian in the field of Moab, reigned in his stead; and the name of his city was Avith.

v47 And Hadad died, and Samlah of Masrekah reigned in his stead.

v48 And Samlah died, and Shaul of Rehoboth by the River reigned in his stead.

v49 And Shaul died, and Baal-hanan the son of Achbor reigned in his stead.

v50 And Baal-hanan died, and Hadad reigned in his stead; and the name of his city was Pai: and his wife's name was Mehetabel, the daughter of Matred, the daughter of Me-zahab.

v51 And Hadad died.   And the chiefs of Edom were: chief Timna, chief Aliah, chief Jetheth,

v52 chief Oholibamah, chief Elah, chief Pinon,

v53 chief Kenaz, chief Teman, chief Mibzar,

v54 chief Magdiel, chief Iram. These are the chiefs of Edom.
