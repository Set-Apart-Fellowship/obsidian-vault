# James 2 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/59 - James/James 1|← James 1]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/59 - James/James]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/59 - James/James 3|James 3 →]]

***

v1 My brethren, hold not the faith of our Lord Jesus Christ, [the Lord] of glory, with respect of persons.

v2 For if there come into your synagogue a man with a gold ring, in fine clothing, and there come in also a poor man in vile clothing;

v3 and ye have regard to him that weareth the fine clothing, and say, Sit thou here in a good place; and ye say to the poor man, Stand thou there, or sit under my footstool;

v4 Do ye not make distinctions among yourselves, and become judges with evil thoughts?

v5 Hearken, my beloved brethren; did not God choose them that are poor as to the world [to be] rich in faith, and heirs of the kingdom which he promised to them that love him?

v6 But ye have dishonored the poor man. Do not the rich oppress you, and themselves drag you before the judgment-seats?

v7 Do not they blaspheme the honorable name by which ye are called?

v8 Howbeit if ye fulfil the royal law, according to the scripture, Thou shalt love thy neighbor as thyself, ye do well:

v9 but if ye have respect of persons, ye commit sin, being convicted by the law as transgressors.

v10 For whosoever shall keep the whole law, and yet stumble in one [point], he is become guilty of all.

v11 For he that said, Do not commit adultery, said also, Do not kill. Now if thou dost not commit adultery, but killest, thou art become a transgressor of the law.

v12 So speak ye, and so do, as men that are to be judged by a law of liberty.

v13 For judgment [is] without mercy to him that hath showed no mercy: mercy glorieth against judgment.

v14 What doth it profit, my brethren, if a man say he hath faith, but have not works? can that faith save him?

v15 If a brother or sister be naked and in lack of daily food,

v16 and one of you say unto them, Go in peace, be ye warmed and filled; and yet ye give them not the things needful to the body; what doth it profit?

v17 Even so faith, if it have not works, is dead in itself.

v18 Yea, a man will say, Thou hast faith, and I have works: show me thy faith apart from [thy] works, and I by my works will show thee [my] faith.

v19 Thou believest that God is one; thou doest well: the demons also believe, and shudder.

v20 But wilt thou know, O vain man, that faith apart from works is barren?

v21 Was not Abraham our father justified by works, in that he offered up Isaac his son upon the altar?

v22 Thou seest that faith wrought with his works, and by works was faith made perfect;

v23 and the scripture was fulfilled which saith, And Abraham believed God, and it was reckoned unto him for righteousness; and he was called the friend of God.

v24 Ye see that by works a man is justified, and not only by faith.

v25 And in like manner was not also Rahab the harlot justified by works, in that she received the messengers, and sent them out another way?

v26 For as the body apart from the spirit is dead, even so faith apart from works is dead.
