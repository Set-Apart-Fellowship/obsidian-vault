# Proverbs 17 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 16|← Proverbs 16]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 18|Proverbs 18 →]]

***

v1 Better is a dry morsel, and quietness therewith, Than a house full of feasting with strife.

v2 A servant that dealeth wisely shall have rule over a son    that causeth shame, And shall have part in the inheritance among the brethren.

v3 The refining pot is for silver, and the furnace for gold; But Jehovah trieth the hearts.

v4 An evil-doer giveth heed to wicked lips; [And] a liar giveth ear to a mischievous tongue.

v5 Whoso mocketh the poor reproacheth his Maker; [And] he that is glad at calamity shall not be unpunished.

v6 Children's children are the crown of old men; And the glory of children are their fathers.

v7 Excellent speech becometh not a fool; Much less do lying lips a prince.

v8 A bribe is [as] a precious stone in the eyes of him that    hath it; Whithersoever it turneth, it prospereth.

v9 He that covereth a transgression seeketh love; But he that harpeth on a matter separateth chief friends.

v10 A rebuke entereth deeper into one that hath understanding Than a hundred stripes into a fool.

v11 An evil man seeketh only rebellion; Therefore a cruel messenger shall be sent against him.

v12 Let a bear robbed of her whelps meet a man, Rather than a fool in his folly.

v13 Whoso rewardeth evil for good, Evil shall not depart from his house.

v14 The beginning of strife is [as] when one letteth out    water: Therefore leave off contention, before there is quarrelling.

v15 He that justifieth the wicked, and he that condemneth    the righteous, Both of them alike are an abomination to Jehovah.

v16 Wherefore is there a price in the hand of a fool to buy    wisdom, Seeing he hath no understanding?

v17 A friend loveth at all times; And a brother is born for adversity.

v18 A man void of understanding striketh hands, And becometh surety in the presence of his neighbor.

v19 He loveth transgression that loveth strife: He that raiseth high his gate seeketh destruction.

v20 He that hath a wayward heart findeth no good; And he that hath a perverse tongue falleth into mischief.

v21 He that begetteth a fool [doeth it] to his sorrow; And the father of a fool hath no joy.

v22 A cheerful heart is a good medicine; But a broken spirit drieth up the bones.

v23 A wicked man receiveth a bribe out of the bosom, To pervert the ways of justice.

v24 Wisdom is before the face of him that hath understanding; But the eyes of a fool are in the ends of the earth.

v25 A foolish son is a grief to his father, And bitterness to her that bare him.

v26 Also to punish the righteous is not good, [Nor] to smite the noble for [their] uprightness.

v27 He that spareth his words hath knowledge; And he that is of a cool spirit is a man of understanding.

v28 Even a fool, when he holdeth his peace, is counted wise; When he shutteth his lips, he is [esteemed as] prudent.
