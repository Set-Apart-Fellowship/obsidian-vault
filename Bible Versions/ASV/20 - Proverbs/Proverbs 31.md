# Proverbs 31 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 30|← Proverbs 30]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]]

***

v1 The words of king Lemuel; the oracle which his mother taught him.

v2 What, my son? and what, O son of my womb? And what, O son of my vows?

v3 Give not thy strength unto women, Nor thy ways to that which destroyeth kings.

v4 It is not for kings, O Lemuel, it is not for kings to    drink wine; Nor for princes [to say], Where is strong drink?

v5 Lest they drink, and forget the law, And pervert the justice [due] to any that is afflicted.

v6 Give strong drink unto him that is ready to perish, And wine unto the bitter in soul:

v7 Let him drink, and forget his poverty, And remember his misery no more.

v8 Open thy mouth for the dumb, In the cause of all such as are left desolate.

v9 Open thy mouth, judge righteously, And minister justice to the poor and needy.

v10 A worthy woman who can find? For her price is far above rubies.

v11 The heart of her husband trusteth in her, And he shall have no lack of gain.

v12 She doeth him good and not evil All the days of her life.

v13 She seeketh wool and flax, And worketh willingly with her hands.

v14 She is like the merchant-ships; She bringeth her bread from afar.

v15 She riseth also while it is yet night, And giveth food to her household, And their task to her maidens.

v16 She considereth a field, and buyeth it; With the fruit of her hands she planteth a vineyard.

v17 She girdeth her loins with strength, And maketh strong her arms.

v18 She perceiveth that her merchandise is profitable: Her lamp goeth not out by night.

v19 She layeth her hands to the distaff, And her hands hold the spindle.

v20 She stretcheth out her hand to the poor; Yea, she reacheth forth her hands to the needy.

v21 She is not afraid of the snow for her household; For all her household are clothed with scarlet.

v22 She maketh for herself carpets of tapestry; Her clothing is fine linen and purple.

v23 Her husband is known in the gates, When he sitteth among the elders of the land.

v24 She maketh linen garments and selleth them, And delivereth girdles unto the merchant.

v25 Strength and dignity are her clothing; And she laugheth at the time to come.

v26 She openeth her mouth with wisdom; And the law of kindness is on her tongue.

v27 She looketh well to the ways of her household, And eateth not the bread of idleness.

v28 Her children rise up, and call her blessed; Her husband [also], and he praiseth her, [saying]:

v29 Many daughters have done worthily, But thou excellest them all.

v30 Grace is deceitful, and beauty is vain; [But] a woman that feareth Jehovah, she shall be praised.

v31 Give her of the fruit of her hands; And let her works praise her in the gates.
