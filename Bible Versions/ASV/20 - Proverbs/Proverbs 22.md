# Proverbs 22 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 21|← Proverbs 21]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 23|Proverbs 23 →]]

***

v1 A [good] name is rather to be chosen than great riches, [And] loving favor rather than silver and gold.

v2 The rich and the poor meet together: Jehovah is the maker of them all.

v3 A prudent man seeth the evil, and hideth himself; But the simple pass on, and suffer for it.

v4 The reward of humility [and] the fear of Jehovah [Is] riches, and honor, and life.

v5 Thorns [and] snares are in the way of the perverse: He that keepeth his soul shall be far from them.

v6 Train up a child in the way he should go, And even when he is old he will not depart from it.

v7 The rich ruleth over the poor; And the borrower is servant to the lender.

v8 He that soweth iniquity shall reap calamity; And the rod of his wrath shall fail.

v9 He that hath a bountiful eye shall be blessed; For he giveth of his bread to the poor.

v10 Cast out the scoffer, and contention will go out; Yea, strife and ignominy will cease.

v11 He that loveth pureness of heart, [For] the grace of his lips the king will be his friend.

v12 The eyes of Jehovah preserve [him that hath] knowledge; But he overthroweth the words of the treacherous man.

v13 The sluggard saith, There is a lion without: I shall be slain in the streets.

v14 The mouth of strange women is a deep pit: He that is abhorred of Jehovah shall fall therein.

v15 Foolishness is bound up in the heart of a child; [But] the rod of correction shall drive it far from him.

v16 He that oppresseth the poor to increase his [gain], [And] he that giveth to the rich, [shall come] only to want.

v17 Incline thine ear, and hear the words of the wise, And apply thy heart unto my knowledge.

v18 For it is a pleasant thing if thou keep them within thee, If they be established together upon thy lips.

v19 That thy trust may be in Jehovah, I have made [them] known to thee this day, even to thee.

v20 Have not I written unto thee excellent things Of counsels and knowledge,

v21 To make thee know the certainty of the words of truth, That thou mayest carry back words of truth to them that send    thee?

v22 Rob not the poor, because he is poor; Neither oppress the afflicted in the gate:

v23 For Jehovah will plead their cause, And despoil of life those that despoil them.

v24 Make no friendship with a man that is given to anger; And with a wrathful man thou shalt not go:

v25 Lest thou learn this ways, And get a snare to thy soul.

v26 Be thou not one of them that strike hands, [Or] of them that are sureties for debts.

v27 If thou hast not wherewith to pay, Why should he take away thy bed from under thee?

v28 Remove not the ancient landmark, Which thy fathers have set.

v29 Seest thou a man diligent in his business? he shall    stand before kings; He shall not stand before mean men.
