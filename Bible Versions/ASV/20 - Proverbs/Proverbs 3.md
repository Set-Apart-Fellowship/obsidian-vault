# Proverbs 3 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 2|← Proverbs 2]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 4|Proverbs 4 →]]

***

v1 My son, forget not my law; But let thy heart keep my commandments:

v2 For length of days, and years of life, And peace, will they add to thee.

v3 Let not kindness and truth forsake thee: Bind them about thy neck; Write them upon the tablet of thy heart:

v4 So shalt thou find favor and good understanding In the sight of God and man.

v5 Trust in Jehovah with all thy heart, And lean not upon thine own understanding:

v6 In all thy ways acknowledge him, And he will direct thy paths.

v7 Be not wise in thine own eyes; Fear Jehovah, and depart from evil:

v8 It will be health to thy navel, And marrow to thy bones.

v9 Honor Jehovah with thy substance, And with the first-fruits of all thine increase:

v10 So shall thy barns be filled with plenty, And thy vats shall overflow with new wine.

v11 My son, despise not the chastening of Jehovah; Neither be weary of his reproof:

v12 For whom Jehovah loveth he reproveth; Even as a father the son in whom he delighteth.

v13 Happy is the man that findeth wisdom, And the man that getteth understanding.

v14 For the gaining of it is better than the gaining of    silver, And the profit thereof than fine gold.

v15 She is more precious than rubies: And none of the things thou canst desire are to be compared unto    her.

v16 Length of days is in her right hand; In her left hand are riches and honor.

v17 Her ways are ways of pleasantness, And all her paths are peace.

v18 She is a tree of life to them that lay hold upon her: And happy is every one that retaineth her.

v19 Jehovah by wisdom founded the earth; By understanding he established the heavens.

v20 By his knowledge the depths were broken up, And the skies drop down the dew.

v21 My son, let them not depart from thine eyes; Keep sound wisdom and discretion:

v22 So shall they be life unto thy soul, And grace to thy neck.

v23 Then shalt thou walk in thy way securely, And thy foot shall not stumble.

v24 When thou liest down, thou shalt not be afraid: Yea, thou shalt lie down, and thy sleep shall be sweet.

v25 Be not afraid of sudden fear, Neither of the desolation of the wicked, when it cometh:

v26 For Jehovah will be thy confidence, And will keep thy foot from being taken.

v27 Withhold not good from them to whom it is due, When it is in the power of thy hand to do it.

v28 Say not unto thy neighbor, Go, and come again, And to-morrow I will give; When thou hast it by thee.

v29 Devise not evil against thy neighbor, Seeing he dwelleth securely by thee.

v30 Strive not with a man without cause, If he have done thee no harm.

v31 Envy thou not the man of violence, And choose none of his ways.

v32 For the perverse is an abomination to Jehovah; But his friendship is with the upright.

v33 The curse of Jehovah is in the house of the wicked; But he blesseth the habitation of the righteous.

v34 Surely he scoffeth at the scoffers; But he giveth grace unto the lowly.

v35 The wise shall inherit glory; But shame shall be the promotion of fools.
