# Proverbs 30 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 29|← Proverbs 29]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 31|Proverbs 31 →]]

***

v1 The words of Agur the son of Jakeh; The oracle.   The man saith unto Ithiel, unto Ithiel and Ucal:

v2 Surely I am more brutish than any man, And have not the understanding of a man;

v3 And I have not learned wisdom, Neither have I the knowledge of the Holy One.

v4 Who hath ascended up into heaven, and descended? Who hath gathered the wind in his fists? Who hath bound the waters in his garment? Who hath established all the ends of the earth? What is his name, and what is his son's name, if thou knowest?

v5 Every word of God is tried: He is a shield unto them that take refuge in him.

v6 Add thou not unto his words, Lest he reprove thee, and thou be found a liar.

v7 Two things have I asked of thee; Deny me [them] not before I die:

v8 Remove far from me falsehood and lies; Give me neither poverty nor riches; Feed me with the food that is needful for me:

v9 Lest I be full, and deny [thee], and say, Who is Jehovah? Or lest I be poor, and steal, And use profanely the name of my God.

v10 Slander not a servant unto his master, Lest he curse thee, and thou be held guilty.

v11 There is a generation that curse their father, And bless not their mother.

v12 There is a generation that are pure in their own eyes, And [yet] are not washed from their filthiness.

v13 There is a generation, oh how lofty are their eyes! And their eyelids are lifted up.

v14 There is a generation whose teeth are [as] swords, and    their jaw teeth [as] knives, To devour the poor from off the earth, and the needy from among    men.

v15 The horseleach hath two daughters, [crying], Give, give. There are three things that are never satisfied, [Yea], four that say not, Enough:

v16 Sheol; and the barren womb; The earth that is not satisfied with water; And the fire that saith not, Enough.

v17 The eye that mocketh at his father, And despiseth to obey his mother, The ravens of the valley shall pick it out, And the young eagles shall eat it.

v18 There are three things which are too wonderful for me, Yea, four which I know not:

v19 The way of an eagle in the air; The way of a serpent upon a rock; The way of a ship in the midst of the sea; And the way of a man with a maiden.

v20 So is the way of an adulterous woman; She eateth, and wipeth her mouth, And saith, I have done no wickedness.

v21 For three things the earth doth tremble, And for four, [which] it cannot bear:

v22 For a servant when he is king; And a fool when he is filled with food;

v23 For an odious woman when she is married; And a handmaid that is heir to her mistress.

v24 There are four things which are little upon the earth, But they are exceeding wise:

v25 The ants are a people not strong, Yet they provide their food in the summer;

v26 The conies are but a feeble folk, Yet make they their houses in the rocks;

v27 The locusts have no king, Yet go they forth all of them by bands;

v28 The lizard taketh hold with her hands, Yet is she in kings' palaces.

v29 There are three things which are stately in their march, Yea, four which are stately in going:

v30 The lion, which is mightiest among beasts, And turneth not away for any;

v31 The greyhound; the he-goat also; And the king against whom there is no rising up.

v32 If thou hast done foolishly in lifting up thyself, Or if thou hast thought evil, [Lay] thy hand upon thy mouth.

v33 For the churning of milk bringeth forth butter, And the wringing of the nose bringeth forth blood; So the forcing of wrath bringeth forth strife.
