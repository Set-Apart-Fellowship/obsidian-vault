# Proverbs 23 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 22|← Proverbs 22]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 24|Proverbs 24 →]]

***

v1 When thou sittest to eat with a ruler, Consider diligently him that is before thee;

v2 And put a knife to thy throat, If thou be a man given to appetite.

v3 Be not desirous of his dainties; Seeing they are deceitful food.

v4 Weary not thyself to be rich; Cease from thine own wisdom.

v5 Wilt thou set thine eyes upon that which is not? For [riches] certainly make themselves wings, Like an eagle that flieth toward heaven.

v6 Eat thou not the bread of him that hath an evil eye, Neither desire thou his dainties:

v7 For as he thinketh within himself, so is he: Eat and drink, saith he to thee; But his heart is not with thee.

v8 The morsel which thou hast eaten shalt thou vomit up, And lose thy sweet words.

v9 Speak not in the hearing of a fool; For he will despise the wisdom of thy words.

v10 Remove not the ancient landmark; And enter not into the fields of the fatherless:

v11 For their Redeemer is strong; He will plead their cause against thee.

v12 Apply thy heart unto instruction, And thine ears to the words of knowledge.

v13 Withhold not correction from the child; [For] if thou beat him with the rod, he will not die.

v14 Thou shalt beat him with the rod, And shalt deliver his soul from Sheol.

v15 My son, if thy heart be wise, My heart will be glad, even mine:

v16 Yea, my heart will rejoice, When thy lips speak right things.

v17 Let not thy heart envy sinners; But [be thou] in the fear of Jehovah all the day long:

v18 For surely there is a reward; And thy hope shall not be cut off.

v19 Hear thou, my son, and be wise, And guide thy heart in the way.

v20 Be not among winebibbers, Among gluttonous eaters of flesh:

v21 For the drunkard and the glutton shall come to poverty; And drowsiness will clothe [a man] with rags.

v22 Hearken unto thy father that begat thee, And despise not thy mother when she is old.

v23 Buy the truth, and sell it not; [Yea], wisdom, and instruction, and understanding.

v24 The father of the righteous will greatly rejoice; And he that begetteth a wise child will have joy of him.

v25 Let thy father and thy mother be glad, And let her that bare thee rejoice.

v26 My son, give me thy heart; And let thine eyes delight in my ways.

v27 For a harlot is a deep ditch; And a foreign woman is a narrow pit.

v28 Yea, she lieth in wait as a robber, And increaseth the treacherous among men.

v29 Who hath woe? who hath sorrow? who hath contentions? Who hath complaining? who hath wounds without cause? Who hath redness of eyes?

v30 They that tarry long at the wine; They that go to seek out mixed wine.

v31 Look not thou upon the wine when it is red, When it sparkleth in the cup, When it goeth down smoothly:

v32 At the last it biteth like a serpent, And stingeth like an adder.

v33 Thine eyes shall behold strange things, And thy heart shall utter perverse things.

v34 Yea, thou shalt be as he that lieth down in the midst of    the sea, Or as he that lieth upon the top of a mast.

v35 They have stricken me, [shalt thou say], and I was not    hurt; They have beaten me, and I felt it not: When shall I awake? I will seek it yet again.
