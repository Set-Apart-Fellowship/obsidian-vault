# Proverbs 14 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 13|← Proverbs 13]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 15|Proverbs 15 →]]

***

v1 Every wise woman buildeth her house; But the foolish plucketh it down with her own hands.

v2 He that walketh in his uprightness feareth Jehovah; But he that is perverse in his ways despiseth him.

v3 In the mouth of the foolish is a rod for [his] pride; But the lips of the wise shall preserve them.

v4 Where no oxen are, the crib is clean; But much increase is by the strength of the ox.

v5 A faithful witness will not lie; But a false witness uttereth lies.

v6 A scoffer seeketh wisdom, and [findeth it] not; But knowledge is easy unto him that hath understanding.

v7 Go into the presence of a foolish man, And thou shalt not perceive [in him] the lips of knowledge.

v8 The wisdom of the prudent is to understand his way; But the folly of fools is deceit.

v9 A trespass-offering mocketh fools; But among the upright there is good will.

v10 The heart knoweth its own bitterness; And a stranger doth not intermeddle with its joy.

v11 The house of the wicked shall be overthrown; But the tent of the upright shall flourish.

v12 There is a way which seemeth right unto a man; But the end thereof are the ways of death.

v13 Even in laughter the heart is sorrowful; And the end of mirth is heaviness.

v14 The backslider in heart shall be filled with his own    ways; And a good man [shall be satisfied] from himself.

v15 The simple believeth every word; But the prudent man looketh well to his going.

v16 A wise man feareth, and departeth from evil; But the fool beareth himself insolently, and is confident.

v17 He that is soon angry will deal foolishly; And a man of wicked devices is hated.

v18 The simple inherit folly; But the prudent are crowned with knowledge.

v19 The evil bow down before the good; And the wicked, at the gates of the righteous.

v20 The poor is hated even of his own neighbor; But the rich hath many friends.

v21 He that despiseth his neighbor sinneth; But he that hath pity on the poor, happy is he.

v22 Do they not err that devise evil? But mercy and truth [shall be to] them that devise good.

v23 In all labor there is profit; But the talk of the lips [tendeth] only to penury.

v24 The crown of the wise is their riches; [But] the folly of fools is [only] folly.

v25 A true witness delivereth souls; But he that uttereth lies [causeth] deceit.

v26 In the fear of Jehovah is strong confidence; And his children shall have a place of refuge.

v27 The fear of Jehovah is a fountain of life, That one may depart from the snares of death.

v28 In the multitude of people is the king's glory; But in the want of people is the destruction of the prince.

v29 He that is slow to anger is of great understanding; But he that is hasty of spirit exalteth folly.

v30 A tranquil heart is the life of the flesh; But envy is the rottenness of the bones.

v31 He that oppresseth the poor reproacheth his Maker; But he that hath mercy on the needy honoreth him.

v32 The wicked is thrust down in his evil-doing; But the righteous hath a refuge in his death.

v33 Wisdom resteth in the heart of him that hath    understanding; But [that which is] in the inward part of fools is made known.

v34 Righteousness exalteth a nation; But sin is a reproach to any people.

v35 The king's favor is toward a servant that dealeth wisely; But his wrath will be [against] him that causeth shame.
