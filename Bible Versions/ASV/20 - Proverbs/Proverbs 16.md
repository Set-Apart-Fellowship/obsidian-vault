# Proverbs 16 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 15|← Proverbs 15]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 17|Proverbs 17 →]]

***

v1 The plans of the heart belong to man; But the answer of the tongue is from Jehovah.

v2 All the ways of a man are clean in his own eyes; But Jehovah weigheth the spirits.

v3 Commit thy works unto Jehovah, And thy purposes shall be established.

v4 Jehovah hath made everything for its own end; Yea, even the wicked for the day of evil.

v5 Every one that is proud in heart is an abomination to    Jehovah: [Though] hand [join] in hand, he shall not be unpunished.

v6 By mercy and truth iniquity is atoned for; And by the fear of Jehovah men depart from evil.

v7 When a man's ways please Jehovah, He maketh even his enemies to be at peace with him.

v8 Better is a little, with righteousness, Than great revenues with injustice.

v9 A man's heart deviseth his way; But Jehovah directeth his steps.

v10 A divine sentence is in the lips of the king; His mouth shall not transgress in judgment.

v11 A just balance and scales are Jehovah's; All the weights of the bag are his work.

v12 It is an abomination to kings to commit wickedness; For the throne is established by righteousness.

v13 Righteous lips are the delight of kings; And they love him that speaketh right.

v14 The wrath of a king is [as] messengers of death; But a wise man will pacify it.

v15 In the light of the king's countenance is life; And his favor is as a cloud of the latter rain.

v16 How much better is it to get wisdom than gold! Yea, to get understanding is rather to be chosen than silver.

v17 The highway of the upright is to depart from evil: He that keepeth his way preserveth his soul.

v18 Pride [goeth] before destruction, And a haughty spirit before a fall.

v19 Better it is to be of a lowly spirit with the poor, Than to divide the spoil with the proud.

v20 He that giveth heed unto the word shall find good; And whoso trusteth in Jehovah, happy is he.

v21 The wise in heart shall be called prudent; And the sweetness of the lips increaseth learning.

v22 Understanding is a well-spring of life unto him that    hath it; But the correction of fools is [their] folly.

v23 The heart of the wise instructeth his mouth, And addeth learning to his lips.

v24 Pleasant words are [as] a honeycomb, Sweet to the soul, and health to the bones.

v25 There is a way which seemeth right unto a man, But the end thereof are the ways of death.

v26 The appetite of the laboring man laboreth for him; For his mouth urgeth him [thereto].

v27 A worthless man deviseth mischief; And in his lips there is as a scorching fire.

v28 A perverse man scattereth abroad strife; And a whisperer separateth chief friends.

v29 A man of violence enticeth his neighbor, And leadeth him in a way that is not good.

v30 He that shutteth his eyes, [it is] to devise perverse    things: He that compresseth his lips bringeth evil to pass.

v31 The hoary head is a crown of glory; It shall be found in the way of righteousness.

v32 He that is slow to anger is better than the mighty; And he that ruleth his spirit, than he that taketh a city.

v33 The lot is cast into the lap; But the whole disposing thereof is of Jehovah.
