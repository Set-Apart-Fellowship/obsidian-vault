# Proverbs 18 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 17|← Proverbs 17]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 19|Proverbs 19 →]]

***

v1 He that separateth himself seeketh [his own] desire, And rageth against all sound wisdom.

v2 A fool hath no delight in understanding, But only that his heart may reveal itself.

v3 When the wicked cometh, there cometh also contempt, And with ignominy [cometh] reproach.

v4 The words of a man's mouth are [as] deep waters; The wellspring of wisdom is [as] a flowing brook.

v5 To respect the person of the wicked is not good, [Nor] to turn aside the righteous in judgment.

v6 A fool's lips enter into contention, And his mouth calleth for stripes.

v7 A fool's mouth is his destruction, And his lips are the snare of his soul.

v8 The words of a whisperer are as dainty morsels, And they go down into the innermost parts.

v9 He also that is slack in his work Is brother to him that is a destroyer.

v10 The name of Jehovah is a strong tower; The righteous runneth into it, and is safe.

v11 The rich man's wealth is his strong city, And as a high wall in his own imagination.

v12 Before destruction the heart of man is haughty; And before honor [goeth] humility.

v13 He that giveth answer before he heareth, It is folly and shame unto him.

v14 The spirit of a man will sustain his infirmity; But a broken spirit who can bear?

v15 The heart of the prudent getteth knowledge; And the ear of the wise seeketh knowledge.

v16 A man's gift maketh room for him, And bringeth him before great men.

v17 He that pleadeth his cause first [seemeth] just; But his neighbor cometh and searcheth him out.

v18 The lot causeth contentions to cease, And parteth between the mighty.

v19 A brother offended [is harder to be won] than a strong    city; And [such] contentions are like the bars of a castle.

v20 A man's belly shall be filled with the fruit of his    mouth; With the increase of his lips shall he be satisfied.

v21 Death and life are in the power of the tongue; And they that love it shall eat the fruit thereof.

v22 Whoso findeth a wife findeth a good thing, And obtaineth favor of Jehovah.

v23 The poor useth entreaties; But the rich answereth roughly.

v24 He that maketh many friends [doeth it] to his own    destruction; But there is a friend that sticketh closer than a brother.
