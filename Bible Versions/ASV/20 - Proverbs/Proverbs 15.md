# Proverbs 15 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 14|← Proverbs 14]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 16|Proverbs 16 →]]

***

v1 A soft answer turneth away wrath; But a grievous word stirreth up anger.

v2 The tongue of the wise uttereth knowledge aright; But the mouth of fools poureth out folly.

v3 The eyes of Jehovah are in every place, Keeping watch upon the evil and the good.

v4 A gentle tongue is a tree of life; But perverseness therein is a breaking of the spirit.

v5 A fool despiseth his father's correction; But he that regardeth reproof getteth prudence.

v6 In the house of the righteous is much treasure; But in the revenues of the wicked is trouble.

v7 The lips of the wise disperse knowledge; But the heart of the foolish [doeth] not so.

v8 The sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination to Jehovah; But the prayer of the upright is his delight.

v9 The way of the wicked is an abomination to Jehovah; But he loveth him that followeth after righteousness.

v10 There is grievous correction for him that forsaketh the    way; [And] he that hateth reproof shall die.

v11 Sheol and Abaddon are before Jehovah: How much more then the hearts of the children of men!

v12 A scoffer loveth not to be reproved; He will not go unto the wise.

v13 A glad heart maketh a cheerful countenance; But by sorrow of heart the spirit is broken.

v14 The heart of him that hath understanding seeketh    knowledge; But the mouth of fools feedeth on folly.

v15 All the days of the afflicted are evil; But he that is of a cheerful heart [hath] a continual feast.

v16 Better is little, with the fear of Jehovah, Than great treasure and trouble therewith.

v17 Better is a dinner of herbs, where love is, Than a stalled ox and hatred therewith.

v18 A wrathful man stirreth up contention; But he that is slow to anger appeaseth strife.

v19 The way of the sluggard is as a hedge of thorns; But the path of the upright is made a highway.

v20 A wise son maketh a glad father; But a foolish man despiseth his mother.

v21 Folly is joy to him that is void of wisdom; But a man of understanding maketh straight his going.

v22 Where there is no counsel, purposes are disappointed; But in the multitude of counsellors they are established.

v23 A man hath joy in the answer of his mouth; And a word in due season, how good is it!

v24 To the wise the way of life [goeth] upward, That he may depart from Sheol beneath.

v25 Jehovah will root up the house of the proud; But he will establish the border of the widow.

v26 Evil devices are an abomination to Jehovah; But pleasant words [are] pure.

v27 He that is greedy of gain troubleth his own house; But he that hateth bribes shall live.

v28 The heart of the righteous studieth to answer; But the mouth of the wicked poureth out evil things.

v29 Jehovah is far from the wicked; But he heareth the prayer of the righteous.

v30 The light of the eyes rejoiceth the heart; [And] good tidings make the bones fat.

v31 The ear that hearkeneth to the reproof of life Shall abook_ide among the wise.

v32 He that refuseth correction despiseth his own soul; But he that hearkeneth to reproof getteth understanding.

v33 The fear of Jehovah is the instruction of wisdom; And before honor [goeth] humility.
