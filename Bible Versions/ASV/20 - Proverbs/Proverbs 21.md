# Proverbs 21 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 20|← Proverbs 20]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 22|Proverbs 22 →]]

***

v1 The king's heart is in the hand of Jehovah as the    watercourses: He turneth it whithersoever he will.

v2 Every way of a man is right in his own eyes; But Jehovah weigheth the hearts.

v3 To do righteousness and justice Is more acceptable to Jehovah than sacrifice.

v4 A high look, and a proud heart, [Even] the lamp of the wicked, is sin.

v5 The thoughts of the diligent [tend] only to plenteousness; But every one that is hasty [hasteth] only to want.

v6 The getting of treasures by a lying tongue Is a vapor driven to and fro by them that seek death.

v7 The violence of the wicked shall sweep them away, Because they refuse to do justice.

v8 The way of him that is laden with guilt is exceeding    crooked; But as for the pure, his work is right.

v9 It is better to dwell in the corner of the housetop, Than with a contentious woman in a wide house.

v10 The soul of the wicked desireth evil: His neighbor findeth no favor in his eyes.

v11 When the scoffer is punished, the simple is made wise; And when the wise is instructed, he receiveth knowledge.

v12 The righteous man considereth the house of the wicked, [How] the wicked are overthrown to [their] ruin.

v13 Whoso stoppeth his ears at the cry of the poor, He also shall cry, but shall not be heard.

v14 A gift in secret pacifieth anger; And a present in the bosom, strong wrath.

v15 It is joy to the righteous to do justice; But it is a destruction to the workers of iniquity.

v16 The man that wandereth out of the way of understanding Shall rest in the assembly of the dead.

v17 He that loveth pleasure shall be a poor man: He that loveth wine and oil shall not be rich.

v18 The wicked is a ransom for the righteous; And the treacherous [cometh] in the stead of the upright.

v19 It is better to dwell in a desert land, Than with a contentious and fretful woman.

v20 There is precious treasure and oil in the dwelling of    the wise; But a foolish man swalloweth it up.

v21 He that followeth after righteousness and kindness Findeth life, righteousness, and honor.

v22 A wise man scaleth the city of the mighty, And bringeth down the strength of the confidence thereof.

v23 Whoso keepeth his mouth and his tongue Keepeth his soul from troubles.

v24 The proud and haughty man, scoffer is his name; He worketh in the arrogance of pride.

v25 The desire of the sluggard killeth him; For his hands refuse to labor.

v26 There is that coveteth greedily all the day long; But the righteous giveth and withholdeth not.

v27 The sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination: How much more, when he bringeth it with a wicked mind!

v28 A false witness shall perish; But the man that heareth shall speak so as to endure.

v29 A wicked man hardeneth his face; But as for the upright, he establisheth his ways.

v30 There is no wisdom nor understanding Nor counsel against Jehovah.

v31 The horse is prepared against the day of battle; But victory is of Jehovah.
