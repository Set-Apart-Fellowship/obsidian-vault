# Proverbs 2 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 1|← Proverbs 1]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 3|Proverbs 3 →]]

***

v1 My son, if thou wilt receive my words, And lay up my commandments with thee;

v2 So as to incline thine ear unto wisdom, And apply thy heart to understanding;

v3 Yea, if thou cry after discernment, And lift up thy voice for understanding;

v4 If thou seek her as silver, And search for her as for hid treasures:

v5 Then shalt thou understand the fear of Jehovah, And find the knowledge of God.

v6 For Jehovah giveth wisdom; Out of his mouth [cometh] knowledge and understanding:

v7 He layeth up sound wisdom for the upright; [He is] a shield to them that walk in integrity;

v8 That he may guard the paths of justice, And preserve the way of his saints.

v9 Then shalt thou understand righteousness and justice, And equity, [yea], every good path.

v10 For wisdom shall enter into thy heart, And knowledge shall be pleasant unto thy soul;

v11 Discretion shall watch over thee; Understanding shall keep thee:

v12 To deliver thee from the way of evil, From the men that speak perverse things;

v13 Who forsake the paths of uprightness, To walk in the ways of darkness;

v14 Who rejoice to do evil, And delight in the perverseness of evil;

v15 Who are crooked in their ways, And wayward in their paths:

v16 To deliver thee from the strange woman, Even from the foreigner that flattereth with her words;

v17 That forsaketh the friend of her youth, And forgetteth the covenant of her God:

v18 For her house inclineth unto death, And her paths unto the dead;

v19 None that go unto her return again, Neither do they attain unto the paths of life:

v20 That thou mayest walk in the way of good men, And keep the paths of the righteous.

v21 For the upright shall dwell in the land, And the perfect shall remain in it.

v22 But the wicked shall be cut off from the land, And the treacherous shall be rooted out of it.
