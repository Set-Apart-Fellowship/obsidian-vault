# Proverbs 25 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 24|← Proverbs 24]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 26|Proverbs 26 →]]

***

v1 These also are proverbs of Solomon, which the men of Hezekiah king of Judah copied out.

v2 It is the glory of God to conceal a thing; But the glory of kings is to search out a matter.

v3 As the heavens for height, and the earth for depth, So the heart of kings is unsearchable.

v4 Take away the dross from the silver, And there cometh forth a vessel for the refiner:

v5 Take away the wicked [from] before the king, And his throne shall be established in righteousness.

v6 Put not thyself forward in the presence of the king, And stand not in the place of great men:

v7 For better is it that it be said unto thee, Come up    hither, Than that thou shouldest be put lower in the presence of the    prince, Whom thine eyes have seen.

v8 Go not forth hastily to strive, Lest [thou know not] what to do in the end thereof, When thy neighbor hath put thee to shame.

v9 Debate thy cause with thy neighbor [himself], And disclose not the secret of another;

v10 Lest he that heareth it revile thee, And thine infamy turn not away.

v11 A word fitly spoken Is [like] apples of gold in network of silver.

v12 [As] an ear-ring of gold, and an ornament of fine gold, [So is] a wise reprover upon an obedient ear.

v13 As the cold of snow in the time of harvest, [So is] a faithful messenger to them that send him; For he refresheth the soul of his masters.

v14 [As] clouds and wind without rain, [So is] he that boasteth himself of his gifts falsely.

v15 By long forbearing is a ruler persuaded, And a soft tongue breaketh the bone.

v16 Hast thou found honey? eat so much as is sufficient for    thee, Lest thou be filled therewith, and vomit it.

v17 Let thy foot be seldom in thy neighbor's house, Lest he be weary of thee, and hate thee.

v18 A man that beareth false witness against his neighbor Is a maul, and a sword, and a sharp arrow.

v19 Confidence in an unfaithful man in time of trouble Is [like] a broken tooth, and a foot out of joint.

v20 [As] one that taketh off a garment in cold weather, [and    as] vinegar upon soda, So is he that singeth songs to a heavy heart.

v21 If thine enemy be hungry, give him bread to eat; And if he be thirsty, give him water to drink:

v22 For thou wilt heap coals of fire upon his head, And Jehovah will reward thee.

v23 The north wind bringeth forth rain: So doth a backbiting tongue an angry countenance.

v24 It is better to dwell in the corner of the housetop, Than with a contentious woman in a wide house.

v25 [As] cold waters to a thirsty soul, So is good news from a far country.

v26 [As] a troubled fountain, and a corrupted spring, [So is] a righteous man that giveth way before the wicked.

v27 It is not good to eat much honey: So [for men] to search out their own glory is grievous.

v28 He whose spirit is without restraint Is [like] a city that is broken down and without walls.
