# Proverbs 7 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 6|← Proverbs 6]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 8|Proverbs 8 →]]

***

v1 My son, keep my words, And lay up my commandments with thee.

v2 Keep my commandments and live; And my law as the apple of thine eye.

v3 Bind them upon thy fingers; Write them upon the tablet of thy heart.

v4 Say unto wisdom, Thou art my sister; And call understanding [thy] kinswoman:

v5 That they may keep thee from the strange woman, From the foreigner that flattereth with her words.

v6 For at the window of my house I looked forth through my lattice;

v7 And I beheld among the simple ones, I discerned among the youths, A young man void of understanding,

v8 Passing through the street near her corner; And he went the way to her house,

v9 In the twilight, in the evening of the day, In the middle of the night and in the darkness.

v10 And, behold, there met him a woman With the attire of a harlot, and wily of heart.

v11 She is clamorous and wilful; Her feet abook_ide not in her house:

v12 Now she is in the streets, now in the broad places, And lieth in wait at every corner.

v13 So she caught him, and kissed him, [And] with an impudent face she said unto him:

v14 Sacrifices of peace-offerings are with me; This day have I paid my vows.

v15 Therefore came I forth to meet thee, Diligently to seek thy face, and I have found thee.

v16 I have spread my couch with carpets of tapestry, With striped cloths of the yarn of Egypt.

v17 I have perfumed my bed With myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon.

v18 Come, let us take our fill of love until the morning; Let us solace ourselves with loves.

v19 For the man is not at home; He is gone a long journey:

v20 He hath taken a bag of money with him; He will come home at the full moon.

v21 With her much fair speech she causeth him to yield; With the flattering of her lips she forceth him along.

v22 He goeth after her straightway, As an ox goeth to the slaughter, Or as [one in] fetters to the correction of the fool;

v23 Till an arrow strike through his liver; As a bird hasteth to the snare, And knoweth not that it is for his life.

v24 Now therefore, [my] sons, hearken unto me, And attend to the words of my mouth.

v25 Let not thy heart decline to her ways; Go not astray in her paths.

v26 For she hath cast down many wounded: Yea, all her slain are a mighty host.

v27 Her house is the way to Sheol, Going down to the chambers of death.
