# 1 Corinthians 16 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15|← 1 Corinthians 15]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians]]

***

v1 Now concerning the collection for the saints, as I gave order to the churches of Galatia, so also do ye.

v2 Upon the first day of the week let each one of you lay by him in store, as he may prosper, that no collections be made when I come.

v3 And when I arrive, whomsoever ye shall approve, them will I send with letters to carry your bounty unto Jerusalem:

v4 and if it be meet for me to go also, they shall go with me.

v5 But I will come unto you, when I shall have passed through Macedonia; for I pass through Macedonia;

v6 but with you it may be that I shall abook_ide, or even winter, that ye may set me forward on my journey whithersoever I go.

v7 For I do not wish to see you now by the way; for I hope to tarry a while with you, if the Lord permit.

v8 But I will tarry at Ephesus until Pentecost;

v9 for a great door and effectual is opened unto me, and there are many adversaries.

v10 Now if Timothy come, see that he be with you without fear; for he worketh the work of the Lord, as I also do:

v11 let no man therefore despise him. But set him forward on his journey in peace, that he may come unto me: for I expect him with the brethren.

v12 But as touching Apollos the brother, I besought him much to come unto you with the brethren: and it was not all [his] will to come now; but he will come when he shall have opportunity.

v13 Watch ye, stand fast in the faith, quit you like men, be strong.

v14 Let all that ye do be done in love.

v15 Now I beseech you, brethren (ye know the house of Stephanas, that it is the firstfruits of Achaia, and that they have set themselves to minister unto the saints),

v16 that ye also be in subjection unto such, and to every one that helpeth in the work and laboreth.

v17 And I rejoice at the coming of Stephanas and Fortunatus and Achaicus: for that which was lacking on your part they supplied.

v18 For they refreshed my spirit and yours: acknowledge ye therefore them that are such.

v19 The churches of Asia salute you. Aquila and Prisca salute you much in the Lord, with the church that is in their house.

v20 All the brethren salute you. Salute one another with a holy kiss.

v21 The salutation of me Paul with mine own hand.

v22 If any man loveth not the Lord, let him be anathema. Maranatha.

v23 The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with you.

v24 My love be with you all in Christ Jesus. Amen.
