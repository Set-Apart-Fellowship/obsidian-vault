# 1 Corinthians 12 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 11|← 1 Corinthians 11]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 13|1 Corinthians 13 →]]

***

v1 Now concerning spiritual [gifts], brethren, I would not have you ignorant.

v2 Ye know that when ye were Gentiles [ye were] led away unto those dumb idols, howsoever ye might led.

v3 Wherefore I make known unto you, that no man speaking in the Spirit of God saith, Jesus is anathema; and no man can say, Jesus is Lord, but in the Holy Spirit.

v4 Now there are diversities of gifts, but the same Spirit.

v5 And there are diversities of ministrations, and the same Lord.

v6 And there are diversities of workings, but the same God, who worketh all things in all.

v7 But to each one is given the manifestation of the Spirit to profit withal.

v8 For to one is given through the Spirit the word of wisdom; and to another the word of knowledge, according to the same Spirit:

v9 to another faith, in the same Spirit; and to another gifts of healings, in the one Spirit;

v10 and to another workings of miracles; and to another prophecy; and to another discernings of spirits; to another [divers] kinds of tongues; and to another the interpretation of tongues:

v11 but all these worketh the one and the same Spirit, dividing to each one severally even as he will.

v12 For as the body is one, and hath many members, and all the members of the body, being many, are one body; so also is Christ.

v13 For in one Spirit were we all baptized into one body, whether Jews or Greeks, whether bond or free; and were all made to drink of one Spirit.

v14 For the body is not one member, but many.

v15 If the foot shall say, Because I am not the hand, I am not of the body; it is not therefore not of the body.

v16 And if the ear shall say, Because I am not the eye, I am not of the body; it is not therefore not of the body.

v17 If the whole body were an eye, where were the hearing? If the whole were hearing, where were the smelling?

v18 But now hath God set the members each one of them in the body, even as it pleased him.

v19 And if they were all one member, where were the body?

v20 But now they are many members, but one body.

v21 And the eye cannot say to the hand, I have no need of thee: or again the head to the feet, I have no need of you.

v22 Nay, much rather, those members of the body which seem to be more feeble are necessary:

v23 and those [parts] of the body, which we think to be less honorable, upon these we bestow more abundant honor; and our uncomely [parts] have more abundant comeliness;

v24 whereas our comely [parts] have no need: but God tempered the body together, giving more abundant honor to that [part] which lacked;

v25 that there should be no schism in the body; but [that] the members should have the same care one for another.

v26 And whether one member suffereth, all the members suffer with it; or [one] member is honored, all the members rejoice with it.

v27 Now ye are the body of Christ, and severally members thereof.

v28 And God hath set some in the church, first apostles, secondly prophets, thirdly teachers, then miracles, then gifts of healings, helps, governments, [divers] kinds of tongues.

v29 Are all apostles? are all prophets? are all teachers? are all [workers of] miracles?

v30 have all gifts of healings? do all speak with tongues? do all interpret?

v31 But desire earnestly the greater gifts. And moreover a most excellent way show I unto you.
