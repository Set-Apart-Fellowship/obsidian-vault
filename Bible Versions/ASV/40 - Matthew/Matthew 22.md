# Matthew 22 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/40 - Matthew/Matthew 21|← Matthew 21]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/40 - Matthew/Matthew]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/40 - Matthew/Matthew 23|Matthew 23 →]]

***

v1 And Jesus answered and spake again in parables unto them, saying,

v2 The kingdom of heaven is likened unto a certain king, who made a marriage feast for his son,

v3 and sent forth his servants to call them that were book_idden to the marriage feast: and they would not come.

v4 Again he sent forth other servants, saying, Tell them that are book_idden, Behold, I have made ready my dinner; my oxen and my fatlings are killed, and all things are ready: come to the marriage feast.

v5 But they made light of it, and went their ways, one to his own farm, another to his merchandise;

v6 and the rest laid hold on his servants, and treated them shamefully, and killed them.

v7 But the king was wroth; and he sent his armies, and destroyed those murderers, and burned their city.

v8 Then saith he to his servants, The wedding is ready, but they that were book_idden were not worthy.

v9 Go ye therefore unto the partings of the highways, and as many as ye shall find, book_id to the marriage feast.

v10 And those servants went out into the highways, and gathered together all as many as they found, both bad and good: and the wedding was filled with guests.

v11 But when the king came in to behold the guests, he saw there a man who had not on a wedding-garment:

v12 and he saith unto him, Friend, how camest thou in hither not having a wedding-garment? And he was speechless.

v13 Then the king said to the servants, Bind him hand and foot, and cast him out into the outer darkness; there shall be the weeping and the gnashing of teeth.

v14 For many are called, but few chosen.

v15 Then went the Pharisees, and took counsel how they might ensnare him in [his] talk.

v16 And they send to him their disciples, with the Herodians, saying, Teacher, we know that thou art true, and teachest the way of God in truth, and carest not for any one: for thou regardest not the person of men.

v17 Tell us therefore, What thinkest thou? Is it lawful to give tribute unto Caesar, or not?

v18 But Jesus perceived their wickedness, and said, Why make ye trial of me, ye hypocrites?

v19 Show me the tribute money. And they brought unto him a denarius.

v20 And he saith unto them, Whose is this image and superscription?

v21 They say unto him, Caesar's. Then saith he unto them, Render therefore unto Caesar the things that are Caesar's; and unto God the things that are God's.

v22 And when they heard it, they marvelled, and left him, and went away.

v23 On that day there came to him Sadducees, they that say that there is no resurrection: and they asked him,

v24 saying, Teacher, Moses said, If a man die, having no children, his brother shall marry his wife, and raise up seed unto his brother.

v25 Now there were with us seven brethren: and the first married and deceased, and having no seed left his wife unto his brother;

v26 in like manner the second also, and the third, unto the seventh.

v27 And after them all, the woman died.

v28 In the resurrection therefore whose wife shall she be of the seven? for they all had her.

v29 But Jesus answered and said unto them, Ye do err, not knowing the scriptures, nor the power of God.

v30 For in the resurrection they neither marry, nor are given in marriage, but are as angels in heaven.

v31 But as touching the resurrection of the dead, have ye not read that which was spoken unto you by God, saying,

v32 I am the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob? God is not [the God] of the dead, but of the living.

v33 And when the multitudes heard it, they were astonished at his teaching.

v34 But the Pharisees, when they heard that he had put the Sadducees to silence, gathered themselves together.

v35 And one of them, a lawyer, asked him a question, trying him:

v36 Teacher, which is the great commandment in the law?

v37 And he said unto him, Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thy heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy mind.

v38 This is the great and first commandment.

v39 And a second like [unto it] is this, Thou shalt love thy neighbor as thyself.

v40 On these two commandments the whole law hangeth, and the prophets.

v41 Now while the Pharisees were gathered together, Jesus asked them a question,

v42 saying, What think ye of the Christ? whose son is he? They say unto him, [The son] of David.

v43 He saith unto them, How then doth David in the Spirit call him Lord, saying,

v44 The Lord said unto my Lord, Sit thou on my right hand, Till I put thine enemies underneath thy feet?

v45 If David then calleth him Lord, how is he his son?

v46 And no one was able to answer him a word, neither durst any man from that day forth ask him any more questions.
