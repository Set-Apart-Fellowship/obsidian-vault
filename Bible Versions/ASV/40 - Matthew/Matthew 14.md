# Matthew 14 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/40 - Matthew/Matthew 13|← Matthew 13]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/40 - Matthew/Matthew]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/40 - Matthew/Matthew 15|Matthew 15 →]]

***

v1 At that season Herod the tetrarch heard the report concerning Jesus,

v2 and said unto his servants, This is John the Baptist; he is risen from the dead; and therefore do these powers work in him.

v3 For Herod had laid hold on John, and bound him, and put him in prison for the sake of Herodias, his brother Philip's wife.

v4 For John said unto him, It is not lawful for thee to have her.

v5 And when he would have put him to death, he feared the multitude, because they counted him as a prophet.

v6 But when Herod's birthday came, the daughter of Herodias danced in the midst, and pleased Herod.

v7 Whereupon he promised with an oath to give her whatsoever she should ask.

v8 And she, being put forward by her mother, saith, Give me here on a platter the head of John the Baptist.

v9 And the king was grieved; but for the sake of his oaths, and of them that sat at meat with him, he commanded it to be given;

v10 and he sent and beheaded John in the prison.

v11 And his head was brought on a platter, and given to the damsel: and she brought it to her mother.

v12 And his disciples came, and took up the corpse, and buried him; and they went and told Jesus.

v13 Now when Jesus heard [it], he withdrew from thence in a boat, to a desert place apart: and when the multitudes heard [thereof,] they followed him on foot from the cities.

v14 And he came forth, and saw a great multitude, and he had compassion on them, and healed their sick.

v15 And when even was come, the disciples came to him, saying, The place is desert, and the time is already past; send the multitudes away, that they may go into the villages, and buy themselves food.

v16 But Jesus said unto them, They have no need to go away; give ye them to eat.

v17 And they say unto him, We have here but five loaves, and two fishes.

v18 And he said, Bring them hither to me.

v19 And he commanded the multitudes to sit down on the grass; and he took the five loaves, and the two fishes, and looking up to heaven, he blessed, and brake and gave the loaves to the disciples, and the disciples to the multitudes.

v20 And they all ate, and were filled: and they took up that which remained over of the broken pieces, twelve baskets full.

v21 And they that did eat were about five thousand men, besides women and children.

v22 And straightway he constrained the disciples to enter into the boat, and to go before him unto the other side, till he should send the multitudes away.

v23 And after he had sent the multitudes away, he went up into the mountain apart to pray: and when even was come, he was there alone.

v24 But the boat was now in the midst of the sea, distressed by the waves; for the wind was contrary.

v25 And in the fourth watch of the night he came unto them, walking upon the sea.

v26 And when the disciples saw him walking on the sea, they were troubled, saying, It is a ghost; and they cried out for fear.

v27 But straightway Jesus spake unto them, saying Be of good cheer; it is I; be not afraid.

v28 And Peter answered him and said, Lord, if it be thou, book_id me come unto the upon the waters.

v29 And he said, Come. And Peter went down from the boat, and walked upon the waters to come to Jesus.

v30 But when he saw the wind, he was afraid; and beginning to sink, he cried out, saying, Lord, save me.

v31 And immediately Jesus stretched forth his hand, and took hold of him, and saith unto him, O thou of little faith, wherefore didst thou doubt?

v32 And when they were gone up into the boat, the wind ceased.

v33 And they that were in the boat worshipped him, saying, Of a truth thou art the Son of God.

v34 And when they had crossed over, they came to the land, unto Gennesaret.

v35 And when the men of that place knew him, they sent into all that region round about, and brought unto him all that were sick,

v36 and they besought him that they might only touch the border of his garment: and as many as touched were made whole.
