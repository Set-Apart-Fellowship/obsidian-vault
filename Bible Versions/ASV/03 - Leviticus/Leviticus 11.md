# Leviticus 11 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 10|← Leviticus 10]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 12|Leviticus 12 →]]

***

v1 And Jehovah spake unto Moses and to Aaron, saying unto them,

v2 Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, These are the living things which ye may eat among all the beasts that are on the earth.

v3 Whatsoever parteth the hoof, and is clovenfooted, [and] cheweth the cud, among the beasts, that may ye eat.

v4 Nevertheless these shall ye not eat of them that chew the cud, or of them that part the hoof: the camel, because he cheweth the cud but parteth not the hoof, he is unclean unto you.

v5 And the coney, because he cheweth the cud but parteth not the hoof, he is unclean unto you.

v6 And the hare, because she cheweth the cud but parteth not the hoof, she is unclean unto you.

v7 And the swine, because he parteth the hoof, and is clovenfooted, but cheweth not the cud, he is unclean unto you.

v8 Of their flesh ye shall not eat, and their carcasses ye shall not touch; they are unclean unto you.

v9 These may ye eat of all that are in the waters: whatsoever hath fins and scales in the waters, in the seas, and in the rivers, that may ye eat.

v10 And all that have not fins and scales in the seas, and in the rivers, of all that move in the waters, and of all the living creatures that are in the waters, they are an abomination unto you,

v11 and they shall be an abomination unto you; ye shall not eat of their flesh, and their carcasses ye shall have in abomination.

v12 Whatsoever hath no fins nor scales in the waters, that is an abomination unto you.

v13 And these ye shall have in abomination among the birds; they shall not be eaten, they are an abomination: the eagle, and the gier-eagle, and the ospray,

v14 and the kite, and the falcon after its kind,

v15 every raven after its kind,

v16 and the ostrich, and the night-hawk, and the seamew, and the hawk after its kind,

v17 and the little owl, and the cormorant, and the great owl,

v18 and the horned owl, and the pelican, and the vulture,

v19 and the stork, the heron after its kind, and the hoopoe, and the bat.

v20 All winged creeping things that go upon all fours are an abomination unto you.

v21 Yet these may ye eat of all winged creeping things that go upon all fours, which have legs above their feet, wherewith to leap upon the earth.

v22 Even these of them ye may eat: the locust after its kind, and the bald locust after its kind, and the cricket after its kind, and the grasshopper after its kind.

v23 But all winged creeping things, which have four feet, are an abomination unto you.

v24 And by these ye shall become unclean: whosoever toucheth the carcass of them shall be unclean until the even;

v25 And whosoever beareth [aught] of the carcass of them shall wash his clothes, and be unclean until the even.

v26 Every beast which parteth the hoof, and is not clovenfooted, nor cheweth the cud, is unclean unto you: every one that toucheth them shall be unclean.

v27 And whatsoever goeth upon its paws, among all beasts that go on all fours, they are unclean unto you: whoso toucheth their carcass shall be unclean until the even.

v28 And he that beareth the carcass of them shall wash his clothes, and be unclean until the even: they are unclean unto you.

v29 And these are they which are unclean unto you among the creeping things that creep upon the earth: the weasel, and the mouse, and the great lizard after its kind,

v30 and the gecko, and the land-crocodile, and the lizard, and the sand-lizard, and the chameleon.

v31 These are they which are unclean to you among all that creep: whosoever doth touch them, when they are dead, shall be unclean until the even.

v32 And upon whatsoever any of them, when they are dead, doth fall, it shall be unclean; whether it be any vessel of wood, or raiment, or skin, or sack, whatsoever vessel it be, wherewith any work is done, it must be put into water, and it shall be unclean until the even; then shall it be clean.

v33 And every earthen vessel, whereinto any of them falleth, whatsoever is in it shall be unclean, and it ye shall break.

v34 All food [therein] which may be eaten, that on which water cometh, shall be unclean; and all drink that may be drunk in every [such] vessel shall be unclean.

v35 And every thing whereupon [any part] of their carcass falleth shall be unclean; whether oven, or range for pots, it shall be broken in pieces: they are unclean, and shall be unclean unto you.

v36 Nevertheless a fountain or a pit wherein is a gathering of water shall be clean: but that which toucheth their carcass shall be unclean.

v37 And if [aught] of their carcass fall upon any sowing seed which is to be sown, it is clean.

v38 But if water be put upon the seed, and [aught] of their carcass fall thereon, it is unclean unto you.

v39 And if any beast, of which ye may eat, die; he that toucheth the carcass thereof shall be unclean until the even.

v40 And he that eateth of the carcass of it shall wash his clothes, and be unclean until the even: he also that beareth the carcass of it shall wash his clothes, and be unclean until the even.

v41 And every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth is an abomination; it shall not be eaten.

v42 Whatsoever goeth upon the belly, and whatsoever goeth upon all fours, or whatsoever hath many feet, even all creeping things that creep upon the earth, them ye shall not eat; for they are an abomination.

v43 Ye shall not make yourselves abominable with any creeping thing that creepeth, neither shall ye make yourselves unclean with them, that ye should be defiled thereby.

v44 For I am Jehovah your God: sanctify yourselves therefore, and be ye holy; for I am holy: neither shall ye defile yourselves with any manner of creeping thing that moveth upon the earth.

v45 For I am Jehovah that brought you up out of the land of Egypt, to be your God: ye shall therefore be holy, for I am holy.

v46 This is the law of the beast, and of the bird, and of every living creature that moveth in the waters, and of every creature that creepeth upon the earth;

v47 to make a distinction between the unclean and the clean, and between the living thing that may be eaten and the living thing that may not be eaten.
