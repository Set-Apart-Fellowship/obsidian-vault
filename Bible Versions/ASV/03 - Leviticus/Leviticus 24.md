# Leviticus 24 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 23|← Leviticus 23]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 25|Leviticus 25 →]]

***

v1 And Jehovah spake unto Moses, saying,

v2 Command the children of Israel, that they bring unto thee pure olive oil beaten for the light, to cause a lamp to burn continually.

v3 Without the veil of the testimony, in the tent of meeting, shall Aaron keep it in order from evening to morning before Jehovah continually: it shall be a statute for ever throughout your generations.

v4 He shall keep in order the lamps upon the pure candlestick before Jehovah continually.

v5 And thou shalt take fine flour, and bake twelve cakes thereof: two tenth parts [of an ephah] shall be in one cake.

v6 And thou shalt set them in two rows, six on a row, upon the pure table before Jehovah.

v7 And thou shalt put pure frankincense upon each row, that it may be to the bread for a memorial, even an offering made by fire unto Jehovah.

v8 Every sabbath day he shall set it in order before Jehovah continually; it is on the behalf of the children of Israel, an everlasting covenant.

v9 And it shall be for Aaron and his sons; and they shall eat it in a holy place: for it is most holy unto him of the offerings of Jehovah made by fire by a perpetual statute.

v10 And the son of an Israelitish woman, whose father was an Egyptian, went out among the children of Israel; and the son of the Israelitish woman and a man of Israel strove together in the camp:

v11 and the son of the Israelitish woman blasphemed the Name, and cursed; and they brought him unto Moses. And his mother's name was Shelomith, the daughter of Dibri, of the tribe of Dan.

v12 And they put him in ward, that it might be declared unto them at the mouth of Jehovah.

v13 And Jehovah spake unto Moses, saying,

v14 Bring forth him that hath cursed without the camp; and let all that heard him lay their hands upon his head, and let all the congregation stone him.

v15 And thou shalt speak unto the children of Israel, saying, Whosoever curseth his God shall bear his sin.

v16 And he that blasphemeth the name of Jehovah, he shall surely be put to death; all the congregation shall certainly stone him: as well the sojourner, as the home-born, when he blasphemeth the name [of Jehovah], shall be put to death.

v17 And he that smiteth any man mortally shall surely be put to death.

v18 And he that smiteth a beast mortally shall make it good, life for life.

v19 And if a man cause a blemish in his neighbor; as he hath done, so shall it be done to him:

v20 breach for breach, eye for eye, tooth for tooth; as he hath caused a blemish in a man, so shall it be rendered unto him.

v21 And he that killeth a beast shall make it good: and he that killeth a man shall be put to death.

v22 Ye shall have one manner of law, as well for the sojourner, as for the home-born: for I am Jehovah your God.

v23 And Moses spake to the children of Israel; and they brought forth him that had cursed out of the camp, and stoned him with stones. And the children of Israel did as Jehovah commanded Moses.
