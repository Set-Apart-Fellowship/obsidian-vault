# 2 Timothy 2 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy 1|← 2 Timothy 1]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy 3|2 Timothy 3 →]]

***

v1 Thou therefore, my child, be strengthened in the grace that is in Christ Jesus.

v2 And the things which thou hast heard from me among many witnesses, the same commit thou to faithful men, who shall be able to teach others also.

v3 Suffer hardship with [me], as a good soldier of Christ Jesus.

v4 No soldier on service entangleth himself in the affairs of [this] life; that he may please him who enrolled him as a soldier.

v5 And if also a man contend in the games, he is not crowded, except he have contended lawfully.

v6 The husbandmen that laboreth must be the first to partake of the fruits.

v7 Consider what I say; for the Lord shall give thee understanding in all things.

v8 Remember Jesus Christ, risen from the dead, of the seed of David, according to my gospel:

v9 wherein I suffer hardship unto bonds, as a malefactor; but the word of God is not bound.

v10 Therefore I endure all things for the elect's sake, that they also may obtain the salvation which is in Christ Jesus with eternal glory.

v11 Faithful is the saying: For if we died with him, we shall also live with him:

v12 if we endure, we shall also reign with him: if we shall deny him, he also will deny us:

v13 if we are faithless, he abook_ideth faithful; for he cannot deny himself.

v14 Of these things put them in remembrance, charging [them] in the sight of the Lord, that they strive not about words, to no profit, to the subverting of them that hear.

v15 Give diligence to present thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, handling aright the word of truth.

v16 But shun profane babblings: for they will proceed further in ungodliness,

v17 and their word will eat as doth a gangrene: or whom is Hymenaeus an Philetus;

v18 men who concerning the truth have erred, saying that the resurrection is past already, and overthrow the faith of some.

v19 Howbeit the firm foundation of God standeth, having this seal, The Lord knoweth them that are his: and, Let every one that nameth the name of the Lord depart from unrighteousness.

v20 Now in a great house there are not only vessels of gold and of silver, but also of wood and of earth; and some unto honor, and some unto dishonor.

v21 If a man therefore purge himself from these, he shall be a vessel unto honor, sanctified, meet for the master's use, prepared unto every good work.

v22 after righteousness, faith, love, pace, with them that call on the Lord out of a pure heart.

v23 But foolish and ignorant questionings refuse, knowing that they gender strifes.

v24 And the Lord's servant must not strive, but be gentle towards all, apt to teach, forbearing,

v25 in meekness correcting them that oppose themselves; if peradventure God may give them repentance unto the knowledge of the truth,

v26 and they may recover themselves out of the snare of the devil, having been taken captive by him unto his will.
