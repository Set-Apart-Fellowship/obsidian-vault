# Ephesians 5 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 4|← Ephesians 4]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 6|Ephesians 6 →]]

***

v1 Be ye therefore imitators of God, as beloved children;

v2 and walk in love, even as Christ also loved you, and gave himself up for us, an offering and a sacrifice to God for an odor of a sweet smell.

v3 But fornication, and all uncleanness, or covetousness, let it not even be named among you, as becometh saints;

v4 nor filthiness, nor foolish talking, or jesting, which are not befitting: but rather giving of thanks.

v5 For this ye know of a surety, that no fornicator, nor unclean person, nor covetous man, who is an idolater, hath any inheritance in the kingdom of Christ and God.

v6 Let no man deceive you with empty words: for because of these things cometh the wrath of God upon the sons of disobedience.

v7 Be not ye therefore partakers with them;

v8 For ye were once darkness, but are now light in the Lord: walk as children of light

v9 (for the fruit of the light is in all goodness and righteousness and truth),

v10 proving what is well-pleasing unto the Lord;

v11 and have no fellowship with the unfruitful works of darkness, but rather even reprove them;

v12 for the things which are done by them in secret it is a shame even to speak of.

v13 But all things when they are reproved are made manifest by the light: for everything that is made manifest is light.

v14 Wherefore [he] saith, Awake, thou that sleepest, and arise from the dead, and Christ shall shine upon thee.

v15 Look therefore carefully how ye walk, not as unwise, but as wise;

v16 redeeming the time, because the days are evil.

v17 Wherefore be ye not foolish, but understand what the will of the Lord is.

v18 And be not drunken with wine, wherein is riot, but be filled with the Spirit;

v19 speaking one to another in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing and making melody with your heart to the Lord;

v20 giving thanks always for all things in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ to God, even the Father;

v21 subjecting yourselves one to another in the fear of Christ.

v22 Wives, [be in subjection] unto your own husbands, as unto the Lord.

v23 For the husband is the head of the wife, and Christ also is the head of the church, [being] himself the saviour of the body.

v24 But as the church is subject to Christ, so [let] the wives also [be] to their husbands in everything.

v25 Husbands, love your wives, even as Christ also loved the church, and gave himself up for it;

v26 that he might sanctify it, having cleansed it by the washing of water with the word,

v27 that he might present the church to himself a glorious [church], not having spot or wrinkle or any such thing; but that it should be holy and without blemish.

v28 Even so ought husbands also to love their own wives as their own bodies. He that loveth his own wife loveth himself:

v29 for no man ever hated his own flesh; but nourisheth and cherisheth it, even as Christ also the church;

v30 because we are members of his body.

v31 For this cause shall a man leave his father and mother, and shall cleave to his wife; and the two shall become one flesh.

v32 This mystery is great: but I speak in regard of Christ and of the church.

v33 Nevertheless do ye also severally love each one his own wife even as himself; and [let] the wife [see] that she fear her husband.
