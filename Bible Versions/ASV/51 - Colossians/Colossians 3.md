# Colossians 3 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2|← Colossians 2]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/51 - Colossians/Colossians]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/51 - Colossians/Colossians 4|Colossians 4 →]]

***

v1 If then ye were raised together with Christ, seek the things that are above, where Christ is, seated on the right hand of God.

v2 Set your mind on the things that are above, not on the things that are upon the earth.

v3 For ye died, and your life is hid with Christ in God.

v4 When Christ, [who is] our life, shall be manifested, then shall ye also with him be manifested in glory.

v5 Put to death therefore your members which are upon the earth: fornication, uncleanness, passion, evil desire, and covetousness, which is idolatry;

v6 for which things' sake cometh the wrath of God upon the sons of disobedience:

v7 wherein ye also once walked, when ye lived in these things;

v8 but now do ye also put them all away: anger, wrath, malice, railing, shameful speaking out of your mouth:

v9 lie not one to another; seeing that ye have put off the old man with his doings,

v10 and have put on the new man, that is being renewed unto knowledge after the image of him that created him:

v11 where there cannot be Greek and Jew, circumcision and uncircumcision, barbarian, Scythian, bondman, freeman; but Christ is all, and in all.

v12 Put on therefore, as God's elect, holy and beloved, a heart of compassion, kindness, lowliness, meekness, longsuffering;

v13 forbearing one another, and forgiving each other, if any man have a complaint against any; even as the Lord forgave you, so also do ye:

v14 and above all these things [put on] love, which is the bond of perfectness.

v15 And let the peace of Christ rule in your hearts, to the which also ye were called in one body; and be ye thankful.

v16 Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly; in all wisdom teaching and admonishing one another with psalms [and] hymns [and] spiritual songs, singing with grace in your hearts unto God.

v17 And whatsoever ye do, in word or in deed, [do] all in the name of the Lord Jesus, giving thanks to God the Father through him.

v18 Wives, be in subjection to your husbands, as is fitting in the Lord.

v19 Husbands, love your wives, and be not bitter against them.

v20 Children, obey your parents in all things, for this is well-pleasing in the Lord.

v21 Fathers, provoke not your children, that they be not discouraged.

v22 Servants, obey in all things them that are your masters according to the flesh; not with eye-service, as men-pleasers, but in singleness of heart, fearing the Lord:

v23 whatsoever ye do, work heartily, as unto the Lord, and not unto men;

v24 knowing that from the Lord ye shall receive the recompense of the inheritance: ye serve the Lord Christ.

v25 For he that doeth wrong shall receive again for the wrong that he hath done: and there is no respect of persons.
