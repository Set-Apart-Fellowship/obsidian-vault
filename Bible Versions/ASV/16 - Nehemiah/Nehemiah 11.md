# Nehemiah 11 (American Standard Version)

[[Bible Versions/ASV/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 10|← Nehemiah 10]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah]] | [[Bible Versions/ASV/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 12|Nehemiah 12 →]]

***

v1 And the princes of the people dwelt in Jerusalem: the rest of the people also cast lots, to bring one of ten to dwell in Jerusalem the holy city, and nine parts in the [other] cities.

v2 And the people blessed all the men that willingly offered themselves to dwell in Jerusalem.

v3 Now these are the chiefs of the province that dwelt in Jerusalem: but in the cities of Judah dwelt every one in his possession in their cities, [to wit], Israel, the priests, and the Levites, and the Nethinim, and the children of Solomon's servants.

v4 And in Jerusalem dwelt certain of the children of Judah, and of the children of Benjamin. Of the children of Judah: Athaiah the son of Uzziah, the son of Zechariah, the son of Amariah, the son of Shephatiah, the son of Mahalalel, of the children of Perez;

v5 and Maaseiah the son of Baruch, the son of Colhozeh, the son of Hazaiah, the son of Adaiah, the son of Joiarib, the son of Zechariah, the son of the Shilonite.

v6 All the sons of Perez that dwelt in Jerusalem were four hundred threescore and eight valiant men.

v7 And these are the sons of Benjamin: Sallu the son of Meshullam, the son of Joed, the son of Pedaiah, the son of Kolaiah, the son of Maaseiah, the son of Ithiel, the son of Jeshaiah.

v8 And after him Gabbai, Sallai, nine hundred twenty and eight.

v9 And Joel the son of Zichri was their overseer; and Judah the son of Hassenuah was second over the city.

v10 Of the priests: Jedaiah the son of Joiarib, Jachin,

v11 Seraiah the son of Hilkiah, the son of Meshullam, the son of Zadok, the son of Meraioth, the son of Ahitub, the ruler of the house of God,

v12 and their brethren that did the work of the house, eight hundred twenty and two; and Adaiah the son of Jeroham, the son of Pelaliah, the son of Amzi, the son of Zechariah, the son of Pashhur, the son of Malchijah,

v13 and his brethren, chiefs of fathers' [houses], two hundred forty and two; and Amashsai the son of Azarel, the son of Ahzai, the son of Meshillemoth, the son of Immer,

v14 and their brethren, mighty men of valor, a hundred twenty and eight; and their overseer was Zabdiel, the son of Haggedolim.

v15 And of the Levites: Shemaiah the son of Hasshub, the son of Azrikam, the son of Hashabiah, the son of Bunni;

v16 and Shabbethai and Jozabad, of the chiefs of the Levites, who had the oversight of the outward business of the house of God;

v17 and Mattaniah the son of Mica, the son of Zabdi, the son of Asaph, who was the chief to begin the thanksgiving in prayer, and Bakbukiah, the second among his brethren; and Abda the son of Shammua, the son of Galal, the son of Jeduthun.

v18 All the Levites in the holy city were two hundred fourscore and four.

v19 Moreover the porters, Akkub, Talmon, and their brethren, that kept watch at the gates, were a hundred seventy and two.

v20 And the residue of Israel, of the priests, the Levites, were in all the cities of Judah, every one in his inheritance.

v21 But the Nethinim dwelt in Ophel: and Ziha and Gishpa were over the Nethinim.

v22 The overseer also of the Levites at Jerusalem was Uzzi the son of Bani, the son of Hashabiah, the son of Mattaniah, the son of Mica, of the sons of Asaph, the singers, over the business of the house of God.

v23 For there was a commandment from the king concerning them, and a settled provision for the singers, as every day required.

v24 And Pethahiah the son of Meshezabel, of the children of Zerah the son of Judah, was at the king's hand in all matters concerning the people.

v25 And as for the villages, with their fields, some of the children of Judah dwelt in Kiriath-arba and the towns thereof, and in Dibon and the towns thereof, and in Jekabzeel and the villages thereof,

v26 and in Jeshua, and in Moladah, and Beth-pelet,

v27 and in Hazar-shual, and in Beer-sheba and the towns thereof,

v28 and in Ziklag, and in Meconah and in the towns thereof,

v29 and in En-rimmon, and in Zorah, and in Jarmuth,

v30 Zanoah, Adullam, and their villages, Lachish and the fields thereof, Azekah and the towns thereof. So they encamped from Beer-sheba unto the valley of Hinnom.

v31 The children of Benjamin also [dwelt] from Geba [onward], at Michmash and Aija, and at Beth-el and the towns thereof,

v32 at Anathoth, Nob, Ananiah,

v33 Hazor, Ramah, Gittaim,

v34 Hadid, Zeboim, Neballat,

v35 Lod, and Ono, the valley of craftsmen.

v36 And of the Levites, certain courses in Judah [were joined] to Benjamin.
