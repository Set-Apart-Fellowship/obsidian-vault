# John 7 (Weymouth's New Testament)

[[Bible Versions/WEYMOUTH/43 - John/John 6|← John 6]] | [[Bible Versions/WEYMOUTH/43 - John/John]] | [[Bible Versions/WEYMOUTH/43 - John/John 8|John 8 →]]

v1 After this Jesus moved from place to place in Galilee. He would not go about in Judaea, because the Jews were seeking an opportunity to kill Him.

v2 But the Jewish Festival of the Tent-Pitching was approaching.

v3 So His brothers said to Him, "Leave these parts and go into Judaea, that not only we but your disciples also may witness the miracles which you perform.

v4 For no one acts in secret, desiring all the while to be himself known publicly. Since you are doing these things, show yourself openly to the world."

v5 For even His brothers were not believers in Him.

v6 "My time," replied Jesus, "has not yet come, but for you any time is suitable.

v7 It is impossible for the world to hate you; but me it does hate, because I give testimony concerning it that its conduct is evil.

v8 As for you, go up to the Festival. I do not now go up to this Festival, because my time is not yet fully come."

v9 Such was His answer, and He remained in Galilee.

v10 When however His brothers had gone up to the Festival, then He also went up, not openly, but as it were privately.

v11 Meanwhile the Jews at the Festival were looking for Him and were inquiring, "Where is he?"

v12 Among the mass of the people there was much muttered debate about Him. Some said, "He is a good man." Others said, "Not so: he is imposing on the people."

v13 Yet for fear of the Jews no one spoke out boldly about Him.

v14 But when the Festival was already half over, Jesus went up to the Temple and commenced teaching.

v15 The Jews were astonished. "How does this man know anything of books," they said, "although he has never been at any of the schools?"

v16 Jesus answered their question by saying, "My teaching does not belong to me, but comes from Him who sent me.

v17 If any one is willing to do His will, he shall know about the teaching, whether it is from God or originates with me.

v18 The man whose teaching originates with himself aims at his own glory. He who aims at the glory of Him who sent him teaches the truth, and there is no deception in him.

v19 Did not Moses give you the Law? And yet not a man of you obeys the Law. Why do you want to kill me?"

v20 "You are possessed by a demon," replied the crowd; "no one wants to kill you."

v21 "One deed I have done," replied Jesus, "and you are all full of wonder.

v22 Consider therefore. Moses gave you the rite of circumcision (not that it began with Moses, but with your earlier forefathers), and even on a Sabbath day you circumcise a child.

v23 If a child is circumcised even on a Sabbath day, are you bitter against me because I have restored a man to perfect health on a Sabbath day?

v24 Do not form superficial judgements, but form the judgements that are just."

v25 Some however of the people of Jerusalem said, "Is not this the man they are wanting to kill?

v26 But here he is, speaking openly and boldly, and they say nothing to him! Can the Rulers really have ascertained that this man is the Christ?

v27 And yet we know this man, and we know where he is from; but as for the Christ, when He comes, no one can tell where He is from."

v28 Jesus therefore, while teaching in the Temple, cried aloud, and said, "Yes, you know me, and you know where I am from. And yet I have not come of my own accord; but there is One who has sent me, an Authority indeed, of whom you have no knowledge.

v29 I know Him, because I came from Him, and He sent me."

v30 On hearing this they wanted to arrest Him; yet not a hand was laid on Him, because His time had not yet come.

v31 But from among the crowd a large number believed in Him. "When the Christ comes," they said, "will He perform more miracles than this teacher has performed?"

v32 The Pharisees heard the people thus expressing their various doubts about Him, and the High Priests and the Pharisees sent some officers to apprehend Him.

v33 So Jesus said, "Still for a short time I am with you, and then I go my way to Him who sent me.

v34 You will look for me and will not find me, and where I am you cannot come."

v35 The Jews therefore said to one another, "Where is he about to betake himself, so that we shall not find him? Will he betake himself to the Dispersion among the Gentiles, and teach the Gentiles?

v36 What do those words of his mean, 'You will look for me, but will not find me, and where I am you cannot come'?"

v37 On the last day of the Festival--the great day--Jesus stood up and cried aloud. "Whoever is thirsty," He said, "let him come to me and drink.

v38 He who believes in me, from within him--as the Scripture has said--rivers of living water shall flow."

v39 He referred to the Spirit which those who believed in Him were to receive; for the Spirit was not bestowed as yet, because Jesus had not yet been glorified.

v40 After listening to these discourses, some of the crowd began to say, "This is beyond doubt the Prophet."

v41 Others said, "He is the Christ." But others again, "Not so, for is the Christ to come from Galilee?

v42 Has not the Scripture declared that the Christ is to come of the family of David and from Bethlehem, David's village?"

v43 So there was a violent dissension among the people on His account.

v44 Some of them wanted at once to arrest Him, but no one laid hands upon Him.

v45 Meanwhile the officers returned to the High Priests and Pharisees, who asked them, "Why have you not brought him?"

v46 "No mere man has ever spoken as this man speaks," said the officers.

v47 "Are *you* deluded too?" replied the Pharisees;

v48 "has any one of the Rulers or of the Pharisees believed in him?

v49 But this rabble who understand nothing about the Law are accursed!"

v50 Nicodemus interposed--he who had formerly gone to Jesus, being himself one of them.

v51 "Does our Law," he asked, "judge a man without first hearing what he has to say and ascertaining what his conduct is?"

v52 "Do you also come from Galilee?" they asked in reply. "Search and see for yourself that no Prophet is of Galilaean origin."
