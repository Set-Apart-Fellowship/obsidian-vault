# John 10 (Weymouth's New Testament)

[[Bible Versions/WEYMOUTH/43 - John/John 9|← John 9]] | [[Bible Versions/WEYMOUTH/43 - John/John]] | [[Bible Versions/WEYMOUTH/43 - John/John 11|John 11 →]]

***

v1 "In most solemn truth I tell you that the man who does not enter the sheepfold by the door, but climbs over some other way, is a thief and a robber.

v2 But he who enters by the door is the shepherd of the sheep.

v3 To him the porter opens the door, and the sheep hear his voice; and he calls his own sheep by their names and leads them out.

v4 When he has brought out his own sheep--all of them--he walks at the head of them; and the sheep follow him, because they know his voice.

v5 But a stranger they will by no means follow, but will run away from him, because they do not know the voice of strangers."

v6 Jesus spoke to them in this figurative language, but they did not understand what He meant.

v7 Again therefore Jesus said to them, "In most solemn truth I tell you that I am the Door of the sheep.

v8 All who have come before me are thieves and robbers; but the sheep would not listen to them.

v9 I am the Door. If any one enters by me, he will find safety, and will go in and out and find pasture.

v10 The thief comes only to steal and kill and destroy: I have come that they may have Life, and may have it in abundance.

v11 "I am the Good Shepherd. A good shepherd lays down his very life for the sheep.

v12 The hired servant--one who is not a shepherd and does not own the sheep--no sooner sees the wolf coming than he leaves the sheep and runs away; and the wolf worries and scatters them.

v13 For he is only a hired servant and cares nothing for the sheep.

v14 "I am the Good Shepherd. And I know my sheep and my sheep know me,

v15 just as the Father knows me and I know the Father; and I am laying down my life for the sheep.

v16 I have also other sheep--which do not belong to this fold. Those also I must bring, and they will listen to my voice; and they shall become one flock under one Shepherd.

v17 For this reason my Father loves me, because I am laying down my life in order to receive it back again.

v18 No one is taking it away from me, but I myself am laying it down. I am authorized to lay it down, and I am authorized to receive it back again. This is the command I received from my Father."

v19 Again there arose a division among the Jews because of these words.

v20 Many of them said, "He is possessed by a demon and is mad. Why do you listen to him?"

v21 Others argued, "That is not the language of a demoniac: and can a demon open blind men's eyes?"

v22 The Dedication Festival came on in Jerusalem. It was winter,

v23 and Jesus was walking in the Temple in Solomon's Portico,

v24 when the Jews gathered round Him and kept asking Him, "How long do you mean to keep us in suspense? If you are the Christ, tell us so plainly."

v25 "I have told you," answered Jesus, "and you do not believe. The deeds that I do in my Father's name--they give testimony about me.

v26 But you do not believe, because you are not my sheep.

v27 My sheep listen to my voice, and I know them, and they follow me.

v28 I give them the Life of the Ages, and they shall never, never perish, nor shall any one wrest them from my hand.

v29 What my Father has given me is more precious than all besides; and no one is able to wrest anything from my Father's hand.

v30 I and the Father are one."

v31 Again the Jews brought stones with which to stone Him.

v32 Jesus remonstrated with them. "Many good deeds," He said, "have I shown you as coming from the Father; for which of them are you going to stone me?"

v33 "For no good deed," the Jews replied, "are we going to stone you, but for blasphemy, and because you, who are only a man, are making yourself out to be God."

v34 "Does it not stand written in your Law," replied Jesus, <"'I said, you are gods'?>

v35 If those to whom God's word was addressed are called gods (and the Scripture cannot be annulled),

v36 how is it that you say to one whom the Father consecrated and sent into the world, 'You are blaspheming,' because I said, 'I am God's Son'?

v37 If the deeds I do are not my Father's deeds, do not believe me.

v38 But if they are, then even if you do not believe me, at least believe the deeds, that you may know and see clearly that the Father is in me, and that I am in the Father."

v39 This made them once more try to arrest Him, but He withdrew out of their power.

v40 Then He went away again to the other side of the Jordan, to the place where John had been baptizing at first; and there He stayed.

v41 Large numbers of people also came to Him. Their report was, "John did not work any miracle, but all that John said about this Teacher was true."

v42 And many became believers in Him there.
