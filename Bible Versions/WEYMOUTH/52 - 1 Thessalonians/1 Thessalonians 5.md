# 1 Thessalonians 5 (Weymouth's New Testament)

[[Bible Versions/WEYMOUTH/52 - 1 Thessalonians/1 Thessalonians 4|← 1 Thessalonians 4]] | [[Bible Versions/WEYMOUTH/52 - 1 Thessalonians/1 Thessalonians]]

***

v1 But as to times and dates it is unnecessary that anything be written to you.

v2 For you yourselves know perfectly well that the day of the Lord comes like a thief in the night.

v3 While they are saying "Peace and safety!" then in a moment destruction falls upon them, like birth-pains on a woman who is with child; and escape there is none.

v4 But you, brethren, are not in darkness, that daylight should surprise you like a thief;

v5 for all of you are sons of Light and sons of the day. We belong neither to the night nor to darkness.

v6 So then let us not sleep, like the rest of the world, but let us keep awake and be sober.

v7 For those who sleep, sleep at night, and those who drink freely, drink at night.

v8 But let us, belonging--as we do--to the day, be sober, putting on the breastplate of faith and love, and for a helmet the hope of salvation.

v9 For God has not pre-destined us to meet His anger, but to obtain salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ;

v10 who died on our behalf, so that whether we are awake or are sleeping we may share His Life.

v11 Therefore encourage one another, and let each one help to strengthen his friend, as in fact you do.

v12 Now we beg you, brethren, to show respect for those who labour among you and are your leaders in Christian work, and are your advisers;

v13 and to esteem them very highly in love for their work's sake. Be at peace among yourselves.

v14 And we exhort you, brethren, admonish the unruly, comfort the timid, sustain the weak, and be patient towards all.

v15 See to it that no one ever repays another with evil for evil; but always seek opportunities of doing good both to one another and to all the world.

v16 Be always joyful.

v17 Be unceasing in prayer.

v18 In every circumstance of life be thankful; for this is God's will in Christ Jesus respecting you.

v19 Do not quench the Spirit.

v20 Do not think meanly of utterances of prophecy;

v21 but test all such, and retain hold of the good.

v22 Hold yourselves aloof from every form of evil.

v23 And may God Himself who gives peace, make you entirely holy; and may your spirits, souls and bodies be preserved complete and be found blameless at the Coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.

v24 Faithful is He who calls you, and He will also perfect His work.

v25 Brethren, pray for us.

v26 Greet all the brethren with a holy kiss.

v27 I solemnly charge you in the Lord's name to have this Letter read to all the brethren.

v28 May the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you.
