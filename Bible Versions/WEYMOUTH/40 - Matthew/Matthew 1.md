# Matthew 1 (Weymouth's New Testament)

[[Bible Versions/WEYMOUTH/40 - Matthew/Matthew]] | [[Bible Versions/WEYMOUTH/40 - Matthew/Matthew 2|Matthew 2 →]]

***

v1 The Genealogy of Jesus Christ, the son of David, the son of Abraham.

v2 Abraham was the father of Isaac; Isaac of Jacob; Jacob of Judah and his brothers.

v3 Judah was the father (by Tamar) of Perez and Zerah; Perez of Hezron; Hezron of Ram;

v4 Ram of Amminadab; Amminadab of Nahshon; Nahshon of Salmon;

v5 Salmon (by Rahab) of Boaz; Boaz (by Ruth) of Obed; Obed of Jesse;

v6 Jesse of David--the King. David (by Uriah's widow) was the father of Solomon;

v7 Solomon of Rehoboam; Rehoboam of Abijah; Abijah of Asa;

v8 Asa of Jehoshaphat; Jehoshaphat of Jehoram; Jehoram of Uzziah;

v9 Uzziah of Jotham; Jotham of Ahaz; Ahaz of Hezekiah;

v10 Hezekiah of Manasseh; Manasseh of Amon; Amon of Josiah;

v11 Josiah of Jeconiah and his brothers at the period of the Removal to Babylon.

v12 After the Removal to Babylon Jeconiah had a son Shealtiel; Shealtiel was the father of Zerubbabel;

v13 Zerubbabel of Abiud; Abiud of Eliakim; Eliakim of Azor;

v14 Azor of Zadok; Zadok of Achim; Achim of Eliud;

v15 Eliud of Eleazar; Eleazar of Matthan; Matthan of Jacob;

v16 and Jacob of Joseph the husband of Mary, who was the mother of JESUS who is called CHRIST.

v17 There are therefore, in all, fourteen generations from Abraham to David; fourteen from David to the Removal to Babylon; and fourteen from the Removal to Babylon to the Christ.

v18 The circumstances of the birth of Jesus Christ were these. After his mother Mary was betrothed to Joseph, before they were united in marriage, she was found to be with child through the Holy Spirit.

v19 But Joseph her husband, being a kind-hearted man and unwilling publicly to disgrace her, had determined to release her privately from the betrothal.

v20 But while he was contemplating this step, an angel of the Lord appeared to him in a dream and said, "Joseph, son of David, do not be afraid to bring home your wife Mary, for she is with child through the Holy Spirit.

v21 She will give birth to a Son, and you are to call His name JESUS for He it is who will save His People from their sins."

v22 All this took place in fulfilment of what the Lord had spoken through the Prophet,

v23 <"Mark! The maiden will be with child and will give birth to a son, and they will call His name Immanuel"> --a word which signifies <'God with us'>.

v24 When Joseph awoke, he did as the angel of the Lord had commanded, and brought home his wife,

v25 but did not live with her until she had given birth to a son. The child's name he called JESUS.
