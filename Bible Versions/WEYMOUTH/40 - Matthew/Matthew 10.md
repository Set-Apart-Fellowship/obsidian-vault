# Matthew 10 (Weymouth's New Testament)

[[Bible Versions/WEYMOUTH/40 - Matthew/Matthew 9|← Matthew 9]] | [[Bible Versions/WEYMOUTH/40 - Matthew/Matthew]] | [[Bible Versions/WEYMOUTH/40 - Matthew/Matthew 11|Matthew 11 →]]

***

v1 Then He called to Him His twelve disciples and gave them authority over foul spirits, to drive them out; and to cure every kind of disease and infirmity.

v2 Now the names of the twelve Apostles were these: first, Simon called Peter, and his brother Andrew; James the son of Zabdi, and his brother John;

v3 Philip and Bartholomew, Thomas and Matthew the tax-gatherer, James the son of Alphaeus, and Thaddaeus;

v4 Simon the Cananaean, and Judas the Iscariot, who also betrayed Him.

v5 These twelve Jesus sent on a mission, after giving them their instructions: "Go not," He said, "among the Gentiles, and enter no Samaritan town;

v6 but, instead of that, go to the lost sheep of Israel's race.

v7 And as you go, preach and say, 'The Kingdom of the Heavens is close at hand.'

v8 Cure the sick, raise the dead to life, cleanse lepers, drive out demons: you have received without payment, give without payment.

v9 "Provide no gold, nor even silver nor copper to carry in your pockets;

v10 no bag for your journey, nor change of linen, nor shoes, nor stick; for the labourer deserves his food.

v11 "Whatever town or village you enter, inquire for some good man; and make his house your home till you leave the place.

v12 When you enter the house, salute it;

v13 and if the house deserves it, the peace you invoke shall come upon it. If not, your peace shall return to you.

v14 And whoever refuses to receive you or even to listen to your Message, as you leave that house or town, shake off the very dust from your feet.

v15 I solemnly tell you that it will be more endurable for the land of Sodom and Gomorrah on the day of Judgement than for that town.

v16 "Remember it is I who am sending you out, as sheep into the midst of wolves; prove yourselves as sagacious as serpents, and as innocent as doves.

v17 But beware of men; for they will deliver you up to appear before Sanhedrins, and will flog you in their synagogues;

v18 and you will even be put on trial before governors and kings for my sake, to bear witness to them and to the Gentiles.

v19 But when they have delivered you up, have no anxiety as to how you shall speak or what you shall say; for at that very time it shall be given you what to say;

v20 for it is not you who will speak: it will be the Spirit of your Father speaking through you.

v21 Brother will betray brother to death, and father, child; and children will rise against their own parents and will put them to death.

v22 And you will be objects of universal hatred because you are called by my name; but he who holds out to the End--he will be saved.

v23 Whenever they persecute you in one town, escape to the next; for I solemnly tell you that you will not have gone the round of all the towns of Israel before the Son of Man comes.

v24 "The learner is never superior to his teacher, and the servant is never superior to his master.

v25 Enough for the learner to be on a level with his teacher, and for the servant to be on a level with his master. If they have called the master of the house Baal-zebul, how much more will they slander his servants?

v26 Fear them not, however; there is nothing veiled which will not be uncovered, nor secret which will not become known.

v27 What I tell you in the dark, speak in the light; and what is whispered into your ear, proclaim upon the roofs of the houses.

v28 "And do not fear those who kill the body, but cannot kill the soul; but rather fear him who is able to destroy both soul and body in Gehenna.

v29 Do not two sparrows sell for a halfpenny? Yet not one of them will fall to the ground without your Father's leave.

v30 But as for you, the very hairs on your heads are all numbered.

v31 Away then with fear; you are more precious than a multitude of sparrows.

v32 "Every man who acknowledges me before men I also will acknowledge before my Father who is in Heaven.

v33 But whoever disowns me before men I also will disown before my Father who is in Heaven.

v34 "Do not suppose that I came to bring peace to the earth: I did not come to bring peace but a sword.

v35 For I came to set a man against his father, <a daughter against her mother, and a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law;

v36 and a man's own family will be his foes.>

v37 Any one who loves father or mother more than me is not worthy of me, and any one who loves son or daughter more than me is not worthy of me;

v38 and any one who does not take up his cross and follow where I lead is not worthy of me.

v39 To save your life is to lose it, and to lose your life for my sake is to save it.

v40 "Whoever receives you receives me, and whoever receives me receives Him who sent me.

v41 Every one who receives a prophet, because he is a prophet, will receive a prophet's reward, and every one who receives a righteous man, because he is a righteous man, will receive a righteous man's reward.

v42 And whoever gives one of these little ones even a cup of cold water to drink because he is a disciple, I solemnly tell you that he will not lose his reward."
