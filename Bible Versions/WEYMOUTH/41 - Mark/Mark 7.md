# Mark 7 (Weymouth's New Testament)

[[Bible Versions/WEYMOUTH/41 - Mark/Mark 6|← Mark 6]] | [[Bible Versions/WEYMOUTH/41 - Mark/Mark]] | [[Bible Versions/WEYMOUTH/41 - Mark/Mark 8|Mark 8 →]]

***

v1 Then the Pharisees, with certain Scribes who had come from Jerusalem, came to Him in a body.

v2 They had noticed that some of His disciples were eating their food with 'unclean' (that is to say, unwashed) hands.

v3 (For the Pharisees and all the Jews--being, as they are, zealous for the traditions of the Elders--never eat without first carefully washing their hands,

v4 and when they come from market they will not eat without bathing first; and they have a good many other customs which they have received traditionally and cling to, such as the rinsing of cups and pots and of bronze utensils, and the washing of beds.)

v5 So the Pharisees and Scribes put the question to Him: "Why do your disciples transgress the traditions of the Elders, and eat their food with unclean hands?"

v6 "Rightly did Isaiah prophesy of you hypocrites," He replied; "as it is written, <"'This People honour Me with their lips, while their hearts are far away from Me:

v7 But idle is their devotion while they lay down precepts which are mere human rules.'>

v8 "You neglect God's Commandment: you hold fast to men's traditions."

v9 "Praiseworthy indeed!" He added, "to set at nought God's Commandment in order to observe your own traditions!

v10 For Moses said, <'Honour thy father and thy mother'> and again, <'He who curses father or mother, let him die the death.'>

v11 But *you* say, 'If a man says to his father or mother, It is a Korban (that is, a thing devoted to God), whatever it is, which otherwise you would have received from me--'

v12 And so you no longer allow him to do anything for his father or mother,

v13 thus nullifying God's precept by your tradition which you have handed down. And many things of that kind you do."

v14 Then Jesus called the people to Him again. "Listen to me, all of you," He said, "and understand.

v15 There is nothing outside a man which entering him can make him unclean; but it is the things which come out of a man that make him unclean."

v16 []

v17 After He had left the crowd and gone indoors, His disciples began to ask Him about this figure of speech.

v18 "Have *you* also so little understanding?" He replied; "do you not understand that anything whatever that enters a man from outside cannot make him unclean,

v19 because it does not go into his heart, but into his stomach, and passes away ejected from him?" By these words Jesus pronounced all kinds of food clean.

v20 "What comes out of a man," He added, "that it is which makes him unclean.

v21 For from within, out of men's hearts, their evil purposes proceed--fornication, theft, murder, adultery,

v22 covetousness, wickedness, deceit, licentiousness, envy, reviling, pride, reckless folly:

v23 all these wicked things come out from within and make a man unclean."

v24 Then He rose and left that place and went into the neighbourhood of Tyre and Sidon. Here He entered a house and wished no one to know it, but He could not escape observation.

v25 Forthwith a woman whose little daughter was possessed by a foul spirit heard of Him, and came and flung herself at His feet.

v26 She was a Gentile woman, a Syro-phoenician by nation: and again and again she begged Him to expel the demon from her daughter.

v27 "Let the children first eat all they want," He said; "it is not right to take the children's bread and throw it to the dogs."

v28 "True, Sir," she replied, "and yet the dogs under the table eat the children's scraps."

v29 "For those words of yours, go home," He replied; "the demon has gone out of your daughter."

v30 So she went home, and found the child lying on the bed, and the demon gone.

v31 Returning from the neighbourhood of Tyre, He came by way of Sidon to the Lake of Galilee, passing through the district of the Ten Towns.

v32 Here they brought to Him a deaf man that stammered, on whom they begged Him to lay His hands.

v33 So Jesus taking him aside, apart from the crowd, put His fingers into his ears, and spat, and moistened his tongue;

v34 and looking up to Heaven He sighed, and said to him, "Ephphatha!" (that is, "Open!")

v35 And the man's ears were opened, and his tongue became untied, and he began to speak perfectly.

v36 Then Jesus charged them to tell no one; but the more He charged them, all the more did they spread the news far and wide.

v37 The amazement was extreme. "He succeeds in everything he attempts," they exclaimed; "he even makes deaf men hear and dumb men speak!"
