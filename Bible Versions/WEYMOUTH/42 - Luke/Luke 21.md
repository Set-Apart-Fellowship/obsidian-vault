# Luke 21 (Weymouth's New Testament)

[[Bible Versions/WEYMOUTH/42 - Luke/Luke 20|← Luke 20]] | [[Bible Versions/WEYMOUTH/42 - Luke/Luke]] | [[Bible Versions/WEYMOUTH/42 - Luke/Luke 22|Luke 22 →]]

***

v1 Looking up He saw the people throwing their gifts into the Treasury--the rich people.

v2 He also saw a poor widow dropping in two farthings,

v3 and He said, "In truth I tell you that this widow, so poor, has thrown in more than any of them.

v4 For from what they could well spare they have all of them contributed to the offerings, but she in her need has thrown in all she had to live on."

v5 When some were remarking about the Temple, how it was embellished with beautiful stones and dedicated gifts, He said,

v6 "As to these things which you now admire, the time is coming when there will not be one stone left here upon another which will not be pulled down."

v7 "Rabbi, when will this be?" they asked Him, "and what will be the token given when these things are about to take place?"

v8 "See to it," He replied, "that you are not misled; for many will come assuming my name and professing, 'I am He,' or saying, 'The time is close at hand.' Do not go and follow them.

v9 But when you hear of wars and turmoils, be not afraid; for these things must happen first, but the end does not come immediately."

v10 Then He said to them, <"Nation will rise in arms against nation, and kingdom against kingdom.>

v11 And there will be great earthquakes, and in places famines and pestilence; and there will be terrible sights and wonderful tokens from Heaven.

v12 "But before all these things happen they will lay hands on you and persecute you. They will deliver you up to synagogues and to prison, and you will be brought before kings and governors for my sake.

v13 In the end all this will be evidence of your fidelity.

v14 "Make up your minds, however, not to prepare a defence beforehand,

v15 for I will give you utterance and wisdom which none of your opponents will be able to withstand or reply to.

v16 You will be betrayed even by parents, brothers, relatives, friends; and some of you they will put to death.

v17 You will be the objects of universal hatred because you are called by my name;

v18 and yet not a hair of your heads shall perish.

v19 By your patient endurance you will purchase your lives.

v20 "But when you see Jerusalem with armies encamping round her on every side, then be certain that her overthrow is close at hand.

v21 Then let those who shall be in Judaea escape to the hills; let those who are in the city leave it, and those who are in the country not enter in.

v22 For those are the <days of vengeance> and of fulfilling all that is written.

v23 "Alas for the women who at that time are with child or who have infants; for there will be great distress in the land, and anger towards this People.

v24 They will fall by the sword, or be carried off into slavery among all the Gentiles. And Jerusalem will be trampled under foot by the Gentiles, till the appointed times of the Gentiles have expired.

v25 "There will be signs in sun, moon, and stars; and on earth anguish among the nations in their bewilderment at the roaring of the sea and its billows;

v26 while men's hearts are fainting for fear, and for anxious expectation of what is coming on the world. For <the forces which control the heavens will be disordered and disturbed.>

v27 And then will they see the <Son of Man coming in a cloud> with great power and glory.

v28 But when all this is beginning to take place, grieve no longer. Lift up your heads, because your deliverance is drawing near."

v29 And He spoke a parable to them. "See," He said, "the fig-tree and all the trees.

v30 As soon as they have shot out their leaves, you know at a glance that summer is now near.

v31 So also, when you see these things happening, you may be sure that the Kingdom of God is near.

v32 I tell you in solemn truth that the present generation will certainly not pass away without all these things having first taken place.

v33 Earth and sky will pass away, but it is certain that my words will not pass away.

v34 "But take heed to yourselves, lest your souls be weighed down with self-indulgence and drunkenness or the anxieties of this life, and that day come upon you, suddenly, like a falling trap;

v35 for it will come on all dwellers on the face of the whole earth.

v36 But beware of slumbering; and every moment pray that you may be fully strengthened to escape from all these coming evils, and to take your stand in the presence of the Son of Man."

v37 His habit at this time was to teach in the Temple by day, but to go out and spend the night on the Mount called the Oliveyard.

v38 And all the people came to Him in the Temple, early in the morning, to listen to Him.
