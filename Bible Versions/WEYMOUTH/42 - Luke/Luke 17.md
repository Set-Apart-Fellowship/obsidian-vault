# Luke 17 (Weymouth's New Testament)

[[Bible Versions/WEYMOUTH/42 - Luke/Luke 16|← Luke 16]] | [[Bible Versions/WEYMOUTH/42 - Luke/Luke]] | [[Bible Versions/WEYMOUTH/42 - Luke/Luke 18|Luke 18 →]]

***

v1 Jesus said to His disciples, "It is inevitable that causes of stumbling should come; but alas for him through whom they come!

v2 It would be well for him if, with a millstone round his neck, he were lying at the bottom of the sea, rather than that he should cause even one of these little ones to fall.

v3 Be on your guard. "If your brother acts wrongly, reprove him; and if he is sorry, forgive him;

v4 and if seven times in a day he acts wrongly towards you, and seven times turns again to you and says, 'I am sorry,' you must forgive him."

v5 And the Apostles said to the Lord, "Give us faith."

v6 "If your faith," replied the Lord, "is like a mustard seed, you might command this black-mulberry-tree, 'Tear up your roots and plant yourself in the sea,' and instantly it would obey you.

v7 But which of you who has a servant ploughing, or tending sheep, will say to him when he comes in from the farm, 'Come at once and take your place at table,'

v8 and will not rather say to him, 'Get my dinner ready, make yourself tidy, and wait upon me till I have finished my dinner, and then you shall have yours'?

v9 Does he thank the servant for obeying his orders?

v10 So you also, when you have obeyed all the orders given you, must say, "'There is no merit in our service: what we have done is only what we were in duty bound to do.'"

v11 As they pursued their journey to Jerusalem, He passed through Samaria and Galilee.

v12 And as He entered a certain village, ten men met Him who were lepers and stood at a distance.

v13 In loud voices they cried out, "Jesus, Rabbi, take pity on us."

v14 Perceiving this, He said to them, "Go and show yourselves to the Priests." And while on their way to do this they were made clean.

v15 One of them, seeing that he was cured, came back, adoring and praising God in a loud voice,

v16 and he threw himself at the feet of Jesus, thanking Him. He was a Samaritan.

v17 "Were not all ten made clean?" Jesus asked; "but where are the nine?

v18 Have none been found to come back and give glory to God except this foreigner?"

v19 And He said to him, "Rise and go: your faith has cured you."

v20 Being asked by the Pharisees when the Kingdom of God was coming, He answered, "The Kingdom of God does not so come that you can stealthily watch for it.

v21 Nor will they say, 'See here!' or 'See there!' --for the Kingdom of God is within you."

v22 Then, turning to His disciples, He said, "There will come a time when you will wish you could see a single one of the days of the Son of Man, but will not see one.

v23 And they will say to you, 'See there!' 'See here!' Do not start off and go in pursuit.

v24 For just as the lightning, when it flashes, shines from one part of the horizon to the opposite part, so will the Son of Man be on His day.

v25 But first He must endure much suffering, and be rejected by the present generation.

v26 "And as it was in the time of Noah, so will it also be in the time of the Son of Man.

v27 Men were eating and drinking, taking wives and giving wives, up to the very day on which Noah entered the Ark, and the Deluge came and destroyed them all.

v28 The same was true in the time of Lot: they were eating and drinking, buying and selling, planting and building;

v29 but on the day that Lot left Sodom, God rained fire and brimstone from the sky and destroyed them all.

v30 Exactly so will it be on the day that the veil is lifted from the Son of Man.

v31 "On that day, if a man is on the roof and his property indoors, let him not go down to fetch it; and, in the same way, he who is in the field, let him not turn back.

v32 Remember Lot's wife.

v33 Any man who makes it his object to keep his own life safe, will lose it; but whoever loses his life will preserve it.

v34 On that night, I tell you, there will be two men in one bed: one will be taken away and the other left behind.

v35 There will be two women turning the mill together: one will be taken away and the other left behind."

v36 []

v37 "Where, Master?" they inquired. "Where the dead body is," He replied, "there also will the vultures flock together."
