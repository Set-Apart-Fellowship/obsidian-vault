# Job 30 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 29|← Job 29]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 31|Job 31 →]]

***

v1 But now they that are younger than I have me in derision, whose fathers I would have disdained to set with the dogs of my flock.

v2 Yea, whereto [should] the strength of their hands [profit] me, [men] in whom vigour hath perished?

v3 Withered up through want and hunger, they flee into waste places long since desolate and desert:

v4 They gather the salt-wort among the bushes, and the roots of the broom for their food.

v5 They are driven forth from among [men] -- they cry after them as after a thief --

v6 To dwell in gloomy gorges, in caves of the earth and the rocks:

v7 They bray among the bushes; under the brambles they are gathered together:

v8 Sons of fools, and sons of nameless sires, they are driven out of the land.

v9 And now I am their song, yea, I am their byword.

v10 They abhor me, they stand aloof from me, yea, they spare not to spit in my face.

v11 For he hath loosed my cord and afflicted me; so they cast off the bridle before me.

v12 At [my] right hand rise the young brood; they push away my feet, and raise up against me their pernicious ways;

v13 They mar my path, they set forward my calamity, without any to help them;

v14 They come in as through a wide breach: amid the confusion they roll themselves onward.

v15 Terrors are turned against me; they pursue mine honour as the wind; and my welfare is passed away like a cloud.

v16 And now my soul is poured out in me; days of affliction have taken hold upon me.

v17 The night pierceth through my bones [and detacheth them] from me, and my gnawing pains take no rest:

v18 By their great force they have become my raiment; they bind me about as the collar of my coat.

v19 He hath cast me into the mire, and I have become like dust and ashes.

v20 I cry unto thee, and thou answerest me not; I stand up, and thou lookest at me.

v21 Thou art changed to a cruel one to me; with the strength of thy hand thou pursuest me.

v22 Thou liftest me up to the wind; thou causest me to be borne away, and dissolvest my substance.

v23 For I know that thou wilt bring me to death, and into the house of assemblage for all living.

v24 Indeed, no prayer [availeth] when he stretcheth out [his] hand: though they cry when he destroyeth.

v25 Did not I weep for him whose days were hard? was not my soul grieved for the needy?

v26 For I expected good, and there came evil; and I waited for light, but there came darkness.

v27 My bowels well up, and rest not; days of affliction have confronted me.

v28 I go about blackened, but not by the sun; I stand up, I cry in the congregation.

v29 I am become a brother to jackals, and a companion of ostriches.

v30 My skin is become black [and falleth] off me, and my bones are parched with heat.

v31 My harp also is [turned] to mourning, and my pipe into the voice of weepers.
