# Job 34 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 33|← Job 33]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 35|Job 35 →]]

v1 Moreover Elihu answered and said,

v2 Hear my words, ye wise [men]; and give ear unto me, ye that have knowledge.

v3 For the ear trieth words, as the palate tasteth food.

v4 Let us choose for ourselves what is right; let us know among ourselves what is good!

v5 For Job hath said, I am righteous, and Ã¹God hath taken away my judgment:

v6 Should I lie against my right? My wound is incurable without transgression.

v7 What man is like Job? he drinketh up scorning like water,

v8 And goeth in company with workers of iniquity, and walketh with wicked men.

v9 For he hath said, It profiteth not a man if he delight himself in God.

v10 Therefore hearken unto me, ye men of understanding: Far be wickedness from Ã¹God, and wrong from the Almighty!

v11 For a man's work will he render to him, and cause every one to find according to [his] way.

v12 Yea, surely, Ã¹God acteth not wickedly, and the Almighty perverteth not judgment.

v13 Who hath entrusted to him the earth? and who hath disposed the whole world?

v14 If he only thought of himself, [and] gathered unto him his spirit and his breath,

v15 All flesh would expire together, and man would return to the dust.

v16 If now [thou hast] understanding, hear this: give ear to the voice of my words!

v17 Should he that hateth right indeed govern? and wilt thou condemn the All-just?

v18 Shall one say to a king, Belial? to nobles, Wicked?

v19 [How then to him] that accepteth not the persons of princes, nor regardeth the rich man more than the poor? for they are all the work of his hands.

v20 In a moment they die, even at midnight the people are convulsed and pass away; and the strong are taken away without hand.

v21 For his eyes are upon the ways of man, and he seeth all his steps.

v22 There is no darkness, nor shadow of death, where the workers of iniquity may hide themselves.

v23 For he doth not long consider a man, to bring him before Ã¹God in judgment.

v24 He breaketh in pieces mighty men without inquiry, and setteth others in their stead;

v25 Since he knoweth their actions; and he overthroweth [them] in the night, and they are crushed.

v26 He striketh them as wicked men in the open sight of others,

v27 Because they have turned back from him, and would consider none of his ways;

v28 So that they cause the cry of the poor to come unto him, and he heareth the cry of the afflicted.

v29 When he giveth quietness, who then will disturb? and when he hideth [his] face, who shall behold him? and this towards a nation, or towards a man alike;

v30 That the ungodly man reign not, that the people be not ensnared.

v31 For hath he said unto Ã¹God, I bear [chastisement], I will not offend;

v32 What I see not, teach thou me; if I have done wrong, I will do so no more?

v33 Shall he recompense according to thy mind? for thou hast refused [his judgment]; for thou so choosest, and not I; speak then what thou knowest.

v34 Men of understanding will say to me, and a wise man who heareth me:

v35 Job hath spoken without knowledge, and his words were not with intelligence.

v36 Would that Job may be tried unto the end, because of [his] answers after the manner of evil men!

v37 For he addeth rebellion unto his sin, he clappeth [his hands] among us, and multiplieth his words against Ã¹God.
