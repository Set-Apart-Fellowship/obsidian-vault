# Job 41 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 40|← Job 40]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 42|Job 42 →]]

***

v1 Wilt thou draw out the leviathan with the hook, and press down his tongue with a cord?

v2 Wilt thou put a rush-rope into his nose, and pierce his jaw with a spike?

v3 Will he make many supplications unto thee? or will he speak softly unto thee?

v4 Will he make a covenant with thee? wilt thou take him as a bondman for ever?

v5 Wilt thou play with him as with a bird, and wilt thou bind him for thy maidens?

v6 Shall partners make traffic of him, will they divide him among merchants?

v7 Wilt thou fill his skin with darts, and his head with fish-spears?

v8 Lay thy hand upon him; remember the battle, -- do no more!

v9 Lo, hope as to him is belied: is not one cast down even at the sight of him?

v10 None is so bold as to stir him up; and who is he that will stand before me?

v11 Who hath first given to me, that I should repay [him]? [Whatsoever is] under the whole heaven is mine.

v12 I will not be silent as to his parts, the story of his power, and the beauty of his structure.

v13 Who can uncover the surface of his garment? who can come within his double jaws?

v14 Who can open the doors of his face? Round about his teeth is terror.

v15 The rows of his shields are a pride, shut up together [as with] a close seal.

v16 One is so near to another that no air can come between them;

v17 They are joined each to its fellow; they stick together, and cannot be sundered.

v18 His sneezings flash light, and his eyes are like the eyelids of the morning.

v19 Out of his mouth go forth flames; sparks of fire leap out:

v20 Out of his nostrils goeth smoke, as out of a boiling pot and cauldron.

v21 His breath kindleth coals, and a flame goeth out of his mouth.

v22 In his neck lodgeth strength, and terror danceth before him.

v23 The flakes of his flesh are joined together: they are fused upon him, they cannot be moved.

v24 His heart is firm as a stone, yea, firm as the nether [millstone].

v25 When he raiseth himself up, the mighty are afraid: they are beside themselves with consternation.

v26 If any reach him with a sword, it cannot hold; neither spear, nor dart, nor harpoon.

v27 He esteemeth iron as straw, bronze as rotten wood.

v28 The arrow will not make him flee; slingstones are turned with him into stubble.

v29 Clubs are counted as stubble; he laugheth at the shaking of a javelin.

v30 His under parts are sharp potsherds: he spreadeth a threshing-sledge upon the mire.

v31 He maketh the deep to boil like a pot; he maketh the sea like a pot of ointment;

v32 He maketh the path to shine after him: one would think the deep to be hoary.

v33 Upon earth there is not his like, who is made without fear.

v34 He beholdeth all high things; he is king over all the proud beasts.
