# Job 38 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 37|← Job 37]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 39|Job 39 →]]

***

v1 And Jehovah answered Job out of the whirlwind and said,

v2 Who is this that darkeneth counsel by words without knowledge?

v3 Gird up now thy loins like a man; and I will demand of thee, and inform thou me.

v4 Where wast thou when I founded the earth? Declare, if thou hast understanding.

v5 Who set the measures thereof -- if thou knowest? or who stretched a line upon it?

v6 Whereupon were the foundations thereof sunken? or who laid its corner-stone,

v7 When the morning stars sang together, and all the sons of God shouted for joy?

v8 And who shut up the sea with doors, when it burst forth, issuing out of the womb?

v9 When I made the cloud its garment, and thick darkness a swaddling band for it;

v10 When I cut out for it my boundary, and set bars and doors,

v11 And said, Hitherto shalt thou come and no further, and here shall thy proud waves be stayed?

v12 Hast thou since thy days commanded the morning? hast thou caused the dawn to know its place,

v13 That it might take hold of the ends of the earth, and the wicked might be shaken out of it?

v14 It is changed like the signet-clay; and [all things] stand forth as in a garment:

v15 And from the wicked their light is withholden, and the uplifted arm is broken.

v16 Hast thou entered as far as the springs of the sea? and hast thou walked in the recesses of the deep?

v17 Have the gates of death been revealed unto thee? and hast thou seen the gates of the shadow of death?

v18 Hath thine understanding compassed the breadths of the earth? Declare if thou knowest it all.

v19 Where is the way to where light dwelleth? and the darkness, where is its place,

v20 That thou shouldest take it to its bound, and that thou shouldest know the paths to its house?

v21 Thou knowest, for thou wast then born, and the number of thy days is great!

v22 Hast thou entered into the storehouses of the snow, and hast thou seen the treasuries of the hail,

v23 Which I have reserved for the time of distress, for the day of battle and war?

v24 By what way is the light parted, [and] the east wind scattered upon the earth?

v25 Who hath divided a channel for the rain-flood, and a way for the thunder's flash;

v26 To cause it to rain on the earth, where no one is; on the wilderness wherein there is not a man;

v27 To satisfy the desolate and waste [ground], and to cause the sprout of the grass to spring forth?

v28 Hath the rain a father? or who begetteth the drops of dew?

v29 Out of whose womb cometh the ice? and the hoary frost of heaven, who bringeth it forth?

v30 When the waters lie hidden as in stone, and the face of the deep holdeth fast together.

v31 Canst thou fasten the bands of the Pleiades, or loosen the cords of Orion?

v32 Dost thou bring forth the constellations each in its season? or dost thou guide the Bear with her sons?

v33 Knowest thou the ordinances of the heavens? dost thou determine their rule over the earth?

v34 Dost thou lift up thy voice to the clouds, that floods of waters may cover thee?

v35 Dost thou send forth lightnings that they may go, and say unto thee, Here we are?

v36 Who hath put wisdom in the inward parts? or who hath given understanding to the mind?

v37 Who numbereth the clouds with wisdom? or who poureth out the bottles of the heavens,

v38 When the dust runneth as into a molten mass, and the clods cleave fast together?

v39 Dost thou hunt the prey for the lioness, and dost thou satisfy the appetite of the young lions,

v40 When they crouch in [their] dens, [and] abook_ide in the thicket to lie in wait?
