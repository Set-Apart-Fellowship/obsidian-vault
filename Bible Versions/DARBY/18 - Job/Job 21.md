# Job 21 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 20|← Job 20]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 22|Job 22 →]]

***

v1 And Job answered and said,

v2 Hear attentively my speech, and let this replace your consolations.

v3 Suffer me and I will speak; and after I have spoken, mock on!

v4 As for me, is my complaint to a man? or wherefore should not my spirit be impatient?

v5 Mark me, and be astonished, and lay the hand upon the mouth.

v6 Even when I think [thereon], I am affrighted, and trembling taketh hold of my flesh.

v7 Wherefore do the wicked live, grow old, yea, become mighty in power?

v8 Their seed is established with them in their sight, and their offspring before their eyes.

v9 Their houses are safe from fear, neither is the rod of +God upon them.

v10 Their bull gendereth, and faileth not; their cow calveth, and casteth not her calf.

v11 They send forth their little ones like a flock, and their children dance.

v12 They shout to the tambour and harp, and rejoice at the sound of the pipe.

v13 They spend their days in prosperity, and in a moment go down to Sheol.

v14 And they say unto Ã¹God, Depart from us, for we desire not the knowledge of thy ways!

v15 What is the Almighty that we should serve him? and what are we profited if we pray unto him?

v16 Behold, their prosperity is not in their hand. The counsel of the wicked be far from me!

v17 How often is the lamp of the wicked put out, and cometh their calamity upon them? Doth he distribute sorrows [to them] in his anger?

v18 Do they become as stubble before the wind, and as chaff that the storm carrieth away?

v19 +God layeth up [the punishment of] his iniquity for his children; he rewardeth him, and he shall know [it]:

v20 His eyes shall see his destruction, and he shall drink of the fury of the Almighty.

v21 For what pleasure should he have in his house after him, when the number of his months is cut off?

v22 Can any teach Ã¹God knowledge? And he it is that judgeth those that are high.

v23 One dieth in his full strength, being wholly at ease and quiet;

v24 His sides are full of fat, and the marrow of his bones is moistened;

v25 And another dieth in bitterness of soul, and hath not tasted good:

v26 Together they lie down in the dust, and the worms cover them.

v27 Lo, I know your thoughts, and the devices ye wrongfully imagine against me.

v28 For ye say, Where is the house of the noble? and where the tent of the dwellings of the wicked?

v29 Have ye not asked the wayfarers? and do ye not regard their tokens:

v30 That the wicked is reserved for the day of calamity? They are led forth to the day of wrath.

v31 Who shall declare his way to his face? and who shall repay him what he hath done?

v32 Yet is he carried to the graves, and watch is kept over the tomb.

v33 The clods of the valley are sweet unto him; and every man followeth suit after him, as there were innumerable before him.

v34 How then comfort ye me in vain? Your answers remain perfidious.
