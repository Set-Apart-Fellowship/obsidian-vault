# Job 23 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 22|← Job 22]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 24|Job 24 →]]

***

v1 And Job answered and said,

v2 Even to-day is my complaint bitter: my stroke is heavier than my groaning.

v3 Oh that I knew where I might find him, that I might come to his seat!

v4 I would order the cause before him, and fill my mouth with arguments;

v5 I would know the words he would answer me, and understand what he would say unto me.

v6 Would he plead against me with [his] great power? Nay; but he would give heed unto me.

v7 There would an upright man reason with him; and I should be delivered for ever from my judge.

v8 Lo, I go forward, but he is not there; and backward, but I do not perceive him;

v9 On the left hand, where he doth work, but I behold [him] not; he hideth himself on the right hand, and I see [him] not.

v10 But he knoweth the way that I take; he trieth me, I shall come forth as gold.

v11 My foot hath held to his steps; his way have I kept, and not turned aside.

v12 Neither have I gone back from the commandment of his lips; I have laid up the words of his mouth more than the purpose of my own heart.

v13 But he is in one [mind], and who can turn him? And what his soul desireth, that will he do.

v14 For he will perform [what] is appointed for me; and many such things are with him.

v15 Therefore am I troubled at his presence; I consider, and I am afraid of him.

v16 For Ã¹God hath made my heart soft, and the Almighty troubleth me;

v17 Because I was not cut off before the darkness, neither hath he hidden the gloom from me.
