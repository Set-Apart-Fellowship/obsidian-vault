# Job 36 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 35|← Job 35]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 37|Job 37 →]]

***

v1 And Elihu proceeded and said,

v2 Suffer me a little, and I will shew thee that I have yet words for +God.

v3 I will fetch my knowledge from afar, and will ascribe righteousness to my Creator.

v4 For truly my words shall be no falsehood: one perfect in knowledge is with thee.

v5 Lo, Ã¹God is mighty, but despiseth not [any]; mighty in strength of understanding:

v6 He saveth not the wicked alive; but he doeth justice to the afflicted.

v7 He withdraweth not his eyes from the righteous, but with kings on the throne doth he even set them for ever; and they are exalted.

v8 And if, bound in fetters, they be held in cords of affliction,

v9 Then he sheweth them their work, and their transgressions, because they have increased.

v10 And he openeth their ear to discipline, and commandeth that they return from iniquity.

v11 If they hearken and serve [him], they shall accomplish their days in prosperity, and their years in pleasures.

v12 But if they hearken not, they shall pass away by the sword, and expire without knowledge.

v13 But the godless in heart heap up anger; they cry not when he bindeth them:

v14 Their soul dieth in youth, and their life is among the unclean.

v15 But he delivereth the afflicted in his affliction, and openeth their ear in [their] oppression.

v16 Even so would he have allured thee out of the jaws of distress into a broad place, where there is no straitness; and the supply of thy table [would be] full of fatness.

v17 But thou art full of the judgments of the wicked: judgment and justice take hold [on thee].

v18 Because there is wrath, [beware] lest it take thee away through chastisement: then a great ransom could not avail thee.

v19 Will he esteem thy riches? Not gold, nor all the resources of strength!

v20 Desire not the night, when peoples are cut off from their place.

v21 Take heed, turn not to iniquity; for this hast thou chosen rather than affliction.

v22 Lo, Ã¹God is exalted in his power: who teacheth as he?

v23 Who hath appointed him his way? or who hath said, Thou hast wrought unrighteousness?

v24 Remember that thou magnify his work, which men celebrate.

v25 All men look at it; man beholdeth [it] afar off.

v26 Lo, Ã¹God is great, and we comprehend [him] not, neither can the number of his years be searched out.

v27 For he draweth up the drops of water: they distil in rain from the vapour which he formeth,

v28 Which the skies pour down [and] drop upon man abundantly.

v29 But can any understand the spreadings of the clouds, [or] the crashing of his pavilion?

v30 Lo, he spreadeth his light around him, and covereth the bottom of the sea.

v31 For with them he judgeth the peoples; he giveth food in abundance.

v32 [His] hands he covereth with lightning, and commandeth it where it is to strike.

v33 His thundering declareth concerning him; the cattle even, concerning its coming.
