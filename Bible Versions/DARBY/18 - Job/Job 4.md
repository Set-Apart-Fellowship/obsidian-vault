# Job 4 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 3|← Job 3]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 5|Job 5 →]]

***

v1 And Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said,

v2 If a word were essayed to thee, wouldest thou be grieved? But who can refrain from speaking?

v3 Behold, thou hast instructed many, and thou hast strengthened the weak hands;

v4 Thy words have upholden him that was stumbling, and thou hast braced up the bending knees:

v5 But now it is come upon thee, and thou grievest; it toucheth thee, and thou art troubled.

v6 Hath not thy piety been thy confidence, and the perfection of thy ways thy hope?

v7 Remember, I pray thee, who that was innocent has perished? and where were the upright cut off?

v8 Even as I have seen, they that plough iniquity and sow mischief, reap the same.

v9 By the breath of +God they perish, and by the blast of his nostrils are they consumed.

v10 The roar of the lion, and the voice of the fierce lion, and the teeth of the young lions, are broken;

v11 The old lion perisheth for lack of prey, and the whelps of the lioness are scattered.

v12 Now to me a word was secretly brought, and mine ear received a whisper thereof.

v13 In thoughts from visions of the night, when deep sleep falleth on men: --

v14 Fear came on me, and trembling, and made all my bones to shake;

v15 And a spirit passed before my face -- the hair of my flesh stood up --

v16 It stood still; I could not discern the appearance thereof: a form was before mine eyes; I heard a slight murmur and a voice:

v17 Shall [mortal] man be more just than +God? Shall a man be purer than his Maker?

v18 Lo, he trusteth not his servants, and his angels he chargeth with folly:

v19 How much more them that dwell in houses of clay, whose foundation is in the dust, who are crushed as the moth!

v20 From morning to evening are they smitten: without any heeding it, they perish for ever.

v21 Is not their tent-cord torn away in them? they die, and without wisdom.
