# Job 15 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 14|← Job 14]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 16|Job 16 →]]

***

v1 And Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said,

v2 Should a wise man answer with windy knowledge, and fill his belly with the east wind,

v3 Reasoning with unprofitable talk, and with speeches which do no good?

v4 Yea, thou makest piety of none effect, and restrainest meditation before Ã¹God.

v5 For thy mouth uttereth thine iniquity, and thou hast chosen the tongue of the crafty.

v6 Thine own mouth condemneth thee, and not I; and thy lips testify against thee.

v7 Art thou the first man that was born? and wast thou brought forth before the hills?

v8 Hast thou listened in the secret council of +God? And hast thou absorbed wisdom for thyself?

v9 What knowest thou that we know not? [what] understandest thou which is not in us?

v10 Both the greyheaded and the aged are with us, older than thy father.

v11 Are the consolations of Ã¹God too small for thee? and the word gently spoken to thee?

v12 Why doth thy heart carry thee away? and why do thine eyes wink?

v13 That thou turnest thy spirit against Ã¹God, and lettest words go out of thy mouth?

v14 What is man, that he should be pure? and he that is born of a woman, that he should be righteous?

v15 Behold, he putteth no trust in his holy ones, and the heavens are not pure in his sight:

v16 How much less the abominable and corrupt, -- man, that drinketh unrighteousness like water!

v17 I will shew thee, listen to me; and what I have seen I will declare;

v18 Which wise men have told from their fathers, and have not hidden;

v19 Unto whom alone the earth was given, and no stranger passed among them.

v20 All his days the wicked man is tormented, and numbered years are allotted to the violent.

v21 The sound of terrors is in his ears: in prosperity the destroyer cometh upon him.

v22 He believeth not that he shall return out of darkness, and he is singled out for the sword.

v23 He wandereth abroad for bread, -- where may it be? He knoweth that the day of darkness is ready at his hand.

v24 Distress and anguish make him afraid; they prevail against him, as a king ready for the battle.

v25 For he hath stretched out his hand against Ã¹God, and strengthened himself against the Almighty:

v26 He runneth against him, with [outstretched] neck, with the thick bosses of his bucklers;

v27 For he hath covered his face with his fatness, and gathered fat upon [his] flanks.

v28 And he dwelleth in desolate cities, in houses that no man inhabiteth, which are destined to become heaps.

v29 He shall not become rich, neither shall his substance continue, and their possessions shall not extend upon the earth.

v30 He shall not depart out of darkness; the flame shall dry up his branches; and by the breath of his mouth shall he go away.

v31 Let him not trust in vanity: he is deceived, for vanity shall be his recompense;

v32 It shall be complete before his day, and his branch shall not be green.

v33 He shall shake off his unripe grapes as a vine, and shall cast his flower as an olive.

v34 For the family of the ungodly shall be barren, and fire shall consume the tents of bribery.

v35 They conceive mischief, and bring forth iniquity, and their belly prepareth deceit.
