# Job 37 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 36|← Job 36]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 38|Job 38 →]]

***

v1 Aye, my heart trembleth at this also, and leapeth up out of its place:

v2 Hear attentively the roar of his voice, and the murmur going forth from his mouth.

v3 He sendeth it forth under the whole heaven, and his lightning unto the ends of the earth.

v4 After it a voice roareth: he thundereth with the voice of his excellency, and holdeth not back the flashes when his voice is heard.

v5 Ã¹God thundereth marvellously with his voice, doing great things which we do not comprehend.

v6 For he saith to the snow, Fall on the earth! and to the pouring rain, even the pouring rains of his might.

v7 He sealeth up the hand of every man; that all men may know his work.

v8 And the wild beast goeth into its lair, and they remain in their dens.

v9 From the chamber [of the south] cometh the whirlwind; and cold from the winds of the north.

v10 By the breath of Ã¹God ice is given; and the breadth of the waters is straitened.

v11 Also with plentiful moisture he loadeth the thick clouds, his light dispels the cloud;

v12 And they are turned every way by his guidance, that they may do whatsoever he commandeth them upon the face of the circuit of the earth,

v13 Whether he cause it to come as a rod, or for his land, or in mercy.

v14 Hearken unto this, Job; stand still and discern the wondrous works of Ã¹God.

v15 Dost thou know how +God hath disposed them, and how he causeth the lightning of his cloud to flash?

v16 Dost thou know about the balancings of the clouds, the wondrous works of him that is perfect in knowledge?

v17 How thy garments become warm when he quieteth the earth by the south wind?

v18 Hast thou with him spread out the sky, firm, like a molten mirror?

v19 Teach us what we shall say unto him! We cannot order [our words] by reason of darkness.

v20 Shall it be told him if I would speak? if a man [so] say, surely he shall be swallowed up.

v21 And now [men] see not the light as it gleameth, it is [hidden] in the skies. But the wind passeth by and cleareth them.

v22 From the north cometh gold; with +God is terrible majesty.

v23 The Almighty, we cannot find him out: excellent in power, and in judgment, and in abundance of justice, he doth not afflict.

v24 Men do therefore fear him: he respecteth not any that are wise of heart.
