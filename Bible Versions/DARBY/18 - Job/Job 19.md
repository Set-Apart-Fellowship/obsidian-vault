# Job 19 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 18|← Job 18]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 20|Job 20 →]]

***

v1 And Job answered and said,

v2 How long will ye vex my soul, and crush me with words?

v3 These ten times have ye reproached me; ye are not ashamed to stupefy me.

v4 And be it [that] I have erred, mine error remaineth with myself.

v5 If indeed ye will magnify yourselves against me, and prove against me my reproach,

v6 Know now that +God hath overthrown me, and hath surrounded me with his net.

v7 Behold, I cry out of wrong, and I am not heard; I cry aloud, but there is no judgment.

v8 He hath hedged up my way that I cannot pass, and he hath set darkness in my paths.

v9 He hath stripped me of my glory, and taken the crown from my head.

v10 He breaketh me down on every side, and I am gone; and my hope hath he torn up as a tree.

v11 And he hath kindled his anger against me, and hath counted me unto him as one of his enemies.

v12 His troops have come together and cast up their way against me, and have encamped round about my tent.

v13 He hath put my brethren far from me, and mine acquaintance are quite estranged from me.

v14 My kinsfolk have failed, and my known friends have forgotten me.

v15 The sojourners in my house and my maids count me as a stranger; I am an alien in their sight.

v16 I called my servant, and he answered not; I entreated him with my mouth.

v17 My breath is strange to my wife, and my entreaties to the children of my [mother's] womb.

v18 Even young children despise me; I rise up, and they speak against me.

v19 All my intimate friends abhor me, and they whom I loved are turned against me.

v20 My bones cleave to my skin and to my flesh, and I am escaped with the skin of my teeth.

v21 Have pity upon me, have pity upon me, ye my friends; for the hand of +God hath touched me.

v22 Why do ye persecute me as Ã¹God, and are not satisfied with my flesh?

v23 Oh would that my words were written! oh that they were inscribed in a book!

v24 That with an iron style and lead they were graven in the rock for ever!

v25 And [as for] me, I know that my Redeemer liveth, and the Last, he shall stand upon the earth;

v26 And [if] after my skin this shall be destroyed, yet from out of my flesh shall I see +God;

v27 Whom I shall see for myself, and mine eyes shall behold, and not another: -- my reins are consumed within me.

v28 If ye say, How shall we persecute him? when the root of the matter is found in me,

v29 Be ye yourselves afraid of the sword! for the sword is fury against misdeeds, that ye may know there is a judgment.
