# Job 40 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 39|← Job 39]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 41|Job 41 →]]

***

v1 And Jehovah answered Job and said,

v2 Shall he that will contend with the Almighty instruct [him]? he that reproveth +God, let him answer it.

v3 And Job answered Jehovah and said,

v4 Behold, I am nought: what shall I answer thee? I will lay my hand upon my mouth.

v5 Once have I spoken, and I will not answer; yea twice, but I will proceed no further.

v6 And Jehovah answered Job out of the whirlwind and said,

v7 Gird up now thy loins like a man: I will demand of thee, and inform thou me.

v8 Wilt thou also annul my judgment? wilt thou condemn me that thou mayest be righteous?

v9 Hast thou an arm like Ã¹God? or canst thou thunder with a voice like him?

v10 Deck thyself now with glory and excellency, and clothe thyself with majesty and splendour.

v11 Cast abroad the ragings of thine anger, and look on every one that is proud, and abase him:

v12 Look on every one that is proud, bring him low, and tread down the wicked in their place:

v13 Hide them in the dust together; bind their faces in secret.

v14 Then will I also praise thee, because thy right hand saveth thee.

v15 See now the behemoth, which I made with thee: he eateth grass as an ox.

v16 Behold now, his strength is in his loins, and his force is in the muscles of his belly.

v17 He bendeth his tail like a cedar; the sinews of his thighs are woven together.

v18 His bones are tubes of bronze, his members are like bars of iron.

v19 He is the chief of Ã¹God's ways: he that made him gave him his sword.

v20 For the mountains bring him forth food, where all the beasts of the field play.

v21 He lieth under lotus-bushes, in the covert of the reed and fen:

v22 Lotus-bushes cover him with their shade; the willows of the brook surround him.

v23 Lo, the river overfloweth -- he startleth not: he is confident though a Jordan break forth against his mouth.

v24 Shall he be taken in front? will they pierce through [his] nose in the trap?
