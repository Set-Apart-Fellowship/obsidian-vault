# Job 22 (Darby Translation)

v1 And Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said,

v2 Can a man be profitable to Ã¹God? surely it is unto himself that the wise man is profitable.

v3 Is it any pleasure to the Almighty if thou art righteous? And is it gain [to him] that thou makest thy ways perfect?

v4 Will he reason with thee for fear of thee? Will he enter with thee into judgment?

v5 Is not thy wickedness great? and thine iniquities without end?

v6 For thou hast taken a pledge of thy brother for nought, and stripped off the clothing of the naked.

v7 Thou hast not given water to the fainting to drink, and thou hast withholden bread from the hungry.

v8 But the powerful man, he had the land; and the man of high rank dwelt in it.

v9 Widows hast thou sent empty away, and the arms of the fatherless have been broken.

v10 Therefore snares are round about thee, and sudden fear troubleth thee;

v11 Or darkness, that thou canst not see, and floods of waters cover thee.

v12 Is not +God in the height of the heavens? And behold the summit of the stars: how exalted are they!

v13 And thou sayest, What doth Ã¹God know? will he judge through the dark cloud?

v14 Thick clouds are a covering to him, that he seeth not; and he walketh on the vault of the heavens.

v15 Dost thou mark the ancient path which wicked men have trodden?

v16 Who were carried off before the time, whose foundation was overflowed with a flood;

v17 Who said unto Ã¹God, Depart from us! and what could the Almighty do to them?

v18 Yet he filled their houses with good. But the counsel of the wicked is far from me.

v19 The righteous see it, and are glad; and the innocent laugh them to scorn:

v20 Is not he who rose against us destroyed, and doth not the fire consume his residue?

v21 Reconcile thyself now with him, and be at peace: thereby good shall come unto thee.

v22 Receive, I pray thee, instruction from his mouth, and lay up his words in thy heart.

v23 If thou return to the Almighty, thou shalt be built up. If thou remove unrighteousness far from thy tents,

v24 And put the precious ore with the dust, and [the gold of] Ophir among the stones of the torrents,

v25 Then the Almighty will be thy precious ore, and silver heaped up unto thee;

v26 Yea, then shalt thou delight thyself in the Almighty, and shalt lift up thy face unto +God:

v27 Thou shalt make thy prayer unto him, and he will hear thee, and thou shalt pay thy vows;

v28 And thou shalt decree a thing, and it shall be established unto thee; and light shall shine upon thy ways.

v29 When they are made low, then thou shalt say, Rise up! and he shall save him that is of downcast eyes.

v30 [Even] him that is not innocent shall he deliver; yea, he shall be delivered by the pureness of thy hands.
