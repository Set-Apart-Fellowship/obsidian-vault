# Job 31 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 30|← Job 30]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 32|Job 32 →]]

***

v1 I made a covenant with mine eyes; and how should I fix my regard upon a maid?

v2 For what would have been [my] portion of +God from above, and what the heritage of the Almighty from on high?

v3 Is not calamity for the unrighteous? and misfortune for the workers of iniquity?

v4 Doth not he see my ways, and number all my steps?

v5 If I have walked with falsehood, and my foot hath hasted to deceit,

v6 (Let me be weighed in an even balance, and +God will take knowledge of my blamelessness;)

v7 If my step have turned out of the way, and my heart followed mine eyes, and if any blot cleaveth to my hands;

v8 Let me sow, and another eat; and let mine offspring be rooted out.

v9 If my heart have been enticed unto a woman, so that I laid wait at my neighbour's door,

v10 Let my wife grind for another, and let others bow down upon her.

v11 For this is an infamy; yea, it is an iniquity [to be judged by] the judges:

v12 For it is a fire that consumeth to destruction, and would root out all mine increase.

v13 If I have despised the cause of my bondman or of my bondmaid, when they contended with me,

v14 What then should I do when Ã¹God riseth up? and if he visited, what should I answer him?

v15 Did not he that made me in the womb make him? and did not One fashion us in the womb?

v16 If I have withheld the poor from [their] desire, or caused the eyes of the widow to fail;

v17 Or have eaten my morsel alone, so that the fatherless ate not thereof,

v18 (For from my youth he grew up with me as with a father, and I have guided the [widow] from my mother's womb;)

v19 If I have seen any perishing for want of clothing, or any needy without covering;

v20 If his loins have not blessed me, and if he were not warmed with the fleece of my lambs;

v21 If I have lifted up my hand against an orphan, because I saw my help in the gate:

v22 [Then] let my shoulder fall from the shoulder-blade, and mine arm be broken from the bone!

v23 For calamity from Ã¹God was a terror to me, and by reason of his excellency I was powerless.

v24 If I have made gold my hope, or said to the fine gold, My confidence!

v25 If I rejoiced because my wealth was great, and because my hand had gotten much;

v26 If I beheld the sun when it shone, or the moon walking in brightness,

v27 And my heart have been secretly enticed, so that my mouth kissed my hand:

v28 This also would be an iniquity for the judge, for I should have denied the Ã¹God who is above.

v29 If I rejoiced at the destruction of him that hated me, and exulted when evil befell him;

v30 (Neither have I suffered my mouth to sin by asking his life with a curse;)

v31 If the men of my tent said not, Who shall find one that hath not been satisfied with his meat? --

v32 The stranger did not lodge without; I opened my doors to the pathway.

v33 If I covered my transgressions as Adam, by hiding mine iniquity in my bosom,

v34 Because I feared the great multitude, and the contempt of families terrified me, so that I kept silence, and went not out of the door, ...

v35 Oh that I had one to hear me! Behold my signature: let the Almighty answer me! And let mine opponent write an accusation!

v36 Would I not take it upon my shoulder? I would bind it on to me [as] a crown;

v37 I would declare unto him the number of my steps; as a prince would I come near to him.

v38 If my land cry out against me, and its furrows weep together;

v39 If I have eaten the fruits thereof without money, and have tormented to death the souls of its owners:

v40 Let thistles grow instead of wheat, and tares instead of barley. The words of Job are ended.
