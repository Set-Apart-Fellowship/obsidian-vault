# Job 32 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 31|← Job 31]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 33|Job 33 →]]

***

v1 And these three men ceased to answer Job, because he was righteous in his own eyes.

v2 Then was kindled the anger of Elihu the son of Barachel, the Buzite, of the family of Ram: against Job was his anger kindled, because he justified himself rather than God;

v3 and against his three friends was his anger kindled, because they found no answer, and [yet] condemned Job.

v4 But Elihu had waited till Job had finished speaking, because they were older than he.

v5 And Elihu saw that there was no answer in the mouth of the three men, and his anger was kindled.

v6 And Elihu the son of Barachel the Buzite answered and said, I am young, and ye are aged; wherefore I was timid, and feared to shew you what I know.

v7 I said, Let days speak, and multitude of years teach wisdom.

v8 But there is a spirit which is in man; and the breath of the Almighty giveth them understanding.

v9 It is not the great that are wise; neither do the aged understand judgment.

v10 Therefore I say, Hearken to me; I also will shew what I know.

v11 Lo, I waited for your words; I gave ear to your reasonings, until ye searched out what to say.

v12 Yea, I gave you mine attention, and behold, there was none of you that confuted Job, that answered his words;

v13 That ye may not say, We have found out wisdom; Ã¹God will make him yield, not man.

v14 Now he hath not directed [his] words against me; and I will not answer him with your speeches. ...

v15 They were amazed, they answered no more; words failed them.

v16 And I waited, for they spoke not, but stood still, and answered no more; --

v17 I will answer, I also in my turn, I also will shew what I know:

v18 For I am full of matter, the spirit within me constraineth me.

v19 Behold, my belly is as wine which hath no vent; like new flasks, it is ready to burst.

v20 I will speak, that I may find relief; I will open my lips and answer.

v21 Let me not, I pray you, accept any man's person; neither will I give flattery to man.

v22 For I know not how to flatter; my Maker would soon take me away.
