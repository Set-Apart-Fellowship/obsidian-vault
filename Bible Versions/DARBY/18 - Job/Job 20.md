# Job 20 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 19|← Job 19]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 21|Job 21 →]]

***

v1 And Zophar the Naamathite answered and said,

v2 Therefore do my thoughts give me an answer, and for this is my haste within me.

v3 I hear a reproof putting me to shame; and [my] spirit answereth me by mine understanding.

v4 Knowest thou [not] this, that of old, since man was placed upon earth,

v5 The exultation of the wicked is short, and the joy of the ungodly man but for a moment?

v6 Though his height mount up to the heavens, and his head reach unto the clouds,

v7 Like his own dung doth he perish for ever; they which have seen him shall say, Where is he?

v8 He flieth away as a dream, and is not found; and is chased away as a vision of the night.

v9 The eye which saw him shall [see him] not again; and his place beholdeth him no more.

v10 His children shall seek the favour of the poor, and his hands restore his wealth.

v11 His bones were full of his youthful strength; but it shall lie down with him in the dust.

v12 Though wickedness be sweet in his mouth [and] he hide it under his tongue,

v13 [Though] he spare it, and forsake it not, but keep it within his mouth,

v14 His food is turned in his bowels; it is the gall of asps within him.

v15 He hath swallowed down riches, but he shall vomit them up again: Ã¹God shall cast them out of his belly.

v16 He shall suck the poison of asps; the viper's tongue shall kill him.

v17 He shall not see streams, rivers, brooks of honey and butter.

v18 That which he laboured for shall he restore, and not swallow down; its restitution shall be according to the value, and he shall not rejoice [therein].

v19 For he hath oppressed, hath forsaken the poor; he hath violently taken away a house that he did not build.

v20 Because he knew no rest in his craving, he shall save nought of what he most desired.

v21 Nothing escaped his greediness; therefore his prosperity shall not endure.

v22 In the fulness of his sufficiency he shall be in straits; every hand of the wretched shall come upon him.

v23 It shall be that, to fill his belly, he will cast his fierce anger upon him, and will rain it upon him into his flesh.

v24 If he have fled from the iron weapon, the bow of brass shall strike him through.

v25 He draweth it forth; it cometh out of his body, and the glittering point out of his gall: terrors are upon him.

v26 All darkness is laid up for his treasures: a fire not blown shall devour him; it shall feed upon what is left in his tent.

v27 The heavens shall reveal his iniquity, and the earth shall rise up against him.

v28 The increase of his house shall depart, flowing away in the day of his anger.

v29 This is the portion of the wicked man from God, and the heritage appointed to him by Ã¹God.
