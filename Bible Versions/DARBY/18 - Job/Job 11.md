# Job 11 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 10|← Job 10]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 12|Job 12 →]]

***

v1 And Zophar the Naamathite answered and said,

v2 Should not the multitude of words be answered? and should a man of much talk be justified?

v3 Should thy fictions make men hold their peace? and shouldest thou mock, and no one make [thee] ashamed?

v4 For thou sayest, My doctrine is pure, and I am clean in thine eyes.

v5 But oh that +God would speak, and open his lips against thee;

v6 And that he would shew thee the secrets of wisdom, how that they are the double of what is realised; and know that +God passeth by [much] of thine iniquity!

v7 Canst thou by searching find out +God? canst thou find out the Almighty to perfection?

v8 [It is as] the heights of heaven; what wilt thou do? deeper than Sheol; what canst thou know?

v9 The measure thereof is longer than the earth, and broader than the sea.

v10 If he pass by, and shut up, and call to judgment, who can hinder him?

v11 For he knoweth vain men, and seeth wickedness when [man] doth not consider it;

v12 Yet a senseless man will make bold, though man be born [like] the foal of a wild ass.

v13 If thou prepare thy heart and stretch out thy hands toward him,

v14 If thou put far away the iniquity which is in thy hand, and let not wrong dwell in thy tents;

v15 Surely then shalt thou lift up thy face without spot, and thou shalt be stedfast and shalt not fear:

v16 For thou shalt forget misery; as waters that are passed away shalt thou remember it;

v17 And life shall arise brighter than noonday; though thou be enshrouded in darkness, thou shalt be as the morning,

v18 And thou shalt have confidence, because there shall be hope; and having searched about [thee], thou shalt take rest in safety.

v19 Yea, thou shalt lie down, and none shall make thee afraid; and many shall seek thy favour.

v20 But the eyes of the wicked shall fail, and [all] refuge shall vanish from them, and their hope [shall be] the breathing out of life.
