# Job 3 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 2|← Job 2]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 4|Job 4 →]]

***

v1 After this, Job opened his mouth and cursed his day.

v2 And Job answered and said,

v3 Let the day perish in which I was born, and the night that said, There is a man child conceived.

v4 That day -- let it be darkness, let not +God care for it from above, neither let light shine upon it:

v5 Let darkness and the shadow of death claim it; let clouds dwell upon it; let darkeners of the day terrify it.

v6 That night -- let gloom seize upon it; let it not rejoice among the days of the year; let it not come into the number of the months.

v7 Behold, let that night be barren; let no joyful sound come therein;

v8 Let them curse it that curse the day, who are ready to rouse Leviathan;

v9 Let the stars of its twilight be dark; let it wait for light, and have none, neither let it see the eyelids of the dawn:

v10 Because it shut not up the doors of the womb that bore me, and hid not trouble from mine eyes.

v11 Wherefore did I not die from the womb, -- come forth from the belly and expire?

v12 Why did the knees meet me? and wherefore the breasts, that I should suck?

v13 For now should I have lain down and been quiet; I should have slept: then had I been at rest,

v14 With kings and counsellors of the earth, who build desolate places for themselves,

v15 Or with princes who had gold, who filled their houses with silver;

v16 Or as a hidden untimely birth I had not been; as infants that have not seen the light.

v17 There the wicked cease from troubling; and there the wearied are at rest.

v18 The prisoners together are at ease; they hear not the voice of the taskmaster.

v19 The small and great are there, and the bondman freed from his master.

v20 Wherefore is light given to him that is in trouble, and life to those bitter of soul,

v21 Who long for death, and it [cometh] not, and dig for it more than for hidden treasures;

v22 Who rejoice even exultingly and are glad when they find the grave? --

v23 To the man whose way is hidden, and whom +God hath hedged in?

v24 For my sighing cometh before my bread, and my groanings are poured out like the waters.

v25 For I feared a fear, and it hath come upon me, and that which I dreaded hath come to me.

v26 I was not in safety, neither had I quietness, neither was I at rest, and trouble came.
