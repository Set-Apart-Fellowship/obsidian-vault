# Job 6 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 5|← Job 5]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 7|Job 7 →]]

***

v1 And Job answered and said,

v2 Oh that my grief were thoroughly weighed, and all my calamity laid in the balances!

v3 For now it would be heavier than the sand of the seas; therefore my words are vehement.

v4 For the arrows of the Almighty are within me, their poison drinketh up my spirit: the terrors of +God are arrayed against me.

v5 Doth the wild ass bray by the grass? loweth an ox over his fodder?

v6 Shall that which is insipid be eaten without salt? Is there any taste in the white of an egg?

v7 What my soul refuseth to touch, that is as my loathsome food.

v8 Oh that I might have my request, and that +God would grant my desire!

v9 And that it would please +God to crush me, that he would let loose his hand and cut me off!

v10 Then should I yet have comfort; and in the pain which spareth not I would rejoice that I have not denied the words of the Holy One.

v11 What is my strength, that I should hope? and what is mine end, that I should have patience?

v12 Is my strength the strength of stones? is my flesh of brass?

v13 Is it not that there is no help in me, and soundness is driven away from me?

v14 For him that is fainting kindness [is meet] from his friend; or he forsaketh the fear of the Almighty.

v15 My brethren have dealt deceitfully as a stream, as the channel of streams which pass away,

v16 Which are turbook_id by reason of the ice, in which the snow hideth itself:

v17 At the time they diminish, they are dried up; when heat affecteth them, they vanish from their place:

v18 They wind about in the paths of their course, they go off into the waste and perish.

v19 The caravans of Tema looked, the companies of Sheba counted on them:

v20 They are ashamed at their hope; they come thither, and are confounded.

v21 So now ye are nothing; ye see a terrible object and are afraid.

v22 Did I say, Bring unto me, and make me a present from your substance?

v23 Or, rescue me from the hand of the oppressor, and redeem me from the hand of the violent?

v24 Teach me, and I will hold my tongue; and cause me to understand wherein I have erred.

v25 How forcible are right words! but what doth your upbraiding reprove?

v26 Do ye imagine to reprove words? The speeches of one that is desperate are indeed for the wind.

v27 Yea, ye overwhelm the fatherless, and dig [a pit] for your friend.

v28 Now therefore if ye will, look upon me; and it shall be to your face if I lie.

v29 Return, I pray you, let there be no wrong; yea, return again, my righteousness shall be in it.

v30 Is there wrong in my tongue? cannot my taste discern mischievous things?
