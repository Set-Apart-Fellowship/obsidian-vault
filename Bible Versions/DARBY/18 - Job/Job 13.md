# Job 13 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 12|← Job 12]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 14|Job 14 →]]

***

v1 Lo, mine eye hath seen all [this], mine ear hath heard and understood it.

v2 What ye know, I know also: I am not inferior to you.

v3 But I will speak to the Almighty, and will find pleasure in reasoning with Ã¹God;

v4 For ye indeed are forgers of lies, ye are all physicians of no value.

v5 Oh that ye would be altogether silent! and it would be your wisdom.

v6 Hear now my defence, and hearken to the pleadings of my lips.

v7 Will ye speak unrighteously for Ã¹God? and for him speak deceit?

v8 Will ye accept his person? will ye contend for Ã¹God?

v9 Will it be well if he should search you out? or as one mocketh at a man, will ye mock at him?

v10 He will certainly reprove you, if ye do secretly accept persons.

v11 Shall not his excellency terrify you? and his dread fall upon you?

v12 Your memorable sayings are proverbs of ashes, your bulwarks are bulwarks of mire.

v13 Hold your peace from me, and I will speak, and let come on me what [will]!

v14 Wherefore should I take my flesh in my teeth, and put my life in my hand?

v15 Behold, if he slay me, yet would I trust in him; but I will defend mine own ways before him.

v16 This also shall be my salvation, that a profane man shall not come before his face.

v17 Hear attentively my speech and my declaration with your ears.

v18 Behold now, I have ordered the cause; I know that I shall be justified.

v19 Who is he that contendeth with me? For if I were silent now, I should expire.

v20 Only do not two things unto me; then will I not hide myself from thee.

v21 Withdraw thy hand far from me; and let not thy terror make me afraid:

v22 Then call, and I will answer; or I will speak, and answer thou me.

v23 How many are mine iniquities and sins? Make me to know my transgression and my sin.

v24 Wherefore dost thou hide thy face, and countest me for thine enemy?

v25 Wilt thou terrify a driven leaf? and wilt thou pursue dry stubble?

v26 For thou writest bitter things against me, and makest me to possess the iniquities of my youth;

v27 And thou puttest my feet in the stocks, and markest all my paths; thou settest a bound about the soles of my feet; --

v28 One who, as a rotten thing consumeth, as a garment that the moth eateth.
