# Job 39 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 38|← Job 38]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/18 - Job/Job 40|Job 40 →]]

***

v1 Knowest thou the time when the wild goats of the rock bring forth? dost thou mark the calving of the hinds?

v2 Dost thou number the months that they fulfil? and knowest thou the time when they bring forth?

v3 They bow themselves, they give birth to their young ones, they cast out their pains;

v4 Their young ones become strong, they grow up in the open field, they go forth, and return not unto them.

v5 Who hath sent out the wild ass free? and who hath loosed the bands of the onager,

v6 Whose house I made the wilderness, and the salt plain his dwellings?

v7 He laugheth at the tumult of the city, and heareth not the shouts of the driver;

v8 The range of the mountains is his pasture, and he searcheth after every green thing.

v9 Will the buffalo be willing to serve thee, or will he lodge by thy crib?

v10 Canst thou bind the buffalo with his cord in the furrow? or will he harrow the valleys after thee?

v11 Wilt thou put confidence in him, because his strength is great? and wilt thou leave thy labour to him?

v12 Wilt thou trust him to bring home thy seed, and gather it into thy threshing-floor?

v13 The wing of the ostrich beats joyously -- But is it the stork's pinion and plumage?

v14 For she leaveth her eggs to the earth, and warmeth them in the dust,

v15 And forgetteth that the foot may crush them, or that the beast of the field may trample them.

v16 She is hardened against her young ones, as though they were not hers; her labour is in vain, without her concern.

v17 For +God hath deprived her of wisdom, and hath not furnished her with understanding.

v18 What time she lasheth herself on high, she scorneth the horse and his rider.

v19 Hast thou given strength to the horse? hast thou clothed his neck with the quivering mane?

v20 Dost thou make him to leap as a locust? His majestic snorting is terrible.

v21 He paweth in the valley, and rejoiceth in [his] strength; he goeth forth to meet the armed host.

v22 He laugheth at fear, and is not affrighted; neither turneth he back from before the sword.

v23 The quiver rattleth upon him, the glittering spear and the javelin.

v24 He swalloweth the ground with fierceness and rage, and cannot contain himself at the sound of the trumpet:

v25 At the noise of the trumpets he saith, Aha! and he smelleth the battle afar off, the thunder of the captains, and the shouting.

v26 Doth the hawk fly by thine intelligence, [and] stretch his wings toward the south?

v27 Doth the eagle mount up at thy command, and make his nest on high?

v28 He inhabiteth the rock and maketh his dwelling on the point of the cliff, and the fastness:

v29 From thence he spieth out the prey, his eyes look into the distance;

v30 And his young ones suck up blood; and where the slain are, there is he.
