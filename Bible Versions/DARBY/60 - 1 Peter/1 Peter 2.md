# 1 Peter 2 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1|← 1 Peter 1]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 3|1 Peter 3 →]]

***

v1 Laying aside therefore all malice and all guile and hypocrisies and envyings and all evil speakings,

v2 as newborn babes desire earnestly the pure mental milk of the word, that by it ye may grow up to salvation,

v3 if indeed ye have tasted that the Lord [is] good.

v4 To whom coming, a living stone, cast away indeed as worthless by men, but with God chosen, precious,

v5 yourselves also, as living stones, are being built up a spiritual house, a holy priesthood, to offer spiritual sacrifices acceptable to God by Jesus Christ.

v6 Because it is contained in the scripture: Behold, I lay in Zion a corner stone, elect, precious: and he that believes on him shall not be put to shame.

v7 To you therefore who believe [is] the preciousness; but to the disobedient, the stone which the builders cast away as worthless, this is become head of [the] corner,

v8 and a stone of stumbling and rock of offence; [who] stumble at the word, being disobedient to which also they have been appointed.

v9 But *ye* [are] a chosen race, a kingly priesthood, a holy nation, a people for a possession, that ye might set forth the excellencies of him who has called you out of darkness to his wonderful light;

v10 who once [were] not a people, but now God's people; who were not enjoying mercy, but now have found mercy.

v11 Beloved, I exhort [you], as strangers and sojourners, to abstain from fleshly lusts, which war against the soul;

v12 having your conversation honest among the Gentiles, that [as to that] in which they speak against you as evildoers, they may through [your] good works, [themselves] witnessing [them], glorify God in [the] day of visitation.

v13 Be in subjection [therefore] to every human institution for the Lord's sake; whether to [the] king as supreme,

v14 or to rulers as sent by him, for vengeance on evildoers, and praise to them that do well.

v15 Because so is the will of God, that by well-doing ye put to silence the ignorance of senseless men;

v16 as free, and not as having liberty as a cloak of malice, but as God's bondmen.

v17 Shew honour to all, love the brotherhood, fear God, honour the king.

v18 Servants, [be] subject with all fear to your masters, not only to the good and gentle, but also to the ill-tempered.

v19 For this [is] acceptable, if one, for conscience sake towards God, endure griefs, suffering unjustly.

v20 For what glory [is it], if sinning and being buffeted ye shall bear [it]? but if, doing good and suffering, ye shall bear [it], this is acceptable with God.

v21 For to this have ye been called; for Christ also has suffered for you, leaving you a model that ye should follow in his steps:

v22 who did no sin, neither was guile found in his mouth;

v23 who, [when] reviled, reviled not again; [when] suffering, threatened not; but gave [himself] over into the hands of him who judges righteously;

v24 who himself bore our sins in his body on the tree, in order that, being dead to sins, we may live to righteousness: by whose stripes ye have been healed.

v25 For ye were going astray as sheep, but have now returned to the shepherd and overseer of your souls.
