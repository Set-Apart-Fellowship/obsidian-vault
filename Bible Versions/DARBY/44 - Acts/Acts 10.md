# Acts 10 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/44 - Acts/Acts 9|← Acts 9]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/44 - Acts/Acts]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/44 - Acts/Acts 11|Acts 11 →]]

***

v1 But a certain man in Caesarea, -- by name Cornelius, a centurion of the band called Italic,

v2 pious, and fearing God with all his house, [both] giving much alms to the people, and supplicating God continually,

v3 -- saw plainly in a vision, about the ninth hour of the day, an angel of God coming unto him, and saying to him, Cornelius.

v4 But he, having fixed his eyes upon him, and become full of fear, said, What is it, Lord? And he said to him, Thy prayers and thine alms have gone up for a memorial before God.

v5 And now send men to Joppa and fetch Simon, who is surnamed Peter.

v6 He lodges with a certain Simon, a tanner, whose house is by the sea.

v7 And when the angel who was speaking to him had departed, having called two of his household and a pious soldier of those who were constantly with him,

v8 and related all things to them, he sent them to Joppa.

v9 And on the morrow, as these were journeying and drawing near to the city, Peter went up on the house to pray, about the sixth hour.

v10 And he became hungry and desired to eat. But as they were making ready an ecstasy came upon him:

v11 and he beholds the heaven opened, and a certain vessel descending, as a great sheet, [bound] by [the] four corners [and] let down to the earth;

v12 in which were all the quadrupeds and creeping things of the earth, and the fowls of the heaven.

v13 And there was a voice to him, Rise, Peter, slay and eat.

v14 And Peter said, In no wise, Lord; for I have never eaten anything common or unclean.

v15 And [there was] a voice again the second time to him, What God has cleansed, do not *thou* make common.

v16 And this took place thrice, and the vessel was straightway taken up into heaven.

v17 And as Peter doubted in himself what the vision which he had seen might mean, behold also the men who were sent by Cornelius, having sought out the house of Simon, stood at the gate,

v18 and having called [some one], they inquired if Simon who was surnamed Peter was lodged there.

v19 But as Peter continued pondering over the vision, the Spirit said to him, Behold, three men seek thee;

v20 but rise up, go down, and go with them, nothing doubting, because *I* have sent them.

v21 And Peter going down to the men said, Behold, *I* am he whom ye seek: what is the cause for which ye come?

v22 And they said, Cornelius, a centurion, a righteous man, and fearing God, and borne witness to by the whole nation of the Jews, has been divinely instructed by a holy angel to send for thee to his house, and hear words from thee.

v23 Having therefore invited them in, he lodged them. And on the morrow, rising up he went away with them, and certain of the brethren from Joppa went with him.

v24 And on the morrow they came to Caesarea. But Cornelius was looking for them, having called together his kinsmen and [his] intimate friends.

v25 And when Peter was now coming in, Cornelius met him, and falling down did [him] homage.

v26 But Peter made him rise, saying, Rise up: *I* myself also am a man.

v27 And he went in, talking with him, and found many gathered together.

v28 And he said to them, *Ye* know how it is unlawful for a Jew to be joined or come to one of a strange race, and to *me* God has shewn to call no man common or unclean.

v29 Wherefore also, having been sent for, I came without saying anything against it. I inquire therefore for what reason ye have sent for me.

v30 And Cornelius said, Four days ago I had been [fasting] unto this hour, and the ninth [I was] praying in my house, and lo, a man stood before me in bright clothing,

v31 and said, Cornelius, thy prayer has been heard, and thy alms have come in remembrance before God.

v32 Send therefore to Joppa and fetch Simon, who is surnamed Peter; he lodges in the house of Simon, a tanner, by the sea [who when he is come will speak to thee].

v33 Immediately therefore I sent to thee, and *thou* hast well done in coming. Now therefore *we* are all present before God to hear all things that are commanded thee of God.

v34 And Peter opening his mouth said, Of a truth I perceive that God is no respecter of persons,

v35 but in every nation he that fears him and works righteousness is acceptable to him.

v36 The word which he sent to the sons of Israel, preaching peace by Jesus Christ, (*he* is Lord of all things,)

v37 *ye* know; the testimony which has spread through the whole of Judaea, beginning from Galilee after the baptism which John preached --

v38 Jesus who [was] of Nazareth: how God anointed him with [the] Holy Spirit and with power; who went through [all quarters] doing good, and healing all that were under the power of the devil, because God was with him.

v39 *We* also [are] witnesses of all things which he did both in the country of the Jews and in Jerusalem; whom they also slew, having hanged him on a cross.

v40 This [man] God raised up the third day and gave him to be openly seen,

v41 not of all the people, but of witnesses who were chosen before of God, *us* who have eaten and drunk with him after he arose from among [the] dead.

v42 And he commanded us to preach to the people, and to testify that *he* it is who was determinately appointed of God [to be] judge of living and dead.

v43 To him all the prophets bear witness that every one that believes on him will receive through his name remission of sins.

v44 While Peter was yet speaking these words the Holy Spirit fell upon all those who were hearing the word.

v45 And the faithful of the circumcision were astonished, as many as came with Peter, that upon the nations also the gift of the Holy Spirit was poured out:

v46 for they heard them speaking with tongues and magnifying God. Then Peter answered,

v47 Can any one forbook_id water that these should not be baptised, who have received the Holy Spirit as we also [did]?

v48 And he commanded them to be baptised in the name of the Lord. Then they begged him to stay some days.
