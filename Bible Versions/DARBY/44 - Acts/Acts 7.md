# Acts 7 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/44 - Acts/Acts 6|← Acts 6]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/44 - Acts/Acts]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/44 - Acts/Acts 8|Acts 8 →]]

***

v1 And the high priest said, Are these things then so?

v2 And he said, Brethren and fathers, hearken. The God of glory appeared to our father Abraham when he was in Mesopotamia, before he dwelt in Charran,

v3 and said to him, Go out of thy land and out of thy kindred, and come into the land which I will shew thee.

v4 Then going out of the land of the Chaldeans he dwelt in Charran, and thence, after his father died, he removed him into this land in which *ye* now dwell.

v5 And he did not give him an inheritance in it, not even what his foot could stand on; and promised to give it to him for a possession, and to his seed after him, when he had no child.

v6 And God spoke thus: His seed shall be a sojourner in a strange land, and they shall enslave them and evil entreat [them] four hundred years;

v7 and the nation to which they shall be in bondage will *I* judge, said God; and after these things they shall come forth and serve me in this place.

v8 And he gave to him [the] covenant of circumcision; and thus he begat Isaac and circumcised him the eighth day; and Isaac Jacob, and Jacob the twelve patriarchs.

v9 And the patriarchs, envying Joseph, sold him away into Egypt. And God was with him,

v10 and delivered him out of all his tribulations, and gave him favour and wisdom in the sight of Pharaoh king of Egypt, and he appointed him chief over Egypt and all his house.

v11 But a famine came upon all the land of Egypt and Canaan, and great distress, and our fathers found no food.

v12 But Jacob, having heard of there being corn in Egypt, sent out our fathers first;

v13 and the second time Joseph was made known to his brethren, and the family of Joseph became known to Pharaoh.

v14 And Joseph sent and called down to him his father Jacob and all [his] kindred, seventy-five souls.

v15 And Jacob went down into Egypt and died, he and our fathers,

v16 and were carried over to Sychem and placed in the sepulchre which Abraham bought for a sum of money of the sons of Emmor the [father] of Sychem.

v17 But as the time of promise drew near which God had promised to Abraham, the people increased and multiplied in Egypt,

v18 until another king over Egypt arose who did not know Joseph.

v19 *He* dealt subtilly with our race, and evil entreated the fathers, casting out their infants that they might not live.

v20 In which time Moses was born, and was exceedingly lovely, who was nourished three months in the house of his father.

v21 And when he was cast out, the daughter of Pharaoh took him up, and brought him up for herself [to be] for a son.

v22 And Moses was instructed in all [the] wisdom of the Egyptians, and he was mighty in his words and deeds.

v23 And when a period of forty years was fulfilled to him, it came into his heart to look upon his brethren, the sons of Israel;

v24 and seeing a certain one wronged, he defended [him], and avenged him that was being oppressed, smiting the Egyptian.

v25 For he thought that his brethren would understand that God by his hand was giving them deliverance. But they understood not.

v26 And on the morrow he shewed himself to them as they were contending, and compelled them to peace, saying, *Ye* are brethren, why do ye wrong one another?

v27 But he that was wronging his neighbour thrust him away, saying, Who established thee ruler and judge over us?

v28 Dost *thou* wish to kill me as thou killedst the Egyptian yesterday?

v29 And Moses fled at this saying, and became a sojourner in the land of Madiam, where he begat two sons.

v30 And when forty years were fulfilled, an angel appeared to him in the wilderness of mount Sinai, in a flame of fire of a bush.

v31 And Moses seeing it wondered at the vision; and as he went up to consider it, there was a voice of [the] Lord,

v32 *I* am the God of thy fathers, the God of Abraham, and of Isaac, and of Jacob. And Moses trembled, and durst not consider [it].

v33 And the Lord said to him, Loose the sandal of thy feet, for the place on which thou standest is holy ground.

v34 I have surely seen the ill treatment of my people which is in Egypt, and I have heard their groan, and have come down to take them out of it; and now, come, I will send thee to Egypt.

v35 This Moses, whom they refused, saying, Who made thee ruler and judge? him did God send [to be] a ruler and deliverer with the hand of the angel who appeared to him in the bush.

v36 *He* led them out, having wrought wonders and signs in the land of Egypt, and in the Red sea, and in the wilderness forty years.

v37 This is the Moses who said to the sons of Israel, A prophet shall God raise up to you out of your brethren like me [him shall ye hear].

v38 This is he who was in the assembly in the wilderness, with the angel who spoke to him in the mount Sinai, and with our fathers; who received living oracles to give to us;

v39 to whom our fathers would not be subject, but thrust [him] from them, and in their hearts turned back to Egypt,

v40 saying to Aaron, Make us gods who shall go before us; for this Moses, who brought us out of the land of Egypt, we know not what has happened to him.

v41 And they made a calf in those days, and offered sacrifice to the idol, and rejoiced in the works of their own hands.

v42 But God turned and delivered them up to serve the host of heaven; as it is written in [the] book of the prophets, Have ye offered me victims and sacrifices forty years in the wilderness, O house of Israel?

v43 Yea, ye took up the tent of Moloch, and the star of [your] god Remphan, the forms which ye made to do homage to them; and I will transport you beyond Babylon.

v44 Our fathers had the tent of the testimony in the wilderness, as he that spoke to Moses commanded to make it according to the model which he had seen;

v45 which also our fathers, receiving from their predecessors, brought in with Joshua when they entered into possession of [the lands of] the nations, whom God drove out from [the] face of our fathers, until the days of David;

v46 who found favour before God, and asked to find a tabernacle for the God of Jacob;

v47 but Solomon built him a house.

v48 But the Most High dwells not in [places] made with hands; as says the prophet,

v49 The heaven [is] my throne and the earth the footstool of my feet: what house will ye build me? saith [the] Lord, or where [is the] place of my rest?

v50 has not my hand made all these things?

v51 O stiffnecked and uncircumcised in heart and ears, *ye* do always resist the Holy Spirit; as your fathers, *ye* also.

v52 Which of the prophets have not your fathers persecuted? and they have slain those who announced beforehand concerning the coming of the Just One, of whom *ye* have now become deliverers up and murderers!

v53 who have received the law as ordained by [the] ministry of angels, and have not kept [it].

v54 And hearing these things they were cut to the heart, and gnashed their teeth against him.

v55 But being full of [the] Holy Spirit, having fixed his eyes on heaven, he saw [the] glory of God, and Jesus standing at the right hand of God,

v56 and said, Lo, I behold the heavens opened, and the Son of man standing at the right hand of God.

v57 And they cried out with a loud voice, and held their ears, and rushed upon him with one accord;

v58 and having cast [him] out of the city, they stoned [him]. And the witnesses laid aside their clothes at the feet of a young man called Saul.

v59 And they stoned Stephen, praying, and saying, Lord Jesus, receive my spirit.

v60 And kneeling down, he cried with a loud voice, Lord, lay not this sin to their charge. And having said this, he fell asleep.
