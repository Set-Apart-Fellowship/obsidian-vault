# Isaiah 33 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 32|← Isaiah 32]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 34|Isaiah 34 →]]

***

v1 Woe to thee that spoilest, and thou wast not spoiled; and that dealest treacherously, and they dealt not treacherously with thee! When thou shalt cease to spoil, thou shalt be spoiled; when thou shalt make an end of dealing treacherously, they shall deal treacherously with thee.

v2 Jehovah, be gracious unto us; we have waited for thee: be their arm every morning, yea, our salvation in the time of trouble.

v3 At the noise of the tumult the peoples fled; at the lifting up of thyself the nations were scattered.

v4 And your spoil shall be gathered [like] the gathering of the caterpillar: as the running of locusts shall they run upon it.

v5 Jehovah is exalted; for he dwelleth on high: he hath filled Zion with justice and righteousness;

v6 and he shall be the stability of thy times, the riches of salvation, wisdom and knowledge: the fear of Jehovah shall be your treasure.

v7 Behold, their valiant ones cry without; the messengers of peace weep bitterly.

v8 The highways are desolate, the wayfaring man ceaseth. He hath broken the covenant, he hath despised the cities, he regardeth no man.

v9 The land mourneth, it languisheth; Lebanon is ashamed, is withered; the Sharon is become as a desert, and Bashan and Carmel are stripped.

v10 Now will I arise, saith Jehovah; now will I be exalted, now will I lift up myself.

v11 Ye shall conceive dry grass, ye shall bring forth stubble: your breath shall devour you [as] fire.

v12 And the peoples shall be [as] burnings of lime, [as] thorns cut up shall they be burned in the fire.

v13 Hear, ye that are far off, what I have done; and ye that are near, acknowledge my might.

v14 The sinners in Zion are afraid; trembling hath surprised the hypocrites: Who among us shall dwell with the consuming fire? who among us shall dwell with everlasting flames?

v15 -- He that walketh righteously, and speaketh uprightly; he that despiseth the gain of oppressions, that shaketh his hands from taking hold of bribes, that stoppeth his ears from hearing of blood, and shutteth his eyes from seeing evil:

v16 he shall dwell on high, the fortresses of the rocks shall be his high retreat; bread shall be given him, his water shall be sure.

v17 Thine eyes shall see the King in his beauty; they shall behold the land that is far off.

v18 Thy heart shall meditate on terror: Where is the scribe? where is the receiver? where is he that counted the towers?

v19 Thou shalt no more see the fierce people, a people of a deeper speech than thou canst comprehend, of a stammering tongue that cannot be understood.

v20 Look upon Zion, the city of our solemnities: thine eyes shall see Jerusalem a quiet habitation, a tent that shall not be removed, the stakes whereof shall never be pulled up, neither shall any of its cords be broken;

v21 but there Jehovah is unto us glorious, -- a place of rivers, of broad streams: no galley with oars shall go there, neither shall gallant ship pass thereby.

v22 For Jehovah is our judge, Jehovah, our lawgiver, Jehovah, our king: he will save us.

v23 Thy tacklings are loosed; they strengthen not the socket of their mast, they cannot spread the sail: then is the prey of a great spoil divided; the lame take the prey.

v24 And the inhabitant shall not say, I am sick: the people that dwell therein shall be forgiven [their] iniquity.
