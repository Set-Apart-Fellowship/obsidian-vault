# Isaiah 24 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 23|← Isaiah 23]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 25|Isaiah 25 →]]

***

v1 Behold, Jehovah maketh the land empty, and maketh it waste, and turneth it upside down, and scattereth abroad its inhabitants.

v2 And it shall be, as with the people, so with the priest; as with the servant, so with his master; as with the maid, so with her mistress; as with the buyer, so with the seller; as with the lender, so with the borrower; as with the taker of usury, so with him from whom usury is taken.

v3 The land shall be utterly emptied, and utterly spoiled; for Jehovah hath spoken this word.

v4 The land mourneth, it fadeth away; the world languisheth, it fadeth away: the haughty people of the land do languish.

v5 And the land is polluted under the inhabitants thereof; for they have violated the laws, changed the statute, broken the everlasting covenant.

v6 Therefore doth the curse devour the earth, and they that dwell therein are held guilty; therefore the inhabitants of the earth are consumed, and few men are left.

v7 The new wine mourneth, the vine languisheth, all that were merry-hearted do sigh;

v8 the mirth of tambours ceaseth, the noise of them that rejoice endeth, the joy of the harp ceaseth.

v9 They do not drink wine with a song; strong drink is bitter to them that drink it.

v10 The city of solitude is broken down; every house is shut up, so that none entereth in.

v11 There is a crying for wine in the streets; all joy is darkened, the mirth of the land is gone;

v12 desolation remaineth in the city, and the gate is smitten, -- a ruin.

v13 For so will it be in the midst of the land among the peoples, as the shaking of an olive-tree, as the grape-gleanings when the vintage is done.

v14 These shall lift up their voice, they shall shout for the majesty of Jehovah, they shall cry aloud from the sea.

v15 Therefore glorify Jehovah in the east, the name of Jehovah, the God of Israel, in the isles of the west.

v16 From the end of the earth have we heard songs: Glory to the righteous! And I said, My leanness, my leanness, woe unto me! The treacherous have dealt treacherously; yea, the treacherous have dealt very treacherously.

v17 Fear, and the pit, and the snare are upon thee, inhabitant of the land.

v18 And it shall come to pass, [that] he who fleeth from the sound of the fear shall fall into the pit; and he that cometh up out of the midst of the pit shall be taken in the snare; for the windows on high are open, and the foundations of the earth shake.

v19 The earth is utterly broken down, the earth is completely dissolved, the earth is violently moved.

v20 The earth reeleth to and fro like a drunkard, and is shaken like a night hut; and its transgression is heavy upon it; and it falleth and shall not rise again.

v21 And it shall come to pass in that day, [that] Jehovah will punish the host of the high ones on high, and the kings of the earth upon the earth.

v22 And they shall be brought together, [as] an assemblage of prisoners for the pit, and shall be shut up in prison, and after many days shall they be visited.

v23 And the moon shall be confounded, and the sun ashamed; for Jehovah of hosts shall reign on mount Zion, and in Jerusalem, and before his ancients in glory.
