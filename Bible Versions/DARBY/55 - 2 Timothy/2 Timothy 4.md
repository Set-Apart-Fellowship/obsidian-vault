# 2 Timothy 4 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy 3|← 2 Timothy 3]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy]]

***

v1 I testify before God and Christ Jesus, who is about to judge living and dead, and by his appearing and his kingdom,

v2 proclaim the word; be urgent in season [and] out of season, convict, rebuke, encourage, with all longsuffering and doctrine.

v3 For the time shall be when they will not bear sound teaching; but according to their own lusts will heap up to themselves teachers, having an itching ear;

v4 and they will turn away their ear from the truth, and will have turned aside to fables.

v5 But *thou*, be sober in all things, bear evils, do [the] work of an evangelist, fill up the full measure of thy ministry.

v6 For *I* am already being poured out, and the time of my release is come.

v7 I have combated the good combat, I have finished the race, I have kept the faith.

v8 Henceforth the crown of righteousness is laid up for me, which the Lord, the righteous Judge, will render to me in that day; but not only to me, but also to all who love his appearing.

v9 Use diligence to come to me quickly;

v10 for Demas has forsaken me, having loved the present age, and is gone to Thessalonica; Crescens to Galatia, Titus to Dalmatia.

v11 Luke alone is with me. Take Mark, and bring [him] with thyself, for he is serviceable to me for ministry.

v12 But Tychicus I have sent to Ephesus.

v13 The cloak which I left behind [me] in Troas at Carpus's, bring when thou comest, and the books, especially the parchments.

v14 Alexander the smith did many evil things against me. The Lord will render to him according to his works.

v15 Against whom be *thou* also on thy guard, for he has greatly withstood our words.

v16 At my first defence no man stood with me, but all deserted me. May it not be imputed to them.

v17 But the Lord stood with [me], and gave me power, that through me the proclamation might be fully made, and all [those of] the nations should hear; and I was delivered out of the lion's mouth.

v18 The Lord shall deliver me from every wicked work, and shall preserve [me] for his heavenly kingdom; to whom [be] glory for the ages of ages. Amen.

v19 Salute Prisca and Aquila, and the house of Onesiphorus.

v20 Erastus remained in Corinth, but Trophimus I left behind in Miletus sick.

v21 Use diligence to come before winter. Eubulus salutes thee, and Pudens, and Linus, and Claudia, and the brethren all.

v22 The Lord Jesus Christ [be] with your spirit. Grace [be] with you.
