# 2 Timothy 3 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy 2|← 2 Timothy 2]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy 4|2 Timothy 4 →]]

***

v1 But this know, that in [the] last days difficult times shall be there;

v2 for men shall be lovers of self, lovers of money, boastful, arrogant, evil speakers, disobedient to parents, ungrateful, profane,

v3 without natural affection, implacable, slanderers, of unsubdued passions, savage, having no love for what is good,

v4 traitors, headlong, of vain pretensions, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God;

v5 having a form of piety but denying the power of it: and from these turn away.

v6 For of these are they who are getting into houses, and leading captive silly women, laden with sins, led by various lusts,

v7 always learning, and never able to come to [the] knowledge of [the] truth.

v8 Now in the same manner in which Jannes and Jambres withstood Moses, thus these also withstand the truth; men corrupted in mind, found worthless as regards the faith.

v9 But they shall not advance farther; for their folly shall be completely manifest to all, as that of those also became.

v10 But *thou* hast been thoroughly acquainted with my teaching, conduct, purpose, faith, longsuffering, love, endurance,

v11 persecutions, sufferings: what [sufferings] happened to me in Antioch, in Iconium, in Lystra; what persecutions I endured; and the Lord delivered me out of all.

v12 And all indeed who desire to live piously in Christ Jesus will be persecuted.

v13 But wicked men and juggling impostors shall advance in evil, leading and being led astray.

v14 But *thou*, abook_ide in those things which thou hast learned, and [of which] thou hast been fully persuaded, knowing of whom thou hast learned [them];

v15 and that from a child thou hast known the sacred letters, which are able to make thee wise unto salvation, through faith which [is] in Christ Jesus.

v16 Every scripture [is] divinely inspired, and profitable for teaching, for conviction, for correction, for instruction in righteousness;

v17 that the man of God may be complete, fully fitted to every good work.
