# Matthew 9 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/40 - Matthew/Matthew 8|← Matthew 8]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/40 - Matthew/Matthew]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/40 - Matthew/Matthew 10|Matthew 10 →]]

***

v1 And going on board the ship, he passed over and came to his own city.

v2 And behold, they brought to him a paralytic, laid upon a bed; and Jesus, seeing their faith, said to the paralytic, Be of good courage, child; thy sins are forgiven.

v3 And behold, certain of the scribes said to themselves, This [man] blasphemes.

v4 And Jesus, seeing their thoughts, said, Why do *ye* think evil things in your hearts?

v5 For which is easier: to say, Thy sins are forgiven; or to say, Rise up and walk?

v6 But that ye may know that the Son of man has power on earth to forgive sins, (then he says to the paralytic,) Rise up, take up thy bed and go to thy house.

v7 And he rose up and went to his house.

v8 But the crowds seeing [it], were in fear, and glorified God who gave such power to men.

v9 And Jesus, passing on thence, saw a man sitting at the tax-office, called Matthew, and says to him, Follow me. And he rose up and followed him.

v10 And it came to pass, as he lay at table in the house, that behold, many tax-gatherers and sinners came and lay at table with Jesus and his disciples.

v11 And the Pharisees seeing [it], said to his disciples, Why does your teacher eat with tax-gatherers and sinners?

v12 But [Jesus] hearing it, said, They that are strong have not need of a physician, but those that are ill.

v13 But go and learn what [that] is -- I will have mercy and not sacrifice; for I have not come to call righteous [men] but sinners.

v14 Then come to him the disciples of John, saying, Why do we and the Pharisees often fast, but thy disciples fast not?

v15 And Jesus said to them, Can the sons of the bridechamber mourn so long as the bridegroom is with them? But days will come when the bridegroom will have been taken away from them, and then they will fast.

v16 But no one puts a patch of new cloth on an old garment, for its filling up takes from the garment and a worse rent takes place.

v17 Nor do men put new wine into old skins, otherwise the skins burst and the wine is poured out, and the skins will be destroyed; but they put new wine into new skins, and both are preserved together.

v18 As he spoke these things to them, behold, a ruler coming in did homage to him, saying, My daughter has by this died; but come and lay thy hand upon her and she shall live.

v19 And Jesus rose up and followed him, and [so did] his disciples.

v20 And behold, a woman, who had had a bloody flux [for] twelve years, came behind and touched the hem of his garment;

v21 for she said within herself, If I should only touch his garment I shall be healed.

v22 But Jesus turning and seeing her, said, Be of good courage, daughter; thy faith has healed thee. And the woman was healed from that hour.

v23 And when Jesus was come to the house of the ruler, and saw the flute-players and the crowd making a tumult,

v24 he said, Withdraw, for the damsel is not dead, but sleeps. And they derided him.

v25 But when the crowd had been put out, he went in and took her hand; and the damsel rose up.

v26 And the fame of it went out into all that land.

v27 And as Jesus passed on thence, two blind [men] followed him, crying and saying, Have mercy on us, Son of David.

v28 And when he was come to the house, the blind [men] came to him. And Jesus says to them, Do ye believe that I am able to do this? They say to him, Yea, Lord.

v29 Then he touched their eyes, saying, According to your faith, be it unto you.

v30 And their eyes were opened; and Jesus charged them sharply, saying, See, let no man know it.

v31 But they, when they were gone out, spread his name abroad in all that land.

v32 But as these were going out, behold, they brought to him a dumb man possessed by a demon.

v33 And the demon having been cast out, the dumb spake. And the crowds were astonished, saying, It has never been seen thus in Israel.

v34 But the Pharisees said, He casts out the demons through the prince of the demons.

v35 And Jesus went round all the cities and the villages, teaching in their synagogues, and preaching the glad tidings of the kingdom, and healing every disease and every bodily weakness.

v36 But when he saw the crowds he was moved with compassion for them, because they were harassed, and cast away as sheep not having a shepherd.

v37 Then saith he to his disciples, The harvest [is] great and the workmen [are] few;

v38 supplicate therefore the Lord of the harvest, that he send forth workmen unto his harvest.
