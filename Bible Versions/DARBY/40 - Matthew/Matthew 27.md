# Matthew 27 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/40 - Matthew/Matthew 26|← Matthew 26]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/40 - Matthew/Matthew]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28|Matthew 28 →]]

***

v1 And when it was morning all the chief priests and the elders of the people took counsel against Jesus so that they might put him to death.

v2 And having bound him they led him away, and delivered him up to Pontius Pilate, the governor.

v3 Then Judas, who delivered him up, seeing that he had been condemned, filled with remorse, returned the thirty pieces of silver to the chief priests and the elders,

v4 saying, I have sinned [in] having delivered up guiltless blood. But they said, What is that to us? see *thou* [to that].

v5 And having cast down the pieces of silver in the temple, he left the place, and went away and hanged himself.

v6 And the chief priests took the pieces of silver and said, It is not lawful to cast them into the Corban, since it is [the] price of blood.

v7 And having taken counsel, they bought with them the field of the potter for a burying-ground for strangers.

v8 Wherefore that field has been called Blood-field unto this day.

v9 Then was fulfilled that which was spoken through Jeremias the prophet, saying, And I took the thirty pieces of silver, the price of him that was set a price on, whom [they who were] of the sons of Israel had set a price on,

v10 and they gave them for the field of the potter, according as [the] Lord commanded me.

v11 But Jesus stood before the governor. And the governor questioned him, saying, Art *thou* the King of the Jews? And Jesus said to him, Thou sayest.

v12 And when he was accused of the chief priests and the elders, he answered nothing.

v13 Then says Pilate to him, Hearest thou not how many things they witness against thee?

v14 And he answered him not so much as one word, so that the governor wondered exceedingly.

v15 Now at [the] feast the governor was accustomed to release one prisoner to the crowd, whom they would.

v16 And they had then a notable prisoner, named Barabbas.

v17 They therefore being gathered together, Pilate said to them, Whom will ye that I release to you, Barabbas, or Jesus who is called Christ?

v18 For he knew that they had delivered him up through envy.

v19 But, as he was sitting on the judgment-seat, his wife sent to him, saying, Have thou nothing to do with that righteous [man]; for I have suffered to-day many things in a dream because of him.

v20 But the chief priests and the elders persuaded the crowds that they should beg for Barabbas, and destroy Jesus.

v21 And the governor answering said to them, Which of the two will ye that I release unto you? And they said, Barabbas.

v22 Pilate says to them, What then shall I do with Jesus, who is called Christ? They all say, Let him be crucified.

v23 And the governor said, What evil then has he done? But they cried more than ever, saying, Let him be crucified.

v24 And Pilate, seeing that it availed nothing, but that rather a tumult was arising, having taken water, washed his hands before the crowd, saying, I am guiltless of the blood of this righteous one: see *ye* [to it].

v25 And all the people answering said, His blood [be] on us and on our children.

v26 Then he released to them Barabbas; but Jesus, having scourged [him], he delivered up that he might be crucified.

v27 Then the soldiers of the governor, having taken Jesus with [them] to the praetorium, gathered against him the whole band,

v28 and having taken off his garment, put on him a scarlet cloak;

v29 and having woven a crown out of thorns, they put it on his head, and a reed in his right hand; and, bowing the knee before him, they mocked him, saying, Hail, King of the Jews!

v30 And having spit upon him, they took the reed and beat [him] on his head.

v31 And when they had mocked him, they took the cloak off him, and put his own clothes on him, and led him away to crucify.

v32 And as they went forth they found a man of Cyrene, Simon by name; him they compelled to go [with them] that he might bear his cross.

v33 And having come to a place called Golgotha, which means Place of a skull,

v34 they gave to him to drink vinegar mingled with gall; and having tasted [it], he would not drink.

v35 And having crucified him, they parted his clothes amongst [themselves], casting lots.

v36 And sitting down, they kept guard over him there.

v37 And they set up over his head his accusation written: This is Jesus, the King of the Jews.

v38 Then are crucified with him two robbers, one on the right hand and one on the left.

v39 But the passers-by reviled him, shaking their heads

v40 and saying, Thou that destroyest the temple and buildest it in three days, save thyself. If thou art Son of God, descend from the cross.

v41 [And] in like manner the chief priests also, mocking, with the scribes and elders, said,

v42 He saved others, himself he cannot save. He is King of Israel: let him descend now from the cross, and we will believe on him.

v43 He trusted upon God; let him save him now if he will [have] him. For he said, I am Son of God.

v44 And the robbers also who had been crucified with him cast the same reproaches on him.

v45 Now from [the] sixth hour there was darkness over the whole land until [the] ninth hour;

v46 but about the ninth hour Jesus cried out with a loud voice, saying, Eli, Eli, lama sabachthani? that is, My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me?

v47 And some of those who stood there, when they heard [it], said, This [man] calls for Elias.

v48 And immediately one of them running and getting a sponge, having filled [it] with vinegar and fixed [it] on a reed, gave him to drink.

v49 But the rest said, Let be; let us see if Elias comes to save him.

v50 And Jesus, having again cried with a loud voice, gave up the ghost.

v51 And lo, the veil of the temple was rent in two from the top to the bottom, and the earth was shaken, and the rocks were rent,

v52 and the tombs were opened; and many bodies of the saints fallen asleep arose,

v53 and going out of the tombs after his arising, entered into the holy city and appeared unto many.

v54 But the centurion, and they who were with him on guard over Jesus, seeing the earthquake and the things that took place, feared greatly, saying, Truly this [man] was Son of God.

v55 And there were there many women beholding from afar off, who had followed Jesus from Galilee ministering to him,

v56 among whom was Mary of Magdala, and Mary the mother of James and Joses, and the mother of the sons of Zebedee.

v57 Now when even was come there came a rich man of Arimathaea, his name Joseph, who also himself was a disciple to Jesus.

v58 *He*, going to Pilate, begged the body of Jesus. Then Pilate commanded the body to be given up.

v59 And Joseph having got the body, wrapped it in a clean linen cloth,

v60 and laid it in his new tomb which he had hewn in the rock; and having rolled a great stone to the door of the tomb, went away.

v61 But Mary of Magdala was there, and the other Mary, sitting opposite the sepulchre.

v62 Now on the morrow, which is after the preparation, the chief priests and the Pharisees came together to Pilate,

v63 saying, Sir, we have called to mind that that deceiver said when he was still alive, After three days I arise.

v64 Command therefore that the sepulchre be secured until the third day, lest his disciples should come and steal him away, and say to the people, He is risen from the dead; and the last error shall be worse than the first.

v65 And Pilate said to them, Ye have a watch: go, secure it as well as ye know how.

v66 And they went and secured the sepulchre, having sealed the stone, with the watch [besides].
