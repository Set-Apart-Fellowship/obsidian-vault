# Matthew 13 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/40 - Matthew/Matthew 12|← Matthew 12]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/40 - Matthew/Matthew]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/40 - Matthew/Matthew 14|Matthew 14 →]]

***

v1 And that [same] day Jesus went out from the house and sat down by the sea.

v2 And great crowds were gathered together to him, so that going on board ship himself he sat down, and the whole crowd stood on the shore.

v3 And he spoke to them many things in parables, saying, Behold, the sower went out to sow:

v4 and as he sowed, some [grains] fell along the way, and the birds came and devoured them;

v5 and others fell upon the rocky places where they had not much earth, and immediately they sprang up out of [the ground] because of not having [any] depth of earth,

v6 but when the sun rose they were burned up, and because of not having [any] root were dried up;

v7 and others fell upon the thorns, and the thorns grew up and choked them;

v8 and others fell upon the good ground, and produced fruit, one a hundred, one sixty, and one thirty.

v9 He that has ears, let him hear.

v10 And the disciples came up and said to him, Why speakest thou to them in parables?

v11 And he answering said to them, Because to you it is given to know the mysteries of the kingdom of the heavens, but to them it is not given;

v12 for whoever has, to him shall be given, and he shall be caused to be in abundance; but he who has not, even what he has shall be taken away from him.

v13 For this cause I speak to them in parables, because seeing they do not see, and hearing they do not hear nor understand;

v14 and in them is filled up the prophecy of Esaias, which says, Hearing ye shall hear and shall not understand, and beholding ye shall behold and not see;

v15 for the heart of this people has grown fat, and they have heard heavily with their ears, and they have closed their eyes as asleep, lest they should see with the eyes, and hear with the ears, and understand with the heart, and should be converted, and I should heal them.

v16 But blessed are *your* eyes because they see, and your ears because they hear;

v17 for verily I say unto you, that many prophets and righteous [men] have desired to see the things which ye behold and did not see [them], and to hear the things which ye hear and did not hear [them].

v18 *Ye*, therefore, hear the parable of the sower.

v19 From every one who hears the word of the kingdom and does not understand [it], the wicked one comes and catches away what was sown in his heart: this is he that is sown by the wayside.

v20 But he that is sown on the rocky places -- this is he who hears the word and immediately receives it with joy,

v21 but has no root in himself, but is for a time only; and when tribulation or persecution happens on account of the word, he is immediately offended.

v22 And he that is sown among the thorns -- this is he who hears the word, and the anxious care of this life, and the deceit of riches choke the word, and he becomes unfruitful.

v23 But he that is sown upon the good ground -- this is he who hears and understands the word, who bears fruit also, and produces, one a hundred, one sixty, and one thirty.

v24 Another parable set he before them, saying, The kingdom of the heavens has become like a man sowing good seed in his field;

v25 but while men slept, his enemy came and sowed darnel amongst the wheat, and went away.

v26 But when the blade shot up and produced fruit, then appeared the darnel also.

v27 And the bondmen of the householder came up and said to him, Sir, hast thou not sown good seed in thy field? whence then has it darnel?

v28 And he said to them, A man [that is] an enemy has done this. And the bondmen said to him, Wilt thou then that we should go and gather it [up]?

v29 But he said, No; lest [in] gathering the darnel ye should root up the wheat with it.

v30 Suffer both to grow together unto the harvest, and in time of the harvest I will say to the harvestmen, Gather first the darnel, and bind it into bundles to burn it; but the wheat bring together into my granary.

v31 Another parable set he before them, saying, The kingdom of the heavens is like a grain of mustard [seed] which a man took and sowed in his field;

v32 which is less indeed than all seeds, but when it is grown is greater than herbs, and becomes a tree, so that the birds of heaven come and roost in its branches.

v33 He spoke another parable to them: The kingdom of the heavens is like leaven, which a woman took and hid in three measures of meal until it had been all leavened.

v34 All these things Jesus spoke to the crowds in parables, and without a parable he did not speak to them,

v35 so that that should be fulfilled which was spoken through the prophet, saying, I will open my mouth in parables; I will utter things hidden from [the] world's foundation.

v36 Then, having dismissed the crowds, he went into the house; and his disciples came to him, saying, Expound to us the parable of the darnel of the field.

v37 But he answering said, He that sows the good seed is the Son of man,

v38 and the field is the world; and the good seed, these are the sons of the kingdom, but the darnel are the sons of the evil [one];

v39 and the enemy who has sowed it is the devil; and the harvest is [the] completion of [the] age, and the harvestmen are angels.

v40 As then the darnel is gathered and is burned in the fire, thus it shall be in the completion of the age.

v41 The Son of man shall send his angels, and they shall gather out of his kingdom all offences, and those that practise lawlessness;

v42 and they shall cast them into the furnace of fire; there shall be the weeping and the gnashing of teeth.

v43 Then the righteous shall shine forth as the sun in the kingdom of their Father. He that has ears, let him hear.

v44 The kingdom of the heavens is like a treasure hid in the field, which a man having found has hid, and for the joy of it goes and sells all whatever he has, and buys that field.

v45 Again, the kingdom of the heavens is like a merchant seeking beautiful pearls;

v46 and having found one pearl of great value, he went and sold all whatever he had and bought it.

v47 Again, the kingdom of the heavens is like a seine which has been cast into the sea, and which has gathered together of every kind,

v48 which, when it has been filled, having drawn up on the shore and sat down, they gathered the good into vessels and cast the worthless out.

v49 Thus shall it be in the completion of the age: the angels shall go forth and sever the wicked from the midst of the just,

v50 and shall cast them into the furnace of fire; there shall be the weeping and the gnashing of teeth.

v51 Jesus says to them, Have ye understood all these things? They say to him, Yea, [Lord].

v52 And he said to them, For this reason every scribe discipled to the kingdom of the heavens is like a man [that is] a householder who brings out of his treasure things new and old.

v53 And it came to pass when Jesus had finished these parables he withdrew thence.

v54 And having come into his own country, he taught them in their synagogue, so that they were astonished, and said, Whence has this [man] this wisdom and these works of power?

v55 Is not this the son of the carpenter? Is not his mother called Mary, and his brethren James, and Joseph, and Simon, and Judas?

v56 And his sisters, are they not all with us? Whence then has this [man] all these things?

v57 And they were offended in him. And Jesus said to them, A prophet is not without honour, unless in his country and in his house.

v58 And he did not there many works of power, because of their unbelief.
