# Matthew 12 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/40 - Matthew/Matthew 11|← Matthew 11]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/40 - Matthew/Matthew]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/40 - Matthew/Matthew 13|Matthew 13 →]]

***

v1 At that time Jesus went on the sabbath through the cornfields; and his disciples were hungry, and began to pluck the ears and to eat.

v2 But the Pharisees, seeing [it], said to him, Behold, thy disciples are doing what is not lawful to do on sabbath.

v3 But he said to them, Have ye not read what David did when he was hungry, and they that were with him?

v4 How he entered into the house of God, and ate the shewbread, which it was not lawful for him to eat, nor for those with him, but for the priests only?

v5 Or have ye not read in the law that on the sabbaths the priests in the temple profane the sabbath, and are blameless?

v6 But I say unto you, that there is here what is greater than the temple.

v7 But if ye had known what is: I will have mercy and not sacrifice, ye would not have condemned the guiltless.

v8 For the Son of man is Lord of the sabbath.

v9 And, going away from thence, he came into their synagogue.

v10 And behold, there was a man having his hand withered. And they asked him, saying, Is it lawful to heal on the sabbath? that they might accuse him.

v11 But he said to them, What man shall there be of you who has one sheep, and if this fall into a pit on the sabbath, will not lay hold of it and raise [it] up?

v12 How much better then is a man than a sheep! So that it is lawful to do well on the sabbath.

v13 Then he says to the man, Stretch out thy hand. And he stretched [it] out, and it was restored sound as the other.

v14 But the Pharisees, having gone out, took counsel against him, how they might destroy him.

v15 But Jesus knowing [it], withdrew thence, and great crowds followed him; and he healed them all:

v16 and charged them strictly that they should not make him publicly known:

v17 that that might be fulfilled which was spoken through Esaias the prophet, saying,

v18 Behold my servant, whom I have chosen, my beloved, in whom my soul has found its delight. I will put my Spirit upon him, and he shall shew forth judgment to the nations.

v19 He shall not strive or cry out, nor shall any one hear his voice in the streets;

v20 a bruised reed shall he not break, and smoking flax shall he not quench, until he bring forth judgment unto victory;

v21 and on his name shall [the] nations hope.

v22 Then was brought to him one possessed by a demon, blind and dumb, and he healed him, so that the dumb [man] spake and saw.

v23 And all the crowds were amazed and said, Is this [man] the Son of David?

v24 But the Pharisees, having heard [it], said, This [man] does not cast out demons, but by Beelzebub, prince of demons.

v25 But he, knowing their thoughts, said to them, Every kingdom divided against itself is brought to desolation, and every city or house divided against itself will not subsist.

v26 And if Satan casts out Satan, he is divided against himself; how then shall his kingdom subsist?

v27 And if *I* cast out demons by Beelzebub, your sons, by whom do they cast [them] out? For this reason *they* shall be your judges.

v28 But if *I* by [the] Spirit of God cast out demons, then indeed the kingdom of God is come upon you.

v29 Or how can any one enter into the house of the strong [man] and plunder his goods, unless first he bind the strong [man]? and then he will plunder his house.

v30 He that is not with me is against me, and he that gathers not with me scatters.

v31 For this reason I say unto you, Every sin and injurious speaking shall be forgiven to men, but speaking injuriously of the Spirit shall not be forgiven to men.

v32 And whosoever shall have spoken a word against the Son of man, it shall be forgiven him; but whosoever shall speak against the Holy Spirit, it shall not be forgiven him, neither in this age nor in the coming [one].

v33 Either make the tree good, and its fruit good; or make the tree corrupt, and its fruit corrupt. For from the fruit the tree is known.

v34 Offspring of vipers! how can ye speak good things, being wicked? For of the abundance of the heart the mouth speaks.

v35 The good man out of the good treasure brings forth good things; and the wicked man out of the wicked treasure brings forth wicked things.

v36 But I say unto you, that every idle word which men shall say, they shall render an account of it in judgment-day:

v37 for by thy words thou shalt be justified, and by thy words thou shalt be condemned.

v38 Then answered him some of the scribes and Pharisees, saying, Teacher, we desire to see a sign from thee.

v39 But he, answering, said to them, A wicked and adulterous generation seeks after a sign, and a sign shall not be given to it save the sign of Jonas the prophet.

v40 For even as Jonas was in the belly of the great fish three days and three nights, thus shall the Son of man be in the heart of the earth three days and three nights.

v41 Ninevites shall stand up in the judgment with this generation, and shall condemn it: for they repented at the preaching of Jonas; and behold, more than Jonas [is] here.

v42 A queen of [the] south shall rise up in the judgment with this generation, and shall condemn it; for she came from the ends of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon; and behold, more than Solomon [is] here.

v43 But when the unclean spirit has gone out of the man, he goes through dry places, seeking rest, and does not find [it].

v44 Then he says, I will return to my house whence I came out; and having come, he finds [it] unoccupied, swept, and adorned.

v45 Then he goes and takes with himself seven other spirits worse than himself, and entering in, they dwell there; and the last condition of that man becomes worse than the first. Thus shall it be to this wicked generation also.

v46 But while he was yet speaking to the crowds, behold, his mother and his brethren stood without, seeking to speak to him.

v47 Then one said unto him, Behold, thy mother and thy brethren are standing without, seeking to speak to thee.

v48 But he answering said to him that spoke to him, Who is my mother, and who are my brethren?

v49 And, stretching out his hand to his disciples, he said, Behold my mother and my brethren;

v50 for whosoever shall do the will of my Father who is in [the] heavens, he is my brother, and sister, and mother.
