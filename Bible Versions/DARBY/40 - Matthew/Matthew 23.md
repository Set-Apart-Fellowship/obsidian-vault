# Matthew 23 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/40 - Matthew/Matthew 22|← Matthew 22]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/40 - Matthew/Matthew]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24|Matthew 24 →]]

***

v1 Then Jesus spoke to the crowds and to his disciples,

v2 saying, The scribes and the Pharisees have set themselves down in Moses' seat:

v3 all things therefore, whatever they may tell you, do and keep. But do not after their works, for they say and do not,

v4 but bind burdens heavy and hard to bear, and lay them on the shoulders of men, but will not move them with their finger.

v5 And all their works they do to be seen of men: for they make broad their phylacteries and enlarge the borders [of their garments],

v6 and love the chief place in feasts and the first seats in the synagogues,

v7 and salutations in the market-places, and to be called of men, Rabbi, Rabbi.

v8 But *ye*, be not ye called Rabbi; for one is your instructor, and all *ye* are brethren.

v9 And call not [any one] your father upon the earth; for one is your Father, he who is in the heavens.

v10 Neither be called instructors, for one is your instructor, the Christ.

v11 But the greatest of you shall be your servant.

v12 And whoever shall exalt himself shall be humbled, and whoever shall humble himself shall be exalted.

v13 But woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, for ye shut up the kingdom of the heavens before men; for *ye* do not enter, nor do ye suffer those that are entering to go in.

v14 

v15 Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, for ye compass the sea and the dry [land] to make one proselyte, and when he is become [such], ye make him twofold more [the] son of hell than yourselves.

v16 Woe to you, blind guides, who say, Whosoever shall swear by the temple, it is nothing; but whosoever shall swear by the gold of the temple, he is a debtor.

v17 Fools and blind, for which is greater, the gold, or the temple which sanctifies the gold?

v18 And, Whosoever shall swear by the altar, it is nothing; but whosoever shall swear by the gift that is upon it is a debtor.

v19 [Fools and] blind ones, for which is greater, the gift, or the altar which sanctifies the gift?

v20 He therefore that swears by the altar swears by it and by all things that are upon it.

v21 And he that swears by the temple swears by it and by him that dwells in it.

v22 And he that swears by heaven swears by the throne of God and by him that sits upon it.

v23 Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, for ye pay tithes of mint and anise and cummin, and ye have left aside the weightier matters of the law, judgment and mercy and faith: these ye ought to have done and not have left those aside.

v24 Blind guides, who strain out the gnat, but drink down the camel.

v25 Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, for ye make clean the outside of the cup and of the dish, but within they are full of rapine and intemperance.

v26 Blind Pharisee, make clean first the inside of the cup and of the dish, that their outside also may become clean.

v27 Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, for ye are like whited sepulchres, which appear beautiful outwardly, but within are full of dead men's bones and all uncleanness.

v28 Thus also *ye*, outwardly ye appear righteous to men, but within are full of hypocrisy and lawlessness.

v29 Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, for ye build the sepulchres of the prophets and adorn the tombs of the just,

v30 and ye say, If we had been in the days of our fathers we would not have been partakers with them in the blood of the prophets.

v31 So that ye bear witness of yourselves that ye are sons of those who slew the prophets:

v32 and *ye*, fill ye up the measure of your fathers.

v33 Serpents, offspring of vipers, how should ye escape the judgment of hell?

v34 Therefore, behold, *I* send unto you prophets, and wise men, and scribes; and [some] of them ye will kill and crucify, and [some] of them ye will scourge in your synagogues, and will persecute from city to city;

v35 so that all righteous blood shed upon the earth should come upon *you*, from the blood of righteous Abel to the blood of Zacharias son of Barachias, whom ye slew between the temple and the altar.

v36 Verily I say unto you, All these things shall come upon this generation.

v37 Jerusalem, Jerusalem, [the city] that kills the prophets and stones those that are sent unto her, how often would I have gathered thy children as a hen gathers her chickens under her wings, and ye would not!

v38 Behold, your house is left unto you desolate;

v39 for I say unto you, Ye shall in no wise see me henceforth until ye say, Blessed [be] he that comes in the name of [the] Lord.
