# 1 John 3 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/62 - 1 John/1 John 2|← 1 John 2]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/62 - 1 John/1 John]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/62 - 1 John/1 John 4|1 John 4 →]]

***

v1 See what love the Father has given to us, that we should be called [the] children of God. For this reason the world knows us not, because it knew him not.

v2 Beloved, now are we children of God, and what we shall be has not yet been manifested; we know that if it is manifested we shall be like him, for we shall see him as he is.

v3 And every one that has this hope in him purifies himself, even as *he* is pure.

v4 Every one that practises sin practises also lawlessness; and sin is lawlessness.

v5 And ye know that *he* has been manifested that he might take away our sins; and in him sin is not.

v6 Whoever abook_ides in him, does not sin: whoever sins, has not seen him or known him.

v7 Children, let no man lead you astray; he that practises righteousness is righteous, even as *he* is righteous.

v8 He that practises sin is of the devil; for from [the] beginning the devil sins. To this end the Son of God has been manifested, that he might undo the works of the devil.

v9 Whoever has been begotten of God does not practise sin, because his seed abook_ides in him, and he cannot sin, because he has been begotten of God.

v10 In this are manifest the children of God and the children of the devil. Whoever does not practise righteousness is not of God, and he who does not love his brother.

v11 For this is the message which ye have heard from the beginning, that we should love one another:

v12 not as Cain was of the wicked one, and slew his brother; and on account of what slew he him? because his works were wicked, and those of his brother righteous.

v13 Do not wonder, brethren, if the world hate you.

v14 *We* know that we have passed from death to life, because we love the brethren. He who does not love [his] brother abook_ides in death.

v15 Every one that hates his brother is a murderer, and ye know that no murderer has eternal life abook_iding in him.

v16 Hereby we have known love, because *he* has laid down his life for us; and *we* ought for the brethren to lay down [our] lives.

v17 But whoso may have the world's substance, and see his brother having need, and shut up his bowels from him, how abook_ides the love of God in him?

v18 Children, let us not love with word, nor with tongue, but in deed and in truth.

v19 And hereby we shall know that we are of the truth, and shall persuade our hearts before him --

v20 that if our heart condemn us, God is greater than our heart and knows all things.

v21 Beloved, if our heart condemn us not, we have boldness towards God,

v22 and whatsoever we ask we receive from him, because we keep his commandments, and practise the things which are pleasing in his sight.

v23 And this is his commandment, that we believe on the name of his Son Jesus Christ, and that we love one another, even as he has given us commandment.

v24 And he that keeps his commandments abook_ides in him, and he in him. And hereby we know that he abook_ides in us, by the Spirit which he has given to us.
