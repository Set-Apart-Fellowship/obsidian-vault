# Romans 2 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/45 - Romans/Romans 1|← Romans 1]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/45 - Romans/Romans]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/45 - Romans/Romans 3|Romans 3 →]]

***

v1 Therefore thou art inexcusable, O man, every one who judgest, for in that in which thou judgest another, thou condemnest thyself; for thou that judgest doest the same things.

v2 But we know that the judgment of God is according to truth upon those who do such things.

v3 And thinkest thou this, O man, who judgest those that do such things, and practisest them [thyself], that *thou* shalt escape the judgment of God?

v4 or despisest thou the riches of his goodness, and forbearance, and long-suffering, not knowing that the goodness of God leads thee to repentance?

v5 but, according to thy hardness and impenitent heart, treasurest up to thyself wrath, in [the] day of wrath and revelation of [the] righteous judgment of God,

v6 who shall render to each according to his works:

v7 to them who, in patient continuance of good works, seek for glory and honour and incorruptibility, life eternal.

v8 But to those that are contentious, and are disobedient to the truth, but obey unrighteousness, [there shall be] wrath and indignation,

v9 tribulation and distress, on every soul of man that works evil, both of Jew first, and of Greek;

v10 but glory and honour and peace to every one that works good, both to Jew first and to Greek:

v11 for there is no acceptance of persons with God.

v12 For as many as have sinned without law shall perish also without law; and as many as have sinned under law shall be judged by law,

v13 (for not the hearers of the law [are] just before God, but the doers of the law shall be justified.

v14 For when [those of the] nations, which have no law, practise by nature the things of the law, these, having no law, are a law to themselves;

v15 who shew the work of the law written in their hearts, their conscience also bearing witness, and their thoughts accusing or else excusing themselves between themselves;)

v16 in [the] day when God shall judge the secrets of men, according to my glad tidings, by Jesus Christ.

v17 But if *thou* art named a Jew, and restest in the law, and makest thy boast in God,

v18 and knowest the will, and discerningly approvest the things that are more excellent, being instructed out of the law;

v19 and hast confidence that thou thyself art a leader of the blind, a light of those who [are] in darkness,

v20 an instructor of the foolish, a teacher of babes, having the form of knowledge and of truth in the law:

v21 thou then that teachest another, dost thou not teach thyself? thou that preachest not to steal, dost thou steal?

v22 thou that sayest [man should] not commit adultery, dost thou commit adultery? thou that abhorrest idols, dost thou commit sacrilege?

v23 thou who boastest in law, dost thou by transgression of the law dishonour God?

v24 For the name of God is blasphemed on your account among the nations, according as it is written.

v25 For circumcision indeed profits if thou keep [the] law; but if thou be a law-transgressor, thy circumcision is become uncircumcision.

v26 If therefore the uncircumcision keep the requirements of the law, shall not his uncircumcision be reckoned for circumcision,

v27 and uncircumcision by nature, fulfilling the law, judge thee, who, with letter and circumcision, [art] a law-transgressor?

v28 For he is not a Jew who [is] one outwardly, neither that circumcision which is outward in flesh;

v29 but he [is] a Jew [who is so] inwardly; and circumcision, of the heart, in spirit, not in letter; whose praise [is] not of men, but of God.
