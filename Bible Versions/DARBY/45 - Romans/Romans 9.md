# Romans 9 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/45 - Romans/Romans 8|← Romans 8]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/45 - Romans/Romans]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/45 - Romans/Romans 10|Romans 10 →]]

***

v1 I say [the] truth in Christ, I lie not, my conscience bearing witness with me in [the] Holy Spirit,

v2 that I have great grief and uninterrupted pain in my heart,

v3 for I have wished, I myself, to be a curse from the Christ for my brethren, my kinsmen, according to flesh;

v4 who are Israelites; whose [is] the adoption, and the glory, and the covenants, and the law-giving, and the service, and the promises;

v5 whose [are] the fathers; and of whom, as according to flesh, [is] the Christ, who is over all, God blessed for ever. Amen.

v6 Not however as though the word of God had failed; for not all [are] Israel which [are] of Israel;

v7 nor because they are seed of Abraham [are] all children: but, In Isaac shall a seed be called to thee.

v8 That is, [they that are] the children of the flesh, these [are] not the children of God; but the children of the promise are reckoned as seed.

v9 For this word [is] of promise, According to this time I will come, and there shall be a son to Sarah.

v10 And not only [that], but Rebecca having conceived by one, Isaac our father,

v11 [the children] indeed being not yet born, or having done anything good or worthless (that the purpose of God according to election might abook_ide, not of works, but of him that calls),

v12 it was said to her, The greater shall serve the less:

v13 according as it is written, I have loved Jacob, and I have hated Esau.

v14 What shall we say then? [Is there] unrighteousness with God? Far be the thought.

v15 For he says to Moses, I will shew mercy to whom I will shew mercy, and I will feel compassion for whom I will feel compassion.

v16 So then [it is] not of him that wills, nor of him that runs, but of God that shews mercy.

v17 For the scripture says to Pharaoh, For this very thing I have raised thee up from amongst [men], that I might thus shew in thee my power, and so that my name should be declared in all the earth.

v18 So then, to whom he will he shews mercy, and whom he will he hardens.

v19 Thou wilt say to me then, Why does he yet find fault? for who resists his purpose?

v20 Aye, but thou, O man, who art *thou* that answerest again to God? Shall the thing formed say to him that has formed it, Why hast thou made me thus?

v21 Or has not the potter authority over the clay, out of the same lump to make one vessel to honour, and another to dishonour?

v22 And if God, minded to shew his wrath and to make his power known, endured with much long-suffering vessels of wrath fitted for destruction;

v23 and that he might make known the riches of his glory upon vessels of mercy, which he had before prepared for glory,

v24 us, whom he has also called, not only from amongst [the] Jews, but also from amongst [the] nations?

v25 As he says also in Hosea, I will call not-my-people My people; and the-not-beloved Beloved.

v26 And it shall be, in the place where it was said to them, *Ye* [are] not my people, there shall they be called Sons of [the] living God.

v27 But Esaias cries concerning Israel, Should the number of the children of Israel be as the sand of the sea, the remnant shall be saved:

v28 for [he] is bringing the matter to an end, and [cutting [it] short in righteousness; because] a cutting short of the matter will [the] Lord accomplish upon the earth.

v29 And according as Esaias said before, Unless [the] Lord of hosts had left us a seed, we had been as Sodom, and made like even as Gomorrha.

v30 What then shall we say? That [they of the] nations, who did not follow after righteousness, have attained righteousness, but [the] righteousness that is on the principle of faith.

v31 But Israel, pursuing after a law of righteousness, has not attained to [that] law.

v32 Wherefore? Because [it was] not on the principle of faith, but as of works. They have stumbled at the stumblingstone,

v33 according as it is written, Behold, I place in Zion a stone of stumbling and rock of offence: and he that believes on him shall not be ashamed.
