# Romans 16 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/45 - Romans/Romans 15|← Romans 15]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/45 - Romans/Romans]]

***

v1 But I commend to you Phoebe, our sister, who is minister of the assembly which is in Cenchrea;

v2 that ye may receive her in [the] Lord worthily of saints, and that ye may assist her in whatever matter she has need of you; for *she* also has been a helper of many, and of myself.

v3 Salute Prisca and Aquila, my fellow-workmen in Christ Jesus,

v4 (who for my life staked their own neck; to whom not *I* only am thankful, but also all the assemblies of the nations,)

v5 and the assembly at their house. Salute Epaenetus, my beloved, who is [the] first-fruits of Asia for Christ.

v6 Salute Maria, who laboured much for you.

v7 Salute Andronicus and Junias, my kinsmen and fellow-captives, who are of note among the apostles; who were also in Christ before me.

v8 Salute Amplias, my beloved in the Lord.

v9 Salute Urbanus, our fellow-workman in Christ, and Stachys, my beloved.

v10 Salute Apelles, approved in Christ. Salute those who belong to Aristobulus.

v11 Salute Herodion, my kinsman. Salute those who belong to Narcissus, who are in [the] Lord.

v12 Salute Tryphaena and Tryphosa, who labour in [the] Lord. Salute Persis, the beloved, who has laboured much in [the] Lord.

v13 Salute Rufus, chosen in [the] Lord; and his mother and mine.

v14 Salute Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermes, Patrobas, Hermas, and the brethren with them.

v15 Salute Philologus, and Julia, Nereus, and his sister, and Olympas, and all the saints with them.

v16 Salute one another with a holy kiss. All the assemblies of Christ salute you.

v17 But I beseech you, brethren, to consider those who create divisions and occasions of falling, contrary to the doctrine which *ye* have learnt, and turn away from them.

v18 For such serve not our Lord Christ, but their own belly, and by good words and fair speeches deceive the hearts of the unsuspecting.

v19 For your obedience has reached to all. I rejoice therefore as it regards you; but I wish you to be wise [as] to that which is good, and simple [as] to evil.

v20 But the God of peace shall bruise Satan under your feet shortly. The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ [be] with you.

v21 Timotheus, my fellow-workman, and Lucius, and Jason, and Sosipater, my kinsmen, salute you.

v22 I Tertius, who have written this epistle, salute you in [the] Lord.

v23 Gaius, my host and of the whole assembly, salutes you. Erastus, the steward of the city, salutes you, and the brother Quartus.

v24 The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ [be] with you all. Amen.

v25 Now to him that is able to establish you, according to my glad tidings and the preaching of Jesus Christ, according to [the] revelation of [the] mystery, as to which silence has been kept in [the] times of the ages,

v26 but [which] has now been made manifest, and by prophetic scriptures, according to commandment of the eternal God, made known for obedience of faith to all the nations --

v27 [the] only wise God, through Jesus Christ, to whom be glory for ever. Amen.
