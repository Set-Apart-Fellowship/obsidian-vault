# Mark 3 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/41 - Mark/Mark 2|← Mark 2]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/41 - Mark/Mark]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/41 - Mark/Mark 4|Mark 4 →]]

***

v1 And he entered again into the synagogue; and there was there a man having his hand dried up.

v2 And they watched him if he would heal him on the sabbath, that they might accuse him.

v3 And he says to the man who had his hand dried up, Rise up [and come] into the midst.

v4 And he says to them, Is it lawful on the sabbath to do good or to do evil, to save life or to kill? But they were silent.

v5 And looking round upon them with anger, distressed at the hardening of their heart, he says to the man, Stretch out thy hand. And he stretched [it] out, and his hand was restored.

v6 And the Pharisees going out straightway with the Herodians took counsel against him, how they might destroy him.

v7 And Jesus withdrew with his disciples to the sea; and a great multitude from Galilee followed him, and from Judaea,

v8 and from Jerusalem, and from Idumaea and beyond the Jordan; and they of around Tyre and Sidon, a great multitude, having heard what things he did, came to him.

v9 And he spoke to his disciples, in order that a little ship should wait upon him on account of the crowd, that they might not press upon him.

v10 For he healed many, so that they beset him that they might touch him, as many as had plagues.

v11 And the unclean spirits, when they beheld him, fell down before him, and cried saying, *Thou* art the Son of God.

v12 And he rebuked them much, that they might not make him manifest.

v13 And he goes up into the mountain, and calls whom he himself would, and they went to him.

v14 And he appointed twelve that they might be with him, and that he might send them to preach,

v15 and to have power [to heal diseases, and] to cast out demons.

v16 And he gave to Simon the surname of Peter;

v17 and James the [son] of Zebedee, and John the brother of James, and he gave them the surname of Boanerges, that is, Sons of thunder;

v18 and Andrew, and Philip, and Bartholomew, and Matthew, and Thomas, and James the [son] of Alphaeus, and Thaddaeus, and Simon the Cananaean,

v19 and Judas Iscariote, who also delivered him up. And they come to [the] house.

v20 And again a crowd comes together, so that they cannot even eat bread.

v21 And his relatives having heard [of it] went out to lay hold on him, for they said, He is out of his mind.

v22 And the scribes who had come down from Jerusalem said, He has Beelzebub, and, By the prince of the demons he casts out demons.

v23 And having called them to [him], he said to them in parables, How can Satan cast out Satan?

v24 And if a kingdom has become divided against itself, that kingdom cannot subsist.

v25 And if a house has become divided against itself, that house cannot subsist.

v26 And if Satan rise up against himself, and is divided, he cannot subsist, but has an end.

v27 But no one can, having entered into his house, plunder the goods of the strong [man] unless he first bind the strong [man], and then he will plunder his house.

v28 Verily I say unto you, that all sins shall be forgiven to the sons of men, and all the injurious speeches [with] which they may speak injuriously;

v29 but whosoever shall speak injuriously against the Holy Spirit, to eternity has no forgiveness; but lies under the guilt of an everlasting sin;

v30 -- because they said, He has an unclean spirit.

v31 And his brethren and his mother come, and standing without sent to him calling him.

v32 And a crowd sat around him. And they said to him, Behold, thy mother and thy brethren seek thee without.

v33 And he answered them, saying, Who is my mother or my brethren?

v34 And looking around in a circuit at those that were sitting around him, he says, Behold my mother and my brethren:

v35 for whosoever shall do the will of God, *he* is my brother, and sister, and mother.
