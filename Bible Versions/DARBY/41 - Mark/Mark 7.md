# Mark 7 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/41 - Mark/Mark 6|← Mark 6]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/41 - Mark/Mark]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/41 - Mark/Mark 8|Mark 8 →]]

***

v1 And the Pharisees and some of the scribes, coming from Jerusalem, are gathered together to him,

v2 and seeing some of his disciples eat bread with defiled, that is, unwashed, hands,

v3 (for the Pharisees and all the Jews, unless they wash their hands diligently, do not eat, holding what has been delivered by the ancients;

v4 and [on coming] from the market-place, unless they are washed, they do not eat; and there are many other things which they have received to hold, the washing of cups and vessels, and brazen utensils, and couches),

v5 then the Pharisees and the scribes ask him, Why do thy disciples not walk according to what has been delivered by the ancients, but eat the bread with defiled hands?

v6 But he answering said to them, Well did Esaias prophesy concerning you hypocrites, as it is written, This people honour me with their lips, but their heart is far away from me.

v7 But in vain do they worship me, teaching [as their] teachings commandments of men.

v8 [For], leaving the commandment of God, ye hold what is delivered by men [to keep] -- washings of vessels and cups, and many other such like things ye do.

v9 And he said to them, Well do ye set aside the commandment of God, that ye may observe what is delivered by yourselves [to keep].

v10 For Moses said, Honour thy father and thy mother; and, he who speaks ill of father or mother, let him surely die.

v11 But *ye* say, If a man say to his father or his mother, [It is] corban (that is, gift), whatsoever thou mightest have profit from me by ...

v12 And ye no longer suffer him to do anything for his father or his mother;

v13 making void the word of God by your traditional teaching which ye have delivered; and many such like things ye do.

v14 And having called again the crowd, he said to them, Hear me, all [of you], and understand:

v15 There is nothing from outside a man entering into him which can defile him; but the things which go out from him, those it is which defile the man.

v16 If any one have ears to hear, let him hear.

v17 And when he went indoors from the crowd, his disciples asked him concerning the parable.

v18 And he says to them, Are *ye* also thus unintelligent? Do ye not perceive that all that is outside entering into the man cannot defile him,

v19 because it does not enter into his heart but into his belly, and goes out into the draught, purging all meats?

v20 And he said, That which goes forth out of the man, that defiles the man.

v21 For from within, out of the heart of men, go forth evil thoughts, adulteries, fornications, murders,

v22 thefts, covetousness, wickednesses, deceit, licentiousness, a wicked eye, injurious language, haughtiness, folly;

v23 all these wicked things go forth from within and defile the man.

v24 And he rose up and went away thence into the borders of Tyre and Sidon; and having entered into a house he would not have any one know [it], and he could not be hid.

v25 But immediately a woman, whose little daughter had an unclean spirit, having heard of him, came and fell at his feet

v26 (and the woman was a Greek, Syrophenician by race), and asked him that he would cast the demon out of her daughter.

v27 But [Jesus] said to her, Suffer the children to be first filled; for it is not right to take the children's bread and cast it to the dogs.

v28 But she answered and says to him, Yea, Lord; for even the dogs under the table eat of the children's crumbs.

v29 And he said to her, Because of this word, go thy way, the demon is gone out of thy daughter.

v30 And having gone away to her house she found the demon gone out, and her daughter lying on the bed.

v31 And again having left the borders of Tyre and Sidon, he came to the sea of Galilee, through the midst of the coasts of Decapolis.

v32 And they bring to him a deaf [man] who could not speak right, and they beseech him that he might lay his hand on him.

v33 And having taken him away from the crowd apart, he put his fingers to his ears; and having spit, he touched his tongue;

v34 and looking up to heaven he groaned, and says to him, Ephphatha, that is, Be opened.

v35 And immediately his ears were opened, and the band of his tongue was loosed and he spoke right.

v36 And he charged them that they should speak to no one [of it]. But so much the more *he* charged them, so much the more abundantly *they* proclaimed it;

v37 and they were astonished above measure, saying, He does all things well; he makes both the deaf to hear, and the speechless to speak.
