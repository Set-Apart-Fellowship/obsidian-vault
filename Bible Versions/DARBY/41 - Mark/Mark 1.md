# Mark 1 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/41 - Mark/Mark]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/41 - Mark/Mark 2|Mark 2 →]]

***

v1 Beginning of the glad tidings of Jesus Christ, Son of God;

v2 as it is written in [Isaiah] the prophet, Behold, *I* send my messenger before thy face, who shall prepare thy way.

v3 Voice of one crying in the wilderness, Prepare the way of [the] Lord, make his paths straight.

v4 There came John baptising in the wilderness, and preaching [the] baptism of repentance for remission of sins.

v5 And there went out to him all the district of Judaea, and all they of Jerusalem, and were baptised by him in the river Jordan, confessing their sins.

v6 And John was clothed in camel's hair, and a leathern girdle about his loins, and ate locusts and wild honey.

v7 And he preached, saying, There comes he that is mightier than I after me, the thong of whose sandals I am not fit to stoop down and unloose.

v8 *I* indeed have baptised you with water, but *he* shall baptise you with [the] Holy Spirit.

v9 And it came to pass in those days [that] Jesus came from Nazareth of Galilee, and was baptised by John at the Jordan.

v10 And straightway going up from the water, he saw the heavens parting asunder, and the Spirit, as a dove, descending upon him.

v11 And there came a voice out of the heavens: *Thou* art my beloved Son, in thee I have found my delight.

v12 And immediately the Spirit drives him out into the wilderness.

v13 And he was in the wilderness forty days tempted by Satan, and was with the wild beasts; and the angels ministered to him.

v14 But after John was delivered up, Jesus came into Galilee preaching the glad tidings of the kingdom of God,

v15 and saying, The time is fulfilled and the kingdom of God has drawn nigh; repent and believe in the glad tidings.

v16 And walking by the sea of Galilee, he saw Simon, and Andrew, [Simon's] brother, casting out a net in the sea, for they were fishers.

v17 And Jesus said to them, Come after me, and I will make you become fishers of men;

v18 and straightway leaving their trawl-nets they followed him.

v19 And going on thence a little, he saw James the [son] of Zebedee, and John his brother, and these [were] in the ship repairing the trawl-nets;

v20 and straightway he called them; and leaving their father Zebedee in the ship with the hired servants, they went away after him.

v21 And they go into Capernaum. And straightway on the sabbath he entered into the synagogue and taught.

v22 And they were astonished at his doctrine, for he taught them as having authority, and not as the scribes.

v23 And there was in their synagogue a man [possessed] by an unclean spirit, and he cried out

v24 saying, Eh! what have we to do with thee, Jesus, Nazarene? Art thou come to destroy us? I know thee who thou art, the holy one of God.

v25 And Jesus rebuked him, saying, Hold thy peace and come out of him.

v26 And the unclean spirit, having torn him, and uttered a cry with a loud voice, came out of him.

v27 And all were amazed, so that they questioned together among themselves, saying, What is this? what new doctrine is this? for with authority he commands even the unclean spirits, and they obey him.

v28 And his fame went out straightway into the whole region of Galilee around.

v29 And straightway going out of the synagogue, they came with James and John into the house of Simon and Andrew.

v30 And the mother-in-law of Simon lay in a fever. And straightway they speak to him about her.

v31 And he went up to [her] and raised her up, having taken her by the hand, and straightway the fever left her, and she served them.

v32 But evening being come, when the sun had gone down, they brought to him all that were suffering, and those possessed by demons;

v33 and the whole city was gathered together at the door.

v34 And he healed many suffering from various diseases; and he cast out many demons, and did not suffer the demons to speak because they knew him.

v35 And rising in the morning long before day, he went out and went away into a desert place, and there prayed.

v36 And Simon and those with him went after him:

v37 and having found him, they say to him, All seek thee.

v38 And he says to them, Let us go elsewhere into the neighbouring country towns, that I may preach there also, for for this purpose am I come forth.

v39 And he was preaching in their synagogues in the whole of Galilee, and casting out demons.

v40 And there comes to him a leper, beseeching him, and falling on his knees to him, and saying to him, If thou wilt thou canst cleanse me.

v41 But Jesus, moved with compassion, having stretched out his hand, touched him, and says to him, I will, be thou cleansed.

v42 And as he spoke straightway the leprosy left him, and he was cleansed.

v43 And having sharply charged him, he straightway sent him away,

v44 and says to him, See thou say nothing to any one, but go, shew thyself to the priest, and offer for thy cleansing what Moses ordained, for a testimony to them.

v45 But he, having gone forth, began to proclaim [it] much, and to spread the matter abroad, so that he could no longer enter openly into the city, but was without in desert places, and they came to him from every side.
