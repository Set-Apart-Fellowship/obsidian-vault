# Lamentations 3 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 2|← Lamentations 2]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 4|Lamentations 4 →]]

***

v1 I am the man that hath seen affliction by the rod of his wrath.

v2 Me hath he led, and brought into darkness, and not into light.

v3 Surely against me hath he turned again and again his hand all the day.

v4 My flesh and my skin hath he wasted away, he hath broken my bones.

v5 He hath built against me, and encompassed [me] with gall and toil.

v6 He hath made me to dwell in dark places as those that have been long dead.

v7 He hath hedged me about that I cannot get out: he hath made my chain heavy.

v8 Even when I cry and shout, he shutteth out my prayer.

v9 He hath inclosed my ways with hewn stone, he hath made my paths crooked.

v10 He is unto me [as] a bear lying in wait, a lion in secret places.

v11 He hath turned aside my ways, and pulled me in pieces; he hath made me desolate.

v12 He hath bent his bow, and set me as a mark for the arrow.

v13 He hath caused the arrows of his quiver to enter into my reins.

v14 I am become a derision to all my people; their song all the day.

v15 He hath sated me with bitterness, he hath made me drunk with wormwood.

v16 He hath also broken my teeth with gravel stones, he hath covered me with ashes.

v17 And thou hast removed my soul far off from peace: I have forgotten prosperity.

v18 And I said, My strength is perished, and my hope in Jehovah.

v19 Remember thou mine affliction and my wandering, the wormwood and the gall.

v20 My soul hath [them] constantly in remembrance, and is humbled in me.

v21 -- This I recall to heart, therefore have I hope.

v22 It is of Jehovah's loving-kindness we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not;

v23 they are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness.

v24 Jehovah is my portion, saith my soul; therefore will I hope in him.

v25 Jehovah is good unto them that wait for him, to the soul [that] seeketh him.

v26 It is good that one should both wait, and that in silence, for the salvation of Jehovah.

v27 It is good for a man that he bear the yoke in his youth:

v28 He sitteth solitary and keepeth silence, because he hath laid it upon him;

v29 he putteth his mouth in the dust, if so be there may be hope;

v30 he giveth his cheek to him that smiteth him; he is filled full with reproach.

v31 For the Lord will not cast off for ever;

v32 but if he have caused grief, he will have compassion according to the multitude of his loving-kindnesses:

v33 for he doth not willingly afflict or grieve the children of men.

v34 To crush under foot all the prisoners of the earth,

v35 to turn aside the right of a man before the face of the Most High,

v36 to wrong a man in his cause, -- will not the Lord see it?

v37 Who is he that saith, and there cometh to pass, what the Lord hath not commanded?

v38 Out of the mouth of the Most High doth not there proceed evil and good?

v39 Wherefore doth a living man complain, a man for the punishment of his sins?

v40 Let us search and try our ways, and turn again to Jehovah.

v41 Let us lift up our heart with [our] hands unto Ã¹God in the heavens.

v42 We have transgressed and have rebelled: thou hast not pardoned.

v43 Thou hast covered thyself with anger, and pursued us; thou hast slain, thou hast not spared.

v44 Thou hast covered thyself with a cloud, that prayer should not pass through.

v45 Thou hast made us the offscouring and refuse in the midst of the peoples.

v46 All our enemies have opened their mouth against us.

v47 Fear and the pit are come upon us, devastation and ruin.

v48 Mine eye runneth down with streams of water for the ruin of the daughter of my people.

v49 Mine eye poureth down, and ceaseth not, without any intermission,

v50 till Jehovah look down and behold from the heavens.

v51 Mine eye affecteth my soul, because of all the daughters of my city.

v52 They that are mine enemies without cause have chased me sore like a bird.

v53 They have cut off my life in a pit, and cast a stone upon me.

v54 Waters streamed over my head; I said, I am cut off.

v55 I called upon thy name, Jehovah, out of the lowest pit.

v56 Thou hast heard my voice: hide not thine ear at my sighing, at my cry.

v57 Thou drewest near in the day that I called upon thee; thou saidst, Fear not.

v58 Lord, thou hast pleaded the cause of my soul, thou hast redeemed my life.

v59 Jehovah, thou hast seen my wrong: judge thou my cause.

v60 Thou hast seen all their vengeance, all their imaginations against me.

v61 Thou hast heard their reproach, O Jehovah, all their imaginations against me;

v62 the lips of those that rise up against me and their meditation against me all the day.

v63 Behold thou their sitting down and their rising up: I am their song.

v64 Render unto them a recompence, O Jehovah, according to the work of their hands;

v65 give them obduracy of heart, thy curse unto them;

v66 pursue them in anger, and destroy them from under the heavens of Jehovah.
