# 1 Thessalonians 5 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/52 - 1 Thessalonians/1 Thessalonians 4|← 1 Thessalonians 4]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/52 - 1 Thessalonians/1 Thessalonians]]

***

v1 But concerning the times and the seasons, brethren, ye have no need that ye should be written to,

v2 for ye know perfectly well yourselves, that the day of [the] Lord so comes as a thief by night.

v3 When they may say, Peace and safety, then sudden destruction comes upon them, as travail upon her that is with child; and they shall in no wise escape.

v4 But *ye*, brethren, are not in darkness, that the day should overtake you as a thief:

v5 for all *ye* are sons of light and sons of day; we are not of night nor of darkness.

v6 So then do not let us sleep as the rest do, but let us watch and be sober;

v7 for they that sleep sleep by night, and they that drink drink by night;

v8 but *we* being of [the] day, let us be sober, putting on [the] breastplate of faith and love, and as helmet [the] hope of salvation;

v9 because God has not set us for wrath, but for obtaining salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ,

v10 who has died for us, that whether we may be watching or sleep, we may live together with him.

v11 Wherefore encourage one another, and build up each one the other, even as also ye do.

v12 But we beg you, brethren, to know those who labour among you, and take the lead among you in [the] Lord, and admonish you,

v13 and to regard them exceedingly in love on account of their work. Be in peace among yourselves.

v14 But we exhort you, brethren, admonish the disorderly, comfort the faint-hearted, sustain the weak, be patient towards all.

v15 See that no one render to any evil for evil, but pursue always what is good towards one another and towards all;

v16 rejoice always;

v17 pray unceasingly;

v18 in everything give thanks, for this is [the] will of God in Christ Jesus towards you;

v19 quench not the Spirit;

v20 do not lightly esteem prophecies;

v21 but prove all things, hold fast the right;

v22 hold aloof from every form of wickedness.

v23 Now the God of peace himself sanctify you wholly: and your whole spirit, and soul, and body be preserved blameless at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.

v24 He [is] faithful who calls you, who will also perform [it].

v25 Brethren, pray for us.

v26 Greet all the brethren with a holy kiss.

v27 I adjure you by the Lord that the letter be read to all the [holy] brethren.

v28 The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ [be] with you.
