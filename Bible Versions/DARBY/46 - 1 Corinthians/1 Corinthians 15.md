# 1 Corinthians 15 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 14|← 1 Corinthians 14]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 16|1 Corinthians 16 →]]

***

v1 But I make known to you, brethren, the glad tidings which I announced to you, which also ye received, in which also ye stand,

v2 by which also ye are saved, (if ye hold fast the word which I announced to you as the glad tidings,) unless indeed ye have believed in vain.

v3 For I delivered to you, in the first place, what also I had received, that Christ died for our sins, according to the scriptures;

v4 and that he was buried; and that he was raised the third day, according to the scriptures;

v5 and that he appeared to Cephas, then to the twelve.

v6 Then he appeared to above five hundred brethren at once, of whom the most remain until now, but some also have fallen asleep.

v7 Then he appeared to James; then to all the apostles;

v8 and last of all, as to an abortion, he appeared to *me* also.

v9 For *I* am the least of the apostles, who am not fit to be called apostle, because I have persecuted the assembly of God.

v10 But by God's grace I am what I am; and his grace, which [was] towards me, has not been vain; but I have laboured more abundantly than they all, but not *I*, but the grace of God which [was] with me.

v11 Whether, therefore, I or they, thus we preach, and thus ye have believed.

v12 Now if Christ is preached that he is raised from among [the] dead, how say some among you that there is not a resurrection of [those that are] dead?

v13 But if there is not a resurrection of [those that are] dead, neither is Christ raised:

v14 but if Christ is not raised, then, indeed, vain also [is] our preaching, and vain also your faith.

v15 And we are found also false witnesses of God; for we have witnessed concerning God that he raised the Christ, whom he has not raised if indeed [those that are] dead are not raised.

v16 For if [those that are] dead are not raised, neither is Christ raised;

v17 but if Christ be not raised, your faith [is] vain; ye are yet in your sins.

v18 Then indeed also those who have fallen asleep in Christ have perished.

v19 If in this life only we have hope in Christ, we are [the] most miserable of all men.

v20 (But now Christ is raised from among [the] dead, first-fruits of those fallen asleep.

v21 For since by man [came] death, by man also resurrection of [those that are] dead.

v22 For as in the Adam all die, thus also in the Christ all shall be made alive.

v23 But each in his own rank: [the] first-fruits, Christ; then those that are the Christ's at his coming.

v24 Then the end, when he gives up the kingdom to him [who is] God and Father; when he shall have annulled all rule and all authority and power.

v25 For he must reign until he put all enemies under his feet.

v26 [The] last enemy [that] is annulled [is] death.

v27 For he has put all things in subjection under his feet. But when he says that all things are put in subjection, [it is] evident that [it is] except him who put all things in subjection to him.

v28 But when all things shall have been brought into subjection to him, then the Son also himself shall be placed in subjection to him who put all things in subjection to him, that God may be all in all.)

v29 Since what shall the baptised for the dead do if [those that are] dead rise not at all? why also are they baptised for them?

v30 Why do *we* also endanger ourselves every hour?

v31 Daily I die, by your boasting which I have in Christ Jesus our Lord.

v32 If, [to speak] after the manner of man, I have fought with beasts in Ephesus, what is the profit to me if [those that are] dead do not rise? let us eat and drink; for to-morrow we die.

v33 Be not deceived: evil communications corrupt good manners.

v34 Awake up righteously, and sin not; for some are ignorant of God: I speak to you as a matter of shame.

v35 But some one will say, How are the dead raised? and with what body do they come?

v36 Fool; what *thou* sowest is not quickened unless it die.

v37 And what thou sowest, thou sowest not the body that shall be, but a bare grain: it may be of wheat, or some one of the rest:

v38 and God gives to it a body as he has pleased, and to each of the seeds its own body.

v39 Every flesh [is] not the same flesh, but one [is] of men, and another flesh of beasts, and another [flesh] of birds, and another of fishes.

v40 And [there are] heavenly bodies, and earthly bodies: but different is the glory of the heavenly, different that of the earthly:

v41 one [the] sun's glory, and another [the] moon's glory, and another [the] stars' glory; for star differs from star in glory.

v42 Thus also [is] the resurrection of the dead. It is sown in corruption, it is raised in incorruptibility.

v43 It is sown in dishonour, it is raised in glory. It is sown in weakness, it is raised in power.

v44 It is sown a natural body, it is raised a spiritual body: if there is a natural body, there is also a spiritual [one].

v45 Thus also it is written, The first man Adam became a living soul; the last Adam a quickening spirit.

v46 But that which is spiritual [was] not first, but that which is natural, then that which is spiritual:

v47 the first man out of [the] earth, made of dust; the second man, out of heaven.

v48 Such as he made of dust, such also those made of dust; and such as the heavenly [one], such also the heavenly [ones].

v49 And as we have borne the image of the [one] made of dust, we shall bear also the image of the heavenly [one].

v50 But this I say, brethren, that flesh and blood cannot inherit God's kingdom, nor does corruption inherit incorruptibility.

v51 Behold, I tell you a mystery: We shall not all fall asleep, but we shall all be changed,

v52 in an instant, in [the] twinkling of an eye, at the last trumpet; for the trumpet shall sound, and the dead shall be raised incorruptible, and *we* shall be changed.

v53 For this corruptible must needs put on incorruptibility, and this mortal put on immortality.

v54 But when this corruptible shall have put on incorruptibility, and this mortal shall have put on immortality, then shall come to pass the word written: Death has been swallowed up in victory.

v55 Where, O death, [is] thy sting? where, O death, thy victory?

v56 Now the sting of death [is] sin, and the power of sin the law;

v57 but thanks to God, who gives us the victory by our Lord Jesus Christ.

v58 So then, my beloved brethren, be firm, immovable, abounding always in the work of the Lord, knowing that your toil is not in vain in [the] Lord.
