# 1 Corinthians 10 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 9|← 1 Corinthians 9]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 11|1 Corinthians 11 →]]

***

v1 For I would not have you ignorant, brethren, that all our fathers were under the cloud, and all passed through the sea;

v2 and all were baptised unto Moses in the cloud and in the sea;

v3 and all ate the same spiritual food,

v4 and all drank the same spiritual drink, for they drank of a spiritual rock which followed [them]: (now the rock was the Christ;)

v5 yet God was not pleased with the most of them, for they were strewed in the desert.

v6 But these things happened [as] types of us, that we should not be lusters after evil things, as they also lusted.

v7 Neither be ye idolaters, as some of them; as it is written, The people sat down to eat and to drink, and rose up to play.

v8 Neither let us commit fornication, as some of them committed fornication, and fell in one day three and twenty thousand.

v9 Neither let us tempt the Christ, as some of them tempted, and perished by serpents.

v10 Neither murmur ye, as some of them murmured, and perished by the destroyer.

v11 Now all these things happened to them [as] types, and have been written for our admonition, upon whom the ends of the ages are come.

v12 So that let him that thinks that he stands take heed lest he fall.

v13 No temptation has taken you but such as is according to man's nature; and God is faithful, who will not suffer you to be tempted above what ye are able [to bear], but will with the temptation make the issue also, so that [ye] should be able to bear [it].

v14 Wherefore, my beloved, flee from idolatry.

v15 I speak as to intelligent [persons]: do *ye* judge what I say.

v16 The cup of blessing which we bless, is it not [the] communion of the blood of the Christ? The bread which we break, is it not [the] communion of the body of the Christ?

v17 Because we, [being] many, are one loaf, one body; for we all partake of that one loaf.

v18 See Israel according to flesh: are not they who eat the sacrifices in communion with the altar?

v19 What then do I say? that what is sacrificed to an idol is anything, or that an idol is anything?

v20 But that what [the nations] sacrifice they sacrifice to demons, and not to God. Now I do not wish you to be in communion with demons.

v21 Ye cannot drink [the] Lord's cup, and [the] cup of demons: ye cannot partake of [the] Lord's table, and of [the] table of demons.

v22 Do we provoke the Lord to jealousy? are we stronger than he?

v23 All things are lawful, but all are not profitable; all things are lawful, but all do not edify.

v24 Let no one seek his own [advantage], but that of the other.

v25 Everything sold in the shambles eat, making no inquiry for conscience sake.

v26 For the earth [is] the Lord's and its fulness.

v27 But if any one of the unbelievers invite you, and ye are minded to go, all that is set before you eat, making no inquiry for conscience sake.

v28 But if any one say to you, This is offered to holy purposes, do not eat, for his sake that pointed it out, and conscience sake;

v29 but conscience, I mean, not thine own, but that of the other: for why is my liberty judged by another conscience?

v30 If *I* partake with thanksgiving, why am I spoken evil of for what *I* give thanks for?

v31 Whether therefore ye eat, or drink, or whatever ye do, do all things to God's glory.

v32 Give no occasion to stumbling, whether to Jews, or Greeks, or the assembly of God.

v33 Even as *I* also please all in all things; not seeking my own profit, but that of the many, that they may be saved.
