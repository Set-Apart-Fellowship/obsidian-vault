# 1 Corinthians 12 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 11|← 1 Corinthians 11]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 13|1 Corinthians 13 →]]

***

v1 But concerning spiritual [manifestations], brethren, I do not wish you to be ignorant.

v2 Ye know that when ye were [of the] nations [ye were] led away to dumb idols, in whatever way ye might be led.

v3 I give you therefore to know, that no one, speaking in [the power of the] Spirit of God, says, Curse [on] Jesus; and no one can say, Lord Jesus, unless in [the power of the] Holy Spirit.

v4 But there are distinctions of gifts, but the same Spirit;

v5 and there are distinctions of services, and the same Lord;

v6 and there are distinctions of operations, but the same God who operates all things in all.

v7 But to each the manifestation of the Spirit is given for profit.

v8 For to one, by the Spirit, is given [the] word of wisdom; and to another [the] word of knowledge, according to the same Spirit;

v9 and to a different one faith, in [the power of] the same Spirit; and to another gifts of healing in [the power of] the same Spirit;

v10 and to another operations of miracles; and to another prophecy; and to another discerning of spirits; and to a different one kinds of tongues; and to another interpretation of tongues.

v11 But all these things operates the one and the same Spirit, dividing to each in particular according as he pleases.

v12 For even as the body is one and has many members, but all the members of the body, being many, are one body, so also [is] the Christ.

v13 For also in [the power of] one Spirit *we* have all been baptised into one body, whether Jews or Greeks, whether bondmen or free, and have all been given to drink of one Spirit.

v14 For also the body is not one member but many.

v15 If the foot say, Because I am not a hand I am not of the body, is it on account of this not indeed of the body?

v16 And if the ear say, Because I am not an eye I am not of the body, is it on account of this not indeed of the body?

v17 If the whole body [were] an eye, where the hearing? if all hearing, where the smelling?

v18 But now God has set the members, each one of them in the body, according as it has pleased [him].

v19 But if all were one member, where the body?

v20 But now the members [are] many, and the body one.

v21 The eye cannot say to the hand, I have not need of thee; or again, the head to the feet, I have not need of you.

v22 But much rather, the members of the body which seem to be weaker are necessary;

v23 and those [parts] of the body which we esteem to be the more void of honour, these we clothe with more abundant honour; and our uncomely [parts] have more abundant comeliness;

v24 but our comely [parts] have not need. But God has tempered the body together, having given more abundant honour to [the part] that lacked;

v25 that there might be no division in the body, but that the members might have the same concern one for another.

v26 And if one member suffer, all the members suffer with [it]; and if one member be glorified, all the members rejoice with [it].

v27 Now *ye* are Christ's body, and members in particular.

v28 And God has set certain in the assembly: first, apostles; secondly, prophets; thirdly, teachers; then miraculous powers; then gifts of healings; helps; governments; kinds of tongues.

v29 [Are] all apostles? [are] all prophets? [are] all teachers? [are] all [in possession of] miraculous powers?

v30 have all gifts of healings? do all speak with tongues? do all interpret?

v31 But desire earnestly the greater gifts, and yet shew I unto you a way of more surpassing excellence.
