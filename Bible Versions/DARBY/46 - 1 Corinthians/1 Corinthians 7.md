# 1 Corinthians 7 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 6|← 1 Corinthians 6]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 8|1 Corinthians 8 →]]

***

v1 But concerning the things of which ye have written [to me]: [It is] good for a man not to touch a woman;

v2 but on account of fornications, let each have his own wife, and each [woman] have her own husband.

v3 Let the husband render her due to the wife, and in like manner the wife to the husband.

v4 The wife has not authority over her own body, but the husband: in like manner also the husband has not authority over his own body, but the wife.

v5 Defraud not one another, unless, it may be, by consent for a time, that ye may devote yourselves to prayer, and again be together, that Satan tempt you not because of your incontinency.

v6 But this I say, as consenting [to], not as commanding [it].

v7 Now I wish all men to be even as myself: but every one has his own gift of God: one man thus, and another thus.

v8 But I say to the unmarried and to the widows, It is good for them that they remain even as I.

v9 But if they have not control over themselves, let them marry; for it is better to marry than to burn.

v10 But to the married I enjoin, not *I*, but the Lord, Let not wife be separated from husband;

v11 (but if also she shall have been separated, let her remain unmarried, or be reconciled to her husband;) and let not husband leave wife.

v12 But as to the rest, *I* say, not the Lord, If any brother have an unbelieving wife, and *she* consent to dwell with him, let him not leave her.

v13 And a woman who has an unbelieving husband, and he consents to dwell with her, let her not leave [her] husband.

v14 For the unbelieving husband is sanctified in the wife, and the unbelieving wife is sanctified in the brother; since [otherwise] indeed your children are unclean, but now they are holy.

v15 But if the unbeliever go away, let them go away; a brother or a sister is not bound in such [cases], but God has called us in peace.

v16 For what knowest thou, O wife, if thou shalt save thy husband? or what knowest thou, O husband, if thou shalt save thy wife?

v17 However, as the Lord has divided to each, as God has called each, so let him walk; and thus I ordain in all the assemblies.

v18 Has any one been called circumcised? let him not become uncircumcised: has any one been called in uncircumcision? let him not be circumcised.

v19 Circumcision is nothing, and uncircumcision is nothing; but keeping God's commandments.

v20 Let each abook_ide in that calling in which he has been called.

v21 Hast thou been called [being] a bondman, let it not concern thee; but and if thou canst become free, use [it] rather.

v22 For the bondman that is called in [the] Lord is the Lord's freedman; in like manner [also] the freeman being called is Christ's bondman.

v23 Ye have been bought with a price; do not be the bondmen of men.

v24 Let each, wherein he is called, brethren, therein abook_ide with God.

v25 But concerning virgins, I have no commandment of [the] Lord; but I give my opinion, as having received mercy of [the] Lord to be faithful.

v26 I think then that this is good, on account of the present necessity, that [it is] good for a man to remain so as he is.

v27 Art thou bound to a wife? seek not to be loosed; art thou free from a wife? do not seek a wife.

v28 But if thou shouldest also marry, thou hast not sinned; and if the virgin marry, they have not sinned: but such shall have tribulation in the flesh; but I spare you.

v29 But this I say, brethren, the time is straitened. For the rest, that they who have wives, be as not having [any]:

v30 and they that weep, as not weeping; and they that rejoice, as not rejoicing; and they that buy, as not possessing;

v31 and they that use the world, as not disposing of it as their own; for the fashion of this world passes.

v32 But I wish you to be without care. The unmarried cares for the things of the Lord, how he shall please the Lord;

v33 but he that has married cares for the things of the world, how he shall please his wife.

v34 There is a difference between the wife and the virgin. The unmarried cares for the things of the Lord, that she may be holy both in body and spirit; but she that has married cares for the things of the world, how she shall please her husband.

v35 But I say this for your own profit; not that I may set a snare before you, but for what [is] seemly, and waiting on the Lord without distraction.

v36 But if any one think that he behaves unseemly to his virginity, if he be beyond the flower of his age, and so it must be, let him do what he will, he does not sin: let them marry.

v37 But he who stands firm in his heart, having no need, but has authority over his own will, and has judged this in his heart to keep his own virginity, he does well.

v38 So that he that marries himself does well; and he that does not marry does better.

v39 A wife is bound for whatever time her husband lives; but if the husband be fallen asleep, she is free to be married to whom she will, only in [the] Lord.

v40 But she is happier if she so remain, according to my judgment; but I think that *I* also have God's Spirit.
