# 1 Corinthians 3 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 2|← 1 Corinthians 2]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 4|1 Corinthians 4 →]]

***

v1 And *I*, brethren, have not been able to speak to you as to spiritual, but as to fleshly; as to babes in Christ.

v2 I have given you milk to drink, not meat, for ye have not yet been able, nor indeed are ye yet able;

v3 for ye are yet carnal. For whereas [there are] among you emulation and strife, are ye not carnal, and walk according to man?

v4 For when one says, *I* am of Paul, and another, *I* of Apollos, are ye not men?

v5 Who then is Apollos, and who Paul? Ministering servants, through whom ye have believed, and as the Lord has given to each.

v6 *I* have planted; Apollos watered; but God has given the increase.

v7 So that neither the planter is anything, nor the waterer; but God the giver of the increase.

v8 But the planter and the waterer are one; but each shall receive his own reward according to his own labour.

v9 For we are God's fellow-workmen; ye are God's husbandry, God's building.

v10 According to the grace of God which has been given to me, as a wise architect, I have laid the foundation, but another builds upon it. But let each see how he builds upon it.

v11 For other foundation can no man lay besides that which [is] laid, which is Jesus Christ.

v12 Now if any one build upon [this] foundation, gold, silver, precious stones, wood, grass, straw,

v13 the work of each shall be made manifest; for the day shall declare [it], because it is revealed in fire; and the fire shall try the work of each what it is.

v14 If the work of any one which he has built upon [the foundation] shall abook_ide, he shall receive a reward.

v15 If the work of any one shall be consumed, he shall suffer loss, but *he* shall be saved, but so as through [the] fire.

v16 Do ye not know that ye are [the] temple of God, and [that] the Spirit of God dwells in you?

v17 If any one corrupt the temple of God, *him* shall God destroy; for the temple of God is holy, and such are *ye*.

v18 Let no one deceive himself: if any one thinks himself to be wise among you in this world, let him become foolish, that he may be wise.

v19 For the wisdom of this world is foolishness with God; for it is written, He who takes the wise in their craftiness.

v20 And again, [The] Lord knows the reasonings of the wise that they are vain.

v21 So that let no one boast in men; for all things are yours.

v22 Whether Paul, or Apollos, or Cephas, or [the] world, or life, or death, or things present, or things coming, all are yours;

v23 and *ye* [are] Christ's, and Christ [is] God's.
