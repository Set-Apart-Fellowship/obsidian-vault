# Galatians 4 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3|← Galatians 3]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/48 - Galatians/Galatians]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/48 - Galatians/Galatians 5|Galatians 5 →]]

***

v1 Now I say, As long as the heir is a child, he differs nothing from a bondman, though he be lord of all;

v2 but he is under guardians and stewards until the period fixed by the father.

v3 So we also, when we were children, were held in bondage under the principles of the world;

v4 but when the fulness of the time was come, God sent forth his Son, come of woman, come under law,

v5 that he might redeem those under law, that we might receive sonship.

v6 But because ye are sons, God has sent out the Spirit of his Son into our hearts, crying, Abba, Father.

v7 So thou art no longer bondman, but son; but if son, heir also through God.

v8 But then indeed, not knowing God, ye were in bondage to those who by nature are not gods;

v9 but now, knowing God, but rather being known by God, how do ye turn again to the weak and beggarly principles to which ye desire to be again anew in bondage?

v10 Ye observe days and months and times and years.

v11 I am afraid of you, lest indeed I have laboured in vain as to you.

v12 Be as *I* [am], for *I* also [am] as *ye*, brethren, I beseech you: ye have not at all wronged me.

v13 But ye know that in weakness of the flesh I announced the glad tidings to you at the first;

v14 and my temptation, which [was] in my flesh, ye did not slight nor reject with contempt; but ye received me as an angel of God, as Christ Jesus.

v15 What then [was] your blessedness? for I bear you witness that, if possible, plucking out your own eyes ye would have given [them] to me.

v16 So I have become your enemy in speaking the truth to you?

v17 They are not rightly zealous after you, but desire to shut you out [from us], that ye may be zealous after them.

v18 But [it is] right to be zealous at all times in what is right, and not only when I am present with you --

v19 my children, of whom I again travail in birth until Christ shall have been formed in you:

v20 and I should wish to be present with you now, and change my voice, for I am perplexed as to you.

v21 Tell me, ye who are desirous of being under law, do ye not listen to the law?

v22 For it is written that Abraham had two sons; one of the maid servant, and one of the free woman.

v23 But he [that was] of the maid servant was born according to flesh, and he [that was] of the free woman through the promise.

v24 Which things have an allegorical sense; for these are two covenants: one from mount Sinai, gendering to bondage, which is Hagar.

v25 For Hagar is mount Sinai in Arabia, and corresponds to Jerusalem which [is] now, for she is in bondage with her children;

v26 but the Jerusalem above is free, which is our mother.

v27 For it is written, Rejoice, thou barren that bearest not; break out and cry, thou that travailest not; because the children of the desolate are more numerous than [those] of her that has a husband.

v28 But *ye*, brethren, after the pattern of Isaac, are children of promise.

v29 But as then he that was born according to flesh persecuted him [that was born] according to Spirit, so also [it is] now.

v30 But what says the scripture? Cast out the maid servant and her son; for the son of the maid servant shall not inherit with the son of the free woman.

v31 So then, brethren, we are not maid servant's children, but [children] of the free woman.
