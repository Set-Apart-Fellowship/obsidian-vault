# Psalms 143 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 142|← Psalms 142]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 144|Psalms 144 →]]

***

v1 {A Psalm of David.} Jehovah, hear my prayer; give ear to my supplications: in thy faithfulness answer me, in thy righteousness.

v2 And enter not into judgment with thy servant; for in thy sight no man living shall be justified.

v3 For the enemy persecuteth my soul: he hath crushed my life down to the earth; he hath made me to dwell in dark places, as those that have been long dead.

v4 And my spirit is overwhelmed within me; my heart within me is desolate.

v5 I remember the days of old: I meditate on all thy doing; I muse on the work of thy hands.

v6 I stretch forth my hands unto thee: my soul, as a parched land, [thirsteth] after thee. Selah.

v7 Answer me speedily, O Jehovah; my spirit faileth: hide not thy face from me, or I shall be like unto them that go down into the pit.

v8 Cause me to hear thy loving-kindness in the morning, for in thee do I confide; make me to know the way wherein I should walk, for unto thee do I lift up my soul.

v9 Deliver me, O Jehovah, from mine enemies: unto thee do I flee for refuge.

v10 Teach me to do thy will; for thou art my God: let thy good Spirit lead me in a plain country.

v11 Revive me, O Jehovah, for thy name's sake; in thy righteousness bring my soul out of trouble;

v12 And in thy loving-kindness cut off mine enemies, and destroy all them that oppress my soul: for I am thy servant.
