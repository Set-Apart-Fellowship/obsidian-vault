# Psalms 123 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 122|← Psalms 122]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 124|Psalms 124 →]]

***

v1 {A Song of degrees.} Unto thee do I lift up mine eyes, O thou that dwellest in the heavens.

v2 Behold, as the eyes of servants [look] unto the hand of their masters, as the eyes of a maiden unto the hand of her mistress, so our eyes [are directed] to Jehovah our God, until he be gracious unto us.

v3 Be gracious unto us, O Jehovah, be gracious unto us; for we are exceedingly filled with contempt.

v4 Our soul is exceedingly filled with the scorning of those that are at ease, with the contempt of the proud.
