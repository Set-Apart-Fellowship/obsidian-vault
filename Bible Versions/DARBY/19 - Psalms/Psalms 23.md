# Psalms 23 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 22|← Psalms 22]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 24|Psalms 24 →]]

***

v1 {A Psalm of David.} Jehovah is my shepherd; I shall not want.

v2 He maketh me to lie down in green pastures; he leadeth me beside still waters.

v3 He restoreth my soul; he leadeth me in paths of righteousness for his name's sake.

v4 Yea, though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil: for thou art with me; thy rod and thy staff, they comfort me.

v5 Thou preparest a table before me in the presence of mine enemies; thou hast anointed my head with oil; my cup runneth over.

v6 Surely, goodness and loving-kindness shall follow me all the days of my life; and I will dwell in the house of Jehovah for the length of the days.
