# Psalms 111 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 110|← Psalms 110]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 112|Psalms 112 →]]

***

v1 Hallelujah! I will celebrate Jehovah with [my] whole heart, in the council of the upright, and in the assembly.

v2 Great are the works of Jehovah; sought out of all that delight in them.

v3 His work is majesty and splendour, and his righteousness abook_ideth for ever.

v4 He hath made his wonders to be remembered: Jehovah is gracious and merciful.

v5 He hath given meat unto them that fear him; he is ever mindful of his covenant.

v6 He hath shewn his people the power of his works, to give them the heritage of the nations.

v7 The works of his hands are truth and judgment; all his precepts are faithful:

v8 Maintained for ever and ever, done in truth and uprightness.

v9 He sent deliverance unto his people; he hath commanded his covenant for ever: holy and terrible is his name.

v10 The fear of Jehovah is the beginning of wisdom; a good understanding have all they that do [his precepts]: his praise abook_ideth for ever.
