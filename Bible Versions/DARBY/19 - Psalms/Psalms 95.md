# Psalms 95 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 94|← Psalms 94]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 96|Psalms 96 →]]

***

v1 Come, let us sing aloud to Jehovah, let us shout for joy to the rock of our salvation;

v2 Let us come before his face with thanksgiving; let us shout aloud unto him with psalms.

v3 For Jehovah is a great Ã¹God, and a great king above all gods.

v4 In his hand are the deep places of the earth; the heights of the mountains are his also:

v5 The sea is his, and he made it, and his hands formed the dry [land].

v6 Come, let us worship and bow down; let us kneel before Jehovah our Maker.

v7 For he is our God; and we are the people of his pasture and the sheep of his hand. To-day if ye hear his voice,

v8 Harden not your heart, as at Meribah, as [in] the day of Massah, in the wilderness;

v9 When your fathers tempted me, proved me, and saw my work.

v10 Forty years was I grieved with the generation, and said, It is a people that do err in their heart, and they have not known my ways;

v11 So that I swore in mine anger, that they should not enter into my rest.
