# Psalms 128 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 127|← Psalms 127]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 129|Psalms 129 →]]

***

v1 {A Song of degrees.} Blessed is every one that feareth Jehovah, that walketh in his ways.

v2 For thou shalt eat the labour of thy hands; happy shalt thou be, and it shall be well with thee.

v3 Thy wife shall be as a fruitful vine in the inner part of thy house; thy children like olive-plants round about thy table.

v4 Behold, thus shall the man be blessed that feareth Jehovah.

v5 Jehovah will bless thee out of Zion; and mayest thou see the good of Jerusalem all the days of thy life,

v6 And see thy children's children. Peace be upon Israel!
