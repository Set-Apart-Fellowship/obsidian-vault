# Psalms 55 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 54|← Psalms 54]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 56|Psalms 56 →]]

***

v1 {To the chief Musician. On stringed instruments: an instruction. Of David.} Give ear to my prayer, O God; and hide not thyself from my supplication.

v2 Attend unto me, and answer me: I wander about in my plaint, and I moan aloud,

v3 Because of the voice of the enemy; because of the oppression of the wicked: for they cast iniquity upon me, and in anger they persecute me.

v4 My heart is writhing within me, and the terrors of death are fallen upon me.

v5 Fear and trembling are come upon me, and horror hath overwhelmed me.

v6 And I said, Oh that I had wings like a dove! I would fly away, and be at rest;

v7 Behold, I would flee afar off, I would lodge in the wilderness; Selah;

v8 I would hasten my escape from the stormy wind, from the tempest.

v9 Swallow [them] up, Lord; divide their tongue: for I have seen violence and strife in the city.

v10 Day and night they go about it upon the walls thereof; and iniquity and mischief are in the midst of it.

v11 Perversities are in the midst thereof; and oppression and deceit depart not from its streets.

v12 For it is not an enemy that hath reproached me -- then could I have borne it; neither is it he that hateth me that hath magnified [himself] against me -- then would I have hidden myself from him;

v13 But it was thou, a man mine equal, mine intimate, my familiar friend. ...

v14 We who held sweet intercourse together. To the house of God we walked amid the throng.

v15 Let death seize upon them, let them go down alive into Sheol. For wickedness is in their dwellings, in their midst.

v16 As for me, unto God will I call; and Jehovah will save me.

v17 Evening, and morning, and at noon, will I pray and moan aloud; and he will hear my voice.

v18 He hath redeemed my soul in peace from the battle against me: for there were many about me.

v19 Ã¹God will hear, and afflict them: he that is seated of old, (Selah) ... because there is no change in them, and they fear not God.

v20 He hath put forth his hands against such as are at peace with him; he hath profaned his covenant.

v21 Smooth were the milky [words] of his mouth, but his heart was war; his words were softer than oil, yet are they drawn swords.

v22 Cast thy burden upon Jehovah, and *he* will sustain thee: he will never suffer the righteous to be moved.

v23 And thou, O God, wilt bring them down into the pit of destruction: bloody and deceitful men shall not live out half their days. But as for me, I will confide in thee.
