# Psalms 146 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 145|← Psalms 145]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 147|Psalms 147 →]]

***

v1 Hallelujah! Praise Jehovah, O my soul.

v2 As long as I live will I praise Jehovah; I will sing psalms unto my God while I have my being.

v3 Put not confidence in nobles, in a son of man, in whom there is no salvation.

v4 His breath goeth forth, he returneth to his earth; in that very day his purposes perish.

v5 Blessed is he who hath the Ã¹God of Jacob for his help, whose hope is in Jehovah his God,

v6 Who made the heavens and the earth, the sea and all that is therein; who keepeth truth for ever;

v7 Who executeth judgment for the oppressed, who giveth bread to the hungry. Jehovah looseth the prisoners;

v8 Jehovah openeth [the eyes of] the blind; Jehovah raiseth up them that are bowed down; Jehovah loveth the righteous;

v9 Jehovah preserveth the strangers; he lifteth up the fatherless and the widow; but the way of the wicked doth he subvert.

v10 Jehovah will reign for ever, [even] thy God, O Zion, from generation to generation. Halleluiah!
