# Psalms 102 (Darby Translation)

***

v1 {A Prayer of the afflicted, when he is overwhelmed, and poureth out his complaint before Jehovah.} Jehovah, hear my prayer, and let my cry come unto thee.

v2 Hide not thy face from me: in the day of my trouble, incline thine ear unto me; in the day I call, answer me speedily.

v3 For my days are consumed like smoke, and my bones are burned as a firebrand.

v4 My heart is smitten and withered like grass; yea, I have forgotten to eat my bread.

v5 By reason of the voice of my groaning, my bones cleave to my flesh.

v6 I am become like the pelican of the wilderness, I am as an owl in desolate places;

v7 I watch, and am like a sparrow alone upon the housetop.

v8 Mine enemies reproach me all the day; they that are mad against me swear by me.

v9 For I have eaten ashes like bread, and mingled my drink with weeping,

v10 Because of thine indignation and thy wrath; for thou hast lifted me up, and cast me down.

v11 My days are like a lengthened-out shadow, and I, I am withered like grass.

v12 But thou, Jehovah, abook_idest for ever, and thy memorial from generation to generation.

v13 *Thou* wilt rise up, thou wilt have mercy upon Zion: for it is the time to be gracious to her, for the set time is come.

v14 For thy servants take pleasure in her stones, and favour her dust.

v15 And the nations shall fear the name of Jehovah, and all the kings of the earth thy glory.

v16 When Jehovah shall build up Zion, he will appear in his glory.

v17 He will regard the prayer of the destitute one, and not despise their prayer.

v18 This shall be written for the generation to come; and a people that shall be created shall praise Jah:

v19 For he hath looked down from the height of his sanctuary; from the heavens hath Jehovah beheld the earth,

v20 To hear the groaning of the prisoner, to loose those that are appointed to die;

v21 That the name of Jehovah may be declared in Zion, and his praise in Jerusalem,

v22 When the peoples shall be gathered together, and the kingdoms, to serve Jehovah.

v23 He weakened my strength in the way, he shortened my days.

v24 I said, My Ã¹God, take me not away in the midst of my days! ... Thy years are from generation to generation.

v25 Of old hast thou founded the earth, and the heavens are the work of thy hands:

v26 *They* shall perish, but *thou* continuest; and all of them shall grow old as a garment: as a vesture shalt thou change them, and they shall be changed.

v27 But thou art the Same, and thy years shall have no end.

v28 The children of thy servants shall abook_ide, and their seed shall be established before thee.
