# Psalms 94 (Darby Translation)

v1 O Ã¹God of vengeances, Jehovah, Ã¹God of vengeances, shine forth;

v2 Lift up thyself, thou judge of the earth, render the reward to the proud.

v3 How long shall the wicked, O Jehovah, how long shall the wicked triumph?

v4 [How long] shall they utter [and] speak insolence -- all the workers of iniquity boast themselves?

v5 They crush thy people, O Jehovah, and afflict thine inheritance;

v6 They slay the widow and the stranger, and murder the fatherless,

v7 And say, Jah will not see, neither will the God of Jacob regard [it].

v8 Understand, ye brutish among the people; and ye fools, when will ye be wise?

v9 He that planted the ear, shall he not hear? He that formed the eye, shall he not see?

v10 He that instructeth the nations, shall not he correct -- he that teacheth man knowledge?

v11 Jehovah knoweth the thoughts of man, that they are vanity.

v12 Blessed is the man whom thou chastenest, O Jah, and whom thou teachest out of thy law;

v13 That thou mayest give him rest from the days of evil, until the pit be digged for the wicked.

v14 For Jehovah will not cast off his people, neither will he forsake his inheritance;

v15 For judgment shall return unto righteousness, and all the upright in heart shall follow it.

v16 Who will rise up for me against the evil-doers? who will stand for me against the workers of iniquity?

v17 If Jehovah had not been my help, my soul had almost dwelt in silence.

v18 When I said, My foot slippeth, thy loving-kindness, O Jehovah, held me up.

v19 In the multitude of my anxious thoughts within me thy comforts have delighted my soul.

v20 Shall the throne of wickedness be united to thee, which frameth mischief into a law?

v21 They band together against the soul of the righteous, and condemn innocent blood.

v22 But Jehovah will be my high tower; and my God the rock of my refuge.

v23 And he will bring upon them their iniquity, and will cut them off in their own evil: Jehovah our God will cut them off.
