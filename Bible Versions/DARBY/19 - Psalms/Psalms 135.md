# Psalms 135 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 134|← Psalms 134]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 136|Psalms 136 →]]

***

v1 Hallelujah! Praise the name of Jehovah; praise, ye servants of Jehovah,

v2 Ye that stand in the house of Jehovah, in the courts of the house of our God.

v3 Praise ye Jah; for Jehovah is good: sing psalms unto his name; for it is pleasant.

v4 For Jah hath chosen Jacob unto himself, Israel for his own possession.

v5 For *I* know that Jehovah is great, and our Lord is above all gods.

v6 Whatsoever Jehovah pleased, he hath done in the heavens and on the earth, in the seas and all deeps;

v7 Who causeth the vapours to ascend from the ends of the earth; who maketh lightnings for the rain; who bringeth the wind out of his treasuries:

v8 Who smote the firstborn of Egypt, both of man and beast;

v9 Who sent signs and miracles into the midst of thee, O Egypt, upon Pharaoh and upon all his servants;

v10 Who smote great nations, and slew mighty kings,

v11 Sihon king of the Amorites, and Og king of Bashan, and all the kingdoms of Canaan;

v12 And gave their land for an inheritance, an inheritance unto Israel his people.

v13 Thy name, O Jehovah, is for ever; thy memorial, O Jehovah, from generation to generation.

v14 For Jehovah will judge his people, and will repent in favour of his servants.

v15 The idols of the nations are silver and gold, the work of men's hands:

v16 They have a mouth, and they speak not; eyes have they, and they see not;

v17 They have ears, and they hear not; neither is there any breath in their mouth.

v18 They that make them are like unto them, -- every one that confideth in them.

v19 House of Israel, bless ye Jehovah; house of Aaron, bless ye Jehovah;

v20 House of Levi, bless ye Jehovah; ye that fear Jehovah, bless Jehovah.

v21 Blessed be Jehovah out of Zion, who dwelleth at Jerusalem! Hallelujah!
