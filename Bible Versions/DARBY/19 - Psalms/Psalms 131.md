# Psalms 131 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 130|← Psalms 130]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 132|Psalms 132 →]]

***

v1 {A Song of degrees. Of David.} Jehovah, my heart is not haughty, nor mine eyes lofty; neither do I exercise myself in great matters, and in things too wonderful for me.

v2 Surely I have restrained and composed my soul, like a weaned child with its mother: my soul within me is as a weaned child.

v3 Let Israel hope in Jehovah, from henceforth and for evermore.
