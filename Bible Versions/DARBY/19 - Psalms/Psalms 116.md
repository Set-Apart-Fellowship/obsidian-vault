# Psalms 116 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 115|← Psalms 115]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 117|Psalms 117 →]]

***

v1 I love Jehovah, for he hath heard my voice [and] my supplications;

v2 For he hath inclined his ear unto me, and I will call upon him during [all] my days.

v3 The bands of death encompassed me, and the anguish of Sheol took hold of me; I found trouble and sorrow:

v4 Then called I upon the name of Jehovah: I beseech thee, Jehovah, deliver my soul.

v5 Gracious is Jehovah and righteous; and our God is merciful.

v6 Jehovah keepeth the simple: I was brought low, and he saved me.

v7 Return unto thy rest, O my soul; for Jehovah hath dealt bountifully with thee.

v8 For thou hast delivered my soul from death, mine eyes from tears, my feet from falling.

v9 I will walk before Jehovah in the land of the living.

v10 I believed, therefore have I spoken. As for me, I was greatly afflicted.

v11 I said in my haste, All men are liars.

v12 What shall I render unto Jehovah, [for] all his benefits toward me?

v13 I will take the cup of salvation, and call upon the name of Jehovah.

v14 I will perform my vows unto Jehovah, yea, before all his people.

v15 Precious in the sight of Jehovah is the death of his saints.

v16 Yea, Jehovah! for I am thy servant; I am thy servant, the son of thy handmaid: thou hast loosed my bonds.

v17 I will offer to thee the sacrifice of thanksgiving, and will call upon the name of Jehovah.

v18 I will perform my vows unto Jehovah, yea, before all his people,

v19 In the courts of Jehovah's house, in the midst of thee, O Jerusalem. Hallelujah!
