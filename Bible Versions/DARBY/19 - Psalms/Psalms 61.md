# Psalms 61 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 60|← Psalms 60]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 62|Psalms 62 →]]

***

v1 {To the chief Musician. On a stringed instrument. [A Psalm] of David.} Hear, O God, my cry; attend unto my prayer.

v2 From the end of the earth will I call unto thee, when my heart is overwhelmed: thou wilt lead me on to a rock which is too high for me.

v3 For thou hast been a refuge for me, a strong tower from before the enemy.

v4 I will sojourn in thy tent for ever; I will take refuge in the covert of thy wings. Selah.

v5 For thou, O God, hast heard my vows; thou hast given [me] the inheritance of those that fear thy name.

v6 Thou wilt add days to the days of the king: his years shall be as many generations.

v7 He shall abook_ide before God for ever: bestow loving-kindness and truth, that they may preserve him.

v8 So will I sing forth thy name for ever, performing my vows from day to day.
