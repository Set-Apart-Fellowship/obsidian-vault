# Psalms 112 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 111|← Psalms 111]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 113|Psalms 113 →]]

***

v1 Hallelujah! Blessed is the man that feareth Jehovah, that delighteth greatly in his commandments.

v2 His seed shall be mighty in the land; the generation of the upright shall be blessed.

v3 Wealth and riches [shall be] in his house; and his righteousness abook_ideth for ever.

v4 Unto the upright there ariseth light in the darkness; he is gracious, and merciful, and righteous.

v5 It is well with the man that is gracious and lendeth; he will sustain his cause in judgment.

v6 For he shall not be moved for ever: the righteous shall be in everlasting remembrance.

v7 He shall not be afraid of evil tidings; his heart is fixed confiding in Jehovah;

v8 His heart is maintained, he is not afraid, until he see [his desire] upon his oppressors.

v9 He scattereth abroad, he giveth to the needy; his righteousness abook_ideth for ever: his horn shall be exalted with honour.

v10 The wicked [man] shall see [it] and be vexed; he shall gnash with his teeth, and melt away: the desire of the wicked shall perish.
