# Psalms 100 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 99|← Psalms 99]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 101|Psalms 101 →]]

***

v1 {A Psalm of thanksgiving.} Shout aloud unto Jehovah, all the earth!

v2 Serve Jehovah with joy: come before his presence with exultation.

v3 Know that Jehovah is God: it is he that hath made us, and not we ourselves; [we are] his people, and the sheep of his pasture.

v4 Enter into his gates with thanksgiving [and] into his courts with praise; give thanks unto him, bless his name:

v5 For Jehovah is good; his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever; and his faithfulness from generation to generation.
