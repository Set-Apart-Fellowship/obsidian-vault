# Psalms 92 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 91|← Psalms 91]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 93|Psalms 93 →]]

***

v1 {A Psalm, a Song, for the Sabbath day.} It is good to give thanks unto Jehovah, and to sing psalms unto thy name, O Most High;

v2 To declare thy loving-kindness in the morning, and thy faithfulness in the nights,

v3 Upon an instrument of ten strings and upon the lute; upon the Higgaion with the harp.

v4 For thou, Jehovah, hast made me glad through thy work; I will triumph in the works of thy hands.

v5 Jehovah, how great are thy works! Thy thoughts are very deep:

v6 A brutish man knoweth not, neither doth a fool understand it.

v7 When the wicked spring as the grass, and when all the workers of iniquity flourish, it is that they may be destroyed for ever.

v8 And thou, Jehovah, art on high for evermore.

v9 For lo, thine enemies, O Jehovah, for lo, thine enemies shall perish; all the workers of iniquity shall be scattered.

v10 But my horn shalt thou exalt like a buffalo's: I shall be anointed with fresh oil.

v11 And mine eye shall see [its desire] on mine enemies; mine ears shall hear [it] of the evil-doers that rise up against me.

v12 The righteous shall shoot forth like a palm-tree; he shall grow like a cedar on Lebanon.

v13 Those that are planted in the house of Jehovah shall flourish in the courts of our God:

v14 They are still vigorous in old age, they are full of sap and green;

v15 To shew that Jehovah is upright: [he is] my rock, and there is no unrighteousness in him.
