# Psalms 91 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 90|← Psalms 90]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 92|Psalms 92 →]]

***

v1 He that dwelleth in the secret place of the Most High shall abook_ide under the shadow of the Almighty.

v2 I say of Jehovah, My refuge and my fortress; my God, I will confide in him.

v3 Surely *he* shall deliver thee from the snare of the fowler, [and] from the destructive pestilence.

v4 He shall cover thee with his feathers, and under his wings shalt thou find refuge: his truth is a shield and buckler.

v5 Thou shalt not be afraid for the terror by night, for the arrow that flieth by day,

v6 For the pestilence that walketh in darkness, for the destruction that wasteth at noonday.

v7 A thousand shall fall at thy side, and ten thousand at thy right hand; [but] it shall not come nigh thee.

v8 Only with thine eyes shalt thou behold, and see the reward of the wicked.

v9 Because *thou* hast made Jehovah, my refuge, the Most High, thy dwelling-place,

v10 There shall no evil befall thee, neither shall any plague come nigh thy tent.

v11 For he shall give his angels charge concerning thee, to keep thee in all thy ways:

v12 They shall bear thee up in [their] hands, lest thou dash thy foot against a stone.

v13 Thou shalt tread upon the lion and the adder; the young lion and the dragon shalt thou trample under foot.

v14 Because he hath set his love upon me, therefore will I deliver him; I will set him on high, because he hath known my name.

v15 He shall call upon me, and I will answer him; I will be with him in trouble, I will deliver him and honour him.

v16 With length of days will I satisfy him, and shew him my salvation.
