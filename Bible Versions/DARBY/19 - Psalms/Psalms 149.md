# Psalms 149 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 148|← Psalms 148]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 150|Psalms 150 →]]

***

v1 Hallelujah! Sing unto Jehovah a new song; [sing] his praise in the congregation of the godly.

v2 Let Israel rejoice in his Maker; let the sons of Zion be joyful in their King.

v3 Let them praise his name in the dance; let them sing psalms unto him with the tambour and harp.

v4 For Jehovah taketh pleasure in his people; he beautifieth the meek with salvation.

v5 Let the godly exult in glory; let them shout for joy upon their beds.

v6 Let the high praises of Ã¹God be in their mouth, and a two-edged sword in their hand:

v7 To execute vengeance against the nations, [and] punishment among the peoples;

v8 To bind their kings with chains, and their nobles with fetters of iron;

v9 To execute upon them the judgment written. This honour have all his saints. Hallelujah!
