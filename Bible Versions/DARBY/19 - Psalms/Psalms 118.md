# Psalms 118 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 117|← Psalms 117]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 119|Psalms 119 →]]

***

v1 Give ye thanks unto Jehovah; for he is good; for his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever.

v2 Oh let Israel say, that his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever.

v3 Oh let the house of Aaron say, that his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever.

v4 Oh let them that fear Jehovah say, that his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever.

v5 I called upon Jah in distress; Jah answered me [and set me] in a large place.

v6 Jehovah is for me, I will not fear; what can man do unto me?

v7 Jehovah is for me among them that help me; and I shall see [my desire] upon them that hate me.

v8 It is better to trust in Jehovah than to put confidence in man;

v9 It is better to trust in Jehovah than to put confidence in nobles.

v10 All nations encompassed me; but in the name of Jehovah have I destroyed them.

v11 They encompassed me, yea, encompassed me; but in the name of Jehovah have I destroyed them.

v12 They encompassed me like bees; they are quenched as the fire of thorns: for in the name of Jehovah have I destroyed them.

v13 Thou hast thrust hard at me that I might fall; but Jehovah helped me.

v14 My strength and song is Jah, and he is become my salvation.

v15 The voice of triumph and salvation is in the tents of the righteous: the right hand of Jehovah doeth valiantly;

v16 The right hand of Jehovah is exalted, the right hand of Jehovah doeth valiantly.

v17 I shall not die, but live, and declare the works of Jah.

v18 Jah hath chastened me sore; but he hath not given me over unto death.

v19 Open to me the gates of righteousness: I will enter into them; Jah will I praise.

v20 This is the gate of Jehovah: the righteous shall enter therein.

v21 I will give thee thanks, for thou hast answered me, and art become my salvation.

v22 [The] stone which the builders rejected hath become the head of the corner:

v23 This is of Jehovah; it is wonderful in our eyes.

v24 This is the day that Jehovah hath made; we will rejoice and be glad in it.

v25 Oh save, Jehovah, I beseech thee; Jehovah, I beseech thee, oh send prosperity!

v26 Blessed be he that cometh in the name of Jehovah. We have blessed you out of the house of Jehovah.

v27 Jehovah is Ã¹God, and he hath given us light: bind the sacrifice with cords, -- up to the horns of the altar.

v28 Thou art my Ã¹God, and I will give thee thanks; my God, I will exalt thee.

v29 Give ye thanks unto Jehovah; for he is good; for his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever.
