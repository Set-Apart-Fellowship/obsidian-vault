# Psalms 108 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 107|← Psalms 107]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 109|Psalms 109 →]]

***

v1 {A Song, a Psalm of David.} My heart is fixed, O God: I will sing, yea, I will sing psalms, even [with] my glory.

v2 Awake, lute and harp: I will wake the dawn.

v3 I will give thee thanks among the peoples, O Jehovah; of thee will I sing psalms among the nations:

v4 For thy loving-kindness is great above the heavens, and thy truth is unto the clouds.

v5 Be thou exalted above the heavens, O God, and thy glory above all the earth.

v6 That thy beloved ones may be delivered: save with thy right hand, and answer me.

v7 God hath spoken in his holiness: I will exult, I will divide Shechem, and mete out the valley of Succoth.

v8 Gilead is mine, Manasseh is mine, and Ephraim is the strength of my head; Judah is my law-giver;

v9 Moab is my wash-pot; upon Edom will I cast my sandal; over Philistia will I shout aloud.

v10 Who will bring me into the strong city? who will lead me unto Edom?

v11 [Wilt] not [thou], O God, who didst cast us off? and didst not go forth, O God, with our armies?

v12 Give us help from trouble; for vain is man's deliverance.

v13 Through God we shall do valiantly; and he it is that will tread down our adversaries.
