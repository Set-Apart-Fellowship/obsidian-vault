# Psalms 64 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 63|← Psalms 63]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 65|Psalms 65 →]]

***

v1 {To the chief Musician. A Psalm of David.} Hear, O God, my voice in my plaint; preserve my life from fear of the enemy:

v2 Hide me from the secret counsel of evil-doers, from the tumultuous crowd of the workers of iniquity,

v3 Who have sharpened their tongue like a sword, [and] have aimed their arrow, a bitter word;

v4 That they may shoot in secret at the perfect: suddenly do they shoot at him, and fear not.

v5 They encourage themselves in an evil matter, they concert to hide snares; they say, Who will see them?

v6 They devise iniquities: We have it ready, the plan is diligently sought out. And each one's inward [thought] and heart is deep.

v7 But God will shoot an arrow at them: suddenly are they wounded;

v8 By their own tongue they are made to fall over one another: all that see them shall flee away.

v9 And all men shall fear, and shall declare God's doing; and they shall wisely consider his work.

v10 The righteous shall rejoice in Jehovah, and trust in him; and all the upright in heart shall glory.
