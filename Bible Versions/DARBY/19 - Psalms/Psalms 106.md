# Psalms 106 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 105|← Psalms 105]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 107|Psalms 107 →]]

***

v1 Hallelujah! Give ye thanks unto Jehovah; for he is good; for his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever.

v2 Who can utter the mighty acts of Jehovah? [who] can shew forth all his praise?

v3 Blessed are they that keep justice, [and] he that doeth righteousness at all times.

v4 Remember me, O Jehovah, with [thy] favour toward thy people; visit me with thy salvation:

v5 That I may see the prosperity of thy chosen ones, that I may rejoice in the joy of thy nation, that I may glory with thine inheritance.

v6 We have sinned with our fathers, we have committed iniquity, we have done wickedly.

v7 Our fathers in Egypt considered not thy wondrous works; they remembered not the multitude of thy loving-kindnesses; but they rebelled at the sea, at the Red Sea.

v8 Yet he saved them for his name's sake, that he might make known his might.

v9 And he rebuked the Red Sea, and it dried up; and he led them through the deeps as through a wilderness.

v10 And he saved them from the hand of him that hated [them], and redeemed them from the hand of the enemy.

v11 And the waters covered their oppressors: there was not one of them left.

v12 Then believed they his words; they sang his praise.

v13 They soon forgot his works; they waited not for his counsel:

v14 And they lusted exceedingly in the wilderness, and tempted Ã¹God in the desert.

v15 Then he gave them their request, but sent leanness into their soul.

v16 And they envied Moses in the camp, [and] Aaron, the saint of Jehovah.

v17 The earth opened and swallowed up Dathan, and covered the company of Abiram;

v18 And fire was kindled in their company; a flame burned up the wicked.

v19 They made a calf in Horeb, and did homage to a molten image;

v20 And they changed their glory into the similitude of an ox that eateth grass.

v21 They forgot Ã¹God their Saviour, who had done great things in Egypt,

v22 Wondrous works in the land of Ham, terrible things by the Red Sea.

v23 And he said that he would destroy them, had not Moses, his chosen, stood before him in the breach, to turn away his fury, lest he should destroy [them].

v24 And they despised the pleasant land; they believed not his word,

v25 But murmured in their tents: they hearkened not unto the voice of Jehovah.

v26 And he lifted up his hand to them, that he would make them fall in the wilderness;

v27 And that he would make their seed fall among the nations, and disperse them through the countries.

v28 And they joined themselves unto Baal-Peor, and ate the sacrifices of the dead;

v29 And they provoked [him] to anger with their doings; and a plague broke out among them.

v30 Then stood up Phinehas and executed judgment, and the plague was stayed;

v31 And that was reckoned unto him for righteousness, from generation to generation, for evermore.

v32 And they moved him to wrath at the waters of Meribah, and it went ill with Moses on their account;

v33 For they provoked his spirit, so that he spoke unadvisedly with his lips.

v34 They did not destroy the peoples, as Jehovah commanded them;

v35 But they mingled with the nations, and learned their works;

v36 And they served their idols; and they were a snare unto them:

v37 And they sacrificed their sons and their daughters unto demons,

v38 And shed innocent blood, the blood of their sons and of their daughters, whom they sacrificed unto the idols of Canaan; and the land was polluted with blood.

v39 And they were defiled with their works, and went a-whoring in their doings.

v40 Then was the anger of Jehovah kindled against his people, and he abhorred his inheritance;

v41 And he gave them into the hand of the nations; and they that hated them ruled over them:

v42 And their enemies oppressed them, and they were brought into subjection under their hand.

v43 Often did he deliver them; but as for them they provoked [him] by their counsel, and they were brought low by their iniquity.

v44 But he regarded their distress, when he heard their cry;

v45 And he remembered for them his covenant, and repented according to the multitude of his loving-kindnesses;

v46 And he caused them to find compassion of all those that had carried them captives.

v47 Save us, Jehovah our God, and gather us from among the nations, to give thanks unto thy holy name, [and] to triumph in thy praise.

v48 Blessed be Jehovah the God of Israel, from eternity and to eternity! And let all the people say, Amen! Hallelujah!
