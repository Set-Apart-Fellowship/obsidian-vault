# Psalms 7 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 6|← Psalms 6]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 8|Psalms 8 →]]

***

v1 {Shiggaion of David, which he sang to Jehovah, concerning the words of Cush the Benjaminite.} Jehovah my God, in thee have I trusted: save me from all my pursuers, and deliver me;

v2 Lest he tear my soul like a lion, crushing it while there is no deliverer.

v3 Jehovah my God, if I have done this, if there be iniquity in my hands;

v4 If I have rewarded evil to him that was at peace with me; (indeed I have freed him that without cause oppressed me;)

v5 Let the enemy pursue after my soul, and take [it], and let him tread down my life to the earth, and lay my glory in the dust. Selah.

v6 Arise, Jehovah, in thine anger; lift thyself up against the raging of mine oppressors, and awake for me: thou hast commanded judgment.

v7 And the assembly of the peoples shall encompass thee; and for their sakes return thou on high.

v8 Jehovah shall minister judgment to the peoples. Judge me, Jehovah, according to my righteousness, and according to mine integrity which is in me.

v9 Oh let the wrong of the wicked come to an end, and establish thou the righteous [man]; even thou that triest the hearts and reins, the righteous God.

v10 My shield is with God, who saveth the upright in heart.

v11 God is a righteous judge, and a Ã¹God who is indignant all the day.

v12 If one turn not, he will sharpen his sword; he hath bent his bow and made it ready,

v13 And he hath prepared for him instruments of death; his arrows hath he made burning.

v14 Behold, he travaileth with iniquity, yea, he hath conceived mischief, and brought forth falsehood:

v15 He digged a pit, and hollowed it out, and is fallen into the hole that he made.

v16 His mischief shall return upon his own head, and his violence shall come down upon his own pate.

v17 I will praise Jehovah according to his righteousness, and will sing forth the name of Jehovah the Most High.
