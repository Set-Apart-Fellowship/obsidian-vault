# Psalms 109 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 108|← Psalms 108]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 110|Psalms 110 →]]

***

v1 {To the chief Musician. Of David. A Psalm.} O God of my praise, be not silent:

v2 For the mouth of the wicked [man] and the mouth of deceit are opened against me: they have spoken against me with a lying tongue,

v3 And with words of hatred have they encompassed me; and they fight against me without a cause.

v4 For my love they are mine adversaries; but I [give myself unto] prayer.

v5 And they have rewarded me evil for good, and hatred for my love.

v6 Set a wicked [man] over him, and let [the] adversary stand at his right hand;

v7 When he shall be judged, let him go out guilty, and let his prayer become sin;

v8 Let his days be few, let another take his office;

v9 Let his sons be fatherless, and his wife a widow;

v10 Let his sons be vagabonds and beg, and let them seek [their bread] far from their desolate places;

v11 Let the usurer cast the net over all that he hath, and let strangers despoil his labour;

v12 Let there be none to extend kindness unto him, neither let there be any to favour his fatherless children;

v13 Let his posterity be cut off; in the generation following let their name be blotted out:

v14 Let the iniquity of his fathers be remembered with Jehovah, and let not the sin of his mother be blotted out;

v15 Let them be before Jehovah continually, that he may cut off the memory of them from the earth:

v16 Because he remembered not to shew kindness, but persecuted the afflicted and needy man, and the broken in heart, to slay him.

v17 And he loved cursing; so let it come unto him. And he delighted not in blessing; and let it be far from him.

v18 And he clothed himself with cursing like his vestment; so let it come into his bowels like water, and like oil into his bones;

v19 Let it be unto him as a garment with which he covereth himself, and for a girdle wherewith he is constantly girded.

v20 Let this be the reward of mine adversaries from Jehovah, and of them that speak evil against my soul.

v21 But do *thou* for me, Jehovah, Lord, for thy name's sake; because thy loving-kindness is good, deliver me:

v22 For I am afflicted and needy, and my heart is wounded within me.

v23 I am gone like a shadow when it lengtheneth; I am tossed about like the locust;

v24 My knees are failing through fasting, and my flesh hath lost its fatness;

v25 And I am become a reproach unto them; [when] they look upon me they shake their heads.

v26 Help me, Jehovah my God; save me according to thy loving-kindness:

v27 That they may know that this is thy hand; that *thou*, Jehovah, hast done it.

v28 Let *them* curse, but bless *thou*; when they rise up, let them be ashamed, and let thy servant rejoice.

v29 Let mine adversaries be clothed with confusion, and let them cover themselves with their shame as with a mantle.

v30 I will greatly celebrate Jehovah with my mouth; yea, I will praise him among the multitude.

v31 For he standeth at the right hand of the needy, to save him from those that judge his soul.
