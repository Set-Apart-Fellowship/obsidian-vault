# Psalms 124 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 123|← Psalms 123]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 125|Psalms 125 →]]

***

v1 {A Song of degrees. Of David.} If it had not been Jehovah who was for us -- oh let Israel say --

v2 If it had not been Jehovah who was for us, when men rose up against us,

v3 Then they had swallowed us up alive, when their anger was kindled against us;

v4 Then the waters had overwhelmed us, a torrent had gone over our soul;

v5 Then the proud waters had gone over our soul.

v6 Blessed be Jehovah, who gave us not up a prey to their teeth!

v7 Our soul is escaped as a bird out of the snare of the fowlers: the snare is broken, and we have escaped.

v8 Our help is in the name of Jehovah, the maker of heavens and earth.
