# Psalms 21 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 20|← Psalms 20]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 22|Psalms 22 →]]

***

v1 {To the chief Musician. A Psalm of David.} The king shall joy in thy strength, Jehovah; and in thy salvation how greatly shall he rejoice.

v2 Thou hast given him his heart's desire, and hast not withholden the request of his lips. Selah.

v3 For thou hast met him with the blessings of goodness; thou hast set a crown of pure gold on his head.

v4 He asked life of thee; thou gavest [it] him, length of days for ever and ever.

v5 His glory is great through thy salvation; majesty and splendour hast thou laid upon him.

v6 For thou hast made him to be blessings for ever; thou hast filled him with joy by thy countenance.

v7 For the king confideth in Jehovah: and through the loving-kindness of the Most High he shall not be moved.

v8 Thy hand shall find out all thine enemies; thy right hand shall find out those that hate thee.

v9 Thou shalt make them as a fiery furnace in the time of thy presence; Jehovah shall swallow them up in his anger, and the fire shall devour them:

v10 Their fruit shalt thou destroy from the earth, and their seed from among the children of men.

v11 For they intended evil against thee; they imagined a mischievous device, which they could not execute.

v12 For thou wilt make them turn their back; thou wilt make ready thy bowstring against their face.

v13 Be thou exalted, Jehovah, in thine own strength: we will sing and celebrate thy power.
