# Psalms 29 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 28|← Psalms 28]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 30|Psalms 30 →]]

***

v1 {A Psalm of David.} Give unto Jehovah, ye sons of the mighty ones, give unto Jehovah glory and strength;

v2 Give unto Jehovah the glory of his name; worship Jehovah in holy splendour.

v3 The voice of Jehovah is upon the waters: the Ã¹God of glory thundereth, -- Jehovah upon great waters.

v4 The voice of Jehovah is powerful; the voice of Jehovah is full of majesty.

v5 The voice of Jehovah breaketh cedars; yea, Jehovah breaketh the cedars of Lebanon:

v6 And he maketh them to skip like a calf, Lebanon and Sirion like a young buffalo.

v7 The voice of Jehovah cleaveth out flames of fire.

v8 The voice of Jehovah shaketh the wilderness; Jehovah shaketh the wilderness of Kadesh.

v9 The voice of Jehovah maketh the hinds to calve, and layeth bare the forests; and in his temple doth every one say, Glory!

v10 Jehovah sitteth upon the flood; yea, Jehovah sitteth as king for ever.

v11 Jehovah will give strength unto his people; Jehovah will bless his people with peace.
