# Psalms 114 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 113|← Psalms 113]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 115|Psalms 115 →]]

***

v1 When Israel went out of Egypt, the house of Jacob from a people of strange language,

v2 Judah was his sanctuary, Israel his dominion.

v3 The sea saw it and fled, the Jordan turned back;

v4 The mountains skipped like rams, the hills like lambs.

v5 What ailed thee, thou sea, that thou fleddest? thou Jordan, that thou turnedst back?

v6 Ye mountains, that ye skipped like rams? ye hills, like lambs?

v7 Tremble, thou earth, at the presence of the Lord, at the presence of the +God of Jacob,

v8 Who turned the rock into a pool of water, the flint into a fountain of waters.
