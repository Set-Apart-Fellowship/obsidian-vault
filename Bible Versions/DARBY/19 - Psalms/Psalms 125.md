# Psalms 125 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 124|← Psalms 124]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 126|Psalms 126 →]]

***

v1 {A Song of degrees.} They that confide in Jehovah are as mount Zion, which cannot be moved; it abook_ideth for ever.

v2 Jerusalem! -- mountains are round about her, and Jehovah is round about his people, from henceforth and for evermore.

v3 For the sceptre of wickedness shall not rest upon the lot of the righteous; lest the righteous put forth their hands unto iniquity.

v4 Do good, O Jehovah, unto the good, and to them that are upright in their hearts.

v5 But as for such as turn aside unto their crooked ways, Jehovah will lead them forth with the workers of iniquity. Peace be upon Israel!
