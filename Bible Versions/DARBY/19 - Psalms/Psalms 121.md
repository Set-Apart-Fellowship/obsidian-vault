# Psalms 121 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 120|← Psalms 120]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 122|Psalms 122 →]]

***

v1 {A Song of degrees.} I lift up mine eyes unto the mountains: whence shall my help come?

v2 My help [cometh] from Jehovah, who made the heavens and the earth.

v3 He will not suffer thy foot to be moved; he that keepeth thee will not slumber.

v4 Behold, he that keepeth Israel will neither slumber nor sleep.

v5 Jehovah is thy keeper, Jehovah is thy shade upon thy right hand;

v6 The sun shall not smite thee by day, nor the moon by night.

v7 Jehovah will keep thee from all evil; he will keep thy soul.

v8 Jehovah will keep thy going out and thy coming in, from henceforth and for evermore.
