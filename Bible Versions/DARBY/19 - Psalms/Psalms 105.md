# Psalms 105 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 104|← Psalms 104]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 106|Psalms 106 →]]

***

v1 Give ye thanks unto Jehovah, call upon his name; make known his acts among the peoples.

v2 Sing unto him, sing psalms unto him; meditate upon all his wondrous works.

v3 Glory ye in his holy name: let the heart of them rejoice that seek Jehovah.

v4 Seek Jehovah and his strength, seek his face continually;

v5 Remember his wondrous works which he hath done, his miracles and the judgments of his mouth:

v6 Ye seed of Abraham his servant, ye sons of Jacob, his chosen ones.

v7 He, Jehovah, is our God; his judgments are in all the earth.

v8 He is ever mindful of his covenant, -- the word which he commanded to a thousand generations, --

v9 Which he made with Abraham, and of his oath unto Isaac;

v10 And he confirmed it unto Jacob for a statute, unto Israel for an everlasting covenant,

v11 Saying, Unto thee will I give the land of Canaan, the lot of your inheritance;

v12 When they were a few men in number, of small account, and strangers in it.

v13 And they went from nation to nation, from one kingdom to another people.

v14 He suffered no man to oppress them, and reproved kings for their sakes,

v15 [Saying,] Touch not mine anointed ones, and do my prophets no harm.

v16 And he called for a famine upon the land; he broke the whole staff of bread.

v17 He sent a man before them: Joseph was sold for a bondman.

v18 They afflicted his feet with fetters; his soul came into irons;

v19 Until the time when what he said came about: the word of Jehovah tried him.

v20 The king sent and loosed him -- the ruler of peoples -- and let him go free.

v21 He made him lord of his house, and ruler over all his possessions:

v22 To bind his princes at his pleasure, and teach his elders wisdom.

v23 And Israel came into Egypt, and Jacob sojourned in the land of Ham.

v24 And he made his people exceeding fruitful, and made them mightier than their oppressors.

v25 He turned their heart to hate his people, to deal subtilly with his servants.

v26 He sent Moses his servant, [and] Aaron whom he had chosen:

v27 They set his signs among them, and miracles in the land of Ham.

v28 He sent darkness, and made it dark; and they rebelled not against his word.

v29 He turned their waters into blood, and caused their fish to die.

v30 Their land swarmed with frogs, -- in the chambers of their kings.

v31 He spoke, and there came dog-flies, [and] gnats in all their borders.

v32 He gave them hail for rain, [and] flaming fire in their land;

v33 And he smote their vines and their fig-trees, and broke the trees of their borders.

v34 He spoke, and the locust came, and the cankerworm, even without number;

v35 And they devoured every herb in their land, and ate up the fruit of their ground.

v36 And he smote every firstborn in their land, the firstfruits of all their vigour.

v37 And he brought them forth with silver and gold; and there was not one feeble among their tribes.

v38 Egypt rejoiced at their departure; for the fear of them had fallen upon them.

v39 He spread a cloud for a covering, and fire to give light in the night.

v40 They asked, and he brought quails, and satisfied them with the bread of heaven.

v41 He opened the rock, and waters gushed forth; they ran in the dry places [like] a river.

v42 For he remembered his holy word, [and] Abraham his servant;

v43 And he brought forth his people with gladness, his chosen with rejoicing;

v44 And he gave them the lands of the nations, and they took possession of the labour of the peoples:

v45 That they might keep his statutes, and observe his laws. Hallelujah!
