# Psalms 87 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 86|← Psalms 86]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 88|Psalms 88 →]]

***

v1 {Of the sons of Korah. A Psalm. A Song.} His foundation is in the mountains of holiness.

v2 Jehovah loveth the gates of Zion more than all the habitations of Jacob.

v3 Glorious things are spoken of thee, O city of God. Selah.

v4 I will make mention of Rahab and Babylon among them that know me; behold Philistia, and Tyre, with Ethiopia: this [man] was born there.

v5 And of Zion it shall be said, This one and that one was born in her; and the Most High himself shall establish her.

v6 Jehovah will count, when he inscribeth the peoples, This [man] was born there. Selah.

v7 As well the singers as the dancers [shall say], All my springs are in thee.
