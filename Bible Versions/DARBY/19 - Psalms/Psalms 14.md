# Psalms 14 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 13|← Psalms 13]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 15|Psalms 15 →]]

***

v1 {To the chief Musician. [A Psalm] of David.} The fool hath said in his heart, There is no God. They have corrupted themselves, they have done abominable works: there is none that doeth good.

v2 Jehovah looked down from the heavens upon the children of men, to see if there were any that did understand, that did seek God.

v3 They have all gone aside, they are together become corrupt: there is none that doeth good, not even one.

v4 Have all the workers of iniquity no knowledge, eating up my people [as] they eat bread? They call not upon Jehovah.

v5 There were they in great fear; for God is in the generation of the righteous.

v6 Ye have shamed the counsel of the afflicted, because Jehovah [was] his refuge.

v7 Oh that the salvation of Israel were come out of Zion! When Jehovah turneth again the captivity of his people, Jacob shall be glad, Israel shall rejoice.
