# Psalms 80 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 79|← Psalms 79]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 81|Psalms 81 →]]

***

v1 {To the chief Musician. On Shoshannim-Eduth. Of Asaph. A Psalm.} Give ear, O Shepherd of Israel, thou that leadest Joseph like a flock; thou that sittest [between] the cherubim, shine forth.

v2 Before Ephraim and Benjamin and Manasseh, stir up thy strength, and come to our deliverance.

v3 O God, restore us; and cause thy face to shine, and we shall be saved.

v4 Jehovah, God of hosts, how long will thine anger smoke against the prayer of thy people?

v5 Thou hast fed them with the bread of tears, and given them tears to drink in large measure:

v6 Thou hast made us a strife unto our neighbours, and our enemies mock among themselves.

v7 Restore us, O God of hosts; and cause thy face to shine, and we shall be saved.

v8 Thou broughtest a vine out of Egypt; thou didst cast out the nations, and plant it:

v9 Thou preparedst space before it, and it took deep root, and filled the land;

v10 The mountains were covered with its shadow, and the branches thereof were [like] cedars of Ã¹God;

v11 It sent out its boughs unto the sea, and its shoots unto the river.

v12 Why hast thou broken down its fences, so that all who pass by the way do pluck it?

v13 The boar out of the forest doth waste it, and the beast of the field doth feed off it.

v14 O God of hosts, return, we beseech thee; look down from the heavens, and behold, and visit this vine;

v15 Even the stock which thy right hand hath planted, and the young plant thou madest strong for thyself.

v16 It is burned with fire, it is cut down; they perish at the rebuke of thy countenance.

v17 Let thy hand be upon the man of thy right hand, upon the son of man whom thou hast made strong for thyself.

v18 So will we not go back from thee. Revive us, and we will call upon thy name.

v19 Restore us, O Jehovah, God of hosts; cause thy face to shine, and we shall be saved.
