# Psalms 76 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 75|← Psalms 75]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 77|Psalms 77 →]]

***

v1 {To the chief Musician. On stringed instruments. A Psalm of Asaph: a Song.} In Judah is God known, his name is great in Israel;

v2 And in Salem is his tent, and his dwelling-place in Zion.

v3 There broke he the flashings of the bow, shield and sword and battle. Selah.

v4 More glorious art thou, more excellent, than the mountains of prey.

v5 The stout-hearted are made a spoil, they have slept their sleep; and none of the men of might have found their hands.

v6 At thy rebuke, O God of Jacob, both chariot and horse are cast into a dead sleep.

v7 Thou, thou art to be feared, and who can stand before thee when once thou art angry?

v8 Thou didst cause judgment to be heard from the heavens; the earth feared, and was still,

v9 When God rose up to judgment, to save all the meek of the earth. Selah.

v10 For the fury of man shall praise thee; the remainder of fury wilt thou gird on thyself.

v11 Vow and pay unto Jehovah your God: let all that are round about him bring presents unto him that is to be feared.

v12 He cutteth off the spirit of princes; [he] is terrible to the kings of the earth.
