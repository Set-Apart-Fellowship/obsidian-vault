# Psalms 18 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 17|← Psalms 17]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 19|Psalms 19 →]]

***

v1 {To the chief Musician. [A Psalm] of David, the servant of Jehovah, who spoke to Jehovah the words of this song in the day that Jehovah had delivered him out of the hand of all his enemies and out of the hand of Saul. And he said,} I will love thee, O Jehovah, my strength.

v2 Jehovah is my rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer; my Ã¹God, my rock, in whom I will trust; my shield, and the horn of my salvation, my high tower.

v3 I will call upon Jehovah, who is to be praised: so shall I be saved from mine enemies.

v4 The bands of death encompassed me, and torrents of Belial made me afraid.

v5 The bands of Sheol surrounded me, the cords of death encountered me.

v6 In my distress I called upon Jehovah, and I cried out to my God; he heard my voice out of his temple, and my cry came before him, into his ears.

v7 Then the earth shook and quaked, and the foundations of the mountains trembled and shook, because he was wroth.

v8 There went up a smoke out of his nostrils, and fire out of his mouth devoured: coals burned forth from it.

v9 And he bowed the heavens, and came down; and darkness was under his feet.

v10 And he rode upon a cherub and did fly; yea, he flew fast upon the wings of the wind.

v11 He made darkness his secret place, his tent round about him: darkness of waters, thick clouds of the skies.

v12 From the brightness before him his thick clouds passed forth: hail and coals of fire.

v13 And Jehovah thundered in the heavens, and the Most High uttered his voice: hail and coals of fire.

v14 And he sent his arrows, and scattered [mine enemies]; and he shot forth lightnings, and discomfited them.

v15 And the beds of the waters were seen, and the foundations of the world were uncovered at thy rebuke, Jehovah, at the blast of the breath of thy nostrils.

v16 He reached forth from above, he took me, he drew me out of great waters:

v17 He delivered me from my strong enemy, and from them that hated me; for they were mightier than I.

v18 They encountered me in the day of my calamity, but Jehovah was my stay.

v19 And he brought me forth into a large place; he delivered me, because he delighted in me.

v20 Jehovah hath rewarded me according to my righteousness; according to the cleanness of my hands hath he recompensed me.

v21 For I have kept the ways of Jehovah, and have not wickedly departed from my God.

v22 For all his ordinances were before me, and I did not put away his statutes from me;

v23 And I was upright with him, and kept myself from mine iniquity.

v24 And Jehovah hath recompensed me according to my righteousness, according to the cleanness of my hands in his sight.

v25 With the gracious thou dost shew thyself gracious; with the upright man thou dost shew thyself upright;

v26 With the pure thou dost shew thyself pure; and with the perverse thou dost shew thyself contrary.

v27 For it is thou that savest the afflicted people; but the haughty eyes wilt thou bring down.

v28 For it is thou that makest my lamp to shine: Jehovah my God enlighteneth my darkness.

v29 For by thee I have run through a troop; and by my God have I leaped over a wall.

v30 As for Ã¹God, his way is perfect; the word of Jehovah is tried: he is a shield to all that trust in him.

v31 For who is +God save Jehovah? and who is a rock if not our God?

v32 The Ã¹God who girdeth me with strength, and maketh my way perfect,

v33 Who maketh my feet like hinds' [feet], and setteth me upon my high places;

v34 Who teacheth my hands to war, and mine arms bend a bow of brass;

v35 And thou didst give me the shield of thy salvation, and thy right hand held me up; and thy condescending gentleness hath made me great.

v36 Thou didst enlarge my steps under me, and mine ankles have not wavered.

v37 I pursued mine enemies, and overtook them; and I turned not again till they were consumed.

v38 I crushed them, and they were not able to rise: they fell under my feet.

v39 And thou girdedst me with strength to battle; thou didst subdue under me those that rose up against me.

v40 And mine enemies didst thou make to turn their backs unto me, and those that hated me I destroyed.

v41 They cried, and there was none to save; -- unto Jehovah, and he answered them not.

v42 And I did beat them small as dust before the wind; I did cast them out as the mire of the streets.

v43 Thou hast delivered me from the strivings of the people; thou hast made me the head of the nations: a people I knew not doth serve me.

v44 At the hearing of the ear, they obey me: strangers come cringing unto me.

v45 Strangers have faded away, and they come trembling forth from their close places.

v46 Jehovah liveth; and blessed be my rock; and exalted be the God of my salvation,

v47 The Ã¹God who hath avenged me, and hath subjected the peoples to me;

v48 Who hath delivered me from mine enemies: yea, thou hast lifted me up above them that rose up against me; from the man of violence hast thou delivered me.

v49 Therefore will I give thanks to thee, Jehovah, among the nations, and will sing psalms to thy name.

v50 [It is he] who giveth great deliverances to his king, and sheweth loving-kindness to his anointed, to David, and to his seed for evermore.
