# Psalms 78 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 77|← Psalms 77]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 79|Psalms 79 →]]

***

v1 {An instruction. Of Asaph.} Give ear, O my people, to my law; incline your ears to the words of my mouth.

v2 I will open my mouth in a parable; I will utter riddles from of old,

v3 Which we have heard and known, and our fathers have told us:

v4 We will not hide [them] from their sons, shewing forth to the generation to come the praises of Jehovah, and his strength, and his marvellous works which he hath done.

v5 For he established a testimony in Jacob, and appointed a law in Israel, which he commanded our fathers, that they should make them known to their children;

v6 That the generation to come might know [them], the children that should be born; that they might rise up and tell [them] to their children,

v7 And that they might set their hope in God, and not forget the works of Ã¹God, but observe his commandments;

v8 And might not be as their fathers, a stubborn and rebellious generation, a generation that prepared not their heart, and whose spirit was not stedfast with Ã¹God.

v9 The sons of Ephraim, armed bowmen, turned back in the day of battle.

v10 They kept not the covenant of God, and refused to walk in his law;

v11 And forgot his doings, and his marvellous works which he had shewn them.

v12 In the sight of their fathers had he done wonders, in the land of Egypt, the field of Zoan.

v13 He clave the sea, and caused them to pass through; and made the waters to stand as a heap;

v14 And he led them with a cloud in the daytime, and all the night with the light of fire.

v15 He clave rocks in the wilderness, and gave [them] drink as out of the depths, abundantly;

v16 And he brought streams out of the rock, and caused waters to run down like rivers.

v17 Yet they still went on sinning against him, provoking the Most High in the desert;

v18 And they tempted Ã¹God in their heart, by asking meat for their lust;

v19 And they spoke against God: they said, Is Ã¹God able to prepare a table in the wilderness?

v20 Behold, he smote the rock, and waters gushed out, and streams overflowed; is he able to give bread also, or provide flesh for his people?

v21 Therefore Jehovah heard, and was wroth; and fire was kindled against Jacob, and anger also went up against Israel:

v22 Because they believed not in God, and confided not in his salvation;

v23 Though he had commanded the clouds from above, and had opened the doors of the heavens,

v24 And had rained down manna upon them to eat, and had given them the corn of the heavens.

v25 Man did eat the bread of the mighty; he sent them provision to the full.

v26 He caused the east wind to rise in the heavens, and by his strength he brought the south wind;

v27 And he rained flesh upon them as dust, and feathered fowl as the sand of the seas,

v28 And he let it fall in the midst of their camp, round about their habitations:

v29 And they did eat, and were well filled; for that they lusted after, he brought to them.

v30 They were not alienated from their lust, their meat was yet in their mouths,

v31 When the anger of God went up against them; and he slew the fattest of them, and smote down the chosen men of Israel.

v32 For all this, they sinned still, and believed not in his marvellous works;

v33 And he consumed their days in vanity, and their years in terror.

v34 When he slew them, then they sought him, and returned and sought early after Ã¹God;

v35 And they remembered that God was their rock, and Ã¹God, the Most High, their redeemer.

v36 But they flattered him with their mouth, and lied unto him with their tongue;

v37 For their heart was not firm toward him, neither were they stedfast in his covenant.

v38 But he was merciful: he forgave the iniquity, and destroyed [them] not; but many a time turned he his anger away, and did not stir up all his fury:

v39 And he remembered that they were flesh, a breath that passeth away and cometh not again.

v40 How oft did they provoke him in the wilderness, and grieve him in the desert!

v41 And they turned again and tempted Ã¹God, and grieved the Holy One of Israel.

v42 They remembered not his hand, the day when he delivered them from the oppressor,

v43 How he set his signs in Egypt, and his miracles in the field of Zoan;

v44 And turned their rivers into blood, and their streams, that they could not drink;

v45 He sent dog-flies among them, which devoured them, and frogs, which destroyed them;

v46 And he gave their increase unto the caterpillar, and their labour unto the locust;

v47 He killed their vines with hail, and their sycamore trees with hail-stones;

v48 And he delivered up their cattle to the hail, and their flocks to thunderbolts.

v49 He cast upon them the fierceness of his anger, wrath, and indignation, and distress, -- a mission of angels of woes.

v50 He made a way for his anger; he spared not their soul from death, but gave their life over to the pestilence;

v51 And he smote all the firstborn in Egypt, the first-fruits of their vigour in the tents of Ham.

v52 And he made his own people to go forth like sheep, and guided them in the wilderness like a flock;

v53 And he led them safely, so that they were without fear; and the sea covered their enemies.

v54 And he brought them to his holy border, this mountain, which his right hand purchased;

v55 And he drove out the nations before them, and allotted them for an inheritance by line, and made the tribes of Israel to dwell in their tents.

v56 But they tempted and provoked God, the Most High, and kept not his testimonies,

v57 And they drew back and dealt treacherously like their fathers: they turned like a deceitful bow.

v58 And they provoked him to anger with their high places, and moved him to jealousy with their graven images.

v59 God heard, and was wroth, and greatly abhorred Israel:

v60 And he forsook the tabernacle at Shiloh, the tent where he had dwelt among men,

v61 And gave his strength into captivity, and his glory into the hand of the oppressor;

v62 And delivered up his people unto the sword, and was very wroth with his inheritance:

v63 The fire consumed their young men, and their maidens were not praised in [nuptial] song;

v64 Their priests fell by the sword, and their widows made no lamentation.

v65 Then the Lord awoke as one out of sleep, like a mighty man that shouteth aloud by reason of wine;

v66 And he smote his adversaries in the hinder part, and put them to everlasting reproach.

v67 And he rejected the tent of Joseph, and chose not the tribe of Ephraim,

v68 But chose the tribe of Judah, the mount Zion which he loved;

v69 And he built his sanctuary like the heights, like the earth which he hath founded for ever.

v70 And he chose David his servant, and took him from the sheepfolds:

v71 From following the suckling-ewes, he brought him to feed Jacob his people, and Israel his inheritance.

v72 And he fed them according to the integrity of his heart, and led them by the skilfulness of his hands.
