# Psalms 93 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 92|← Psalms 92]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 94|Psalms 94 →]]

***

v1 Jehovah reigneth, he hath clothed himself with majesty: Jehovah hath clothed himself, he hath girded himself with strength; yea, the world is established, it shall not be moved.

v2 Thy throne is established of old; thou art from eternity.

v3 The floods lifted up, O Jehovah, the floods lifted up their voice; the floods lifted up their roaring waves.

v4 Jehovah on high is mightier than the voices of many waters, than the mighty breakers of the sea.

v5 Thy testimonies are very sure: holiness becometh thy house, O Jehovah, for ever.
