# Psalms 126 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 125|← Psalms 125]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 127|Psalms 127 →]]

***

v1 {A Song of degrees.} When Jehovah turned the captivity of Zion, we were like them that dream.

v2 Then was our mouth filled with laughter, and our tongue with rejoicing: then said they among the nations, Jehovah hath done great things for them.

v3 Jehovah hath done great things for us; [and] we are joyful.

v4 Turn our captivity, O Jehovah, as the streams in the south.

v5 They that sow in tears shall reap with rejoicing:

v6 He goeth forth and weepeth, bearing seed for scattering; he cometh again with rejoicing, bearing his sheaves.
