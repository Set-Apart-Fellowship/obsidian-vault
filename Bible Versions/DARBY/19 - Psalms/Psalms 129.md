# Psalms 129 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 128|← Psalms 128]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 130|Psalms 130 →]]

***

v1 {A Song of degrees.} Many a time have they afflicted me from my youth -- oh let Israel say --

v2 Many a time have they afflicted me from my youth; yet they have not prevailed against me.

v3 The ploughers ploughed upon my back; they made long their furrows.

v4 Jehovah is righteous: he hath cut asunder the cords of the wicked.

v5 Let them be ashamed and turned backward, all that hate Zion;

v6 Let them be as the grass upon the house-tops, which withereth before it is plucked up,

v7 Wherewith the mower filleth not his hand, nor he that bindeth sheaves his bosom;

v8 Neither do the passers-by say, The blessing of Jehovah be upon you; we bless you in the name of Jehovah!
