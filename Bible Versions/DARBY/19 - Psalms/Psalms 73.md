# Psalms 73 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 72|← Psalms 72]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 74|Psalms 74 →]]

***

v1 {A Psalm of Asaph.} Truly God is good to Israel, to such as are of a pure heart.

v2 But as for me, my feet were almost gone, my steps had well nigh slipped;

v3 For I was envious at the arrogant, seeing the prosperity of the wicked.

v4 For they have no pangs in their death, and their body is well nourished;

v5 They have not the hardships of mankind, neither are they plagued like [other] men:

v6 Therefore pride encompasseth them as a neck-chain, violence covereth them [as] a garment;

v7 Their eyes stand out from fatness, they exceed the imaginations of their heart:

v8 They mock and speak wickedly of oppression, they speak loftily:

v9 They set their mouth in the heavens, and their tongue walketh through the earth.

v10 Therefore his people turn hither, and waters in fulness are wrung out to them.

v11 And they say, How can Ã¹God know, and is there knowledge in the Most High?

v12 Behold, these are the wicked, and they prosper in the world: they heap up riches.

v13 Truly have I purified my heart in vain, and washed my hands in innocency:

v14 For all the day have I been plagued, and chastened every morning.

v15 If I said, I will speak thus, behold, I should be faithless to the generation of thy children.

v16 When I thought to be able to know this, it was a grievous task in mine eyes;

v17 Until I went into the sanctuaries of Ã¹God; [then] understood I their end.

v18 Truly thou settest them in slippery places, thou castest them down in ruins.

v19 How are they suddenly made desolate! they pass away, consumed with terrors.

v20 As a dream, when one awaketh, wilt thou, Lord, on arising despise their image.

v21 When my heart was in a ferment, and I was pricked in my reins,

v22 Then I was brutish and knew nothing; I was [as] a beast with thee.

v23 Nevertheless I am continually with thee: thou hast holden my right hand;

v24 Thou wilt guide me by thy counsel, and after the glory, thou wilt receive me.

v25 Whom have I in the heavens? and there is none upon earth I desire beside thee.

v26 My flesh and my heart faileth: God is the rock of my heart and my portion for ever.

v27 For behold, they that are far from thee shall perish; thou destroyest every one that goeth a whoring from thee.

v28 But as for me, it is good for me to draw near to God: I have put my trust in the Lord Jehovah, that I may declare all thy works.
