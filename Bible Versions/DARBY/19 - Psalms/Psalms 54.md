# Psalms 54 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 53|← Psalms 53]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 55|Psalms 55 →]]

***

v1 {To the chief Musician. On stringed instruments: an instruction. Of David; when the Ziphites came, and said to Saul, Is not David hiding himself with us?} O God, by thy name save me, and by thy strength do me justice.

v2 O God, hear my prayer; give ear to the words of my mouth.

v3 For strangers are risen up against me, and the violent seek after my life: they have not set God before them. Selah.

v4 Behold, God is my helper; the Lord is among them that uphold my soul.

v5 He will requite evil to mine enemies: in thy truth cut them off.

v6 I will freely sacrifice unto thee; I will praise thy name, O Jehovah, because it is good.

v7 For he hath delivered me out of all trouble; and mine eye hath seen [its desire] upon mine enemies.
