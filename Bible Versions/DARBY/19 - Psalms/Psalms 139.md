# Psalms 139 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 138|← Psalms 138]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 140|Psalms 140 →]]

***

v1 {To the chief Musician. A Psalm of David.} Jehovah, thou hast searched me, and known [me].

v2 *Thou* knowest my down-sitting and mine uprising, thou understandest my thought afar off;

v3 Thou searchest out my path and my lying down, and art acquainted with all my ways;

v4 For there is not yet a word on my tongue, [but] lo, O Jehovah, thou knowest it altogether.

v5 Thou hast beset me behind and before, and laid thy hand upon me.

v6 O knowledge too wonderful for me! it is high, I cannot [attain] unto it.

v7 Whither shall I go from thy Spirit? and whither flee from thy presence?

v8 If I ascend up into the heavens thou art there; or if I make my bed in Sheol, behold, thou [art there];

v9 [If] I take the wings of the dawn [and] dwell in the uttermost parts of the sea,

v10 Even there shall thy hand lead me, and thy right hand shall hold me.

v11 And if I say, Surely darkness shall cover me, and the light about me be night;

v12 Even darkness hideth not from thee, and the night shineth as the day: the darkness is as the light.

v13 For thou hast possessed my reins; thou didst cover me in my mother's womb.

v14 I will praise thee, for I am fearfully, wonderfully made. Marvellous are thy works; and [that] my soul knoweth right well.

v15 My bones were not hidden from thee when I was made in secret, curiously wrought in the lower parts of the earth.

v16 Thine eyes did see my unformed substance, and in thy book all [my members] were written; [during many] days were they fashioned, when [as yet] there was none of them.

v17 But how precious are thy thoughts unto me, O Ã¹God! how great is the sum of them!

v18 [If] I would count them, they are more in number than the sand. When I awake, I am still with thee.

v19 Oh that thou wouldest slay the wicked, O +God! And ye men of blood, depart from me.

v20 For they speak of thee wickedly, they take [thy name] in vain, thine enemies.

v21 Do not I hate them, O Jehovah, that hate thee? and do not I loathe them that rise up against thee?

v22 I hate them with perfect hatred; I account them mine enemies.

v23 Search me, O Ã¹God, and know my heart; prove me, and know my thoughts;

v24 And see if there be any grievous way in me; and lead me in the way everlasting.
