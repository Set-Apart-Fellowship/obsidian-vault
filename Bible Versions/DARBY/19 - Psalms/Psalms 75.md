# Psalms 75 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 74|← Psalms 74]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 76|Psalms 76 →]]

***

v1 {To the chief Musician. 'Destroy not.' A Psalm of Asaph: a Song.} Unto thee we give thanks, O God, we give thanks; and thy name is near: thy marvellous works declare it.

v2 When I shall receive the assembly, I will judge with equity.

v3 The earth and all the inhabitants thereof are dissolved: I have established its pillars. Selah.

v4 I said unto the boastful, Boast not; and to the wicked, Lift not up the horn:

v5 Lift not up your horn on high; speak not arrogantly with a [stiff] neck.

v6 For not from the east nor from the west, nor yet from the south doth exaltation [come]:

v7 For God is the judge; he putteth down one and exalteth another.

v8 For in the hand of Jehovah there is a cup, and it foameth with wine, it is full of mixture; and he poureth out of the same; yea, the dregs thereof shall all the wicked of the earth drain off, [and] drink.

v9 But as for me, I will declare for ever; I will sing psalms to the God of Jacob.

v10 And all the horns of the wicked will I cut off; [but] the horns of the righteous shall be exalted.
