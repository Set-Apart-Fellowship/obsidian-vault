# Psalms 144 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 143|← Psalms 143]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 145|Psalms 145 →]]

***

v1 {[A Psalm] of David.} Blessed be Jehovah my rock, who teacheth my hands to war, my fingers to fight;

v2 My mercy and my fortress, my high tower and my deliverer, my shield and he in whom I trust, who subdueth my people under me!

v3 Jehovah, what is man, that thou takest knowledge of him, the son of man, that thou takest thought of him?

v4 Man is like to vanity; his days are as a shadow that passeth away.

v5 Jehovah, bow thy heavens, and come down; touch the mountains, that they smoke;

v6 Cast forth lightnings, and scatter them; send forth thine arrows, and discomfit them:

v7 Stretch out thy hands from above; rescue me, and deliver me out of great waters, from the hand of aliens,

v8 Whose mouth speaketh vanity, and their right hand is a right hand of falsehood.

v9 O God, I will sing a new song unto thee; with the ten-stringed lute will I sing psalms unto thee:

v10 Who givest salvation unto kings; who rescuest David thy servant from the hurtful sword.

v11 Rescue me, and deliver me from the hand of aliens, whose mouth speaketh vanity, and their right hand is a right hand of falsehood.

v12 That our sons may be as plants grown up in their youth; our daughters as corner-columns, sculptured after the fashion of a palace:

v13 Our granaries full, affording all manner of store; our sheep bringing forth thousands, ten thousands in our pastures;

v14 Our kine laden [with young]; no breaking in and no going forth, and no outcry in our streets.

v15 Blessed the people that is in such a case! Blessed the people whose God is Jehovah!
