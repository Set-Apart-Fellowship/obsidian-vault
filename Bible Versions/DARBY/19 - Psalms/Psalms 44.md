# Psalms 44 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 43|← Psalms 43]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 45|Psalms 45 →]]

***

v1 {To the chief Musician. Of the sons of Korah. An instruction.} O God, with our ears have we heard, our fathers have told us, the work thou wroughtest in their days, in the days of old:

v2 Thou, by thy hand, didst dispossess the nations, but them thou didst plant; thou didst afflict the peoples, but them didst thou cause to spread out.

v3 For not by their own sword did they take possession of the land, neither did their own arm save them; but thy right hand, and thine arm, and the light of thy countenance, because thou hadst delight in them.

v4 Thou thyself art my king, O God: command deliverance for Jacob.

v5 Through thee will we push down our adversaries; through thy name will we tread them under that rise up against us.

v6 For I will not put confidence in my bow, neither shall my sword save me.

v7 For thou hast saved us from our adversaries, and hast put them to shame that hate us.

v8 In God will we boast all the day, and we will praise thy name for ever. Selah.

v9 But thou hast cast off, and put us to confusion, and dost not go forth with our armies;

v10 Thou hast made us to turn back from the adversary, and they that hate us spoil for themselves;

v11 Thou hast given us over like sheep [appointed] for meat, and hast scattered us among the nations;

v12 Thou hast sold thy people for nought, and hast not increased [thy wealth] by their price;

v13 Thou makest us a reproach to our neighbours, a mockery and a derision for them that are round about us;

v14 Thou makest us a byword among the nations, a shaking of the head among the peoples.

v15 All the day my confusion is before me, and the shame of my face hath covered me,

v16 Because of the voice of him that reproacheth and blasphemeth; by reason of the enemy and the avenger.

v17 All this is come upon us; yet have we not forgotten thee, neither have we dealt falsely against thy covenant:

v18 Our heart is not turned back, neither have our steps declined from thy path;

v19 Though thou hast crushed us in the place of jackals, and covered us with the shadow of death.

v20 If we had forgotten the name of our God, and stretched out our hands to a strange Ã¹god,

v21 Would not God search this out? for he knoweth the secrets of the heart.

v22 But for thy sake are we killed all the day long; we are reckoned as sheep for slaughter.

v23 Awake, why sleepest thou, Lord? arise, cast [us] not off for ever.

v24 Wherefore hidest thou thy face, [and] forgettest our affliction and our oppression?

v25 For our soul is bowed down to the dust; our belly cleaveth unto the earth.

v26 Rise up for our help, and redeem us for thy loving-kindness' sake.
