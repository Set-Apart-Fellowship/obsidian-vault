# Psalms 107 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 106|← Psalms 106]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 108|Psalms 108 →]]

***

v1 Give ye thanks unto Jehovah; for he is good; for his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever.

v2 Let the redeemed of Jehovah say so, whom he hath redeemed from the hand of the oppressor,

v3 And gathered out of the countries, from the east and from the west, from the north and from the sea.

v4 They wandered in the wilderness in a desert way, they found no city of habitation;

v5 Hungry and thirsty, their soul fainted in them:

v6 Then they cried unto Jehovah in their trouble, [and] he delivered them out of their distresses,

v7 And he led them forth by a right way, that they might go to a city of habitation.

v8 Let them give thanks unto Jehovah for his loving-kindness, and for his wondrous works to the children of men;

v9 For he hath satisfied the longing soul and filled the hungry soul with good.

v10 Such as inhabit darkness and the shadow of death, bound in affliction and iron,

v11 Because they had rebelled against the words of Ã¹God, and had despised the counsel of the Most High; ...

v12 And he bowed down their heart with labour; they stumbled, and there was none to help:

v13 Then they cried unto Jehovah in their trouble, [and] he saved them out of their distresses;

v14 He brought them out of darkness and the shadow of death, and broke their bands in sunder.

v15 Let them give thanks unto Jehovah for his loving-kindness, and for his wondrous works to the children of men;

v16 For he hath broken the gates of bronze, and cut asunder the bars of iron.

v17 Fools, because of their way of transgression, and because of their iniquities, are afflicted;

v18 Their soul abhorreth all manner of food, and they draw near unto the gates of death:

v19 Then they cry unto Jehovah in their trouble, [and] he saveth them out of their distresses;

v20 He sendeth his word, and healeth them, and delivereth them from their destructions.

v21 Let them give thanks unto Jehovah for his loving-kindness, and for his wondrous works to the children of men,

v22 And let them offer the sacrifices of thanksgiving, and declare his works in joyful song.

v23 They that go down to the sea in ships, that do business in great waters,

v24 These see the works of Jehovah, and his wonders in the deep.

v25 For he speaketh, and raiseth the stormy wind, which lifteth up the waves thereof:

v26 They mount up to the heavens, they go down to the depths; their soul is melted because of trouble;

v27 They reel to and fro, and stagger like a drunken man, and they are at their wits' end:

v28 Then they cry unto Jehovah in their trouble, and he bringeth them out of their distresses;

v29 He maketh the storm a calm, and the waves thereof are still:

v30 And they rejoice because they are quiet; and he bringeth them unto their desired haven.

v31 Let them give thanks unto Jehovah for his loving-kindness, and for his wondrous works to the children of men;

v32 Let them exalt him also in the congregation of the people, and praise him in the session of the elders.

v33 He maketh rivers into a wilderness, and water-springs into dry ground;

v34 A fruitful land into a plain of salt, for the wickedness of them that dwell therein.

v35 He maketh the wilderness into a pool of water, and the dry land into water-springs;

v36 And there he maketh the hungry to dwell, and they establish a city of habitation;

v37 And sow fields, and plant vineyards, which yield fruits of increase;

v38 And he blesseth them, so that they are multiplied greatly; and he suffereth not their cattle to decrease.

v39 And they are diminished and brought low, through oppression, adversity, and sorrow:

v40 He poureth contempt upon nobles, and causeth them to wander in a pathless waste;

v41 But he secureth the needy one on high from affliction, and maketh [him] families like flocks.

v42 The upright shall see it, and rejoice; and all unrighteousness shall stop its mouth.

v43 Whoso is wise, let him observe these things, and let them understand the loving-kindnesses of Jehovah.
