# Psalms 96 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 95|← Psalms 95]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 97|Psalms 97 →]]

***

v1 Sing ye unto Jehovah a new song: sing unto Jehovah, all the earth.

v2 Sing unto Jehovah, bless his name; publish his salvation from day to day.

v3 Declare his glory among the nations, his wondrous works among all the peoples.

v4 For Jehovah is great and exceedingly to be praised; he is terrible above all gods.

v5 For all the gods of the peoples are idols; but Jehovah made the heavens.

v6 Majesty and splendour are before him; strength and beauty are in his sanctuary.

v7 Give unto Jehovah, ye families of peoples, give unto Jehovah glory and strength;

v8 Give unto Jehovah the glory of his name; bring an oblation and come into his courts;

v9 Worship Jehovah in holy splendour; tremble before him, all the earth.

v10 Say among the nations, Jehovah reigneth! yea, the world is established, it shall not be moved; he will execute judgment upon the peoples with equity.

v11 Let the heavens rejoice, and let the earth be glad; let the sea roar, and the fulness thereof;

v12 Let the field exult and all that is therein. Then shall all the trees of the forest sing for joy,

v13 Before Jehovah, for he cometh; for he cometh to judge the earth: he will judge the world with righteousness, and the peoples in his faithfulness.
