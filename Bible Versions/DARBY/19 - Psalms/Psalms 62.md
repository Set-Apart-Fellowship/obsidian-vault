# Psalms 62 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 61|← Psalms 61]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 63|Psalms 63 →]]

***

v1 {To the chief Musician. On Jeduthun. A Psalm of David.} Upon God alone doth my soul rest peacefully; from him is my salvation.

v2 He only is my rock and my salvation; my high fortress: I shall not be greatly moved.

v3 How long will ye assail a man; will ye [seek], all of you, to break him down as a bowing wall or a tottering fence?

v4 They only consult to thrust [him] down from his excellency; they delight in lies; they bless with their mouth, but in their inward part they curse. Selah.

v5 Upon God alone, O my soul, rest peacefully; for my expectation is from him.

v6 He only is my rock and my salvation; my high fortress: I shall not be moved.

v7 With God is my salvation and my glory; the rock of my strength, my refuge is in God.

v8 Confide in him at all times, ye people; pour out your heart before him: God is our refuge. Selah.

v9 Men of low degree are only vanity; men of high degree, a lie: laid in the balance, they go up together [lighter] than vanity.

v10 Put not confidence in oppression, and become not vain in robbery; if wealth increase, set not your heart upon it.

v11 Once hath God spoken, twice have I heard this, that strength [belongeth] unto God.

v12 And unto thee, O Lord, [belongeth] loving-kindness; for *thou* renderest to every man according to his work.
