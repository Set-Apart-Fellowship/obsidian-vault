# Psalms 104 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 103|← Psalms 103]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 105|Psalms 105 →]]

***

v1 Bless Jehovah, O my soul! Jehovah my God, thou art very great; thou art clothed with majesty and splendour;

v2 Covering thyself with light as with a garment, stretching out the heavens like a tent-curtain; --

v3 Who layeth the beams of his upper chambers in the waters, who maketh clouds his chariot, who walketh upon the wings of the wind;

v4 Who maketh his angels spirits; his ministers a flame of fire.

v5 He laid the earth upon its foundations: it shall not be removed for ever.

v6 Thou hadst covered it with the deep, as with a vesture; the waters stood above the mountains:

v7 At thy rebuke they fled, at the voice of thy thunder they hasted away; --

v8 The mountains rose, the valleys sank, unto the place which thou hadst founded for them; --

v9 Thou hast set a bound which they may not pass over, that they turn not again to cover the earth.

v10 He sendeth the springs into the valleys: they run among the mountains;

v11 They give drink to every beast of the field; the wild asses quench their thirst.

v12 The birds of heaven dwell by them; they give forth their voice from among the branches.

v13 He watereth the mountains from his upper-chambers: the earth is satisfied with the fruit of thy works.

v14 He maketh the grass to grow for the cattle, and herb for the service of man; bringing forth bread out of the earth,

v15 And wine which gladdeneth the heart of man; making [his] face shine with oil; and with bread he strengtheneth man's heart.

v16 The trees of Jehovah are satisfied, the cedars of Lebanon, which he hath planted,

v17 Where the birds make their nests; [as for] the stork, the fir trees are her house.

v18 The high mountains are for the wild goats; the cliffs, a refuge for the rock-badgers.

v19 He made the moon for seasons: the sun knoweth its going down.

v20 Thou makest darkness, and it is night, wherein all the beasts of the forest creep forth:

v21 The young lions roar after the prey, and to seek their food from Ã¹God.

v22 The sun ariseth, they retreat, and lay them down in their dens.

v23 Man goeth forth unto his work, and to his labour until the evening.

v24 How manifold are thy works, O Jehovah! in wisdom hast thou made them all: the earth is full of thy riches.

v25 Yonder is the great and wide sea: therein are moving things innumerable, living creatures small and great.

v26 There go the ships; [there] that leviathan, which thou hast formed to play therein.

v27 These all look unto thee, that thou mayest give their food in its season:

v28 That thou givest unto them, they gather; thou openest thy hand, they are filled with good.

v29 Thou hidest thy face, they are troubled; thou takest away their breath, they expire and return to their dust.

v30 Thou sendest forth thy spirit, they are created, and thou renewest the face of the earth.

v31 The glory of Jehovah will endure for ever; Jehovah will rejoice in his works.

v32 He looketh on the earth, and it trembleth; he toucheth the mountains, and they smoke.

v33 I will sing unto Jehovah as long as I live; I will sing psalms to my God while I have my being.

v34 My meditation shall be pleasant unto him; I will rejoice in Jehovah.

v35 Sinners shall be consumed out of the earth, and the wicked shall be no more. Bless Jehovah, O my soul. Hallelujah!
