# Psalms 33 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 32|← Psalms 32]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 34|Psalms 34 →]]

***

v1 Exult, ye righteous, in Jehovah: praise is comely for the upright.

v2 Give thanks unto Jehovah with the harp; sing psalms unto him with the ten-stringed lute.

v3 Sing unto him a new song; play skilfully with a loud sound.

v4 For the word of Jehovah is right, and all his work is in faithfulness.

v5 He loveth righteousness and judgment: the earth is full of the loving-kindness of Jehovah.

v6 By the word of Jehovah were the heavens made, and all the host of them by the breath of his mouth.

v7 He gathereth the waters of the sea together as a heap; he layeth up the deeps in storehouses.

v8 Let all the earth fear Jehovah; let all the inhabitants of the world stand in awe of him.

v9 For *he* spoke, and it was [done]; *he* commanded, and it stood fast.

v10 Jehovah frustrateth the counsel of the nations; he maketh the thoughts of the peoples of none effect.

v11 The counsel of Jehovah standeth for ever, the thoughts of his heart from generation to generation.

v12 Blessed is the nation whose God is Jehovah, the people that he hath chosen for his inheritance!

v13 Jehovah looketh from the heavens; he beholdeth all the sons of men:

v14 From the place of his habitation he looketh forth upon all the inhabitants of the earth;

v15 He who fashioneth the hearts of them all, who considereth all their works.

v16 The king is not saved by the multitude of [his] forces; a mighty man is not delivered by much strength.

v17 The horse is a vain thing for safety; neither doth he deliver by his great power.

v18 Behold, the eye of Jehovah is upon them that fear him, upon them that hope in his loving-kindness,

v19 To deliver their soul from death, and to keep them alive in famine.

v20 Our soul waiteth for Jehovah: he is our help and our shield.

v21 For in him shall our heart rejoice, because we have confided in his holy name.

v22 Let thy loving-kindness, O Jehovah, be upon us, according as we have hoped in thee.
