# Psalms 38 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 37|← Psalms 37]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 39|Psalms 39 →]]

***

v1 {A Psalm of David, to bring to remembrance.} Jehovah, rebuke me not in thy wrath; neither chasten me in thy hot displeasure.

v2 For thine arrows stick fast in me, and thy hand cometh down upon me.

v3 There is no soundness in my flesh because of thine indignation; no peace in my bones, because of my sin.

v4 For mine iniquities are gone over my head: as a heavy burden they are too heavy for me.

v5 My wounds stink, they are corrupt, because of my foolishness.

v6 I am depressed; I am bowed down beyond measure; I go mourning all the day.

v7 For my loins are full of burning, and there is no soundness in my flesh.

v8 I am faint and broken beyond measure; I roar by reason of the agitation of my heart.

v9 Lord, all my desire is before thee, and my sighing is not hid from thee.

v10 My heart throbbeth, my strength hath left me; and the light of mine eyes, it also is no more with me.

v11 My lovers and mine associates stand aloof from my stroke; and my kinsmen stand afar off.

v12 And they that seek after my life lay snares [for me]; and they that seek my hurt speak mischievous things, and meditate deceits all the day long.

v13 But I, as a deaf [man], hear not; and am as a dumb man that openeth not his mouth.

v14 Yea, I am as a man that heareth not, and in whose mouth are no reproofs.

v15 For in thee, Jehovah, do I hope: *thou* wilt answer, O Lord my God.

v16 For I said, Let them not rejoice over me! When my foot slipped, they magnified [themselves] against me.

v17 For I am ready to halt, and my pain is continually before me.

v18 For I will declare mine iniquity, I am grieved for my sin.

v19 But mine enemies are lively, they are strong; and they that hate me wrongfully are multiplied:

v20 And they that render evil for good are adversaries unto me; because I pursue what is good.

v21 Forsake me not, Jehovah; O my God, be not far from me.

v22 Make haste to help me, O Lord, my salvation.
