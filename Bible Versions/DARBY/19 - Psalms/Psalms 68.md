# Psalms 68 (Darby Translation)

v1 {To the chief Musician. Of David. A Psalm: a Song.} Let God arise, let his enemies be scattered, and let them that hate him flee before him.

v2 As smoke is driven, thou wilt drive them away; as wax melteth before the fire, the wicked shall perish at the presence of God.

v3 But the righteous shall rejoice: they shall exult before God and be glad with joy.

v4 Sing unto God, sing forth his name; cast up a way for him that rideth in the deserts: his name is Jah; and rejoice before him.

v5 A father of the fatherless, and a judge of the widows, is God in his holy habitation.

v6 God maketh the solitary into families; those that were bound he bringeth out into prosperity: but the rebellious dwell in a parched [land].

v7 O God, when thou wentest forth before thy people, when thou didst march through the wilderness -- (Selah) --

v8 The earth trembled, the heavens also dropped at the presence of God, yon Sinai, at the presence of God, the God of Israel.

v9 Thou, O God, didst pour a plentiful rain upon thine inheritance, and when it was weary thou strengthenedst it.

v10 Thy flock hath dwelt therein: thou hast prepared in thy goodness, for the afflicted, O God!

v11 The Lord gives the word: great the host of the publishers.

v12 Kings of armies flee; they flee, and she that tarrieth at home divideth the spoil.

v13 Though ye have lain among the sheepfolds, [ye shall be as] wings of a dove covered with silver, and her feathers with green gold.

v14 When the Almighty scattered kings in it, it became snow-white as Zalmon.

v15 [As] mount Bashan is the mount of God, a many-peaked mountain, [as] mount Bashan.

v16 Why do ye look with envy, ye many-peaked mountains, upon the mount that God hath desired for his abode? yea, Jehovah will dwell [there] for ever.

v17 The chariots of God are twenty thousand, thousands upon thousands; the Lord is among them: it is a Sinai in holiness.

v18 Thou hast ascended on high, thou hast led captivity captive: thou hast received gifts in Man, and even [for] the rebellious, for the dwelling [there] of Jah Elohim.

v19 Blessed be the Lord: day by day doth he load us [with good], the Ã¹God who is our salvation. Selah.

v20 Our Ã¹God is the Ã¹God of salvation; and with Jehovah, the Lord, are the goings forth [even] from death.

v21 Verily God will smite the head of his enemies, the hairy scalp of him that goeth on still in his trespasses.

v22 The Lord said, I will bring again from Bashan, I will bring [them] again from the depth of the sea;

v23 That thou mayest dip thy foot in blood: the tongue of thy dogs has its portion from enemies.

v24 They have seen thy goings, O God, the goings of my Ã¹God, my King, in the sanctuary.

v25 The singers went before, the players on stringed instruments after, in the midst of maidens playing on tabrets.

v26 In the congregations bless ye God, the Lord, -- [ye] from the fountain of Israel.

v27 There is little Benjamin, their ruler; the princes of Judah, their company; the princes of Zebulun, the princes of Naphtali.

v28 Thy God hath commanded thy strength: strengthen, O God, that which thou hast wrought for us.

v29 Because of thy temple at Jerusalem shall kings bring presents unto thee.

v30 Rebuke the beast of the reeds, the assembly of the strong, with the calves of the peoples: [every one] submitteth himself with pieces of silver. Scatter the peoples that delight in war.

v31 Great ones shall come out of Egypt; Ethiopia shall quickly stretch out her hands unto God.

v32 Ye kingdoms of the earth, sing unto God; sing psalms of the Lord, (Selah,)

v33 Of him that rideth upon the heavens, the heavens which are of old: lo, he uttereth his voice, a mighty voice.

v34 Ascribe ye strength unto God: his excellency is over Israel, and his strength is in the clouds.

v35 Terrible art thou, O God, out of thy sanctuaries, -- the Ã¹God of Israel! He it is that giveth strength and might unto the people. Blessed be God!
