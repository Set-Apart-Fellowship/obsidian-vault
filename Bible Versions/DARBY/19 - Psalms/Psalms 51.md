# Psalms 51 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 50|← Psalms 50]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 52|Psalms 52 →]]

***

v1 {To the chief Musician. A Psalm of David; when Nathan the prophet came to him, after he had gone in to Bath-sheba.} Be gracious unto me, O God, according to thy loving-kindness; according to the abundance of thy tender mercies, blot out my transgressions.

v2 Wash me fully from mine iniquity, and cleanse me from my sin.

v3 For I acknowledge my transgressions, and my sin is continually before me.

v4 Against thee, thee only, have I sinned, and done what is evil in thy sight; that thou mayest be justified when thou speakest, be clear when thou judgest.

v5 Behold, in iniquity was I brought forth, and in sin did my mother conceive me.

v6 Behold, thou wilt have truth in the inward parts; and in the hidden [part] thou wilt make me to know wisdom.

v7 Purge me with hyssop, and I shall be clean; wash me, and I shall be whiter than snow.

v8 Make me to hear gladness and joy; [that] the bones which thou hast broken may rejoice.

v9 Hide thy face from my sins, and blot out all mine iniquities.

v10 Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a steadfast spirit within me.

v11 Cast me not away from thy presence, and take not the spirit of thy holiness from me.

v12 Restore unto me the joy of thy salvation, and let a willing spirit sustain me.

v13 I will teach transgressors thy ways, and sinners shall return unto thee.

v14 Deliver me from blood-guiltiness, O God, thou God of my salvation: my tongue shall sing aloud of thy righteousness.

v15 Lord, open my lips, and my mouth shall declare thy praise.

v16 For thou desirest not sacrifice; else would I give it: thou hast no pleasure in burnt-offering.

v17 The sacrifices of God are a broken spirit: a broken and a contrite heart, O God, thou wilt not despise.

v18 Do good in thy good pleasure unto Zion; build the walls of Jerusalem.

v19 Then shalt thou have sacrifices of righteousness, burnt-offering, and whole burnt-offering; then shall they offer up bullocks upon thine altar.
