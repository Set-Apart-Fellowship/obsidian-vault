# Psalms 67 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 66|← Psalms 66]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 68|Psalms 68 →]]

***

v1 {To the chief Musician. On stringed instruments. A Psalm: a Song.} God be gracious unto us, and bless us, [and] cause his face to shine upon us; Selah,

v2 That thy way may be known upon earth, thy salvation among all nations.

v3 Let the peoples praise thee, O God, let all the peoples praise thee.

v4 Let the nations rejoice and sing for joy: for thou wilt judge the peoples equitably; and the nations upon earth, thou wilt guide them. Selah.

v5 Let the peoples praise thee, O God; let all the peoples praise thee.

v6 The earth will yield her increase; God, our God, will bless us:

v7 God will bless us; and all the ends of the earth shall fear him.
