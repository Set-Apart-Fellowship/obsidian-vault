# Psalms 6 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 5|← Psalms 5]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 7|Psalms 7 →]]

***

v1 {To the chief Musician. On stringed instruments, upon Sheminith. A Psalm of David.} Jehovah, rebuke me not in thine anger, and chasten me not in thy hot displeasure.

v2 Be gracious unto me, Jehovah, for I am withered; Jehovah, heal me, for my bones tremble.

v3 And my soul trembleth exceedingly: and thou, Jehovah, till how long?

v4 Return, Jehovah, free my soul; save me for thy loving-kindness' sake.

v5 For in death there is no remembrance of thee; in Sheol who shall give thanks unto thee?

v6 I am wearied with my groaning; all the night make I my bed to swim; I dissolve my couch with my tears.

v7 Mine eye wasteth away through grief; it hath grown old because of all mine oppressors.

v8 Depart from me, all ye workers of iniquity; for Jehovah hath heard the voice of my weeping.

v9 Jehovah hath heard my supplication; Jehovah receiveth my prayer.

v10 All mine enemies shall be ashamed and tremble exceedingly; they will turn, they will be ashamed suddenly.
