v1 {To the chief Musician. On Jeduthun. Of Asaph. A Psalm.} My voice is unto God, and I will cry; my voice is unto God, and he will give ear unto me.

v2 In the day of my trouble, I sought the Lord: my hand was stretched out in the night, and slacked not; my soul refused to be comforted.

v3 I remembered God, and I moaned; I complained, and my spirit was overwhelmed. Selah.

v4 Thou holdest open mine eyelids; I am full of disquiet and cannot speak.

v5 I consider the days of old, the years of ancient times.

v6 I remember my song in the night; I muse in mine own heart, and my spirit maketh diligent search.

v7 Will the Lord cast off for ever? and will he be favourable no more?

v8 Hath his loving-kindness ceased for ever? hath [his] word come to an end from generation to generation?

v9 Hath Ã¹God forgotten to be gracious? or hath he in anger shut up his tender mercies? Selah.

v10 Then said I, This is my weakness: -- the years of the right hand of the Most High

v11 Will I remember, -- the works of Jah; for I will remember thy wonders of old,

v12 And I will meditate upon all thy work, and muse upon thy doings.

v13 O God, thy way is in the sanctuary: who is so great a Ã¹god as God?

v14 Thou art the Ã¹God that doest wonders; thou hast declared thy strength among the peoples.

v15 Thou hast with [thine] arm redeemed thy people, the sons of Jacob and Joseph. Selah.

v16 The waters saw thee, O God, the waters saw thee; they trembled, yea, the depths were troubled:

v17 The thick clouds poured out water; the skies sent out a sound, yea, thine arrows went abroad:

v18 The voice of thy thunder was in the whirlwind, lightnings lit up the world; the earth was troubled and it quaked.

v19 Thy way is in the sea, and thy paths are in the great waters; and thy footsteps are not known.

v20 Thou leddest thy people like a flock by the hand of Moses and Aaron.
