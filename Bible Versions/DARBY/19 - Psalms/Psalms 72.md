# Psalms 72 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 71|← Psalms 71]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 73|Psalms 73 →]]

***

v1 {For Solomon.} O God, give the king thy judgments, and thy righteousness unto the king's son.

v2 He will judge thy people with righteousness, and thine afflicted with judgment.

v3 The mountains shall bring peace to the people, and the hills, by righteousness.

v4 He will do justice to the afflicted of the people; he will save the children of the needy, and will break in pieces the oppressor.

v5 They shall fear thee as long as sun and moon endure, from generation to generation.

v6 He shall come down like rain on the mown grass, as showers that water the earth.

v7 In his days shall the righteous flourish, and abundance of peace till the moon be no more.

v8 And he shall have dominion from sea to sea, and from the river unto the ends of the earth.

v9 The dwellers in the desert shall bow before him, and his enemies shall lick the dust.

v10 The kings of Tarshish and of the isles shall render presents; the kings of Sheba and Seba shall offer tribute:

v11 Yea, all kings shall bow down before him; all nations shall serve him.

v12 For he will deliver the needy who crieth, and the afflicted, who hath no helper;

v13 He will have compassion on the poor and needy, and will save the souls of the needy:

v14 He will redeem their souls from oppression and violence, and precious shall their blood be in his sight.

v15 And he shall live; and to him shall be given of the gold of Sheba; and prayer shall be made for him continually: all the day shall he be blessed.

v16 There shall be abundance of corn in the earth, upon the top of the mountains; the fruit thereof shall shake like Lebanon; and they of the city shall bloom like the herb of the earth.

v17 His name shall endure for ever; his name shall be continued as long as the sun: and [men] shall bless themselves in him; all nations shall call him blessed.

v18 Blessed be Jehovah Elohim, the God of Israel, who alone doeth wondrous things!

v19 And blessed be his glorious name for ever! and let the whole earth be filled with his glory! Amen, and Amen.

v20 The prayers of David the son of Jesse are ended.
