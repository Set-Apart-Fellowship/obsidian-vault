# Psalms 49 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 48|← Psalms 48]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 50|Psalms 50 →]]

***

v1 {To the chief Musician. Of the sons of Korah. A Psalm.} Hear this, all ye peoples; give ear, all inhabitants of the world:

v2 Both men of low and men of high degree, rich and poor alike.

v3 My mouth shall speak wisdom, and the meditation of my heart shall be of understanding:

v4 I will incline mine ear to a parable, I will open my riddle upon the harp.

v5 Wherefore should I fear in the days of adversity, [when] the iniquity of my supplanters encompasseth me? --

v6 They depend upon their wealth, and boast themselves in the abundance of their riches. ...

v7 None can by any means redeem his brother, nor give to God a ransom for him,

v8 (For the redemption of their soul is costly, and must be given up for ever,)

v9 That he should still live perpetually, [and] not see corruption.

v10 For he seeth that wise men die; all alike, the fool and the brutish perish, and they leave their wealth to others.

v11 Their inward thought is, that their houses are for ever, their dwelling-places from generation to generation: they call the lands after their own names.

v12 Nevertheless, man being in honour abook_ideth not: he is like the beasts that perish.

v13 This their way is their folly, yet they that come after them delight in their sayings. Selah.

v14 Like sheep are they laid in Sheol: Death feedeth on them; and the upright shall have dominion over them in the morning; and their comeliness shall be for Sheol to consume, that there be no habitation for them.

v15 But God will redeem my soul from the power of Sheol: for he will receive me. Selah.

v16 Be not afraid when a man becometh rich, when the glory of his house is increased:

v17 For when he dieth, he shall carry nothing away; his glory shall not descend after him.

v18 Though he blessed his soul in his lifetime, -- and men will praise thee when thou doest well to thyself, --

v19 It shall go to the generation of his fathers: they shall never see light.

v20 Man that is in honour, and understandeth not, is like the beasts that perish.
