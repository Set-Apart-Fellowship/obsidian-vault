# Psalms 27 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 26|← Psalms 26]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 28|Psalms 28 →]]

***

v1 {[A Psalm] of David.} Jehovah is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear? Jehovah is the strength of my life; of whom shall I be afraid?

v2 When evil-doers, mine adversaries and mine enemies, came upon me to eat up my flesh, they stumbled and fell.

v3 If a host encamp against me, my heart shall not fear; if war rise against me, in this will I be confident.

v4 One [thing] have I asked of Jehovah, that will I seek after: that I may dwell in the house of Jehovah all the days of my life, to behold the beauty of Jehovah, and to inquire [of him] in his temple.

v5 For in the day of evil he will hide me in his pavilion; in the secret of his tent will he keep me concealed: he will set me high upon a rock.

v6 And now shall my head be lifted up above mine enemies round about me; and I will offer in his tent sacrifices of shouts of joy: I will sing, yea, I will sing psalms unto Jehovah.

v7 Hear, Jehovah; with my voice do I call; be gracious unto me, and answer me.

v8 My heart said for thee, Seek ye my face. Thy face, O Jehovah, will I seek.

v9 Hide not thy face from me; put not thy servant away in anger: thou hast been my help; cast me not off, neither forsake me, O God of my salvation.

v10 For had my father and my mother forsaken me, then had Jehovah taken me up.

v11 Teach me thy way, Jehovah, and lead me in an even path, because of mine enemies.

v12 Deliver me not over to the will of mine adversaries; for false witnesses are risen up against me, and such as breathe out violence.

v13 Unless I had believed to see the goodness of Jehovah in the land of the living ...!

v14 Wait for Jehovah; be strong and let thy heart take courage: yea, wait for Jehovah.
