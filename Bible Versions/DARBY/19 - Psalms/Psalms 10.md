# Psalms 10 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 9|← Psalms 9]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/19 - Psalms/Psalms 11|Psalms 11 →]]

***

v1 Why, Jehovah, standest thou afar off? [Why] hidest thou thyself in times of distress?

v2 The wicked, in his pride, doth hotly pursue the afflicted. They shall be taken in the devices that they have imagined.

v3 For the wicked boasteth of his soul's desire, and he blesseth the covetous; he contemneth Jehovah.

v4 The wicked [saith], in the haughtiness of his countenance, He doth not search out: all his thoughts are, There is no God!

v5 His ways always succeed; thy judgments are far above out of his sight; [as for] all his adversaries, he puffeth at them.

v6 He saith in his heart, I shall not be moved; from generation to generation I shall be in no adversity.

v7 His mouth is full of cursing, and deceit, and oppression; under his tongue is mischief and iniquity.

v8 He sitteth in the lurking-places of the villages; in the secret places doth he slay the innocent: his eyes watch for the wretched.

v9 He lieth in wait secretly, like a lion in his thicket; he lieth in wait to catch the afflicted: he doth catch the afflicted, drawing him into his net.

v10 He croucheth, he boweth down, that the wretched may fall by his strong ones.

v11 He saith in his heart, Ã¹God hath forgotten, he hideth his face, he will never see [it].

v12 Arise, Jehovah; O Ã¹God, lift up thy hand: forget not the afflicted.

v13 Wherefore doth the wicked contemn God? He hath said in his heart, Thou wilt not require [it].

v14 Thou hast seen [it], for thou thyself beholdest trouble and vexation, to requite by thy hand. The wretched committeth himself unto thee; thou hast been the helper of the fatherless.

v15 Break thou the arm of the wicked, and as for the evil man, seek out his wickedness [till] thou find none.

v16 Jehovah is King for ever and ever: the nations have perished out of his land.

v17 Jehovah, thou hast heard the desire of the meek, thou hast established their heart: thou causest thine ear to hear,

v18 To do justice to the fatherless and the oppressed one, that the man of the earth may terrify no more.
