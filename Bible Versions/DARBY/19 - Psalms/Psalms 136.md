# Psalms 136 (Darby Translation)

v1 Give ye thanks unto Jehovah, for he is good; for his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever:

v2 Give thanks unto the God of gods, for his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever;

v3 Give thanks unto the Lord of lords, for his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever.

v4 To him who alone doeth great wonders, for his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever:

v5 To him that by understanding made the heavens, for his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever;

v6 To him that stretched out the earth above the waters, for his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever;

v7 To him that made great lights, for his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever;

v8 The sun for rule over the day, for his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever,

v9 The moon and stars for rule over the night, for his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever:

v10 To him that smote Egypt in their firstborn, for his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever,

v11 And brought out Israel from among them, for his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever,

v12 With a powerful hand and with a stretched-out arm, for his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever;

v13 To him that divided the Red sea into parts, for his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever,

v14 And made Israel to pass through the midst of it, for his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever,

v15 And overturned Pharaoh and his host in the Red sea, for his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever;

v16 To him that led his people through the wilderness, for his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever;

v17 To him that smote great kings, for his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever,

v18 And slew famous kings, for his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever;

v19 Sihon king of the Amorites, for his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever,

v20 And Og king of Bashan, for his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever;

v21 And gave their land for an inheritance, for his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever,

v22 An inheritance unto Israel his servant, for his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever:

v23 Who hath remembered us in our low estate, for his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever;

v24 And hath delivered us from our oppressors, for his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever:

v25 Who giveth food to all flesh, for his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever.
