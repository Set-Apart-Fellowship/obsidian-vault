# Genesis 10 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9|← Genesis 9]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/01 - Genesis/Genesis]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/01 - Genesis/Genesis 11|Genesis 11 →]]

***

v1 And these are the generations of the sons of Noah: Shem, Ham, and Japheth; and to them were sons born after the flood.

v2 The sons of Japheth: Gomer, and Magog, and Madai, and Javan, and Tubal, and Meshech, and Tiras.

v3 And the sons of Gomer: Ashkenaz, and Riphath, and Togarmah.

v4 And the sons of Javan: Elishah, and Tarshish, Kittim, and Dodanim.

v5 From these came the distribution of the isles of the nations, according to their lands, every one after his tongue, after their families, in their nations.

v6 And the sons of Ham: Cush, and Mizraim, and Phut, and Canaan.

v7 And the sons of Cush: Seba, and Havilah, and Sabtah, and Raamah, and Sabtecha. And the sons of Raamah: Sheba and Dedan.

v8 And Cush begot Nimrod: he began to be mighty on the earth.

v9 He was a mighty hunter before Jehovah; therefore it is said, As Nimrod, the mighty hunter before Jehovah!

v10 And the beginning of his kingdom was Babel, and Erech, and Accad, and Calneh, in the land of Shinar.

v11 From that land went out Asshur, and built Nineveh, and Rehoboth-Ir, and Calah,

v12 and Resen, between Nineveh and Calah: this is the great city.

v13 -- And Mizraim begot the Ludim, and the Anamim, and the Lehabim, and the Naphtuhim,

v14 and the Pathrusim, and the Casluhim, out of whom came the Philistines, and the Caphtorim.

v15 -- And Canaan begot Sidon, his firstborn, and Heth,

v16 and the Jebusite, and the Amorite, and the Girgashite,

v17 and the Hivite, and the Arkite, and the Sinite,

v18 and the Arvadite, and the Zemarite, and the Hamathite. And afterwards the families of the Canaanites spread themselves abroad.

v19 And the border of the Canaanite was from Sidon, as one goes to Gerar, up to Gazah; as one goes to Sodom, and Gomorrah, and Admah, and Zeboim, up to Lesha.

v20 These are the sons of Ham, after their families, after their tongues, in their lands, in their nations.

v21 And to Shem -- to him also were [sons] born; he is the father of all the sons of Eber, the brother of Japheth the elder.

v22 The sons of Shem: Elam, and Asshur, and Arphaxad, and Lud, and Aram.

v23 And the sons of Aram: Uz, and Hul, and Gether, and Mash.

v24 -- And Arphaxad begot Shelah; and Shelah begot Eber.

v25 And to Eber were born two sons: the name of the one was Peleg, for in his days was the earth divided; and his brother's name was Joktan.

v26 And Joktan begot Almodad, and Sheleph, and Hazarmaveth, and Jerah,

v27 and Hadoram, and Uzal, and Diklah,

v28 and Obal, and Abimael, and Sheba,

v29 and Ophir, and Havilah, and Jobab: all these were sons of Joktan.

v30 And their dwelling was from Mesha, as one goes to Sephar, the eastern mountain.

v31 These are the sons of Shem, after their families, after their tongues, in their lands, after their nations.

v32 These are the families of the sons of Noah, after their generations, in their nations. And from these came the distribution of the nations on the earth after the flood.
