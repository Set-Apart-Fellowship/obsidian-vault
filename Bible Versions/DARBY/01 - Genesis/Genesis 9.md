# Genesis 9 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/01 - Genesis/Genesis 8|← Genesis 8]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/01 - Genesis/Genesis]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/01 - Genesis/Genesis 10|Genesis 10 →]]

***

v1 And God blessed Noah and his sons, and said to them, Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the earth.

v2 And let the fear of you and the dread of you be upon every animal of the earth, and upon all fowl of the heavens: upon all that moveth [on] the ground; and upon all the fishes of the sea: into your hand are they delivered.

v3 Every moving thing that liveth shall be food for you: as the green herb I give you everything.

v4 Only, the flesh with its life, its blood, ye shall not eat.

v5 And indeed your blood, [the blood] of your lives, will I require: at the hand of every animal will I require it, and at the hand of Man, at the hand of each [the blood] of his brother, will I require the life of Man.

v6 Whoso sheddeth Man's blood, by Man shall his blood be shed; for in the image of God he hath made Man.

v7 And ye, be fruitful and multiply: swarm on the earth, and multiply on it.

v8 And God spoke to Noah, and to his sons with him, saying,

v9 And I, behold, I establish my covenant with you, and with your seed after you;

v10 and with every living soul which is with you, fowl as well as cattle, and all the animals of the earth with you, of all that has gone out of the ark -- every animal of the earth.

v11 And I establish my covenant with you, neither shall all flesh be cut off any more by the waters of a flood, and henceforth there shall be no flood to destroy the earth.

v12 And God said, This is the sign of the covenant that I set between me and you and every living soul that is with you, for everlasting generations:

v13 I set my bow in the clouds, and it shall be for a sign of the covenant between me and the earth.

v14 And it shall come to pass when I bring clouds over the earth, that the bow shall be seen in the cloud,

v15 and I will remember my covenant which is between me and you and every living soul of all flesh; and the waters shall not henceforth become a flood to destroy all flesh.

v16 And the bow shall be in the cloud; and I will look upon it, that I may remember the everlasting covenant between God and every living soul of all flesh that is upon the earth.

v17 And God said to Noah, This is the sign of the covenant which I have established between me and all flesh that is upon the earth.

v18 And the sons of Noah who went out of the ark were Shem, and Ham, and Japheth. And Ham is the father of Canaan.

v19 These three are the sons of Noah; and from these was [the population of] the whole earth spread abroad.

v20 And Noah began [to be] a husbandman, and planted a vineyard.

v21 And he drank of the wine, and was drunken, and he uncovered himself in his tent.

v22 And Ham the father of Canaan saw the nakedness of his father, and told his two brethren outside.

v23 And Shem and Japheth took the upper garment and both laid [it] upon their shoulders, and went backwards, and covered the nakedness of their father. And their faces were turned away, that they saw not their father's nakedness.

v24 And Noah awoke from his wine, and learned what his youngest son had done to him.

v25 And he said, Cursed be Canaan; Let him be a bondman of bondmen to his brethren.

v26 And he said, Blessed be Jehovah, the God of Shem, And let Canaan be his bondman.

v27 Let God enlarge Japheth, and let him dwell in the tents of Shem, And let Canaan be his bondman.

v28 And Noah lived after the flood three hundred and fifty years.

v29 And all the days of Noah were nine hundred and fifty years; and he died.
