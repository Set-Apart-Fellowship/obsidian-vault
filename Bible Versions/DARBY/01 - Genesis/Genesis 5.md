# Genesis 5 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4|← Genesis 4]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/01 - Genesis/Genesis]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6|Genesis 6 →]]

***

v1 This is the book of Adam's generations. In the day that God created man, in the likeness of God made he him.

v2 Male and female created he them; and blessed them, and called their name Adam, in the day when they were created.

v3 And Adam lived a hundred and thirty years, and begot [a son] in his likeness, after his image, and called his name Seth.

v4 And the days of Adam after he had begotten Seth were eight hundred years; and he begot sons and daughters.

v5 And all the days of Adam that he lived were nine hundred and thirty years; and he died.

v6 And Seth lived a hundred and five years, and begot Enosh.

v7 And Seth lived after he had begotten Enosh eight hundred and seven years, and begot sons and daughters.

v8 And all the days of Seth were nine hundred and twelve years; and he died.

v9 And Enosh lived ninety years, and begot Cainan.

v10 And Enosh lived after he had begotten Cainan eight hundred and fifteen years, and begot sons and daughters.

v11 And all the days of Enosh were nine hundred and five years; and he died.

v12 And Cainan lived seventy years, and begot Mahalaleel.

v13 And Cainan lived after he had begotten Mahalaleel eight hundred and forty years, and begot sons and daughters.

v14 And all the days of Cainan were nine hundred and ten years; and he died.

v15 And Mahalaleel lived sixty-five years, and begot Jared.

v16 And Mahalaleel lived after he had begotten Jared eight hundred and thirty years, and begot sons and daughters.

v17 And all the days of Mahalaleel were eight hundred and ninety-five years; and he died.

v18 And Jared lived a hundred and sixty-two years, and begot Enoch.

v19 And Jared lived after he had begotten Enoch eight hundred years, and begot sons and daughters.

v20 And all the days of Jared were nine hundred and sixty-two years; and he died.

v21 And Enoch lived sixty-five years, and begot Methushelah.

v22 And Enoch walked with God after he had begotten Methushelah three hundred years, and begot sons and daughters.

v23 And all the days of Enoch were three hundred and sixty-five years.

v24 And Enoch walked with God; and he was not, for God took him.

v25 And Methushelah lived a hundred and eighty-seven years, and begot Lemech.

v26 And Methushelah lived after he had begotten Lemech seven hundred and eighty-two years, and begot sons and daughters.

v27 And all the days of Methushelah were nine hundred and sixty-nine years; and he died.

v28 And Lemech lived a hundred and eighty-two years, and begot a son.

v29 And he called his name Noah, saying, This [one] shall comfort us concerning our work and concerning the toil of our hands, because of the ground which Jehovah has cursed.

v30 And Lemech lived after he had begotten Noah five hundred and ninety-five years, and begot sons and daughters.

v31 And all the days of Lemech were seven hundred and seventy-seven years; and he died.

v32 And Noah was five hundred years old, and Noah begot Shem, Ham, and Japheth.
