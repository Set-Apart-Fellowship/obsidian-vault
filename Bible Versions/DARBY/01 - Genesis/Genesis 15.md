# Genesis 15 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/01 - Genesis/Genesis 14|← Genesis 14]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/01 - Genesis/Genesis]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/01 - Genesis/Genesis 16|Genesis 16 →]]

***

v1 After these things the word of Jehovah came to Abram in a vision, saying, Fear not, Abram; I am thy shield, thy exceeding great reward.

v2 And Abram said, Lord Jehovah, what wilt thou give me? seeing I go childless, and the steward of my house is this Eliezer of Damascus.

v3 And Abram said, Lo, to me thou hast given no seed, and behold, a son of my house will be mine heir.

v4 And behold, the word of Jehovah [came] to him, saying, This shall not be thine heir, but he that will come forth out of thy body shall be thine heir.

v5 And he led him out, and said, Look now toward the heavens, and number the stars, if thou be able to number them. And he said to him, So shall thy seed be!

v6 And he believed Jehovah; and he reckoned it to him [as] righteousness.

v7 And he said to him, I am Jehovah who brought thee out of Ur of the Chaldeans, to give thee this land to possess it.

v8 And he said, Lord Jehovah, how shall I know that I shall possess it?

v9 And he said to him, Take me a heifer of three years old, and a she-goat of three years old, and a ram of three years old, and a turtle-dove, and a young pigeon.

v10 And he took all these, and divided them in the midst, and laid the half of each opposite its fellow; but the birds he did not divide.

v11 And the birds of prey came down on the carcases; and Abram scared them away.

v12 And as the sun was just going down, a deep sleep fell upon Abram; and behold, a horror, a great darkness, fell upon him.

v13 And he said to Abram, Know assuredly that thy seed will be a sojourner in a land [that is] not theirs, and they shall serve them; and they shall afflict them four hundred years.

v14 But also that nation which they shall serve I will judge; and afterwards they shall come out with great property.

v15 And thou shalt go to thy fathers in peace; thou shalt be buried in a good old age.

v16 And [in the] fourth generation they shall come hither again; for the iniquity of the Amorites is not yet full.

v17 And it came to pass when the sun had gone down, and it was dark, that behold, there was a smoking furnace, and a flame of fire which passed between those pieces.

v18 On the same day Jehovah made a covenant with Abram, saying, Unto thy seed I give this land, from the river of Egypt to the great river, the river Euphrates;

v19 the Kenites, and the Kenizzites, and the Kadmonites,

v20 and the Hittites, and the Perizzites, and the Rephaim,

v21 and the Amorites, and the Canaanites, and the Girgashites, and the Jebusites.
