# Luke 22 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/42 - Luke/Luke 21|← Luke 21]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/42 - Luke/Luke]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/42 - Luke/Luke 23|Luke 23 →]]

***

v1 Now the feast of unleavened bread, which [is] called the passover, drew nigh,

v2 and the chief priests and the scribes sought how they might kill him; for they feared the people.

v3 And Satan entered into Judas, who was surnamed Iscariote, being of the number of the twelve.

v4 And he went away and spoke with the chief priests and captains as to how he should deliver him up to them.

v5 And they were rejoiced, and agreed to give him money.

v6 And he came to an agreement to do it, and sought an opportunity to deliver him up to them away from the crowd.

v7 And the day of unleavened bread came, in which the passover was to be killed.

v8 And he sent Peter and John, saying, Go and prepare the passover for us, that we may eat [it].

v9 But they said to him, Where wilt thou that we prepare [it]?

v10 And he said to them, Behold, as ye enter into the city a man will meet you, carrying an earthen pitcher of water; follow him into the house where he goes in;

v11 and ye shall say to the master of the house, The Teacher says to thee, Where is the guest-chamber where I may eat the passover with my disciples?

v12 And *he* will shew you a large upper room furnished: there make ready.

v13 And having gone they found it as he had said to them; and they prepared the passover.

v14 And when the hour was come, he placed himself at table, and the [twelve] apostles with him.

v15 And he said to them, With desire I have desired to eat this passover with you before I suffer.

v16 For I say unto you, that I will not eat any more at all of it until it be fulfilled in the kingdom of God.

v17 And having received a cup, when he had given thanks he said, Take this and divide it among yourselves.

v18 For I say unto you, that I will not drink at all of the fruit of the vine until the kingdom of God come.

v19 And having taken a loaf, when he had given thanks, he broke [it], and gave [it] to them, saying, This is my body which is given for you: this do in remembrance of me.

v20 In like manner also the cup, after having supped, saying, This cup [is] the new covenant in my blood, which is poured out for you.

v21 Moreover, behold, the hand of him that delivers me up [is] with me on the table;

v22 and the Son of man indeed goes as it is determined, but woe unto that man by whom he is delivered up.

v23 And *they* began to question together among themselves who then it could be of them who was about to do this.

v24 And there was also a strife among them which of them should be held to be [the] greatest.

v25 And he said to them, The kings of the nations rule over them, and they that exercise authority over them are called benefactors.

v26 But *ye* [shall] not [be] thus; but let the greater among you be as the younger, and the leader as he that serves.

v27 For which [is] greater, he that is at table or he that serves? [Is] not he that is at table? But *I* am in the midst of you as the one that serves.

v28 But *ye* are they who have persevered with me in my temptations.

v29 And *I* appoint unto you, as my Father has appointed unto me, a kingdom,

v30 that ye may eat and drink at my table in my kingdom, and sit on thrones judging the twelve tribes of Israel.

v31 And the Lord said, Simon, Simon, behold, Satan has demanded to have you, to sift [you] as wheat;

v32 but *I* have besought for thee that thy faith fail not; and *thou*, when once thou hast been restored, confirm thy brethren.

v33 And he said to him, Lord, with thee I am ready to go both to prison and to death.

v34 And he said, I tell thee, Peter, [the] cock shall not crow to-day before that thou shalt thrice deny that thou knowest me.

v35 And he said to them, When I sent you without purse and scrip and sandals, did ye lack anything? And they said, Nothing.

v36 He said therefore to them, But now he that has a purse let him take [it], in like manner also a scrip, and he that has none let him sell his garment and buy a sword;

v37 for I say unto you, that this that is written must yet be accomplished in me, And he was reckoned with [the] lawless: for also the things concerning me have an end.

v38 And they said, Lord, behold here are two swords. And he said to them, It is enough.

v39 And going forth he went according to his custom to the mount of Olives, and the disciples also followed him.

v40 And when he was at the place he said to them, Pray that ye enter not into temptation.

v41 And he was withdrawn from them about a stone's throw, and having knelt down he prayed,

v42 saying, Father, if thou wilt remove this cup from me: -- but then, not my will, but thine be done.

v43 And an angel appeared to him from heaven strengthening him.

v44 And being in conflict he prayed more intently. And his sweat became as great drops of blood, falling down upon the earth.

v45 And rising up from his prayer, coming to the disciples, he found them sleeping from grief.

v46 And he said to them, Why sleep ye? rise up and pray that ye enter not into temptation.

v47 As he was yet speaking, behold, a crowd, and he that was called Judas, one of the twelve, went on before them, and drew near to Jesus to kiss him.

v48 And Jesus said to him, Judas, deliverest thou up the Son of man with a kiss?

v49 And they who were around him, seeing what was going to follow, said [to him], Lord, shall we smite with [the] sword?

v50 And a certain one from among them smote the bondman of the high priest and took off his right ear.

v51 And Jesus answering said, Suffer thus far; and having touched his ear, he healed him.

v52 And Jesus said to the chief priests and captains of the temple and elders, who were come against him, Have ye come out as against a robber with swords and sticks?

v53 When I was day by day with you in the temple ye did not stretch out your hands against me; but this is your hour and the power of darkness.

v54 And having laid hold on him, they led him [away], and they led [him] into the house of the high priest. And Peter followed afar off.

v55 And they having lit a fire in the midst of the court and sat down together, Peter sat among them.

v56 And a certain maid, having seen him sitting by the light, and having fixed her eyes upon him, said, And this [man] was with him.

v57 But he denied [him], saying, Woman, I do not know him.

v58 And after a short time another seeing him said, And *thou* art of them. But Peter said, Man, I am not.

v59 And after the lapse of about one hour another stoutly maintained it, saying, In truth this [man] also was with him, for also he is a Galilaean.

v60 And Peter said, Man, I know not what thou sayest. And immediately, while he was yet speaking, [the] cock crew.

v61 And the Lord, turning round, looked at Peter; and Peter remembered the word of the Lord, how he said to him, Before [the] cock crow thou shalt deny me thrice.

v62 And Peter, going forth without, wept bitterly.

v63 And the men who held him mocked him, beating [him];

v64 and covering him up, asked him saying, Prophesy, who is it that struck thee?

v65 And they said many other injurious things to him.

v66 And when it was day, the elderhood of the people, both [the] chief priests and scribes, were gathered together, and led him into their council, saying,

v67 If *thou* art the Christ, tell us. And he said to them, If I tell you, ye will not at all believe;

v68 and if I should ask [you], ye would not answer me at all, nor let me go;

v69 but henceforth shall the Son of man be sitting on the right hand of the power of God.

v70 And they all said, *Thou* then art the Son of God? And he said to them, *Ye* say that I am.

v71 And they said, What need have we any more of witness, for we have heard ourselves out of his mouth?
