# Luke 2 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/42 - Luke/Luke 1|← Luke 1]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/42 - Luke/Luke]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/42 - Luke/Luke 3|Luke 3 →]]

***

v1 But it came to pass in those days that a decree went out from Caesar Augustus, that a census should be made of all the habitable world.

v2 The census itself first took place when Cyrenius had the government of Syria.

v3 And all went to be inscribed in the census roll, each to his own city:

v4 and Joseph also went up from Galilee out of the city Nazareth to Judaea, to David's city, the which is called Bethlehem, because he was of the house and family of David,

v5 to be inscribed in the census roll with Mary who was betrothed to him [as his] wife, she being great with child.

v6 And it came to pass, while they were there, the days of her giving birth [to her child] were fulfilled,

v7 and she brought forth her first-born son, and wrapped him up in swaddling-clothes and laid him in the manger, because there was no room for them in the inn.

v8 And there were shepherds in that country abook_iding without, and keeping watch by night over their flock.

v9 And lo, an angel of [the] Lord was there by them, and [the] glory of [the] Lord shone around them, and they feared [with] great fear.

v10 And the angel said to them, Fear not, for behold, I announce to you glad tidings of great joy, which shall be to all the people;

v11 for to-day a Saviour has been born to you in David's city, who is Christ [the] Lord.

v12 And this is the sign to you: ye shall find a babe wrapped in swaddling-clothes, and lying in a manger.

v13 And suddenly there was with the angel a multitude of the heavenly host, praising God and saying,

v14 Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace, good pleasure in men.

v15 And it came to pass, as the angels departed from them into heaven, that the shepherds said to one another, Let us make our way then now as far as Bethlehem, and let us see this thing that is come to pass, which the Lord has made known to us.

v16 And they came with haste, and found both Mary and Joseph, and the babe lying in the manger;

v17 and having seen [it] they made known about the country the thing which had been said to them concerning this child.

v18 And all who heard [it] wondered at the things said to them by the shepherds.

v19 But Mary kept all these things [in her mind], pondering [them] in her heart.

v20 And the shepherds returned, glorifying and praising God for all things which they had heard and seen, as it had been said to them.

v21 And when eight days were fulfilled for circumcising him, his name was called Jesus, which was the name given by the angel before he had been conceived in the womb.

v22 And when the days were fulfilled for their purifying according to the law of Moses, they brought him to Jerusalem to present [him] to the Lord

v23 (as it is written in the law of [the] Lord: Every male that opens the womb shall be called holy to the Lord),

v24 and to offer a sacrifice according to what is said in the law of [the] Lord: A pair of turtle doves, or two young pigeons.

v25 And behold, there was a man in Jerusalem whose name was Simeon; and this man was just and pious, awaiting the consolation of Israel, and [the] Holy Spirit was upon him.

v26 And it was divinely communicated to him by the Holy Spirit, that he should not see death before he should see [the] Lord's Christ.

v27 And he came in the Spirit into the temple; and as the parents brought in the child Jesus that they might do for him according to the custom of the law,

v28 *he* received him into his arms, and blessed God, and said,

v29 Lord, now thou lettest thy bondman go, according to thy word, in peace;

v30 for mine eyes have seen thy salvation,

v31 which thou hast prepared before the face of all peoples;

v32 a light for revelation of [the] Gentiles and [the] glory of thy people Israel.

v33 And his father and mother wondered at the things which were said concerning him.

v34 And Simeon blessed them, and said to Mary his mother, Lo, this [child] is set for the fall and rising up of many in Israel, and for a sign spoken against;

v35 (and even a sword shall go through thine own soul;) so that [the] thoughts may be revealed from many hearts.

v36 And there was a prophetess, Anna, daughter of Phanuel, of [the] tribe of Asher, who was far advanced in years, having lived with [her] husband seven years from her virginity,

v37 and herself a widow up to eighty-four years; who did not depart from the temple, serving night and day with fastings and prayers;

v38 and she coming up the same hour gave praise to the Lord, and spoke of him to all those who waited for redemption in Jerusalem.

v39 And when they had completed all things according to the law of [the] Lord, they returned to Galilee to their own city Nazareth.

v40 And the child grew and waxed strong [in spirit], filled with wisdom, and God's grace was upon him.

v41 And his parents went yearly to Jerusalem at the feast of the passover.

v42 And when he was twelve years old, and they went up [to Jerusalem] according to the custom of the feast

v43 and had completed the days, as they returned, the boy Jesus remained behind in Jerusalem, and his parents knew not [of it];

v44 but, supposing him to be in the company that journeyed together, they went a day's journey, and sought him among their relations and acquaintances:

v45 and not having found him they returned to Jerusalem seeking him.

v46 And it came to pass, after three days they found him in the temple, sitting in the midst of the teachers and hearing them and asking them questions.

v47 And all who heard him were astonished at his understanding and answers.

v48 And when they saw him they were amazed: and his mother said to him, Child, why hast thou dealt thus with us? behold, thy father and I have sought thee distressed.

v49 And he said to them, Why [is it] that ye have sought me? did ye not know that I ought to be [occupied] in my Father's business?

v50 And they understood not the thing that he said to them.

v51 And he went down with them and came to Nazareth, and he was in subjection to them. And his mother kept all these things in her heart.

v52 And Jesus advanced in wisdom and stature, and in favour with God and men.
