# Luke 23 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/42 - Luke/Luke 22|← Luke 22]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/42 - Luke/Luke]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/42 - Luke/Luke 24|Luke 24 →]]

***

v1 And the whole multitude of them, rising up, led him to Pilate.

v2 And they began to accuse him, saying, We have found this [man] perverting our nation, and forbook_idding to give tribute to Caesar, saying that he himself is Christ, a king.

v3 And Pilate demanded of him saying, Art *thou* the king of the Jews? And he answering him said, Thou sayest.

v4 And Pilate said to the chief priests and the crowds, I find no guilt in this man.

v5 But they insisted, saying, He stirs up the people, teaching throughout all Judaea, beginning from Galilee even on to here.

v6 But Pilate, having heard Galilee [named], demanded if the man were a Galilaean;

v7 and having learned that he was of Herod's jurisdiction, remitted him to Herod, who himself also was at Jerusalem in those days.

v8 And when Herod saw Jesus he greatly rejoiced, for he had been a long while desirous of seeing him, because of hearing many things concerning him, and he hoped to see some sign done by him;

v9 and he questioned him in many words, but *he* answered him nothing.

v10 And the chief priests and the scribes stood and accused him violently.

v11 And Herod with his troops having set him at nought and mocked him, having put a splendid robe upon him, sent him back to Pilate.

v12 And Pilate and Herod became friends with one another the same day, for they had been at enmity before between themselves.

v13 And Pilate, having called together the chief priests and the rulers and the people,

v14 said to them, Ye have brought to me this man as turning away the people [to rebellion], and behold, I, having examined him before you, have found nothing criminal in this man as to the things of which ye accuse him;

v15 nor Herod either, for I remitted you to him, and behold, nothing worthy of death is done by him.

v16 Having chastised him therefore, I will release him.

v17 ( Now he was obliged to release one for them at the feast.)

v18 But they cried out in a mass saying, Away with this [man] and release Barabbas to us;

v19 who was one who, for a certain tumult which had taken place in the city, and [for] murder, had been cast into prison.

v20 Pilate therefore, desirous to release Jesus, again addressed [them].

v21 But they cried out in reply saying, Crucify, crucify him.

v22 And he said the third time to them, What evil then has this [man] done? I have found no cause of death in him: I will chastise him therefore and release him.

v23 But they were urgent with loud voices, begging that he might be crucified. And their voices [and those of the chief priests] prevailed.

v24 And Pilate adjudged that what they begged should take place.

v25 And he released him who, for tumult and murder, had been cast into prison, whom they begged for, and Jesus he delivered up to their will.

v26 And as they led him away, they laid hold on a certain Simon, a Cyrenian, coming from the field, and put the cross upon him to bear it behind Jesus.

v27 And a great multitude of the people, and of women who wailed and lamented him, followed him.

v28 And Jesus turning round to them said, Daughters of Jerusalem, do not weep over me, but weep over yourselves and over your children;

v29 for behold, days are coming in which they will say, Blessed [are] the barren, and wombs that have not borne, and breasts that have not given suck.

v30 Then shall they begin to say to the mountains, Fall upon us; and to the hills, Cover us:

v31 for if these things are done in the green tree, what shall take place in the dry?

v32 Now two others also, malefactors, were led with him to be put to death.

v33 And when they came to the place which is called Skull, there they crucified him, and the malefactors, one on the right hand, the other on the left.

v34 And Jesus said, Father, forgive them, for they know not what they do. And, parting out his garments, they cast lots.

v35 And the people stood beholding, and the rulers also [with them] sneered, saying, He has saved others; let him save himself if this is the Christ, the chosen one of God.

v36 And the soldiers also made game of him, coming up offering him vinegar,

v37 and saying, If *thou* be the king of the Jews, save thyself.

v38 And there was also an inscription [written] over him in Greek, and Roman, and Hebrew letters: This is the King of the Jews.

v39 Now one of the malefactors who had been hanged spoke insultingly to him, saying, Art not thou the Christ? save thyself and us.

v40 But the other answering rebuked him, saying, Dost *thou* too not fear God, thou that art under the same judgment?

v41 and *we* indeed justly, for we receive the just recompense of what we have done; but this [man] has done nothing amiss.

v42 And he said to Jesus, Remember me, [Lord,] when thou comest in thy kingdom.

v43 And Jesus said to him, Verily I say to thee, To-day shalt thou be with me in paradise.

v44 And it was about [the] sixth hour, and there came darkness over the whole land until [the] ninth hour.

v45 And the sun was darkened, and the veil of the temple rent in the midst.

v46 And Jesus, having cried with a loud voice, said, Father, into thy hands I commit my spirit. And having said this, he expired.

v47 Now the centurion, seeing what took place, glorified God, saying, In very deed this man was just.

v48 And all the crowds who had come together to that sight, having seen the things that took place, returned, beating [their] breasts.

v49 And all those who knew him stood afar off, the women also who had followed him from Galilee, beholding these things.

v50 And behold, a man named Joseph, who was a councillor, a good man and a just

v51 (this [man] had not assented to their counsel and deed), of Arimathaea, a city of the Jews, who also waited, [himself also,] for the kingdom of God

v52 -- he having gone to Pilate begged the body of Jesus;

v53 and having taken it down, wrapped it in fine linen and placed him in a tomb hewn in the rock, where no one had ever been laid.

v54 And it was preparation day, and [the] sabbath twilight was coming on.

v55 And women, who had come along with him out of Galilee, having followed, saw the sepulchre and how his body was placed.

v56 And having returned they prepared aromatic spices and ointments, and remained quiet on the sabbath, according to the commandment.
