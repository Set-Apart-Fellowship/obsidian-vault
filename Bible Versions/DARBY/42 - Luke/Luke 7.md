# Luke 7 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/42 - Luke/Luke 6|← Luke 6]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/42 - Luke/Luke]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/42 - Luke/Luke 8|Luke 8 →]]

***

v1 And when he had completed all his words in the hearing of the people, he entered into Capernaum.

v2 And a certain centurion's bondman who was dear to him was ill and about to die;

v3 and having heard of Jesus, he sent to him elders of the Jews, begging him that he might come and save his bondman.

v4 But they, being come to Jesus, besought him diligently, saying, He is worthy to whom thou shouldest grant this,

v5 for he loves our nation, and himself has built the synagogue for us.

v6 And Jesus went with them. But already, when he was not far from the house, the centurion sent to him friends, saying to him, Lord, do not trouble thyself, for I am not worthy that thou shouldest enter under my roof.

v7 Wherefore neither did I count myself worthy to come to thee. But say by a word and my servant shall be healed.

v8 For *I* also am a man placed under authority, having under myself soldiers, and I say to this [one], Go, and he goes; and to another, Come, and he comes; and to my bondman, Do this, and he does [it].

v9 And Jesus hearing this wondered at him, and turning to the crowd following him said, I say to you, Not even in Israel have I found so great faith.

v10 And they who had been sent returning to the house found the bondman, who was ill, in good health.

v11 And it came to pass afterwards he went into a city called Nain, and many of his disciples and a great crowd went with him.

v12 And as he drew near to the gate of the city, behold, a dead man was carried out, the only son of his mother, and she a widow, and a very considerable crowd of the city [was] with her.

v13 And the Lord, seeing her, was moved with compassion for her, and said to her, Weep not;

v14 and coming up he touched the bier, and the bearers stopped. And he said, Youth, I say to thee, Wake up.

v15 And the dead sat up and began to speak; and he gave him to his mother.

v16 And fear seized on all, and they glorified God, saying, A great prophet has been raised up amongst us; and God has visited his people.

v17 And this report went out in all Judaea concerning him, and in all the surrounding country.

v18 And the disciples of John brought him word concerning all these things:

v19 and John, having called two of his disciples, sent to Jesus, saying, Art *thou* he that is coming, or are we to wait for another?

v20 But the men having come to him said, John the baptist has sent us to thee, saying, Art *thou* he that is coming, or are we to wait for another?

v21 In that hour he healed many of diseases and plagues and evil spirits, and to many blind he granted sight.

v22 And Jesus answering said to them, Go, bring back word to John of what ye have seen and heard: that blind see, lame walk, lepers are cleansed, deaf hear, dead are raised, poor are evangelized;

v23 and blessed is whosoever shall not be offended in me.

v24 And the messengers of John having departed, he began to speak to the crowds concerning John: What went ye out into the wilderness to behold? a reed shaken by the wind?

v25 But what went ye out to see? a man clothed in delicate garments? Behold, those who are in splendid clothing and live luxuriously are in the courts of kings.

v26 But what went ye out to see? a prophet? Yea, I say to you, and [what is] more excellent than a prophet.

v27 This is he concerning whom it is written, Behold, *I* send my messenger before thy face, who shall prepare thy way before thee;

v28 for I say unto you, Among them that are born of women a greater [prophet] is no one than John [the baptist]; but he who is a little one in the kingdom of God is greater than he.

v29 (And all the people who heard [it], and the tax-gatherers, justified God, having been baptised with the baptism of John;

v30 but the Pharisees and the lawyers rendered null as to themselves the counsel of God, not having been baptised by him.)

v31 To whom therefore shall I liken the men of this generation, and to whom are they like?

v32 They are like children sitting in the market-place, and calling one to another and saying, We have piped to you, and ye have not danced; we have mourned to you, and ye have not wept.

v33 For John the baptist has come neither eating bread nor drinking wine, and ye say, He has a demon.

v34 The Son of man has come eating and drinking, and ye say, Behold an eater and wine-drinker, a friend of tax-gatherers and sinners;

v35 and wisdom has been justified of all her children.

v36 But one of the Pharisees begged him that he would eat with him. And entering into the house of the Pharisee he took his place at table;

v37 and behold, a woman in the city, who was a sinner, and knew that he was sitting at meat in the house of the Pharisee, having taken an alabaster box of myrrh,

v38 and standing at his feet behind [him] weeping, began to wash his feet with tears; and she wiped them with the hairs of her head, and kissed his feet, and anointed [them] with the myrrh.

v39 And the Pharisee who had invited him, seeing it, spoke with himself saying, This [person] if he were a prophet would have known who and what the woman is who touches him, for she is a sinner.

v40 And Jesus answering said to him, Simon, I have somewhat to say to thee. And he says, Teacher, say [it].

v41 There were two debtors of a certain creditor: one owed five hundred denarii and the other fifty;

v42 but as they had nothing to pay, he forgave both of them [their debt]: [say,] which of them therefore will love him most?

v43 And Simon answering said, I suppose he to whom he forgave the most. And he said to him, Thou hast rightly judged.

v44 And turning to the woman he said to Simon, Seest thou this woman? I entered into thy house; thou gavest me not water on my feet, but *she* has washed my feet with tears, and wiped them with her hair.

v45 Thou gavest me not a kiss, but *she* from the time I came in has not ceased kissing my feet.

v46 My head with oil thou didst not anoint, but *she* has anointed my feet with myrrh.

v47 For which cause I say to thee, Her many sins are forgiven; for she loved much; but he to whom little is forgiven loves little.

v48 And he said to her, Thy sins are forgiven.

v49 And they that were with [them] at table began to say within themselves, Who is this who forgives also sins?

v50 And he said to the woman, Thy faith has saved thee; go in peace.
