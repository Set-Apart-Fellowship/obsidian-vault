# 1 Chronicles 24 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 23|← 1 Chronicles 23]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 25|1 Chronicles 25 →]]

***

v1 And the divisions of the sons of Aaron: the sons of Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.

v2 And Nadab and Abihu died before their father, and had no children; and Eleazar and Ithamar exercised the priesthood.

v3 And David distributed them, both Zadok of the sons of Eleazar, and Ahimelech of the sons of Ithamar, according to their office in their service.

v4 And there were more head-men found of the sons of Eleazar than of the sons of Ithamar, and [thus] were they divided: of the sons of Eleazar there were sixteen heads of fathers' houses, and eight of the sons of Ithamar according to their fathers' houses.

v5 And they were divided by lot, one with another; for the princes of the sanctuary and the princes of God were of the sons of Eleazar and of the sons of Ithamar.

v6 And Shemaiah the son of Nethaneel the scribe, [one] of the Levites, inscribed them before the king, and the princes, and Zadok the priest, and Ahimelech the son of Abiathar, and the chief fathers of the priests and Levites: one father's house was drawn for Eleazar, and one drawn for Ithamar.

v7 And the first lot came forth for Jehoiarib, the second for Jedaiah,

v8 the third for Harim, the fourth for Seorim,

v9 the fifth for Malchijah, the sixth for Mijamin,

v10 the seventh for Hakkoz, the eighth for Abijah,

v11 the ninth for Jeshuah, the tenth for Shecaniah,

v12 the eleventh for Eliashib, the twelfth for Jakim,

v13 the thirteenth for Huppah, the fourteenth for Jeshebeab,

v14 the fifteenth for Bilgah, the sixteenth for Immer,

v15 the seventeenth for Hezir, the eighteenth for Happizez,

v16 the nineteenth for Pethahiah, the twentieth for Ezekiel,

v17 the twenty-first for Jachin, the twenty-second for Gamul,

v18 the twenty-third for Delaiah, the twenty-fourth for Maaziah.

v19 This is their ordering in their service to come into the house of Jehovah, according to their ordinance, through Aaron their father, as Jehovah the God of Israel had commanded him.

v20 And as for the rest of the sons of Levi: of the sons of Amram, Shubael; of the sons of Shubael, Jehdeiah.

v21 Of Rehabiah, of the sons of Rehabiah, the head was Jishijah.

v22 Of the Jizharites, Shelomoth; of the sons of Shelomoth, Jahath.

v23 -- And the sons [of Hebron]: Jerijah [the head], Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, Jekameam the fourth.

v24 The sons of Uzziel, Micah; of the sons of Micah, Shamir;

v25 the brother of Micah was Jishijah; of the sons of Jishijah, Zechariah.

v26 The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi; the sons of Jaaziah, his son.

v27 The sons of Merari by Jaaziah his son: Shoham, and Zaccur, and Ibri.

v28 Of Mahli: Eleazar, who had no sons.

v29 Of Kish, the sons of Kish: Jerahmeel.

v30 And the sons of Mushi: Mahli, and Eder, and Jerimoth. These were the sons of the Levites according to their fathers' houses.

v31 These likewise cast lots just as their brethren the sons of Aaron before David the king, and Zadok, and Ahimelech, and the chief fathers of the priests and Levites, -- the chief fathers just as the youngest of their brethren.
