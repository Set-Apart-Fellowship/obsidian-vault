# 1 Chronicles 16 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 15|← 1 Chronicles 15]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 17|1 Chronicles 17 →]]

***

v1 And they brought in the ark of God, and set it in the midst of the tent that David had spread for it; and they presented burnt-offerings and peace-offerings before God.

v2 And when David had ended offering up the burnt-offerings and the peace-offerings, he blessed the people in the name of Jehovah.

v3 And he dealt to every one of Israel, both man and woman, to every one a loaf of bread and a measure [of wine] and a raisin-cake.

v4 And he appointed certain of the Levites to do the service before the ark of Jehovah, and to celebrate, and to thank and praise Jehovah the God of Israel:

v5 Asaph the chief, and second to him Zechariah, Jeiel, and Shemiramoth, and Jehiel, and Mattithiah, and Eliab, and Benaiah and Obed-Edom, and Jeiel, with instruments of lutes and with harps; and Asaph sounding with cymbals;

v6 and Benaiah and Jahaziel the priests with trumpets continually, before the ark of the covenant of God.

v7 Then on that day David delivered first [this psalm] to give thanks to Jehovah through Asaph and his brethren.

v8 Give thanks unto Jehovah, call upon his name; Make known his acts among the peoples.

v9 Sing unto him, sing psalms unto him; Meditate upon all his wondrous works.

v10 Glory in his holy name: Let the heart of them rejoice that seek Jehovah.

v11 Seek Jehovah and his strength, Seek his face continually;

v12 Remember his wondrous works which he hath done; His miracles, and the judgments of his mouth:

v13 Ye seed of Israel his servant, Ye sons of Jacob, his chosen ones.

v14 He, Jehovah, is our God; His judgments are in all the earth.

v15 Be ye ever mindful of his covenant, The word which he commanded to a thousand generations, --

v16 Which he made with Abraham, And of his oath unto Isaac;

v17 And he confirmed it unto Jacob for a statute, Unto Israel for an everlasting covenant,

v18 Saying, Unto thee will I give the land of Canaan, The lot of your inheritance;

v19 When ye were a few men in number, Of small account, and strangers in it.

v20 And they went from nation to nation, And from one kingdom to another people.

v21 He suffered no man to oppress them, And reproved kings for their sakes,

v22 [Saying,] Touch not mine anointed ones, And do my prophets no harm.

v23 Sing unto Jehovah, all the earth; Publish his salvation from day to day.

v24 Declare his glory among the nations, His wondrous works among all peoples.

v25 For Jehovah is great, and exceedingly to be praised; And he is terrible above all gods.

v26 For all the gods of the peoples are idols; But Jehovah made the heavens.

v27 Majesty and splendour are before him; Strength and gladness in his place.

v28 Give unto Jehovah, ye families of peoples, Give unto Jehovah glory and strength!

v29 Give unto Jehovah the glory of his name! Bring an oblation, and come before him: Worship Jehovah in holy splendour.

v30 Tremble before him, all the earth: The world also is established, it shall not be moved.

v31 Let the heavens rejoice, and let the earth be glad; And let them say among the nations, Jehovah reigneth!

v32 Let the sea roar, and the fulness thereof; Let the field exult, and all that is therein.

v33 Then shall the trees of the forest sing out at the presence of Jehovah, For he cometh to judge the earth.

v34 Give thanks unto Jehovah, for he is good; For his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever.

v35 And say, Save us, O God of our salvation, And gather us, and deliver us from the nations, To give thanks unto thy holy name, To triumph in thy praise.

v36 Blessed be Jehovah the God of Israel, from eternity and to eternity! And all the people said, Amen! and praised Jehovah.

v37 And he left there, before the ark of the covenant of Jehovah, Asaph and his brethren, to do the service before the ark continually, as every day's duty required;

v38 and Obed-Edom, and their brethren, sixty-eight; Obed-Edom also, the son of Jeduthun, and Hosah as doorkeepers.

v39 And Zadok the priest, and his brethren the priests, before the tabernacle of Jehovah in the high place that was at Gibeon,

v40 to offer up burnt-offerings to Jehovah on the altar of burnt-offering continually, morning and evening, and according to all that is written in the law of Jehovah, which he commanded Israel;

v41 and with them Heman and Jeduthun, and the rest that were chosen, who were expressed by name, to give thanks to Jehovah, because his loving-kindness [endureth] for ever;

v42 and with them, [with] Heman and Jeduthun, trumpets and cymbals for those that should sound aloud; and the musical instruments of God. And the sons of Jeduthun were at the gate.

v43 And all the people departed every one to his house; and David returned to bless his household.
