# 1 Chronicles 1 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 2|1 Chronicles 2 →]]

***

v1 Adam, Seth, Enosh,

v2 Cainan, Mahalaleel, Jared,

v3 Enoch, Methushelah, Lemech,

v4 Noah; Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

v5 The sons of Japheth: Gomer, and Magog, and Madai, and Javan, and Tubal, and Meshech, and Tiras.

v6 -- And the sons of Gomer: Ashkenaz, and Diphath, and Togarmah.

v7 And the sons of Javan: Elishah and Tarshish, Kittim and Rodanim.

v8 The sons of Ham: Cush and Mizraim, Phut and Canaan.

v9 -- And the sons of Cush: Seba and Havilah, and Sabta, and Raama, and Sabtecha. And the sons of Raamah: Sheba and Dedan.

v10 And Cush begot Nimrod: he began to be mighty on the earth.

v11 -- And Mizraim begot the Ludim, and the Anamim, and the Lehabim, and the Naphtuhim,

v12 and the Pathrusim, and the Casluhim out of whom came the Philistines, and the Caphtorim.

v13 -- And Canaan begot Zidon his firstborn, and Heth,

v14 and the Jebusite, and the Amorite, and the Girgashite,

v15 and the Hivite, and the Arkite, and the Sinite,

v16 and the Arvadite, and the Zemarite, and the Hamathite.

v17 The sons of Shem: Elam, and Asshur, and Arphaxad, and Lud, and Aram, and Uz, and Hul, and Gether, and Meshech.

v18 -- And Arphaxad begot Shelah, and Shelah begot Eber.

v19 And to Eber were born two sons: the name of the one was Peleg, for in his days was the earth divided; and his brother's name was Joktan.

v20 And Joktan begot Almodad, and Sheleph, and Hazarmaveth, and Jerah,

v21 and Hadoram, and Uzal, and Diklah,

v22 and Ebal, and Abimael, and Sheba,

v23 and Ophir, and Havilah, and Jobab: all these were sons of Joktan.

v24 Shem, Arphaxad, Shelah,

v25 Eber, Peleg, Reu,

v26 Serug, Nahor, Terah,

v27 Abram: the same is Abraham.

v28 The sons of Abraham: Isaac and Ishmael.

v29 These are their generations: the firstborn of Ishmael, Nebaioth; and Kedar, and Adbeel, and Mibsam;

v30 Mishma and Dumah; Massa, Hadad, and Tema;

v31 Jetur, Naphish, and Kedmah: those are the sons of Ishmael.

v32 -- And the sons of Keturah, Abraham's concubine: she bore Zimran, and Jokshan, and Medan, and Midian, and Ishbak, and Shuah. And the sons of Jokshan: Sheba and Dedan.

v33 And the sons of Midian: Ephah, and Epher, and Enoch, and Abook_ida, and Eldaah. All these were sons of Keturah.

v34 And Abraham begot Isaac. The sons of Isaac: Esau and Israel.

v35 The sons of Esau: Eliphaz, Reuel, and Jeush, and Jaalam, and Korah.

v36 -- The sons of Eliphaz: Teman and Omar, Zephi and Gatam, Kenaz, and Timna, and Amalek.

v37 The sons of Reuel: Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah.

v38 And the sons of Seir: Lotan, and Shobal, and Zibeon, and Anah, and Dishon, and Ezer, and Dishan.

v39 -- And the sons of Lotan: Hori and Homam. And Lotan's sister, Timna.

v40 -- The sons of Shobal: Alian, and Manahath, and Ebal, Shephi and Onam. -- And the sons of Zibeon: Ajah and Anah.

v41 -- The sons of Anah: Dishon. -- And the sons of Dishon: Hamran, and Eshban, and Jithran, and Cheran.

v42 -- The sons of Ezer: Bilhan, and Zaavan, [and] Jaakan. -- The sons of Dishan: Uz and Aran.

v43 And these are the kings that reigned in the land of Edom before there reigned a king over the children of Israel: -- Bela the son of Beor; and the name of his city was Dinhabah.

v44 And Bela died; and Jobab the son of Zerah of Bozrah reigned in his stead.

v45 And Jobab died; and Husham of the land of the Temanites reigned in his stead.

v46 And Husham died; and Hadad the son of Bedad, who smote Midian in the fields of Moab, reigned in his stead: and the name of his city was Avith.

v47 And Hadad died; and Samlah of Masrekah reigned in his stead.

v48 And Samlah died; and Saul of Rehoboth on the river reigned in his stead.

v49 And Saul died; and Baal-hanan the son of Achbor reigned in his stead.

v50 And Baal-hanan died; and Hadad reigned in his stead: and the name of his city was Pai; and his wife's name was Mehetabel the daughter of Matred, daughter of Mezahab.

v51 And Hadad died. And the chiefs of Edom were: chief Timna, chief Aliah, chief Jetheth,

v52 chief Oholibamah, chief Elah, chief Pinon,

v53 chief Kenaz, chief Teman, chief Mibzar,

v54 chief Magdiel, chief Iram. These were the chiefs of Edom.
