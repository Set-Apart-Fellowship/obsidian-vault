# 1 Chronicles 8 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 7|← 1 Chronicles 7]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 9|1 Chronicles 9 →]]

***

v1 And Benjamin begot Bela his firstborn, Ashbel the second, and Aharah the third,

v2 Nohah the fourth, and Rapha the fifth.

v3 And Bela had sons: Addar, and Gera, and Abihud,

v4 and Abishua, and Naaman, and Ahoah,

v5 and Gera, and Shephuphan, and Huram.

v6 -- And these are the sons of Ehud (these were the chief fathers of the inhabitants of Geba; and they carried them away to Manahath;

v7 even Naaman, and Ahijah, and Gera; he removed them), and he begot Uzza and Ahihud.

v8 And Shaharaim begot [children] in the land of Moab after he had sent away Hushim and Baara his wives.

v9 And he begot of Hodesh his wife: Jobab, and Zibia, and Mesha, and Malcham,

v10 and Jeuz, and Shobia, and Mirmah; these were his sons, chief fathers.

v11 And of Hushim he begot Abitub, and Elpaal.

v12 And the sons of Elpaal: Eber, and Misham, and Shemer, who built Ono, and Lod and its dependent villages;

v13 and Beriah, and Shema, who were chief fathers of the inhabitants of Ajalon; they drove away the inhabitants of Gath.

v14 And Ahio, Shashak, and Jeremoth,

v15 and Zebadiah, and Arad, and Eder,

v16 and Michael, and Jishpah, and Joha were the sons of Beriah.

v17 And Zebadiah, and Meshullam, and Hiski, and Heber,

v18 and Jishmerai, and Jizliah, and Jobab were the sons of Elpaal.

v19 And Jakim, and Zichri, and Zabdi,

v20 and Elienai, and Zilthai, and Eliel,

v21 and Adaiah, and Beraiah, and Shimrath were the sons of Shimei.

v22 And Jishpah, and Eber, and Eliel,

v23 and Abdon, and Zichri, and Hanan,

v24 and Hananiah, and Elam, and Antothijah,

v25 and Jiphdeiah, and Penuel were the sons of Shashak.

v26 And Shamsherai, and Shehariah, and Athaliah,

v27 and Jaareshiah, and Elijah, and Zichri were the sons of Jeroham.

v28 These were the chief fathers, according to their generations, principal men; these dwelt in Jerusalem.

v29 And at Gibeon dwelt the father of Gibeon; and his wife's name was Maachah.

v30 And his son, the firstborn, was Abdon; and Zur, and Kish, and Baal, and Nadab,

v31 and Gedor, and Ahio, and Zecher.

v32 And Mikloth begot Shimeah. And these also dwelt beside their brethren in Jerusalem, with their brethren.

v33 And Ner begot Kish; and Kish begot Saul; and Saul begot Jonathan, and Malchishua, and Abinadab, and Esh-baal.

v34 And the son of Jonathan was Merib-Baal; and Merib-Baal begot Micah.

v35 And the sons of Micah: Pithon, and Melech, and Tarea, and Ahaz.

v36 And Ahaz begot Jehoaddah; and Jehoaddah begot Alemeth, and Azmaveth, and Zimri; and Zimri begot Moza;

v37 and Moza begot Binea: Rapha was his son, Eleasah his son, Azel his son.

v38 And Azel had six sons, and these are their names: Azrikam, Bochru, and Ishmael, and Sheariah, and Obadiah, and Hanan; all these were sons of Azel.

v39 And the sons of Eshek his brother were Ulam his firstborn, Jeush the second, and Eliphelet the third.

v40 And the sons of Ulam were mighty men of valour, archers; and they had many sons, and sons' sons, a hundred and fifty. All these were of the sons of Benjamin.
