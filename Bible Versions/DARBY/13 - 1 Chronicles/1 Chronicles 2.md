# 1 Chronicles 2 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 1|← 1 Chronicles 1]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 3|1 Chronicles 3 →]]

***

v1 These are the sons of Israel: Reuben, Simeon, Levi and Judah, Issachar and Zebulun,

v2 Dan, Joseph and Benjamin, Naphtali, Gad and Asher.

v3 The sons of Judah: Er, and Onan, and Shelah: [which] three were born to him of the daughter of Shua the Canaanitess. And Er, Judah's firstborn, was wicked in the sight of Jehovah; and he slew him.

v4 And Tamar his daughter-in-law bore him Pherez and Zerah. All the sons of Judah were five.

v5 The sons of Pherez: Hezron and Hamul.

v6 And the sons of Zerah: Zimri, and Ethan, and Heman, and Calcol, and Dara: five of them in all.

v7 And the sons of Carmi: Achar, the troubler of Israel, who transgressed in the accursed thing.

v8 And the sons of Ethan: Azariah.

v9 And the sons of Hezron, who were born to him: Jerahmeel, and Ram, and Chelubai.

v10 And Ram begot Amminadab; and Amminadab begot Nahshon, prince of the children of Judah;

v11 and Nahshon begot Salma, and Salma begot Boaz,

v12 and Boaz begot Obed, and Obed begot Jesse;

v13 and Jesse begot his firstborn Eliab, and Abinadab the second, and Shimea the third,

v14 Nethaneel the fourth, Raddai the fifth,

v15 Ozem the sixth, David the seventh;

v16 and their sisters were Zeruiah and Abigail. And the sons of Zeruiah: Abishai, and Joab, and Asahel, three.

v17 And Abigail bore Amasa; and the father of Amasa was Jether the Ishmaelite.

v18 And Caleb the son of Hezron had children by Azubah [his] wife, and by Jerioth: her sons are these: Jesher, and Shobab, and Ardon.

v19 And Azubah died, and Caleb took him Ephrath, and she bore him Hur.

v20 And Hur begot Uri, and Uri begot Bezaleel.

v21 And afterwards Hezron went to the daughter of Machir, the father of Gilead, and he took her when he was sixty years old; and she bore him Segub.

v22 And Segub begot Jair, who had twenty-three cities in the land of Gilead;

v23 and Geshur and Aram took the villages of Jair from them, with Kenath and its dependent towns, sixty cities. All these were sons of Machir the father of Gilead.

v24 And after the death of Hezron in Caleb-Ephratah, Abijah, Hezron's wife, bore him Ashhur, the father of Tekoa.

v25 And the sons of Jerahmeel the firstborn of Hezron were: Ram the firstborn, and Bunah, and Oren, and Ozem, of Ahijah.

v26 And Jerahmeel had another wife, whose name was Atarah; she was the mother of Onam.

v27 And the sons of Ram, the firstborn of Jerahmeel, were Maaz, and Jamin, and Eker.

v28 And the sons of Onam were Shammai and Jada. And the sons of Shammai: Nadab and Abishur.

v29 And the name of the wife of Abishur was Abihail, and she bore him Ahban and Molid.

v30 And the sons of Nadab: Seled and Appaim; and Seled died without sons.

v31 And the sons of Appaim: Jishi; and the sons of Jishi: Sheshan; and the sons of Sheshan: Ahlai.

v32 And the sons of Jada, the brother of Shammai: Jether and Jonathan; and Jether died without sons.

v33 And the sons of Jonathan: Peleth and Zaza. These were the sons of Jerahmeel.

v34 And Sheshan had no sons, but daughters; and Sheshan had an Egyptian servant, whose name was Jarha;

v35 and Sheshan gave his daughter to Jarha his servant as wife, and she bore him Attai.

v36 And Attai begot Nathan, and Nathan begot Zabad,

v37 and Zabad begot Ephlal, and Ephlal begot Obed,

v38 and Obed begot Jehu, and Jehu begot Azariah,

v39 and Azariah begot Helez, and Helez begot Elasah,

v40 and Elasah begot Sismai, and Sismai begot Shallum,

v41 and Shallum begot Jekamiah, and Jekamiah begot Elishama.

v42 And the sons of Caleb the brother of Jerahmeel were Mesha his firstborn, who was the father of Ziph; and the sons of Mareshah the father of Hebron.

v43 And the sons of Hebron: Korah, and Tappuah, and Rekem, and Shema.

v44 And Shema begot Raham, the father of Jorkeam. And Rekem begot Shammai;

v45 and the son of Shammai was Maon; and Maon was the father of Beth-zur.

v46 And Ephah, Caleb's concubine, bore Haran, and Moza, and Gazez; and Haran begot Gazez.

v47 And the sons of Jehdai: Regem, and Jotham, and Geshan, and Pelet, and Ephah, and Shaaph.

v48 Maachah, Caleb's concubine, bore Sheber and Tirhanah;

v49 and she bore Shaaph the father of Madmannah, Sheva the father of Machbena and the father of Gibea. And the daughter of Caleb was Achsah.

v50 These are the sons of Caleb. The sons of Hur, the firstborn of Ephratah: Shobal the father of Kirjath-jearim,

v51 Salma the father of Bethlehem, Hareph the father of Beth-gader.

v52 And Shobal the father of Kirjath-jearim had sons: Haroeh, Hazi-Hammenuhoth.

v53 And the families of Kirjath-jearim were the Jithrites, and the Puthites, and the Shumathites, and the Mishraites; of them came the Zoreathites and the Eshtaolites.

v54 The sons of Salma: Bethlehem, and the Netophathites. Atroth-Beth-Joab, and the Hazi-Hammana-hethites, the Zorites;

v55 and the families of the scribes who dwelt at Jabez: the Tireathites, the Shimeathites, the Suchathites. These are the Kenites that came of Hammath, the father of the house of Rechab.
