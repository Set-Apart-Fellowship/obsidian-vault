# 1 Chronicles 25 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 24|← 1 Chronicles 24]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 26|1 Chronicles 26 →]]

***

v1 And David and the captains of the host separated for the service those of the sons of Asaph and of Heman and of Jeduthun who were to prophesy with harps and lutes and cymbals; and the number of the men employed according to their service was:

v2 of the sons of Asaph: Zaccur, and Joseph, and Nethaniah, and Asharelah, the sons of Asaph under the direction of Asaph, who prophesied at the direction of the king.

v3 Of Jeduthun, the sons of Jeduthun: Gedaliah, and Zeri, and Isaiah, Hashabiah, and Mattithiah, [and Shimei] six, under the direction of their father Jeduthun, who prophesied with the harp, to give thanks and to praise Jehovah.

v4 Of Heman, the sons of Heman: Bukkijah, Mattaniah, Uzziel, Shebuel, and Jerimoth, Hananiah, Hanani, Eliathah, Giddalti, and Romamti-ezer, Joshbekashah, Mallothi, Hothir, Mahazioth:

v5 all these were sons of Heman the king's seer in the words of God, to exalt his power; and God had given to Heman fourteen sons and three daughters.

v6 All these were under the direction of their fathers Asaph, Jeduthun, and Heman, for song in the house of Jehovah, with cymbals, lutes and harps, for the service of the house of God, under the direction of the king.

v7 And the number of them, with their brethren that were instructed in the songs of Jehovah, all of them skilful, was two hundred and eighty-eight.

v8 And they cast lots with one another over the charges, the small as well as the great, the teacher with the scholar.

v9 And the first lot came forth for Asaph to Joseph; to Gedaliah the second: he and his brethren and his sons were twelve.

v10 The third to Zaccur; his sons and his brethren, twelve.

v11 The fourth to Jizri; his sons and his brethren, twelve.

v12 The fifth to Nethaniah; his sons and his brethren, twelve.

v13 The sixth to Bukkijah; his sons and his brethren, twelve.

v14 The seventh to Jesharelah; his sons and his brethren, twelve.

v15 The eighth to Isaiah; his sons and his brethren, twelve.

v16 The ninth to Mattaniah; his sons and his brethren, twelve.

v17 The tenth to Shimei; his sons and his brethren, twelve.

v18 The eleventh to Azareel; his sons and his brethren, twelve.

v19 The twelfth to Hashabiah; his sons and his brethren, twelve.

v20 The thirteenth to Shubael; his sons and his brethren, twelve.

v21 The fourteenth to Mattithiah; his sons and his brethren, twelve.

v22 The fifteenth to Jeremoth; his sons and his brethren, twelve.

v23 The sixteenth to Hananiah; his sons and his brethren, twelve.

v24 The seventeenth to Joshbekashah; his sons and his brethren, twelve.

v25 The eighteenth to Hanani; his sons and his brethren, twelve.

v26 The nineteenth to Mallothi; his sons and his brethren, twelve.

v27 The twentieth to Elijathah; his sons and his brethren, twelve.

v28 The twenty-first to Hothir; his sons and his brethren, twelve.

v29 The twenty-second to Giddalti; his sons and his brethren, twelve.

v30 The twenty-third to Mahazioth; his sons and his brethren, twelve.

v31 The twenty-fourth to Romamti-ezer; his sons and his brethren, twelve.
