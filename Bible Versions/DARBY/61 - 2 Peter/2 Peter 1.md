# 2 Peter 1 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 2|2 Peter 2 →]]

***

v1 Simon Peter, bondman and apostle of Jesus Christ, to them that have received like precious faith with us through [the] righteousness of our God and Saviour Jesus Christ:

v2 Grace and peace be multiplied to you in [the] knowledge of God and of Jesus our Lord.

v3 As his divine power has given to us all things which relate to life and godliness, through the knowledge of him that has called us by glory and virtue,

v4 through which he has given to us the greatest and precious promises, that through these ye may become partakers of [the] divine nature, having escaped the corruption that is in the world through lust.

v5 But for this very reason also, using therewith all diligence, in your faith have also virtue, in virtue knowledge,

v6 in knowledge temperance, in temperance endurance, in endurance godliness,

v7 in godliness brotherly love, in brotherly love love:

v8 for these things existing and abounding in you make [you] to be neither idle nor unfruitful as regards the knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ;

v9 for he with whom these things are not present is blind, short-sighted, and has forgotten the purging of his former sins.

v10 Wherefore the rather, brethren, use diligence to make your calling and election sure, for doing these things ye will never fall;

v11 for thus shall the entrance into the everlasting kingdom of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ be richly furnished unto you.

v12 Wherefore I will be careful to put you always in mind of these things, although knowing [them] and established in the present truth.

v13 But I account it right, as long as I am in this tabernacle, to stir you up by putting [you] in remembrance,

v14 knowing that the putting off of my tabernacle is speedily [to take place], as also our Lord Jesus Christ has manifested to me;

v15 but I will use diligence, that after my departure ye should have also, at any time, [in your power] to call to mind these things.

v16 For we have not made known to you the power and coming of our Lord Jesus Christ, following cleverly imagined fables, but having been eyewitnesses of *his* majesty.

v17 For he received from God [the] Father honour and glory, such a voice being uttered to him by the excellent glory: This is my beloved Son, in whom *I* have found my delight;

v18 and this voice *we* heard uttered from heaven, being with him on the holy mountain.

v19 And we have the prophetic word [made] surer, to which ye do well taking heed (as to a lamp shining in an obscure place) until [the] day dawn and [the] morning star arise in your hearts;

v20 knowing this first, that [the scope of] no prophecy of scripture is had from its own particular interpretation,

v21 for prophecy was not ever uttered by [the] will of man, but holy men of God spake under the power of [the] Holy Spirit.
