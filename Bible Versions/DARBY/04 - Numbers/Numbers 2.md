# Numbers 2 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/04 - Numbers/Numbers 1|← Numbers 1]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/04 - Numbers/Numbers]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/04 - Numbers/Numbers 3|Numbers 3 →]]

***

v1 And Jehovah spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,

v2 The children of Israel shall encamp every one by his standard, with the ensign of their father's house; round about the tent of meeting, afar off, opposite to it shall they encamp.

v3 And [for] those encamping eastward toward the sun-rising [there shall be] the standard of the camp of Judah according to their hosts; and the prince of the sons of Judah shall be Nahshon the son of Amminadab;

v4 and his host, even those that were numbered of them, were seventy-four thousand six hundred.

v5 And those that encamp next unto him shall be the tribe of Issachar; and the prince of the sons of Issachar shall be Nethaneel the son of Zuar;

v6 and his host, even those that were numbered thereof, fifty-four thousand four hundred.

v7 [With them shall be] the tribe of Zebulun; and the prince of the sons of Zebulun shall be Eliab the son of Helon;

v8 and his host, even those that were numbered thereof, fifty-seven thousand four hundred.

v9 All that were numbered of the camp of Judah were a hundred and eighty-six thousand four hundred, according to their hosts. They shall set forth first.

v10 The standard of the camp of Reuben shall be southward according to their hosts; and the prince of the sons of Reuben shall be Elizur the son of Shedeur;

v11 and his host, even those that were numbered thereof, forty-six thousand five hundred.

v12 And those that encamp by him shall be the tribe of Simeon; and the prince of the sons of Simeon shall be Shelumiel the son of Zurishaddai;

v13 and his host, even those that were numbered of them, fifty-nine thousand three hundred.

v14 And [with them shall be] the tribe of Gad; and the prince of the sons of Gad shall be Eliasaph the son of Reuel;

v15 and his host, even those that were numbered of them, forty-five thousand six hundred and fifty.

v16 All that were numbered of the camp of Reuben were a hundred and fifty-one thousand four hundred and fifty, according to their hosts. And they shall set forth second.

v17 And the tent of meeting shall set forth, the camp of the Levites in the midst of the camps; as they encamp, so shall they set forth, every man in his place, according to their standards.

v18 The standard of the camp of Ephraim according to their hosts shall be westward; and the prince of the sons of Ephraim shall be Elishama the son of Ammihud;

v19 and his host, even those that were numbered of them, forty thousand five hundred.

v20 And by him shall be the tribe of Manasseh; and the prince of the sons of Manasseh shall be Gamaliel the son of Pedahzur;

v21 and his host, even those that were numbered of them, thirty-two thousand two hundred.

v22 And [with them shall be] the tribe of Benjamin; and the prince of the sons of Benjamin shall be Abook_idan the son of Gideoni;

v23 and his host, even those that were numbered of them, thirty-five thousand four hundred.

v24 All that were numbered of the camp of Ephraim were a hundred and eight thousand one hundred, according to their hosts. And they shall set forth third.

v25 The standard of the camp of Dan shall be northward according to their hosts; and the prince of the sons of Dan shall be Ahiezer the son of Ammishaddai;

v26 and his host, even those that were numbered of them, sixty-two thousand seven hundred.

v27 And those that encamp by him shall be the tribe of Asher; and the prince of the sons of Asher shall be Pagiel the son of Ocran;

v28 and his host, even those that were numbered of them, forty-one thousand five hundred.

v29 And [with them shall be] the tribe of Naphtali; and the prince of the sons of Naphtali shall be Ahira the son of Enan;

v30 and his host, even those that were numbered of them, fifty-three thousand four hundred.

v31 All that were numbered of the camp of Dan were a hundred and fifty-seven thousand six hundred. They shall set forth last according to their standards.

v32 These are those that were numbered of the children of Israel, according to their fathers' houses: all those that were numbered of the camps, according to their hosts, were six hundred and three thousand five hundred and fifty.

v33 But the Levites were not numbered among the children of Israel; as Jehovah had commanded Moses.

v34 And the children of Israel did according to all that Jehovah had commanded Moses: so they encamped according to their standards, and so they journeyed, every one according to their families, according to their fathers' houses.
