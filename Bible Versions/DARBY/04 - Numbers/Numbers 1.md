# Numbers 1 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/04 - Numbers/Numbers]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/04 - Numbers/Numbers 2|Numbers 2 →]]

***

v1 And Jehovah spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai in the tent of meeting, on the first of the second month, in the second year after their departure from the land of Egypt, saying,

v2 Take the sum of the whole assembly of the children of Israel, after their families, according to their fathers' houses, by the number of the names, every male, according to their polls;

v3 from twenty years and upward, all that go forth to military service in Israel: ye shall number them according to their hosts, thou and Aaron.

v4 And with you there shall be a man for every tribe, a man who is the head of his father's house.

v5 And these are the names of the men that shall stand with you: for Reuben, Elizur the son of Shedeur;

v6 for Simeon, Shelumiel the son of Zurishaddai;

v7 for Judah, Nahshon the son of Amminadab;

v8 for Issachar, Nethaneel the son of Zuar;

v9 for Zebulun, Eliab the son of Helon;

v10 for the children of Joseph: for Ephraim, Elishama the son of Ammihud; for Manasseh, Gamaliel the son of Pedahzur;

v11 for Benjamin, Abook_idan the son of Gideoni;

v12 for Dan, Ahiezer the son of Ammishaddai;

v13 for Asher, Pagiel the son of Ocran;

v14 for Gad, Eliasaph the son of Deuel;

v15 for Naphtali, Ahira the son of Enan.

v16 These were those summoned of the assembly, princes of the tribes of their fathers, the heads of the thousands of Israel.

v17 And Moses and Aaron took these men who are expressed by their names,

v18 and gathered the whole assembly together on the first of the second month. And they declared their pedigrees after their families, according to their fathers' houses, by the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, according to their polls.

v19 As Jehovah had commanded Moses, so he numbered them in the wilderness of Sinai.

v20 And the sons of Reuben, Israel's eldest son, their generations, after their families, according to their fathers' houses, by the number of the names, according to their polls, every male from twenty years old and upward, all that went forth to military service:

v21 those that were numbered of them, of the tribe of Reuben, were forty-six thousand five hundred.

v22 Of the sons of Simeon: their generations, after their families, according to their fathers' houses, those that were numbered of them, by the number of the names, according to their polls, every male from twenty years old and upward, all that went forth to military service:

v23 those that were numbered of them, of the tribe of Simeon, were fifty-nine thousand three hundred.

v24 Of the sons of Gad: their generations, after their families, according to their fathers' houses, by the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all that went forth to military service:

v25 those that were numbered of them, of the tribe of Gad, were forty-five thousand six hundred and fifty.

v26 Of the sons of Judah: their generations, after their families, according to their fathers' houses, by the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all that went forth to military service:

v27 those that were numbered of them, of the tribe of Judah, were seventy-four thousand six hundred.

v28 Of the sons of Issachar: their generations, after their families, according to their fathers' houses, by the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all that went forth to military service:

v29 those that were numbered of them, of the tribe of Issachar, were fifty-four thousand four hundred.

v30 Of the sons of Zebulun: their generations, after their families, according to their fathers' houses, by the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all that went forth to military service:

v31 those that were numbered of them, of the tribe of Zebulun, were fifty-seven thousand four hundred.

v32 Of the sons of Joseph: of the children of Ephraim: their generations, after their families, according to their fathers' houses, by the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all that went forth to military service:

v33 those that were numbered of them, of the tribe of Ephraim, were forty thousand five hundred.

v34 Of the children of Manasseh: their generations, after their families, according to their fathers' houses, by the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all that went forth to military service:

v35 those that were numbered of them, of the tribe of Manasseh, were thirty-two thousand two hundred.

v36 Of the sons of Benjamin: their generations, after their families, according to their fathers' houses, by the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all that went forth to military service:

v37 those that were numbered of them, of the tribe of Benjamin, were thirty-five thousand four hundred.

v38 Of the sons of Dan: their generations, after their families, according to their fathers' houses, by the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all that went forth to military service:

v39 those that were numbered of them, of the tribe of Dan, were sixty-two thousand seven hundred.

v40 Of the sons of Asher: their generations, after their families, according to their fathers' houses, by the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all that went forth to military service:

v41 those that were numbered of them, of the tribe of Asher, were forty-one thousand five hundred.

v42 Of the sons of Naphtali: their generations, after their families, according to their fathers' houses, by the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, all that went forth to military service:

v43 those that were numbered of them, of the tribe of Naphtali, were fifty-three thousand four hundred.

v44 These are those that were numbered, whom Moses and Aaron numbered, and the princes of Israel, the twelve men: each one was for the house of his fathers.

v45 And all those that were numbered of the children of Israel, according to their fathers' houses, from twenty years old and upward, all that went forth to military service in Israel,

v46 all they that were numbered were six hundred and three thousand five hundred and fifty.

v47 But the Levites after the tribe of their fathers were not numbered among them.

v48 For Jehovah had spoken to Moses, saying,

v49 Only thou shalt not number the tribe of Levi, neither take the sum of them among the children of Israel.

v50 But thou, appoint the Levites over the tabernacle of testimony, and over all the vessels thereof, and over all things that belong to it: they shall bear the tabernacle, and all its vessels; and they shall serve it, and round about the tabernacle shall they encamp;

v51 and when the tabernacle setteth forward, the Levites shall take it down; and when the tabernacle encampeth, the Levites shall set it up; and the stranger that cometh near shall be put to death.

v52 And the children of Israel shall encamp every man in his camp, and every man by his own standard, according to their hosts;

v53 but the Levites shall encamp round about the tabernacle of testimony, that there come not wrath upon the assembly of the children of Israel; and the Levites shall keep the charge of the tabernacle of testimony.

v54 And the children of Israel did so; according to all that Jehovah had commanded Moses, so did they.
