# Ephesians 3 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2|← Ephesians 2]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 4|Ephesians 4 →]]

***

v1 For this reason *I* Paul, prisoner of the Christ Jesus for you nations,

v2 (if indeed ye have heard of the administration of the grace of God which has been given to me towards you,

v3 that by revelation the mystery has been made known to me, (according as I have written before briefly,

v4 by which, in reading it, ye can understand my intelligence in the mystery of the Christ,)

v5 which in other generations has not been made known to the sons of men, as it has now been revealed to his holy apostles and prophets in [the power of the] Spirit,

v6 that [they who are of] the nations should be joint heirs, and a joint body, and joint partakers of [his] promise in Christ Jesus by the glad tidings;

v7 of which I am become minister according to the gift of the grace of God given to me, according to the working of his power.

v8 To me, less than the least of all saints, has this grace been given, to announce among the nations the glad tidings of the unsearchable riches of the Christ,

v9 and to enlighten all [with the knowledge of] what is the administration of the mystery hidden throughout the ages in God, who has created all things,

v10 in order that now to the principalities and authorities in the heavenlies might be made known through the assembly the all-various wisdom of God,

v11 according to [the] purpose of the ages, which he purposed in Christ Jesus our Lord,

v12 in whom we have boldness and access in confidence by the faith of him.

v13 Wherefore I beseech [you] not to faint through my tribulations for you, which is your glory.

v14 For this reason I bow my knees to the Father [of our Lord Jesus Christ],

v15 of whom every family in [the] heavens and on earth is named,

v16 in order that he may give you according to the riches of his glory, to be strengthened with power by his Spirit in the inner man;

v17 that the Christ may dwell, through faith, in your hearts, being rooted and founded in love,

v18 in order that ye may be fully able to apprehend with all the saints what [is] the breadth and length and depth and height;

v19 and to know the love of the Christ which surpasses knowledge; that ye may be filled [even] to all the fulness of God.

v20 But to him that is able to do far exceedingly above all which we ask or think, according to the power which works in us,

v21 to him be glory in the assembly in Christ Jesus unto all generations of the age of ages. Amen).
