# Ephesians 6 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5|← Ephesians 5]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians]]

***

v1 Children, obey your parents in [the] Lord, for this is just.

v2 Honour thy father and thy mother, which is the first commandment with a promise,

v3 that it may be well with thee, and that thou mayest be long-lived on the earth.

v4 And [ye] fathers, do not provoke your children to anger, but bring them up in [the] discipline and admonition of [the] Lord.

v5 Bondmen, obey masters according to flesh, with fear and trembling, in simplicity of your heart as to the Christ;

v6 not with eye-service as men-pleasers; but as bondmen of Christ, doing the will of God from [the] soul,

v7 serving with good will as to the Lord, and not to men;

v8 knowing that whatever good each shall do, this he shall receive of [the] Lord, whether bond or free.

v9 And, masters, do the same things towards them, giving up threatening, knowing that both their and your Master is in heaven, and there is no acceptance of persons with him.

v10 For the rest, brethren, be strong in [the] Lord, and in the might of his strength.

v11 Put on the panoply of God, that ye may be able to stand against the artifices of the devil:

v12 because our struggle is not against blood and flesh, but against principalities, against authorities, against the universal lords of this darkness, against spiritual [power] of wickedness in the heavenlies.

v13 For this reason take [to you] the panoply of God, that ye may be able to withstand in the evil day, and, having accomplished all things, to stand.

v14 Stand therefore, having girt about your loins with truth, and having put on the breastplate of righteousness,

v15 and shod your feet with [the] preparation of the glad tidings of peace:

v16 besides all [these], having taken the shield of faith with which ye will be able to quench all the inflamed darts of the wicked one.

v17 Have also the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is God's word;

v18 praying at all seasons, with all prayer and supplication in [the] Spirit, and watching unto this very thing with all perseverance and supplication for all the saints;

v19 and for me in order that utterance may be given to me in [the] opening of my mouth to make known with boldness the mystery of the glad tidings,

v20 for which I am an ambassador [bound] with a chain, that I may be bold in it as I ought to speak.

v21 But in order that *ye* also may know what concerns me, how I am getting on, Tychicus, the beloved brother and faithful minister in [the] Lord, shall make all things known to you;

v22 whom I have sent to you for this very thing, that ye may know of our affairs and that he may encourage your hearts.

v23 Peace to the brethren, and love with faith, from God [the] Father and [the] Lord Jesus Christ.

v24 Grace with all them that love our Lord Jesus Christ in incorruption.
