# Ephesians 4 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 3|← Ephesians 3]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5|Ephesians 5 →]]

***

v1 *I*, the prisoner in [the] Lord, exhort you therefore to walk worthy of the calling wherewith ye have been called,

v2 with all lowliness and meekness, with long-suffering, bearing with one another in love;

v3 using diligence to keep the unity of the Spirit in the uniting bond of peace.

v4 [There is] one body and one Spirit, as ye have been also called in one hope of your calling;

v5 one Lord, one faith, one baptism;

v6 one God and Father of all, who is over all, and through all, and in us all.

v7 But to each one of us has been given grace according to the measure of the gift of the Christ.

v8 Wherefore he says, Having ascended up on high, he has led captivity captive, and has given gifts to men.

v9 But that he ascended, what is it but that he also descended into the lower parts of the earth?

v10 He that descended is the same who has also ascended up above all the heavens, that he might fill all things;

v11 and *he* has given some apostles, and some prophets, and some evangelists, and some shepherds and teachers,

v12 for the perfecting of the saints; with a view to [the] work of [the] ministry, with a view to the edifying of the body of Christ;

v13 until we all arrive at the unity of the faith and of the knowledge of the Son of God, at [the] full-grown man, at [the] measure of the stature of the fulness of the Christ;

v14 in order that we may be no longer babes, tossed and carried about by every wind of *that* teaching [which is] in the sleight of men, in unprincipled cunning with a view to systematized error;

v15 but, holding the truth in love, we may grow up to him in all things, who is the head, the Christ:

v16 from whom the whole body, fitted together, and connected by every joint of supply, according to [the] working in [its] measure of each one part, works for itself the increase of the body to its self-building up in love.

v17 This I say therefore, and testify in [the] Lord, that ye should no longer walk as [the rest of] the nations walk in [the] vanity of their mind,

v18 being darkened in understanding, estranged from the life of God by reason of the ignorance which is in them, by reason of the hardness of their hearts,

v19 who having cast off all feeling, have given themselves up to lasciviousness, to work all uncleanness with greedy unsatisfied lust.

v20 But *ye* have not thus learnt the Christ,

v21 if ye have heard him and been instructed in him according as [the] truth is in Jesus;

v22 [namely] your having put off according to the former conversation the old man which corrupts itself according to the deceitful lusts;

v23 and being renewed in the spirit of your mind;

v24 and [your] having put on the new man, which according to God is created in truthful righteousness and holiness.

v25 Wherefore, having put off falsehood, speak truth every one with his neighbour, because we are members one of another.

v26 Be angry, and do not sin; let not the sun set upon your wrath,

v27 neither give room for the devil.

v28 Let the stealer steal no more, but rather let him toil, working what is honest with [his] hands, that he may have to distribute to him that has need.

v29 Let no corrupt word go out of your mouth, but if [there be] any good one for needful edification, that it may give grace to those that hear [it].

v30 And do not grieve the Holy Spirit of God, with which ye have been sealed for [the] day of redemption.

v31 Let all bitterness, and heat of passion, and wrath, and clamour, and injurious language, be removed from you, with all malice;

v32 and be to one another kind, compassionate, forgiving one another, so as God also in Christ has forgiven you.
