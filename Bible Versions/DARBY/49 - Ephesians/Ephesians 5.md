# Ephesians 5 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 4|← Ephesians 4]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 6|Ephesians 6 →]]

***

v1 Be ye therefore imitators of God, as beloved children,

v2 and walk in love, even as the Christ loved us, and delivered himself up for us, an offering and sacrifice to God for a sweet-smelling savour.

v3 But fornication and all uncleanness or unbridled lust, let it not be even named among you, as it becomes saints;

v4 and filthiness and foolish talking, or jesting, which are not convenient; but rather thanksgiving.

v5 For this ye are [well] informed of, knowing that no fornicator, or unclean person, or person of unbridled lust, who is an idolater, has inheritance in the kingdom of the Christ and God.

v6 Let no one deceive you with vain words, for on account of these things the wrath of God comes upon the sons of disobedience.

v7 Be not ye therefore fellow-partakers with them;

v8 for ye were once darkness, but now light in [the] Lord; walk as children of light,

v9 (for the fruit of the light [is] in all goodness and righteousness and truth,)

v10 proving what is agreeable to the Lord;

v11 and do not have fellowship with the unfruitful works of darkness, but rather also reprove [them],

v12 for the things that are done by them in secret it is shameful even to say.

v13 But all things having their true character exposed by the light are made manifest; for that which makes everything manifest is light.

v14 Wherefore he says, Wake up, [thou] that sleepest, and arise up from among the dead, and the Christ shall shine upon thee.

v15 See therefore how ye walk carefully, not as unwise but as wise,

v16 redeeming the time, because the days are evil.

v17 For this reason be not foolish, but understanding what [is] the will of the Lord.

v18 And be not drunk with wine, in which is debauchery; but be filled with the Spirit,

v19 speaking to yourselves in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing and chanting with your heart to the Lord;

v20 giving thanks at all times for all things to him [who is] God and [the] Father in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ,

v21 submitting yourselves to one another in [the] fear of Christ.

v22 Wives, [submit yourselves] to your own husbands, as to the Lord,

v23 for a husband is head of the wife, as also the Christ [is] head of the assembly. *He* [is] Saviour of the body.

v24 But even as the assembly is subjected to the Christ, so also wives to their own husbands in everything.

v25 Husbands, love your own wives, even as the Christ also loved the assembly, and has delivered himself up for it,

v26 in order that he might sanctify it, purifying [it] by the washing of water by [the] word,

v27 that *he* might present the assembly to himself glorious, having no spot, or wrinkle, or any of such things; but that it might be holy and blameless.

v28 So ought men also to love their own wives as their own bodies: he that loves his own wife loves himself.

v29 For no one has ever hated his own flesh, but nourishes and cherishes it, even as also the Christ the assembly:

v30 for we are members of his body; [we are of his flesh, and of his bones.]

v31 Because of this a man shall leave his father and mother, and shall be united to his wife, and the two shall be one flesh.

v32 This mystery is great, but *I* speak as to Christ, and as to the assembly.

v33 But *ye* also, every one of you, let each so love his own wife as himself; but as to the wife [I speak] that she may fear the husband.
