# Joshua 19 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/06 - Joshua/Joshua 18|← Joshua 18]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/06 - Joshua/Joshua]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/06 - Joshua/Joshua 20|Joshua 20 →]]

***

v1 And the second lot came forth to Simeon, for the tribe of the children of Simeon according to their families; and their inheritance was within the inheritance of the children of Judah.

v2 And they had in their inheritance Beer-sheba, and Sheba, and Moladah,

v3 and Hazar-Shual, and Balah, and Ezem,

v4 and Eltolad, and Bethul, and Hormah,

v5 and Ziklag, and Beth-marcaboth, and Hazar-susah,

v6 and Beth-lebaoth, and Sharuhen: thirteen cities and their hamlets;

v7 Ain, Rimmon, and Ether, and Ashan: four cities and their hamlets;

v8 and all the hamlets that are round these cities to Baalath-beer, [that is] Ramah of the south. This is the inheritance of the tribe of the children of Simeon according to their families.

v9 Out of the lot of the children of Judah was the inheritance of the children of Simeon; for the portion of the children of Judah was too great for them, and the children of Simeon inherited within their inheritance.

v10 And the third lot came up for the children of Zebulun according to their families. And the border of their inheritance was unto Sarid;

v11 and their border went up westwards, and [to] Marealah, and reached to Dabbesheth, and reached to the torrent that is before Jokneam;

v12 and turned from Sarid eastward, toward the sun-rising, to the border of Chisloth-Tabor, and went out to Dabrath, and went up to Japhia;

v13 and from thence it passed eastward toward the sun-rising to Gath-Hepher, to Eth-kazin, and went out to Rimmon which reaches to Neah;

v14 and the border turned round it northwards to Hannathon: and ended in the valley of Jiphthah-el; ...

v15 and Kattath, and Nahalal, and Shimron, and Jidalah, and Beth-lehem: twelve cities and their hamlets.

v16 This was the inheritance of the children of Zebulun according to their families, these cities and their hamlets.

v17 The fourth lot came forth to Issachar, for the children of Issachar according to their families.

v18 And their territory was toward Jizreel, and Chesulloth, and Shunem,

v19 and Hapharaim, and Shion, and Anaharath,

v20 and Rabbith, and Kishion, and Ebez,

v21 and Remeth, and En-gannim, and En-haddah, and Beth-pazzez;

v22 and the border reached to Tabor, and Shahazimah, and Beth-shemesh; and their border ended at the Jordan: sixteen cities and their hamlets.

v23 This was the inheritance of the tribe of the children of Issachar according to their families, the cities and their hamlets.

v24 And the fifth lot came forth for the tribe of the children of Asher according to their families.

v25 And their territory was Helkath, and Hali, and Beten, and Acshaph,

v26 and Allammelech, and Amead, and Mishal; and [the border] reached to Carmel westwards, and to Shihor-libnath,

v27 and turned towards the sun-rising to Beth-Dagon, and reached to Zebulun, and to the valley of Jiphthah-el northward [to] Beth-emek and Neiel, and went out to Cabul on the left,

v28 and Ebron, and Rehob, and Hammon, and Kanah, as far as great Zidon;

v29 and the border turned to Ramah, and as far as the fortified city of Tyre; and the border turned to Hosah; and ended at the sea by the tract of country of Achzib;

v30 and Ummah, and Aphek, and Rehob: twenty-two cities and their hamlets.

v31 This was the inheritance of the tribe of the children of Asher according to their families, these cities and their hamlets.

v32 The sixth lot came forth to the children of Naphtali, for the children of Naphtali according to their families.

v33 And their border was from Heleph, from the oak of Zaanannim; and Adami-nekeb and Jabneel to Lakkum; and ended at the Jordan;

v34 and the border turned westwards to Aznoth-Tabor, and went out from thence to Hukkok, and reached to Zebulun on the south, and reached to Asher on the west, and to Judah upon Jordan towards the sun-rising.

v35 And the fortified cities were Ziddim, Zer, and Hammath, Rakkath, and Chinnereth,

v36 and Adamah, and Ramah, and Hazor,

v37 and Kedesh, and Edrei, and En-Hazor,

v38 and Jiron, and Migdal-el, Horem, and Beth-anath, and Beth-shemesh: nineteen cities and their hamlets.

v39 This was the inheritance of the tribe of the children of Naphtali according to their families, the cities and their hamlets.

v40 The seventh lot came forth for the tribe of the children of Dan according to their families.

v41 And the territory of their inheritance was Zoreah, and Eshtaol, and Ir-shemesh,

v42 and Shaalabbin, and Ajalon, and Jithlah,

v43 and Elon, and Timnathah, and Ekron,

v44 and El-tekeh, and Gibbethon, and Baalath,

v45 and Jehud, and Beneberak, and Gath-Rimmon,

v46 and Me-jarkon, and Rakkon, with the border over against Japho.

v47 And the border of the children of Dan ended with these; and the children of Dan went up and fought against Leshem, and took it, and smote it with the edge of the sword, and took possession of it, and dwelt in it, and called Leshem, Dan, after the name of Dan their father.

v48 This was the inheritance of the tribe of the children of Dan according to their families, these cities and their hamlets.

v49 And they ended dividing the land for inheritance according to its borders. And the children of Israel gave an inheritance to Joshua the son of Nun among them.

v50 According to the word of Jehovah they gave him the city that he asked, Timnath-serah in the hill-country of Ephraim; and he built the city, and dwelt in it.

v51 These were the inheritances, which Eleazar the priest, and Joshua the son of Nun, and the chief fathers of the tribes of the children of Israel, distributed by lot in Shiloh before Jehovah, at the entrance of the tent of meeting. And they ended dividing the land.
