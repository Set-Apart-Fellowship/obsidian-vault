# Joshua 15 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/06 - Joshua/Joshua 14|← Joshua 14]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/06 - Joshua/Joshua]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/06 - Joshua/Joshua 16|Joshua 16 →]]

***

v1 And the lot of the tribe of the children of Judah according to their families was: to the border of Edom, the wilderness of Zin, southward, in the extreme south.

v2 And their southern border was from the end of the salt sea, from the tongue that turns southward;

v3 and it went out south of the ascent of Akrabbim, and passed on to Zin, and went up on the south of Kadesh-barnea, and passed to Hezron, and went up toward Addar, and turned toward Karkaah,

v4 and passed on to Azmon, and went out by the torrent of Egypt; and the border ended at the sea. That shall be your border southward.

v5 -- And the eastern border was the salt sea as far as the end of the Jordan. -- And the border on the north side was from the tongue of the salt sea, at the end of the Jordan;

v6 and the border went up toward Beth-hoglah, and passed north of Beth-Arabah; and the border went up to the stone of Bohan, the son of Reuben;

v7 and the border went up toward Debir from the valley of Achor, and turned northward to Gilgal, which is opposite to the ascent of Adummim, which is on the south side of the torrent; and the border passed to the waters of En-shemesh, and ended at En-rogel;

v8 and the border went up to the valley of the son of Hinnom, toward the south side of the Jebusite, that is, Jerusalem; and the border went up to the top of the mountain that is before the valley of Hinnom westward, which is at the end of the valley of Rephaim northward;

v9 and the border reached along from the top of the mountain toward the spring of the waters of Nephtoah, and went out toward the cities of mount Ephron; and the border reached along to Baalah, that is, Kirjath-jearim;

v10 and the border turned from Baalah westwards toward mount Seir, and passed toward the side of Har-jearim, that is, Chesalon, northwards, and went down to Beth-shemesh, and passed Timnah.

v11 And the border went out to the side of Ekron northwards, and the border reached along toward Shicron, and passed mount Baalah, and went toward Jabneel; and the border ended at the sea.

v12 -- And the west border is the great sea and [its] coast. This is the border of the children of Judah round about, according to their families.

v13 And to Caleb the son of Jephunneh he gave a portion among the children of Judah according to the commandment of Jehovah to Joshua, the city of Arba, the father of Anak, that is, Hebron.

v14 And thence Caleb dispossessed the three sons of Anak, Sheshai and Ahiman and Talmai, who were born of Anak.

v15 And from there he went up against the inhabitants of Debir; now the name of Debir before was Kirjath-sepher.

v16 And Caleb said, He that smites Kirjath-sepher and takes it, to him will I give Achsah my daughter as wife.

v17 And Othniel the son of Kenaz, the brother of Caleb, took it; and he gave him Achsah his daughter as wife.

v18 And it came to pass as she came, that she urged him to ask of her father a field; and she sprang down from the ass. And Caleb said to her, What wouldest thou?

v19 And she said, Give me a blessing; for thou hast given me a southern land; give me also springs of water. Then he gave her the upper springs and the lower springs.

v20 This is the inheritance of the tribe of the children of Judah according to their families.

v21 The cities at the extremity of the tribe of the children of Judah, toward the border of Edom in the south, were: Kabzeel, and Eder, and Jagur,

v22 and Kinah, and Dimonah, and Adadah,

v23 and Kedesh, and Hazor, and Jithnan,

v24 Ziph, and Telem, and Bealoth,

v25 and Hazor-hadattah, and Kerioth-Hezron, that is Hazor,

v26 Amam, and Shema, and Molada,

v27 and Hazar-gaddah, and Heshmon, and Beth-Pelet,

v28 and Hazar-Shual, and Beer-sheba, and Biziothiah;

v29 Baalah, and Ijim, and Ezem,

v30 and Eltolad, and Chesil, and Hormah,

v31 and Ziklag, and Madmannah, and Sansanna,

v32 and Lebaoth, and Shilhim, and Ain, and Rimmon: all the cities twenty-nine, and their hamlets.

v33 -- In the lowland: Eshtaol, and Zoreah, and Ashnah,

v34 and Zanoah, and En-gannim, Tappuah and Enam,

v35 Jarmuth and Adullam, Sochoh and Azekah,

v36 and Shaaraim, and Adithaim, and Gederah, and Gederothaim: fourteen cities and their hamlets.

v37 Zenan, and Hadashah, and Migdal-Gad,

v38 and Dilean, and Mizpeh, and Joktheel,

v39 Lachish, and Bozkath, and Eglon,

v40 and Cabbon, and Lahmas, and Chithlish,

v41 and Gederoth, Beth-Dagon, and Naamah, and Makkedah: sixteen cities and their hamlets.

v42 Libna, and Ether, and Ashan,

v43 and Jiphthah, and Ashnah, and Nezib,

v44 and Keilah, and Achzib, and Mareshah: nine cities and their hamlets.

v45 Ekron and its dependent villages and its hamlets.

v46 From Ekron and westward all that are by the side of Ashdod, and their hamlets.

v47 Ashdod, its dependent villages and its hamlets; Gazah, its dependent villages and its hamlets, as far as the torrent of Egypt, and the great sea and [its] coast.

v48 -- And in the hill-country: Shamir, and Jattir, and Sochoh,

v49 and Dannah, and Kirjath-sannah, that is, Debir,

v50 and Anab, and Eshtemoh, and Anim,

v51 and Goshen, and Holon, and Giloh: eleven cities and their hamlets.

v52 Arab, and Dumah, and Eshan,

v53 and Janum, and Beth-Tappuah, and Aphekah,

v54 and Humtah, and Kirjath-Arba, that is, Hebron, and Zior: nine cities and their hamlets.

v55 Maon, Carmel, and Ziph, and Jutah,

v56 and Jizreel, and Jokdeam, and Zanoah,

v57 Kain, Gibeah and Timnah: ten cities and their hamlets.

v58 Halhul, Beth-Zur, and Gedor,

v59 and Maarath, and Beth-anoth, and Eltekon: six cities and their hamlets.

v60 Kirjath-Baal, that is, Kirjath-jearim, and Rabbah: two cities and their hamlets.

v61 -- In the wilderness: Beth-Arabah, Middin, and Secacah,

v62 and Nibshan, and Ir-Hammelah, and En-gedi: six cities and their hamlets.

v63 But as for the Jebusites, the inhabitants of Jerusalem, the children of Judah could not dispossess them; and the Jebusites dwell with the children of Judah at Jerusalem to this day.
