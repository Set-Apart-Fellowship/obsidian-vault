# 2 Samuel 22 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 21|← 2 Samuel 21]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 23|2 Samuel 23 →]]

***

v1 And David spoke to Jehovah the words of this song in the day that Jehovah had delivered him out of the hand of all his enemies, and out of the hand of Saul.

v2 And he said, Jehovah is my rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer;

v3 God is my rock, in him will I trust -- My shield, and the horn of my salvation, My high tower, and my refuge, My saviour: thou wilt save me from violence.

v4 I will call upon Jehovah, who is to be praised; So shall I be saved from mine enemies.

v5 For the waves of death encompassed me, Torrents of Belial made me afraid.

v6 The bands of Sheol surrounded me; The cords of death encountered me;

v7 In my distress I called upon Jehovah, And I cried to my God; And he heard my voice out of his temple, And my cry [came] into his ears.

v8 Then the earth shook, and quaked; The foundations of the heavens trembled And shook because he was wroth.

v9 There went up a smoke out of his nostrils, And fire out of his mouth devoured: Coals burned forth from it.

v10 And he bowed the heavens, and came down; And darkness was under his feet.

v11 And he rode upon a cherub, and did fly; And he was seen upon the wings of the wind.

v12 And he made darkness round about him a tent, Gatherings of waters, thick clouds of the skies.

v13 From the brightness before him Burned forth coals of fire.

v14 Jehovah thundered from the heavens, And the Most High uttered his voice.

v15 And he sent arrows, and scattered [mine enemies]; Lightning, and discomfited them.

v16 And the beds of the sea were seen, The foundations of the world were uncovered At the rebuke of Jehovah, At the blast of the breath of his nostrils.

v17 He reached forth from above, he took me, He drew me out of great waters;

v18 He delivered me from my strong enemy, From them that hated me; For they were mightier than I.

v19 They encountered me in the day of my calamity; But Jehovah was my stay.

v20 And he brought me forth into a large place; He delivered me, because he delighted in me.

v21 Jehovah hath rewarded me according to my righteousness, According to the cleanness of my hands hath he recompensed me.

v22 For I have kept the ways of Jehovah, And have not wickedly departed from my God.

v23 For all his ordinances were before me, And his statutes, I did not depart from them,

v24 And I was upright before him, And kept myself from mine iniquity.

v25 And Jehovah hath recompensed me according to my righteousness, According to my cleanness in his sight.

v26 With the gracious thou dost shew thyself gracious; With the upright man thou dost shew thyself upright;

v27 With the pure thou dost shew thyself pure; And with the perverse thou dost shew thyself contrary.

v28 And the afflicted people thou dost save; And thine eyes are upon the haughty, [whom] thou bringest down.

v29 For thou art my lamp, Jehovah; And Jehovah enlighteneth my darkness.

v30 For by thee I have run through a troop; By my God have I leaped over a wall.

v31 As for Ã¹God, his way is perfect; The word of Jehovah is tried: He is a shield to all that trust in him.

v32 For who is Ã¹God, save Jehovah? And who is a rock, save our God?

v33 Ã¹God is my strong fortress, And he maketh my way perfectly smooth.

v34 He maketh my feet like hinds' [feet], And setteth me upon my high places.

v35 He teacheth my hands to war, And mine arms bend a bow of brass.

v36 And thou didst give me the shield of thy salvation, And thy condescending gentleness hath made me great.

v37 Thou enlargedst my steps under me; And mine ankles did not slip.

v38 I pursued mine enemies, and destroyed them, And I turned not again till they were consumed.

v39 And I have consumed them and have crushed them, and they rose not again; Yea, they fell under my feet.

v40 And thou girdedst me with strength to battle: Thou didst subdue under me those that rose up against me.

v41 And mine enemies didst thou make to turn their backs unto me, And those that hated me I destroyed.

v42 They looked, and there was none to save -- Unto Jehovah, and he answered them not.

v43 And I did beat them small as the dust of the earth, I trod them as the mire of the streets; I stamped upon them.

v44 And thou hast delivered me from the strivings of my people, Thou hast kept me to be head of the nations: A people I knew not doth serve me:

v45 Strangers come cringing unto me: At the hearing of the ear, they obey me.

v46 Strangers have faded away, And they come trembling forth from their close places.

v47 Jehovah liveth; and blessed be my rock; And exalted be the God, the rock of my salvation,

v48 The Ã¹God who hath avenged me, And hath brought the peoples under me.

v49 He brought me forth from mine enemies: Yea, thou hast lifted me up above them that rose up against me; From the man of violence hast thou delivered me.

v50 Therefore will I give thanks to thee, Jehovah, among the nations, And will sing psalms to thy name.

v51 [It is he] who giveth great deliverances to his king, And sheweth loving-kindness to his anointed, To David, and to his seed for evermore.
