# Philemon 1 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/57 - Philemon/Philemon]]

***

v1 Paul, prisoner of Christ Jesus, and Timotheus the brother, to Philemon the beloved and our fellow-workman,

v2 and to the sister Apphia and to Archippus our fellow-soldier, and to the assembly which [is] in thine house.

v3 Grace to you and peace from God our Father, and [the] Lord Jesus Christ.

v4 I thank my God, always making mention of thee at my prayers,

v5 hearing of thy love and the faith which thou hast towards the Lord Jesus, and towards all the saints,

v6 in such sort that thy participation in the faith should become operative in the acknowledgment of every good thing which is in us towards Christ [Jesus].

v7 For we have great thankfulness and encouragement through thy love, because the bowels of the saints are refreshed by thee, brother.

v8 Wherefore having much boldness in Christ to enjoin thee what is fitting,

v9 for love's sake I rather exhort, being such a one as Paul the aged, and now also prisoner of Jesus Christ.

v10 I exhort thee for *my* child, whom I have begotten in [my] bonds, Onesimus,

v11 once unserviceable to thee, but now serviceable to thee and to me:

v12 whom I have sent back to thee: [but do *thou* receive] him, that is, *my* bowels:

v13 whom *I* was desirous of keeping with myself, that for thee he might minister to me in the bonds of the glad tidings;

v14 but I have wished to do nothing without thy mind, that thy good might not be as of necessity but of willingness:

v15 for perhaps for this reason he has been separated [from thee] for a time, that thou mightest possess him fully for ever;

v16 not any longer as a bondman, but above a bondman, a beloved brother, specially to me, and how much rather to thee, both in [the] flesh and in [the] Lord?

v17 If therefore thou holdest me to be a partner [with thee], receive him as me;

v18 but if he have wronged thee anything or owe anything [to thee], put this to my account.

v19 *I* Paul have written [it] with mine own hand; *I* will repay [it]: that I say not to thee that thou owest even thine own self also to me.

v20 Yea, brother, *I* would have profit of *thee* in [the] Lord: refresh my bowels in Christ.

v21 Being confident of thine obedience, I have written to thee, knowing that thou wilt do even more than I say.

v22 But withal prepare me also a lodging; for I hope that I shall be granted to you through your prayers.

v23 Epaphras salutes thee, my fellow-prisoner in Christ Jesus;

v24 Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, Luke, my fellow-workmen.

v25 The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ [be] with your spirit.
