# Exodus 39 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/02 - Exodus/Exodus 38|← Exodus 38]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/02 - Exodus/Exodus]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/02 - Exodus/Exodus 40|Exodus 40 →]]

***

v1 And of the blue and purple and scarlet they made garments of service, for service in the sanctuary, and made the holy garments for Aaron; as Jehovah had commanded Moses.

v2 And he made the ephod of gold, blue, and purple, and scarlet, and twined byssus.

v3 And they beat the gold into thin plates, and cut it [into] wires, to work it artistically into the blue, and into the purple, and into the scarlet, and into the byssus.

v4 They made shoulder-pieces for it, joining it: at its two ends was it joined together.

v5 And the girdle of his ephod, which was on it, was of the same, according to its work, of gold, blue, and purple, and scarlet, and twined byssus; as Jehovah had commanded Moses.

v6 And they wrought the onyx stones mounted in enclosures of gold, engraved with the engravings of a seal, according to the names of the sons of Israel.

v7 And he put them on the shoulder-pieces of the ephod, [as] stones of memorial for the children of Israel; as Jehovah had commanded Moses.

v8 And he made the breastplate of artistic work, like the work of the ephod, of gold, blue, and purple, and scarlet, and twined byssus.

v9 It was square; double did they make the breastplate, a span the length thereof, and a span the breadth thereof, doubled.

v10 And they set in it four rows of stones: [one] row, a sardoin, a topaz, and an emerald -- the first row;

v11 and the second row, a carbuncle, a sapphire, and a diamond;

v12 and the third row, an opal, an agate, and an amethyst;

v13 and the fourth row, a chrysolite, an onyx, and a jasper; mounted in enclosures of gold in their settings.

v14 And the stones were according to the names of the children of Israel, twelve, according to their names, engraved as a seal, every one according to his name, for the twelve tribes.

v15 And they made on the breastplate chains of laced work of wreathen work, of pure gold.

v16 And they made two settings of gold, and two gold rings, and put the two rings on the two ends of the breastplate.

v17 And they put the two wreathen [cords] of gold in the two rings on the ends of the breastplate;

v18 and the two ends of the two wreathen [cords] they fastened to the two settings, and put them on the shoulder-pieces of the ephod, on the front thereof.

v19 And they made two rings of gold, and put [them] on the two ends of the breastplate, on the border thereof, which faceth the ephod inwards.

v20 And they made two rings of gold, and put them upon the two shoulder-pieces of the ephod underneath, to the front thereof, just by the coupling thereof, above the girdle of the ephod.

v21 And they bound the breastplate with its rings to the rings of the ephod with lace of blue, that it might be above the girdle of the ephod, and that the breastplate might not be loosed from the ephod; as Jehovah had commanded Moses.

v22 And he made the cloak of the ephod of woven work, all of blue;

v23 and the opening of the cloak in its middle, as the opening of a coat of mail; a binding was round about the opening, that it should not rend.

v24 And they made on the skirts of the cloak pomegranates of blue and purple and scarlet, twined.

v25 And they made bells of pure gold, and put the bells between the pomegranates, in the skirts of the cloak, round about, between the pomegranates:

v26 a bell and a pomegranate, a bell and a pomegranate, in the skirts of the cloak round about, for service; as Jehovah had commanded Moses.

v27 And they made the vests of byssus of woven work, for Aaron, and for his sons;

v28 and the turban of byssus; and the ornamental caps, of byssus; and the linen trousers, of twined byssus;

v29 and the girdle, of twined byssus, and blue, and purple, and scarlet, of embroidery; as Jehovah had commanded Moses.

v30 And they made the thin plate, the holy diadem, of pure gold, and wrote on it with the writing of the engravings of a seal, Holiness to Jehovah!

v31 And they fastened to it a lace of blue, to fasten [it] on the turban above; as Jehovah had commanded Moses.

v32 And all the labour of the tabernacle of the tent of meeting was ended; and the children of Israel had done [it] according to all that Jehovah had commanded Moses -- so had they done [it].

v33 And they brought the tabernacle to Moses -- the tent, and all its utensils, its clasps, its boards, its bars, and its pillars, and its bases;

v34 and the covering of rams' skins dyed red, and the covering of badgers' skins, and the veil of separation;

v35 the ark of the testimony, and its staves, and the mercy-seat;

v36 the table, all its utensils, and the shewbread;

v37 the pure candlestick, its lamps, the lamps set in order, and all its utensils, and the oil for the light;

v38 and the golden altar, and the anointing oil, and the incense of fragrant drugs; and the curtain for the entrance of the tent;

v39 the copper altar, and the copper grating that was for it, its staves and all its utensils; the laver and its stand;

v40 the hangings of the court, its pillars, and its bases; and the curtain for the gate of the court, its cords, and its pegs; and all the vessel's of service of the tabernacle, for the tent of meeting;

v41 the garments of service, for service in the sanctuary; the holy garments for Aaron the priest, and his sons' garments, to serve as priests.

v42 According to all that Jehovah had commanded Moses, so had the children of Israel done all the labour.

v43 And Moses saw all the work, and behold, they had done it as Jehovah had commanded -- so had they done [it]; and Moses blessed them.
