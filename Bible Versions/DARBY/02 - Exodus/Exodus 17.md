# Exodus 17 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/02 - Exodus/Exodus 16|← Exodus 16]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/02 - Exodus/Exodus]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/02 - Exodus/Exodus 18|Exodus 18 →]]

***

v1 And all the assembly of the children of Israel journeyed from the wilderness of Sin, according to their journeys, at the command of Jehovah; and they encamped in Rephidim; and there was no water for the people to drink.

v2 And the people contended with Moses, and said, Give us water, that we may drink! And Moses said to them, Why do ye dispute with me? Why do ye tempt Jehovah?

v3 And the people thirsted there for water; and the people murmured against Moses, and said, Why is it that thou hast brought us up out of Egypt, to kill us and our children and our cattle with thirst?

v4 And Moses cried to Jehovah, saying, What shall I do with this people? Yet a little, and they will stone me!

v5 And Jehovah said to Moses, Go on before the people, and take with thee of the elders of Israel, and thy staff with which thou didst smite the river, take in thy hand, and go.

v6 Behold, I will stand before thee there upon the rock on Horeb; and thou shalt strike the rock, and there shall come water out of it, that the people may drink. And Moses did so before the eyes of the elders of Israel.

v7 And he called the name of the place Massah, and Meribah, because of the contention of the children of Israel, and because they had tempted Jehovah, saying, Is Jehovah among us, or not?

v8 And Amalek came and fought with Israel in Rephidim.

v9 And Moses said to Joshua, Choose us men, and go out, fight with Amalek; to-morrow I will stand on the top of the hill with the staff of God in my hand.

v10 And Joshua did as Moses had said to him, to fight with Amalek; and Moses, Aaron and Hur went up to the top of the hill.

v11 And it came to pass when Moses raised his hand, that Israel prevailed; and when he let down his hand, Amalek prevailed.

v12 And Moses' hands were heavy; then they took a stone, and put [it] under him, and he sat on it; and Aaron and Hur supported his hands, one on this side, and one on that side; and his hands were steady until the going down of the sun.

v13 And Joshua broke the power of Amalek and his people with the edge of the sword.

v14 And Jehovah said to Moses, Write this [for] a memorial in the book, and rehearse [it] in the ears of Joshua, that I will utterly blot out the remembrance of Amalek from under the heavens.

v15 And Moses built an altar, and called the name of it Jehovah-nissi.

v16 And he said, For the hand is on the throne of Jah; Jehovah will have war with Amalek from generation to generation!
