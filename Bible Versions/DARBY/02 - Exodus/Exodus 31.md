# Exodus 31 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/02 - Exodus/Exodus 30|← Exodus 30]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/02 - Exodus/Exodus]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/02 - Exodus/Exodus 32|Exodus 32 →]]

***

v1 And Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,

v2 See, I have called by name Bezaleel the son of Uri, the son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah,

v3 and have filled him with the spirit of God, in wisdom, and in understanding, and in knowledge, and in every work,

v4 to devise artistic work -- to work in gold, and in silver, and in copper,

v5 and in cutting of stones, for setting, and for carving of timber -- to work in all manner of work.

v6 And I, behold, I have given with him Aholiab the son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan; and in the heart of every one that is wise-hearted have I given wisdom, that they may make all that I have commanded thee --

v7 the tent of meeting and the ark of the testimony, and the mercy-seat that is thereupon, and all the utensils of the tent,

v8 and the table and its utensils, and the pure lamp-stand and all its utensils, and the altar of incense;

v9 and the altar of burnt-offering and all its utensils, and the laver and its stand;

v10 and the garments of service, both the holy garments of Aaron the priest, and the garments of his sons, to serve as priests;

v11 and the anointing oil, and the incense of fragrant drugs for the sanctuary: according to all that I have commanded thee shall they do.

v12 And Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,

v13 And thou, speak thou unto the children of Israel, saying, Surely my sabbaths shall ye keep; for this is a sign between me and you throughout your generations, that ye may know that it is I, Jehovah, who do hallow you.

v14 Keep the sabbath, therefore; for it is holy unto you; every one that profaneth it shall certainly be put to death: yea, whoever doeth work on it, that soul shall be cut off from among his peoples.

v15 Six days shall work be done; but on the seventh day is the sabbath of rest, holy to Jehovah: whoever doeth work on the sabbath day shall certainly be put to death.

v16 And the children of Israel shall keep the sabbath, to observe the sabbath throughout their generations -- [it is] an everlasting covenant.

v17 It shall be a sign between me and the children of Israel for ever; for [in] six days Jehovah made the heavens and the earth, and on the seventh day he rested, and was refreshed.

v18 And he gave to Moses, when he had ended speaking with him on mount Sinai, the two tables of testimony, tables of stone, written with the finger of God.
