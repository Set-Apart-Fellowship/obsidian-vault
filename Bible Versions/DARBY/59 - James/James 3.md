# James 3 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/59 - James/James 2|← James 2]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/59 - James/James]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/59 - James/James 4|James 4 →]]

***

v1 Be not many teachers, my brethren, knowing that we shall receive greater judgment.

v2 For we all often offend. If any one offend not in word, *he* [is] a perfect man, able to bridle the whole body too.

v3 Behold, we put the bits in the mouths of the horses, that they may obey us, and we turn round their whole bodies.

v4 Behold also the ships, which are so great, and driven by violent winds, are turned about by a very small rudder, wherever the pleasure of the helmsman will.

v5 Thus also the tongue is a little member, and boasts great things. See how little a fire, how large a wood it kindles!

v6 and the tongue [is] fire, the world of unrighteousness; the tongue is set in our members, the defiler of the whole body, and which sets fire to the course of nature, and is set on fire of hell.

v7 For every species both of beasts and of birds, both of creeping things and of sea animals, is tamed and has been tamed by the human species;

v8 but the tongue can no one among men tame; [it is] an unsettled evil, full of death-bringing poison.

v9 Therewith bless we the Lord and Father, and therewith curse we men made after [the] likeness of God.

v10 Out of the same mouth goes forth blessing and cursing. It is not right, my brethren, that these things should be thus.

v11 Does the fountain, out of the same opening, pour forth sweet and bitter?

v12 Can, my brethren, a fig produce olives, or a vine figs? Neither [can] salt [water] make sweet water.

v13 Who [is] wise and understanding among you; let him shew out of a good conversation his works in meekness of wisdom;

v14 but if ye have bitter emulation and strife in your hearts, do not boast and lie against the truth.

v15 This is not the wisdom which comes down from above, but earthly, natural, devilish.

v16 For where emulation and strife [are], there [is] disorder and every evil thing.

v17 But the wisdom from above first is pure, then peaceful, gentle, yielding, full of mercy and good fruits, unquestioning, unfeigned.

v18 But [the] fruit of righteousness in peace is sown for them that make peace.
