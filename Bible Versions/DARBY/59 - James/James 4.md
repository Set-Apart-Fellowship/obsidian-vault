# James 4 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/59 - James/James 3|← James 3]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/59 - James/James]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/59 - James/James 5|James 5 →]]

***

v1 Whence [come] wars and whence fightings among you? [Is it] not thence, -- from your pleasures, which war in your members?

v2 Ye lust and have not: ye kill and are full of envy, and cannot obtain; ye fight and war; ye have not because ye ask not.

v3 Ye ask and receive not, because ye ask evilly, that ye may consume [it] in your pleasures.

v4 Adulteresses, know ye not that friendship with the world is enmity with God? Whoever therefore is minded to be [the] friend of the world is constituted enemy of God.

v5 Think ye that the scripture speaks in vain? Does the Spirit which has taken his abode in us desire enviously?

v6 But he gives more grace. Wherefore he says, God sets himself against [the] proud, but gives grace to [the] lowly.

v7 Subject yourselves therefore to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.

v8 Draw near to God, and he will draw near to you. Cleanse [your] hands, sinners, and purify [your] hearts, ye double-minded.

v9 Be wretched, and mourn, and weep: let your laughter be turned to mourning, and [your] joy to heaviness.

v10 Humble yourselves before [the] Lord, and he shall exalt you.

v11 Speak not against one another, brethren. He that speaks against [his] brother, or judges his brother, speaks against [the] law and judges [the] law. But if thou judgest [the] law, thou art not doer of [the] law, but judge.

v12 One is the lawgiver and judge, who is able to save and to destroy: but who art *thou* who judgest thy neighbour?

v13 Go to now, ye who say, To-day or to-morrow will we go into such a city and spend a year there, and traffic and make gain,

v14 ye who do not know what will be on the morrow, ([for] what [is] your life? It is even a vapour, appearing for a little while, and then disappearing,)

v15 instead of your saying, If the Lord should [so] will and we should live, we will also do this or that.

v16 But now ye glory in your vauntings: all such glorying is evil.

v17 To him therefore who knows how to do good, and does it not, to him it is sin.
