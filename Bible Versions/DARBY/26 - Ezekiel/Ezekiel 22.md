# Ezekiel 22 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 21|← Ezekiel 21]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 23|Ezekiel 23 →]]

***

v1 And the word of Jehovah came unto me, saying,

v2 And thou, son of man, wilt thou judge, wilt thou judge the bloody city? Yea, cause her to know all her abominations,

v3 and say, Thus saith the Lord Jehovah: A city that sheddeth blood in her midst, that her time may come, and maketh idols against herself to defile herself.

v4 Thou art become guilty by thy blood which thou hast shed, and hast defiled thyself with thine idols which thou hast made; and thou hast caused thy days to draw near, and art come unto thy years: therefore have I made thee a reproach unto the nations, and a mocking unto all countries.

v5 Those that are near, and those that are far from thee, shall mock thee, who art infamous [and] full of tumult.

v6 Behold, the princes of Israel have been in thee to shed blood, each according to his power.

v7 In thee have they made light of father and mother; in the midst of thee have they dealt by oppression with the stranger; in thee have they vexed the fatherless and the widow.

v8 Thou hast despised my holy things, and hast profaned my sabbaths.

v9 In thee there have been slanderous men to shed blood; and in thee have they eaten upon the mountains; in the midst of thee they have committed lewdness;

v10 in thee have they discovered their fathers' nakedness; in thee have they humbled her that was unclean in her separation.

v11 And one hath committed abomination with his neighbour's wife; and another hath lewdly defiled his daughter-in-law; and another in thee hath humbled his sister, his father's daughter.

v12 In thee have they taken gifts to shed blood; thou hast taken usury and increase, and thou hast overreached thy neighbours by oppression, and hast forgotten me, saith the Lord Jehovah.

v13 And behold, I have smitten mine hand at thine overreaching which thou hast done, and at thy bloodshed which hath been in the midst of thee.

v14 Shall thy heart endure, shall thy hands be strong, in the days that I shall deal with thee? I Jehovah have spoken, and will do [it].

v15 And I will scatter thee among the nations, and disperse thee through the countries, and will consume thy filthiness out of thee.

v16 And thou shalt be polluted through thyself in the sight of the nations, and thou shalt know that I [am] Jehovah.

v17 And the word of Jehovah came unto me, saying,

v18 Son of man, the house of Israel is become dross to me: they are all copper, and tin, and iron, and lead, in the midst of the furnace: they are become the dross of silver.

v19 Therefore thus saith the Lord Jehovah: Because ye are all become dross, therefore behold, I will gather you into the midst of Jerusalem.

v20 [As] they gather silver, and copper, and iron, and lead, and tin, into the midst of the furnace, to blow the fire upon it, to melt it, so will I gather you in mine anger and in my fury, and I will lay you on and melt you.

v21 Yea, I will collect you, and blow upon you the fire of my wrath, and ye shall be melted in the midst thereof.

v22 As silver is melted in the midst of the furnace, so shall ye be melted in the midst thereof: and ye shall know that I Jehovah have poured out my fury upon you.

v23 And the word of Jehovah came unto me, saying,

v24 Son of man, say unto her, Thou art a land that is not cleansed, nor rained upon in the day of indignation.

v25 There is a conspiracy of her prophets in the midst of her like a roaring lion ravening the prey; they devour souls; they take away treasure and precious things; they increase her widows in the midst of her;

v26 her priests do violence to my law, and profane my holy things: they put no difference between the holy and profane, neither do they make known [the difference] between the unclean and the clean, and they hide their eyes from my sabbaths, and I am profaned among them.

v27 Her princes in the midst of her are like wolves ravening the prey, to shed blood, to destroy souls, to get dishonest gain.

v28 And her prophets have daubed for them with untempered [mortar], seeing vanity and divining lies unto them, saying, Thus saith the Lord Jehovah! and Jehovah hath not spoken.

v29 The people of the land use oppression and practise robbery; and they vex the poor and needy, and oppress the stranger wrongfully.

v30 And I sought for a man among them, that should make up the fence, and stand in the breach before me for the land, that I should not destroy it; but I found none.

v31 And I will pour out mine indignation upon them; I will consume them in the fire of my wrath: their own way will I recompense upon their head, saith the Lord Jehovah.
