# Leviticus 22 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 21|← Leviticus 21]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 23|Leviticus 23 →]]

***

v1 And Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,

v2 Speak unto Aaron, and to his sons, that they separate themselves from the holy things of the children of Israel, and that they profane not my holy name in the things that they hallow unto me: I am Jehovah.

v3 Say unto them, Every one of all your seed, throughout your generations, that approacheth the holy things, which the children of Israel hallow unto Jehovah, having his uncleanness upon him, that soul shall be cut off from before me: I am Jehovah.

v4 Whatsoever man of the seed of Aaron is a leper, or hath a flux, he shall not eat of the holy things, until he is clean. And he that toucheth any one that is unclean by a dead person, or a man whose seed of copulation hath passed from him;

v5 or a man that toucheth any crawling thing whereby he becometh unclean, or a man by whom he may become unclean, whatever may be his uncleanness,

v6 -- a person that toucheth any such shall be unclean until even, and shall not eat of the holy things; but he shall bathe his flesh with water,

v7 and when the sun goeth down, he shall be clean, and may afterwards eat of the holy things; for it is his food.

v8 Of a dead carcase and what is torn shall he not eat, to make himself unclean therewith: I am Jehovah.

v9 And they shall keep my charge, lest they bear sin for it, and die by it, if they profane it: I am Jehovah who do hallow them.

v10 And no stranger shall eat the holy thing; the sojourner with the priest, and the hired servant, shall not eat of the holy thing.

v11 But if a priest buy any one for money, he may eat of it, and he that is born in his house: they may eat of his food.

v12 And a priest's daughter who is [married] to a stranger may not eat of the heave-offering of the holy things.

v13 But a priest's daughter that becometh a widow, or is divorced, and hath no seed, and returneth unto her father's house, as in her youth, she may eat of her father's food; but no stranger shall eat thereof.

v14 And if a man eat of a holy thing through inadvertence, then he shall put the fifth part thereof unto it, and shall give it unto the priest with the holy thing.

v15 And they shall not profane the holy things of the children of Israel which they offer unto Jehovah,

v16 and cause them to bear the iniquity of trespass when they eat their holy things; for I am Jehovah who do hallow them.

v17 And Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,

v18 Speak unto Aaron, and to his sons, and unto all the children of Israel, and say unto them, Whatever man of the house of Israel, or of the sojourners in Israel, that presenteth his offering for any of his vows, and for any of his voluntary offerings, which they present to Jehovah as a burnt-offering,

v19 it shall be for your acceptance, without blemish, a male of the oxen, of the sheep, and of the goats.

v20 Nothing that hath a defect shall ye present; for it shall not be acceptable for you.

v21 And if any present a sacrifice of peace-offering to Jehovah to accomplish a vow, or a voluntary offering of oxen or small cattle, it shall be without blemish to be accepted: there shall be no defect therein.

v22 Blind, or broken, or maimed, or ulcerous, or with itch, or scabbed -- ye shall not present these to Jehovah, nor make an offering by fire of them on the altar to Jehovah.

v23 A bullock and a sheep that hath a member too long or too short, that mayest thou offer as a voluntary offering; but as a vow it shall not be accepted.

v24 That which is bruised, or crushed, or broken, or cut shall ye not present to Jehovah; neither in your land shall ye do [the like].

v25 Nor from the hand of the stranger shall ye present the bread of your God, of any of these; for their corruption is in them: a defect is in them; they shall not be accepted for you.

v26 And Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,

v27 An ox, or a sheep, or a goat, when it is brought forth, shall be seven days under its dam; and from the eighth day and thenceforth it shall be accepted for an offering by fire to Jehovah.

v28 A cow, or sheep -- it and its young shall ye not slaughter in one day.

v29 And when ye sacrifice a sacrifice of thanksgiving to Jehovah, ye shall sacrifice it for your acceptance.

v30 On that day shall it be eaten: ye shall leave none of it until morning: I am Jehovah.

v31 And ye shall observe my commandments and do them: I am Jehovah.

v32 And ye shall not profane my holy name; but I will be hallowed among the children of Israel: I am Jehovah who do hallow you,

v33 who brought you out of the land of Egypt, to be your God: I am Jehovah.
