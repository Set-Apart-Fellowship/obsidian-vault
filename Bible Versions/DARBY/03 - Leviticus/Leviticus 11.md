# Leviticus 11 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 10|← Leviticus 10]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 12|Leviticus 12 →]]

***

v1 And Jehovah spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying to them,

v2 Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, These are the animals which ye shall eat of all the beasts which are on the earth.

v3 Whatever hath cloven hoofs, and feet quite split open, and cheweth the cud, among the beasts -- that shall ye eat.

v4 Only these shall ye not eat of those that chew the cud, or of those with cloven hoofs: the camel, for it cheweth the cud, but hath not cloven hoofs -- it shall be unclean unto you;

v5 and the rock-badger, for it cheweth the cud, but hath not cloven hoofs -- it shall be unclean unto you;

v6 and the hare, for it cheweth the cud, but hath not cloven hoofs -- it shall be unclean unto you;

v7 and the swine, for it hath cloven hoofs, and feet quite split open, but it cheweth not the cud -- it shall be unclean unto you.

v8 Of their flesh shall ye not eat, and their carcase shall ye not touch: they shall be unclean unto you.

v9 These shall ye eat of all that are in the waters: whatever hath fins and scales in waters, in seas and in rivers, these shall ye eat;

v10 but all that have not fins and scales in seas and in rivers, of all that swarm in the waters, and of every living soul which is in the waters -- they shall be an abomination unto you.

v11 They shall be even an abomination unto you: of their flesh shall ye not eat, and their carcase ye shall have in abomination.

v12 Whatever in the waters hath no fins and scales, that shall be an abomination unto you.

v13 And these shall ye have in abomination of the fowls; they shall not be eaten; an abomination shall they be: the eagle, and the ossifrage, and the sea-eagle,

v14 and the falcon, and the kite, after its kind;

v15 every raven after its kind;

v16 and the female ostrich and the male ostrich, and the sea-gull, and the hawk, after its kind;

v17 and the owl, and the gannet, and the ibis,

v18 and the swan, and the pelican, and the carrion vulture,

v19 and the stork; the heron after its kind, and the hoopoe, and the bat.

v20 Every winged crawling thing that goeth upon all four shall be an abomination unto you.

v21 Yet these shall ye eat of every winged crawling thing that goeth upon all four: those which have legs above their feet with which to leap upon the earth.

v22 These shall ye eat of them: the arbeh after its kind, and the solam after its kind, and the hargol after its kind, and the hargab after its kind.

v23 But every winged crawling thing that hath four feet shall be an abomination unto you.

v24 And by these ye shall make yourselves unclean; whoever toucheth their carcase shall be unclean until the even.

v25 And whoever carrieth [ought] of their carcase shall wash his garments, and be unclean until the even.

v26 Every beast that hath cloven hoofs, but not feet quite split open, nor cheweth the cud, shall be unclean unto you: every one that toucheth them shall be unclean.

v27 And whatever goeth on its paws, among all manner of beasts that go upon all four, those are unclean unto you: whoever toucheth their carcase shall be unclean until the even.

v28 And he that carrieth their carcase shall wash his garments, and be unclean until the even: they shall be unclean unto you.

v29 And these shall be unclean unto you among the crawling things which crawl on the earth: the mole, and the field-mouse, and the lizard, after its kind;

v30 and the groaning lizard, and the great red lizard, and the climbing lizard, and the chomet, and the chameleon.

v31 These shall be unclean unto you among all that crawl: whoever toucheth them when they are dead, shall be unclean until the even.

v32 And on whatever any of them when they are dead doth fall, it shall be unclean; all vessels of wood, or garment, or skin, or sack, every vessel wherewith work is done -- it shall be put into water, and be unclean until the even; then shall it be clean.

v33 And every earthen vessel into which [any] of them falleth -- whatever is in it shall be unclean; and ye shall break it.

v34 All food that is eaten on which [such] water hath come shall be unclean; and all drink that is drunk shall be unclean, in every [such] vessel.

v35 And everything where upon [any part] of their carcase falleth shall be unclean; oven and hearth shall be broken down: they are unclean, and shall be unclean unto you.

v36 Nevertheless, a spring or a well, a quantity of water, shall be clean. But he that toucheth their carcase shall be unclean.

v37 And if any part of their carcase fall upon any sowing-seed which is to be sown, it shall be clean;

v38 but if water have been put on the seed, and any part of their carcase fall thereon, it shall be unclean unto you.

v39 And if any beast which is to you for food die, he that toucheth the carcase thereof shall be unclean until the even.

v40 And he that eateth of its carcase shall wash his garments, and be unclean until the even: he also that carrieth its carcase shall wash his garments, and be unclean until the even.

v41 And every crawling thing which crawleth on the earth shall be an abomination; it shall not be eaten.

v42 Whatever goeth on the belly, and whatever goeth on all four, and all that have a great many feet, of every manner of crawling thing which crawleth on the earth -- these ye shall not eat; for they are an abomination.

v43 Ye shall not make yourselves abominable through any crawling thing which crawleth, neither shall ye make yourselves unclean with them, that ye should be defiled thereby.

v44 For I am Jehovah your God; and ye shall hallow yourselves, and ye shall be holy; for I am holy; and ye shall not make yourselves unclean through any manner of crawling thing which creepeth on the earth.

v45 For I am Jehovah who brought you up out of the land of Egypt, to be your God: ye shall therefore be holy, for I am holy.

v46 This is the law of cattle, and of fowl, and of every living soul that moveth in the waters, and of every soul that crawleth on the earth;

v47 to make a difference between the unclean and the clean, and between the beast that is to be eaten and the beast that is not to be eaten.
