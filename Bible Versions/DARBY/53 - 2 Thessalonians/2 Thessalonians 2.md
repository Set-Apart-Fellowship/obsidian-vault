# 2 Thessalonians 2 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/53 - 2 Thessalonians/2 Thessalonians 1|← 2 Thessalonians 1]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/53 - 2 Thessalonians/2 Thessalonians]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/53 - 2 Thessalonians/2 Thessalonians 3|2 Thessalonians 3 →]]

***

v1 Now we beg you, brethren, by the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ and our gathering together to him,

v2 that ye be not soon shaken in mind, nor troubled, neither by spirit, nor by word, nor by letter, as [if it were] by us, as that the day of the Lord is present.

v3 Let not any one deceive you in any manner, because [it will not be] unless the apostasy have first come, and the man of sin have been revealed, the son of perdition;

v4 who opposes and exalts himself on high against all called God, or object of veneration; so that he himself sits down in the temple of God, shewing himself that he is God.

v5 Do ye not remember that, being yet with you, I said these things to you?

v6 And now ye know that which restrains, that he should be revealed in his own time.

v7 For the mystery of lawlessness already works; only [there is] he who restrains now until he be gone,

v8 and then the lawless one shall be revealed, whom the Lord Jesus shall consume with the breath of his mouth, and shall annul by the appearing of his coming;

v9 whose coming is according to the working of Satan in all power and signs and wonders of falsehood,

v10 and in all deceit of unrighteousness to them that perish, because they have not received the love of the truth that they might be saved.

v11 And for this reason God sends to them a working of error, that they should believe what is false,

v12 that all might be judged who have not believed the truth, but have found pleasure in unrighteousness.

v13 But we ought to give thanks to God always for you, brethren beloved of [the] Lord, that God has chosen you from [the] beginning to salvation in sanctification of [the] Spirit and belief of [the] truth:

v14 whereto he has called you by our glad tidings, to [the] obtaining of [the] glory of our Lord Jesus Christ.

v15 So then, brethren, stand firm, and hold fast the instructions which ye have been taught, whether by word or by our letter.

v16 But our Lord Jesus Christ himself, and our God and Father, who has loved us, and given [us] eternal consolation and good hope by grace,

v17 encourage your hearts, and establish you in every good work and word.
