# John 9 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/43 - John/John 8|← John 8]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/43 - John/John]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/43 - John/John 10|John 10 →]]

***

v1 And as he passed on, he saw a man blind from birth.

v2 And his disciples asked him, saying, Rabbi, who sinned, this [man] or his parents, that he should be born blind?

v3 Jesus answered, Neither has this [man] sinned nor his parents, but that the works of God should be manifested in him.

v4 I must work the works of him that has sent me while it is day. [The] night is coming, when no one can work.

v5 As long as I am in the world, I am [the] light of the world.

v6 Having said these things, he spat on the ground and made mud of the spittle, and put the mud, as ointment, on his eyes.

v7 And he said to him, Go, wash in the pool of Siloam, which is interpreted, Sent. He went therefore and washed, and came seeing.

v8 The neighbours therefore, and those who used to see him before, that he was a beggar, said, Is not this he that was sitting and begging?

v9 Some said, It is he; others said, No, but he is like him: *he* said, It is I.

v10 They said therefore to him, How have thine eyes been opened?

v11 He answered [and said], A man called Jesus made mud and anointed mine eyes, and said to me, Go to Siloam and wash: and having gone and washed, I saw.

v12 They said therefore to him, Where is he? He says, I do not know.

v13 They bring him who was before blind to the Pharisees.

v14 Now it was sabbath when Jesus made the mud and opened his eyes.

v15 The Pharisees therefore also again asked him how he received his sight. And he said to them, He put mud upon mine eyes, and I washed, and I see.

v16 Some of the Pharisees therefore said, This man is not of God, for he does not keep the sabbath. Others said, How can a sinful man perform such signs? And there was a division among them.

v17 They say therefore again to the blind [man], What dost thou say of him, that he has opened thine eyes? And he said, He is a prophet.

v18 The Jews therefore did not believe concerning him that he was blind and had received sight, until they had called the parents of him that had received sight.

v19 And they asked them saying, This is your son, of whom ye say that he was born blind: how then does he now see?

v20 His parents answered [them] and said, We know that this is our son, and that he was born blind;

v21 but how he now sees we do not know, or who has opened his eyes we do not know. *He* is of age: ask *him*; *he* will speak concerning himself.

v22 His parents said these things because they feared the Jews, for the Jews had already agreed that if any one confessed him [to be the] Christ, he should be excommunicated from the synagogue.

v23 On this account his parents said, He is of age: ask *him*.

v24 They called therefore a second time the man who had been blind, and said to him, Give glory to God: we know that this man is sinful.

v25 He answered therefore, If he is sinful I know not. One thing I know, that, being blind [before], now I see.

v26 And they said to him again, What did he do to thee? how opened he thine eyes?

v27 He answered them, I told you already and ye did not hear: why do ye desire to hear again? do ye also wish to become his disciples?

v28 They railed at him, and said, Thou art his disciple, but we are disciples of Moses.

v29 We know that God spoke to Moses; but [as to] this [man], we know not whence he is.

v30 The man answered and said to them, Now in this is a wonderful thing, that *ye* do not know whence he is, and he has opened mine eyes.

v31 [But] we know that God does not hear sinners; but if any one be God-fearing and do his will, him he hears.

v32 Since time was, it has not been heard that any one opened the eyes of one born blind.

v33 If this [man] were not of God he would be able to do nothing.

v34 They answered and said to him, Thou hast been wholly born in sins, and thou teachest us? And they cast him out.

v35 Jesus heard that they had cast him out, and having found him, he said to him, Thou, dost thou believe on the Son of God?

v36 He answered and said, And who is he, Lord, that I may believe on him?

v37 And Jesus said to him, Thou hast both seen him, and he that speaks with thee is he.

v38 And he said, I believe, Lord: and he did him homage.

v39 And Jesus said, For judgment am I come into this world, that they which see not may see, and they which see may become blind.

v40 And [some] of the Pharisees who were with him heard these things, and they said to him, Are we blind also?

v41 Jesus said to them, If ye were blind ye would not have sin; but now ye say, We see, your sin remains.
