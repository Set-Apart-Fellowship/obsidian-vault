# John 6 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/43 - John/John 5|← John 5]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/43 - John/John]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/43 - John/John 7|John 7 →]]

***

v1 After these things Jesus went away beyond the sea of Galilee, [or] of Tiberias,

v2 and a great crowd followed him, because they saw the signs which he wrought upon the sick.

v3 And Jesus went up into the mountain, and there sat with his disciples:

v4 but the passover, the feast of the Jews, was near.

v5 Jesus then, lifting up his eyes and seeing that a great crowd is coming to him, says to Philip, Whence shall we buy loaves that these may eat?

v6 But this he said trying him, for he knew what he was going to do.

v7 Philip answered him, Loaves for two hundred denarii are not sufficient for them, that each may have some little [portion].

v8 One of his disciples, Andrew, Simon Peter's brother, says to him,

v9 There is a little boy here who has five barley loaves and two small fishes; but this, what is it for so many?

v10 [And] Jesus said, Make the men sit down. Now there was much grass in the place: the men therefore sat down, in number about five thousand.

v11 And Jesus took the loaves, and having given thanks, distributed [them] to those that were set down; and in like manner of the small fishes as much as they would.

v12 And when they had been filled, he says to his disciples, Gather together the fragments which are over and above, that nothing may be lost.

v13 They gathered [them] therefore together, and filled twelve hand-baskets full of fragments of the five barley loaves, which were over and above to those that had eaten.

v14 The men therefore, having seen the sign which Jesus had done, said, This is truly the prophet which is coming into the world.

v15 Jesus therefore knowing that they were going to come and seize him, that they might make [him] king, departed again to the mountain himself alone.

v16 But when evening was come, his disciples went down to the sea,

v17 and having gone on board ship, they went over the sea to Capernaum. And it had already become dark, and Jesus had not come to them,

v18 and the sea was agitated by a strong wind blowing.

v19 Having rowed then about twenty-five or thirty stadia, they see Jesus walking on the sea and coming near the ship; and they were frightened.

v20 But he says to them, It is I: be not afraid.

v21 They were willing therefore to receive him into the ship; and immediately the ship was at the land to which they went.

v22 On the morrow the crowd which stood on the other side of the sea, having seen that there was no other little ship there except that into which his disciples had got, and that Jesus had not gone with his disciples into the ship, but [that] his disciples had gone away alone;

v23 (but other little ships out of Tiberias came near to the place where they ate bread after the Lord had given thanks;)

v24 when therefore the crowd saw that Jesus was not there, nor his disciples, *they* got into the ships, and came to Capernaum, seeking Jesus.

v25 And having found him the other side of the sea, they said to him, Rabbi, when art thou arrived here?

v26 Jesus answered them and said, Verily, verily, I say to you, Ye seek me not because ye have seen signs, but because ye have eaten of the loaves and been filled.

v27 Work not [for] the food which perishes, but [for] the food which abook_ides unto life eternal, which the Son of man shall give to you; for him has the Father sealed, [even] God.

v28 They said therefore to him, What should we do that we may work the works of God?

v29 Jesus answered and said to them, This is the work of God, that ye believe on him whom *he* has sent.

v30 They said therefore to him, What sign then doest thou that we may see and believe thee? what dost thou work?

v31 Our fathers ate the manna in the wilderness, as it is written, He gave them bread out of heaven to eat.

v32 Jesus therefore said to them, Verily, verily, I say to you, [It is] not Moses that has given you the bread out of heaven; but my Father gives you the true bread out of heaven.

v33 For the bread of God is he who comes down out of heaven and gives life to the world.

v34 They said therefore to him, Lord, ever give to us this bread.

v35 [And] Jesus said to them, I am the bread of life: he that comes to me shall never hunger, and he that believes on me shall never thirst at any time.

v36 But I have said to you, that ye have also seen me and do not believe.

v37 All that the Father gives me shall come to me, and him that comes to me I will not at all cast out.

v38 For I am come down from heaven, not that I should do *my* will, but the will of him that has sent me.

v39 And this is the will of him that has sent me, that of all that he has given me I should lose nothing, but should raise it up in the last day.

v40 For this is the will of my Father, that every one who sees the Son, and believes on him, should have life eternal; and I will raise him up at the last day.

v41 The Jews therefore murmured about him, because he said, I am the bread which has come down out of heaven.

v42 And they said, Is not this Jesus the son of Joseph, whose father and mother we have known? how then does *he* say, I am come down out of heaven?

v43 Jesus therefore answered and said to them, Murmur not among yourselves.

v44 No one can come to me except the Father who has sent me draw him, and I will raise him up in the last day.

v45 It is written in the prophets, And they shall be all taught of God. Every one that has heard from the Father [himself], and has learned [of him], comes to me;

v46 not that any one has seen the Father, except he who is of God, he has seen the Father.

v47 Verily, verily, I say to you, He that believes [on me] has life eternal.

v48 I am the bread of life.

v49 Your fathers ate the manna in the wilderness and died.

v50 This is the bread which comes down out of heaven, that one may eat of it and not die.

v51 I am the living bread which has come down out of heaven: if any one shall have eaten of this bread he shall live for ever; but the bread withal which I shall give is my flesh, which I will give for the life of the world.

v52 The Jews therefore contended among themselves, saying, How can he give us this flesh to eat?

v53 Jesus therefore said to them, Verily, verily, I say unto you, Unless ye shall have eaten the flesh of the Son of man, and drunk his blood, ye have no life in yourselves.

v54 He that eats my flesh and drinks my blood has life eternal, and I will raise him up at the last day:

v55 for my flesh is truly food and my blood is truly drink.

v56 He that eats my flesh and drinks my blood dwells in me and I in him.

v57 As the living Father has sent me and I live on account of the Father, *he* also who eats me shall live also on account of me.

v58 This is the bread which has come down out of heaven. Not as the fathers ate and died: he that eats this bread shall live for ever.

v59 These things he said in [the] synagogue, teaching in Capernaum.

v60 Many therefore of his disciples having heard [it] said, This word is hard; who can hear it?

v61 But Jesus, knowing in himself that his disciples murmur concerning this, said to them, Does this offend you?

v62 If then ye see the Son of man ascending up where he was before?

v63 It is the Spirit which quickens, the flesh profits nothing: the words which I have spoken unto you are spirit and are life.

v64 But there are some of you who do not believe. For Jesus knew from the beginning who they were who did not believe, and who would deliver him up.

v65 And he said, Therefore said I unto you, that no one can come to me unless it be given to him from the Father.

v66 From that [time] many of his disciples went away back and walked no more with him.

v67 Jesus therefore said to the twelve, Will ye also go away?

v68 Simon Peter answered him, Lord, to whom shall we go? thou hast words of life eternal;

v69 and we have believed and known that thou art the holy one of God.

v70 Jesus answered them, Have not I chosen you the twelve? and of you one is a devil.

v71 Now he spoke of Judas [the son] of Simon, Iscariote, for he [it was who] should deliver him up, being one of the twelve.
