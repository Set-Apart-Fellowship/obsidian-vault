# Judges 5 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/07 - Judges/Judges 4|← Judges 4]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/07 - Judges/Judges]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/07 - Judges/Judges 6|Judges 6 →]]

***

v1 Then sang Deborah and Barak, the son of Abinoam, on that day, saying,

v2 For that leaders led in Israel, For that the people willingly offered themselves, Bless Jehovah!

v3 Hear, ye kings; give ear, ye princes, I, [even] I, will sing to Jehovah; I will hymn to Jehovah the God of Israel.

v4 Jehovah, when thou wentest forth from Seir, When thou marchedst out of the fields of Edom, The earth trembled, and the heavens dropped, Yea, the clouds dropped water.

v5 The mountains quaked before the face of Jehovah, That Sinai, from before Jehovah the God of Israel.

v6 In the days of Shamgar the son of Anath, in the days of Jael, The roads were unused, and the travellers on highways went by crooked paths.

v7 The villages ceased in Israel, Ceased until that I Deborah arose, That I arose a mother in Israel.

v8 They chose new gods; then was war in the gates: Was there a shield or spear seen among forty thousand in Israel?

v9 My heart is toward the governors of Israel, who offered themselves willingly among the people. Bless Jehovah!

v10 Ye that ride on white she-asses, ye that sit on carpets, and ye that walk by the way, consider.

v11 Because of the voice of those who divide [the spoil] in the midst of the places of drawing water; There they rehearse the righteous acts of Jehovah, His righteous acts toward his villages in Israel. Then the people of Jehovah went down to the gates.

v12 Awake, awake, Deborah! awake, awake, utter a song! Arise, Barak, and lead captive thy captives, thou son of Abinoam!

v13 Then come down, thou, the remnant of nobles, [as his] people; Jehovah! come down with me in the midst of the mighty ones.

v14 Out of Ephraim [came] those whose root was in Amalek; After thee was Benjamin among thy peoples. Out of Machir came down governors, And out of Zebulun they that handled the staff of the ruler.

v15 And the princes in Issachar were with Deborah; And Issachar, like Barak; They were sent into the valley at his feet. In the divisions of Reuben there were great resolves of heart!

v16 Why abodest thou among the sheepfolds, To hear the bleating of the flocks? In the divisions of Reuben there were great deliberations of heart!

v17 Gilead abode beyond Jordan; And Dan, why did he remain in ships? Asher sat on the sea-shore, And abode in his creeks.

v18 Zebulun is a people [that] jeoparded their lives unto death, Naphtali also, on the high places of the field.

v19 Kings came, -- they fought; Then fought the kings of Canaan; At Taanach, by the waters of Megiddo; They took no spoil of silver.

v20 From heaven was the fight; The stars from their courses fought with Sisera.

v21 The torrent of Kishon swept them away, That ancient torrent, the torrent Kishon. My soul, thou hast trodden down strength!

v22 Then did the horse-hoofs clatter with the coursings, The coursings of their steeds.

v23 Curse Meroz, saith the Angel of Jehovah; Curse, curse the inhabitants thereof; For they came not to the help of Jehovah, To the help of Jehovah among the mighty.

v24 Blessed above women shall Jael the wife of Heber the Kenite be, Blessed above women in the tent!

v25 He asked water, she gave milk; In the nobles' bowl she brought forth cream.

v26 She put her hand to the tent-pin, And her right hand to the workmen's hammer; And she smote Sisera, she struck through his head, Shattered and pierced through his temples.

v27 Between her feet he bowed, he fell, he lay down: Between her feet he bowed, he fell; Where he bowed, there he fell, overcome.

v28 Them other of Sisera looketh out at the window, And crieth through the lattice, Why is his chariot so long in coming? Why tarry the trampings of his chariots?

v29 The wise amongst her ladies answer [her], Yea, she returneth answer to herself,

v30 Have they not found, divided the booty, A damsel, two damsels, to each? A booty of dyed stuffs for Sisera, A booty of dyed stuffs of embroidery, Dyed stuff of double embroidery for the neck of a spoiler?

v31 So let all thine enemies perish, Jehovah! But let them that love him be as the rising of the sun in its might. And the land had rest forty years.
