# Proverbs 10 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 9|← Proverbs 9]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 11|Proverbs 11 →]]

***

v1 The Proverbs of Solomon. A wise son maketh a glad father; but a foolish son is the grief of his mother.

v2 Treasures of wickedness profit nothing; but righteousness delivereth from death.

v3 Jehovah suffereth not the soul of the righteous [man] to famish; but he repelleth the craving of the wicked.

v4 He cometh to want that dealeth with a slack hand; but the hand of the diligent maketh rich.

v5 He that gathereth in summer is a wise son; he that sleepeth in harvest is a son that causeth shame.

v6 Blessings are upon the head of a righteous [man]; but the mouth of the wicked covereth violence.

v7 The memory of the righteous [man] shall be blessed; but the name of the wicked shall rot.

v8 The wise in heart receiveth commandments; but a prating fool shall fall.

v9 He that walketh in integrity walketh securely; but he that perverteth his ways shall be known.

v10 He that winketh with the eye causeth grief, and a prating fool shall fall.

v11 The mouth of a righteous [man] is a fountain of life; but the mouth of the wicked covereth violence.

v12 Hatred stirreth up strifes; but love covereth all transgressions.

v13 In the lips of an intelligent [man] wisdom is found; but a rod is for the back of him that is void of understanding.

v14 The wise lay up knowledge; but the mouth of the fool is near destruction.

v15 The rich man's wealth is his strong city; the destruction of the poor is their poverty.

v16 The labour of a righteous [man] [tendeth] to life; the revenue of a wicked [man], to sin.

v17 Keeping instruction is the path to life; but he that forsaketh reproof goeth astray.

v18 He that covereth hatred hath lying lips, and he that sendeth forth a slander is a fool.

v19 In the multitude of words there wanteth not transgression; but he that restraineth his lips doeth wisely.

v20 The tongue of the righteous [man] is [as] choice silver; the heart of the wicked is little worth.

v21 The lips of a righteous [man] feed many; but fools die for want of understanding.

v22 The blessing of Jehovah, it maketh rich, and he addeth no sorrow to it.

v23 It is as sport to a foolish [man] to do wickedness; but a man of understanding hath wisdom.

v24 The fear of a wicked [man], it shall come upon him; but the desire of the righteous shall be granted.

v25 As a whirlwind passeth, so is the wicked no [more]; but the righteous is an everlasting foundation.

v26 As vinegar to the teeth, and as smoke to the eyes, so is the sluggard to them that send him.

v27 The fear of Jehovah prolongeth days; but the years of the wicked shall be shortened.

v28 The hope of the righteous is joy; but the expectation of the wicked shall perish.

v29 The way of Jehovah is strength to the perfect [man], but destruction to the workers of iniquity.

v30 The righteous [man] shall never be moved; but the wicked shall not inhabit the land.

v31 The mouth of a righteous [man] putteth forth wisdom; but the froward tongue shall be cut out.

v32 The lips of a righteous [man] know what is acceptable; but the mouth of the wicked is frowardness.
