# Proverbs 8 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 7|← Proverbs 7]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 9|Proverbs 9 →]]

***

v1 Doth not wisdom cry? and understanding give forth her voice?

v2 On the top of high places by the way, at the cross-paths she taketh her stand.

v3 Beside the gates, at the entry of the city, at the coming in at the doors, she crieth aloud.

v4 Unto you, men, I call, and my voice is to the sons of man:

v5 O ye simple, understand prudence; and ye foolish, understand sense.

v6 Hear, for I will speak excellent things, and the opening of my lips shall be right things.

v7 For my palate shall meditate truth, and wickedness is an abomination to my lips.

v8 All the words of my mouth are in righteousness; there is nothing tortuous or perverse in them.

v9 They are all plain to him that understandeth, and right to them that find knowledge.

v10 Receive my instruction, and not silver; and knowledge rather than choice gold:

v11 for wisdom is better than rubies, and all the things that may be desired are not equal to it.

v12 I wisdom dwell [with] prudence, and find the knowledge [which cometh] of reflection.

v13 The fear of Jehovah is to hate evil; pride, and arrogancy, and the evil way, and the froward mouth do I hate.

v14 Counsel is mine, and sound wisdom: I am intelligence; I have strength.

v15 By me kings reign, and rulers make just decrees;

v16 by me princes rule, and nobles, all the judges of the earth.

v17 I love those that love me; and they that seek me early shall find me.

v18 Riches and honour are with me; durable wealth and righteousness.

v19 My fruit is better than fine gold, yea, than pure gold; and my revenue than choice silver.

v20 I walk in the path of righteousness, in the midst of the paths of judgment:

v21 that I may cause those that love me to inherit substance; and I will fill their treasuries.

v22 Jehovah possessed me in the beginning of his way, before his works of old.

v23 I was set up from eternity, from the beginning, before the earth was.

v24 When there were no depths, I was brought forth, when there were no fountains abounding with water.

v25 Before the mountains were settled, before the hills was I brought forth;

v26 while as yet he had not made the earth, nor the fields, nor the beginning of the dust of the world.

v27 When he prepared the heavens I was there; when he ordained the circle upon the face of the deep;

v28 when he established the skies above, when the fountains of the deep became strong;

v29 when he imposed on the sea his decree that the waters should not pass his commandment, when he appointed the foundations of the earth:

v30 then I was by him [his] nursling, and I was daily his delight, rejoicing always before him;

v31 rejoicing in the habitable part of his earth, and my delights [were] with the sons of men.

v32 And now, sons, hearken unto me, and blessed are they that keep my ways:

v33 hear instruction and be wise, and refuse it not.

v34 Blessed is the man that heareth me, watching daily at my gates, waiting at the posts of my doors.

v35 For whoso findeth me findeth life, and obtaineth favour of Jehovah;

v36 but he that sinneth against me doeth violence to his own soul: all they that hate me love death.
