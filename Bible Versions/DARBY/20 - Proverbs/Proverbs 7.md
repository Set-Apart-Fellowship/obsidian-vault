# Proverbs 7 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 6|← Proverbs 6]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 8|Proverbs 8 →]]

***

v1 My son, keep my words, and lay up my commandments with thee.

v2 Keep my commandments, and live; and my teaching, as the apple of thine eye.

v3 Bind them upon thy fingers, write them upon the tablet of thy heart.

v4 Say unto wisdom, Thou art my sister, and call intelligence [thy] kinswoman:

v5 that they may keep thee from the strange woman, from the stranger who flattereth with her words.

v6 For at the window of my house, I looked forth through my lattice,

v7 and I beheld among the simple ones, I discerned among the sons, a young man void of understanding,

v8 passing through the street near her corner; and he went the way to her house,

v9 in the twilight, in the evening of the day, in the blackness of night and the darkness.

v10 And behold, there met him a woman in the attire of a harlot, and subtle of heart.

v11 She is clamorous and unmanageable; her feet abook_ide not in her house:

v12 now without, now in the broadways, -- and she lieth in wait at every corner.

v13 And she caught him and kissed him, and with an impudent face said unto him,

v14 I have peace-offerings with me; this day have I paid my vows:

v15 therefore came I forth to meet thee, to seek earnestly thy face, and I have found thee.

v16 I have decked my bed with tapestry coverlets of variegated linen from Egypt;

v17 I have perfumed my couch with myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon.

v18 Come, let us revel in love until the morning, let us delight ourselves with loves.

v19 For the husband is not at home, he is gone a long journey;

v20 he hath taken the money-bag with him, he will come home on the day of the full moon.

v21 With her much enticement she beguiled him; with the smoothness of her lips she constrained him.

v22 He went after her straightway, as an ox goeth to the slaughter, and as stocks [serve] for the correction of the fool;

v23 till an arrow strike through his liver: as a bird hasteth to the snare, and knoweth not that it is for its life.

v24 And now, ye sons, hearken unto me, and attend to the words of my mouth.

v25 Let not thy heart decline to her ways, go not astray in her paths:

v26 for she hath cast down many wounded, and all slain by her were strong.

v27 Her house is the way to Sheol, going down to the chambers of death.
