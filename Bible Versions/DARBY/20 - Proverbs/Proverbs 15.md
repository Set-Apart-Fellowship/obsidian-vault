# Proverbs 15 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 14|← Proverbs 14]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 16|Proverbs 16 →]]

***

v1 A soft answer turneth away fury; but a grievous word stirreth up anger.

v2 The tongue of the wise useth knowledge aright; but the mouth of the foolish poureth out folly.

v3 The eyes of Jehovah are in every place, beholding the evil and the good.

v4 Gentleness of tongue is a tree of life; but crookedness therein is a breaking of the spirit.

v5 A fool despiseth his father's instruction; but he that regardeth reproof becometh prudent.

v6 In the house of a righteous [man] is much treasure; but in the revenue of a wicked [man] is disturbance.

v7 The lips of the wise disperse knowledge, but not so the heart of the foolish.

v8 The sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination to Jehovah; but the prayer of the upright is his delight.

v9 The way of a wicked [man] is an abomination to Jehovah; but him that pursueth righteousness he loveth.

v10 Grievous correction is for him that forsaketh the path; he that hateth reproof shall die.

v11 Sheol and destruction are before Jehovah; how much more then the hearts of the children of men!

v12 A scorner loveth not one that reproveth him; he will not go unto the wise.

v13 A joyful heart maketh a cheerful countenance; but by sorrow of heart the spirit is broken.

v14 The heart of an intelligent [man] seeketh knowledge; but the mouth of the foolish feedeth on folly.

v15 All the days of the afflicted are evil; but a cheerful heart is a continual feast.

v16 Better is little with the fear of Jehovah than great store and disquietude therewith.

v17 Better is a meal of herbs where love is, than a fatted ox and hatred therewith.

v18 A furious man stirreth up contention; but he that is slow to anger appeaseth strife.

v19 The way of the sluggard is as a hedge of thorns; but the path of the upright is made plain.

v20 A wise son maketh a glad father; but a foolish man despiseth his mother.

v21 Folly is joy to him that is void of sense; but a man of understanding regulateth his walk.

v22 Without counsel purposes are disappointed; but in the multitude of counsellors they are established.

v23 A man hath joy by the answer of his mouth; and a word in its season, how good is it!

v24 The path of life is upwards for the wise, that he may depart from Sheol beneath.

v25 Jehovah plucketh up the house of the proud; but he establisheth the boundary of the widow.

v26 The thoughts of the evil [man] are an abomination to Jehovah; but pure words are pleasant.

v27 He that is greedy of gain troubleth his own house; but he that hateth gifts shall live.

v28 The heart of a righteous [man] studieth to answer; but the mouth of the wicked poureth out evil things.

v29 Jehovah is far from the wicked; but he heareth the prayer of the righteous.

v30 That which enlighteneth the eyes rejoiceth the heart; good tidings make the bones fat.

v31 The ear that heareth the reproof of life shall abook_ide among the wise.

v32 He that refuseth instruction despiseth his own soul; but he that heareth reproof getteth sense.

v33 The fear of Jehovah is the discipline of wisdom, and before honour [goeth] humility.
