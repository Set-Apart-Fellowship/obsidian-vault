# Proverbs 17 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 16|← Proverbs 16]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 18|Proverbs 18 →]]

***

v1 Better is a dry morsel, and quietness therewith, than a house full of feasting [with] strife.

v2 A wise servant shall rule over a son that causeth shame, and shall have part in the inheritance among the brethren.

v3 The fining-pot is for silver, and the furnace for gold; but Jehovah trieth the hearts.

v4 The evil-doer giveth heed to iniquitous lips; the liar giveth ear to a mischievous tongue.

v5 Whoso mocketh a poor [man] reproacheth his Maker; he that is glad at calamity shall not be held innocent.

v6 Children's children are the crown of old men; and the glory of children are their fathers.

v7 Excellent speech becometh not a vile [man]; how much less do lying lips a noble!

v8 A gift is a precious stone in the eyes of the possessor: whithersoever it turneth it prospereth.

v9 He that covereth transgression seeketh love; but he that bringeth a matter up again separateth very friends.

v10 A reproof entereth more deeply into him that hath understanding than a hundred stripes into a fool.

v11 An evil [man] seeketh only rebellion; but a cruel messenger shall be sent against him.

v12 Let a bear robbed of her whelps meet a man rather than a fool in his folly.

v13 Whoso rewardeth evil for good, evil shall not depart from his house.

v14 The beginning of contention is [as] when one letteth out water; therefore leave off strife before it become vehement.

v15 He that justifieth the wicked, and he that condemneth the righteous, even they both are abomination to Jehovah.

v16 To what purpose is there a price in the hand of a fool to get wisdom, seeing [he] hath no sense?

v17 The friend loveth at all times, and a brother is born for adversity.

v18 A senseless man striketh hands, becoming surety for his neighbour.

v19 He loveth transgression that loveth a quarrel; he that maketh high his gate seeketh destruction.

v20 He that hath a perverse heart findeth no good; and he that shifteth about with his tongue falleth into evil.

v21 He that begetteth a fool [doeth it] to his sorrow, and the father of a vile [man] hath no joy.

v22 A joyful heart promoteth healing; but a broken spirit drieth up the bones.

v23 A wicked [man] taketh a gift out of the bosom, to pervert the paths of judgment.

v24 Wisdom is before him that hath understanding; but the eyes of a fool are in the ends of the earth.

v25 A foolish son is a grief to his father, and bitterness to her that bore him.

v26 To punish a righteous [man] is not good, nor to strike nobles because of [their] uprightness.

v27 He that hath knowledge spareth his words; and a man of understanding is of a cool spirit.

v28 Even a fool when he holdeth his peace is reckoned wise, [and] he that shutteth his lips, intelligent.
