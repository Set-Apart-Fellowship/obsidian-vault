# Proverbs 13 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 12|← Proverbs 12]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 14|Proverbs 14 →]]

***

v1 A wise son [heareth] his father's instruction; but a scorner heareth not rebuke.

v2 A man shall eat good by the fruit of his mouth; but the soul of the treacherous, violence.

v3 He that guardeth his mouth keepeth his soul; destruction shall be to him that openeth wide his lips.

v4 A sluggard's soul desireth and hath nothing; but the soul of the diligent shall be made fat.

v5 A righteous [man] hateth lying; but the wicked maketh himself odious and cometh to shame.

v6 Righteousness preserveth him that is perfect in the way; but wickedness overthroweth the sinner.

v7 There is that feigneth himself rich, and hath nothing; there is that maketh himself poor, and hath great wealth.

v8 The ransom of a man's life is his riches; but the indigent heareth not rebuke.

v9 The light of the righteous rejoiceth; but the lamp of the wicked shall be put out.

v10 By pride there only cometh contention; but with the well-advised is wisdom.

v11 Wealth [gotten] by vanity diminisheth; but he that gathereth by manual-labour shall increase [it].

v12 Hope deferred maketh the heart sick; but the desire [that] cometh to pass is a tree of life.

v13 Whoso despiseth the word shall be held by it; but he that feareth the commandment shall be rewarded.

v14 The teaching of the wise [man] is a fountain of life, to turn away from the snares of death.

v15 Good understanding procureth favour; but the way of the treacherous is hard.

v16 Every prudent [man] acteth with knowledge; but the foolish layeth open [his] folly.

v17 A wicked messenger falleth into evil; but a faithful ambassador is health.

v18 Poverty and shame shall be [to] him that refuseth instruction; but he that regardeth reproof shall be honoured.

v19 The desire accomplished is sweet to the soul; but it is abomination to the foolish to depart from evil.

v20 He that walketh with wise [men] becometh wise; but a companion of the foolish will be depraved.

v21 Evil pursueth sinners; but to the righteous good shall be repaid.

v22 A good man leaveth an inheritance to his children's children; but the wealth of the sinner is laid up for the righteous [man].

v23 Much food is in the tillage of the poor; but there is that is lost for want of judgment.

v24 He that spareth his rod hateth his son; but he that loveth him chasteneth him betimes.

v25 The righteous eateth to the satisfying of his soul; but the belly of the wicked shall want.
