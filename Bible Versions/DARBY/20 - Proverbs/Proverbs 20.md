# Proverbs 20 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 19|← Proverbs 19]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 21|Proverbs 21 →]]

***

v1 Wine is a scorner, strong drink is raging; and whoso erreth thereby is not wise.

v2 The terror of a king is as the roaring of a lion: he that provoketh him to anger sinneth against his own soul.

v3 It is an honour for a man to cease from strife; but every fool rusheth into it.

v4 The sluggard will not plough by reason of the winter; he shall beg in harvest, and have nothing.

v5 Counsel in the heart of man is deep water, and a man of understanding draweth it out.

v6 Most men will proclaim every one his own kindness; but a faithful man who shall find?

v7 The righteous walketh in his integrity: blessed are his children after him!

v8 A king sitting on the throne of judgment scattereth away all evil with his eyes.

v9 Who can say, I have made my heart clean, I am pure from my sin?

v10 Divers weights, divers measures, even both of them are abomination to Jehovah.

v11 Even a child is known by his doings, whether his work be pure, and whether it be right.

v12 The hearing ear, and the seeing eye, Jehovah hath made even both of them.

v13 Love not sleep, lest thou come to poverty; open thine eyes, [and] thou shalt be satisfied with bread.

v14 Bad! bad! saith the buyer; but when he is gone his way, then he boasteth.

v15 There is gold, and a multitude of rubies; but the lips of knowledge are a precious Jewel.

v16 Take his garment that is become surety [for] another, and hold him in pledge for strangers.

v17 Bread of falsehood is sweet to a man, but afterwards his mouth shall be filled with gravel.

v18 Plans are established by counsel; and with good advice make war.

v19 He that goeth about talebearing revealeth secrets; therefore meddle not with him that openeth his lips.

v20 Whoso curseth his father or his mother, his lamp shall be put out in the blackest darkness.

v21 An inheritance obtained hastily at the beginning will not be blessed in the end.

v22 Say not, I will recompense evil: wait on Jehovah, and he shall save thee.

v23 Divers weights are an abomination unto Jehovah; and a false balance is not good.

v24 The steps of a man are from Jehovah; and how can a man understand his own way?

v25 It is a snare to a man rashly to say, It is hallowed, and after vows to make inquiry.

v26 A wise king scattereth the wicked, and bringeth the wheel over them.

v27 Man's spirit is the lamp of Jehovah, searching all the inner parts of the belly.

v28 Mercy and truth preserve the king; and he upholdeth his throne by mercy.

v29 The glory of young men is their strength; and the beauty of old men is the grey head.

v30 Wounding stripes purge away evil, and strokes [purge] the inner parts of the belly.
