# Proverbs 3 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 2|← Proverbs 2]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 4|Proverbs 4 →]]

***

v1 My son, forget not my teaching, and let thy heart observe my commandments;

v2 for length of days, and years of life, and peace shall they add to thee.

v3 Let not loving-kindness and truth forsake thee; bind them about thy neck, write them upon the tablet of thy heart:

v4 and thou shalt find favour and good understanding in the sight of God and man.

v5 Confide in Jehovah with all thy heart, and lean not unto thine own intelligence;

v6 in all thy ways acknowledge him, and he will make plain thy paths.

v7 Be not wise in thine own eyes; fear Jehovah, and depart from evil:

v8 it shall be health for thy navel, and moisture for thy bones.

v9 Honour Jehovah with thy substance, and with the first-fruits of all thine increase;

v10 so shall thy barns be filled with plenty, and thy vats shall overflow with new wine.

v11 My son, despise not the instruction of Jehovah, neither be weary of his chastisement;

v12 for whom Jehovah loveth he chasteneth, even as a father the son in whom he delighteth.

v13 Blessed is the man that findeth wisdom, and the man that getteth understanding.

v14 For the gain thereof is better than the gain of silver, and her revenue than fine gold.

v15 She is more precious than rubies; and all the things thou canst desire are not equal unto her.

v16 Length of days is in her right hand; in her left hand riches and honour.

v17 Her ways are ways of pleasantness, and all her paths are peace.

v18 She is a tree of life to them that lay hold upon her; and happy is he that retaineth her.

v19 Jehovah by wisdom founded the earth; by understanding he established the heavens.

v20 By his knowledge the deeps were broken up, and the skies drop down the dew.

v21 My son, let them not depart from thine eyes; keep sound wisdom and discretion:

v22 so shall they be life unto thy soul, and grace unto thy neck.

v23 Then shalt thou walk in thy way securely, and thy foot shall not stumble;

v24 when thou liest down, thou shalt not be afraid, but thou shalt lie down and thy sleep shall be sweet.

v25 Be not afraid of sudden fear, neither of the destruction of the wicked, when it cometh;

v26 for Jehovah shall be thy confidence, and he will keep thy foot from being taken.

v27 Withhold not good from them to whom it is due, when it is in the power of thy hand to do it.

v28 Say not unto thy neighbour, Go, and come again, and to-morrow I will give, when thou hast it by thee.

v29 Devise not evil against thy neighbour, seeing he dwelleth securely by thee.

v30 Strive not with a man without cause, if he have done thee no harm.

v31 Envy not the man of violence, and choose none of his ways.

v32 For the perverse is an abomination to Jehovah; but his secret is with the upright.

v33 The curse of Jehovah is in the house of the wicked; but he blesseth the habitation of the righteous.

v34 He indeed scorneth the scorners; but he giveth grace unto the lowly.

v35 The wise shall inherit glory; but shame shall be the promotion of the foolish.
