# Proverbs 1 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 2|Proverbs 2 →]]

***

v1 Proverbs of Solomon, son of David, king of Israel:

v2 to know wisdom and instruction; to discern the words of understanding;

v3 to receive the instruction of wisdom, righteousness and judgment, and equity;

v4 to give prudence to the simple, to the young man knowledge and discretion.

v5 He that is wise will hear, and will increase learning; and the intelligent will gain wise counsels:

v6 to understand a proverb and an allegory, the words of the wise and their enigmas.

v7 The fear of Jehovah is the beginning of knowledge: fools despise wisdom and instruction.

v8 Hear, my son, the instruction of thy father, and forsake not the teaching of thy mother;

v9 for they shall be a garland of grace unto thy head, and chains about thy neck.

v10 My son, if sinners entice thee, consent not.

v11 If they say, Come with us, let us lay wait for blood, let us lurk secretly for the innocent without cause;

v12 let us swallow them up alive as Sheol, and whole, as those that go down into the pit;

v13 we shall find all precious substance, we shall fill our houses with spoil:

v14 cast in thy lot among us; we will all have one purse:

v15 -- my son, walk not in the way with them, keep back thy foot from their path;

v16 for their feet run to evil, and they make haste to shed blood.

v17 For in vain the net is spread in the sight of anything which hath wings.

v18 And these lay wait for their own blood; they lurk secretly for their own lives.

v19 So are the paths of every one that is greedy of gain: it taketh away the life of its possessors.

v20 Wisdom crieth without; she raiseth her voice in the broadways;

v21 she calleth in the chief [place] of concourse, in the entry of the gates; in the city she uttereth her words:

v22 How long, simple ones, will ye love simpleness, and scorners take pleasure in their scorning, and the foolish hate knowledge?

v23 Turn you at my reproof: behold, I will pour forth my spirit unto you, I will make known to you my words.

v24 Because I have called, and ye refused; I have stretched out my hand, and no one regarded;

v25 and ye have rejected all my counsel, and would none of my reproof:

v26 I also will laugh in your calamity, I will mock when your fear cometh;

v27 when your fear cometh as sudden destruction, and your calamity cometh as a whirlwind; when distress and anguish come upon you:

v28 -- then will they call upon me, but I will not answer; they will seek me early, and shall not find me.

v29 Because they hated knowledge, and did not choose the fear of Jehovah;

v30 they would none of my counsel, they despised all my reproof:

v31 therefore shall they eat of the fruit of their way, and be filled with their own devices.

v32 For the turning away of the simple shall slay them, and the prosperity of the foolish shall cause them to perish.

v33 But whoso hearkeneth unto me shall dwell safely, and shall be at rest from fear of evil.
