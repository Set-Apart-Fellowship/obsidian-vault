# Proverbs 22 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 21|← Proverbs 21]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 23|Proverbs 23 →]]

***

v1 A [good] name is rather to be chosen than great riches; loving favour rather than silver and gold.

v2 The rich and poor meet together; Jehovah is the maker of them all.

v3 A prudent [man] seeth the evil, and hideth himself; but the simple pass on, and are punished.

v4 The reward of humility [and] the fear of Jehovah is riches, and honour, and life.

v5 Thorns [and] snares are in the way of the perverse: he that keepeth his soul holdeth himself far from them.

v6 Train up the child according to the tenor of his way, and when he is old he will not depart from it.

v7 The rich ruleth over the poor; and the borrower is servant to the lender.

v8 He that soweth unrighteousness shall reap iniquity, and the rod of his wrath shall have an end.

v9 He that hath a bountiful eye shall be blessed, for he giveth of his bread to the poor.

v10 Cast out the scorner, and contention will depart, and strife and ignominy shall cease.

v11 He that loveth pureness of heart, upon whose lips is grace, the king is his friend.

v12 The eyes of Jehovah preserve knowledge; but he overthroweth the words of the unfaithful.

v13 The sluggard saith, There is a lion without, I shall be killed in the streets!

v14 The mouth of strange women is a deep ditch: he with whom Jehovah is displeased shall fall therein.

v15 Folly is bound in the heart of a child; the rod of correction shall drive it far from him.

v16 He that oppresseth the poor, it is to enrich him; he that giveth to the rich, [bringeth] only to want.

v17 Incline thine ear, and hear the words of the wise, and apply thy heart unto my knowledge.

v18 For it is a pleasant thing if thou keep them within thee: they shall be together fitted on thy lips.

v19 That thy confidence may be in Jehovah, I have made [them] known to thee this day, even to thee.

v20 Have not I written to thee excellent things, in counsels and knowledge,

v21 that I might make thee know the certainty of the words of truth; that thou mightest carry back words of truth to them that send thee?

v22 Rob not the poor, because he is poor, neither oppress the afflicted in the gate;

v23 for Jehovah will plead their cause, and despoil the soul of those that despoil them.

v24 Make no friendship with an angry man, and go not with a furious man;

v25 lest thou learn his paths, and get a snare to thy soul.

v26 Be not of them that strike hands, of them that are sureties for debts:

v27 if thou hast nothing to pay, why should he take away thy bed from under thee?

v28 Remove not the ancient landmark which thy fathers have set.

v29 Hast thou seen a man diligent in his work? He shall stand before kings; he shall not stand before the mean.
