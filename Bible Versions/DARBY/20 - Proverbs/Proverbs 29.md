# Proverbs 29 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 28|← Proverbs 28]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 30|Proverbs 30 →]]

***

v1 He that being often reproved hardeneth his neck, shall suddenly be destroyed, and without remedy.

v2 When the righteous increase, the people rejoice; but when the wicked beareth rule, the people mourn.

v3 Whoso loveth wisdom rejoiceth his father; but he that is a companion of harlots destroyeth [his] substance.

v4 A king by just judgment establisheth the land; but he that taketh gifts overthroweth it.

v5 A man that flattereth his neighbour spreadeth a net for his steps.

v6 In the transgression of an evil man there is a snare; but the righteous shall sing and rejoice.

v7 The righteous taketh knowledge of the cause of the poor; the wicked understandeth not knowledge.

v8 Scornful men set the city in a flame; but the wise turn away anger.

v9 If a wise man contendeth with a fool, whether he rage or laugh, [he] hath no rest.

v10 The bloodthirsty hate the perfect, but the upright care for his soul.

v11 A fool uttereth all his mind; but a wise [man] keepeth it back.

v12 If a ruler hearken to lying words, all his servants are wicked.

v13 The indigent and the oppressor meet together; Jehovah lighteneth the eyes of them both.

v14 A king that faithfully judgeth the poor, his throne shall be established for ever.

v15 The rod and reproof give wisdom; but a child left [to himself] bringeth his mother to shame.

v16 When the wicked increase, transgression increaseth; but the righteous shall see their fall.

v17 Chasten thy son, and he shall give thee rest, and shall give delight unto thy soul.

v18 Where there is no vision the people cast off restraint; but happy is he that keepeth the law.

v19 A servant is not corrected by words: he understandeth indeed, but he will not answer.

v20 Hast thou seen a man hasty in his words? there is more hope of a fool than of him.

v21 He that delicately bringeth up his servant from a child, shall in the end have him as a son.

v22 An angry man exciteth contention; and a furious man aboundeth in transgression.

v23 A man's pride bringeth him low; but the humble in spirit shall obtain honour.

v24 Whoso shareth with a thief hateth his own soul: he heareth the adjuration, and declareth not.

v25 The fear of man bringeth a snare; but whoso putteth his confidence in Jehovah is protected.

v26 Many seek the ruler's face; but a man's right judgment is from Jehovah.

v27 An unjust man is an abomination to the righteous; and he that is of upright way is an abomination to the wicked [man].
