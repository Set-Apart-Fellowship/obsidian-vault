# Proverbs 21 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 20|← Proverbs 20]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 22|Proverbs 22 →]]

***

v1 The king's heart in the hand of Jehovah is [as] brooks of water: he turneth it whithersoever he will.

v2 Every way of a man is right in his own eyes; but Jehovah weigheth the hearts.

v3 To exercise justice and judgment is more acceptable to Jehovah than sacrifice.

v4 Lofty eyes, and a proud heart, the lamp of the wicked, is sin.

v5 The thoughts of the diligent [tend] only to plenteousness; but of every one that is hasty, only to want.

v6 The getting of treasures by a lying tongue is a fleeting breath of them that seek death.

v7 The devastation of the wicked sweepeth them away, because they refuse to do what is right.

v8 Very crooked is the way of a guilty man; but as for the pure, his work is upright.

v9 It is better to dwell in a corner of the housetop, than with a contentious woman, and a house in common.

v10 The soul of the wicked desireth evil: his neighbour findeth no favour in his eyes.

v11 When the scorner is punished, the simple becometh wise; and when the wise is instructed, he receiveth knowledge.

v12 One that is righteous wisely considereth the house of the wicked: he overthroweth the wicked to [their] ruin.

v13 Whoso stoppeth his ears at the cry of the poor, he also himself shall cry, and shall not be heard.

v14 A gift in secret pacifieth anger; and a present in the bosom, vehement fury.

v15 It is joy to a righteous [man] to do what is right; but it is ruin for the workers of iniquity.

v16 The man that wandereth out of the way of wisdom shall abook_ide in the congregation of the dead.

v17 He that loveth mirth shall be a poor man; he that loveth wine and oil shall not be rich.

v18 The wicked is a ransom for the righteous, and a treacherous [man] in the stead of the upright.

v19 It is better to dwell in a desert land. than with a contentious and irritable woman.

v20 There is costly store and oil in the dwelling of a wise [man]; but a foolish man swalloweth it up.

v21 He that followeth after righteousness and mercy findeth life, righteousness, and honour.

v22 A wise [man] scaleth the city of the mighty, and casteth down the strength of the confidence thereof.

v23 Whoso keepeth his mouth and his tongue keepeth his soul from troubles.

v24 Proud, arrogant, scorner is his name who dealeth in proud wrath.

v25 The desire of the sluggard killeth him; for his hands refuse to work:

v26 He coveteth greedily all the day long; but the righteous giveth and spareth not.

v27 The sacrifice of the wicked is abomination: how much more when they bring it with a wicked purpose!

v28 A lying witness shall perish; and a man that heareth shall speak constantly.

v29 A wicked man hardeneth his face; but as for the upright, he establisheth his way.

v30 There is no wisdom, nor understanding, nor counsel against Jehovah.

v31 The horse is prepared for the day of battle; but safety is of Jehovah.
