# Proverbs 23 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 22|← Proverbs 22]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 24|Proverbs 24 →]]

***

v1 When thou sittest to eat with a ruler, consider well who is before thee;

v2 and put a knife to thy throat, if thou be a man given to appetite.

v3 Be not desirous of his dainties; for they are deceitful food.

v4 Weary not thyself to become rich; cease from thine own intelligence:

v5 wilt thou set thine eyes upon it, it is gone; for indeed it maketh itself wings and it flieth away as an eagle towards the heavens.

v6 Eat thou not the food of him that hath an evil eye, neither desire thou his dainties.

v7 For as he thinketh in his soul, so is he. Eat and drink! will he say unto thee; but his heart is not with thee.

v8 Thy morsel which thou hast eaten must thou vomit up, and thou wilt have wasted thy sweet words.

v9 Speak not in the ears of a foolish [man], for he will despise the wisdom of thy words.

v10 Remove not the ancient landmark; and enter not into the fields of the fatherless:

v11 for their redeemer is mighty; he will plead their cause against thee.

v12 Apply thy heart unto instruction, and thine ears to the words of knowledge.

v13 Withhold not correction from the child; for [if] thou beatest him with the rod, he shall not die:

v14 thou shalt beat him with the rod, and shalt deliver his soul from Sheol.

v15 My son, if thy heart be wise, my heart shall rejoice, even mine;

v16 and my reins shall exult, when thy lips speak right things.

v17 Let not thy heart envy sinners, but [be thou] in the fear of Jehovah all the day;

v18 for surely there is a result, and thine expectation shall not be cut off.

v19 Thou, my son, hear and be wise, and direct thy heart in the way.

v20 Be not among winebibbers, among riotous eaters of flesh.

v21 For the drunkard and the glutton shall come to poverty; and drowsiness clotheth with rags.

v22 Hearken unto thy father that begat thee, and despise not thy mother when she is old.

v23 Buy the truth, and sell it not; wisdom, and instruction, and intelligence.

v24 The father of a righteous [man] shall greatly rejoice, and he that begetteth a wise [son] shall have joy of him:

v25 let thy father and thy mother have joy, and let her that bore thee rejoice.

v26 My son, give me thy heart, and let thine eyes observe my ways.

v27 For a whore is a deep ditch; and a strange woman is a narrow pit.

v28 She also lieth in wait as a robber, and increaseth the treacherous among men.

v29 Who hath woe? Who hath sorrow? Who contentions? Who complaining? Who wounds without cause? Who redness of eyes?

v30 -- They that tarry long at the wine; they that go to try mixed wine.

v31 Look not upon the wine when it is red, when it sparkleth in the cup, and goeth down smoothly:

v32 at the last it biteth like a serpent, and stingeth like an adder.

v33 Thine eyes shall behold strange women, and thy heart shall speak froward things;

v34 and thou shalt be as he that lieth down in the midst of the sea, and as he that lieth down upon the top of a mast:

v35 -- ''They have smitten me, [and] I am not sore; they have beaten me, [and] I knew it not. When shall I awake? I will seek it yet again.''
