# Proverbs 6 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 5|← Proverbs 5]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 7|Proverbs 7 →]]

***

v1 My son, if thou hast become surety for thy friend, if thou hast stricken thy hand for a stranger,

v2 thou art snared with the words of thy mouth, thou art taken with the words of thy mouth.

v3 Do this now, my son, and deliver thyself, since thou hast come into the hand of thy friend: go, humble thyself, and be urgent with thy friend.

v4 Give not sleep to thine eyes, nor slumber to thine eyelids:

v5 deliver thyself as a gazelle from the hand [of the hunter], and as a bird from the hand of the fowler.

v6 Go to the ant, thou sluggard; consider her ways and be wise:

v7 which having no chief, overseer, or ruler,

v8 provideth her bread in the summer, [and] gathereth her food in the harvest.

v9 How long, sluggard, wilt thou lie down? When wilt thou arise out of thy sleep?

v10 A little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to rest!

v11 So shall thy poverty come as a roving plunderer, and thy penury as an armed man.

v12 A man of Belial, a wicked person, is he that goeth about with a perverse mouth;

v13 he winketh with his eyes, he speaketh with his feet, he teacheth with his fingers;

v14 deceits are in his heart; he deviseth mischief at all times, he soweth discords.

v15 Therefore shall his calamity come suddenly: in a moment shall he be broken, and without remedy.

v16 These six [things] doth Jehovah hate, yea, seven are an abomination unto him:

v17 haughty eyes, a lying tongue, and hands that shed innocent blood;

v18 a heart that deviseth wicked imaginations; feet that are swift in running to mischief;

v19 a false witness that uttereth lies, and he that soweth discords among brethren.

v20 My son, observe thy father's commandment, and forsake not the teaching of thy mother;

v21 bind them continually upon thy heart, tie them about thy neck:

v22 when thou walkest, it shall lead thee; when thou sleepest, it shall keep thee; and [when] thou awakest, it shall talk with thee.

v23 For the commandment is a lamp, and the teaching a light, and reproofs of instruction are the way of life:

v24 to keep thee from the evil woman, from the flattery of the tongue of a strange woman.

v25 Lust not after her beauty in thy heart, neither let her take thee with her eyelids;

v26 for by means of a whorish woman [a man is brought] to a loaf of bread, and another's wife doth hunt for the precious soul.

v27 Can a man take fire in his bosom, and his garments not be burned?

v28 Can one go upon hot coals, and his feet not be scorched?

v29 So he that goeth in to his neighbour's wife: whosoever toucheth her shall not be innocent.

v30 They do not despise a thief, if he steal to satisfy his soul when he is hungry:

v31 and if he be found, he shall restore sevenfold; he shall give all the substance of his house.

v32 Whoso committeth adultery with a woman is void of understanding: he that doeth it destroyeth his own soul.

v33 A wound and contempt shall he get; and his reproach shall not be wiped away.

v34 For jealousy is the rage of a man, and he will not spare in the day of vengeance;

v35 he will not regard any ransom, neither will he rest content though thou multipliest [thy] gifts.
