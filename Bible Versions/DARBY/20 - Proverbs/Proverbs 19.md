# Proverbs 19 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 18|← Proverbs 18]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 20|Proverbs 20 →]]

***

v1 Better is a poor [man] that walketh in his integrity, than he that is perverse in his lips, and is a fool.

v2 Also that a person be without knowledge is not good; and he that hasteth with his feet maketh false steps.

v3 The folly of man distorteth his way, and his heart is irritated against Jehovah.

v4 Wealth addeth many friends; but the poor is separated from his friend.

v5 A false witness shall not be held innocent, and he that uttereth lies shall not escape.

v6 Many court the favour of a noble; and every one is friend to a man that giveth.

v7 All the brethren of a poor [man] hate him; how much more do his friends go far from him: he pursueth [them] with words, -- they are not [to be found].

v8 He that getteth sense loveth his own soul; he that keepeth understanding shall find good.

v9 A false witness shall not be held innocent, and he that uttereth lies shall perish.

v10 Good living beseemeth not a fool; how much less for a servant to have rule over princes.

v11 The discretion of a man maketh him slow to anger, and it is his glory to pass over a transgression.

v12 The king's displeasure is as the roaring of a lion; but his favour is as dew upon the grass.

v13 A foolish son is the calamity of his father; and the contentions of a wife are a continual dropping.

v14 House and wealth are an inheritance from fathers; but a prudent wife is from Jehovah.

v15 Slothfulness casteth into a deep sleep, and the idle soul shall suffer hunger.

v16 He that keepeth the commandment keepeth his soul; he that is careless of his ways shall die.

v17 He that is gracious to the poor lendeth unto Jehovah; and what he hath bestowed will he repay unto him.

v18 Chasten thy son, seeing there is hope; but set not thy soul upon killing him.

v19 A man of great wrath shall suffer punishment; for if thou deliver [him], yet thou must do it again.

v20 Hear counsel, and receive instruction, that thou mayest be wise in thy latter end.

v21 Many are the thoughts in a man's heart, but the counsel of Jehovah, that doth stand.

v22 The charm of a man is his kindness; and a poor [man] is better than a liar.

v23 The fear of Jehovah [tendeth] to life, and he [that hath it] shall rest satisfied without being visited with evil.

v24 A sluggard burieth his hand in the dish, and will not even bring it to his mouth again.

v25 Smite a scorner, and the simple will beware; reprove the intelligent, and he will understand knowledge.

v26 He that ruineth [his] father and chaseth away [his] mother is a son that causeth shame and bringeth reproach.

v27 Cease, my son, to hear the instruction which causeth to stray from the words of knowledge.

v28 A witness of Belial scorneth judgment, and the mouth of the wicked swalloweth down iniquity.

v29 Judgments are prepared for scorners, and stripes for the back of the foolish.
