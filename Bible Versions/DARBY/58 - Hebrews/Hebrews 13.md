# Hebrews 13 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12|← Hebrews 12]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews]]

***

v1 Let brotherly love abook_ide.

v2 Be not forgetful of hospitality; for by it some have unawares entertained angels.

v3 Remember prisoners, as bound with [them]; those that are evil-treated, as being yourselves also in [the] body.

v4 [Let] marriage [be held] every way in honour, and the bed [be] undefiled; for fornicators and adulterers will God judge.

v5 [Let your] conversation [be] without love of money, satisfied with [your] present circumstances; for *he* has said, I will not leave thee, neither will I forsake thee.

v6 So that, taking courage, we may say, The Lord [is] my helper, and I will not be afraid: what will man do unto me?

v7 Remember your leaders who have spoken to you the word of God; and considering the issue of their conversation, imitate their faith.

v8 Jesus Christ [is] the same yesterday, and to-day, and to the ages [to come].

v9 Be not carried away with various and strange doctrines; for [it is] good that the heart be confirmed with grace, not meats; those who have walked in which have not been profited by [them].

v10 We have an altar of which they have no right to eat who serve the tabernacle;

v11 for of those beasts whose blood is carried [as sacrifices for sin] into the [holy of] holies by the high priest, of these the bodies are burned outside the camp.

v12 Wherefore also Jesus, that he might sanctify the people by his own blood, suffered without the gate:

v13 therefore let us go forth to him without the camp, bearing his reproach:

v14 for we have not here an abook_iding city, but we seek the coming one.

v15 By him therefore let us offer [the] sacrifice of praise continually to God, that is, [the] fruit of [the] lips confessing his name.

v16 But of doing good and communicating [of your substance] be not forgetful, for with such sacrifices God is well pleased.

v17 Obey your leaders, and be submissive; for *they* watch over your souls as those that shall give account; that they may do this with joy, and not groaning, for this [would be] unprofitable for you.

v18 Pray for us: for we persuade ourselves that we have a good conscience, in all things desirous to walk rightly.

v19 But I much more beseech [you] to do this, that I may the more quickly be restored to you.

v20 But the God of peace, who brought again from among [the] dead our Lord Jesus, the great shepherd of the sheep, in [the power of the] blood of [the] eternal covenant,

v21 perfect you in every good work to the doing of his will, doing in you what is pleasing before him through Jesus Christ; to whom [be] glory for the ages of ages. Amen.

v22 But I beseech you, brethren, bear the word of exhortation, for it is but in few words that I have written to you.

v23 Know that our brother Timotheus is set at liberty; with whom, if he should come soon, I will see you.

v24 Salute all your leaders, and all the saints. They from Italy salute you.

v25 Grace [be] with you all. Amen.
