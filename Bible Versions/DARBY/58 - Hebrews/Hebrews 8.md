# Hebrews 8 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7|← Hebrews 7]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9|Hebrews 9 →]]

***

v1 Now a summary of the things of which we are speaking [is], We have such a one high priest who has sat down on [the] right hand of the throne of the greatness in the heavens;

v2 minister of the holy places and of the true tabernacle, which the Lord has pitched, [and] not man.

v3 For every high priest is constituted for the offering both of gifts and sacrifices; whence it is needful that this one also should have something which he may offer.

v4 If then indeed he were upon earth, he would not even be a priest, there being those who offer the gifts according to the law,

v5 (who serve the representation and shadow of heavenly things, according as Moses was oracularly told [when] about to make the tabernacle; for See, saith He, that thou make all things according to the pattern which has been shewn to thee in the mountain.)

v6 But now he has got a more excellent ministry, by so much as he is mediator of a better covenant, which is established on the footing of better promises.

v7 For if that first was faultless, place had not been sought for a second.

v8 For finding fault, he says to them, Behold, days come, saith the Lord, and I will consummate a new covenant as regards the house of Israel, and as regards the house of Juda;

v9 not according to the covenant which I made to their fathers in [the] day of my taking their hand to lead them out of the land of Egypt; because *they* did not continue in my covenant, and *I* did not regard them, saith [the] Lord.

v10 Because this [is] the covenant that I will covenant to the house of Israel after those days, saith the Lord: Giving my laws into their mind, I will write them also upon their hearts; and I will be to them for God, and *they* shall be to me for people.

v11 And they shall not teach each his fellow-citizen, and each his brother, saying, Know the Lord; because all shall know me in themselves, from [the] little one [among them] unto [the] great among them.

v12 Because I will be merciful to their unrighteousnesses, and their sins and their lawlessnesses I will never remember any more.

v13 In that he says New, he has made the first old; but that which grows old and aged [is] near disappearing.
