# Hebrews 3 (Darby Translation)

[[Bible Versions/DARBY/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 2|← Hebrews 2]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews]] | [[Bible Versions/DARBY/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4|Hebrews 4 →]]

***

v1 Wherefore, holy brethren, partakers of [the] heavenly calling, consider the Apostle and High Priest of our confession, Jesus,

v2 who is faithful to him that has constituted him, as Moses also in all his house.

v3 For *he* has been counted worthy of greater glory than Moses, by how much he that has built it has more honour than the house.

v4 For every house is built by some one; but he who has built all things [is] God.

v5 And Moses indeed [was] faithful in all his house, as a ministering servant, for a testimony of the things to be spoken after;

v6 but Christ, as Son over his house, whose house are *we*, if indeed we hold fast the boldness and the boast of hope firm to the end.

v7 Wherefore, even as says the Holy Spirit, To-day if ye will hear his voice,

v8 harden not your hearts, as in the provocation, in the day of temptation in the wilderness;

v9 where your fathers tempted [me], by proving [me], and saw my works forty years.

v10 Wherefore I was wroth with this generation, and said, They always err in heart; and *they* have not known my ways;

v11 so I swore in my wrath, If they shall enter into my rest.

v12 See, brethren, lest there be in any one of you a wicked heart of unbelief, in turning away from [the] living God.

v13 But encourage yourselves each day, as long as it is called To-day, that none of you be hardened by the deceitfulness of sin.

v14 For we are become companions of the Christ if indeed we hold the beginning of the assurance firm to the end;

v15 in that it is said, To-day if ye will hear his voice, do not harden your hearts, as in the provocation;

v16 (for who was it, who, having heard, provoked? but [was it] not all who came out of Egypt by Moses?

v17 And with whom was he wroth forty years? [Was it] not with those who had sinned, whose carcases fell in the wilderness?

v18 And to whom sware he that they should not enter into his rest, but to those who had not hearkened to the word?

v19 And we see that they could not enter in on account of unbelief;)
